# Client on the account

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The client is the person who pays, signs the waiver, and is the default callback — not the dog. Typical US daycare and boarding offices keep a client row so invoices, late-pickup fees, photo-release flags, and emergency callbacks attach to a human, not to a playgroup. Most shops export the client from Gingr, PetExec, Kennel Booker, Time To Pet, DaySmart Pet, or a paper account card they scan into the shared folder. Typical fields are client label or account ID, phones they already store, email if they collect it, and a household note. Two dogs under one person are still one client and two patient-dog rows. A sitter, trainer, or roommate who drops off is not automatically the account holder; that is an authorized-pickup or authorized-agent question on their card. You copy the label they stored. You do not decide legal ownership from a lobby story. You do not rename the account because someone else paid cash today. This desk is a pet-care facility office, not a veterinary hospital front desk: there is no doctor column and no estimate from a clinic price file. Starter / guess until they teach which ID is canonical and which fields may live in a shared pack. Do not present this note as how this shop already works.
