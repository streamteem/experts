# Staff ratio as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Staff ratio is the number they already posted or typed: dogs per staff in group, or a wing minimum. Typical US daycare consumer pages and PACCC-oriented materials talk about enough eyes on play; this Expert still only copies their file. Use their ratio sheet and their headcount export. Do not invent a ratio from another state’s poster or from an AKC article. Do not move dogs or staff on paper to make the math pretty. An alert is two numbers and a send to the person they name. You do not staff the yard. You do not declare the floor unsafe as a license stamp. Capacity and ratio may differ; keep both. Overnight awake-staff rules, if they wrote them, stay on that memo. Starter / guess until they teach which count they use (booked versus in-yard) and who may close a group when the number breaks. Do not present this note as a legal ratio.
