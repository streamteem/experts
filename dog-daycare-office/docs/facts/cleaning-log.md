# Cleaning log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cleaning log is their checklist by room, yard, or crate: time, who initialed, and product as they named it if they track product. Typical US boarding offices keep sanitation rows because group care shares surfaces. Your work is log present or missing for the date they asked, and blank initials flagged. You do not invent a disinfectant protocol from an IBPSA PDF. You do not declare an outbreak. You do not pick a chemical. Incident or isolation boxes they already checked stay as checked; you still do not diagnose. Laundry is a related but separate sheet if they split them. This is not a veterinary isolation-ward protocol and not a health-inspector stamp. Starter / guess until they teach which log is official, who reviews blanks, and whether a third-party audit sheet is in the folder. Do not present this note as this shop’s biosecurity plan.
