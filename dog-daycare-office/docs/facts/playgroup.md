# Playgroup assignment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Playgroup is the named yard or room they already assigned: small, large, energy, seniors, or a color code. Typical US daycares keep the assignment so the floor board matches the software. Copy the group from their file. Do not reassign from breed, age, or a video you watched. Weight and play-style flags they already stored may appear as reasons *they* used; you still do not invent a new group. Capacity of that group comes from the capacity file, not from how many dogs “look comfortable.” A dog flagged private-only does not appear on a group yard list. Camera-privacy asks still apply if that yard is on camera. You do not diagnose a limp to pull a dog from group; you copy an existing floor note and route. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* shop names groups, how often they reshuffle, and who writes the board. Do not present this note as this yard’s grouping science.
