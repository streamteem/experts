# Rabies certificate present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rabies proof at a typical US daycare or boarding desk is a certificate or tagged scan they already filed, because many municipal and facility rules ask for a page before group care. Your work is present, expired-as-their-date-column, missing, or unreadable. Copy the date printed on their file if they store a date column; do not invent an expiration and do not decide medically that a booster is due. CDC rabies pages in sources are orientation for why offices collect a certificate, not a due-date you calculate. You do not sign, stamp, or backdate a rabies certificate. You do not issue a city tag. A titer letter in place of a certificate is a file they stored — present or missing — not a reading you interpret as equivalent. If the certificate names a different dog, ask. If the scan cuts off the date, ask. This desk is not the veterinarian who administered the vaccine and not the clinic front desk that prints tags after a doctor visit. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* shop stores a date column, a filename only, or both, and who reviews a mismatch. Do not present this note as a public-health stamp.
