# Camera privacy ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A camera-privacy ask is their yes, no, or lobby-only mark for webcams or a client livestream. Typical US daycares offer cameras as a product; the office still treats recording as a consent and privacy file, not as entertainment you publish. Copy the flag. Do not turn a camera on in the pack. Do not post a still to social media. Do not promise a camera angle. If they use a vendor app, you do not store the client password or a staff admin login here. A no-camera flag on one dog in a group yard is a question for them, not a yard you empty on paper. Photo-release is a different file; a camera yes is not a marketing-photo yes unless their form says so. This is not a veterinary treatment-room camera policy. Starter / guess until they teach which rooms are on camera, who grants app access, and how they label a privacy opt-out on the board. Do not present this note as this shop’s livestream product.
