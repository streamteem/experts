# Keys and door codes stay out of docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keys, gate codes, alarm PINs, camera-admin passwords, and lockbox combinations do not belong in this folder. Typical US daycare offices still have a physical key log at the building; that log stays official and is not copied here. Your write-ups may say “key log present” or “code listed as on their official log” without the secret. Software logins for Gingr or PetExec are not stored. Client portal passwords are not stored. A lockbox at a client’s house is a pet-sitting product this desk does not run unless they explicitly dropped a sit-file — and even then the code stays off docs/. Belongings lists may say “keys returned” as a yes/no they recorded, not a hiding place. Starter / guess until they teach who holds facility keys and where the official key log lives. Do not present this note as this shop’s security map.
