# Play style as their flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Play style is a flag they already set: gentle, wrestle, herding, toy-possessive-as-they-coded, or a color. Typical US daycares use it to build yards after an eval. Copy the flag. You do not watch a video and restyle the dog. You do not invent a play-style from breed. You do not treat a play-style word as a diagnosis of compulsion or aggression. If the flag is blank and they require one for group, list it missing. Temperament flags and play-style flags may both exist; keep both codes. A change mid-stay is theirs to record after the floor sees the dog. Do not move a dog on the occupancy list to a different yard because a play-style code looks tidy. Incident rows after a scuffle still route; they do not become a new style you author. Starter / guess until they teach the code list and who may change it after a scuffle. Do not present this note as this shop’s behavior program.
