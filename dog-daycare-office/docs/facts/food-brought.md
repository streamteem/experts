# Food brought by the client

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Food-brought is the bag or container the client already sent, labeled as they labeled it. Typical US boarding offices prefer the dog’s usual food and keep the bag with the belongings list. Copy brand and instructions as written on the bag or card. You do not switch to house food because it is easier. You do not declare raw or homemade “unsafe” as a clinician; if their policy file bans raw, quote that file as a policy completeness row, not as a diagnosis. Allergy phrases stay their words. Quantity left at go-home is a belongings check, not waste you invent. House food, if they keep a backup, is a named SKU on their sheet, not a diet you prescribe. This is not a veterinary nutrition consult. Starter / guess until they teach where bags are stored, how they label two dogs’ food in one household, and whether they allow raw. Do not present this note as this kennel’s kitchen rule.
