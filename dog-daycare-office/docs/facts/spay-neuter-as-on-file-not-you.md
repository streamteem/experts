# Spay or neuter as on file, not you

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reproductive status is the word they already stored: spayed, neutered, intact, or unknown. Typical US daycares use it for playgroup rules they already wrote, such as intact-adult limits. Copy the word. You do not decide the dog is intact from a glance. You do not recommend surgery. You do not schedule a veterinary procedure. You do not invent a heat-cycle date. If their policy file says intact adults stay private, that is a setting to copy, not a diagnosis. Age-as-they-typed and this flag stay separate; do not infer one from the other. A shelter “unknown” stays unknown. A client story on the phone is not a flag change until they update the file. This is not a veterinary surgery coordinator desk. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* shop bars intact dogs from group and who updates the flag after a client reports surgery. Do not present this note as this shop’s medical advice.
