# Boarding card

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A boarding card is the stay packet for one patient-dog: dates, daycare versus overnight, feeding as written, belongings, pickup window, emergency contact, and meds-to-give as they wrote them. Typical US kennels keep a paper card on the run door and a software twin in Gingr, PetExec, Kennel Booker, or a scanned PDF folder. Completeness is yes or no on the fields they printed, plus signatures if the card requires them. You copy. You do not improve feeding, invent a walk time, or add a treat the card does not mention. You do not convert medication units. Unreadable writing is a question, not a guess. Surgery drop-off packets belong to a veterinary hospital; this card is a pet-care facility stay, even when the same dog also sees a vet elsewhere. If two cards exist for one stay, quote both and ask which is live. A missing card on a dog already in a suite is a flag for the closer they name, not a card you invent from memory. Starter / guess until they teach which packet is current for daycare versus board and who reviews meds before the dog is kenneled. Do not present this note as how this kennel already prints cards.
