# Age as they typed it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Age is the date of birth or the year they typed, or a puppy/adult/senior box they checked. Typical US daycares use a minimum age they already wrote before group care. Copy the figure. Do not estimate age from teeth or a photo. Do not decide a puppy series is incomplete as a clinician — vaccine-file rows stay present or missing. Do not move a dog to a senior group because you think they look old. If age is blank and their card requires it, ask. Playgroup and crate rules that mention age use their number, not yours. Two dogs in one household may have different ages; keep each row. This is not a veterinary wellness-stage assessment. Starter / guess until they teach the minimum age *this* shop wrote for daycare versus board and how they handle “about two years.” Do not present this note as this shop’s puppy policy.
