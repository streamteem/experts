# Go-home bath add-on

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A go-home bath is a named add-on they already booked so the dog leaves cleaner than play. Typical US boarding offices put it on the last-day card and a groom or bath list. Copy yes or no as booked. Do not add a bath because the dog looks muddy. Do not invent a bath fee; quote the fee file. Allergy or skin words stay as written; you do not pick a medicated shampoo as treatment. Fasting is not required unless their bath form says so. Belongings such as a bandana they sent stay on the belongings list. Photo-release still gates any departure portrait. A cancelled bath stays cancelled; do not keep it on the list to fill the tub. This is not a veterinary medicated-bath order. Starter / guess until they teach cutoff time for same-day bath adds and who runs the tub. Do not present this note as this shop’s spa menu.
