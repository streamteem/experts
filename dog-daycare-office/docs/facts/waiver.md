# Waiver on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A waiver is the signed liability or services acknowledgment they already use before care. Typical US daycare and boarding offices will not start a trial or a board without it. Completeness is signed, dated, version if they track versions, or missing. You do not rewrite the waiver. You do not explain what a court would do. You do not say the waiver “covers” a bite as a legal opinion. A complete waiver is not a medical clearance and not a vaccine decision. Photo-release and camera-privacy may be separate pages; list each. If the PDF is a scan of only page one, the rest is missing. E-sign counts only if their process says it counts. This is not a veterinary informed-consent for surgery. Starter / guess until they teach the current version date and whether a yearly re-sign is required on their file. Do not present this note as this shop’s legal advice.
