# Vaccine file present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vaccine file at a typical US daycare or boarding office is a PDF, fax image, or portal download the client already provided so the facility can see that a required page is on file. Your work is present, dated, missing, or unreadable — not a medical reading. Most shops keep an index in Gingr, PetExec, or a vaccine-file folder they named. Typical columns are patient-dog, client, filename, date on the page if printed, and a present/missing flag. You do not decide a vaccine is due. You do not say the dog is overdue as a clinician. You do not compare lines to a CDC or AVMA schedule and declare a pass or fail. You do not interpret a titer in place of a certificate. If their written intake list names rabies, DHPP or DAPP, Bordetella, or canine influenza as required pages, you list those filenames as present or missing only. A page that names a different clinic or a different dog is a mismatch question, not a merge. This is not a veterinary reminder list and not a clinic day book. Starter / guess until they teach which named pages *this* shop requires for daycare versus overnight board and who reviews a questionable scan. Do not present this note as this shop’s medical policy.
