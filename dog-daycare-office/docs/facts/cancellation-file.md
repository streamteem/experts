# Cancellation file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cancellation rules live in a dated policy or a software setting they exported: notice hours, peak exceptions, and whether a prepaid pack burns. Typical US boarding offices tighten notice before holidays. Quote the file. Do not invent a 24-hour or 48-hour rule from another kennel. Do not refund. Do not keep a deposit as a courtesy you decide. A no-show is a status they coded, not a punishment paragraph you write. Waitlist conversion after a cancel follows their waitlist process. You still do not message the waitlist as a product. If the client cancelled by text, that is their recorded process to copy, not a legal acceptance you stamp. This is not a veterinary surgery-deposit policy. Starter / guess until they teach which PDF is current and who may override a late cancel. Do not present this note as this shop’s terms.
