# Belonging photo file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A belonging photo is a picture they already took of inbound items so go-home matches drop-off. Typical US daycare offices drop those images in a stay folder or a photo-folder they named. Index filename, patient-dog, date, and a short item word they used. You do not post the photo. You do not use it as a marketing still unless a photo-release says yes and they asked. You do not decide two leashes are the same item across stays. Camera livestream stills are not belonging photos unless they labeled them that way. Do not store extra client faces if the task only needs the leash. A missing photo on a stay that their process requires is a gap, not a picture you invent. Go-home mismatch flags quote the inbound filename. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* shop photographs every stay or only overnight, and who takes the go-home match photo. Do not present this note as this shop’s camera policy.
