# Peak occupancy period

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Peak is a named window they already marked: holiday week, summer Friday, spring break, or a local event week. Typical US boarding offices attach deposits, blackouts, and a tighter waitlist to peak. Copy the peak flag from their calendar or fee memo. Do not declare a peak because the lobby looks busy. Do not lift a blackout to squeeze one more dog. Capacity still comes from the capacity file; peak does not raise the max unless they wrote a different peak cap. Cancellation windows are often stricter in peak — quote their cancellation file. You do not invent a peak surcharge. Daycare packs may be paused in peak if their memo says so. This is occupancy planning, not a veterinary production goal. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* shop marks peak in the software and who may book past the waitlist. Do not present this note as this town’s busy season.
