# Late or missed med as they coded it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A late-med or missed-med mark is their log code when a scheduled give was late or blank. Typical US boarding offices want the office to flag blanks for the closer, not to invent a makeup dose. Copy the code and the time they recorded. You do not decide clinical harm. You do not write a catch-up plan. You do not convert units to “make up” a miss. Route to the person they name. The medication log present/missing fact is related; this row is the exception code on that log. You still never prescribe. A client story on the phone about a missed pill at home is not a log entry you create unless they asked you to note their words as a message. Starter / guess until they teach who is interrupted for a missed overnight dose versus a daycare lunch pill. Do not present this note as this shop’s medical standing order.
