# Waitlist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A waitlist is an ordered file of client and patient-dog rows that want a date or a playgroup that is already at their capacity. Typical US daycare and boarding offices keep it in the same software as the calendar or in a spreadsheet they named. Copy order as stored. Do not jump a friend to the top. Do not confirm a stay from the waitlist until they asked you to draft the offer from their process. Vaccine-file and waiver completeness may be a gate they already wrote before a waitlist row can convert; list those as present or missing, still without a medical due-date. A deposit, if they collect one to hold a peak night, is a fee-file row, not a card you run. You do not message the list as a blast product. If two waitlists exist (daycare versus board), keep them split. Starter / guess until they teach the sort key *this* shop uses and who calls the next name. Do not present this note as this shop’s fairness policy.
