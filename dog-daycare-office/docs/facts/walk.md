# Walk as a booked extra or routine

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A walk is a potty break, leash walk, or hike they already put on the card or a walk sheet. Typical US boarding offices use walks for private-care dogs or as a paid extra. Copy times and length as written. Do not add a hike because the breed “needs exercise.” Do not invent a distance. Group-yard play is not a walk unless they coded it that way. Escape-log and leash-belongings notes matter on walk rows; you still do not take the dog out. If a walk was missed, that is their log to copy, not a makeup plan you write. Weather closures they posted may cancel walks; quote the calendar. This is not a trainer product and not a veterinary exercise prescription. Starter / guess until they teach which dogs get walks versus yard time on *this* floor and who initials the walk sheet. Do not present this note as this shop’s exercise program.
