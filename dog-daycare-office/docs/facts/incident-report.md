# Incident report file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An incident report is the named form they already use after a floor event. Typical US daycare offices keep a paper or PDF form plus a log row. Completeness is the fields they printed: time, dogs, people, what they wrote happened, first-aid-as-they-checked-not-you, and who was notified. You do not finish a narrative to sound more professional if that changes meaning. You do not diagnose. You do not add a legal conclusion. Photos attach as filenames if they already took them. Bite rows still follow their bite form if that is a separate packet. Do not copy the full narrative into a public write-up when IDs and “form present” will do. This is not a veterinary medical record. Starter / guess until they teach form version, who signs, and where PDFs land. Do not present this note as this shop’s insurance statement.
