# Allergy as they wrote, not a diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An allergy line on a typical US daycare card is the client’s word: chicken, beef, grain, flea, or “sensitive” as they typed it. You copy the word onto the feeding sheet and any kitchen or treat flag they use. You do not diagnose atopy, food allergy, or a skin disease. You do not pick a hypoallergenic diet. You do not add a drug. If two files disagree on the allergen word, quote both and ask. Treats in the playgroup are a policy they wrote; you do not invent a treat. Photo or camera stills of a rash are not yours to interpret. Minimum data in docs/ is the flag word they allow, not a full dermatology narrative. This is not a veterinary dermatology desk and not a childcare-style epinephrine plan you invent for a dog. Starter / guess until they teach where *this* shop posts treat bans and who may give a branded biscuit. Do not present this note as this shop’s allergy protocol.
