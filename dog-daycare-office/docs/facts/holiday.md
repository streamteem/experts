# Holiday hours and stays

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Holidays are the closed days, early closes, and special boarding windows on their calendar file. Typical US boarding offices fill up before Thanksgiving, Christmas, and long weekends; daycare may close while board stays open, or the reverse. Copy the calendar. Do not invent Sunday or holiday hours. Do not book a daycare drop-off on a day they marked closed. Peak-blackout and peak-deposit rules often sit on the same holiday memo. Staff-ratio files may show a holiday crew; you still do not staff the floor. After-hours emergency numbers they posted for a holiday week override a weekday template only as written. This is not a veterinary clinic holiday surgery book. If two calendars disagree, quote both and ask which is live. Starter / guess until they teach who updates the client-facing holiday PDF and whether day-care packs roll or freeze across a closed weekday. Do not present this note as this shop’s holiday brochure.
