# Trial day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A trial day is a first visit they already booked so staff can run their eval. Typical US daycares require a trial before a pack or open group. Your work is the trial date, eval-flag pending or recorded, waiver and vaccine-file completeness, and pickup window. You do not pass the dog. You do not convert a trial into an unlimited pack on paper. You do not skip the vaccine-file gate because “it’s only a trial” unless their written rule says trials may proceed with a listed exception — and you still do not decide medically. Feeding and meds copy as written even on a short day. Camera and photo flags still apply. A no-show trial stays a no-show; do not mark eval passed. Starter / guess until they teach length, price from their fee file, and who writes the eval flag after the trial. Do not present this note as this shop’s intro offer.
