# Late-pickup fee from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late-pickup money is a number from their fee file: grace minutes if they wrote them, then a fee or a per-minute line as printed. Typical US daycare offices charge when a dog is still here after the posted pickup window. Quote the file. Do not invent a fee from another shop or from “what we usually do.” Do not waive on paper. Do not convert a late daycare pickup into an overnight board fee unless their file has that conversion and they asked you to draft it. The occupancy list still shows the dog as here until they record go-home; the fee row is separate. You do not take payment or store a card number. Holiday or peak hours may change the posted close; use the calendar they named. Starter / guess until they teach grace, who clocks the official pickup time, and whether a text “I’m five minutes away” stops the fee on their policy. Do not present this note as this shop’s price list.
