# Microchip as on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A microchip number is an identifier they already stored from the client or a scanner note they typed. Copy the number as written. You do not log into a registry from this folder. You do not decide two dogs are the same animal because chips look close. You do not implant a chip. A missing chip is a blank, not a medical failure you announce. License-tag and rabies-certificate numbers are different fields; do not paste one into the other. Lost-dog or escape description sheets may include the chip as they wrote it; you still do not publish. This is not a veterinary implant visit and not a clinic travel-certificate desk. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* shop scans at intake and which field is canonical when card and software disagree. Do not present this note as this shop’s registry process.
