# Weight from their scale note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Weight, when this office needs it, is the number on their scale note or the last weight they stored — for crate-size or playgroup rules they already wrote, not for drug math. Copy pounds or kilograms as stored. Do not convert unless they asked and both units are on the file. Do not estimate from breed. Do not decide the dog is overweight as a clinical finding. If weight is missing and their crate chart needs it, ask. A stale weight with no date is a question, not a number you refresh from memory. This is the opposite of a veterinary anesthesia calculation: you never compute a dose. Starter / guess until they teach how often *this* shop weighs daycare dogs and whether overnight board requires a same-day scale note. Do not present this note as this shop’s nutrition plan.
