# License tag present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A city or county license tag is a page or a tag number they already filed if their intake asks for it. Your work is present, number-as-written, missing, or expired-as-their-date-column. You do not issue a license. You do not decide a rabies vaccine is due because the city site would want a current certificate — that medical due-date is still forbidden. You may list the license page missing next to a missing rabies-certificate page as two file rows. Do not invent a municipality. If they do not collect licenses, say the field is not on their card. A photo of a tag is still a file check, not a city stamp you apply. This is not a veterinary tag-printing desk after a doctor visit. Starter / guess until they teach which towns *this* shop collects for and whether a photo of the tag counts. Do not present this note as this city’s licensing law.
