# Photo release

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A photo release is the signed yes or no that lets this shop use a picture of the dog, as their form already printed. Typical US daycare offices collect it at intake next to the waiver. Completeness is signed, dated, yes/no as checked, or missing. You do not post. You do not crop a “cute” still into a write-up when a filename and patient-dog label will do. Camera-privacy is a different flag. Belonging photos for lost-item proof may sit in an operations folder under their process and still are not marketing. A missing release means no marketing use in the pack; it does not mean you invent a no and hide a file they already signed. Two dogs in one household need the flag each row if their form is per dog. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* shop’s release covers lobby TV, social, and print, or only some of those, and who may take a go-home portrait. Do not present this note as this brand’s social policy.
