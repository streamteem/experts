# Boarding dates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Boarding dates are the check-in and go-home dates they already booked, plus a pickup window if the card has one. Typical US offices export them from the calendar or boarding-csv. Copy the dates. Do not extend a stay because the client “might fly later.” Do not shorten a stay to free a suite. Confirm the file date before you treat a CSV as today’s occupancy. A daycare date without an overnight is still a date on the occupancy list. Peak-blackout dates stay blocked. If check-in and checkout are the same calendar day, that is daycare or a day-board as they coded it, not a night you invent. Time zones and midnight cutoffs follow their file. This is not a hotel-legal occupancy tax stamp and not a veterinary hospitalization date. Starter / guess until they teach cutoff times for same-day adds and who may change a go-home date in the software. Do not present this note as this shop’s booking rules.
