# Eval flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An eval flag is their yes, no, pending, or private-only mark after a temperament or play assessment they already ran — often on a trial day. Typical US daycares will not book open group until that flag exists. Your work is the flag as stored, the eval date if they store one, and missing-eval rows for dogs already on the calendar. You do not pass or fail the dog. You do not restage the eval from notes. You do not override a “no group” because capacity is short. A trial-day row without an eval flag is a question, not a pass you award so the roster looks full. Failed or incomplete evals stay as they coded them; you do not soften the word. This is not a veterinary exam and not a diagnosis of anxiety or aggression. Starter / guess until they teach how long an eval stays valid on *this* shop’s file and who records the flag after the trial. Do not present this note as this facility’s pass criteria.
