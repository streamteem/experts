# Vet-release form completeness

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vet-release form is their permission page so staff may take the dog to the listed clinic if the client cannot be reached, as their packet already printed. Completeness is signed, dated, clinic name present, and limits as they checked — not a treatment plan you write. Typical US boarding offices keep this next to the waiver. You do not sign for the owner. You do not stamp as a veterinarian. You do not decide the dog must go to the clinic. You do not diagnose to justify transport. If the form is missing, list it missing. Dollar limits they wrote are copied, not invented. This is not a veterinary estimate and not a VCPR. After-hours numbers they posted may appear on the same packet; quote them. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* shop requires the form for daycare as well as board and who keeps the signed PDF. Do not present this note as this shop’s emergency-medicine protocol.
