# Laundry and bedding sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Laundry is their sheet for beds, towels, and client bedding that goes through the wash. Typical US boarding offices split house bedding from client belongings so a go-home bed is not lost in the dryer. Copy inbound bedding on the belongings list and any wash-and-return box they checked. You do not decide a bed is too dirty as a medical finding. You do not throw a bed away on paper. If they charge a laundry add-on, the fee comes from the fee file. Cleaning-log rooms and laundry loads are easy to mix; keep the sheet they named. Allergy flags as they wrote them may appear as “wash separately” only if their card says so — still not a diagnosis. Starter / guess until they teach who owns the laundry closet and how they label two households’ beds. Do not present this note as this shop’s wash recipe.
