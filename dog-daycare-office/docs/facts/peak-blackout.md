# Peak blackout dates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A peak blackout is a date they already marked as no-pack, no-discount, no-new-board, or daycare-closed. Typical US boarding offices print blackouts on a holiday memo next to the calendar. Copy the rule as written. Do not lift a blackout for a regular. Do not invent a blackout because you think the building will be full. Waitlist still follows order. Deposits and cancellation windows on blackout dates come from those files, not from a stricter rule you write. Capacity is unchanged unless they filed a different peak cap. You do not message a “last rooms” blast. If the software calendar and the PDF memo disagree, quote both and ask. A prepaid pack used on a blackout date is a policy question, not a punch you burn on paper. Starter / guess until they teach who edits blackouts and whether a prepaid pack is paused or refunded on those dates as their file says. Do not present this note as this shop’s holiday marketing.
