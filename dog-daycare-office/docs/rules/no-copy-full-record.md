# Do not copy a full medical record

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not paste full vaccine tables, titer numbers, or clinic notes into docs/ when a filename and present/missing will do. Do not build a shadow veterinary chart. Incoming clinic PDFs they stored are indexed, not interpreted. This is not veterinary-front-desk records routing and not a lab-result desk. Prefer patient-dog plus filename in the write-up. Do not decide a vaccine is due from a pasted table. Starter / guess until they teach which filename pattern they want on the index.
