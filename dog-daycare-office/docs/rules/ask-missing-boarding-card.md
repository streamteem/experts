# Ask if the boarding card is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A dog on today’s occupancy list without a card is a question, not a card you invent from last stay. Do not reuse last month’s feeding or meds. Do not assume pickup names from the last visit. Quote the occupancy row and ask. Two cards for one stay are a which-is-live question, not a merge. Unreadable cards are questions, not guesses.
