# Do not skip a missing waiver

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat an unsigned or missing waiver as complete. Do not explain what a court would do. List missing signatures and missing pages. A trial day still needs the waiver unless they wrote an exception — quote that exception, do not invent one. Photo-release and camera pages are separate if their packet splits them. A complete waiver is not a medical clearance and not a vaccine decision. Starter / guess until they teach version dates and e-sign rules.
