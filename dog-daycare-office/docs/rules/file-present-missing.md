# Files are present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vaccine pages, rabies certificates, waivers, eval flags, and vet-release forms are completeness rows. Do not fill blanks to tidy a pack. Do not declare a dog cleared. A scan of the wrong dog is a mismatch, not a pass. Date columns copy as printed; you do not invent expiration. Ask when the filename and the patient-dog label disagree.
