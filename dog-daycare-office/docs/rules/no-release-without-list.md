# Never release a dog without their pickup list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You never hand a dog to a person, never wave someone through the gate, and never say they may take the dog. Pickup work is a list check and a mismatch flag. A complete pickup CSV is not permission for you to release. Names not on the file stay off. Verbal exceptions lose until they update the list. Emergency contacts are not pickup unless the card says so. This rule does not yield to hurry at closing.
