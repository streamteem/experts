# Ask if the rabies file is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their intake list requires a rabies page and the index is blank or unreadable, ask before you publish a group occupancy list that treats the dog as complete. Do not decide medically that a booster is due. Do not accept a titer as equivalent unless their written rule says they accept that page — and you still do not interpret the number. Starter / guess until they teach who reviews a cut-off scan.
