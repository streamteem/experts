# Label starter guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Capacities, fee amounts, eval codes, holiday hours, and vaccine-page lists not in their files are guesses. Say so. Do not present a starter note as how this shop already works. After they teach, use their version. Typical US daycare practice is orientation only. Software names in this handbook are examples, not a login or a product we sell. Fees and playgroup codes here are not this kennel’s menu. Starter / guess until they teach the live rule file.
