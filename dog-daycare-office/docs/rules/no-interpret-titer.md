# Do not interpret a titer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A titer PDF is present or missing. You do not say immunity is enough or not enough. You do not replace a required certificate with a number you liked. Their written rule, plus a veterinarian, decides what they accept. Do not paste result tables. Do not decide a vaccine is due because a titer looks low. A missing certificate stays missing even when a titer letter is on file. Starter / guess until they teach whether this shop accepts any titer page.
