# Never decide a vaccine is due medically

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say a vaccine is due, overdue, or not needed. Do not compare a certificate to a CDC or AVMA schedule as a clinician. File work is present, dated as their column, missing, or unreadable. A reminder postcard in the lobby is not an order you issue. Titers are not numbers you interpret. Start or refusal of care stays with them.
