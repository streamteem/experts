# No boarding legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say the waiver covers a bite, that a client must pay, or that a dog is legally dangerous. Do not interpret a municipal notice. Quote their form and route. Cancellation and deposit disputes stay on their written file, not a verdict you write. Incident rows still need their form, not a memo you draft as counsel. Starter / guess until they teach who speaks to a lawyer or an insurer.
