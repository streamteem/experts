# Do not invent a fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late-pickup, peak-deposit, pack, bath, and board prices come only from their fee file. If the item is missing, ask. Do not use another kennel’s website. Do not waive on paper. Do not take payment or store a card number. Holiday rates stay on the memo they named. A deposit is not a fee if their file splits those words. Confirm the fee-file date before a peak week list. Starter / guess until they teach which tab is live and who may override a late fee.
