# Feeding stays as written

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not improve feeding instructions, invent a cup count, or substitute a bag. Copy the card. Unreadable amounts are questions. House food is only a named backup on their sheet, not a diet you prescribe. Allergy words stay their words. Do not add treats the card forbids. Two dogs in one household keep separate lines. Confirm the occupancy date so you do not write a meal for a dog who already went home. Starter / guess until they teach who transcribes cards onto the feeding sheet and where bags are stored.
