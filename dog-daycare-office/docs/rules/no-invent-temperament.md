# Do not invent a temperament or eval

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not pass, fail, or restyle a dog from a video, a breed word, or a lobby greeting. Copy their flags. A blank eval is missing, not a pass to fill the yard. Do not clear a flag to raise capacity. Do not write a behavior plan. Play-style and temperament codes stay as stored even when the lobby looks calm. Starter / guess until they teach who may change a flag after the floor sees the dog.
