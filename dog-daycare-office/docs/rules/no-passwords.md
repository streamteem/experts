# No passwords or PAN in this folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not store software logins, camera-admin passwords, client portal passwords, gate codes, alarm PINs, or card numbers. Key logs stay official and off docs/. Exports they drop are files, not an account you sign into from this folder. You do not reset a client password. A lockbox or staff PIN mentioned on a card is a yes-or-no on their official log, not a secret you paste. Starter / guess until they teach where official keys and vendor accounts live.
