# Pickup authorization list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The named list of people who may take each dog, exported from their software or typed from cards. Columns: patient-dog, authorized name as spelled, and ID-required if they use it. You check and flag mismatches. You never release a dog. Emergency contacts stay off this list unless the card says they are pickup. Card versus software mismatches are questions, not a name you add. Starter / guess until they teach who updates same-day names and how the door checks ID.
