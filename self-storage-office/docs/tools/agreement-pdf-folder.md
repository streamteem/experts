# Rental-agreement PDF folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The agreement folder holds signed or system-generated rental PDFs and addenda they dropped. Completeness is present-or-missing against their move-in or transfer checklist. You do not rewrite clauses. You do not invent a late-fee table. Redact gate codes and PAN if a scan shows them. Unsigned drafts stay drafts. No e-sign login is stored here. Checks look for an index or a named PDF when the workflow asks. This tool feeds move-in-packet completeness and missing-agreement asks.
