# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is their extra workbook: transfer tickets, cut-lock log extracts, merchandise sold, or a scratch occupancy they maintain beside the official export. Typical US offices keep one numbered book for office tasks. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a unit number or a fee. The Expert may run Python (pandas, matplotlib) to write a summary CSV or a named chart in out/ from columns they named; if a package is missing, say so. No login is stored here. Checks look for the columns the workflow names. This tool supports transfer-list and other office packs.
