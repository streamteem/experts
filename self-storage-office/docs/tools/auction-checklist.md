# Auction-file checklist

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The auction checklist is their required-file list: notice PDFs, certified-mail log, inventory form, photos, calendar date, buyer log. You check present-or-missing. You do not invent a sale date. You do not give auction legal advice. Completeness is not permission to sell. If they have no checklist, ask for one; do not invent a national list. No auction-site password is stored here. Checks look for unit and file or item columns when the workflow asks. This tool feeds the auction-file-completeness pack only.
