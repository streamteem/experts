# Rate and promo file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The rate file is their street, web, in-place, and promo list by size and type, often a CSV or a printed PDF they scanned. You copy rates as labeled. You do not invent a street rate or a first-month special. Expired promos stay labeled expired and become asks. Tax is a separate line if they split it. No revenue-management login belongs here. Checks look for size and rate when the workflow asks. This tool feeds the rate-from-file pack and any quote they will say out loud after they approve.
