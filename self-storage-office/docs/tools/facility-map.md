# Facility map PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The facility map is their site plan or unit-number plot as a PDF or image. You use it to group occupancy and photo packs by building when they asked. You do not invent a unit that is not on the mix and not on the map. If map and mix disagree, quote both. Gate drawings are process, not codes. Camera marks trigger the privacy ask, not a video pull. No CAD login is stored here. Checks look for the map file when a workflow needs it. This tool orients unit-mix and photo-condition packs.
