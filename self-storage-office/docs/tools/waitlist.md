# Waitlist sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The waitlist sheet is name, phone or email as they stored it, size wanted, climate flag, and date added, in their existing order. You do not jump a row. You do not invent a phone. A waitlist seat is not a hold unless they coded a reservation on occupancy. Matching vacants are questions, not assignments. Min-necessary: no extra ID. No CRM password belongs here. Checks look for name or size_wanted when the workflow asks. This tool feeds the waitlist pack.
