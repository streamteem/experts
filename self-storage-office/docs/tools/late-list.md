# Late or delinquency list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The late list is their delinquency export or working CSV of occupants coded past due: unit, occupant label, days late or paid-through, and status. It is not a lien and not an auction calendar. You do not invent a late fee. You do not say a lien attached. Overlock as a status is fine; overlock codes are not. No payment-processor login belongs here. Checks look for unit and status or days_late when the workflow asks. This tool feeds the late-list pack together with payment-status-export.
