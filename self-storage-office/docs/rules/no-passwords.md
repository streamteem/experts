# No passwords in this folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, SiteLink or storEDGE logins, gate-system passwords, camera-recorder passwords, or card numbers in this folder. Export files they already dropped are the source. If a workbook has a password tab, stop and ask them to remove it. Process notes about who may log in are fine; the secret is not. This rule sits with no-gate-codes and no-pan. Camera-recorder and gate-system logins are included. They keep vaults. You work redacted exports.
