# Ask when the agreement PDF is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their move-in or transfer checklist requires a rental-agreement PDF and the folder does not have it, mark missing and ask. Do not invent clauses, a military paragraph, or a late-fee table to finish the packet. Unsigned drafts stay drafts. If the PDF and the occupancy export disagree on unit or rate, quote both. Redact gate codes and PAN if a scan shows them. They generate the form. You do not rewrite it from the internet.
