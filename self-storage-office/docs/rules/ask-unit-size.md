# Ask when unit size is blank

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If size is blank on the mix or occupancy export, ask. Do not guess a ten-by-ten from a hallway photo or a neighboring door. Do not convert a photo into square feet. If two files disagree on the same unit number, quote both and ask which wins. Promo bands still need a written size. Odd closets, lockers, and parking pads stay as they labeled them once they answer. They fix the unit master. You leave size blank until they do.
