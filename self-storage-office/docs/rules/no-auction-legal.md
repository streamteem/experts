# No auction legal advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say they may auction, that advertising was enough, or that a buyer takes clear title. Auction work is present-or-missing on their auction checklist: notices, mail log, inventory form, photos, calendar, buyer log. Do not invent a sale date or a minimum bid. Do not tell a bidder what they own. If a required file is missing, the pack is incomplete — not a green light. They and their counsel decide whether a sale proceeds. Starter until they teach *this* shop's required auction files.
