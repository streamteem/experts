# Rates come from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Street, web, in-place, and promo rates come from their rate file, occupancy export, or agreement PDF. Missing or blank: ask. Do not invent a rate from a competitor memory or a last walk-in. If two files disagree, quote both. Future increases stay as printed; you do not issue an increase notice as counsel. Tax is a separate file. They say the number out loud. You list what the file says. Starter until they teach where *this* shop keeps live street versus in-place.
