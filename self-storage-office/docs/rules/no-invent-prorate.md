# Do not invent a prorate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Partial-month rent comes from their written proration rule or the software estimate they exported. Do not apply a thirty-day custom from another shop or a statute you remember. Blank estimate: ask. Do not net a promo into a new number you made up. Transfer mid-month is the same ask. Tax still follows their tax file. They confirm the first amount they will say. You quote their factor or mark it missing.
