# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US self-storage practice — a starter. After they teach, their exports, checklists, fee tables, and hours win. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. Do not say your policy is unless they stored that policy. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Ask rather than assume SiteLink, storEDGE, or a climate hall. Teach the folder; then follow the folder.
