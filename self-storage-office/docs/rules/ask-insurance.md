# Ask when insurance proof is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their move-in checklist requires a protection plan, tenant-insurance proof, or a signed waiver and the folder has none, mark missing and ask. Do not invent a premium. Do not say goods are covered. Do not invent a waiver to hurry a start. Flood questions are file asks, not FEMA legal determinations. Vehicle pads may need a different proof if their vehicle file says so. They enroll or they accept the waiver. You flag the gap.
