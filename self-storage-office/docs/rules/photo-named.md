# Photos stay named and real

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Index only photos that are in the folder. Use their naming rule; if names are messy, list filename and unit as a question. Do not invent a photo. Do not put card PAN, full ID faces, or gate codes in a renamed file. Camera stills follow the privacy ask. Auction and condition sets stay labeled by type. A missing photo is a missing file, not a hallway memory. They shoot. You index.
