# Do not invent tax

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tax percents and taxable flags come from their tax file or software tax code. Do not invent a county rate. Do not decide whether storage rent is taxable as a legal conclusion. If the agreement and the rate file disagree, quote both. Merchandise tax and rent tax stay split if their file splits them. You do not file a return and you do not send money. They and their bookkeeper apply the code.
