# Only their promo file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Promos come from their dated promo or rate file. Do not invent first-month-free, half off, or a military special. Expired on the file: ask before anyone uses it. Stacking two promos is a question if the file is silent. In-place occupants do not get a new-move-in promo unless the sheet says so. A promo is not a late-fee waiver. They decide what to honor. You list live versus expired as labeled.
