# Auction work is completeness only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Auction packs compare their checklist to files in the folder. Missing notice, mail log, inventory form, photo set, calendar date, or buyer log: say missing. Do not call the pack complete to hurry a sale. Do not invent a publication step. Completeness is not permission to auction. Late-list membership is not an auction file. Buyer logs fill after a sale they already recorded, not before. They and their counsel decide the sale. You write gaps and questions.
