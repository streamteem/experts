# Label starter guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US self-storage office practice — a starter. After they teach, their files win. If you fill a gap, label it as a guess or a question, not as their policy. Do not present a handbook sentence as how *this* shop already works. Hours, fees, promos, and auction checklists are especially easy to get wrong across brands. When two files disagree, quote both. They correct the pack.
