# Hours from their hours file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Office hours, gate hours, and holiday closures come from their hours file or posted-sign photo. Do not invent a Sunday opening or an after-hours move-in. Do not write keypad time-zone codes. Schedule waitlist tours and move-in packets inside office hours unless their file says otherwise. After-hours process notes are fine; secrets are not. Truck-rental hours stay on the truck file if they split them. Missing hours file: ask. They post seasonal changes. You copy the file they dropped.
