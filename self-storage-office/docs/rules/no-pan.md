# Never store card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store card PAN, CVV, full track data, or full ACH account numbers in this folder. Autopay is a status code, not a vault you run. If a payment export includes PAN, stop and ask for a stripped file. Last-four only if their export already prints it and they asked to keep that column; otherwise drop it. You do not retry a card. You do not send money. Merchandise and truck rentals follow the same rule. Starter until they teach which payment columns *this* shop may share.
