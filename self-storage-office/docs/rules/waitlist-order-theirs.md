# Keep their waitlist order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List waitlist rows in the order their file already uses. Do not jump a referral to the top. Do not drop a stale row unless their purge rule says to expire it. A waitlist seat is not a hold and not a reserved unit unless they coded a reservation. If order is ambiguous, ask; do not invent a lottery. Matching vacants are questions for them, not assignments you make. They notify the next person under their process.
