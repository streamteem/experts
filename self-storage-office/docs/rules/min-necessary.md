# Minimum necessary identity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy the least identity needed for the pack: ID type present or missing, occupant label as they already print. Do not copy a Social Security number, a full driver's-license number, or a passport scan into docs. Do not dump extra faces onto a chart. Emergency contacts and alts stay as they stored them, not extra addresses you add. Military orders: present-or-missing, not a full DoD ID on a list. They verify in person. You keep the shared folder thin.
