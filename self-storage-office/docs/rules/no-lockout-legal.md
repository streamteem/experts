# No lockout as a legal stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not tell them they may lock someone out, overlock, cut a lock, or deny gate access as a legal conclusion. Overlock and lock-cut are their process flags and logs. You may list that they coded overlock. You do not hang a lock. You do not invent the day-count that triggers one. Auction-path access limits stay on their checklist without your stamp. They apply metal under their process and counsel. You flag codes and missing process notes.
