# No lien legal advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say a lien attached, a notice was valid, or they may deny access as a legal conclusion. Lien work on this desk is file completeness against their checklist. Typical US offices generate statutory-looking letters from software; you still do not interpret the statute. Quote their form name and date as printed. If they ask whether they can lien, tell them to ask their counsel. DOJ or SSA pages in sources are orientation only. Starter until they teach which notice names *this* shop uses.
