# Do not invent a late fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late fees, admin fees, cut-lock fees, and NSF fees come only from their fee file or the agreement fee table. No file: ask. Do not apply a usual-in-this-state dollar. Do not opine that a printed fee is lawful. Do not add a fee to the late list that the file does not name. Promos do not waive late fees unless the promo sheet says so. They post fees. You quote the table or mark it missing. This is a hard stop of the desk.
