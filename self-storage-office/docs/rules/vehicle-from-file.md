# Vehicle rows only from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List vehicle, RV, boat, or trailer spaces only if their mix, parking, or vehicle addendum says they rent them. Do not invent a pad count. Do not title-search a VIN. Registration or insurance proof is present-or-missing if their checklist asks. Lien or auction of a vehicle is still completeness of their vehicle file, not a DMV opinion. Min-necessary on VIN if they asked to truncate. They decide whether a walk-in may park.
