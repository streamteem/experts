# Dollies and hand trucks

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dollies and hand trucks are equipment they lend or rent per their equipment note, not a moving-company product and not a safety stamp. Typical US offices keep one or two appliance dollies and ask for a license hold or a small deposit on their form if they use one. You copy that process from their file. You do not invent a rental rate. You do not diagnose a broken wheel as a repair ticket beyond their note. You do not store a deposit card PAN. Truck-rental if any is a different product. Injury notes route to their incident file, not an OSHA stamp you issue. Carts stay on the cart fact; do not mix the two counts. This Expert lists dolly policy present or missing. They lend and they inspect. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop lends free, rents, or has no dollies, and where the sign-out sheet lives.
