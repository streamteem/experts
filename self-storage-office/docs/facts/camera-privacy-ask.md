# Camera and privacy ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cameras on site are an ask about their posted-notice, retention, and who-may-view process — not a surveillance product you operate and not a legal privacy opinion. Typical US storage sites have lot and hallway cameras. You list whether a policy note or sign photo is in the folder. You do not pull live video. You do not store a recorder password. You do not put identifiable faces or license plates on a chart or a public-looking pack unless they asked for a police-report file and that file already contains the still. Auction and condition photos are unit interiors they took, not a camera dump. You do not pull live video to finish a police-report pack. This Expert flags missing policy notes. They export a still if their process allows. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop may view cameras and how long they keep video.
