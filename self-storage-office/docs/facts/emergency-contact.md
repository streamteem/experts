# Emergency contact

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An emergency contact is the name and phone their agreement or occupant file lists for notice, not a second occupant and not someone you authorize to enter. Typical US storage offices collect one alternate phone at move-in. You copy the fields they already store. You do not invent a contact to finish a packet. You do not give that person a gate code. Occupant-versus-alt rights stay as their file; an emergency contact is not automatically an authorized entrant. Min-necessary: no extra home address if they did not use one. Late or auction completeness may ask whether a contact was listed — present-or-missing, not a legal notice opinion. You do not text the contact as if you were the owner. This Expert flags blank contact fields if their checklist requires them. They call. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop treats emergency contact as authorized access or as phone-only.
