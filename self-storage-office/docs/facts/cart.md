# Carts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Carts are the rolling carts or wagons the site lends or rents per their hours or equipment note, not a delivery crew and not an inventory you invent. Typical US storage offices park a few carts near the office and ask that they return to a painted stall. You may list cart count if they gave a count sheet, or note that no count file exists. You do not invent a missing-cart fee. You do not store a padlock code on a cart chain. After-hours cart use follows the hours file. Damage notes belong on their incident or merchandise file. Combo move-ins may need two carts — still not a promise you staff a mover. This Expert lists their cart note or marks it missing. They retrieve strays. Starter until they teach how many carts *this* shop owns, whether any are rented, and who walks the property for abandoned carts.
