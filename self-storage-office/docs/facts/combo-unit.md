# Combo or multiple units

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A combo is two or more units rented to the same occupant under their account file, not a merged physical space you assume and not a package price you invent. Typical US offices allow an occupant to add a second unit or a parking pad on the same account. You list each unit number, size, type, and rate from the occupancy export. You do not blend two rates into one made-up number. Late status can differ by unit if their export does; do not smear one unit's late flag onto the other unless their file says the account is late as a whole. Auction-file completeness stays per unit. Gate access is still process, not codes. Corporate accounts may combo many units — same rule, min-necessary names. This Expert lists the set. They decide discounts only from their promo or corporate file. Starter until they teach how *this* shop links units on one account and whether notices print per unit or per account.
