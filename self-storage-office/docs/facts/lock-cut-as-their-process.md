# Lock cut as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A lock cut is a step on their written process or cut-lock log, not a legal stamp that they may enter, lien, or auction. Typical US storage offices log who cut, which unit, date, reason code (move-out leftover, overlock swap, auction inventory, emergency), and the replacement lock if they hang one. You copy that log. You do not tell them they may cut. You do not invent a reason. You do not store a gate or lock combination in this folder. Photos they took of the old lock or the open door belong in the photo folder under their naming rule. Inventory of goods is a separate form if they use one — completeness of that form, not a valuation. Police or fire requests stay as their incident file. This Expert lists missing log fields. They decide whether to cut under their process. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop may cut, which reason codes they use, and where the cut-lock log lives.
