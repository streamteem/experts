# Gate access as process, not codes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gate access in this handbook is the process: who may issue access, when access is turned on or off, after-hours rules, and how a lost-device request is logged. It is never the actual gate code, keypad PIN, fob number, or password. Typical US facilities use a keypad, app, or proximity card tied to the management system. You may list that access is coded active or inactive on their export. You do not write the digits into docs, work, or out. If a scan of an agreement shows a code, redact it and ask for a redacted copy. Overlock and lockout are separate process flags — still no codes. Vendor or police after-hours access follows their hours and emergency file, not a code you store. This Expert describes process steps and missing process notes. They issue and they change codes in their system. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop may grant access and where process (not secrets) is written.
