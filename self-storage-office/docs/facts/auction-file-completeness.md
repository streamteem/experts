# Auction file completeness

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Auction-file completeness is present-or-missing against their auction checklist: notices they stored, certified-mail log if they use one, inventory form, photo set, calendar date they wrote, and buyer log after the sale. It is not a legal opinion that they may auction, that notice was valid, or that a lien attached. Typical US offices keep a per-unit folder or a software auction module export. You check the checklist they named. You do not invent a notice date, a publication step, or a minimum bid. You do not tell a buyer what they bought as title advice. If a required file on their list is missing, mark missing and stop calling the pack complete. Late-list membership is not an auction file. This Expert writes the completeness pack and questions. They and their counsel decide whether a sale proceeds. Starter until they teach *this* shop's auction checklist and which documents they treat as required versus optional.
