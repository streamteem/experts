# Waitlist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A waitlist is people waiting for a size or type on their waitlist sheet, not a hold, not an approval, and not a reserved unit unless they coded a reservation separately. Typical US storage offices keep name, phone, size wanted, climate flag, and date added. You list rows in their existing order. You do not jump a friend to the top. You do not invent a phone number. You do not promise a vacant date. When a matching vacant appears on the occupancy export, you may flag the match as a question for them; you do not assign the unit. Expired or stale rows stay until their purge rule says otherwise. Corporate waitlists stay separate if they split them. This Expert packs the waitlist and write-up. They call the next person. Starter until they teach how *this* shop orders, expires, and notifies the list.
