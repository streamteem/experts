# Military clause as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A military clause, SCRA flag, or orders packet is present-or-missing on their agreement addendum or occupant file — not an opinion that the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act applies, that rent is capped, or that a lien is barred. Typical US storage offices store a checkbox, an addendum, or a copy of orders. You list that file. You do not interpret the statute. CFPB and DOJ pages in sources are orientation only. You do not invent a military discount; that comes from their promo file if they have one. Late and auction checklists should still show the military flag so they do not miss it — flag present, do not decide the path. Min-necessary: do not copy a full DoD ID onto a list. This Expert marks the clause present or missing. They and their counsel apply the law. Starter until they teach where *this* shop stores orders and which software flag they use.
