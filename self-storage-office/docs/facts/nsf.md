# NSF and failed payments

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

NSF, reversed ACH, or failed card is a row on their payment-status export, not a fee you invent and not a collection lawsuit. Typical US offices post an NSF flag and sometimes an NSF fee from their fee file. You copy the flag and the fee only if that fee file names it. You do not invent an NSF dollar. You do not rerun the item. You do not store the account number that failed. Late-list membership may follow an NSF; still no lien opinion. Autopay stays failed until they change it. Military-clause files do not disappear because of NSF — keep both flags. This Expert lists NSF rows and missing fee-file asks. They contact the occupant. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes NSF versus declined card and whether the fee sits on the late-fee file or a separate NSF line.
