# Lock brand — ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Which lock the occupant must use — disc, cylinder brand they sell, or any lock — comes from their merchandise and move-in files, not from a security lecture you invent. Typical US offices sell a disc lock at the counter and note 'facility lock required' on some sites. You ask if the rule is not in the folder. You do not invent a brand. You do not store a combination. Overlocks they hang are their process and usually a different color or brand; still no codes in docs. Cut-lock logs may name the brand they cut; copy that field if present. Merchandise sold is a sales line, not a requirement you made up. This Expert lists the written lock rule or marks it missing. They sell and they overlock under their process. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop requires a sold lock, accepts any disc, and how they mark an overlock in the unit photo.
