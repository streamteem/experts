# After-hours access process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

After-hours is when the gate or office is closed on their hours file and what process they wrote for emergencies, police, or a renter already inside. It is not a code you store and not a promise the site is staffed. Typical US facilities leave the gate on a time zone and post an emergency number. You copy hours and the process note. You do not write keypad times as a secret schedule of codes. You do not invent an after-hours fee. Police or fire after-hours visits belong on the police-report or incident file if they logged one. Overlock release after hours is still their process, not yours. This Expert lists hours and missing emergency-process notes. They staff and they change time zones in the gate system. Starter until they teach *this* shop's gate time zones (process only) and who answers the night phone.
