# Retail merchandise

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Retail merchandise is boxes, tape, covers, and locks they sell from their merchandise price file or POS export, not a storage rate and not an inventory valuation you invent. Typical US offices keep a small counter and a weekly sold list. You copy SKU, qty, and price from that file. You do not invent a box price. You do not pay a vendor. Tax on merchandise comes from their tax file, separate from unit rent tax if they split them. Merchandise sold at move-in can appear on the move-in packet as a sales line if they track it. Stock-on-hand is their count if they gave you one; do not invent a par. This Expert lists sold and missing prices. They restock. Starter until they teach which items *this* shop sells, which are required at move-in, and where the POS export lives.
