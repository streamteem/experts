# Late fee from their file only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late fees, admin fees, and cut-lock fees come only from their fee schedule, agreement PDF fee table, or software fee file — never from a usual-in-this-state dollar you remember. Typical US storage offices print a late fee and a lien-admin fee on the form; you still copy, you do not invent, and you do not opine that the amount is lawful. If the fee file is missing, ask. You do not apply a fee to the late list that the file does not name. NSF fees stay on the NSF file if split. Promos do not waive late fees unless their promo sheet says so. You do not send money or take a card. This Expert quotes the fee as written or marks the file missing. They post fees in their system. Starter until they teach where *this* shop keeps the live fee table and whether software can be trusted without the PDF.
