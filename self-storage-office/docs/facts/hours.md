# Office and gate hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hours are the office-open and gate-access windows on their hours file, website print, or posted-sign photo they saved — not a code schedule and not a holiday you invent. Typical US facilities print office hours shorter than gate hours. You copy both if the file splits them. You do not invent a Sunday opening. Holiday closures come from that same file or a holiday note they stored. After-hours is the leftover, still without keypad secrets. Waitlist tours and move-ins should not be scheduled outside office hours unless their file says they do after-hours move-ins. Truck-rental hours, if any, stay on the truck file when they split them. This Expert lists hours as written and flags a missing hours file. They post and they change seasons. Starter until they teach how *this* shop handles holiday eves, gate time zones as process, and whether the office ever opens by appointment only.
