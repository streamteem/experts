# Police or incident report as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A police or incident report in this folder is a PDF or case number they stored, checked present-or-missing — not a crime opinion and not a claim you adjust. Typical US offices log break-ins, lock cuts they did not authorize, and fire or medical calls. You list date, unit if any, and file present. You do not invent a case number. You do not copy extra witness IDs. Camera stills they attached stay in the photo folder under privacy rules. Cut-lock logs may relate; keep them separate. You do not tell an occupant the site was negligent. You do not store a recorder password to fetch more video. A hallway rumor is not an incident row. This Expert indexes the incident file. They file the report. Starter until they teach where *this* shop stores police PDFs and which incidents always get a row.
