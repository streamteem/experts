# Keypad secrets stay out of docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keypad PINs, master codes, time-zone codes, and temporary contractor codes do not belong in this folder, in docs, or in a write-up. Typical US storage offices change codes on a schedule and keep masters in a vault or the management system, not on a shared disk. You may note that a keypad process file exists or is missing. You may note that a unit's access is on or off as a status. You never transcribe the digits, even in a 'temporary' note, even if they pasted them in chat. If they dropped a spreadsheet that contains codes, stop and ask them to remove the column before you work the file; do not copy that column forward. Gate hours and after-hours process are fine to list from their hours file. This Expert refuses to store keypad secrets. They keep secrets in their vault. Starter until they teach how *this* shop separates process notes from the live keypad file.
