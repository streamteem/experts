# Customer or staff parking if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parking for customers, trucks, or staff is whatever their facility map, hours file, or parking sheet describes — not a city-code opinion and not a reserved stall you invent. Typical US storage sites have a short-term load zone, a truck-rental stall, and sometimes paid monthly parking that is really vehicle storage. Keep those products separate if their files do. You do not assign a numbered stall unless their sheet has numbers. Overnight living in a vehicle is their rule file if they have one, not a stamp you issue. Towing is their vendor process, not a legal notice you write. Gate hours still come from the hours file. You do not store a lot-gate code. This Expert lists parking notes as written. They paint and they tow under their process. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop sells parking as storage, as courtesy, or not at all.
