# Merchandise sold list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Merchandise sold is the POS or daily-sold export: SKU, qty, price, tax as their file, and date — not a cash count you perform and not a rate for a unit. Typical US offices print a day close for boxes, tape, and locks. You copy that export. You do not invent a sold qty to match a shelf count. You do not take the drawer. Card PAN must not appear; if the export includes it, stop and ask for a stripped file. Truck-rental sold if any stays on the truck file unless they commingle — say so if they did. Move-in packets may point at a sold-lock line. This Expert lists sold and missing price rows. They close the drawer. Starter until they teach which POS *this* shop uses and whether day-close PDFs or CSVs are the source of truth.
