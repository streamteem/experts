# Abandoned as their file, not a legal label you apply

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Abandoned on this desk means they already coded a unit or a leftover-goods file as abandoned, or they have an abandonment form in the folder. It is not a legal conclusion you apply to a quiet unit, and it is not an auction opinion. Typical US offices use abandonment for leftover boxes after a notified move-out, which may be a different path than a delinquency auction file. You copy their code and list the forms they named. You do not invent abandoned. You do not tell them they may throw goods away. Inventory and photo completeness still apply to their form. Cut-lock logs may sit beside the file. A quiet late unit is still a late-list row, not abandoned, unless they coded it. This Expert flags their abandoned rows and missing forms. They and their counsel pick the path. Starter until they teach how *this* shop distinguishes leftover-after-vacate from the auction-file path.
