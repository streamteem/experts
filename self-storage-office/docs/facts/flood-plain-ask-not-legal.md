# Flood-plain ask, not a legal determination

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Flood-plain or flood-zone language on this desk is an ask: do they have a FEMA map print, a lender letter, or a site note in the folder. It is not a legal determination that the site is in or out of a floodplain, not a NFIP opinion, and not advice that goods will be safe. Typical US owners keep a map PDF or an insurance binder note. You list that file present or missing. You do not fetch a live FEMA API unless they already saved a map. You do not tell occupants their goods are covered. Climate units are not automatically above flood. A flood-event file after a storm is a different pack. SSA disaster-readiness pages are orientation. This Expert asks for their map or note. They and their insurer decide. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop stores a flood map, a tenant warning, or neither.
