# Facility map

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The facility map is their site plan, building-letter drawing, or unit-number plot, not a survey stamp and not a flood determination. Typical US offices hang a plot by the counter and keep a PDF for the folder. You use it to group occupancy and photo packs by building when they asked. You do not invent a unit that is not on the mix and not on the map. If the map and the unit-mix CSV disagree on a number, quote both. Climate halls versus drive-up rows can be labeled from the map only if the map says so. Camera locations on a map are an ask for the camera-privacy fact, not a surveillance product. Gate and keypad locations may be drawn; still no codes. This Expert indexes the map file. They update numbers after a conversion. Starter until they teach which map version is live and how *this* shop letters buildings.
