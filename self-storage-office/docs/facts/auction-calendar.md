# Auction calendar as their dates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The auction calendar is dates they wrote for listing, sale, or cutoff on their auction sheet or software export — not a legal timeline you compute from a statute. Typical US offices use an online-auction calendar or a Saturday aisle date. You copy those dates. You do not invent a sale date to make a completeness pack look scheduled. You do not say the date is valid notice. Certified-mail dates stay on the mail log; do not overwrite the calendar with a guessed statutory count. Units on the late list are not automatically on the calendar. Buyer log fills after the date, not before. This Expert lists calendar rows and missing dates they still require on their checklist. They pick dates with their process and counsel. Starter until they teach which calendar *this* shop uses and who may add a unit to it.
