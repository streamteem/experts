# Unit condition

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit condition is their coded state — clean, dirty, damaged, wet, or ready — plus any punch notes they wrote, not an engineering stamp and not a deposit legal opinion. Typical US storage offices walk a vacant after move-out and mark the occupancy or make-ready field. You copy that code. You do not invent damage to justify a charge. You do not invent a clean-out fee. Photos support the code if they took them; a missing photo is a missing file, not a guess from memory. Oil stains on a vehicle pad stay on the vehicle file if they use one. Flood-event wet units stay linked to the flood-event file. Ready-to-rent requires their ready code, not your walk. This Expert lists condition and missing photos. They clean and they code ready. Starter until they teach *this* shop's condition codes and who may mark a unit showable.
