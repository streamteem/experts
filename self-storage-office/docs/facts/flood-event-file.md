# Flood or storm event file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A flood or storm event file is the pack they built after water, wind, or a named storm: unit list, photos, occupant notices they stored, and vendor tickets. It is not a coverage opinion and not a flood-plain legal determination. Typical US offices list wet units from a walk and keep a dated photo set. You index those files. You do not invent a wet unit from a news story. You do not tell occupants the facility is liable or not liable. Climate versus standard does not decide damage. Insurance-on-goods remains an ask on each account. FEMA maps stay orientation. You do not invent a wet unit from a news clip. This Expert lists affected units from their walk file. They notify and they call vendors. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names an event folder and which statuses they put on wet units.
