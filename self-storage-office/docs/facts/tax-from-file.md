# Tax from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sales tax, occupancy tax, or rent tax on a storage invoice comes from their tax file, rate sheet, or software tax code — not from a rate you remember from another county. Typical US offices see some states tax storage rent and some do not; you still do not decide the law. You copy the percent or the taxable flag as written. You do not invent a tax line to finish a quote. Merchandise tax and rent tax may differ on their file; keep them split. If the agreement PDF and the rate file disagree on tax, quote both and ask. You do not file a return. You do not send money. This Expert lists tax as labeled. They and their bookkeeper apply the code. Starter until they teach which tax codes *this* shop uses on rent, merchandise, and truck rental if any.
