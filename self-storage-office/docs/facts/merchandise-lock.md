# Lock sold as merchandise

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A lock sold at the counter is a merchandise line on their POS or move-in sales file, not a requirement you invented and not a combination you store. Typical US offices sell a disc lock and ring it up with boxes. You copy SKU, price from the merchandise file, and qty. You do not invent a lock price. You do not write the combination if a combination lock was sold — refuse that field. Lock-brand-ask is the policy question; this fact is the sale. Overlocks are facility property, not merchandise sold, unless their file says they sell the overlock (rare) — do not mix them. Returns of unused locks follow their retail file. This Expert lists locks sold. They ring the sale. Starter until they teach which lock SKUs *this* shop carries and whether a sold lock is required at move-in or optional.
