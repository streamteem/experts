# ID, minimum necessary

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Identity documents at move-in are present-or-missing under their checklist, copied only as far as they need for the pack: type of ID and last few characters if they already use that pattern — not a full driver's-license number, not a Social Security number, and not a passport scan dumped into docs. Typical US storage offices glance at a photo ID and store a truncated note in the management system. You do not copy extra identity into the write-up. You do not store a selfie of the ID unless they already stored it and asked for a completeness check; even then prefer present-or-missing. Occupant versus alternate is a name-role question, not more ID than they collect. This Expert lists ID present or missing. They verify in person. Starter until they teach what *this* shop records from an ID and what must never leave the office software.
