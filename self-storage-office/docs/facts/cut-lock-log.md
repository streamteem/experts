# Cut-lock log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The cut-lock log is their register of locks cut: date, unit, who cut, reason code, and replacement if any. Typical US offices keep a binder or a spreadsheet. You copy those rows. You do not add a cut that they did not log. You do not store a combination. Reason codes such as vacate leftover, overlock swap, auction inventory, or emergency stay as they wrote them — still not a legal stamp. Photos of the cut belong in the photo folder. The log is evidence for their process pack, not proof a lien was valid. If the log and a photo date disagree, quote both. You do not add a row to tidy an auction checklist. This Expert lists missing fields. They complete the row when they cut. Starter until they teach who may write in *this* shop's cut-lock log and whether two initials are required.
