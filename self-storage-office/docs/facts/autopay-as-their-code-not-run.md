# Autopay as their code, not a run you perform

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Autopay is a status code on their payment export — enrolled, failed, or off — not a payment you run and not a card you store. Typical US storage offices enroll ACH or card in SiteLink or a processor vault. You may list the status and last-attempt date as they exported them. You never copy PAN, full ACH account numbers, or CVV into this folder. Last-four only if their export already prints last-four and they asked you to keep that column; otherwise drop it. You do not retry a failed autopay. NSF is a related flag, still not a rerun. They collect. You do not send money. Failed autopay is not a late fee you invent. This Expert lists enrolled versus failed from the file. They repair the method in their vault. Starter until they teach which payment-status codes *this* shop uses and which columns must be stripped before a file is shared.
