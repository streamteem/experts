# Vacancy days

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vacancy days are the count of days a unit sat vacant on their occupancy or unit-history export, not a make-ready legal deadline and not a forecast of when it will rent. Typical US offices compute vacant-since from the last move-out date. You copy those dates and the day count if the file has it. You do not invent a move-out date to make days look short. Damaged, company, or auction-hold vacant is still not 'available' if they coded it otherwise — keep the status. Unit-condition and photo packs explain why a vacant is not showable; they do not change the day count. Promo or rate asks for long-vacant sizes come from their rate file, not a discount you invent. This Expert may chart vacancy days by size from their CSV if they asked, without claiming why a size is slow. They price and they advertise. Starter until they teach how *this* shop starts and stops the vacant clock.
