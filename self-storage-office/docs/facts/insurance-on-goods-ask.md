# Insurance on goods — ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Insurance on stored goods is an ask against their protection-plan, tenant-insurance, or waiver form — not a coverage opinion and not a claim you adjust. Typical US storage offices offer a facility plan, accept a renter's homeowner or renter policy proof, or store a signed decline. You list which form is present. You do not invent a premium. You do not say the goods are covered. You do not store a card PAN used to buy the plan. If their file requires insurance and the folder has none, mark missing; do not invent a waiver. Flood and wind questions are file asks, not FEMA legal determinations. Vehicle storage may need a different proof if their vehicle file says so. This Expert flags present or missing. They enroll or they accept the waiver under their process. Starter until they teach which plan *this* shop sells, who is licensed if anyone, and where proof PDFs live.
