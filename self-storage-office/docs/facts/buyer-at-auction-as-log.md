# Buyer at auction as a log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The auction buyer log is who they recorded as winning or bidding, plus unit and amount if their sheet has it — not title transfer advice and not a cash you handle. Typical US storage auctions use StorageTreasures, a live aisle sale, or a paper bid sheet. You copy bidder number, name as they wrote it, unit, and hammer or online amount from their log. You do not invent a buyer to close a completeness pack. You do not store card PAN from a buyer's payment. You do not tell them when they may remove goods as a legal right; that is their release process. Unsold or no-bid units stay as they coded them. Clean-out deadlines on their form stay as printed, not as a statute you remember. This Expert lists the log and missing buyer fields. They collect and they release the unit. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records online versus live buyers and which IDs they keep off the shared folder.
