# Corporate or multi-unit account

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A corporate or business account is an entity occupant on their file with one billing contact and often many units, not a personal combo you relabeled and not a net-30 you invent. Typical US offices store a legal name, a billing email, and a PO-if-required flag. You copy those fields. You do not invent a corporate discount; that comes from their rate or promo file. Authorized users stay on the occupant-versus-alt list. Late status may be account-level or unit-level as they coded it — keep their grain. You do not store a card PAN on file for the AP department. Invoice copies they dropped are files, not a payment you send. This Expert lists units on the account and missing billing fields. They negotiate terms. Starter until they teach how *this* shop sets up a business account and whether each unit still gets its own agreement PDF.
