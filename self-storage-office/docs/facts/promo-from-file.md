# Promo from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Promotions — first month, half off, web-only, military, or student — come only from the promo sheet or rate file they stored, not from a last weekend's sidewalk sign you remember. Typical US storage offices date their specials and limit them by size, channel, or new-move-in only. You copy the promo name, dollar or percent, start and end if printed, and any size band they wrote. You do not invent a first-month-free to win a walk-in on paper. Stacking two promos is a question if their file is silent. In-place occupants do not get a new-move-in promo unless their sheet says so. A promo is not a late-fee waiver. If the promo expired on the file, ask before anyone uses it. This Expert lists live versus expired as labeled. They decide what to honor. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes web, call-center, and walk-in specials and who may extend an end date.
