# Occupant versus alternate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The occupant is the person or entity on the rental agreement; an alternate or authorized user is whoever their file lists as allowed to enter. Typical US offices print occupant, then one or two authorized names. You keep those roles separate. You do not add an alternate. You do not treat a spouse, truck driver, or emergency contact as authorized unless the file says so. Corporate accounts may list many users — still only from their file. Gate access process follows their authorized list; still no codes in docs. Late notices go to names their process names; you do not invent a mail list. ID min-necessary applies to each person they actually collect. This Expert lists occupant and alts as written. They add names on a change form. Starter until they teach how *this* shop adds an authorized user and whether a new agreement is required.
