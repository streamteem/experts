# Proration — ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Partial-month rent at move-in or move-out comes from their written proration rule or the software estimate they exported — not from a thirty-day custom you apply and not from a statute you remember. Typical US storage offices let the management system compute a first-period amount. You copy that amount or the day-count rule if they printed one. You do not invent a prorate. You do not net a promo into a new number you made up. If the estimate is blank, ask. Transfer mid-month is the same ask. Tax still comes from the tax file on whatever base they use. You do not invent a daily rate to force a neat number. This Expert quotes their factor or marks it missing. They confirm the first draft they will say to the occupant. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts days and whether move-out credits are even allowed.
