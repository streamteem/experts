# Overlock as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An overlock is a facility lock they hang on a hasp per their delinquency or vacancy process, not a legal lockout stamp and not a lien opinion. Typical US storage offices overlock after a late threshold they wrote, or to secure a vacant after a cut. You may list that the occupancy or late export codes overlock. You do not tell them they may overlock. You do not invent the day-count. You do not write the overlock combination into this folder. Removing an overlock after pay is their process; you do not take a card to 'release' it. Auction-path overlocks stay on the auction-file checklist, still without legal advice. A late-list row is not an automatic overlock unless they coded it. This Expert flags coded overlocks and missing process notes. They hang and they remove metal. Starter until they teach *this* shop's overlock threshold, color, and who may remove one.
