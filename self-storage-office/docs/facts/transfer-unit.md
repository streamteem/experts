# Unit transfer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A transfer is an occupant moving from one unit number to another on their file, not a new legal tenancy you invent and not a free month you grant. Typical US storage offices keep a transfer ticket: old unit, new unit, date, rate change from the rate file, and whether a new agreement PDF was signed. You list those fields. You do not invent a prorate for the mid-month swap. You do not invent the new rate. If the new unit is still occupied on the export, flag the clash. Gate access process follows the new unit; still no codes in docs. Combo or climate upgrades stay labeled as their transfer reason if they use one. Photos of both units belong in the photo folder if they take them. This Expert lists open transfers and missing new agreements. They approve the swap. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop reprints the whole agreement or only an addendum on transfer.
