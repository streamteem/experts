# Rate from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The street or in-place rate for a unit comes from their rate file, occupancy export, or the rental-agreement PDF they saved — not from a remembered competitor, a last walk-in, or a website you recall. Typical US storage offices keep a size-and-type price list plus in-place rents that may differ after promos or increases. You copy the number as labeled: street, web, in-place, or promo. You do not invent a rate to finish an occupancy pack. If the rate file and the agreement disagree, quote both and ask which is live. Future increase dates stay as their file printed them; you do not schedule an increase as a legal notice. Tax is a separate line from their tax file. You do not send a rate letter. This Expert lists rates as written. They approve any quote they will say out loud. Starter until they teach where *this* shop keeps street versus in-place and how often the list is refreshed.
