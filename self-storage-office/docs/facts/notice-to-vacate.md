# Notice to vacate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Notice to vacate is the written or software notice their file says the occupant (or the office) gave, with a date if printed — not a legal notice-period opinion and not a lockout. Typical US storage offices want ten or thirty days because their agreement form said so; you still copy the form, you do not invent the period. You list present-or-missing, the stated vacate date, and the unit. You do not invent a date to make vacancy-days look tidy. If they have no NTV product and only a walk-away, say so from their process note. Abandoned and auction paths are different files; do not relabel a missing NTV as abandoned. Gate close follows their move-out process, not a code in docs. This Expert lists notices and clashes with occupancy status. They accept or they chase a written notice. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop requires written NTV and where those PDFs live.
