# Vehicle storage if they have it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vehicle, RV, boat, or trailer storage exists on this desk only if their unit mix, parking file, or agreement addendum says they rent those spaces. Typical US facilities mix enclosed units with outdoor pads; some do not offer vehicles at all. You copy space number, type, and rate from that file. You do not invent a pad count. You do not title-search a VIN. Registration or insurance proof is present-or-missing if their vehicle checklist asks; you do not keep a full VIN on a public-looking list if they asked for min-necessary. Lien or auction of a vehicle is still completeness of their vehicle-auction file, not a DMV or UCC opinion. Gate access for a lot is process, not a code. This Expert lists vehicle rows only from their file. They decide whether a walk-in may park. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes enclosed versus outdoor and which papers they require before a pad is occupied.
