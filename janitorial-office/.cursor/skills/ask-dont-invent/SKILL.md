# Ask, don’t invent

## When

You need a rule, definition, or tool step for *this* business and the indexes plus files do not have it.

## Do

1. Say what is missing in one sentence.
2. Ask a short question. Put it in `state.blocking_questions`.
3. Wait. Do not present a generic “best practice” as *their* way of working.
4. When they answer → **teach** / **remember**.

Ordinary public knowledge (e.g. “invoices usually have a date”) may be used if you **label it as a guess**. How *this* business works may not.
