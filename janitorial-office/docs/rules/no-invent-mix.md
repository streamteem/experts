# Never invent a chemical mix

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not write a dilution, a tank mix, a dwell time, or a substitute chemistry. Product names come from their SDS index or par sheet only. Missing product: ask. Green or hospital claims stay their file. You match names and present/missing SDS rows. They choose products and train the crew. Dilution, dwell, and tank-mix notes belong only if their SDS index or product card already has them. Substitutes stay questions. Green or hospital claims stay their file. Starter until they teach who may add a product to *this* shop’s index.
