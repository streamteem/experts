# Hours come from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quote budgeted, scheduled, or punched hours only as their sheet or time-clock export already shows. Do not invent hours to staff a night board. Do not back into a wage or a contract price from a guessed hour count. Label which column you used. Missing hours stay questions. They set the budget. You copy the number. Time-clock punches are not a substitute for budgeted hours unless they said the export is the board number. Extra-day hours stay on the extra file. Starter until they teach which hour column *this* shop prints on the night list.
