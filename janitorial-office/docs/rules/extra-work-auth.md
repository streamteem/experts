# Extra work needs their authorization

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat add-on, extra-day, strip-wax, or other out-of-scope work as live on the board without the authorization file their rule requires. Copy auth date and words. Do not invent a price. Courtesy callbacks stay labeled courtesy. Missing auth is a hold. They collect the signature. You list the gap. Add-on, extra-day, strip-wax, and billable callbacks follow this rule when their file says they are extra. Verbal go-ahead is missing until they file it. Prices come from their extra file, never a guess. They collect the signature. You list the gap.
