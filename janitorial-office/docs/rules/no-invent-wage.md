# Never invent a wage

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent an hourly wage, piece rate, or night premium. Hours on files are production or punches they recorded, not a payroll you run. Do not back into a price from a guessed wage. If they asked for a labor sketch and no wage file exists, ask. Card numbers stay out of this folder. Piece rates, night premiums, and day-porter rates are wages too. A labor-cost sketch without a wage file stops and asks. You do not run payroll or store bank logins. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even keeps a wage file in this folder.
