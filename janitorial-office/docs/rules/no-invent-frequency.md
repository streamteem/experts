# Never invent a frequency

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Frequency comes from the contract or schedule they stored. Do not add a night to fix a complaint and do not thin a nightly restroom to fit a short crew. Holiday calendars change dates, not contracted frequency, unless they stored an amendment. Missing frequency is a question, not a guessed M-W-F.
