# Hours from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hours on this desk are numbers they already stored: budgeted site hours, scheduled crew hours, or a time-clock export. Typical US cleaning offices put budgeted hours on the contract or a workloading sheet and scheduled hours on the night board. Copy the number and the label (budgeted versus scheduled versus punched). Do not invent hours to make a site look staffed. Do not back into a wage or a price from a guessed hour count. Productivity sheets they saved may show minutes per restroom or per thousand square feet — use those only as their sheet wrote them, not a remembered ISSA figure. Overtime and extra-day hours stay on the extra-work or holiday file, not silently added to the nightly budget. A time-clock export is evidence they punched, not a payroll you run. Missing hours stay missing. This Expert quotes their file. They set the budget. Starter until they teach which hour column *this* shop treats as the board number.
