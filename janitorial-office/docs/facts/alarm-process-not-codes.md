# Alarm process (never codes)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Alarm process is the written steps they stored for who calls the monitoring company, who waits for a late crew, and what to do if the panel will not arm — never the code itself. Typical US night-cleaning offices keep a one-page process: call order, supervisor name, and “do not share codes on paper.” You may list that a process file is present or missing. You never copy, store, or invent an alarm code, keypad PIN, or lock combination in this folder, a night board, or a write-up. If a schedule note pastes a code, strip it and flag that the source file contained a secret they must move out of the working pack. Badge and key-log rows are not substitutes for a code. A missed arm is a complaint or callback they log, not a code you retrieve. This Expert checks process-present. They hold the codes. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files alarm process without putting secrets in the Expert folder.
