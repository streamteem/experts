# Quality checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A quality checklist is their blank or filled inspection form for a site or area: restrooms, floors, trash, glass, and the lines they already printed. Typical US contractors use a house form, a customer scorecard, or an ISSA/CIMS-style sheet they saved as a PDF. You pack the form they use; you do not invent a scoring rubric. Walking-inspection notes and photos attach only if they stored them. Do not turn a complaint into a new checklist line unless their form already has that line. Scores belong on inspection-score-as-their-form, copied, not curved. Hospital or medical-office checklists stay those files if present — you do not stamp infection control. Missing forms for a scheduled inspection stay missing. This Expert assembles the pack. They walk and they score. Starter until they teach which form *this* shop uses per site type and what “pass” means on their sheet.
