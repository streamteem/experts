# Extra-work authorization

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Extra-work authorization is the signed or emailed approval they stored before add-on, strip-wax, extra-day, or other out-of-scope work runs. Typical US building-service offices keep a one-page auth, a customer PO, or an email PDF in the contract folder. Copy the date, site, scope words, and amount if they wrote an amount from their price file — never invent a price. Do not schedule extra work on the night board as live without the auth if their rule requires it. A verbal “just do it” is still missing auth until they file it. Callbacks coded courtesy do not need a price; billable callbacks do if they say so. Extra-invoice later must point at this auth. This Expert lists missing versus present auth. They collect the signature. Starter until they teach which email counts as auth for *this* shop. Customer PO, signed extra sheet, and filed email are different auth kinds; copy the kind they used. Do not treat a text message as auth unless they filed it under their rule. Amounts come from their price file next to the auth, never a remembered extra. Starter until they teach which email mailbox *this* shop treats as a filed authorization.
