# Complaint

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A complaint is a customer or occupant note they logged: site, date, area, and the words they typed. Typical US cleaning offices keep a complaint log in a workbook or a ticketing export. Copy the text; do not clean it into a softer story. Do not diagnose why a floor failed as a chemist. Do not promise a free month. A complaint is not yet a callback until they open a return visit. Photos they attached stay linked; you do not invent a shot. Medical or bloodborne complaints route to their bloodborne file if they have one — you still do not stamp. If the log has no owner or no site, ask. Duplicate complaints on the same night stay separate rows unless they already merged them. This Expert lists open complaints. They call the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes severity and when a complaint becomes a missed-service row.
