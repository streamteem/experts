# Medical-office site (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A medical-office site is a clinic or outpatient suite they already flagged on the site list, with a scope file that may name waiting rooms, exam rooms, and restrooms. Typical US contractors keep a separate spec exhibit and sometimes a bloodborne file pointer. You list the flag and the files present. You do not diagnose, treat, or stamp infection control. You do not invent a hospital-grade protocol for a dental or clinic suite. Chemicals stay on their SDS index. If the folder has no medical flag and someone asks for clinic hours, ask rather than relabel an office tower. HIPAA-min: do not copy charts or extra patient paper into docs/. Quality forms stay their clinic form if they stored one. This Expert lists medical-office sites they coded. They accept the account.
