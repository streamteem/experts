# Hospital site (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A hospital site is an acute-care or similar account they already flagged, often with customer EVS rules, isolation notes as their file, and a tighter badge process. Typical US contractors who hold this work keep a customer SOP folder and a separate quality form. You list the flag and which customer files are present. You do not stamp infection control, invent an isolation mix, or act as hospital EVS leadership. CDC cleaning pages are orientation only. If they have no hospital sites, say this folder has none — do not invent a wing. Bloodborne and SDS files still check present or missing. Square feet and productivity stay their sheets. Night-versus-day windows come from their schedule, not a remembered visiting hour. This Expert lists hospital rows they coded. They staff the account. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names units and which SOP PDF wins.
