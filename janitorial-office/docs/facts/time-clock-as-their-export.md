# Time clock as their export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A time-clock export is a file they dropped of punches: person, site if they track it, in, out, and date. Typical US cleaning offices export from a clock app or a biometric they already run. You list punches and compare to scheduled hours only as both files allow. Do not invent a punch. Do not invent a wage or run payroll. Do not correct a missed punch as if you were the supervisor. Overtime stays their column if present, not a legal opinion. Night-supervisor sign-off if they store it is a label, not a pay approval you issue. Missing exports stay missing. Passwords to the clock vendor stay out of this folder. This Expert packs exceptions they can see. They approve time. Starter until they teach which export *this* shop treats as official and how sites are coded on punches.
