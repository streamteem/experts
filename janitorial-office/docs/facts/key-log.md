# Key log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The key log is who signed a ring, fob, or hard key in or out for a site, and whether it came back. Typical US contract offices keep a paper log, a spreadsheet, or a photo of a lockbox sheet. Copy date, site, key-id as they labeled it, person, out, and in. Do not copy a key, cut a key, or write a bitting. Do not store a lock combination. Missing return on a night that already closed is a completeness flag, not a theft verdict. Site maps may show a key-drop location as a label; still no codes. Badges are a separate fact if they track them that way. Keys-returned at contract end is its own closeout row. If two logs disagree, quote both. This Expert lists completeness. They issue and they collect. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers rings and what “returned” means on a split crew.
