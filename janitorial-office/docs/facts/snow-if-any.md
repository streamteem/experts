# Snow (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Snow work is walkway or lot clearing they already scoped in a snow file or a seasonal addendum. Typical US cleaning offices either do not do snow or keep a separate trigger and site list. If no snow file exists, say this desk has no snow pack — do not invent a push. Copy trigger depth and sites from their file only. Do not invent a rate or a wage. Ice-melt product stays on the SDS index as they named it, not a mix you write. Alarm process still never includes codes. Landscaping-style lot plowing they subcontract stays a vendor row they stored, not a payment you send. Extra-work-auth applies when their contract says snow is extra. This Expert lists snow rows they filed. They call the event. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop owns walks, lots, or neither.
