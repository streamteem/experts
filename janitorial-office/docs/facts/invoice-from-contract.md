# Invoice from the contract

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A contract invoice line is the recurring amount or unit they already stored on the contract or a billing sheet: site, period, and amount as written. Typical US offices bill monthly from the contract, not from a guessed hour total. Copy the amount. Do not invent a price. Do not send the invoice as live payment. Credits for missed-service follow their billing rule file if they have one — do not invent a credit. Extra-invoice is a separate fact. Square feet used for billing stay the contract number, not a new measure. This Expert lists what the contract file says to bill. They invoice.
