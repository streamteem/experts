# Service frequency

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Frequency is how often a site or a task is scheduled: five nights, three nights, weekly floors, monthly strip, and so on. Typical US cleaning offices put frequency on the contract exhibit and again on the schedule CSV. Copy both when they exist; if they disagree, list both and ask. Do not invent a fifth night because a complaint came in. Do not thin a nightly restroom to three times to fit a short crew. Holiday calendars and extra-day rows change which calendar dates fire, not the contracted frequency unless they stored an amendment. Day-porter hours are a different frequency block from night cleaning. Floor-care and carpet cycles stay on their own rows if the spec splits them. Missing frequency on a live site is a hole, not a guessed Monday-Wednesday-Friday pattern. This Expert copies frequency from their files. They sell a change. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes frequency when a campus has mixed areas.
