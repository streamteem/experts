# Crew assignment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A crew is who they assigned to a site or an area-split for a given night or day: lead, helpers, and sometimes a language or badge flag they already recorded. Typical US contract offices keep a roster workbook, a whiteboard photo, or an export from the same system that prints the night board. Do not invent a person, a nickname, or a wage. Do not move someone onto a site because the hours look short. Background-date and uniform flags on the roster are labels they wrote, not a hiring verdict you issue. Sub-crews or day labor they listed stay labeled as they coded them. Call-outs come from their note, not from a guess that someone “usually” stays home Mondays. A crew row is not a timesheet and not a payroll file. If the folder has no roster, say so and ask. This Expert assembles who is listed. They assign and they pay. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names crews and who may lead a site.
