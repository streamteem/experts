# Restroom service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Restroom service is the nightly or day-porter work on restrooms their spec already lists: fixtures, floors, restock of paper, soap, and liners. Typical US offices store a restroom count and sometimes a fixture count on the site or spec file. Copy those counts; do not invent a stall count from a remembered walk. Frequency for restrooms can differ from general cleaning — use the spec. Medical-office and school restrooms stay on those site flags. Quality checklists usually have a restroom block; pack their form, do not invent lines. Bloodborne or sharps notes route to their bloodborne file if present — not a stamp you issue. Par for liners, paper, and soap is a supply fact, not a guessed case. This Expert lists restroom rows and counts they stored. They change the spec. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts all-gender and locker restrooms on the same site.
