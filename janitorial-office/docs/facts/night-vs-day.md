# Night versus day service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Night versus day is which shift the work runs: unoccupied night cleaning, occupied day-porter, or a split the contract already named. Typical US offices code this on the site list and the schedule so keys, alarm process, and walking routes stay on the right shift. Do not move a night site to day to cover a call-out. Do not assume an office building is empty at 6 p.m. if their file says occupied until eight. Day-porter restock and trash are not the same ticket as night vacuum and restrooms. Hospital and school sites often have tighter occupied rules; use only the window they stored. Alarm process (not codes) and key-log rows should match the shift they assigned. A missed night is not automatically a day make-up unless their extra-day or callback file says so. This Expert lists the shift as coded. They change a window.
