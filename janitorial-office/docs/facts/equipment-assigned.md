# Equipment assigned

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Equipment assigned is which named machines sit on a site or ride with a crew: auto-scrubber, vacuum, carpet extractor, or a cart they already tagged. Typical US shops keep an equipment list with site, unit id, and down-or-ready. Copy the assignment. Do not invent a machine so a floor-care night looks possible. Do not diagnose a failure or invent a shop-return date. A down unit stays down as they coded it; do not assign it on the night board. Rentals they listed are their rows, not a rate you invent and not a payment you send. Strip-wax and carpet nights should show the extractor or floor machine only if their list already assigns one. Keys to equipment cages stay on the key log as labels, not copied codes. This Expert lists unit versus site. They repair and they move iron. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tags units and what “ready” means.
