# SDS present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

SDS present is a file check: for each chemical they listed, is a safety data sheet in the binder or folder they pointed at. Typical US offices keep PDFs by product name or a spreadsheet with a present column and a date they filed the sheet. You list present versus missing. You do not interpret the SDS as a hygienist, invent a mix, or stamp Hazard Communication as complete. OSHA pages in sources.md are orientation only. A missing SDS is a question for them, not a downloaded sheet you treat as their official file unless they asked you to fetch a manufacturer PDF they named and they will file it. Do not store a password to a vendor SDS portal. Chemicals on the par sheet with no index row stay listed as index-missing. This Expert packs the gap list. They file and they train. Starter until they teach where *this* shop keeps live SDS files and who may add a product.
