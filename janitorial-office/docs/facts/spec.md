# Cleaning specification

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A spec is the written cleaning standard for a site or area: what is cleaned, to what look, and on what cycle. Typical US contract offices copy APPA appearance levels, ISSA workloading notes, or a customer exhibit into a spec sheet or the contract PDF. Spec is not an OSHA stamp and not a quality form by itself. Copy the words they stored. Do not upgrade a dust-mop spec to a spray-buff because a walking inspection looked dull. Do not invent an APPA level or a hospital isolation protocol from memory. If the contract and a later email disagree, quote both and ask which wins. Area-specific specs (restroom, medical suite, gym floor) stay split if their file splits them. Green-product or chemical limits belong only when the spec or SDS index already names them. This Expert matches schedule rows to the spec they have. They rewrite specs. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files spec changes and who may change a frequency.
