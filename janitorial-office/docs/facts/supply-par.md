# Supply par

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A supply par is the on-hand target they set for a closet or a warehouse SKU: liners, paper, soap, and other consumables they already named. Typical US janitorial offices keep a par sheet per site closet plus a central warehouse sheet. Par is not a purchase order and not a chemical mix. Copy item, unit, par, and on-hand when those columns exist. Do not invent a par because a restroom “usually” needs two cases. Do not substitute a brand to make on-hand look fine. Chemical SKUs belong on the par sheet only as they listed them, and the SDS index must still show present or missing — you do not invent a product. Barcode fields stay as their sheet has them. Below-par flags come from comparing on-hand to their min or par, not from a remembered usage. This Expert lists pars and holes. They buy. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts cases versus each and which closet owns which SKU.
