# Restroom count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Restroom count is the number of restrooms or fixtures they already recorded for a site, used on specs, pars, and quality packs. Typical US workloading sheets show restrooms and sometimes fixtures separately. Copy the number and the unit (rooms versus fixtures). Do not invent a count from a remembered walk or a map scale. If the site list and the spec disagree, quote both. School locker rooms and medical-office restrooms stay in their categories if split. Productivity minutes per restroom come only from their sheet. A remodel note without a new count is a hole, not a guessed plus-two. This Expert lists counts they stored. They recount after construction. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop counts rooms or fixtures on the night board. All-gender, locker, and medical restrooms stay in the categories they split. A fixture count is not a room count. Quality forms that score restrooms still use their count, not a walk you invent. After a remodel, a missing new count is a hole.
