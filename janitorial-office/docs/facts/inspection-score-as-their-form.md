# Inspection score as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An inspection score is the number or pass/fail their form already shows after a walk, not a grade you invent and not an OSHA stamp. Typical US offices export scores from a quality app or write a percent on the paper checklist. Copy the score, date, site, and inspector name as filed. Do not average two walks into a prettier number. Do not raise a score because the customer is important. If the form and a photo folder disagree — photos show soil, form says pass — quote both and ask. Punch-list items stay punch-list, not a silent score change. Customer scorecards and internal audits are different rows if they split them. You do not declare the site “in compliance.” This Expert lists scores they stored. They dispute and they re-walk. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files customer scores versus internal walks.
