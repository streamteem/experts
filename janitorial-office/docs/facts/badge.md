# Badge / access card

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A badge is a building access card or company ID they already issued and logged, not a clearance you grant. Typical US offices note badge number, site, and issued-or-returned on the same sheet as keys or a separate access log. Copy the fields they stored. Do not invent a badge number. Do not store a PIN that arms a door. Lost-badge notes stay as they wrote them; you do not call the building as the security office. School and hospital sites often require a named badge type — use only their label. Background-date on file is not a badge verdict. If the roster says a person is assigned and the badge log has no row, flag missing. You do not photocopy IDs into docs/ beyond a min-necessary number they already typed. This Expert lists issued versus missing. They request cards from the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop ties badges to night-supervisor checkout.
