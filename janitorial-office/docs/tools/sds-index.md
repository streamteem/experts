# SDS index

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The SDS index is their list of products with a present-or-missing file flag and a date if they store one. You list gaps against the par sheet. You do not invent a product or a mix. You do not stamp Hazard Communication complete. Checks may look for product and present columns or file-exists on the index. No SDS-portal password belongs here. They file sheets. You pack missing names. Present versus missing is a file check, not a hygienist read. Green-product names still need a row here if they are on the par sheet.
