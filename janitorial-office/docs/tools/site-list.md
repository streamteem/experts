# Site list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The site list is their workbook or export of accounts: site name, address as they file it, night-versus-day, and flags they already coded (school, hospital, medical). You do not invent a building. Checks often look for site and address or site and shift columns. This tool starts night-board, holiday, and key-log packs. Alarm codes never belong on this sheet. No login is stored here. Medical, hospital, and school flags stay as they coded them. Missing sites stay missing. This list is not a contract and not a night board by itself.
