# Quality form

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The quality form is the blank or filled PDF or sheet they use on a walk: their lines and their score box. You pack that form, not a rubric you invent. Checks may be file-exists on the form or a CSV of scores they exported. This tool starts quality-checklist-pack. Hospital or school forms stay those files if present. No inspection-app password belongs here. Scores you pack are numbers already on this form or its export. You do not invent a rubric. Punch-list lines on the form stay punch-list.
