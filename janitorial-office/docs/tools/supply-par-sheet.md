# Supply par sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The par sheet lists SKU or description, closet or warehouse, on-hand, par, and min if they split them. You flag at or below their min. You do not invent a min or a brand. Chemical rows still need the SDS index. Checks often look for sku and min or item and on_hand. This tool starts supply-below-par and supports sds-index-missing. They buy. You list. Closet versus warehouse rows stay split. Barcode columns stay if they have them. You do not invent on-hand from last month.
