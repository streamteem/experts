# Complaint log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The complaint log is their workbook or export: date, site, text, and status if they code open or closed. You copy the words. You do not invent a callback or a credit. Checks often look for site and date or site and status. This tool starts complaint-callback and can support missed-service when they coded a miss as a complaint. No customer-portal password belongs here. Open versus closed stays their status. You do not invent a callback row. Duplicate complaints stay duplicate unless they merged them.
