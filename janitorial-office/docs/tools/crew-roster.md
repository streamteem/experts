# Crew roster

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The crew roster is who can work: name, crew or site, night-supervisor flag, and dates they already wrote. You do not invent a person or a wage. Call-outs come from their note. Background dates are dates, not verdicts. Checks may look for name and crew columns. This tool starts night-board and holiday-coverage. No password belongs here. Night-supervisor and day-porter flags stay their columns. You do not invent a person. This tool also feeds holiday-coverage when extras need named people.
