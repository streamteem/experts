# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build for a pack: night board, below-par, missed service, or key-log gaps. Columns stay their words plus the check columns the workflow names. You do not invent hours, pars, or scores into blank cells. One site or one SKU per row unless they asked for a roll-up and you still keep detail. This tool is the usual last step that writes out/ write-ups.
