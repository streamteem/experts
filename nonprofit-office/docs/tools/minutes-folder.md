# Minutes folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Draft versus adopted should be obvious from filenames or a column. Do not overwrite adopted files to fix a typo unless they asked for a corrections process. Index meeting dates, committee versus full board, and gaps on the calendar. You may assemble a draft from notes they provided; label it draft. “The board approved” only if a later adopted file says so. Least PII: no extra medical or donor-account detail in drafts. Executive-session minutes stay in the file they named. You do not declare the corporate record complete. They adopt. You index. The next board pack reads this folder first.
