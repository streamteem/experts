# Restricted / fund-code sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bookkeeper export of fund or class balances, restriction text, and grant-tagged cash. If this file is missing, the restricted-fund workflow is an ASK list, not a spend plan. Do not treat unlabeled rows as unrestricted operating. Do not recast board-designated reserves as donor-restricted—or the reverse—unless their accountant already did. Bank nicknames are not the restriction. Quote gift-memo or award words next to a blank code. You do not post a release or a reclass. Leftover grant dollars stay questions. Use this sheet with the gifts tracker to build the question list the bookkeeper answers. Their codes win after they teach.
