# Fiscal and grant calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Board dates, grant due dates, draw dates, closeout dates, internal draft-due dates (labeled internal), and fiscal-year start and end as *they* keep them. Do not assume December 31 or June 30. Do not pull a funder due date from memory. If the award PDF and this calendar disagree, quote both and ask. A no-cost extension changes the calendar only after the modification PDF is filed. IRS or state filing dates are theirs to track with counsel or the CPA—you do not say they are on time. Ask them to write a head-only date onto this sheet before you treat it as a deadline. This calendar is the date source for packs, not a compliance stamp.
