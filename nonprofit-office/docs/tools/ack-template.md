# Acknowledgment template

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their letterhead template with the tax paragraph they already approved—cash, DAF, stock, event, or in-kind if they keep more than one. You fill gift fields from the row: name as they want it, date, amount or property description they recorded. You do not rewrite the tax paragraph, add a deductibility sentence, or invent a plate-cost net. They send and they sign. A filled draft in out/ is not issued. If no template is in the folder, ask; do not compose a Publication 1771 letter from memory as if it were theirs. Quid pro quo estimates come from their event file when the template has a blank. Vehicle and unusual gifts point at policy, not a new paragraph you write.
