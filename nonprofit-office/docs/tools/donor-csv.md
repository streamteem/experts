# Donor CSV export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Export from their donor records, gift log, or cleaned processor file. Typical columns if they have them: donor ID, date, amount, fund or restriction, appeal, acknowledgment flag, soft credit, tribute. You filter and list holes. They communicate. This is not a mail-merge send product, not SMS, and not a CRM journey. Do not mark sent because you drafted. Do not copy full account numbers or Social Security numbers into docs/. Soft credit stays a listing convenience; hard credit is who the receipt names. If pledges and cash share one amount column, ask before totaling. Segmentation is a filtered CSV they will use in their own tools.
