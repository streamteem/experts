# Conflict-of-interest forms folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Annual conflict-of-interest questionnaires and related-party lists they keep. You list who is missing a signed form, whose form is stale versus this year’s roster, and whether a related-party invoice sits next to a blank questionnaire. You do not judge the answers, waive a conflict, or give an excess-benefit opinion. Least PII: do not copy Social Security numbers from a form into docs/. Recusal is their minutes if they recorded it. This folder supports the board-pack completeness list and the private-benefit flag as facts, not as a legal finding. They collect signatures. You list holes.
