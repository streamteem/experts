# In-kind log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Date, donor label, description they wrote, program or site tag, and intake-form filename if they store one. No fair-market values you invent, no wage for donated time, no appraisal. Unusual items (vehicles, real estate, art, crypto) point at their gift-acceptance policy; you list completeness and stop. Volunteer hours belong on the volunteer log unless their files already treat hours as in-kind goods. If the description is blank, ask before the row enters a grant narrative. A third-party processor PDF may be listed as they wrote it. They accept gifts. You list. This log is not a deductibility product and not a 1098-C you complete.
