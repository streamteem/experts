# Board folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The folder names they use for a meeting date: agenda, exhibits, prior minutes, treasurer files, ED or program notes, committee items, and resolution drafts. You list what is present, assemble in *their* packet order, and label draft versus adopted. They circulate the pack and they run the meeting. Do not add outcome claims that are not in a staff file. Do not recast restricted cash as unrestricted on a cover note. Consent items still need their attachments. Executive-session material stays in the subfolder they named. Least PII in the public packet. You do not vote. Missing exhibits stay on the cover as holes. This is packet completeness, not governance advice.
