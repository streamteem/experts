# Fiscal sponsorship if they use it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they are a project under a fiscal sponsor, or they sponsor projects, the sponsor agreement controls whose books the money sits on, who issues the acknowledgment, and whose restriction language applies. Ask. Do not treat project cash as the host’s unrestricted operating. Do not treat the host’s operating cash as the project’s. Acknowledgments may go out on the sponsor’s letterhead—use *that* template if that is the agreement. Grant files may name the sponsor as the legal applicant; quote the agreement. You do not create a sub-award or move money between project and host. Board packs for a sponsored project should not imply the project is a separate 501(c)(3) unless their legal folder says so. This is file work and an ASK list, not a legal structure opinion. If both a host EIN and a project nickname appear on a gift row, copy both and ask who issues the acknowledgment.
