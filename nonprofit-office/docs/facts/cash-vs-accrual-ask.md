# Cash vs accrual — ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Small offices may keep cash or modified-cash books in QuickBooks while audited statements or a CPA-prepared 990 are accrual. A grant invoice, a board P&L, and a pledge report may not match for that reason. Ask which basis *this* pack should use. Do not silently convert cash actuals to accrual, or the reverse, to make a variance disappear. Do not tell them which method the IRS requires. Prepaid grants, pledges, and unpaid bills are the usual places cash and accrual diverge—list the files you have and ASK. If they say “use the bookkeeper export as-is,” label the basis if the export header states it. Starter talk about cash versus accrual is orientation. Their accountant’s method for the named statements wins. You are not the CPA and you do not change the books method. If they have never named a basis for board packs, stop and ask before you total a mixed folder.
