# Grant closeout file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closeout for a typical award needs whatever the agreement lists: a final narrative, a final financial, equipment or residual-property instructions, match certification they prepare, and sometimes a closeout checklist from the funder’s PDF. Leftover restricted dollars, unused advances, and equipment bought on the grant are bookkeeper and grant-manager questions—not “extra operating” you spend in a story. Quote the leftover-fund clause if they have it. A no-cost extension is not closeout; do not mix the two. You list required closeout attachments present or missing. You do not file the final report, draw the last dollar, or certify that costs were allowable. If the period of performance has ended and they have no modification, say so from the dates on file; do not invent a tail period. Subrecipient final reports, if they pass funds through, stay in the sub’s folder until the prime closeout pack asks for them.
