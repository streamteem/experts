# Files are not a fundraising CRM

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Segmentation for *their* next mailing is a filtered CSV they will use in *their* tools. You do not build journeys, drip sequences, lookalike audiences, or predictive scores. You do not operate email, SMS, or a fundraising CRM as the product. Employer-stack messaging platforms are out of scope. Wealth-append, skip-trace, and purchased lists are out of scope. A “major donor” filter is only a column they already have or a dollar cutoff they named on this export—not a scoring model you invent. Soft-credit and household rolls stay as their sheet defines them. You may list holes (missing email, missing fund, missing ack flag) so they can clean the file before *they* send. Thank-you drafts stay in out/ from their template. This desk is donor records as files, not a communications or CRM bureau. Do not log into their donor database to “just send a quick blast,” and do not store that password here.
