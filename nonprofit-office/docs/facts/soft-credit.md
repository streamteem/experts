# Soft credit / household

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Soft credit (spouse, advisor, donor-advised-fund original donor, workplace-giving coworker) is a listing convenience in *their* sheet. Hard credit is who the receipt and the books name. Do not move hard credit to make a major-donor list prettier. Do not hard-credit the original DAF donor when the letter names the sponsoring charity. Do not invent a household roll-up. They communicate with whoever their process names. Acknowledgment drafts follow the hard-credit name on the template unless they asked for a copy list that their sheet already supports. Least PII: do not add family members you inferred from a last name. If two IDs share a household and the sheet does not say so, ASK before combining totals. Soft credit never changes restricted-fund coding; the gift instrument still goes to the bookkeeper. A pretty major-donor ranking is not a reason to move hard credit from a foundation or a DAF sponsor onto an individual.
