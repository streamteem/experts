# Private benefit / related-party flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Payments to board members, founders, family, or businesses they own should appear on their conflict or related-party list if they keep one. List the file’s facts: who, what, amount if present, and which form or invoice is in the folder. Do not give a private-inurement, private-benefit, or excess-benefit legal opinion. Do not say a salary is reasonable. Do not say a rent deal with a director is allowed. Independent Sector–oriented ethics writing treats disclosure and recusal as ordinary governance practice; you still do not decide the conflict. Missing conflict forms next to a related-party payment are a completeness flag for the chair, not a finding you publish. Least PII: do not copy extra family identity documents into docs/. Legal calls go to their counsel or ED. Recusal notes belong in their minutes if they recorded them; you do not invent a recusal or excuse a missing vote.
