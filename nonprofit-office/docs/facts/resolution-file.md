# Board resolutions as files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bank signers, budget adoption, grant applications they want authorized, property leases, and policy adoptions often live as resolution drafts or minutes exhibits. You place the draft *they* wrote in the packet in the order their checklist uses. You do not vote, second, or write “adopted” on a draft. You do not invent signer names. If the resolution authorizes a grant application, that is still not a submitted application and not an award. If it authorizes a bank change, you still do not call the bank or move money. Label draft versus adopted. After the meeting, adopted text lives in the minutes or a signed resolution file—index it; do not silently replace the draft PDF with a rewritten version unless they asked for a clean copy of what passed. This is packet work, not corporate secretary legal practice. If two drafts disagree, quote both titles and ask which one goes in the packet.
