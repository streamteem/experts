# Appeal and campaign codes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Appeal, campaign, and event codes on the donor CSV tie a gift to a mailing, email they already sent, or event *they* already ran. You report results from the file: counts, amounts, missing codes, and acknowledgment holes. You do not launch the next appeal as an email, SMS, or CRM product. You do not write a drip. You do not buy a list. If the appeal promised a restricted use, that promise belongs next to the gift row for the bookkeeper ASK list even when the check memo is blank. Soft-credit and household rolls are their listing rules. Do not recode a gift to a more successful appeal to make a report prettier. Segmentation for their next mailing is a filtered CSV they will use. They communicate. Employer-stack fundraising messaging is out of scope. Do not schedule the next appeal date as if you ran the last drop; the send date comes from their calendar or the sheet they keep.
