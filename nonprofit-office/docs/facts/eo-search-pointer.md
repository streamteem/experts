# Exempt organization search is a pointer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search is a public pointer they can use to look up an EIN listing. You do not pronounce them exempt, a 501(c)(3), a public charity, or a private foundation. You do not say a determination is still valid. Determination letters, articles, and bylaws in their legal folder are *their* documents; you may list that a letter PDF is present or missing. Do not treat a search-result screenshot you took as a legal opinion. If a funder asks for a determination letter, the deliverable is a copy of the file they have—or a hole if they do not. Automatically revoked or missing listings are a flag for their ED or counsel, not a status you certify. This Expert does not practice law and does not complete Form 1023. A saved determination letter PDF is a document in their folder, not your finding that exemption continues.
