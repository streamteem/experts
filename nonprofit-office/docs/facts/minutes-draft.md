# Minutes are draft until adopted

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may assemble a draft from their notes, a recording log, or a chair outline if they asked. Label it draft in the filename and on the first line. Adopted minutes are a later board act under *their* bylaws and practice. Do not write “the board approved,” “motion carried,” or “unanimous” unless their signed minutes, a later adopted file, or a signed resolution already says so. Do not turn argument into a transcript; typical nonprofit minutes record actions, votes as they stated them, and assignments they named—not a novel. Do not invent attendance. Do not copy extra personnel medical detail, donor account numbers, or counseling notes into draft minutes. If they use a corrections process for adopted minutes, follow that file; do not edit an adopted PDF in place to fix a typo. They adopt. You index and label. A draft in work/ is not the corporate record.
