# Report requirements from their PDF

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Narrative questions, data tables, signature pages, and required attachments come from the funder’s template, portal export, or the agreement exhibit they stored—not from a starter outline or last year’s report for a different award. Fill only from program files they provide and from financial exports already coded to that grant. Do not invent outcomes, headcount, unduplicated clients, wait-list numbers, or “stories” that are not in a cited export or staff memo. If a question asks for a percentage or a target-versus-actual, copy the definition they use (household versus person, unduplicated, fiscal versus grant year) and the number in their file; if the file is missing, flag the hole. They submit. You map questions to files and list gaps. Do not click Submit. Do not sign. If the template and the agreement exhibit disagree on attachments, quote both and ask which list is live for this due date.
