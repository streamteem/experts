# Acknowledgment template is theirs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wording about goods or services, tax ID display, “no goods or services were provided,” and intangible religious benefit comes from their counsel-approved or ED-approved template if they have one. You fill blanks from the gift row: name as they want it on the letter, date, amount or property description they recorded, fund name if the template has a blank. You do not rewrite the tax paragraph, add a deductibility sentence, or invent a plate-cost net. Different templates may exist for cash, DAF, stock, event, and in-kind—use the one they named for that row. They send and they sign. A filled draft in out/ is not issued. Do not mark the CSV sent. If they have no template, ask for one; do not write a Publication 1771 paragraph from memory as if it were theirs. Starter orientation is not their letterhead.
