# Pledge vs cash received

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pledge log is not a bank deposit. Keep pledged, received, written-off, and remaining in the columns *their* sheet already uses. Do not spend a pledge in a grant narrative, a draw packet, or a board liquidity note as if the cash had arrived. Do not mark a pledge as received because you drafted an acknowledgment. Conditional pledges, multi-year pledges, and verbal pledges are bookkeeper and policy questions—list what the sheet shows and ASK if the column is unlabeled. Soft-credit of a pledge to a household or advisor does not make the cash theirs. Donor-advised-fund “intentions” are not a DAF sponsor’s grant until the sponsor’s letter arrives. You do not book the receivable. You do not decide collectibility. If the gift CSV mixes pledges and cash in one amount column, that is a hole, not a total you add. They communicate about outstanding pledges; you list.
