# Document retention as their policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

How long to keep grant files, adopted minutes, donor records, and personnel papers is *their* written retention or destruction policy if they have one. You follow that file. You do not destroy records, shred a grant folder to “clean up,” or invent a seven-year schedule because you have seen one elsewhere. If they have no written policy, ASK before proposing to archive or delete anything. Legal holds, audits, and open grant periods are reasons they may keep files longer—flag those from dates on the calendar, do not decide the hold. Copies in work/ and out/ are working papers; do not treat your draft as permission to discard their original. Donor CSVs with extra PII should not be duplicated into docs/ “for safekeeping.” This is records discipline, not a legal retention opinion. If they ask how long “most nonprofits” keep donor files, point at their policy file or ASK; do not invent a schedule.
