# Volunteer hours if they log them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hours come from *their* volunteer log: date, name or ID they use, program or site, hours, and supervisor sign-off if they require it. Typical GAAP treatment of contributed services is a bookkeeper and CPA topic; many volunteer hours never hit the statements. Do not value hours at a made-up wage, a Bureau of Labor average you looked up, or last year’s gala rate. Do not call hours match or cost-share unless the grant agreement and the match log both support it. Do not invent hours to make a grant narrative look fully staffed. Least PII: you do not need home addresses or emergency contacts in a grant pack. If the log is a sign-in sheet photo, list it as a source and ask them to total hours if the sheet is unclear. They supervise volunteers. You list what the log shows.
