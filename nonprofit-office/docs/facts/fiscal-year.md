# Fiscal year

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Board packs, year-to-date dashboards, and many grant reports need *their* fiscal year, which may not be the calendar year. Common small-nonprofit years end June 30 or December 31, but churches, schools, and agencies also use other year-ends. Ask once and write it down after they teach. Do not assume June 30 or December 31. “This year,” “YTD,” and “annual budget” mean their FY unless they said calendar. A grant period of performance is not the fiscal year; do not filter a grant invoice to FY dates unless the funder or their bookkeeper asked for that cut. If the bookkeeper export is calendar and the board wants FY, ASK which file to use; do not silently convert. 990 year on a public PDF in the folder is a labeled year, not a filing you prepare. Their FY start and end belong on the fiscal calendar tool.
