# Effort or timesheets if they use them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Personnel charged to a grant often need time records that match the fund or class code: timesheets, effort reports, or a payroll register already split by program. You may list missing timesheets, unsigned weeks, or rows whose fund code does not match the grant file. You do not certify effort, sign a federal effort report, or invent hours to make a budget line fit. Do not recode someone’s time because the grant is underspent. If they use a sample or after-the-fact activity report, use *that* form’s columns; do not convert it into a weekly timesheet story. Volunteer time is a different log and counts as match only if the agreement and the match log both say so. Payroll taxes and fringe follow their bookkeeper’s method; you do not invent a fringe rate. Missing time records are a hole in the draw packet, not a rounded salary plug.
