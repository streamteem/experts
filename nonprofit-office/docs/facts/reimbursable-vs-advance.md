# Reimbursable vs advance

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reimbursable grants pay after documented spend; advances and some draws pay before or on a schedule. Which one *this* award is lives in the agreement, the payment schedule exhibit, or the funder portal export they saved—not in a starter guess from the funder’s name. A draw or reimbursement packet is files they already coded to the grant: cover invoice or request form, GL detail for that fund, receipts or payroll registers they already filed. You assemble completeness. You do not authorize new spend, move money, or pay a bill. If they want a draw for costs not yet coded, list the gap; do not invent a total. Advances often have unused-fund and interest language; quote the clause if they ask about leftover cash, and send the question to the bookkeeper. Do not treat an advance sitting in operating checking as unrestricted. Federal or state portals are their login; you work from the export.
