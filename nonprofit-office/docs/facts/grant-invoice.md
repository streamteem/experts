# Grant invoice or draw packet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A draw or reimbursement packet is usually a cover invoice or request form plus backup: GL detail for that fund or class, receipts they already coded, payroll registers already split, and any funder cover sheet. Backup must match the fund code and the period. Missing backup is a list, not a rounded total you invent to match the budget line. Do not move cost from another fund to fill a shortfall. Do not include uncoded rows. If the award is reimbursable, the packet is documented spend; if it is an advance, quote the schedule and still attach what they require. They submit. You do not click Draw. You do not certify allowability. If cash-basis books omit an unpaid bill the funder would allow on accrual, ASK which basis this invoice uses. Period of performance dates on the cover come from the award file, not from a guess.
