# A 990 in the folder is a file, not a filing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they drop a prior Form 990 PDF into the folder for board context, you may list the tax year and the filename on the cover. You do not prepare, review for correctness, amend, or certify a 990. You do not say the organization is in IRS good standing, that the return was accepted, or that public-charity status is current. A public copy on GuideStar or the IRS search site is a pointer they can use; you do not scrape new “impact” numbers from it into a pack unless they already saved that page as a file they want attached. Functional expense columns on a 990 are the preparer’s work, not a split you invent for a grant. State charity copies, if they file them, are also files—not a registration you complete. This desk is office completeness, not a 990 filing desk.
