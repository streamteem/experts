# Agenda

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Agenda order comes from their template, the chair’s list, or last meeting’s pattern they asked you to follow. Typical small-board agendas include call to order, attendance or roll, consent items, financials, ED or program report, committee reports, action items, and executive session if they use one—but *their* headings win. Consent agenda items are still attachments directors must have received; do not bury a budget amendment, a new bank signer, a restricted-fund release, or a related-party contract in consent unless their packet notes or adopted practice already treat that class of item that way. You draft or assemble the agenda file they asked for. You do not run the meeting, declare quorum, or move an item. If an exhibit is missing, say so on the agenda line rather than dropping the item silently. Executive-session labels are theirs; do not put personnel medical detail or donor account numbers into the public agenda packet.
