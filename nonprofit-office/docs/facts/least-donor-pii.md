# Least donor PII

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keep work files to what the pack needs. Gift amount, date, and the donor ID or name they already use are enough for most grant, board, and acknowledgment packs. Do not copy full bank account or routing numbers, card numbers, Social Security numbers, driver’s-license images, or extra family medical or counseling detail into docs/. Check-front photos, if they drop them, should be listed as a source file—not transcribed account-by-account into a markdown table. Soft-credit and tribute names that are already on their sheet may be listed; do not add household members you inferred. Passwords, donor-database logins, and processor API keys never belong in this folder. If a conflict questionnaire or gift-acceptance form includes a tax ID, point at the form rather than repeating the number. Minutes and board packs stay on business; extra donor identity documents do not ride along.
