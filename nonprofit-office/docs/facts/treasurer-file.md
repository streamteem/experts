# Treasurer or finance attachment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Board finance tabs are the statements, dashboard, or treasurer memo the bookkeeper or treasurer already produced: P&L, balance sheet, budget-versus-actual, cash, and sometimes a restricted-fund rollforward. You attach and label in packet order. You do not recast restricted totals as unrestricted to make liquidity look easier. You do not silently convert cash books to accrual. If restricted cash is not broken out, ASK before a cover note talks about “what we can spend.” Do not create a new management-and-general percentage. Do not certify that the statements are complete or that the organization can meet payroll. If two exports disagree (bookkeeper P&L versus a grant invoice), quote both and ask which basis and which fund filter the board finance pack should use. Their fiscal year, not the calendar year, is “this year” once they teach it. You are not the CPA.
