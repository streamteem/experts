# Gift-acceptance policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unusual gifts—real estate, closely held stock, crypto, restricted art, vehicles, or gifts with hard-to-administer restrictions—go through *their* gift-acceptance policy and whoever that policy names (ED, committee, or board). You list the proposed gift from their intake form: donor label, description they wrote, any restriction words, and which policy checklist items are present or missing. You do not accept the gift, value it, tell the donor it is deductible, or say the board “will take it.” You do not appraise. If the policy requires an environmental review, title work, or a qualified appraisal, those are holes on the list, not tasks you perform. Cash and publicly traded stock they already have a routine for still follow their template; do not invent a new routine. They decide. You assemble completeness. A verbal “we always take cars” is not a policy substitute until they point at the written file.
