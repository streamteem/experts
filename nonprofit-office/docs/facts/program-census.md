# Program counts from their export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Headcount, unduplicated clients, households, meals, bed-nights, and outcome numbers come from the program spreadsheet or case-system export *they* produce. Definitions—unduplicated versus duplicated, household versus person, fiscal year versus grant year, enrolled versus served—are theirs. Write the definition next to the number when their file has one. Do not inflate a number to meet a grant target. Do not round a missing month. Do not mix two programs’ exports into one total unless they asked and the definitions match. If the ED report and the export disagree, quote both. You do not diagnose, treat, or identify clients in a public pack; least PII applies. Wait-list and “impact” sentences stay out unless they are in the cited file. Missing data is a hole on the grant-report map, not a story you write to fill the funder’s blank. Do not borrow a census from a public annual report that is not this export.
