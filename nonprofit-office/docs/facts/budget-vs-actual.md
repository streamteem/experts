# Grant budget vs actual

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A grant pack often compares the approved budget lines to bookkeeper actuals for *that* fund or class code. Variances are questions for the grant manager and bookkeeper, not silent moves between categories and not a net-zero you type to make the report look clean. If the agreement requires prior written approval to rebudget, say so from the PDF and list whether that approval letter is in the folder. Do not move cost across funds, classes, or awards to “make it fit.” Do not recode payroll or rent because a line is over. If actuals are on a cash basis and the funder wants accrual—or the reverse—ASK which basis this pack should use; do not silently convert. Missing backup for a line is a hole, not a rounded plug. Their chart of accounts and fund codes win. You do not certify that costs are allowable. You assemble the comparison they can review.
