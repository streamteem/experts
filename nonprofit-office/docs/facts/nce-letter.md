# No-cost extension only from a letter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A longer period of performance exists only if their modification letter, amendment, or funder email-PDF says so. Do not assume a late report is automatically extended. Do not assume a verbal “take your time” from a program officer extends the award. Do not copy another grantee’s no-cost-extension practice. If they are *requesting* an extension, you may assemble the request pack from files they named (progress to date, remaining budget, proposed new end date they wrote). They send and they sign. Until the approval PDF lands in the folder, the old end date is the date on the calendar. Budget remaining is not permission to keep spending past the period. If the modification changes only the date and not the budget, do not reopen budget lines. Quote the new end date with the document name. You do not click submit in the portal.
