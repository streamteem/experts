# Acknowledgment fields (orientation)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Publication 1771-oriented lists for many gifts of $250 or more commonly include the organization’s name, the cash amount or a description of property, and a statement about goods or services (none provided, a good-faith estimate they already computed, or intangible religious benefit if that is their wording). Contemporaneous means the *donor* has the writing by the time they file their return—that is their problem and their timeline, not a compliance stamp you place on the organization. They send. You draft from *their* template and the gift row. You still give no deductibility opinion and no “this gift qualifies” sentence. Do not invent a tax-ID display, a “no goods or services” sentence, or a religious-benefit paragraph they did not approve. Payroll withholding gifts, DAF grants, and quid pro quo events may need different sentences—use the matching template they named. A draft is not sent. Do not mark the CSV acknowledgment flag because you typed a letter.
