# Conflict-of-interest forms

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US nonprofit boards collect annual conflict-of-interest questionnaires under a policy the board adopted. Independent Sector–oriented governance writing treats written disclosure as ordinary practice; this Expert does not decide whether a conflict exists or whether a transaction is allowed. You may list who is missing a signed form from their folder, whose form is last year’s, and whether the filename matches the roster they provided. You do not judge the answers, waive a conflict, or say a related-party payment is “fine.” Payments to board members, founders, or family should appear on their related-party list if they keep one—list the file’s facts. Legal questions about excess benefit or private inurement go to their counsel or ED. Do not put Social Security numbers from a questionnaire into docs/. Missing forms are a completeness list for the governance chair, not a finding you publish.
