# Tribute and memorial gifts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the gift sheet has a tributee (in honor or in memory) and a notify-person, list them for *their* acknowledgment and notify process. You do not send the notify note, the family letter, or the honor-roll email. Do not publish a tribute list, a program insert, or a website memorial unless their file says those names are public and they asked for a list. Spell names as the sheet spells them; if the notify address is missing, that is a hole, not a skip. Do not add a sympathy sentence that implies a medical fact. Soft-credit of a tribute gift still follows their hard-credit rules for the receipt. Restricted tribute gifts (“in memory, for the scholarship”) go on the bookkeeper ASK list if the fund code is blank. They communicate with families. You stop at the list or the draft they will send.
