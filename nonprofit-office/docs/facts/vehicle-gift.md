# Vehicle and large noncash

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vehicle donations and certain large noncash gifts often have extra IRS paper the organization or the donor must handle (acknowledgment timing, and in some cases forms they already know about). Point at their gift-acceptance policy, the intake form, and the files they have. You do not complete a valuation, tell the donor what to deduct, or decide whether a qualified appraisal is required. You do not fill a 1098-C or a donee acknowledgment as a legal product. List date, donor label, description they wrote (year, make, model if they recorded it), and whether a title or pickup note is in the folder. Missing title paperwork is a hole for *them*, not a gift you accept. Real estate, boats, and similar unusual property follow the same path: policy first, no appraisal from you. If they already have a third-party processor’s report, you may list what that PDF shows without adding a number.
