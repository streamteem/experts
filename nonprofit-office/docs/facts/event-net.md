# Event proceeds from their event file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gross receipts, cost of direct benefits to donors, and net from *their* event workbook—gala, golf, breakfast, auction. You do not invent a deductible portion for ticket buyers. You do not invent a plate cost. They issue whatever event acknowledgment their template uses, including quid pro quo language their finance person already computed. Auction item values, if present, come from their sheet, not from your appraisal. Sponsorships may be gifts, advertising, or a mix—ASK the bookkeeper before a narrative calls a sponsor table “a donation.” Restricted event appeals (“proceeds to scholarships”) go on the restriction ASK list if the fund code is blank. You do not run registration, the card terminal, or the blast invite. Least PII on guest lists: use the columns they need for seating or acks, not extra identity documents. They communicate. You list and draft from the workbook.
