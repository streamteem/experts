# Restriction language on the gift

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A note on a check memo, an appeal reply form, a donor email they saved, or a grant purpose line can create a donor restriction or a grant-tagged class. Copy the words into the question list for the bookkeeper—quote the source file and the exact phrase. Do not decide the accounting class. Do not treat “for the kids,” “for scholarships,” “for the building,” or “use where needed most” as if you already know the net-asset class. Ambiguous notes are ASK items. If the appeal they mailed promised a restricted use, that appeal PDF belongs next to the gift row even when the check memo is blank. Board-designated language in a minutes file is not the same as a donor note. Time-limited gifts (“this year’s camp”) still go to the bookkeeper for release timing. You do not release, reclass, or spend the dollars in a narrative. Starter only—their instrument and their accountant’s codes win after they teach.
