# Match or cost-share

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some awards require match or cost-share. What counts as match, the percentage or dollar target, the period it must fall in, whether in-kind match is allowed, and what documentation the funder wants all live in *this* agreement—not in a generic federal-match folklore note. List documented match from their match log, volunteer log (only if both the agreement and the log support it), or bookkeeper export of match-coded costs. Do not volunteer volunteer hours as match unless that log and the agreement both support it. Do not value hours at a made-up wage. Do not count the same dollar as match on two awards. Do not treat unrestricted operating spend as match unless their grant manager and bookkeeper already coded it that way. If either the agreement clause or the log is missing, ASK. You do not certify match. Leftover match questions at closeout are bookkeeper questions, not “we probably hit it.”
