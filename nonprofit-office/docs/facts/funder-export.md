# Funder portal export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they use a funder portal (federal, state, community foundation, or a corporate giving site), they export the status screen, the submitted PDF, the amendment history, or the payment schedule. You work from that export. You do not log in, store a password, or click Submit, Accept, or Draw. Assistance listing numbers, CFDA/ALN labels, award IDs, and UEI or DUNS strings appear in your write-up only if their file already has them; do not scrape a public site to “fill in” an identifier. If the export and the paper agreement disagree on period or amount, quote both and ask. Portal due dates still need to match the calendar file they keep; if only the portal has the date, ask them to save a screenshot or PDF into the grant folder before you treat it as the office deadline. This is file work, not grants-management software you operate.
