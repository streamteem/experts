# Minutes index

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A simple index of meeting dates, draft versus adopted, and filenames helps the next board pack and any auditor who asks for the minute book. Adopted versus draft should stay visible in the filename or a column. Do not edit adopted minutes in place to fix a typo unless they asked for a corrections process their bylaws or policy already describe. Do not back-date an index row. Committee minutes stay labeled as committee. Executive-session minutes, if they keep a separate file, stay in that file—do not paste extra personnel or donor PII into the public index. You assemble the index from the minutes folder they pointed to. You do not declare that the corporate record is complete. Missing adopted minutes for a date on the calendar is a hole, not a draft you promote. Do not renumber meetings to hide a gap. If they use a bound minute book and a digital folder, index both if they pointed you at both.
