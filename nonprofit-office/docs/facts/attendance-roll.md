# Attendance roll

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Attendance comes from the sign-in sheet, the virtual-meeting log they saved, or the minutes they provide. Quorum rules live in *their* bylaws file, not in a starter number. You may count names present versus the bylaws number if both files are present, and you may list who is missing a name on the attendance roll. You do not declare a meeting valid, invalid, or “official.” You do not decide whether a proxy, a call-in, or a late arrival counts; quote the bylaws clause if they pasted it and ask. Do not invent attendance to manufacture quorum. If the roster and the sign-in use different name spellings, list the mismatch rather than merging people. Executive-session attendance, if they record it separately, stays in the file they named for that purpose. This is a roll from their papers, not a parliamentary ruling.
