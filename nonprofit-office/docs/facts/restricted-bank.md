# Bank account vs fund code

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some shops use a separate bank account for a grant or building campaign; others use fund or class codes in one operating account. Either way, the restriction is in the books and the gift or award words, not only in the bank nickname. Do not assume the operating checking account is all unrestricted. Do not assume a “grant” sub-account is the only restricted cash. ASK the bookkeeper how cash is held and how it is coded. You do not open accounts, move money, or reconcile as a silent recast of restricted totals. Board-designated reserves may sit in the same bank as undesignated cash. Advances from a funder sitting in operating checking are still grant-tagged until they teach otherwise. Commingling is a bookkeeping and policy topic; you list questions, you do not pronounce a violation. A bank nickname like “building” is a clue for the ASK list, not proof of the net-asset class.
