# Do not call board-designated “restricted”

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unless their accountant already used the word on the statements, ask before you label a reserve, earmark, or quasi-endowment “restricted.” Board-designated set-asides are usually still without donor restriction in typical US GAAP language; the board can generally un-designate later. Do not mix that language with a donor note or a grant purpose line. Do not use “restricted” in a donor-facing draft without asking. Quote the minutes vote if they have one, and send the coding question to the bookkeeper. True endowment is a different file. Starter captions are not *this* shop’s captions until they teach.
