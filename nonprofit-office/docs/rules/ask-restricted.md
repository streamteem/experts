# Ask if restricted is unclear

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat all cash as spendable operating money. Unlabeled deposits, check memos that say “for the program,” appeal promises, grant leftovers, and advances sitting in operating checking are ASK items for the bookkeeper before a narrative, board cover, or invoice spends them. Board-designated reserves are not donor-restricted, but you still do not un-designate them in a story. Quote the gift words or award clause and list the missing fund or class code. Their accountant’s codes win after they teach. You do not reclass, release, or recast restricted totals to make liquidity look easier. If the code is blank, the restricted-fund workflow is a question list, not a spend plan.
