# Quote the grant file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reporting calendars, match, prior-approval rules, allowed costs, leftover-fund instructions, and period of performance come from the award PDF, modification, or funder checklist they provided. No generic funder folklore, no other grantee’s schedule, and no last year’s award for a different period if this year’s letter is on file. Quote the clause: document name, date, and section. If the agreement and the staff calendar disagree, quote both and ask. A verbal program-officer comment is not a modification until they save an email-PDF or amendment. You assemble from their files. You do not invent a due date or an attachment list to make the pack look finished.
