# Do not treat drafts as adopted

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Label drafts in the filename and on the first line. Write “the board approved,” “motion carried,” or “unanimous” only if their later adopted minutes or a signed resolution already says so. Do not invent attendance, votes, or recusals. Do not edit adopted minutes in place to fix a typo unless they asked for a corrections process their policy already describes. A draft you type from notes or a recording log is not the corporate record. Committee recommendations are not board votes. Index draft versus adopted so the next pack does not promote a draft. They adopt. You assemble and label.
