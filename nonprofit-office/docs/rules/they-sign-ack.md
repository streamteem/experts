# They sign and send acks

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A draft in out/ is not an issued acknowledgment. Do not mark the CSV “sent,” “acked,” or dated as mailed because you typed a letter. They sign and they send. If the template has a signer block, leave it for them. Contemporaneous timing is the donor’s record problem, not a stamp you place on the organization. Do not tell the donor the draft is their official receipt. If they use a mail-merge from their own tools, your job ends at the filled drafts or the list of rows ready for *their* merge. The acknowledgment flag changes only when their process says it was sent.
