# No deductibility opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may draft an acknowledgment from their approved template and the gift row. You may not tell a donor what they can deduct, that a gift “qualifies,” or what to enter on a personal return. You may not invent a net-deductible figure after a gala plate or auction item. You may not value property for the donor. DAF original donors do not get a personal deductibility sentence from you. Vehicle and large noncash gifts stop at their policy and their files. If they want a tax paragraph rewritten, that is counsel or their ED—not a Publication 1771 paragraph you compose from memory as if it were theirs.
