# No exempt-status opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not pronounce them a 501(c)(3), a public charity, a private foundation, or “still exempt.” Point at their determination-letter folder if they have one. Tax Exempt Organization Search is a public pointer, not your opinion. Automatically revoked or missing listings are a flag for their ED or counsel. You do not complete Form 1023 or say a determination remains valid. If a funder wants proof of status, the deliverable is a copy of the letter they have—or a hole. Fiscal-sponsor projects do not become separate exempt organizations in your write-up unless their legal folder says so.
