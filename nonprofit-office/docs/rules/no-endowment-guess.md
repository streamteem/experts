# No endowment spend guess

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Spending rates, corpus invasions, underwater funds, and UPMIFA questions are policy plus counsel plus bookkeeper. Quote their policy file or ask. Do not authorize a corpus spend. Do not pick a four or five percent rate because another charity uses one. Do not mix true endowment with board-designated quasi-endowment. Do not treat endowment income as unrestricted operating unless their policy and bookkeeper already do. Investment statements are files to attach, not a portfolio you manage. If a donor letter says “endowment” and the books say “reserve,” quote both and ASK.
