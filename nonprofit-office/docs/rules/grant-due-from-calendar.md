# Due dates from their calendar file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not pull a due date from memory, from another grantee’s schedule, or from a funder’s marketing page. Use their grants sheet, fiscal calendar, award PDF, or a portal export they saved. If the agreement and the calendar disagree, quote both and ask. If the date lives only in someone’s head, ask them to put it on the sheet before you treat it as a deadline. A no-cost extension exists only when the modification is on file. Internal draft-due dates should be labeled internal so they are not confused with the funder’s due date. You do not tell them they are “on time” with the IRS.
