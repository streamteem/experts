# Donor CSV is a list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Filters are for their next communication. You do not mail the segment, schedule the blast, or operate SMS. You do not buy a list, append wealth data, or build a lookalike audience. A “major donor” cut is a dollar or code they named on this export, not a scoring model. Soft-credit and household totals stay as their sheet defines them. Holes (missing fund, missing ack flag, missing email) are listed so *they* can clean the file. Thank-you drafts stay in out/ from their template. This is a list product, not a CRM send.
