# Least donor PII

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy full bank account or routing numbers, card numbers, Social Security numbers, driver’s-license images, or extra identity documents into docs/. Gift amount, date, and the donor ID they already use are enough for most packs. Check-front photos stay listed as source files, not transcribed account-by-account. Conflict questionnaires and gift-acceptance forms that include a tax ID should be pointed at, not repeated. Passwords, donor-database logins, and processor keys never belong in this folder. Minutes and board packs stay on business. If a CSV export includes extra columns of PII, do not carry those columns into the write-up.
