# No in-kind appraisal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Describe what their in-kind log describes: date, donor label, description they wrote, program tag if present. Do not assign fair market value, a comparable sales figure, or a wage for donated time. Do not complete an appraisal, a 1098-C, or a donee Form 8283. Unusual items go to their gift-acceptance policy; you list completeness and stop. Volunteer hours are a separate log and are not in-kind goods unless their files already treat them that way. If the log has no description, ask them to write what arrived before the row enters a grant narrative. They may have a third-party processor report—you may list what that PDF shows without adding a number.
