# Minutes stay on business

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy extra personnel medical detail, counseling notes, donor account numbers, or Social Security numbers into draft minutes or a public board pack. Minutes stay on business: motions they stated, votes they recorded, and assignments they named. Executive-session files stay in the folder they named for that purpose. Tribute and donor lists in minutes follow their public-name rules. Conflict forms are listed as present or missing, not pasted in full if they contain extra identity data. A recording log is a source, not a transcript of private argument. Least PII applies to minutes the same way it applies to donor CSVs.
