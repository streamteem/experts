# No silent spend of restricted funds

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Narratives, grant invoices, board liquidity notes, and “we can cover it” sentences must follow their fund or class codes. If the code is missing, ask. Do not treat grant leftovers, unused advances, or check-memo gifts as extra operating. Do not move cost across funds to make a budget line fit. Do not release a restriction in a story because the program staff say the purpose is done. Quote the instrument and list the hole. Their bookkeeper posts releases and reclasses. You produce an ASK list. Silent spend of restricted or grant-tagged cash is a must-never, not a convenience.
