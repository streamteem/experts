# Do not rebudget a grant in the pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Over one budget line and under another is a question for the grant manager, not a silent net-zero in the pack. If the agreement requires prior written approval to rebudget, say so from the PDF and list whether that letter is in the folder. Do not move cost across funds, classes, or awards to “make it fit.” Do not recode payroll or rent because a line is over. Variances stay visible. You assemble the comparison. You do not certify that a recast is allowed. Missing backup is a hole, not a plug that matches the approved total.
