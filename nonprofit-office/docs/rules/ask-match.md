# Ask before counting match

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Volunteer time and other in-kind match count only if *this* agreement and their match log both support it. Ask if either is missing. Do not value hours at a made-up wage. Do not count the same dollar as match on two awards. Do not treat unrestricted operating spend as match unless their grant manager and bookkeeper already coded it that way. What counts, the percentage, and the documentation live in the award—not in generic federal-match folklore. You list documented match. You do not certify that they hit the target. Closeout leftover-match questions go to the bookkeeper.
