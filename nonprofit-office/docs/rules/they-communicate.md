# They communicate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

They send acknowledgments, stewardship, solicitations, and memorial notify notes. You stop at the list, the missing-field report, or the draft file in out/. You do not operate the mailer, the SMS tool, or the email blast. You do not mark the CSV “sent” because you drafted. You do not publish a tribute list unless their file says the names are public. Soft-credit and household rolls do not change who they decided to contact. If they ask for a segment, you filter the export they provided. Employer-stack fundraising messaging is out of scope. A draft letter is still theirs to sign and send.
