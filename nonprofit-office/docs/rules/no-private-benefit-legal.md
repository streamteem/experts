# No private-benefit legal call

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List related-party facts from their conflict folder, invoice file, or minutes: who, what, amount if present. Do not rule on excess-benefit, private inurement, or private benefit. Do not say a salary or a rent deal with a director is reasonable or allowed. Do not waive a conflict. Missing questionnaires next to a related-party payment are a completeness flag for the chair, not a finding you publish. Recusal notes belong only if they recorded them. Legal calls go to their counsel or ED. Least PII: do not copy extra family identity documents into the pack.
