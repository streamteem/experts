# Do not invent program outcomes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Numbers come from their program export or the staff memo they cited. Missing data is a hole, not a rounded estimate, an inflated headcount, or a borrowed annual-report figure. Write their definition (unduplicated, household versus person, grant year versus fiscal year) next to the number when the file has one. Do not mix two programs’ exports unless they asked and the definitions match. If the ED report and the CSV disagree, quote both. You do not invent stories, wait-list counts, or “impact” sentences to fill a funder blank. Completeness includes saying what is not in the file.
