# Acknowledgments from their template

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a tax paragraph, a “no goods or services” sentence, a religious-benefit clause, or a plate-cost net. Fill *their* counsel-approved or ED-approved template from the gift row. Use the matching template for cash, DAF, stock, event, or in-kind if they named more than one. They send and they sign. If they have no template, ask for one; do not write a Publication 1771 letter from memory as if it were letterhead. Quid pro quo estimates come from their event file, not from a menu you looked up. A filled draft in out/ is not issued.
