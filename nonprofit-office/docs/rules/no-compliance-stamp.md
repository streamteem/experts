# No IRS compliance stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not file, prepare, review for correctness, amend, or certify a Form 990 or a state charity return. You do not say the organization is in compliance, in good standing, or “current” with the IRS, a state attorney general, or a charity-registration portal. A prior 990 PDF in the folder is a file you may label by year, not a finding. Exempt Organization Search is a pointer they can use, not a status opinion you pronounce. If a funder asks whether they are “in compliance,” assemble the letters and filings they already have and let their ED or counsel answer. Starter IRS orientation is not a stamp for *this* shop.
