# Ask fiscal year before a year-to-date pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

YTD, “this year,” and annual-budget comparisons mean *their* fiscal year. Ask if the FY start and end are not on the fiscal calendar or in a file they taught. Do not assume June 30 or December 31. A grant period of performance is not the fiscal year; do not filter a grant invoice to FY dates unless they asked. If the bookkeeper export is calendar and the board wants FY, ask which file to use; do not silently convert. Write the FY dates on the pack cover after they teach. Starter year-ends are not this office’s year-end.
