# Use the DAF or stock letter’s names

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hard-credit the organization the brokerage or DAF letter names. Soft-credit the original donor or advisor only if their sheet already does. Do not move hard credit onto an individual to make a major-donor list prettier. No deductibility talk to the original DAF donor; the sponsor’s grant is not their personal check. Use the letter’s date, amount or share count, and restriction words. You do not value the stock for the donor. Closely held stock is a gift-acceptance path, not a public-trade routine. Acknowledgment drafts follow the hard-credit name on their template. They send.
