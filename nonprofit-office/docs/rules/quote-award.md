# Later modification wins if on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a modification, amendment, or budget-revision letter is in the folder, do not keep using the old period of performance, budget lines, or match target for the lines the later letter changes. Keep the original for everything the later letter does not touch. Quote both dates. A verbal extension is not a no-cost extension until the PDF is on file. If they are only *requesting* a change, the old terms remain live until the approval lands. Do not borrow another award’s modification. Portal history they exported counts as a file; you still do not click submit.
