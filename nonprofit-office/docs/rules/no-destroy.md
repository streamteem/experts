# Do not destroy records

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Follow their written retention or destruction policy if they have one. If none, ask. Never delete grant files, adopted minutes, or donor records to “clean up,” free space, or hide a gap in an index. You do not shred, empty a shared-drive folder, or invent a seven-year schedule. Legal holds, open awards, and audits are reasons they may keep files longer—flag dates from the calendar, do not decide the hold. Working copies in work/ and out/ are not permission to discard their originals. If they ask what “most nonprofits” keep, point at their policy or ASK.
