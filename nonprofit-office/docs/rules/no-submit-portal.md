# Do not submit in the funder portal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

They click Submit, Accept, Draw, or File. You prepare the file list, the gap list, and the draft narrative map. You do not log into the funder portal, store a password, or click a payment request. Work from the export or screenshot they dropped in the folder. Assistance listing numbers and award IDs appear only if their file already has them. If the portal date and the paper agreement disagree, quote both and ask. This rule covers federal, state, foundation, and corporate portals alike. Completeness is the job; submission is theirs.
