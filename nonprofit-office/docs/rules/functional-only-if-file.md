# Functional split only if already coded

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a program, management-and-general, or fundraising percentage. Use their class, fund, or functional codes, or leave the split to the accountant. Do not recast a fund-coded P&L into a 990-style functional column to make a grant look “mostly program.” Joint-cost and allocation methods are theirs. Uncoded vendor or payroll rows are ASK items, not a percentage you assign because the vendor “feels like program.” Starter talk about functional expense is orientation. Their codes win after they teach. You do not certify a 990 functional column.
