# No passwords or portal logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, Caterease or TPP logins, rental-portal logins, or vehicle-GPS logins in this folder. Exports and PDFs they dropped are the inputs. Gate codes, alarm codes, and dock passwords stay out of docs. If a workflow needs a fresh export, they export. Screenshot folders must not show full credentials. This rule is shared across desks and stays on this one. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks.
