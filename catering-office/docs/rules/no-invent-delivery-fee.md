# Never invent a delivery fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a delivery fee, trip charge, or dollar-per-mile. Copy the fee from their fee file or signed proposal. Do not price a drive from a live map. Mileage you copy is not a fee you create. Waived fees need their written waive. You do not invent tax on the fee. You never send fuel money. Missing fee file: ask, do not guess a zone. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
