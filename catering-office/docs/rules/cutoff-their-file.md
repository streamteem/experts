# Cutoff from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The guarantee and change cutoff comes from their contract or event-order footer, not from a remembered hotel banquet clock and not from the venue's room-block cutoff. Missing cutoff: ask. Changes after that clock need the change log and their after-cutoff rule. You do not silently accept a new count. You do not invent a late fee. Tasting dates are not cutoffs unless they said so. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
