# Venue is an address, not a room you book

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Treat venue as a delivery and service address with site notes they wrote. Do not reassign a salon, combine rooms, or promise posted occupancy. Room holds and diagrams belong on the venue desk. A salon name on a saved BEO is a location note. Double loads to the same address-date stay flagged. Gate codes stay out of docs. You list where the truck goes. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
