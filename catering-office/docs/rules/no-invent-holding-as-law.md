**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a hot-hold or leftover time as law on a label, chafer note, or leftover form. Food Code pages are orientation. Copy their label template fields only. You do not pass or fail a temperature. Kitchen-admin temp logs are a different desk unless they filed event temps as their process. They cook and they log. You list files. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks.
