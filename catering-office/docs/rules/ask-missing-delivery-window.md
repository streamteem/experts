# Ask if the delivery window is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the van must roll and the order has no delivery window, ask. Do not invent a window from a map or from “usually forty-five minutes before.” Vehicle-calendar overlaps stay flagged. Load time is not the window. A venue load-in on a saved BEO is a separate clock you may copy. You do not promise a time the vehicle file cannot meet. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
