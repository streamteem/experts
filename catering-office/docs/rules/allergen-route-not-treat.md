# Allergen flags route; you do not treat

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Allergen flags copy from their allergen sheet and the event dietary list. You route them to labels and pack-out notes. You do not treat a guest. You do not invent an allergen status. You do not write a recipe to “make it safe.” You do not stamp a plate free of an allergen. Missing sheet row for a flagged dish: ask. FDA allergy pages are orientation. Kitchen-admin recipe cards apply only if they filed them as the source.
