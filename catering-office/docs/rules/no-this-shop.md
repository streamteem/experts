# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never present a starter note as how *this* catering office already works. Typical US talk about Caterease, seventy-two-hour cutoffs, and chafer math is orientation. Their event order, menu file, and fee file win. When they teach a different cutoff, ratio, or leftover rule, follow their file. Label leftover starter text as a guess. This is the same bar as label-guesses, restated for the whole desk. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
