# Never invent food cost

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a food-cost percent, a recipe cost, or a plate cost. If they stored a costing sheet, you may quote that file as their numbers. Blank stays blank. Prep lists and protein pulls are quantities, not costs you create. You do not back into a per-person price from a guessed cost. Restaurant food-cost-file work is kitchen-admin. This office invoices from the order file, not from a cost you invented.
