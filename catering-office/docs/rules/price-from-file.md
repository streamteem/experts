# Prices come from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a per-person price, a package price, a tasting fee, a kids-meal price, or a bar-package price. Copy dollars from the menu file, signed proposal, or invoice template they stored. Blank stays blank and becomes an ask. A remembered wedding package from another year is not a price. Venue prices on a saved BEO are the hall's unless they said those dollars bill through this office. You do not invent food cost to back into a price.
