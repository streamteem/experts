# Ask if the count is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If guarantee is due and missing, stop and ask before the pack-out looks complete. Do not invent a count from expected RSVP, room size, or a saved BEO occupancy line. Expected may be listed as expected. Vegetarian, kids, and vendor meals that do not add to their guarantee math are asks too. You do not hide a blank behind “typical 150.” They collect the number. You list the hole. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
