# Never invent a service charge

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a service-charge or gratuity percent. Copy the name and amount from their menu file, contract, or invoice template. Use their word; do not relabel service charge as tip. Do not add a tip line they did not write. Do not give a tax opinion on the charge. Venue banquet service charges on a saved BEO stay the hall's number unless they said it bills here. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
