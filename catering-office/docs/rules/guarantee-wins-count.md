# Guarantee wins the count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Kitchen prep, pack-out plate math, and billing counts use the guarantee when it exists, not expected RSVP and not a room-size guess. Expected stays labeled expected. Actual after service bills only as their file bills the higher of guarantee or actual. Vegetarian, kids, and vendor meals follow their split, but they do not replace a missing guarantee. Missing guarantee: ask. You do not lower a guarantee to make a package look cheaper. You do not invent a count from venue occupancy.
