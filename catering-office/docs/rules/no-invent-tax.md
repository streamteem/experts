# Never invent tax

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a sales-tax rate or a split between food and labor. Copy tax from their invoice file or tax table. Blank stays blank. Do not give a tax opinion. IRS Publication 334 is orientation. You do not say they are filed with the state. Venue tax on a saved BEO is the hall's bill. You never send a tax payment. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
