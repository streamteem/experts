# Drop-off vs full service from the order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Whether the job is drop-off or full service comes from the event order. Do not upgrade drop-off to full service to add a captain or china on paper. Do not invent a captain for drop-off. Staffing, leftover forms, and pack-out templates must match the flag; mismatches are asks. A saved venue full-service BEO does not convert their drop-off order unless they filed that change. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
