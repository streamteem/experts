# Ask if the allergen sheet is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the menu has dishes and they rely on an allergen sheet, a missing sheet is an ask before labels look complete. Do not invent flags from memory. Do not treat. Route only rows they wrote. Per-event dietary lists still need the house sheet or recipe cards they pointed at. You do not stamp a plate safe. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
