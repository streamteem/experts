# No card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store customer or vendor card numbers, CVV, or full track data in this folder. Deposits and invoices are files, not cards you run. If a PO PDF shows a full card, do not copy the digits; ask for a redacted page. Client PO numbers are fine. This rule sits next to no-passwords. You never send money. They collect. You list paid/unpaid only as the invoice file shows. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
