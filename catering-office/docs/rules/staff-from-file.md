# Staff counts from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Chef, captain, server, bartender, and driver counts come from their roster, labor grid, or written ratio file. Do not invent a ratio from a remembered banquet rule. Do not invent names. Overlapping windows on one person stay flagged. Bartender is a count, not a license. Overtime dollars need their overtime file. Venue union calls on a saved BEO stay labeled venue. Missing roster: ask. You do not fire anyone. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
