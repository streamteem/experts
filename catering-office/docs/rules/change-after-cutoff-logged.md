# Late changes stay on the log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Count, menu, style, and window changes after cutoff stay on the change log with the cutoff date visible. Do not hide lateness. Do not invent a late fee. Do not silently raise prep. Guarantee-wins-count still applies until they file a new guarantee. Pack-out and prep lists need a revision flag. They accept or refuse. You list old and new. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
