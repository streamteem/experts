# Label guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a typical US SMB catering layout so they can teach, label it a guess or a starter. Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. Blank cells stay blank. Do not dress a guess as their Caterease export. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Ask rather than assume a seventy-two-hour cutoff or a twenty-percent service charge. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
