# Rentals from the vendor file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tables, extra china, tents, and extra chafers come from their rental PO or vendor file. Do not invent a SKU, a price, or a return time. Do not cancel a PO on paper because house kit looks enough. Do not pay the rental house. Venue-provided kit on a saved BEO stays labeled venue. Missing delivery address or return window on a rental-heavy job: ask. Damage notes are their file, not a lawsuit you write.
