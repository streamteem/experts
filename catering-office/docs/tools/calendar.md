# Calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The calendar is their event-date file or export: job, date, venue address, and vehicle if they put it there. Copy dates as written. You do not invent a date or a delivery window. Tasting dates stay labeled tasting. This tool feeds week-of-events. Holidays follow their file, not a guessed close day. A venue room calendar is not this tool unless they saved it as reference. No calendar-account password belongs here.
