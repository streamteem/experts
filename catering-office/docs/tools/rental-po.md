# Rental PO

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The rental PO lists vendor kit: item, qty, deliver window, return window, and price they already wrote. Copy lines. You do not invent a SKU or a price. You do not pay the vendor. This tool feeds rental-pos. House equipment is a different file. Venue vendors on a saved BEO stay labeled venue. Missing return time on a rental-heavy job is a flag. No rental-portal password belongs here. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
