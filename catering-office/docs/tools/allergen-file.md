# Allergen file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The allergen file is their house dish-versus-flag chart. Copy rows. You do not invent a sesame or peanut status. You do not treat a guest. This tool feeds dietary-list and label notes. Per-event flags route here; missing rows are asks. FDA pages are orientation. Kitchen-admin recipe cards apply only if they pointed at them. You never stamp a plate safe. No extra guest medical record belongs in this file. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
