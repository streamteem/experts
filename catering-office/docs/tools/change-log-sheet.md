# Change-log sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The change-log sheet lists dated revisions: count, menu, style, window, and whether the change is after cutoff. Copy old and new as they wrote them. You do not hide a late change. You do not invent a late fee. This tool feeds change-after-cutoff-log. A saved venue BEO revision is a source you may quote, not a silent replace. Blank logs on a signed job that later moved are asks. No passwords belong here.
