# Staff count from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Staff counts — chefs, captains, servers, bartenders, dishwashers — come only from their staffing file, event-order labor grid, or a written house ratio they stored. Typical US SMB caterers keep a ratio sheet by service style and guarantee, or they type names on a roster. You do not invent a server count from a remembered “one per twenty.” You do not stamp a bartender as licensed. If the roster names four and the order labor grid says six, quote both and ask. Overtime dollars stay off the pack unless their overtime file has a rate or a rule. Venue banquet-union calls on a saved BEO are the hall's labor, not yours, unless they said they must staff to that BEO. Restaurant line-cook shift counts are kitchen-admin. Missing staff file: ask. You list numbers and names as written. They hire and they schedule.
