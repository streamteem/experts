# Leftover policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Leftover policy is their written rule for food that remains after service: client release, staff meal, compost, trash, or a donation path they already documented. Typical US SMB caterers print a leftover-release form for full-service jobs and a “do not leave” rule for some drop-offs. You copy the policy file. You do not invent a donation legal status. You do not invent a holding time as law. USDA leftover pages are orientation only. Restaurant waste-log reasons are a different desk unless they said event leftovers post to that log. If the policy requires a signed release and the folder has none, flag completeness — do not assume the client took the pans. You do not tell them the food is safe to keep. They own the pans and the signature. You list whether the form is present and what the policy file says.
