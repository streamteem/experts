# Protein pull

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A protein pull is the thaw or draw list for meats and other proteins for one event date, taken from their recipe or pull file and the guarantee. Typical US catering kitchens pull one or two days ahead and label by event number. You do not invent a pull weight from a room size or from a remembered ounces-per-guest rule. You do not invent a yield. If the recipe file has no protein line, ask. Restaurant walk-in protein-thaw for the dining room is kitchen-admin; do not merge those pars onto this event unless they said the same box feeds both and they filed the split. Allergen flags on a protein (shellfish, sesame marinade) route to the allergen sheet, not to a recipe you write. You list item, quantity, and event. They pull the walk-in. You do not guess on-hand in the freezer.
