# Tasting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A tasting is a scheduled sample meal before the event, with a date, guest count for the tasting, and a menu from their tasting file or the event menu. Typical US wedding caterers book tastings weeks out and keep notes as a file. The tasting is not the event, not a health inspection, and not a price you invent. Deposit rules for tastings come from their deposit schedule. You do not invent a tasting menu. You do not invent a per-person tasting fee. If tasting notes change the event menu, that change needs the change log and a new menu line they approved. Venue-hosted tastings on a saved BEO are the hall's calendar; copy the date if they filed it, and still keep the caterer's tasting row. Missing notes after a tasting they marked complete is an ask. You list date and menu as written. They cook the tasting.
