# Chafers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Chafers are the standard hot-hold pieces on a buffet pack-out: full-size, half-size, soup, and the fuel they use. Typical US catering offices assign chafer counts from service style and guarantee, using a house rule they stored — not a remembered “one per thirty guests.” Fuel, water pans, and lighter notes belong on the pack-out if they list them. You do not invent a fuel quantity from a textbook. Electric chafers need a site-power note they wrote; you do not stamp electrical safety. If two events share a Saturday and the house chafer count is short, flag it and look at the rental PO before you tidy the board. Venue chafers on a saved BEO are venue kit, not house kit, unless they said they will use them. You list size and count as their equipment file and order require. They light the fuel. You do not invent a holding time as law.
