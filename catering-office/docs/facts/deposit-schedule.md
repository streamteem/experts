# Deposit schedule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The deposit schedule is the dollar amounts and due dates they already wrote on the proposal, contract, or invoice file — retainer, second payment, and balance. Typical US SMB caterers collect a percent to hold the date and the rest by a cutoff. You copy amounts and dates from that file. You do not invent a percent. You do not run a card. You do not store PAN. A deposit is not a guarantee count. If the invoice file and the event order disagree on a deposit, quote both and ask. You do not say they are paid in full unless the invoice file says so. Venue banquet deposits on a saved BEO are the hall's money, not yours. Restaurant gift-card balances are a different desk. Missing schedule: ask. They collect. You list what the file shows and you never send money.
