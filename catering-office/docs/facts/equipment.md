# House equipment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

House equipment is the caterer's own kit: chafers, cambros, sheet pans, coffee urns, folding tables they own, and numbered bins on their equipment list. Typical US SMB offices keep a simple inventory with on-hand and reserved-for-event columns, or they count the cage the night before. You copy on-hand from that list, not from a remembered cage. Two Saturday events that both need twelve chafers is an equipment-versus-events fight, not a silent double-book. Rentals are not house on-hand until the vendor PO says they land. Venue-owned tables and the hall's china are not yours to promise unless the BEO they saved says the venue provides them. You do not invent a piece count. You do not pay a rental house to cover a hole. Shortages stay visible. They buy or they rent. You list what their file shows for each event date.
