# Guest count (expected)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Guest count on an early order is expected headcount, not the guarantee and not a fire-occupancy number. Typical US planners change the number several times; catering offices keep expected, guaranteed, and actual as separate columns when their files split them. Do not overwrite expected with a hope that more RSVPs will arrive. Do not use expected as the kitchen produce number if they require a guarantee. Do not invent a count because the venue “looks like it holds 180” — you are not the venue desk and you do not read posted occupancy as law. If expected and vegetarian plus kids plus vendor meals do not add the way their sheet adds, quote the parts and ask. Restaurant covers-forecast is a different fact on the kitchen-admin desk. Setup plate counts should trace to guarantee when the guarantee exists, else to expected with that label. Missing count: ask. You list numbers as filed. They collect RSVPs.
