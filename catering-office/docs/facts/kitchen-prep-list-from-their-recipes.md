# Kitchen prep list from their recipes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The event prep list is a produce-and-prep sheet built only from their recipe cards or yield file plus the guarantee — not from a restaurant daily par and not from a remembered wedding menu. Typical US catering commissaries print prep one or two days out: proteins, sauces, vegetable cuts, and label counts. You do not invent a formula or a yield. If the recipe file is missing for a menu line, ask; do not write a recipe. Protein pull is a child of this list when they thaw from that file. Restaurant 86 boards do not cut this event unless they filed that rule. You do not invent a food cost from the prep list. Mise quantities follow their card math, not a guess. You assemble the list from their recipes and the order. They cook. You do not stamp a holding time as law. This is event prep, not the kitchen-admin standing par cycle.
