# Vendor meals

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor meals are plates for the band, photographer, planner, or venue crew, counted on the order as they wrote them. Typical US full-service jobs add a vendor-meal count that may or may not sit inside the guarantee. You copy the count and whether it bills. You do not invent a vendor-meal price. You do not invent a count from a remembered “always feed the band.” If the saved BEO lists vendor meals and the caterer's order does not, quote both. Dietary flags on vendor meals still need their sheet. You list the number. They cook. You do not add planner meals as a courtesy that changes food cost you also must not invent. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
