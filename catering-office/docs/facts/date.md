# Event date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The event date is the calendar day the food leaves or the service starts, copied from the event order or the signed proposal they stored. Typical US SMB catering offices also keep a setup date, a tasting date, and a return-pickup date as separate fields when rentals or drop-off boxes span more than one day. Do not invent a date from a remembered “Saturday wedding season” or from the venue's public calendar. If the order says Saturday and the vehicle calendar shows the van booked Sunday, quote both and ask. A tasting on Tuesday is not the event date. A rain-move date they wrote is a new date only when they filed the change. Restaurant daily specials and venue room holds use other calendars; do not borrow those dates onto this order. Multi-day corporate programs need one row per service day if that is how they number jobs. You list the date as written. They move the job. You do not promise a same-day add the vehicle file cannot cover.
