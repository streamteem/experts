# Linens

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Linens on this desk are the caterer's napkins, cloths, overlays, and skirting on the pack-out or a linen-rental PO — not the venue's banquet-linen color on a hall BEO unless they saved that BEO and said the venue supplies linen. Typical US SMB caterers either own a small cage or rent from a linen company. Color, size, and count come from the event order or the rental PO. Do not invent a color to match a mood board. Do not assume rounds of ten need a 90-inch cloth; that math lives on their linen file or the rental catalog they stored. If house linen and a rental PO both cover the same event, list both so they do not double-order. Return-soiled counts belong on the return column. You do not pay the linen company. Restaurant dining-room napkin pars are a different desk. You list what the order and the PO show.
