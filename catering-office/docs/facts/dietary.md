# Dietary notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dietary notes are client-written restrictions and preferences: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free as they labeled it, religious notes they typed, and kids meals they counted. Typical US catering offices keep a dietary sheet per event or a column on the order. Copy the words they used. Do not translate a preference into a medical diagnosis. Do not invent a substitute dish from a remembered “we always send the pasta.” Allergen flags route to their allergen sheet and to kitchen labels; dietary counts still need a menu item they already sell or a written substitution. If vegetarian count plus kids plus vendor meals exceed the guarantee, flag the math. Missing dietary sheet when the order says “see attached” is an ask. You do not treat a dietary note as a recipe you invent. Restaurant allergen cards for the dining room are a different file unless they pointed at them. They own substitutions. You list counts and the source words.
