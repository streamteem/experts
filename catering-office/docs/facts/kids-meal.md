# Kids meal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Kids meals are the child-plate count and the kids menu from their file. Typical US wedding and reunion orders list a cheaper kids plate with a cutoff age they wrote — you copy the age rule if present, and you do not invent one. You do not invent a kids price. You do not invent a kids menu. Counts sit beside the guarantee as their sheet splits them. Allergen flags (milk, wheat) still route. Missing kids count when the order says “kids TBD” is an ask before pack-out. You list count and dish. They plate. You do not treat a kids meal as a diagnosis of what a child may eat. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
