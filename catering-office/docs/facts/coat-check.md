# Coat check

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Coat check on this desk is whether the caterer staffs a coat rack or only notes that the venue runs one. Typical US winter full-service jobs either send a rack and an attendant from the staffing file or mark “venue coat check” from a saved BEO. You copy the flag. You do not invent an attendant. You do not invent a coat-check fee. Hangers and racks belong on the pack-out or a rental PO if they supply them. You do not store guest names from a coat ticket in extra PII. Venue coat-check labor on a BEO is hall staff. You list who owns the rack. They staff. You do not promise capacity for coats. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
