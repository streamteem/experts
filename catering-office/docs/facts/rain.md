# Rain plan

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A rain plan is their written indoor move, tent add, or delay for an outdoor service — trigger, decision time, and the indoor address or tent PO they stored. Typical US garden and park caterers keep a rain-call clock on the order. You copy that file. You do not invent a tent. You do not invent a decision hour from a forecast you looked up unless they asked you to quote a forecast file they saved. Venue rain plans on a saved BEO are the hall's move; copy them if filed, and still keep the caterer's pack-out changes (extra flooring, extra chafers) as their list. Indoor-out service style may change; that change needs the change log if it hits menu or count. You do not stamp that the new space holds the guarantee. You list the written plan. They call the rain. You do not pay the tent company.
