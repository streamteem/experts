# Delivery window

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The delivery window is when the van may arrive and when food should be on the table or in the client's fridge — start and end as they wrote them on the order or the timeline. Typical US SMB drop-off caterers print a window, not a single magic minute, because traffic and the previous stop exist. You do not invent a window from a map guess or from “usually forty-five minutes before.” If the vehicle calendar shows the same van in two windows that overlap, flag the conflict. Load time at the kitchen is not the delivery window. Return pickup is a second window. Site rules that forbid Saturday morning dock access stay attached. You do not promise a time the vehicle file cannot meet. A venue load-in on a saved BEO is the hall's dock clock; copy it if they filed it, and still keep the caterer's delivery window as its own field. Missing window: ask.
