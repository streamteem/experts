# Load

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Load is the kitchen-side act of filling the van from the pack-out: who loads, which vehicle, departure time, and cooler or hot-box notes. Typical US catering offices put load time on the day sheet so protein pull and labeling finish first. Load is not the venue's load-in and not a restaurant AM prep start. You do not invent a load time that ignores the delivery window and the drive they recorded. If two events share a van and the load times collide, flag it. You do not put gate codes in the write-up. Ice and cooler counts belong on the pack-out that feeds the load. Photos of a loaded van are files they dropped, not a stamp that food stayed safe. You list the load row as written. They pack the truck. You do not hide a missing pack-out behind “they always know what to take.”
