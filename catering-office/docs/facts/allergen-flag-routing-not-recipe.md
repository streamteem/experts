# Allergen flag routing (not a recipe)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An allergen flag on this desk is a routing label: which dish they marked, which guest count they wrote, and which kitchen or label step their allergen sheet requires. It is not a recipe, not a diagnosis, and not a legal finding that a plate is safe. Typical US SMB caterers keep a house allergen sheet next to the menu file and a per-event flag list. Copy the flag and the sheet row. Do not invent an allergen status from a remembered ingredient. Do not treat or “make it safe.” FDA allergy pages are orientation only. If the event order says sesame or dairy and the allergen sheet has no row, ask — do not write a formula. Restaurant recipe-card allergen quotes stay on the kitchen-admin desk unless they filed those cards as the source for this event. You route the flag to labels and to the pack-out. They decide the plate. You do not stamp the food safe.
