# Pack-out list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pack-out list is what leaves the commissary or kitchen for one event: food pans, labels, chafers, fuel, linens, china, flatware, coolers, ice, serving pieces, and paperwork. Typical US SMB caterers print a pack-out the day before or the morning of, from the event order plus their equipment list. This is not a restaurant daily prep list and not a venue room diagram. Quantities follow guarantee and their recipe or yield file, not a guessed “two extra trays.” If equipment on the pack-out exceeds house inventory plus the rental PO, flag a shortage. Drop-off jobs still need a pack-out; full-service jobs add captain boxes and extra china. You do not invent a missing item to make the truck look complete. Return columns belong on the same list when they use them. You build the list from their files. They load the van. You do not hide a shortage inside “they can borrow from the hall.”
