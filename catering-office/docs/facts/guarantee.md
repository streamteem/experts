# Guarantee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The guarantee is the count the kitchen, pack-out, and billing will use, due by a cutoff on their contract or event order — often seventy-two hours or a named business-day rule they wrote. Typical US catering offices cook and pack from guarantee, then bill the higher of guarantee or actual if that is their file. You do not invent the due date from a remembered hotel banquet rule; that clock lives on the venue desk. Missing guarantee: ask. Do not treat a text “we think 120” as the guarantee unless they said that file is the guarantee. You do not lower a guarantee to make a package look cheaper. Actual plate count after service belongs on the invoice if they record it; it does not silently rewrite the order without a change-log row. Vegetarian, kids, and vendor meals sit inside or beside the guarantee only as their sheet splits them. They collect the number. You list due date, number, and source file.
