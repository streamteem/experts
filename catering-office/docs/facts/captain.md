# Captain

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The captain is the on-site lead for a full-service job — name from the roster, phone as they chose to file, and the timeline they carry. Typical US caterers assign one captain per event or per tent. Drop-off jobs may have no captain; do not invent one. You copy the name. You do not invent a captain fee. You do not put a personal cell in docs if they redacted it. The captain is not a liquor license and not a health inspector. Change-after-cutoff notes the captain should see belong on the change log. You list who they named. They assign. You do not fire a captain or invent a replacement from a remembered freelancer list. Starter practice until they teach how *this* catering office files the same item. This desk is the caterer's event office — pack-out, delivery, and event orders — not restaurant daily BOH pars and not the venue's room-hold BEO desk. Blank fields stay blank and become asks. They price, they staff, they cook, they drive.
