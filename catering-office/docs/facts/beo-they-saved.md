# BEO they saved

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A BEO they saved is a venue or hotel banquet-event-order PDF the client or hall sent, stored in this folder as a reference. This desk may copy times, counts, and menu lines from that file. You do not issue the BEO, hold a room, stamp occupancy, or rewrite the hall's F&B minimum — that is the event-venue-coordinator desk. If the saved BEO fights the caterer's event order on count, menu, or style, quote both and ask which the kitchen follows. Draft BEOs stay labeled draft. You do not invent a BEO when none was saved. You do not treat the BEO as a liquor license. Prices on the BEO are the venue's unless they said those dollars bill through the caterer. You list the file as a copy. They decide the source of truth. Starter notes never pretend this office is the hall.
