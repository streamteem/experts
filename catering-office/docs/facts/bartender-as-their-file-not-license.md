# Bartender as their file (not a license)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A bartender on this desk is a staff-count or a vendor-bartender row from their file — name or quantity, start, end — not a liquor license and not a TTB or state-stamp of legal service. Typical US caterers either send their own bar staff, hire a mobile bar, or let the venue pour under the hall's license. You copy what they wrote. You do not say the service is legal. You do not invent a bartender count from a remembered “one per seventy-five.” Bar packages and last-call times come from their bar-package file or the saved BEO, labeled as whose file. TTB pages are orientation only. You do not store a license PDF's full personal data beyond what they already filed as a present/missing flag. Restaurant alcohol-out 86 is kitchen-admin. You list the count. They staff. You never serve as the license.
