# Never lock someone out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not lock a resident or an occupant out, recore a cylinder, shut off a fob, or advise a self-help eviction. Typical US communities treat lockouts as a manager-plus-legal process on the other desk. You do not keep extra keys to use as leverage. Missing renter insurance or a late packet is a completeness miss, not a reason you withhold keys on your own. Key codes do not go into docs. They issue and they retrieve hardware. Starter until they teach who may touch *this* shop's key control.
