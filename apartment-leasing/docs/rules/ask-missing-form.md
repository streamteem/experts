# Ask when a required form is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their checklist names a form and the folder does not have it, mark missing and ask. Do not silently skip a pet addendum, a lead page, or a roommate form to hurry a start date. Do not invent a substitute form from the internet. Unreadable scans are missing-quality, not present. Completeness is not approval. They chase the applicant. Starter until they teach *this* shop's required list and what counts as a readable copy.
