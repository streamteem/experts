# Packet work is completeness only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lease-packet work is present-or-missing against their checklist, not a legal review of clauses. You do not rewrite an addendum. You do not decide the packet is enforceable. Lead pamphlets and community policies are file checks. Unsigned drafts stay drafts. If the checklist is missing, ask for it; do not invent a national packet. They generate and they send the set. Starter until they teach *this* shop's current form versions and which addenda always travel with the lease.
