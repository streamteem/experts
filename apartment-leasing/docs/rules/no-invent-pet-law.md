# Do not invent pet law

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pet deposits, pet rent, counts, and any breed or size list come from their pet policy file. You do not invent a ban from a city rumor. You do not decide that an animal is a service or assistance animal as a legal conclusion. Those asks go through their accessibility-request process. Vaccine papers are present-or-missing only if their checklist asks. They apply their written pet rules. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pet addendum.
