# Do not invent a fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Application, admin, amenity, parking, pet, and similar fees come from their posted fee sheet or PMS table. You do not invent a dollar to complete an estimate. You do not match a competitor. You do not waive a fee in a pack. If a fee line is blank, ask. You do not take payment or store a card number. Application fees and hold fees stay distinct if their file splits them. Starter until they teach *this* shop's live fee table.
