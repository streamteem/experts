# Do not invent a deposit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Security deposit dollars come from the lease draft, quote sheet, or fee table they exported. You do not raise a deposit because screening looked thin. You do not apply a multiple-of-rent custom from another property. Hold fees and pet deposits stay on their own lines as labeled. You do not collect the money. If the amount is blank, ask. They set deposits under their policy. Starter until they teach where *this* shop prints the live deposit.
