# Label starter guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are starter practice for a typical US leasing desk until they teach how *this* shop already works. If you fill a gap, label it as a guess or a question, not as their policy. Do not present a handbook sentence as their live rule. Hours, fees, specials, and criteria are especially easy to get wrong across brands. When two files disagree, quote both. They correct the pack. Starter means ask early, not invent quietly.
