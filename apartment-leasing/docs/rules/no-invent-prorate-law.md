# Do not invent a prorate rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Partial-month rent comes from their PMS estimate or written day-count rule. You do not apply a thirty-day custom from another state or a statute you remember. You do not invent a rent-control daily cap. If the estimate is blank, ask. Do not net concessions into a new prorate you invented. Quote their factor and their dollar as labeled. They confirm the move-in estimate. Starter until they teach *this* shop's day-count field.
