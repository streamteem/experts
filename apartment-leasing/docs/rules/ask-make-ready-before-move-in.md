# Check make-ready before a move-in date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Before you treat a lease start as workable, read the make-ready sheet. If the unit is not ready, flag the clash; do not silently slide the date and do not promise keys. Typical US turns slip. You do not mark ready from a tour photo. Work orders stay on the other desk; you only read status. They pick a new start or they finish the turn. Starter until they teach *this* shop's ready code and who may change a commence date.
