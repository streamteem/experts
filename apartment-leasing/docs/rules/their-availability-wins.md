# Their availability file wins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit status comes from their availability export, not from a walk-through memory or a photo. If the sheet says on notice, held, or down, the showing list and the specials match follow that code. Do not mark a unit open because the model is empty. If two exports disagree, quote both and ask. Date available is their field. You do not create a vacant ready row to fill a tour. Starter until they teach which report *this* shop treats as live.
