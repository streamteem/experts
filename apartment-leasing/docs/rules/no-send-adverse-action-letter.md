# Do not send an adverse-action letter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not write, mail, or improve an adverse-action notice. You may list that a vendor or PMS letter is present or missing. You do not explain FCRA rights as counsel. You do not tell the applicant they were denied. CFPB and FTC pages are orientation. Deadlines and reasons stay on their generated form. They or their vendor send the notice. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop triggers that letter.
