# Read hours before a showing list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Build a showing list only after you read their hours file, including Saturday, lunch, and holidays. Typical US offices change holiday hours without rewriting the website the same day; the file they dropped wins if they said it is live. Do not book a slot when the office is closed. Do not invent Sunday hours. After-hours exceptions need their after-hours note. If hours are missing, ask before you publish a tour day. Starter until they teach where *this* shop posts live hours.
