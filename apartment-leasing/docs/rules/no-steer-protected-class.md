# Do not steer on protected class

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not sort, code, or comment on guest cards or showing lists using race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or any other class their training named. Do not assign buildings by household type. Language preference is a service note, not a building assignment. Families with children see the same available stock their file would show anyone else, subject only to their written occupancy policy — which you still do not invent. Refuse legal hypotheticals. Starter until they teach *this* shop's training notes.
