# Do not steer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not steer a person toward or away from a building, floor, or floorplan for reasons that are not on their written availability and criteria files. Show the units their process would show any like shopper. Do not code a tour list by family, language, or disability as a sorting key you invented. Accessible units are inventory when they ask, not a wing you hide or push. HUD orientation is why this rule exists; you still give no legal opinion. Starter until they teach *this* shop's showing-order rule.
