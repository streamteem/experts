# Do not invent an occupancy cap

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not invent how many people may live in a floorplan. Occupancy limits, if any, come only from their written community policy or lease. You do not apply a two-plus-one rule from memory. You do not steer household size. Roommate adds follow their forms. If the policy is silent, ask — do not fill the silence with a statute. This is also not a fair-housing opinion. Starter until they teach *this* shop's written occupancy language.
