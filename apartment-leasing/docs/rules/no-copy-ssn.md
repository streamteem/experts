# Do not copy SSNs into docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy Social Security numbers, full account numbers, or card PANs into docs, CSVs, or write-ups. Application PDFs they already store in their PMS stay there. Completeness lists use present or missing. If a PDF they dropped in this folder contains an SSN, do not retype it; ask them to keep official copies in their system. Same rule for guarantors. Passwords and bank logins never belong here. Starter until they teach what *this* shop redacts.
