# Keep their waitlist order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List waitlist rows in the order their file already uses. You do not jump a referral to the top. You do not drop a stale row unless their policy says to expire it. Market lists and affordable lists stay separate. A waitlist seat is not a hold and not an approval. If order is ambiguous, ask; do not invent a lottery. They notify the next person under their process. Starter until they teach how *this* shop orders and purges lists.
