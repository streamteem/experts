# Minimum necessary on IDs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Record that a photo ID is present or missing. Do not type license numbers, SSNs, or barcodes into docs or CSVs. Do not keep extra color copies in this folder when a flag is enough. Name mismatches are questions, not a fraud stamp you apply. Portal passwords do not belong here. Guarantor and roommate IDs follow the same thin rule. Typical US offices keep official images in the PMS, not in a chat pack. Starter until they teach what *this* shop must retain.
