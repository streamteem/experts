# Do not promise a unit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not tell a prospect the unit is theirs, will still be vacant, or is held, unless their hold file already says so for that person. Availability changes. Models are not automatically leasable. Virtual tours may show a like floorplan. A waitlist seat is not a promise. You do not place a hold. If they want a reservation, point at their hold-fee process. They convert holds to leases. Starter until they teach *this* shop's promise-versus-hold words.
