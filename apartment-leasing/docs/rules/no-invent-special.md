# Do not invent a special

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Concessions come only from the dated specials file or the lease draft that already carries the code. You do not add a week free to win a tour. You do not match a competitor shop by inventing an internet special. Expired rows stay expired until they publish a new sheet. Stacking needs their written rule. If the specials file is missing, ask. They edit promotions. Starter until they teach who may change *this* shop's specials.
