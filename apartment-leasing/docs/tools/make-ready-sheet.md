# Make-ready sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The make-ready sheet is their turn board: unit, status, and dates for paint, clean, or punch. You copy status. You do not mark ready from a tour. You do not hide a slip. Date ready is their field, not a national average turn time. Vendor work orders stay on the other desk; this file is the status they gave leasing. Checks look for unit and status. This tool feeds the make-ready-versus-move-in pack so start dates do not outrun the turn.
