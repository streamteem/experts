# Community policy and hours PDFs

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Community-policy PDFs are the dated house rules, pet policy, fee flyer, and sometimes the hours or holiday sheet they printed. You quote only that revision. You do not invent a quiet-hours time or a fee. If two dates disagree, ask which is live. Lead pamphlets may sit beside these files as present or missing. No portal login belongs here. Checks confirm the file exists. This tool supports hours-and-tours and packet checklists.
