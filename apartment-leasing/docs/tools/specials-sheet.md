# Specials sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The specials sheet is their dated concession list: floorplan or unit type, lease term, offer wording, and start and end dates. You attach specials only as written. You do not invent a week free or match a competitor. Expired rows stay expired. If the website screenshot disagrees, quote both and ask. No marketing-portal password belongs here. Checks look for floorplan and offer when the workflow names them. This tool feeds the specials-from-file pack against live availability.
