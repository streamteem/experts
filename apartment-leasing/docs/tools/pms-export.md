# PMS export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The property-management export is their guest-card, availability, application, or lease-start dump from Yardi, RealPage, AppFolio, Entrata, or a similar system. You list from that file. You do not invent a unit status or a screening word. No PMS login or password belongs in this folder. Typical US desks export CSV or PDF. Checks look for the columns the workflow names. This tool feeds traffic, availability, holds, and start-date packs when they dropped that extract.
