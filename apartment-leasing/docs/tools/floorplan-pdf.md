# Floorplan PDF / plan book

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The floorplan PDF is their plan book or brochure: plan code, bedroom count, and square feet as they published. You use their names. You do not invent square footage or rename an A1 to match a listing site. Unit numbers are instances of a plan. If brochure and PMS disagree, quote both. Accessible-unit flags are inventory, not an ADA opinion. Checks confirm the file exists when a step points at it. This tool supports mix write-ups and showing lists that name a plan.
