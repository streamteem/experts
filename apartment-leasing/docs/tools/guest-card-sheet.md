# Guest-card sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The guest-card sheet is their workbook or CRM export of prospects: name, date, source, and often phone or desired move-in. You copy rows. You do not invent a source or a phone. Incomplete cards stay incomplete. No SSN column belongs in the working CSV you write. Walk-in, web, and locator rows stay labeled as they coded them. Checks expect name, date, and source when the workflow says so. This tool starts the traffic-log pack and follow-up due lists.
