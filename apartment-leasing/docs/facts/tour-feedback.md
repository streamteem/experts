# Tour feedback

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tour feedback is what the agent wrote after a showing: liked the kitchen, wanted a different floor, price too high, will talk to a roommate — as their notes, not a survey you invent. Typical US CRM cards have a comments box or a lost-reason code. You copy the note. You do not put words in the prospect's mouth, and you do not code a lost reason as credit or criminal when they only said they would keep looking. Feedback is not a screening verdict. Do not record extra personal details that are not needed for the follow-up. Competitor names they mentioned belong on the competitor-rent ask only as the prospect stated, not as a market study you author. This Expert pulls comments onto the day's showing write-up when they asked for them. They coach the team. Starter until they teach which lost-reason codes *this* shop uses and what must never be written on a card.
