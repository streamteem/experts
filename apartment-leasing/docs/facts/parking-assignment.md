# Parking assignment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A parking assignment is the stall, garage, or permit number their lease or parking addendum names, plus any extra-space rent on the fee sheet. Typical US communities have open lots, numbered stalls, tandem pairs, and a short waitlist for covered spots. You copy the assignment from their parking log or lease draft. You do not invent a stall because the lot looked empty on a tour, and you do not promise a garage that the availability file shows assigned. ADA or accessible-space requests follow their accessibility-request process, not a stall you pick. Fees come from the fee file, not a guess. A roommate's second car is an extra line if their policy says so. Gate clickers and hangtags are key-control cousins; list them if the packet checklist includes them. This Expert matches parking addenda to the log. They assign spaces. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers stalls and what is first-come versus reserved.
