# Lease start / move-in date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lease start is the commence date on their draft or signed lease, which may or may not be the same calendar day as keys and make-ready complete. Typical US desks target a Saturday or first-of-month start but will write mid-month when the unit is ready. You copy the date from the lease file or PMS. You do not invent a start to fill a vacancy week, and you do not promise keys on a date the make-ready sheet still shows not ready. If notice from the current resident and the new start overlap, flag the overlap from their dates; they resolve. Prorate questions are a separate ask from their formula file. Transfers and early starts need their written exception. A desired move-in on a guest card is not a lease start. This Expert lists starts against make-ready. They pick the date with the applicant. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates commence versus key pickup.
