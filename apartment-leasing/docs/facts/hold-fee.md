# Hold / reservation fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A hold or reservation fee is money their policy files as a temporary lock on a named unit for a named applicant, often for a stated number of days, not a security deposit and not an approval by itself. Typical US desks write whether the hold applies to deposit or first month if they lease, and what happens if the applicant cancels or fails screening. You quote those sentences from their form. You do not invent a hold dollar or a hold length. You do not place a hold in the PMS. A hold without a complete application is only allowed if their file says so. You do not promise the unit is theirs. If screening later comes back with their deny code, the hold outcome follows their written refund rule — you still do not decide approve or deny. Do not take or refund the money. This Expert lists holds from their export. They accept the fee and they release units. Starter until they teach *this* shop's hold form and calendar.
