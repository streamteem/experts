# Unit hold

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A unit hold is their PMS or clipboard status that a named unit is reserved for a named applicant for a stated window, often tied to a hold fee. Typical US desks use pending-lease or reserved codes so the availability sheet does not double-book. You list holder name, unit, start of hold, and expiration as written. You do not place or extend a hold. You do not promise the unit to a second person while the hold is live. If the hold expired on their calendar, say expired; they release it. A waitlist row is not a hold. A verbal we like you is not a hold. Make-ready can continue under a hold; ready versus not ready still comes from the make-ready sheet. This Expert shows holds next to open stock. They release or convert to a lease. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates holds and who may override.
