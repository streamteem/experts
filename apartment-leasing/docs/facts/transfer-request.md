# Transfer request

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A transfer request is a current household asking to move to another unit in the same community, logged on their transfer form or PMS ticket. Typical US desks treat transfers as a new lease or an addendum with a transfer fee their file names. You list the request, current unit, wanted floorplan, and desired date from their form. You do not invent a fee. You do not put the household at the front of the waitlist unless their policy says transfers win. You do not approve the transfer. Availability still comes from the availability sheet; a transfer does not create a unit. Screening on a transfer, if they re-screen, is their process and still not your verdict. Make-ready on the destination unit still must be ready. This Expert lists open transfer asks against stock. They approve and they date the move. Starter until they teach *this* shop's transfer fee, notice, and priority rule.
