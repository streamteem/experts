# Emergency contact

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An emergency contact is the name and phone the applicant wrote on their form for someone to call in a true emergency, not a guarantor and not a roommate by default. Typical US applications have two lines; some communities require at least one. You copy the fields onto a completeness check: present or missing. You do not call the contact to gossip about screening. You do not substitute a social-media profile. If the contact is also the guarantor, still keep both roles labeled as their forms do. Do not add medical notes next to the contact. After move-in, the lease file's emergency contact is what they update; a guest-card phone is not enough. This Expert flags a blank required line. They collect a better number. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop requires one contact or two and who may use the number.
