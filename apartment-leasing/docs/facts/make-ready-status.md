# Make-ready status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Make-ready status is the unit-turn code on their make-ready sheet: not started, in progress, punch, ready, or down. Typical US communities track paint, clean, carpet, and vendor dates on a workbook or PMS turn board. You copy that status. You do not mark a unit ready because a tour looked fine, and you do not hide a delay to keep a lease start. Date ready is their field. A lease start before ready is a flag for them, not a date you silently slide. Work orders and vendors belong to the property-manager desk; you only read the status they exported. Do not invent a turn duration from a national average. Photos they stored can support a write-up but do not replace the status code. This Expert matches make-ready to move-in dates. They finish the turn. Starter until they teach *this* shop's ready codes and which file is live.
