# Application fee policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An application or screening fee is the dollar and refund rule on their posted policy or application form, not a fee you invent and not a payment you take. Typical US communities print a per-adult fee, whether it is refundable, and when it is collected. You quote the amount only from that file or the PMS fee table they exported. You do not match a neighbor's fee, and you do not waive a fee in a pack. If the policy is silent or two versions disagree, ask. Holding a card or moving money is out of scope; they collect. A missing fee on a completeness list is a missing step they chase, not a balance you invoice. Application fees and hold fees are different lines if their file splits them. Do not store card numbers. This Expert lists whether the fee shows as paid on their export. They take payment. Starter until they teach *this* shop's fee amount, refund rule, and who can waive.
