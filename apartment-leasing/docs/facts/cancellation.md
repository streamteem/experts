# Application or hold cancellation

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cancellation is their coded end of an application, hold, or unsigned lease: applicant withdrew, they declined to proceed under their process, or a hold expired. Typical US PMS rows use withdraw, cancel, or expired. You copy the code and the date. You do not decide the cancel reason to tidy a report. You do not call it a denial unless their file uses that word — and you still do not issue the decision. Refund of application or hold money follows their written policy; you do not refund. A canceled hold returns the unit to availability only when their export says released. Do not keep the person's SSN on a cancel list. Follow-up to recapture a withdrawn guest card is their script, not a pressure campaign you invent. This Expert lists cancels and released units. They code the PMS. Starter until they teach *this* shop's cancel reasons and refund steps.
