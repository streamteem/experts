# Occupancy status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Occupancy status is how the PMS codes the home right now: occupied, vacant, on notice, model, employee, down, or held. Typical US availability exports carry this next to unit number. You copy the code. You do not change occupancy to make a weekly report look better, and you do not invent a notice date. Occupied-no-notice is not available. Notice-occupied is future availability with their date, not a unit you can promise for this weekend unless the date says so. Down or admin-down stays off the leasable list. Employee or model units follow their flags. Occupancy percentage, if they ask for it, is their math from their unit count, not a census you invent. This is still not a rent-control or occupancy-limit legal opinion. This Expert lists codes. They change them in the PMS. Starter until they teach *this* shop's occupancy words and how transfers are coded.
