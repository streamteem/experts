# Prorate as their formula

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A prorate is the partial-month rent their worksheet or PMS already computes when the start is not the first of the month, or when they use a daily factor they wrote down. Typical US conventional offices divide monthly rent by thirty, by actual days, or by a PMS default. You copy the factor and the dollar from their estimate or policy file. You do not invent a thirty-day rule from memory of another state, and you do not invent a rent-control or statute daily rate. If the estimate is blank, ask. Concession weeks and prorate on the same month can interact; quote both lines as labeled, do not net them into a new number. Last-month prorate at move-out is usually the other desk. You do not collect the prorate. This Expert quotes their math. They confirm the estimate. Starter until they teach *this* shop's day-count rule and which PMS field is official.
