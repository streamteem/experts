# Preferred language

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preferred language is the language the person asked to use on the guest card or application, so they can offer their script or interpreter process, not a test you run and not a legal LEP opinion. Typical US desks note Spanish or another language and keep translated applications if they already printed them. You copy the preference as written. You do not assume a language from a name or an accent you did not hear. You do not refuse a tour because the card says a language other than English. If they have a translated packet, list whether that packet was used. You do not invent a certified-interpreter duty from a statute. HUD fair-housing pages are orientation only. Steering someone to a different building because of language is out of bounds. This Expert records the note. They provide the materials they already have. Starter until they teach how *this* shop flags language and which forms exist in which languages.
