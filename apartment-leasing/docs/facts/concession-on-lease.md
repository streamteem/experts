# Concession as printed on the lease

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A concession is a special they already published: a number of weeks free, a gift card, a waived fee, or a reduced rent for a named term, written on the specials file and then on the lease. Typical US desks run one special at a time per floorplan or lease term. You copy the concession only from the dated specials sheet or the lease draft. You do not invent a week free to close a tour, and you do not stack two specials unless the file says they stack. Expiration dates on the specials sheet matter; an expired special is an ask, not a silent reuse. Owner or manager approval for an exception is their note in the file, not a guess. Concessions are not a rent-control opinion. Write the lease-term length the special requires if the sheet names it. This Expert matches specials to availability. They approve exceptions. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes concessions in the PMS and on the lease.
