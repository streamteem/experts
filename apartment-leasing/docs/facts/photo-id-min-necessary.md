# Photo ID, minimum necessary

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Photo-ID minimum necessary means the leasing pack records that a government photo ID was seen or uploaded, without copying extra identity data into docs. Typical US offices look at a license or passport to match the application name and keep a scan in the PMS, not a second spreadsheet of ID numbers. You write present or missing. You do not type the license number, SSN, or card barcodes into a CSV. You do not store a color photocopy in this folder if a present flag is enough for the completeness list. Name mismatch between ID and application is a question for them, not a fraud verdict you announce. Do not keep passwords to the ID-upload portal here. Guests on a tour do not owe an ID scan unless their sign-in file says so. This Expert keeps lists thin. They store official images in their system. Starter until they teach what *this* shop must retain and what must never land in docs.
