# Incoming notice to vacate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Incoming notice is a current resident's written or PMS-logged intent to vacate, which puts a date on future availability. Typical US conventional leases ask for a notice period they printed; you do not invent that period from another community. You list unit, notice date, and expected vacant date as their file shows. You do not treat a verbal rumor as notice. You do not start showing an occupied home unless their showing-occupied rule and the resident's file allow it. Early move-out, lease-break, or buy-out dollars are their policy file, not a statute you quote. The property-manager desk may own the notice form; this desk only uses the date for the showing and availability list. You do not serve a notice on anyone. This Expert flags units coming vacant. They confirm the notice is valid under their process. Starter until they teach where *this* shop stores notices and when tours of on-notice homes begin.
