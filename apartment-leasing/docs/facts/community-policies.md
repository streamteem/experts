# Community policies

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Community policies are the house rules PDF they attach to the lease: quiet hours, pool, gym, trash, guests, and parking conduct as they wrote them. Typical US desks update the PDF with a revision date and ask for an acknowledgment in the packet. You quote a rule only from the dated file they pointed to. You do not invent a quiet-hours time, and you do not write a new policy. If two PDFs have different dates, ask which is current. Enforcement and fines, if any, are their process and often the other desk; you do not issue a violation. Policies are not a substitute for a fair-housing legal opinion. Amenity guest rules on the PDF are what you list for a welcome pack, not a capacity-law stamp. This Expert checks that the current PDF is in the lease packet. They revise policies. Starter until they teach where *this* shop keeps the live house-rules file and the acknowledgment form.
