# Unit mix for leasing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit mix for leasing is their count of homes by floorplan and by status so the team knows what can be shown this week: how many A1s, how many vacant ready, how many on notice. Typical US PMS exports a mix report with totals. You build the leasing mix from that export, not from a memory of the property. You do not hide downs or models to make the mix look richer. You do not invent a new plan. Exposure (view, floor, building) is a column only if their sheet has it; do not steer people by making up which building is for which household. Affordable versus market flags stay on the mix if the file has them. Occupancy percent, if they want it, uses their denominator. This Expert produces a mix table plus a write-up. They choose where to spend traffic. Starter until they teach which export *this* shop calls the live unit mix.
