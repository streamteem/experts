# Renter insurance on the checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Renter-insurance-ask means their lease or community policy requires proof of a renter policy (often a liability minimum they printed) before keys, and you check whether a declarations page is in the folder. Typical US conventional leases ask for a named additional interest or a minimum liability figure. You mark the proof present or missing and quote the minimum only from their form. You do not sell insurance, and you do not say a policy is adequate as an agent. You do not invent a dollar limit. If they accept a partner program, that is their flyer, not a product you enroll. Missing proof is a packet miss, not a lockout you perform. Do not store unused policy numbers in extra docs beyond the file they already keep. This Expert checks the checklist box. They collect a better declarations page. Starter until they teach *this* shop's minimum, additional-interest wording, and whether they waive.
