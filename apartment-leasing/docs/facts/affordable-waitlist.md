# Affordable / program waitlist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An affordable or program waitlist is the ordered file their compliance process uses for income-restricted homes, often with opening dates, preferences they printed, and contact rules. It is not the conventional market waitlist. Typical US LIHTC or project-based desks keep a tightly controlled workbook or software list. You list rows in their existing order. You do not reorder, and you do not add a name without their process. You do not decide preference points as a reading of a statute. Market leads do not jump this list. When a restricted unit is ready, they pull the next qualified household under their written procedure. You do not announce that someone is income-qualified. Keep program files labeled so they never mix with guest-card traffic. This Expert produces the list pack they asked for. They notify applicants. Starter until they teach how *this* shop opens, purges, and documents the affordable list.
