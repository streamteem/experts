# Showing no-show

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A no-show is a booked showing where the person did not arrive within their grace window, coded on the calendar as no-show, cancel, or late-cancel if they split those. Typical US desks wait ten or fifteen minutes because a note on the calendar says so; if there is no note, ask, do not invent the window. You mark the row from their calendar, not from a guess that they will never come. You do not charge a no-show fee unless their written policy names one, and you still do not take money. Reschedule is a new showing row. Repeat no-shows stay on the card as they coded them; you do not ban a household as a legal act. After-hours lockbox no-shows are still no-shows if they logged the appointment. This Expert lists missed tours for the day. They decide the next follow-up. Starter until they teach *this* shop's grace minutes and cancel-versus-no-show codes.
