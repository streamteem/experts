# Floorplan file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A floorplan file is their named plan (A1, B2, townhome 3) with the square-foot figure and bedroom-bath count they publish, plus a PDF or brochure they store. Typical US communities keep a plan book and a PMS unit-type table. You use their names and their square feet. You do not invent a square-foot number from a tape you did not run, and you do not rename plans to match a listing site. Unit 214 is an instance of a plan; availability is per unit, marketing is often per plan. If the brochure and the PMS disagree on square feet, quote both and ask. Virtual-tour links belong with the plan only if they filed them that way. Accessible or designated units are inventory flags, not a legal ADA opinion. This Expert lists plans next to vacant units. They update brochures. Starter until they teach *this* shop's plan codes and which PDF is the public one.
