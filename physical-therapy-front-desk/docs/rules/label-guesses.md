# Label starter guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Any column meaning, office rule, fee, duration, or remaining number not in their files is a guess. Say so. Teach replaces it. Do not present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. If two files disagree, quote both and ask. Holiday closures, cancel fees, and visit defaults are common guesses — label them until they confirm. Do not silently pick the prettier remaining number when two dated files disagree.
