# Visits used

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Visits used is the count their visit-count export, EHR tally, or dated auth-tracker column already shows as completed or billed against the auth. Typical US PT desks keep used, authorized, and remaining on one tracker. You copy used as the file states. You do not increment used because you remember the patient came yesterday. You do not decrement used to make remaining look healthier. You do not treat. If two files disagree — tracker versus EHR export — quote both and ask which is live. A no-show they coded may or may not count as used; keep their column, do not invent the rule. Do not turn used visits into a diagnosis of progress. Prefer chart number. If used is blank, say blank. Starter / guess until they teach which report *this* clinic treats as the used count. A used count that includes or excludes the visit still in the gym today follows their posted rule, not a habit from another clinic.
