# Late arrival as their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late arrival on a typical US PT book is a timestamp or a late flag they recorded, plus whatever shorten-or-reschedule rule their file already wrote. Copy the flag. You do not decide the patient lost the slot as the therapist. You do not treat a shorter visit. You do not diagnose why they were late. You do not invent a fifteen-minute cutoff. If their rule says late past a number of minutes becomes a reschedule, quote that number from the file and flag the row — they decide. Do not hide a late flag to keep utilization pretty. Prefer chart number. Emergency wording still flags staff now. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic codes late versus no-show. A late flag on an eval slot still does not let you shorten the eval type unless their template says so.
