# Inbound referral paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An inbound referral or script is paperwork that lets this PT clinic see a patient sent from a physician or another office: referring name, referred-to discipline as written, patient label they allow, date of the referral, body region or reason wording as the form states, and whether required pages are in the folder. Typical US PT desks track these on a referral log or EHR workqueue. Completeness only. You do not decide the referral is medically necessary. You do not diagnose from the reason line. You do not treat. If the referral is expired on their written date rule, say the dates and ask — do not invent a thirty-day or ninety-day rule from another payer. Missing referring NPI or missing patient match: ask which chart. Do not copy extra clinical notes from the referring packet into docs/ when a present-or-missing flag will do. Direct-access states still follow *their* process file if they require a referral for some plans. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic logs inbound referrals. Direct-access first visits still follow their process if some plans on their list still require a script.
