# Recert date as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A recertification due date is a date they already stored on a tracker or EHR: when the next signed plan or progress packet is due. You copy that date. You do not invent the interval. You do not write the recert note. You do not treat. You do not diagnose. List upcoming recert dates from their file when they asked, with chart label and therapist if present. If the date is blank, say blank. A recert due is not remaining visits. If their tracker and the POC end date disagree, quote both. Do not copy extra clinical goals into the recert list. Starter / guess until they teach which recert column *this* clinic treats as live. A progress-note due date on the same tracker is a different column; do not mix it with recert due. Prefer chart or MRN on the work row. They correct the live file; you list what the dated export already shows.
