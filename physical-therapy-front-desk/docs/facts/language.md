# Language note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preferred language on a typical US PT registration file is a scheduling and interpreter-flag fact: the language they recorded, and whether their interpreter process is already marked as needed. Copy the language as stored. Do not guess language from a surname. Do not write that the patient “speaks enough English.” HHS limited-English-proficiency pages are orientation, not a legal opinion you issue. You do not hire an interpreter as this product. You flag the row for staff when their sheet says interpreter needed and the day’s booking does not yet show one. Written HEP in another language is present or missing as files they keep; you do not translate clinical content or write a new HEP. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic records language and interpreter need. A language on an old registration row that disagrees with today's interpreter flag is a quote-both ask.
