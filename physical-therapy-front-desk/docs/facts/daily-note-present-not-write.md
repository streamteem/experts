# Daily note present, not written by you

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A daily treatment note in a typical US PT clinic is the therapist’s note for that visit. Front-desk work is whether the note is marked complete in their export or whether a required note file is present for a completed slot. You do not write the daily note. You do not fill SOAP. You do not treat. You do not diagnose. You do not invent units from the note. If a completed visit on the day list has no note-complete flag and their process requires one before close, flag it for staff. Do not copy note text into docs/. Uncompleted notes are a completeness list, not care you finish. Starter / guess until they teach which note-status column *this* clinic exports. Addendum or late-entry flags they stored stay as stored; you do not write the addendum. Prefer chart or MRN on the work row. They correct the live file; you list what the dated export already shows.
