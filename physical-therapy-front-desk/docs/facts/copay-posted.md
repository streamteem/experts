# Copay as their posted amount

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copay collected at a typical US PT window is the amount they posted from their fee-posted sheet, EHR collection screen, or the exact copay line on a dated eligibility screenshot they pointed to. You copy that posted figure. You do not invent a PT-versus-specialist copay from a public brochure. You do not average last week’s collections. If the screenshot and the posted sheet disagree, quote both and ask which they collect today. A copay is not remaining visits and not deductible math you invent. You do not take a card into this folder. Day-close tender later lists what they recorded as collected, not what you think should have been collected. If they have no posted copay file and no screenshot line, ask. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic posts copays by plan and visit type. Sliding or hardship copays exist only if their sliding or hardship sheet already names this visit type and this account.
