# Referral expiration as their date rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Referral expiration is a date math they already wrote: the referral date plus their window, or a printed expiration on the script. Typical US PT desks flag an expired script before an eval or before a new series. You copy the dates and their rule file. You do not invent a thirty-day or one-year rule from another payer. You do not treat. You do not diagnose. If the referral date is missing, ask. If their rule file is missing, say the dates you have and ask the window. An expired referral is a completeness question, not a clinical end. Do not silently book past the date. Prefer chart number. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic expires inbound referrals. A new script they filed later replaces the old expiration only when they say which letter is live. Prefer chart or MRN on the work row. They correct the live file; you list what the dated export already shows.
