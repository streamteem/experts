# Reauthorization paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reauthorization is a new or extended payer packet: updated progress-note present-or-missing, updated POC present-or-missing, and the new auth letter when it arrives. Typical US PT desks start a reauth workqueue when remaining visits on their tracker hit a number *they* already wrote, or when the auth end date approaches. You list completeness. You do not write the clinical justification. You do not invent remaining visits to delay the packet. You do not treat. You do not pick CPT for the extension. Copy a new authorized visit count only from the new dated letter. If the old letter expired and no new letter is in the folder, say so. Do not copy extra note text into docs/. Starter / guess until they teach when *this* clinic opens a reauth row. A pending reauth is not remaining visits you may treat as already approved.
