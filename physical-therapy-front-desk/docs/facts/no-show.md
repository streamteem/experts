# No-show as their codes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No-show and broken-appointment rows on a typical US PT book are status codes they already set: no-show, late cancel, or advance cancel as their sheet splits them. Copy their status words and timestamps. Do not infer a no-show from a blank confirmation. Do not invent a no-show fee, a three-strikes rule, or a discharge. Fees, if any, come from their written cancel-policy file. Do not refuse future booking as your decision. Do not diagnose why the patient missed. A no-show may or may not count against visits-used; keep their column. One clinic date per no-show list unless they asked for a range and the file has dates. Prefer chart number. They decide who is offered a wait-list fill. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic codes broken appointments. Confirmation-blank rows stay confirmation-blank; you do not recode them as no-show to tidy the list.
