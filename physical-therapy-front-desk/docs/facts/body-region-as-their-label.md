# Body region as their label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Body region on a typical US PT book is a scheduling label they already stored: knee, shoulder, lumbar, or a custom list. It helps the gym place the patient and sometimes matches a HEP folder name. Copy the label. You do not diagnose. You do not treat. You do not change “knee” to a meniscus code. You do not pick a HEP because the region matches a library folder unless the therapist already attached that file. If region is blank, say blank. Two regions on one referral stay as their form listed them; you do not invent a primary. Prefer chart number on lists that also show region. Starter / guess until they teach *this* clinic’s region list and whether it may appear on a window print. Bilateral versus left-or-right stays as their label; you do not pick a side to tidy the gym list.
