# Unit count as their export, not you

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Timed-code unit counts on a typical US PT claim or charge export are numbers the therapist or biller already posted. You may list units as the export shows. You do not apply an eight-minute rule. You do not convert slot minutes into units. You do not add a unit to make a claim look complete. You do not treat. If units are blank on a completed visit, say blank and ask — do not invent one unit because the slot was fifteen minutes. CMS therapy pages are orientation, not a calculator you run as a billing opinion. Prefer chart and date of service. Do not copy extra note minutes into docs/ to recompute units. Starter / guess until they teach which charge report *this* clinic drops. Group minutes versus individual units stay as their export split them; you do not reclass the visit.
