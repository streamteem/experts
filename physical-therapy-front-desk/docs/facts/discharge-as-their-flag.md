# Discharge as their flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Discharge on a typical US PT book is a status they already set: discharged, completed, self-discharged, or transferred as their codes read. Copy the flag and the date as stored. You do not discharge the patient as the therapist. You do not decide they are better. You do not treat. You do not invent a discharge because remaining visits hit zero. Remaining visits hitting zero is a file fact, not a clinical end. If the book still has future slots after a discharge flag, quote both and ask. Do not copy the discharge summary into docs/. Prefer chart number. A cancel is not a discharge unless they coded it that way. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic codes discharge versus last-visit-complete. Goals-met wording on a note you must not copy is still not a discharge flag you may set.
