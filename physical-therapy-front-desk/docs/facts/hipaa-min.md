# HIPAA minimum necessary on work files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Work files this Expert writes — day lists, remaining-visit CSVs, referral indexes, HEP print lists, write-ups — should carry the least PHI the task needs. Typical US PT prints still leak extra identifiers; your job is not to copy the whole chart into docs/. Prefer chart or MRN, time, therapist, appointment type, and a yes-or-no completeness or remaining-from-file flag. Drop extra addresses, full dates of birth stacked with names, and clinical narrative when a status row will do. HHS minimum-necessary guidance is orientation. If a scan shows more than you need, do not transcribe the rest. Ask what *this* clinic allows on a list that may sit at the window. Minimum necessary is not a reason to hide a missing auth or a no-show. Starter / guess until they teach this clinic’s print and export rules. Outstanding-balance and remaining-visit flags can stay on the huddle row with a chart label; extra HPI cannot.
