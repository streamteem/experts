# Timed codes as their file, not you

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Timed and untimed procedure codes on a typical US PT ticket — therapeutic exercise, manual therapy, eval codes, and discipline modifiers as they already labeled — stay as their file printed them. You copy labels. You do not choose CPT. You do not add GP, GO, or CQ. You do not swap a code after a denial unless their file shows the office already changed the ticket. You do not treat by choosing a code. Appointment type is not a CPT. Missing code: ask. Do not invent a code from a body-region label. Superbills they already generated may list codes; you still do not create lines. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic names charge tickets versus claim exports. Modifier 59 or other pairing flags stay as their ticket shows; you do not add them to unbundle a denial.
