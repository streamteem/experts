# Cancel as their status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cancel on a typical US PT book is a status they recorded with a timestamp and sometimes a reason code. Copy the status as written. Same-day cancel versus advance cancel stay different if their sheet splits them. You do not invent which bucket a phone call belongs in. You do not invent a cancel fee. You do not treat the cancel as a clinical discharge. You do not diagnose. If they asked you to list cancellations for a date range, use only rows with their cancel status and a date column. A cancel that opens a slot is not a wait-list award you make. Prefer chart number. Extra clinical reason text stays out of docs/ when a status word will do. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic names cancel versus no-show. A reschedule they already coded is not a cancel unless their status word says cancel.
