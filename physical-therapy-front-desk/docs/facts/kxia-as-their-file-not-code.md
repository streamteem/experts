# KX as their file, not a code you apply

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The KX modifier on typical US Medicare therapy claims is an attestation the billing office or therapist already applied or flagged on their claim or tracker. Your job is to copy that flag as their export shows. You do not add KX to make a claim payable. You do not decide medical necessity above a threshold. You do not give a Medicare opinion. You do not treat. If the tracker says KX needed and the claim export does not show it, quote both and ask — do not apply the modifier. If they have no KX column, say so; do not invent one from a CMS page. Do not copy extra chart narrative into docs/ to “support” KX. A KX flag is still not a payment promise. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic labels KX on the auth or claim tracker. A blank KX cell is blank; you do not fill it from a remembered dollar threshold.
