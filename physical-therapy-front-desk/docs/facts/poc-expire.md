# Plan-of-care expiration as their date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Plan-of-care or certification end dates come from the dated POC, the physician-cert page, or their recert tracker. Typical US PT clinics recertify on a written interval. You copy the end date as filed. You do not invent a ninety-day Medicare interval as an opinion. You do not write a new plan. You do not treat. If the booked series runs past the POC end date on their file, flag and ask. Missing end date: ask. Recert-date-as-file is the companion fact for the next due date they stored. Do not copy goal text into the expiration list. Prefer chart label. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic dates POC start and end. A recert packet in flight does not extend the old POC end date until their new signed file is in the folder. Prefer chart or MRN on the work row. They correct the live file; you list what the dated export already shows.
