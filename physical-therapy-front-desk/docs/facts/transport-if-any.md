# Transport if they track it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some US PT clinics note a ride, paratransit, or facility van on the appointment row so the gym knows who may run late. If they have that column or a transport log, copy the label they stored. You do not arrange the ride as a product. You do not store extra driver phone numbers in docs/ when a yes-or-no transport flag will do. You do not treat. You do not diagnose mobility. If they have no transport field, say so — do not invent that a patient needs a van. Facility-to-clinic transport contracts are their vendor file, not a ride you book. Prefer chart number. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* clinic tracks transport and which column is live. Missed-ride notes they recorded are status text, not a reason you discharge or treat. Prefer chart or MRN on the work row. They correct the live file; you list what the dated export already shows.
