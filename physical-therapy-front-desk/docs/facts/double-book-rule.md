# Double-book only from their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Double-booking — two patients in one therapist’s same time — is allowed on this desk only when their written schedule-rule or template file says that visit type or that therapist may overlap, or when the export already shows both rows. Typical US PT gyms sometimes overlap PTA follow-ups or group minutes; many never do. You do not invent an overbook to please a wait list. You flag overlaps that are not on their rule as conflicts; you do not hide them. You do not move the second patient to another therapist to tidy the list unless they asked for draft options from their rule. Duration math still comes from their templates. Telehealth versus in-gym double-books follow the same file. Starter / guess until they teach *this* clinic’s overbook rule. Group or concurrent slots they already templated are not a silent double-book you invent on a one-to-one therapist row.
