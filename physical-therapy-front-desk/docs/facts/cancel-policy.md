# Cancel policy as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cancel and no-show fees exist only on their written policy file or posted sheet. Typical US PT clinics write a same-day window and a fee amount. You copy that file when they asked you to list who might be charged — still their decision to charge. You do not invent a fee. You do not invent a twenty-four-hour rule. You do not refuse care. You do not treat. If they have no policy file, say so. Waivers of the fee are their adjustment code. Do not store card numbers to “hold” a slot. Prefer chart number on any fee-question list. Starter / guess until they teach *this* clinic’s cancel window and who may waive. Prepaid-package forfeits, if any, come only from their package sheet, not from this cancel-policy guess. Prefer chart or MRN on the work row. They correct the live file; you list what the dated export already shows.
