# Insurance verify as screenshot

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Insurance verification at this desk is the same family as eligibility: a dated screenshot or clearinghouse print they saved, plus card images if they use those as paperwork. Quote plan name, active wording, and any visit-limit or PT-benefit sentences as printed. You do not call the payer. You do not log in. You do not invent remaining visits from a verification phone note that was never saved as a dated file. A card image is not verification. If they keep a verify-complete checkbox on the tracker, copy that checkbox as they set it — do not mark verified because you saw a card. Stale or cropped images: ask. Passwords stay out. Least PHI on the quote sheet. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic marks a visit verified. A front-desk checkbox they set without a dated print is still not remaining visits you may quote.
