# Cancel wait-fill

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cancel wait-fill is matching their wait-list rows to a slot that just opened, using type, therapist, duration, and date rules already on those files. Typical US PT desks call down the list when a same-day cancel appears. You list matching candidates in their wait-list order. You do not award the slot. You do not message the patient as a product. You do not invent that someone accepted. You do not treat. You do not diagnose urgency to jump the list. Emergency wording still flags staff. If the open slot’s type does not match the wait-list request, do not stretch the match. Prefer chart number. Extra phones stay off the write-up. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic fills cancelled slots. A same-day opening that does not match type or therapist stays unmatched; they may still choose to offer it.
