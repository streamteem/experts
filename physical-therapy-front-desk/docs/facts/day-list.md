# Day list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US outpatient physical-therapy front desks print or export a day list the night before or at open so therapists know who sits when. The list is a time-and-slot file, not a treatment note: start time, the patient label they allow on a desk print, treating therapist, appointment type as booked, and sometimes a body-region label they already stored. Many clinics export CSV from WebPT, Raintree, Clinicient, Prompt, Jane, or a photographed paper book they drop in the folder. Confirm which identifiers they allow on a list that may sit at check-in. Prefer chart or MRN over stacking full legal name, date of birth, and address on every row. One clinic date per write-up unless they asked for a multi-day book and the file has a date column. Closed blocks, lunch, huddle, and documentation rows stay so the book is honest. You do not diagnose from the reason field, do not treat, and do not invent remaining visits from a remembered plan. If the export has no date or looks like last week, ask before you treat it as tomorrow. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic labels slots and patients. Do not invent a missing column as fact, and do not reassign therapists unless their written rule says to draft options.
