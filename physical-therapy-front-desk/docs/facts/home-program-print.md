# Home-program print

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Home-program print is a completeness and routing task: the named HEP PDF is present, the patient label they allow is on the print list, and they (or the therapist) hand it over. You may list who still needs a print against today’s day list if they asked. You do not coach the exercise. You do not demonstrate. You do not treat. You do not change the program. A print is not a new order you write. If the library file and the chart attachment disagree, quote both and ask which to print. Extra phones and extra clinical notes stay off the print index. Prefer chart number. They print from their own printer or portal; this is not a messaging product. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic logs HEP prints versus portal sends. A portal-sent HEP they logged as sent is a send-status row, not a print you invent as complete.
