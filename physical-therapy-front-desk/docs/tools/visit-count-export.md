# Visit-count export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

EHR or biller export of visits used, authorized, and remaining as they already calculated. Copy the numbers. Do not recompute remaining unless they asked you to copy their remaining column. Do not invent units from minutes. If the export date looks stale, ask. Prefer chart and case or auth id they use. This tool feeds remaining-from-file and visit-count-export-check packs. Starter / guess until they teach which count report *this* clinic drops. Do not mark remaining unlimited because the export used a blank cell.
