# Two quotes — ask threshold

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical practice for non-emergency capex is more than one quote above a dollar threshold this shop already uses. Ask their threshold and whether photos must sit in the bid pack. Emergency work — gas, fire, active flood, no heat in freezing weather — is a flag-now path; do not shop three bids while water is running. Copy amounts from the PDFs; do not round or pick a winner. Never invent a threshold as law, never store vendor-portal passwords, and never pay a vendor deposit from this folder.
