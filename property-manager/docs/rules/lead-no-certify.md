# Do not certify lead-safe work

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List year-built and pamphlet or firm files if present. Do not certify RRP, do not say work was lead-safe, and do not tell a handyman to scrape paint. Do not invent year built. EPA and HUD orientation pages are pointers, not a stamp you apply. If they need a certified firm, mark the firm file missing or present. Never certify the unit as habitable after a paint job, never store contractor-portal passwords, and never pay the renovator from this folder.
