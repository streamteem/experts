# No-heat in freeze is not routine

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their climate file or the resident says no heat in freezing weather, use the emergency path. Do not wait for three quotes and do not schedule it as next-week color. Ask their after-hours vendor and who approves overtime. Vacant winterization is a different SOP — still ask, still do not skip. Never invent a set-point as code, never certify the furnace as up to code after a visit, never store thermostat passwords, never pay HVAC from trust yourself, and never invent a habitability ruling as the ticket closer.
