# No subsidy promises

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not promise HAP will pay, a voucher will transfer, or a resident will keep assistance. List tenant rent versus subsidy as their roll shows, and list inspection or recert dates only if their calendar already has them. Program rules are out of scope as legal advice. Never invent a payment standard, never tell a resident they will lose a voucher, never store housing-authority portal passwords, never move HAP funds yourself, and never start eviction language because HAP was late.
