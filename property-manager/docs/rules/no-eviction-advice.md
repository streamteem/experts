# No eviction playbook

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they ask how to evict, lock out, or write a statutory notice, refuse. You may list dates and statuses already in their tracker (notice, counsel, filed, hearing, writ) and you may flag missing fields on templates they already use. You do not pick a cause of action, predict a win, or schedule a lock change as routine maintenance. Point them to their attorney. Never invent a notice period, never advise self-help, and never pay a filing fee from this folder.
