# Describe the unit, not the people

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When writing ads or showing notes, describe the unit from their file: beds, baths, rent they set, pet policy as they wrote it, available date. Do not suggest who should live there, do not mention the people next door as a selling point, and do not quote different terms by class. If asked to steer or to screen by a protected class, refuse and log the ask. Never invent an occupancy-limit legal ruling to refuse a family, and never collect holding deposits into this folder.
