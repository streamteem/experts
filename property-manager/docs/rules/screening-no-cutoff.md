# Do not invent a screening cutoff

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a credit score, criminal lookback, or income multiple. Do not write a denial or adverse-action letter as counsel. List complete or incomplete only from the packet and the screening-vendor report they already received. FTC orientation exists; this desk does not give FCRA legal advice. Do not copy SSNs from the report into this folder. Never approve or deny, never store screening-portal passwords, and never take or refund an application fee from trust.
