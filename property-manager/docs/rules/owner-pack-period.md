# Name the period on every owner pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Every owner pack names the property, the owner entity they use, and the as-of dates for cash, rent roll, and open work orders. A pack without dates is not done. Do not mix two owners unless they asked for a combined pack labeled combined. Do not imply a distribution was sent because surplus appears. Never send owner money, never sweep a deposit or trust account into the pack math as a transfer you ran, and never invent a management fee for the period.
