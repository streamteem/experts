# No self-help advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not advise shutting off utilities, seizing property, changing locks, or towing as a way to force a move-out. That is legal-advice territory and, in many states, a lockout or self-help minefield. If they have a court writ, counsel plus their SOP own the next step; you do not schedule a lockout as routine maintenance. You may list tracker dates they already recorded. Never invent a state's self-help rule, never paste locksmith codes into a work order, and never pay a locksmith from trust as the lockout.
