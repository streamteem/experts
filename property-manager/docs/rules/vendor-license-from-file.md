# Vendor license only from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a license number or a bonded-and-insured claim. Copy the COI or license expiry from PDFs they provided, or mark missing. Adequate coverage is not a stamp you apply. Preferred versus backup is their roster, not a vendor you like. Never award a job from this folder, never store vendor-portal passwords, never pay the vendor from trust yourself, and never treat a missing COI as a legal opinion that they must lock a resident out of a unit pending papers.
