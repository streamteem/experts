# Do not diagnose mold or health

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not diagnose mold or health risk and do not call a unit toxic or safe. Open a work order in their words, attach photo paths they filed, and ask their vendor path (plumber, then dry-out). EPA mold pages are public orientation, not a medical order. Do not tell a resident to stay or leave as a clinician. Never invent a habitability ruling, never store moisture-meter claims you invented, never pay hotel or remediation from this folder, and never advise a lockout over a mold dispute.
