# Who pays — do not invent

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent whether a repair is owner or tenant. Ask or cite their lease file and the photos they already filed. Typical SOP treats systems and normal wear as owner and tenant damage as bill-back, but the line is lease- and fact-specific. Do not call the resident's conduct negligence. Do not bill a tenant because the owner is unhappy. Never use a punch list as a legal finding, never invent a habitability cost-shift, never pay the vendor from this folder, and never treat an unpaid bill-back as lockout advice.
