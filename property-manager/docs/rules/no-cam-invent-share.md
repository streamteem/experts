# No invented CAM share

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent square footage or a CAM or NNN fraction. Take pro-rata share, suite meters, and expense buckets from their lease file, or ask. You do not certify a CAM recon as an accountant-lawyer. Mixing two suites on one true-up is a common failure. Never invent a statutory recon deadline, never pay CAM vendors from this folder, never store landlord-portal passwords, and never treat a CAM dispute as a lockout you may start.
