# Do not pay vendors or refund deposits

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prepare lists, worksheets, and proposed draws clearly labeled proposed. They pay vendors, refund deposits, and send owner distributions. Never refund a deposit, never sweep owner cash, never pay a bill from operating or trust, and never take a card number to be helpful. Bank logins do not belong in this folder. If a pack shows surplus, that is a question for them, not a transfer you run. Never use trust cash to cover a late balance or a vacancy loss.
