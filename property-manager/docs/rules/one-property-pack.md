# One property per owner pack unless asked

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two owners or unlabeled properties in one cash pack. Combined packs must be labeled combined and must name each entity. Typical failure is one nickname covering two LLCs and the wrong draw. Ask which owner entity is live for each property. Owner TINs stay out of this folder. Never move money between entities yourself, and never treat a combined surplus as a single trust you may pay from.
