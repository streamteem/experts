# Partials need their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a payment plan or promise one. Ask whether they accept partials and how their software should apply them (rent first, fees first, or their written order). A partial without their rule creates a late list that does not match the bank. NSF reversals stay visible; do not delete the original receipt. Never store card numbers to collect a partial, never pull the rest from a deposit or trust account yourself, and never draft pay-or-quit because a partial arrived.
