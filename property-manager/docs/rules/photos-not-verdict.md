# Photos are evidence files, not a verdict

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List photo paths and what the caption in their file says. Do not rule wear versus damage, do not edit photos to help a deposit claim, and do not store ID photos or extra children-focused images beyond their condition report. Naming should follow their unit-date-WO pattern. A photo is evidence they already filed, not a habitability or legal disposition. Never invent a finding from an image, and never use a photo as lockout or eviction advice.
