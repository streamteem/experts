# Do not merge WOs across units

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not silently merge two residents' tickets into one row. A building-wide issue can be a common-area work order plus unit work orders if they work that way — ask. Merged tickets steal photos, bill-backs, and owner-pack lines from the wrong door. Each row needs their unit or common-area label. Never close a merged ticket from one vendor text, never invent a habitability finding for the whole building from one unit, and never pay one vendor invoice against the wrong unit's deposit.
