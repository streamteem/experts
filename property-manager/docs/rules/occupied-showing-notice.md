# Occupied showings need their notice process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not put an occupied unit on a public open-house list unless their file says so. Ask the notice log: date, method, unit, window. Hours of notice vary by state and lease; do not invent twenty-four hours as law. Emergency entry is their SOP, not a showing. Never paste lockbox codes onto the showing sheet, never promise an application will be approved at the door, never steer who may tour, and never treat a refused showing as a lockout or eviction step.
