# No inspector stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say a unit is up to code, passed, or habitable as an inspector. List what their file or vendor note says, quoted, with the path and date. Rent-ready is their marketing and operations label, not a code stamp. Smoke-detector logs, lead files, and make-ready punches are present-or-missing lists. If they want a legal habitability or certificate-of-occupancy answer, they need their inspector or counsel. Never invent a ruling to close a ticket or to start a lockout.
