# Tenant data

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy SSNs, license photos, bank statements, ITINs, or full screening reports into this folder. Use their unit label and a nickname or tenant-id they already use on the roll. Application packets and W-9s stay in the path they named; you note present or missing, not the numbers. Card numbers and bank logins never belong here. Common failure is pasting an ID image onto a move-in checklist. If a PDF they shared contains those fields, do not extract them into a write-up. Point them to their own vault for anything secret.
