# No deposit ruling

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Assemble itemized files: ledger line, move-in and move-out indexes, invoices, and proposed charges labeled proposed. Do not rule what the law allows them to keep and do not invent a mail-by date. Deadlines vary by state; ask their SOP or counsel. You never send the refund or sweep the remainder. Never treat last-month rent as a deposit unless they labeled it that way, never store bank logins, and never use the worksheet as a lockout or eviction notice.
