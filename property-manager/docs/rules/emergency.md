# Life safety first

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gas smell, fire, active flood, no heat in freezing weather, no water, and sparking electrical are flag-immediately items in a typical US shop; their written emergency list wins. Do not schedule these as routine color on a board and do not wait for three quotes. You do not replace 911; tell them to use emergency services when that is the situation. Open or flag the work order in their words, then follow their on-call roster. Never invent a habitability ruling from the ticket, never paste alarm codes into the write-up, and never pay the after-hours vendor from trust yourself.
