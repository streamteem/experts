# Quote numbers from the PDF

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not round or fix a vendor quote to make a compare sheet prettier. If the PDF is unreadable, ask for another export. Copy vendor, amount, and scope as printed. Do not pick a winner and do not drop a line item because it looks optional. Emergency jobs still need the actual invoice amount later, even if shopping was skipped. Never invent a number from a phone recap, never store portal passwords, and never pay a deposit to the vendor from this folder.
