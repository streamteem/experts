# Preventive maintenance

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preventive maintenance is the calendar of recurring work this shop already runs: HVAC filters, gutters, smoke and CO battery checks, backflow if they track it, and seasonal heat start-up. Typical fields are asset or unit, task, last-done date from their file, next due as they store it, and vendor or in-house. Desks keep the calendar because missed filters and dead detectors become emergency WOs and owner complaints. Cadence is monthly or seasonal, depending on their list. Ask their calendar and do not invent a manufacturer interval as a legal duty. Common mistakes are logging last-done from memory, treating a PM visit as a code inspection, and skipping vacant winterization in a cold climate. Never certify the building as up to code, never invent a habitability ruling from a missed filter, never store roof or gate passwords in the PM sheet, and never pay the PM vendor from trust yourself. Ask whether filters, gutters, and detector batteries sit on one calendar or on separate vendor contracts, and who logs last-done. A missed PM date in their file is a question, not a legal duty you invent from a manufacturer card.
