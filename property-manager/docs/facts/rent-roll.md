# Rent roll

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A rent roll is the as-of snapshot of every unit the office manages: who is charged, what is charged, what deposit is held, and whether the door is occupied, on notice, or vacant. Typical columns are property, unit, occupant label, market or asking rent if they store it, charged rent, other recurring charges, deposits held, lease start and end, and status. Software columns differ across AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, Propertyware, and workbooks; their header wins. Desks keep the roll because owner packs, late lists, renewal pipelines, and vacancy counts all start from the same as-of file. Cadence is often month-end plus an ad-hoc pull before an owner call. Ask the as-of date, which product produced the export, and whether concessions sit in charged rent or as a credit. Common mistakes are changing charged rent to what the market should be, leaving a past tenant on an occupied row to hide vacancy, and mixing two properties because the owner uses one nickname. Never invent a state's rent-control cap, never apply a late fee they did not configure, never treat the roll as a legal lease, and never move owner cash or deposit funds because the roll shows a surplus.
