# Vendor and property insurance files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor and property insurance files are the COIs and policy contacts this shop already collects: vendor liability and workers-comp expirations, additional-insured asks they use, and the property policy contact. Typical tracker fields are vendor or policy, type, expiry from the PDF they provided, and missing marked missing. Desks keep the tracker because an expired COI discovered after a slip-and-fall is an owner problem. Cadence is at vendor onboarding and a periodic expiry sweep. Ask which limits they require; do not invent limits. Common mistakes are saying coverage is adequate, inventing additional-insured language, and treating a missing COI as a legal opinion that work cannot proceed. Never claim coverage is enough, never store insurer-portal passwords, never pay a premium from this folder, never certify the building as up to code because a policy exists, and never advise a lockout related to a claim. Ask which policy types they require on the roster and how many days before expiry they chase a new PDF. Copy expiry from the file they provided. Do not invent limits or say coverage is adequate. A missing COI is marked missing.
