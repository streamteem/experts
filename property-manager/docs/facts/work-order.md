# Work order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A work order is the office ticket that records a maintenance request from open through done, so later owner packs, bill-backs, and vendor pay talks have a dated source. Typical US SMB fields are date opened, who reported (resident, owner, staff, inspection), unit or common-area label, the issue in their words, priority they use, assigned vendor or in-house tech, appointment window, status words (open, scheduled, parts, complete), and whether they marked the cost as owner or tenant bill-back. Photos and vendor invoices often live on the same ticket. Desks keep the WO because a chat recap will not survive a deposit dispute or an owner question six weeks later. Cadence is daily for open tickets and weekly for aging. Shop software and status vocabulary differ; ask their words and which export is live. Common mistakes are closing a ticket because a vendor texted done, merging two residents' issues into one row, and coding bill-back without their lease or photo file. Never invent a habitability ruling from a WO description, never dispatch as a 911 replacement, never store vendor portal passwords in this folder, and never pay the vendor from a trust or operating account yourself.
