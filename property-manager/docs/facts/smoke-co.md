# Smoke and CO devices

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Smoke and carbon-monoxide devices are the detectors this shop checks at make-ready and on any annual or PM list they keep. Typical fields are unit, device location if they log it, present yes or no, and date replaced if they record it. Local rules vary. Desks keep the log because a resident report of no working alarm is an urgent safety ticket, and owners ask whether devices were checked at turnover. Cadence is make-ready, PM visits, and any resident report. Ask their SOP and who may mark a device replaced. Common mistakes are certifying code compliance, inventing a replacement interval as law, and treating a missing detector as a routine color. If a resident reports no working smoke alarm, flag as urgent per their emergency rule. Never certify the unit as up to code or habitable, never store lockbox codes next to the device row, never advise a lockout over a detector dispute, and never pay the vendor from trust yourself.
