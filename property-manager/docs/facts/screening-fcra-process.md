# Screening as process, not a verdict

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Screening as process is the office checklist that a screening-vendor report was requested and received for an applicant this shop already authorized, not a verdict on whether they may live there. FTC orientation is that many tenant-screening reports are consumer reports and that adverse-action steps exist; this desk does not give FCRA legal advice. Typical fields are applicant label they use, report received or missing, date, and who will decide. Desks keep the checklist so the file shows the same steps for each packet. Cadence is per application. Ask their vendor name only as a label and where the report lives (not a login). Common mistakes are inventing a score cutoff, writing an adverse-action letter as counsel, and pasting full reports with SSNs into this folder. Never approve or deny, never give a fair-housing opinion, never store screening-portal passwords, and never take or refund an application fee from trust yourself. Ask where the vendor report lives and who in this shop reads it. Same steps for each authorized packet is the process point. Do not invent a cutoff, do not write adverse-action text as counsel, and do not paste SSNs into a write-up.
