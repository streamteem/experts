# Amenities and common areas

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Amenities and common areas are the pool, laundry, gym, and shared spaces that generate work orders and house-rule questions. Hours and rules are their community rules, not a law you write. Typical files are common-area WOs, vendor contracts, and the rules PDF they already use. Desks keep common-area tickets separate from in-unit tickets so owner packs show building cost versus unit bill-back. Cadence is as WOs open, plus seasonal opening of a pool if they have one. Ask who closes a pool and who owns the rule file. Common mistakes are writing a new house rule as law, closing a pool as a health official, and merging a building-wide issue silently into one resident's WO. Never certify the amenity as up to code, never invent a habitability ruling from a closed amenity, never store camera or gate passwords, never pay the pool vendor from this folder, and never advise a lockout over a house-rule fight. Ask who owns the house-rule PDF and who may close a pool or laundry room. Common-area tickets stay off a single resident's work order unless they said to split that way. Do not write a new rule as law or close a pool as a health official.
