# Deposit disposition as a file pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Deposit disposition as a file pack is the worksheet of proposed charges tied to invoices and photos, assembled so they or counsel can send whatever this state requires. Deadlines vary; ask, do not invent. Typical contents are the deposit ledger line, move-in and move-out indexes, vendor invoices, and a proposed-charge column clearly labeled proposed. Desks keep the pack because a missing invoice is how offices lose an owner argument or mail an incomplete letter. Cadence starts at notice and finishes after the last make-ready invoice they want on the worksheet. You assemble the file. You do not decide the legal amount. Common mistakes are inventing a mail-by date, ruling what the law allows them to keep, and mailing a check from this folder. Never send money, never sweep remaining deposit into operating or owner cash yourself, never store bank logins, never invent a habitability offset, and never use the worksheet as a lockout or eviction notice. Ask which invoices they want on the worksheet and who sends the letter this state requires. Proposed is the only charge label you use. Deadlines are ask-not-invent. You assemble; they or counsel decide and they send money.
