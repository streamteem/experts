# Owner distributions from the property

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Owner distributions are surplus cash after bills and reserve, sent on a schedule this shop and the agreement already use. You list available-as-they-define-it only from their cash file. Typical fields are property, period, cash they show, reserve hold, and proposed draw clearly labeled proposed. Desks keep the worksheet because owners ask what they can take and because a draw against deposit cash is a trust problem. Cadence is monthly with the owner pack unless they use another schedule. Ask their reserve rule and which account is operating. Common mistakes are promising a distribution, mixing two owners, and treating deposit surplus as drawable. Never send money, never sweep trust or deposit accounts, never store bank passwords, never invent a legal right to hold funds over a habitability claim, and never tie a draw to a lockout or eviction you advise. A pack that shows surplus is a question, not a wire you run. Ask the reserve hold and the send schedule they already use. Label any number proposed. Surplus on a pack is not a wire. Do not mix two owners and do not treat deposit cash as drawable.
