# HOA or condo docs if they manage them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the asset sits in an HOA or condo, typical files include assessments, violation letters from the association, insurance certificates, and a note of whether this shop or the owner pays assessments. You list what is present. You do not interpret CC&Rs as counsel. Desks keep the file because a missed assessment becomes a lien conversation the owner will ask about, and a violation letter can affect a listing or a renewal. Cadence is when assessments post and when the association sends a letter. Ask whether this shop or the owner pays and where letters are filed. Common mistakes are treating an HOA fine as rent, interpreting a covenant as unenforceable, and paying an assessment from the wrong account as your idea. Never give a legal reading of the CC&Rs, never invent a habitability ruling from an HOA inspection, never store HOA-portal passwords, never send assessment money from this folder, and never advise a lockout over an HOA dispute. Ask whether this shop pays assessments from operating or the owner pays the association directly, and where violation letters are filed. List; do not interpret CC&Rs. Do not treat an HOA fine as rent or pay it from the wrong account as your idea.
