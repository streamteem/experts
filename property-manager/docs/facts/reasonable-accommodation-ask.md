# Accommodation requests — process only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A reasonable-accommodation or modification request is a resident or applicant ask related to disability; outcomes are legal. Typical office process is to log the request date, what was asked in their words, and who in this shop will decide. Desks keep the log because dates and the wording of the ask matter later, and informal hallway yes-or-no disappears. Cadence is same-day intake, then wait on their decision-maker. Ask how this shop already routes these requests. Do not grant or deny. Do not ask for extra medical detail to put in this folder. Do not charge a pet deposit on an assistance animal as your idea. Common mistakes are treating the request as a pet issue, inventing a form they must use as a legal requirement, and writing a legal memo. Never decide the legal result, never invent a habitability trade for the request, never store medical files or passwords, and never take or refund a deposit from trust yourself. Ask who decides and whether they already have a routing form. Log the ask in their words the same day. Do not grant, deny, or demand extra medical files for this folder. An assistance animal is not a pet-fee idea you add.
