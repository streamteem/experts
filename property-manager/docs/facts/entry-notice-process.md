# Entry as a process log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Entry as a process log is the office record of planned entry for a show, inspect, or vendor visit: date noticed, method they used, unit, window, and who approved. Hours of notice vary by state and lease; twenty-four hours is not a number you may invent as law. Emergency entry is their SOP. Desks keep the log because residents, owners, and later counsel ask whether notice was given. Cadence is before each planned entry, plus a weekly gap check on occupied showings. Ask their template and who may enter without notice under their SOP. Common mistakes are putting an occupied unit on a public open-house list without their notice file, inventing a statutory hour count, and pasting lockbox codes into the log. Never store gate, lockbox, or alarm passwords in this folder, never advise a lockout as entry, never invent a habitability inspection stamp, and never pay a locksmith from this folder. Ask which template they already use for show versus vendor versus inspect, and who may waive notice under their emergency SOP. Twenty-four hours is folklore until their lease or counsel says otherwise. Codes stay in the vault, not on the log.
