# Last month vs deposit vs prepaid

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Last month's rent, prepaid rent, and a security deposit are different books in many shops even when the resident paid several amounts at move-in. Typical fields are unit, amount, label they use (last month, prepaid, deposit), and the account it sits in. Desks keep the split because applying last-month to a current delinquency without their rule breaks the roll and can break state deposit rules you must not invent. Cadence is at move-in and when someone asks to apply prepaid to a balance. Ask their labels and whether last-month may be applied only in the final month. Common mistakes are treating prepaid rent as owner cash to sweep, calling last-month a deposit, and applying it to current charges unless they said. Never invent a trust or commingling rule, never move the money yourself, never store bank passwords, never invent a habitability rent abatement against prepaid, and never advise a lockout because prepaid was used up. Ask whether last-month may be applied only in the final month and which account holds prepaid. Mixing prepaid into a security-deposit book is a common error. Do not sweep prepaid as owner cash and do not invent a trust rule.
