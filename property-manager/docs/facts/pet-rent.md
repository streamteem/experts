# Pets vs assistance animals

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pet rent and pet deposits are recurring or prepaid charges this shop already labeled for animals they treat as pets under the lease. Assistance-animal paperwork is a different file. Typical fields are unit, pet description they recorded, pet rent, pet deposit, and a pointer to the addendum. Desks keep the split because mixing an assistance animal into pet fees is a fair-housing legal question this desk does not decide. Cadence is at application, move-in, and when a pet is added. Ask how this shop already labeled the file and who decides assistance-animal requests. Common mistakes are inventing a pet fee, denying or approving an animal, and charging a pet deposit on an assistance animal as your idea. Never give a fair-housing legal opinion, never demand extra medical detail for this folder, never store passwords, and never take a pet deposit from trust or pay a refund yourself. Ask how they already split pet rent, pet deposit, and assistance-animal files, and who in the office routes a new animal request. A hallway yes that a dog is fine is not a lease addendum. Do not invent a breed ban as a legal rule.
