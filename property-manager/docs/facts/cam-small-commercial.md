# Small-commercial CAM — ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Small-commercial CAM or NNN is estimated common-area or triple-net billing that many retail and office leases true-up later against actual invoices. Typical pack contents are what was billed this period versus vendor invoices they already coded as CAM, plus the lease file labels for share and base year if they store them. Desks keep the pack because owners and tenants argue from the file, not from memory. Cadence is monthly estimates and an annual recon if their lease uses one. Ask their lease file labels, which expenses they treat as CAM, and the as-of period. You do not certify a CAM recon as an accountant-lawyer. Common mistakes are inventing a pro-rata share or square footage, mixing two suites on one recon, and calling the recon legally binding. Never invent a statutory deadline, never pay CAM vendors from this folder, never store landlord-portal passwords, and never treat a CAM dispute as a lockout or eviction you may start. Ask which expense buckets they treat as CAM this year and whether a base year or cap sits in the lease file. A recon without the invoices they coded is a question list, not a certified statement. Do not invent a suite's share from a flyer.
