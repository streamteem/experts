# Unit mix and beds/baths

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit mix is the bed, bath, and square-footage row this shop prints on the roll or flyer. Ask their source — old listing versus measured. Desks keep the mix because asking rent, ads, and owner reports all repeat those numbers. Cadence is when a rehab changes a den to a bedroom or when they correct a flyer. Do not invent square footage. Do not advertise a unit as a bedroom if they marked it den; ask. Common mistakes are copying a listing-site number as measured, steering families by bedroom talk as a legal occupancy opinion, and changing mix to justify a rent you invented. Small-commercial mix may be suite square footage from the lease, not a residential bed count. Never invent an occupancy-limit legal ruling, never certify floor area as an appraiser, never store listing-portal passwords, never take a holding deposit into this folder, and never advise a lockout to re-mix a unit. Ask whether beds, baths, and square footage come from a measured file, an old listing, or the lease. Do not invent footage. Do not advertise a den as a bedroom unless they changed the file. Occupancy limits are legal; do not invent one.
