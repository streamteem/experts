# Days vacant

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Days vacant is the downtime metric this shop uses between notice, vacant, rent-ready, and leased. Typical splits are notice-to-vacant, vacant-to-ready, and ready-to-leased. Their targets win; a seven-day punch in one shop is a twenty-day punch in another. Desks keep the dates because owners ask why a door sat empty and because the showing calendar depends on an honest ready date. Cadence is weekly while vacant and at month-end for the owner pack. Ask when they flip each status in software and whether notice-to-vacant starts on the notice date or the keys-in date. Common mistakes are hiding vacancy by leaving a past tenant on the roll, marking rent-ready without their checklist, and inventing a market-rent start date. Never call the unit habitable or up to code as the ready stamp, never schedule a lockout to shorten days vacant, never store lockbox passwords on the metric sheet, and never move deposit or trust cash to paper over vacancy loss. Ask which status flip starts each clock in their software and what target they tell owners. A ready date without their punch complete is a dishonest start. Do not hide a vacant by leaving a past tenant on the roll.
