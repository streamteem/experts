# Concessions and credits

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A concession is a shop-granted reduction or credit — a week free, a recurring discount, or a gift they already booked — sitting on the rent roll or a credit memo, not a deposit. Typical fields are unit, concession type, amount, start and end, and whether they lower charged rent or post a credit. Desks keep the record because occupancy and loss-to-lease talks are dishonest if free rent is hidden inside one hundred percent occupied. Cadence is at lease-up and when a special expires. Ask how this shop books it and whether owner approval is required above a dollar amount. Common mistakes are inventing a concession to make occupancy look better, calling a concession a deposit, and changing charged rent without their file. Never invent a rent-control-safe discount as legal advice, never pay a gift card from this folder, never store payment passwords, and never treat a credit as owner cash you may sweep. Ask whether a one-time special and a recurring concession use the same book and whether owner approval is required above a dollar amount. Occupancy talk that hides free rent inside charged rent is a common owner-pack failure. Do not invent a concession to paper a vacant.
