# Who pays utilities

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who pays utilities is a lease and SOP question: owner-paid, tenant-paid, or ratio-utility billing, sometimes with RUBS or suite meters on small commercial. Typical files are the lease utility addendum, vendor bills, and any allocation workbook they already use. Desks keep the coding so water, trash, and electric do not land on the wrong party and then show up as surprise AR. Cadence is at move-in and whenever a bill-back is coded. Ask before coding a water bill as tenant bill-back, and ask whether small-commercial suites have separate meters. Common mistakes are starting a collection for utilities the lease file does not show, inventing a RUBS formula, and treating a city lien as something you may pay from trust. Never invent a habitability ruling because a utility was shut off, never advise a utility shutoff as self-help, never store utility-portal passwords, and never send money from this folder. Ask whether trash, water, and electric are billed the same way on every unit, and whether small-commercial suites have meters or a ratio they already wrote. A city shutoff notice in their file is a flag, not a self-help shutoff you advise.
