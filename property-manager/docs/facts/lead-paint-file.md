# Lead files (pre-1978) — present/missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lead files for many pre-1978 dwellings are the year-built note, disclosure pamphlet receipt, and certified-firm paperwork if they use a firm for work that disturbs paint. EPA RRP and HUD lead-safe orientation apply to many such repairs; this desk lists present or missing and does not certify the work. Desks keep the file because owners, vendors, and later claims ask whether disclosure and firm papers exist before a scrape, a window job, or a turnover that disturbs paint. Cadence is at lease-up and whenever a repair will disturb paint in a pre-1978 unit. Ask year built from their file; do not invent it. Common mistakes are saying the work was lead-safe, telling a handyman to scrape paint, and certifying a renovator. Never invent year built, never certify RRP, never stamp the unit habitable or up to code, never store contractor-portal passwords, and never pay the renovator from this folder. Ask where year-built lives and whether they already have a certified firm on the roster for paint-disturbing work. Present or missing is the list. Do not invent 1978 as a guess and do not tell a handyman to scrape.
