# Lease-end calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A lease-end calendar is the sixty- or ninety-day look at expirations so renewals and make-ready load are visible. Typical fields are unit, expire date, status they want (renew, MTM, vacant), and proposed rent they set if any. Desks keep the horizon because a surprise expiration becomes a vacant with no punch started and no showing sheet. Cadence is weekly during the horizon they name. Ask the horizon and whether subsidy or month-to-month rows belong on the same list. Do not send renewal offers. Do not assume non-renewal. Common mistakes are inventing an auto-renew, serving a non-renewal as counsel, and omitting subsidy or MTM rows. A unit that expired last week and still sits as fixed-term occupied is a status question, not a legal conversion. Never invent a statutory notice period, never give eviction advice on a non-renewal, never store e-sign passwords, never take a renewal payment into this folder, and never schedule a lockout at expiration. Ask the look-ahead days and whether MTM and subsidy rows sit on the same sheet. Weekly review during that horizon is typical. Do not send offers and do not assume non-renewal. An expired fixed term still marked occupied is a status question.
