# Work order export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A work-order export lists open and closed tickets with unit, dates, status, and often priority or vendor. Ask their status vocabulary before you flag rows. Flag missing unit labels and life-safety words (gas, fire, flood, no heat in freeze, no water, sparking). Do not close a ticket from a chat recap. Do not merge two residents' tickets. Do not paste lockbox codes into the flagged file. Never invent a habitability ruling from a description, and never pay the vendor from this export.
