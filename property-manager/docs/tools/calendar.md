# Schedule file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Showings and vendor appointment windows come from a calendar export or a sheet, not from memory. Ask timezone and who owns the calendar. Typical columns are unit, window, type (showing, vendor, inspect), and whether occupied-notice is logged. Do not put lockbox or alarm codes on the export this folder keeps. Do not schedule noisy small-commercial work against a lease you have not been shown. If an occupied showing is missing a notice row, flag it; do not invent a statutory hour count.
