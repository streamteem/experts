# Make-ready punch sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A make-ready punch sheet is unit, item, vendor or in-house, done yes or no, and photo path, on their checklist. Rent-ready is their label, not an inspection stamp. Ask who may mark done. Do not add hotel-grade items they do not use. Do not mark done without their file. Do not paste lockbox codes onto the sheet. Days-vacant targets are theirs. Never certify the unit as up to code or habitable, and never pay punch vendors from this folder.
