# Notice process log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A notice process log records type, unit, date, delivery method, and who approved — process only, not a legal form bank. They or counsel send legal notices. You flag missing fields on templates they already use. Do not draft statutory pay-or-quit or lockout text. Do not invent a day count. Do not state that a notice was properly served. Never store lockbox codes on the log, and never treat a logged late letter as eviction advice you gave.
