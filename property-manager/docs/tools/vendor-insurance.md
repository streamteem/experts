# Vendor COI tracker

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vendor COI tracker lists vendor, policy type, and expiry copied from PDFs they provided. Missing is marked missing. You do not say coverage is adequate or invent limits. Cadence is onboarding plus a periodic expiry sweep. Ask which additional-insured language they use; do not invent it. Never store insurer-portal passwords, never pay a premium from this folder, never invent a license number, and never treat a missing COI as a reason you advise a resident lockout.
