# Deposit ledger

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A deposit ledger lists unit, amount held, account label they use, and move-in date. The disposition worksheet is a separate proposed-charge pack and is not a legal ruling. Ask whether pet and last-month sit on the same book. Do not apply a deposit to current rent unless they said. Do not invent a return deadline. Never send a refund, never sweep remainder into operating yourself, never store bank logins, and never treat the ledger as a lockout lever.
