# Showing / availability sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A showing or availability sheet lists unit, asking rent they set, available date, and windows. No steering language and no invented comps. Occupied rows need their notice process. Do not promise an application will be approved. Do not paste lockbox codes onto the sheet. Ask lead-source labels they use. Never collect a holding deposit into this folder, never invent market rent as an appraisal, and never treat a no-show as eviction or lockout advice.
