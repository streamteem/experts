# Rent roll export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A rent-roll export is the as-of CSV or workbook they produced from software or a sheet. Read the header; columns vary by product. Typical needs are unit, charged rent, status, and deposits if they store them there. Ask the as-of date. Do not edit charged rent, do not invent market rent, and do not hide vacancy by leaving a past tenant on the row. Late lists and owner packs start from this file. Never apply a late fee they did not configure, and never treat the export as a legal lease.
