# Vendor invoices and quotes folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor invoices, quotes, and completed-work photos live in paths they name — a drive folder, an email drop, or a portal they log into themselves. You read PDFs and photo names; you do not keep portal passwords. Typical compare fields are vendor, amount from the PDF, scope, and date. Do not round, do not pick a winner, and do not invent a license number from a letterhead. Emergency jobs may have only one invoice; still copy the printed amount. Never pay a vendor from this folder or from trust.
