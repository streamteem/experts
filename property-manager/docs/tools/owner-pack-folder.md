# Owner pack folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The owner-pack folder is where they drop bank CSVs, bills, last month's pack, and the period's rent-roll and work-order exports. Ask naming, property, owner entity, and period dates. One property per folder unless they said portfolio and the pack is labeled combined. Do not imply a distribution was sent. No bank logins in the folder notes. Never mix two owners, never invent a fee, and never send owner money or sweep trust or deposit cash from this pack.
