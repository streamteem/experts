# Flag callbacks

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mark callbacks so they are not billed like a new sale unless they say so. Same symptom within their window is a flag. Ask their definition and job type. Do not admit legal fault or write that a warranty statute was breached. Do not invent a no-charge decision. Quote the original job id, the return id if they opened one, and the bill mark they stored. Labor warranty and manufacturer parts warranty are different clocks. If the file is silent on bill versus no-charge, that silence is a question for the person who owns pricing, not a courtesy you invent to calm the board.
