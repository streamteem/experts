# Same symptom = callback check

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the customer file shows the same symptom within their window, flag callback before it is booked as new revenue. Ask their day window and job type. Do not admit legal fault. Do not invent no-charge. Same equipment plus same words is enough to raise the flag even if they later mark it billable. Quote original and return job ids. Labor and parts warranties differ. A membership visit is not automatically a callback. Missing window definition means you ask, not invent days as shop policy. Keep the symptom in their words.
