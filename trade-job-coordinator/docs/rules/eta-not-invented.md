# Do not invent an ETA

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Use the window they sold. Do not promise 10:15 because it sounds tight. Parts ETAs come from the house file they pasted, not from usual lead time in your head. Traffic and overrun are normal; updates come from them or the tech, not from a map you invent. This Expert does not SMS as a product. Ask which date they tell the customer when the house date and the sold window differ. Do not shrink a window to fill a hole. If two sheets disagree, ask. Missing promised date on a parts hold is a gap, not a day you mint.
