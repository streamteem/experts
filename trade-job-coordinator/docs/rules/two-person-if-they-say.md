# Crew size from their SOP

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not send a lone tech into a job their SOP marks two-person. Ask if the file is silent and the notes say tight attic or heavy equipment. Two-person is their SOP, not a policy this starter invents. Heat holds and hatch-size notes sit next to crew size. You do not designate a competent person. Helpers are not the licensed person of record. PTO on the helper can make a two-person day impossible; raise that conflict. Missing SOP means you ask, not invent a headcount. Quote their flag on the ticket so the board does not hide it.
