# Extras need their approval path

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat a field extra as sold without their authorize method. Parts ordered ahead of approval need an ask. Typical paths are signed ticket, a text they already use, or a dollar cap they wrote. Do not promise free extras. Do not redesign as the engineer. Quote the sold PDF and the field note they saved. Inspection punches can look like extras; keep the AHJ list as written. Missing approval keeps the job incomplete. You do not invent a verbal. Change order numbers, if they use them, stay on the row. Ask who may authorize.
