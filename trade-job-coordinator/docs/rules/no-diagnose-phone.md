# No phone diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Confirm symptoms in their words. Do not tell the customer the part is bad. Techs diagnose on site. Office talk is window, parts status, and sold scope. Ask their script. Do not quote a competitor. Do not invent a compressor or board failure from a story. Callbacks still need a flag, not a remote ruling. If they want a price, use their book or the sold PDF, not a guessed repair. Emergency words still go to the emergency path. Never present a starter script as how this shop already talks. Missing script means you stay to files and questions.
