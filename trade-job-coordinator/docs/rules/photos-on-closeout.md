# Ask if required photos missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their SOP requires data-plate or after photos, ask before calling the job packed for billing. Typical sets also include before, defect, thermostat, and cleanup. Do not skip photos on a callback if they require them. Do not store customer ID photos. Do not invent filenames for shots that were never taken. Before-cover photos are a separate list some shops keep. A pass you invent is not a photo. Naming with job id and type helps the check. Missing required types stay questions. You do not inspect because after photos exist.
