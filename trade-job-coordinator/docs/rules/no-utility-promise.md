# No utility or city promises

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not promise a meter spot or an inspector arrival clock. Log the window they were given in a file. Utility and city dates vary and slip. You track numbers and windows; you do not stamp an interconnect or a pass. Do not invent a permit number. Do not say the utility approved unless their file says so. Panel and generator jobs often wait on those files. If two letters disagree, ask which is live. Missing utility window on a service-upgrade day is a question before the crew stays booked. Quote their letter dates only.
