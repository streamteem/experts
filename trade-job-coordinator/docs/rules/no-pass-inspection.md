# No invented inspection pass

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never say passed unless their file names the result and date. Scheduled is not passed. Verbal from a tech is a question until the card or portal screenshot exists. You do not inspect and you do not hold an AHJ stamp. Do not argue a correction list as the city. Do not mark complete because the crew left. Quote the words on the card. A permit number is not a pass. Missing result on a job they flagged as needing inspection stays a gap on the permit pack. Do not write that the work is code-compliant because an inspection was scheduled.
