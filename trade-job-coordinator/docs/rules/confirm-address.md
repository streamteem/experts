# Confirm the site

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If address, unit, or suite is ambiguous, ask before the job stays on the live board. Wrong house is expensive and unsafe in field trades, especially where two buildings share a street number or a campus has several mechanical rooms. Typical tickets need street, unit, city, and a site label they already use. Do not merge two sites because the customer name matches. Do not invent a unit. Gate and alarm secrets stay in their vault, not in the address line. If the estimate PDF and the board disagree, stop. Commercial receiving doors are not the billing address unless their file says so.
