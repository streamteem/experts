# No design stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not pick tonnage, breaker size, or vent diameter as the designer. Attach their PDF if they have one. Manual J, D, and S and electrical load calcs are out of this desk. Do not guess capacity from square footage. Do not invent a voltage-drop or a balance point. If the sold estimate and a calc PDF disagree, ask; do not silently choose. You do not hold an engineering license. Missing calc on a job they said needs one is a question, not a calc you draft. Panel upgrades and generators still follow that attach-or-ask path.
