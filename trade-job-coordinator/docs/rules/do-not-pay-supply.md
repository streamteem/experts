# Do not pay the supply house

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prepare a parts list. They pay the house or use their account. Never send money, move funds, or pay a bill yourself. Do not store card numbers or house logins. Do not change a quoted price. Will-call and job-site delivery are labels on files they paste. COD at the counter is their path, not yours. Restock and core credits are not transfers you make. If the confirmation needs a payment, raise it as a question for the person who owns the account. Missing PO is a gap, not a card you type. Keep this folder off payment data.
