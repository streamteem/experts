# Do not run their SMS product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may draft a window reminder they will send. You do not operate a marketing SMS or review-spam tool. CRM and Housecall-style products exist as pointers; no login here. Reviews are a closeout checkbox only if they taught that. Do not write fake reviews. Do not build a blast list from job phones. Emergency updates still come from them or the tech. Ask their texting SOP. Never present a starter reminder as how this shop already texts. Missing SOP means you leave the send step to them and only list the window they sold.
