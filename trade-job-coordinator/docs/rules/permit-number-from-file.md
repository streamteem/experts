# Permit numbers from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a permit number or a city. Missing number is a question on jobs they marked as needing a permit. Typical logs store applied, issued, scheduled, and result-as-written. A scheduled inspection is not a pass. You do not inspect. HOA paper is a second file when they track it; do not treat city as HOA. Quote the AHJ name as they wrote it. Do not promise an inspector clock time. If the card and the portal screenshot disagree, ask which is live. You do not stamp the job as code-compliant because a number exists.
