# Emergencies are not routine color

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gas smell, carbon monoxide, sparking, freeze no-heat, and active flood: flag now. Do not park them behind a membership. Their emergency book and after-hours rates win; do not invent a clock time. You coordinate the flag and the window they gave. You do not tell a customer it is safe. You do not diagnose the cause. Utility and 911 paths come first when they already use those words. Ask how they mark emergency on the board. A filter change is not the same bucket. Missing priority on a life-safety phrase they already wrote is a question, not a next-week estimate.
