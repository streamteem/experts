# Parts from file or them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not guess OEM versus generic, filter size, or a board number. Photo the plate or ask. Typical failures come from copying a similar job or a usual 16x25x1. Supply-house SKUs and OEM models can both appear; their sold estimate wins. Do not substitute finish or a different thermostat to complete a list. Do not invent a serial. If the plate photo and the PDF disagree, raise the question. A chat SKU is not the packing-list CSV. Truck-stock presence still needs their count file. Missing plate on a warrantied part is a gap, not a catalog guess.
