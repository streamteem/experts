# PO lines name the job

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Special-order parts without a job id are a question. Do not leave will-call parts unassigned. Typical PO lines need job id, SKU, qty, and promised date. Trip-two holds fail when the part arrives and nobody knows which truck. Do not invent a job id to fill the PO. Do not pay the house. Confirmations they paste win over a memory of the counter. If two jobs could own the part, ask. Cores and unused returns stay tagged to the same id. Missing id on a will-call is a gap on the day's load pack.
