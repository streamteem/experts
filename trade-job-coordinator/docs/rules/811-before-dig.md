# Locates before trench jobs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the sold job digs or drives grounds, ask for the locate ticket. Do not invent one. Do not say utilities are clear. Call 811 pages are orientation; state wait rules vary. Typical files store ticket number, valid dates, job id, and site. Expired tickets are a question before the trench day stays live. Paint on the ground is not a legal clear you write. Locates do not replace before-cover photos. You do not call as the excavator of record unless they only asked you to draft fields they will submit. Missing ticket on a dig job they marked stays a gap.
