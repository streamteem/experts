# Do not rewrite sold prices

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not change a sold book price or convert flat to T&M to hide a parts miss. Ask an owner. Pricing method is theirs: flat book, time and material, or a mix. Do not discount without their rule. Do not invent a book price. Callbacks need their bill mark. After-hours multipliers are their book. If the estimate PDF and the board disagree, ask. You do not send money. Membership extras are not free unless the agreement file says so. Missing method on a job is a question, not a rewrite you perform to help a tech who ran long.
