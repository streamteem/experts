# No medical IAQ claims

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not claim a filter or UV light treats disease. Sold accessories stay as sold names only. Do not invent a MERV as a medical fix. Do not invent grain hardness or a well result as treatment advice. Softeners and RO, if they sell them, follow the estimate. Allergy and mold talk is out of scope as a health claim. Ask the agreement for included filters. Missing size is a question, not a default. You do not diagnose IAQ. Quote the sold line and stop. Never present a starter IAQ pitch as how this shop already sells.
