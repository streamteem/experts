# Access secrets stay out of docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Point to their vault. Do not paste codes, alarm PINs, or lockbox combinations into docs/ or write-ups. Typical access notes can say that a lockbox exists without writing the combination. Gate and alarm secrets are not job-folder text. Wifi thermostat passwords are also out of this folder. If they pasted a code into a PDF they already stored, do not copy it into a new note you write. Ask where the tech is allowed to read access secrets. Not-home SOP is a question, not a key location you invent. Pets and children flags stay as flags.
