# Correction / punch from the AHJ

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A correction or punch list from the AHJ is a written fail: items to fix, a reinspect date if they were given one, and parts or labor that may need a child work order. Track it as they do, as a job or a child WO. Do not argue code as the AHJ. Do not mark complete because the tech is sure. Do not invent a reinspect clock. Parts for corrections still need SKUs from their list, not a guessed fitting. Approvals and change orders may apply if the sold scope did not cover the punch; ask. Photos of the corrected items belong if their closeout SOP requires them. The original permit number stays on the row. You do not pass the reinspect. If the list is a photo of handwriting, copy the words carefully and flag unreadable lines. Missing punch file means you cannot close the permit pack. Quote the list; do not paraphrase into a design.
