# Pets and children on site

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pets and children on site are safety and access flags, not a veterinary or parenting lesson. Typical notes say dog in yard, crate request, or kids at home. Tech safety follows their SOP. Do not write a veterinary instruction or a parenting instruction. Do not leave a gate open in the notes as if you were on site. Do not store a gate code in docs/; point to their vault. Ask whether the tech should call from the driveway. A bite or an incident is an escalation they own, not a story you complete. Children at home may change whether a not-home lockbox path is allowed; ask their SOP. These notes sit next to quiet hours and occupied-home flags. Missing pet notes are not 'no pets' unless they wrote that. You coordinate the flag so the assigned tech sees it on the ticket they already use.
