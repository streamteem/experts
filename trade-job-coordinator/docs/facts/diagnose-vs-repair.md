# Diagnose vs repair visits

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many demand-service jobs are two visits in practice: a diagnose trip, then a repair after approval and parts. Typical authorize-by methods are a signed ticket, a text they already use, or a dollar cap they wrote. Ask which path this shop uses for this job. Do not treat diagnose-only as a finished repair. Do not start the repair on the schedule as sold unless they marked approval. Do not diagnose the failed part over the phone. Parts ordered ahead of approval need an ask so the house PO is not a silent commitment. Trip-two or parts-hold jobs stay open with a reason code. The same tech may or may not return; ask. Flat diagnose fees, if they have them, are their book. If the customer declined, keep that mark and do not convert the row to an install. Missing authorize method on an extra is a question, not a verbal you invent for the file.
