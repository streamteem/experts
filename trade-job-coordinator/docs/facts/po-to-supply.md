# Supply-house orders

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Supply-house orders are how most US SMB trade shops buy equipment and fittings: will-call, stocked truck, or job-site delivery against a PO number and a promised time. Confirmations and invoices are files they paste; this desk does not log into Ferguson, Johnstone, or any other house. Ask their house account names as labels only. Typical columns are PO, job id, SKU, qty, promised date, and delivery method. Do not pay the house. Do not change a quoted price. Do not send money. Do not invent a will-call window. If the confirmation and the sold estimate disagree on model, ask before the truck rolls. Backorder slips need a board question, not a silent delay. Core returns and warranty parts often ride the same account; keep those codes as they wrote them. Missing job id on a PO line is a question so the part does not sit unassigned at the counter.
