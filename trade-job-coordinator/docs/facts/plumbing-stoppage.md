# Water and drain emergencies

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Water and drain emergencies on a typical plumbing board include an active leak, no water, sewer backup, and, in some shops' books, no hot water. Ask which of those words they treat as emergency versus next-day service. Shutoff locations belong in their notes if they already captured them; do not invent a curb-stop or a meter pit. You do not snake a line, pull a toilet, or open a septic tank as the plumber. You do not hold a plumbing license. Municipal sewer versus septic changes who they call on a backup; use the customer file. Typical job fields are symptom in their words, access to the cleanout if they noted one, and whether the site is occupied. Do not tell a customer to use a chemical they did not ask about as a medical or finish-safety ruling. Active flood sits above a drip on the priority board unless they said otherwise. Missing access notes are questions.
