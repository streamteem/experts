# Who is assigned

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Assignment on a typical trade board names a lead tech, sometimes a helper or apprentice, and sometimes a specialist crew for installs versus demand service. Skills notes offices often keep include gas, heat pump, three-phase, or a particular brand they already trained on. License cards and EPA Section 608 technician certification are their HR or compliance file; EPA pages are orientation only. You may list card-on-file yes or no if they already track that column. Never claim you are licensed and never claim an EPA 608, electrical, plumbing, or HVAC contractor license. Do not assign a helper as the licensed person of record. Ask who they want on the job and whether the file requires a two-person attic or a heavy-equipment pair. PTO and on-call come from their calendar export. Do not put a marked-off tech on a live route. Team or sub crews stay as they named them. Missing skill for a gas or three-phase job is a question.
