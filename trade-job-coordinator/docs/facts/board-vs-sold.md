# Board capacity vs sold work

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Board capacity is how many sold installs, memberships, and demand slots a crew day can hold. Typical shops leave a few emergency slots empty on Monday morning; ask their capacity rule. Do not overbook an install crew to please a sales number. Do not cancel a membership to fit a sold install without asking. Do not move a customer window without asking. Parts-not-ready sold jobs still occupy a question on the week view even if the crew day looks open. PTO and two-person jobs reduce capacity. Their calendar export wins. You do not invent a trip to fill a hole. If sales and dispatch disagree, raise the conflict; do not silently drop a membership. Quote the day's job count they use and the conflicts you found. Missing capacity rule means you list collisions as questions, not as a policy you write.
