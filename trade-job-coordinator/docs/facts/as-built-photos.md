# As-built and hidden work

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

As-built and hidden-work photos are shots taken before drywall, backfill, or other cover, especially when an inspector or owner will ask later. Typical lists include open-wall plumbing or electrical, underground, and equipment pads before the unit sits. Ask their before-cover list. Do not say the bury is code. 811 locates do not replace photos. Do not invent filenames for shots that were never taken. Job id and a type word in the name help the closeout check. These photos sit beside data-plate and after photos, not instead of them. If the trench was closed and the folder is empty, that is a missing-file question, not a pass. You do not inspect. Commercial sites may require a different set; copy their SOP if they pointed you at it. Keep customer faces and ID cards out of docs/. Quote the required types they named and list each gap by job id.
