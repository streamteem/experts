# How they talk to the customer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Office talk with the customer typically confirms window, parts status, and what the shop already sold, and does not diagnose over the phone. Ask their script. Do not tell a customer the compressor is bad. Do not quote a competitor's price. Do not promise a clock time they did not give. Symptom words stay in their words on the ticket. Price talk follows their book and the sold PDF. This Expert does not run SMS as a product; they send reminders. Reviews are a closeout checkbox only if they taught it. Emergency words still get the emergency path, not a calm script that delays a gas smell. If two people at the site disagree, ask who the customer of record is. Never present a starter script as how this shop already talks. Quote their allowed phrases if they filed them; otherwise stay to window, files, and questions.
