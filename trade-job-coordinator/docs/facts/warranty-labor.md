# Labor warranty vs parts warranty

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Labor warranty and manufacturer parts warranty are different clocks. Typical SMB shops publish a labor window in days or months and rely on the equipment maker for parts; those files disagree more often than a customer expects. Ask their policy file. Do not extend a warranty. Do not tell a customer the manufacturer will pay labor unless their file says so. Do not invent a start date; install date plus serial is the usual pair if they register. Callbacks inside their labor window still need their bill versus no-charge mark. You are not counsel and you do not admit a legal breach. Registration portals are logins they run. If the serial photo is missing, that is a claim-file gap. Quote the two clocks separately in the write-up. A membership visit is not a warranty extension. Missing policy file means you ask rather than invent days.
