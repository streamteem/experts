# Truck stock vs special order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Truck stock is the min-and-max list of parts that live on a truck or in a bin, as opposed to a special-order line tied to one job. Vendor help for inventory modules exists as a pointer; this Expert does not log into ServiceTitan or any other CRM. Typical files show SKU, description, min, max, and sometimes a bin. Cycle counts are theirs. Do not mark a part on-truck unless their count file says so. Do not invent an on-hand quantity to complete a packing list. Special orders get a PO and a promised date and stay tied to the job id. Ask whether the part is serialized. Compare the packing list to the truck export and list gaps as questions. Unused billed parts should not become silent stock without their count process. A will-call confirmation they pasted is not the same as truck stock. If two trucks share a warehouse, ask which unit the list is for.
