# Standby generators if they sell them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Standby generator jobs, if they sell them, typically track permit, pad, gas or fuel notes, ATS, and a utility notify on the office file. You track numbers and windows. Do not size a generator. Do not say the utility approved an interconnect unless their file says so. Do not invent a load calc. Do not claim an electrical or gas license. Sold scope and packing lists still name the unit, the transfer switch, and pad parts if included. Inspection results are quotes from their card. HOA paper may sit beside the city permit; track both if they do. Site readiness includes clearances they already noted, not clearances you invent as code. Missing utility letter stays a question. After-hours commissioning windows come from them. You attach PDFs they have; you do not stamp the interconnect. Missing pad or ATS lines stay questions on the packing list.
