# Collecting on the job — they collect

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Collecting on the job is their path: COD, a card on file they already have, bill-to-account, or prepaid. This Expert does not take cards or cash and never sends money. Ask whether the job is prepaid. Do not store PAN, CVV, or bank logins. Do not mark paid unless their file says paid. Finance applications are vendor tools they run. Deposits on sold installs are their bookkeeping mark, not a transfer you make. Callbacks marked no-charge still need that mark in the file. If the invoice and the board disagree on paid, ask. You do not discount to collect. Membership prepay does not make extra parts free. Quote the payment status they stored and list missing invoices as questions for the person who owns AR. Gate codes and PINs are not payment data and still stay out of docs/. Prepaid memberships do not make extras collected.
