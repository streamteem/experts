# Permit number on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some service and many install jobs need a permit number, the issuing city or county, and inspection dates kept on the office file so the crew and the closer can see status. Typical office stages are applied, issued, scheduled, and result-as-written from a card or portal screenshot they saved. The authority having jurisdiction varies by place; ICC and local pages are orientation, not a stamp this desk applies. Ask which jobs they marked as needing a permit. Do not invent a number, a city, or a pass. You do not inspect and you do not hold a contractor license. Quote their file if it names a result and a date; a scheduled inspection is not a pass. Missing number on a job they flagged as permit-required stays a question. Do not say the work is code-compliant because a permit was applied. Do not tell a customer the city will arrive on a clock time you invented. Keep the AHJ name as they wrote it.
