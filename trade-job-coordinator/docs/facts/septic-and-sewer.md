# Sewer vs septic

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Municipal sewer versus septic changes parts, who to call on a backup, and sometimes whether a pumper is in the loop. Ask the customer file. Do not open a septic tank as the pumper. Do not tell them to pump on a date you invented. Do not invent a municipal tap. Ejector and grinder notes appear only if their file or the sold job names them. Active backup follows their emergency book. Access to a cleanout, if they noted one, belongs on the ticket; do not invent a location. You do not hold a plumbing license. HOA or county septic rules are not a stamp you apply. If the file is silent on sewer versus septic, ask before you treat the job as a simple indoor stoppage. Quote their words. Photos of backups may be on the job; keep them as files, not a diagnosis of cause.
