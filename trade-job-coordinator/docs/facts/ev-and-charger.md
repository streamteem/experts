# EV chargers if they do electrical

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

EV charger jobs, if they do electrical, typically name a sold amp rating, a breaker, a whip, and a permit row. You track permit and parts. You do not load-calc the panel. You do not invent a 60A circuit as safe. You do not claim an electrical license. Panel capacity questions go to their designer or the person who sold the job. Ask the estimate. Finish and indoor versus outdoor rating are sold lines, not substitutions. Utility or HOA paper may apply; quote their file. Inspection results stay as written. Photos of the breaker space and the finished charger may be on closeout. If the sold amp and the packing-list breaker disagree, ask. Missing panel photo or calc PDF they said they needed is a gap. Do not tell a customer the panel 'has room' as an engineering statement. Quote the sold amp and the parts they named.
