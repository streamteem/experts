# Attic and crawl constraints

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Attic and crawl notes capture hatch size, flooring, animals, temperature holds, and whether their SOP wants two people. Typical US SMB HVAC installs fail when the hatch will not pass the air handler. Ask. Do not pass a platform as an engineer. Do not schedule a 95°F attic pull-out against their heat SOP without asking. OSHA heat pages are orientation, not a waiver you write. Photos of the hatch and the path belong if their pre-install list says so. Pets and wildlife flags sit next to these notes. You do not certify clearances as code. Missing hatch size on a sold air-handler swap is a readiness question. Two-person marks come from their SOP, not a starter rule you invent as policy. Quote their hold words and keep the job off the live board if they said to hold. Animals in a crawl are flags, not wildlife advice you write.
