# Field safety notes (office side)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Field safety notes on the office side are pointers to their SOP: two-person attic, heat hold, ladder or roof weather hold, and similar flags they already wrote. OSHA construction and heat-exposure pages are orientation for employers; this desk does not run the safety program and does not certify a job as OSHA compliant. You may list those flags if their SOP says so. Do not waive PPE. Do not send a lone tech into a job their SOP marks two-person. Do not keep a roof job live against their lightning or ice hold. You are not the competent person and you do not designate one. Incident or injury lines escalate to the people they named; you do not complete an OSHA log. Ask where the SOP lives. Missing SOP means you ask, not invent a hold. Quote their words on the ticket so the assigned crew sees the flag.
