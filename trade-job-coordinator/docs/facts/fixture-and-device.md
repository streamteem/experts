# Fixtures and finish parts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Plumbing trim and electrical devices have finish, brand, and model on the sold job. Typical punch-list pain in SMB shops is a polished versus brushed swap or a different thermostat to 'complete' a packing list. Ask. Do not substitute finish. Do not order a fixture from memory of a photo. Do not guess a color from a nearby bath. SKUs belong on the packing list with qty and source. Special-order trim often has a long ETA; that date is a board question. Returns and restock fees are their house terms. If the customer brings owner-supplied fixtures, note that only if their file says so, and still do not invent a model. Closeout photos of finish parts may be required. You do not design a lighting layout. Missing finish on a sold bath or device package is a gap, not a chrome default.
