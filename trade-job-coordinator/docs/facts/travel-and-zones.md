# Zones and travel

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Zones and travel are how typical SMB shops decide whether a job fits a city day or needs a trip charge past a radius they already use. Ask. Do not book a far job into a packed city day without asking. Do not invent a trip fee. Do not invent mileage. Their zone map or ZIP list wins; a starter map is not theirs. Emergency rows may break a zone rule if they said so; still ask. Parts will-call across town can add a travel question on the same day as a far install. Crew calendars and PTO still limit who can take the far job. Windows the customer was sold stay until they change them. If two addresses share a customer name but sit in different zones, they are still two sites. Missing zone on a new city is a question. Quote their zone label and the travel mark they stored, not a drive-time you invented from a map product.
