# Heat pump and dual fuel

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Heat pump and dual-fuel packages typically name an outdoor unit, an air handler or furnace, and a thermostat that can run the sold control mode. Ask their sold package. Do not invent a balance point. Do not tell a customer aux heat is broken as a diagnosis. Do not pick SEER or HSPF as a marketing stamp. Line set, pad, and electrical disconnect still come from the sold scope and the packing list. Refrigerant notes are file amounts only; this desk does not claim 608. Defrost complaints on a callback stay as symptom words until a tech diagnoses on site. Dual fuel may need gas notes and a permit row if they marked them. You do not design the system. If the estimate lists a furnace and the board lists a straight heat pump, raise the mismatch. Missing stat model on a dual-fuel sold job is a packing-list question.
