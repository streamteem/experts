# Install site readiness

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Install site readiness is the office checklist that asks whether the site can take the sold equipment: pad, breaker space, vent path, attic or crawl access, flooring protection, and similar conditions their pre-install form already names. Typical HVAC, water-heater, and electrical replacements fail the morning of the job when a hatch is too small or a disconnect is missing. Ask their pre-install checklist. Do not pass the site as an inspector. Do not invent a vent size, a liner length, or a breaker rating as the designer. Do not say the pad is code. Photos they already require before the crew rolls belong in the job folder. If the sold PDF assumes a condition the notes contradict, raise the mismatch. Commercial sites add receiving hours and roof access; ask the site contact. Missing checklist on an install they marked sold is a question before you treat the day as ready to roll.
