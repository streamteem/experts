# Dig and locates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Jobs that trench, bore, or drive ground rods in the United States often need a one-call locate. Call 811 public pages explain the national number; ticket rules and wait times vary by state. Typical office files store a ticket number, a valid-through date, the job id, and the site address so the crew does not dig on a stale ticket. Ask whether this sold job digs. Do not invent a ticket number. Do not say the locate cleared utilities as if you were the locator. Do not treat a photo of paint on the ground as a legal clear. 811 locates do not replace before-cover photos their SOP may want. If the ticket is expired, that is a question before the trench day stays on the live board. You do not call 811 as the excavator of record unless they asked you only to draft fields they will submit. Missing ticket on a dig job they marked stays a gap, not a pass.
