# HOA plus city permit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Condos, townhomes, and HOAs may need an architectural or association form in addition to a city or county permit. Typical offices track both numbers, both statuses, and any meeting or submission dates the customer already gave. Ask. Do not say the HOA approved because a city permit exists. Do not invent a meeting date or a covenant reading. You are not counsel and you do not opine on CC&Rs. The city AHJ and the association are different files. Equipment that changes the exterior, such as a condenser location or a vent cap, is often the trigger; still ask their sold job rather than inventing a rule. Missing HOA paper on a job they marked as needing it is a question before the install day stays live. Do not stamp the work as association-compliant. Keep form names as they wrote them. Quiet-hour and parking notes from the HOA belong in access notes, not as a permit pass.
