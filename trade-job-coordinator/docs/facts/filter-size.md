# Filter and pad sizes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Filter and pad sizes on membership and service files come from the equipment or from the customer card they already stored. Do not guess 16x25x1. MERV upgrades may be sold extras; ask the agreement or the estimate. Do not claim a filter fixes indoor air quality as a medical claim. Do not claim a pad size from a memory of a usual condenser. Typical fields are width, height, depth, qty, and whether the visit includes the change. Multiple systems at one site need multiple sizes. If the plate photo shows a size and the card shows another, ask. Packing lists for membership days should copy the stored sizes. You do not diagnose airflow. Missing size is a question, not a default case. IAQ accessories stay as sold names only. Quote the file and list blanks. Do not treat a pad or filter size as a design stamp.
