# Photos they want

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closeout photos are the image set the office wants before a job is packed for billing or warranty: before, data plate, defect, completed work, thermostat, and cleanup are common in US SMB trade files. Some insurers and manufacturers want serial photos. Ask their list per job type. Do not skip photos on a callback if they require them. Do not store customer ID photos or driver's licenses in docs/. Naming usually includes job id and a type word so the check and the closer can find the set. Before-cover shots for open wall or underground work are a separate as-built habit some shops keep. A scheduled inspection is not a photo pack. Missing required types become questions, not invented filenames. You do not pass work as an inspector because after photos exist. If the plate is unreadable, ask for another shot rather than typing a guessed serial. Keep only what their SOP already asks the tech to take.
