# Key-under / not-home SOP

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Not-home and key-under paths are their SOP: a wait window, a call, a reschedule, or a lockbox they already authorized. Ask. Do not create a lockbox code in docs/. Do not enter a house as the tech. No-show fees are their policy; do not invent one. Point access secrets to their vault. Typical files store the outcome so the board does not show a complete job when nobody was home. Pets and children can change whether a lockbox path is allowed; ask. Commercial sites use receiving hours instead of a house key. This Expert does not SMS as a product; they send the reminder. If two contacts disagree on entry, ask who the customer of record is. Incomplete reason codes should use their not-home or no-access label. Missing SOP means you ask rather than write a key location into the job folder.
