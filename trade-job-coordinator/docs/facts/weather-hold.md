# Weather holds

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Weather holds keep roofs, ladders, and some attic work off the live board when their SOP says lightning, ice, heat, or rain makes the day unsafe. Ask their SOP. Do not send a crew as if you were the safety officer. Do not waive a hold to keep a sold date. Reschedule windows need the customer file updated; this Expert does not SMS as a product. OSHA heat and construction pages are orientation. Typical flags include no roofs in lightning and no ladders on ice. Commercial after-hours may still collide with a hold; ask. Parts sitting at the house do not override a hold. Quote their hold words and the jobs you moved as questions. Missing SOP means you ask, not invent a temperature number as policy. You do not certify the day as safe when the hold lifts; they do.
