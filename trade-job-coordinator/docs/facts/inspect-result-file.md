# Inspection result is a file quote

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An inspection result is a quote from a card, portal screenshot, or email they saved: inspector name or number, date, and pass, fail, or correction list as written. Never say passed unless that file says passed and dated. A scheduled inspection is not a pass. A tech who is 'sure' is a question until the file exists. You do not inspect and you do not hold an AHJ stamp. Typical office logs keep job, city, permit number, and result-as-written so closeout and billing do not invent status. ICC and city pages are orientation. Do not argue the correction list as the AHJ. Do not mark complete because the crew left. If two screenshots disagree, ask which one is live. Missing result on a job they marked as needing inspection stays a gap. Quote the words on the card. You do not say the install is code-compliant.
