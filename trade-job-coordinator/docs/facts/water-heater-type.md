# Water heater and boiler types

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Water heater and boiler types on a typical plumbing or HVAC board include tank, tankless, heat-pump water heater, and boiler, on gas, electric, or oil. Fuel, vent, and drain need to match the sold job, not a swap this desk invents. Ask the estimate. Do not swap fuel types. Do not size a tankless or a boiler as the engineer. Do not invent a vent diameter. Permit and inspection rows follow their file if they marked the job as needing them. Pull-out and haul-away are sold-scope questions. Serial and install date matter for warranty. Photos of the old and new plates belong if their closeout list says so. Mixing valves and expansion tanks appear only if the sold PDF names them. You do not hold a plumbing license. If the customer asks for a different fuel, that is a new estimate question, not a silent packing-list edit.
