# Water treatment — no health claim

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they sell softeners, RO, or sediment filters, sold equipment and port sizes come from the estimate. Do not claim medical benefits. Do not invent a grain-hardness number. Do not invent a well-depth. Typical packing lists name the unit, bypass, and drain parts if sold. Permits or HOA paper appear only if they marked them. You do not diagnose water as a lab. Test results they already filed may be attached; you do not interpret them as treatment advice. Membership salt or filter changes follow the agreement file. Missing port size is a question. You do not hold a plumbing license and you do not stamp a system as code. If the customer wants a medical outcome, that talk is out of scope; keep the sold name only. Quote the estimate lines and list blanks. Do not invent a treatment dose.
