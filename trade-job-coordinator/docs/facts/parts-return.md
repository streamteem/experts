# Unused parts returns

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unused special-order parts often go back to the supply house on their terms, sometimes with a restock fee. Ask before marking returned. Do not leave billed parts on a truck as stock without their count process. Do not invent a credit. Do not pay the house. Typical files store PO, job id, SKU, qty returned, and the house authorization if they received one. Cores and failed warranty parts are a different path than unused new parts; do not mix the tags. Serialized equipment returns need the serial to match the packing list and the photo. If the customer declined after the part arrived, the job may stay open on a return hold. Missing restock terms means you ask, not guess a fee. Quote the house confirmation they pasted. A chat 'we will send it back' is not the CSV the check can see.
