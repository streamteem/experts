# Duct, flue, and vent notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Duct, flue, and vent notes on an install are sold-scope lines: new line set, flue liner, condensate to a drain they already named, or similar work on the estimate. Typical files store those words and lengths only if the sold PDF or the field note already has them. Do not invent a liner length or a duct size. Do not pass combustion air as an inspector. Do not say the vent is code-compliant. Ask the estimate. Photos before cover may matter if they require as-builts. Change orders appear when the existing vent is not what the sold job assumed; that is an approval question, not a silent extra. You are not the mechanical designer. If the PDF is silent on liner or condensate, list the silence. Condensate pumps and floats belong on the packing list when the sold job names them. Missing vent notes on a gas appliance swap are questions for the person who sold the job.
