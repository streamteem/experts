# Cores, warranties, and failed parts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Failed parts on warrantied equipment often need the old part tagged and returned as a core or as an RMA with serial and install date. Typical office steps include a failed-part tag, photos, the original job id, and a claim number if they already opened one. Ask their RMA path. Do not promise the manufacturer will credit. Do not throw away a tagged failed part if their SOP says keep it. Do not invent a serial to help a claim. Labor warranty and parts warranty clocks differ; quote their policy file if they have one, and do not extend a warranty. Manufacturer portals are logins they run, not this folder. If the packing list and the photo serial disagree, stop. A no-charge callback is not automatically a manufacturer credit. Missing install date is a question, not a date you mint from memory of a season. Keep the failed part on the job pack until they say it shipped.
