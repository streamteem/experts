# Electrical hazards as flags

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sparking, burned smells, and a hot panel are electrical hazard flags the office treats on the emergency path their book already uses. OSHA electrical pages are workplace orientation; this desk does not perform lockout/tagout or energized work and does not design a panel upgrade as the engineer. Do not tell a homeowner to reset a main. Do not specify a breaker size or a service rating as the designer. Ask how they code sparking and burned-smell calls and who they send. You may list 'card on file' for a license if they track it; you never claim an electrical contractor license. Commercial sites may add badging and after-hours shutdown rules; ask the site contact they named. A comfort complaint about a flicker is not automatically the same bucket as sparking; use their words and their priority marks. Missing photos of a burned device stay a closeout or safety-note question, not a diagnosis you write.
