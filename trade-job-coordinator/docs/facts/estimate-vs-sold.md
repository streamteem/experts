# Estimate vs sold job

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An estimate is a priced proposal; a sold job is the same scope after they marked approved or sold and, if they use one, after a deposit rule they already wrote. Typical convert-to-job steps in SMB shops include a signed PDF or a CRM approval flag, a job id, and a schedule question. Finance applications are their vendor; this folder does not log in. Ask what 'sold' means in their file and whether the estimate number stays. Do not schedule an unsold estimate as if it were a booked install. Do not change prices on the estimate. Do not mark sold from a verbal you invented. If the board shows a crew day but the PDF still says estimate, that disagreement is a question. Deposit amounts and refunds are their bookkeeping path, not money this Expert sends. Packing lists for installs follow the sold PDF, not a tech's memory of a usual tonnage. Missing sold mark stays a question.
