# Field extras and change orders

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Field extras are conditions the sold estimate did not cover: hidden rust, a bad disconnect, extra line set, panel capacity, rock in a trench, or similar unforeseen work. Typical shops want a change-order number and an approval before those lines are billed if that is their rule; ask. Do not promise free extras. Do not redesign as the engineer or pick a new tonnage to 'make it work.' Do not treat a tech's memory of the usual as a sold line. Quote the sold PDF, the field note they saved, and whether an authorize method already exists. Parts ordered for an extra ahead of approval need an ask. Pricing follows their book, not a number you invent. If they have no change-order form, you still do not silently add scope. Inspection corrections can look like extras; keep the AHJ list as written and do not argue code. Missing approval on an extra keeps the job incomplete, not closed.
