# Why a job is incomplete

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Incomplete reasons explain why a job is still open: parts, access, approval, permit, weather, or customer not home. Use their reason codes. Do not close as complete to clear the board. Do not blame the customer in the write-up unless their file uses that reason. Typical exports have a reason column and a next-window field. Trip-two and parts-hold rows stay tied to the same job id or a child WO they opened. Photos and packing lists for the return still matter. If two reasons apply, ask which code they want as primary. You do not invent a no-show fee. Inspection pending is not a pass and is not complete. Quote the code and the missing file. A tech who is sure is not a reason code. Missing reason on an open job is itself a question for dispatch. Do not invent a complete to clear capacity.
