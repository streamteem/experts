# Approvals on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Approvals on file are how a typical shop proves a job is sold or an extra is authorized: a signed estimate, a CRM approval flag, or a recorded path they already use, and maybe a deposit. Finance apps are their vendor; no login here. Ask what sold means. Do not mark sold from a verbal you invented. Do not store full card numbers or bank logins. Dollar caps and text-to-approve flows vary; copy theirs. An estimate sitting on the board is not approval. Change orders need the same path they named for extras. Membership add-ons are not free because the customer is on a plan unless the agreement file says so. If two people at the site disagree, you do not pick a signer; you ask who their file treats as the customer of record. Missing approval keeps the install off the sold schedule. Quote the file and the date they stored.
