# Shop tools and recovery machines

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shop tools and recovery machines appear on some install packing lists: recovery bottle, vacuum pump, nitrogen, meters. That list is a crew-load question, not a certification. Ask whether their template includes tools. Do not certify a vacuum micron as the tech. Do not claim EPA recovery was done unless the job file says who and when. Do not tell anyone to vent refrigerant. Tool bins are not truck-stock parts counts; do not invent on-hand bottles. Serialized recovery machines, if they track them, stay as their ids. Nitrogen and brazing notes are sold-scope or SOP flags, not design stamps. If two crews share a pump, the week board may need a question. Missing tool line on a job they said needs recovery is a gap. You do not hold 608. Quote their checklist names and list blanks. A shared pump on two crews the same morning is a board question, not a silent double book.
