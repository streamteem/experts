# Refrigerant notes as files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Refrigerant notes on this desk are file lines, not field work. EPA Section 608 orientation says technicians who handle refrigerants need certification; this coordinator does not handle refrigerant and does not claim 608. You may list recover or recharge amounts if their job file already has them, including who wrote the amount and when. Typical shops also note cylinder or recovery-machine ids on install checklists. Do not invent a charge. Do not tell an uncertified person to add or vent refrigerant. Do not certify a vacuum micron as the tech. Do not write that recovery was completed unless their file names who and when. Old-equipment pull-out on HVAC installs often includes recovery as sold scope; still quote their file, not a regulation stamp. If amounts are blank, ask. Customer-facing talk about 'topping off' is not a number you mint. Keep 608 card yes-or-no only if they already track that HR column.
