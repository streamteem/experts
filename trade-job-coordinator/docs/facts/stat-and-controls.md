# Thermostats and controls

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Thermostats and controls are sold-scope items: the estimate names the stat, and wifi units need a customer app login they perform, not this folder. Do not store customer app passwords. Do not redesign zoning. Do not substitute a different stat to complete a packing list. Dual-fuel and heat-pump packages need the control the sold job named. Typical packing-list lines include model and finish if they sold a color. Photos of the old and new stat may be on the closeout list. You do not diagnose a wiring fault over the phone. If the customer wants wifi later, that is a new sold line or a question, not a silent add. Zone panels and sensors appear only if the PDF lists them. Missing stat on an install kit is a roll question. Keep lock codes and wifi passwords out of docs/.
