# Time window

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A customer window is the arrival block the shop sold, often morning or afternoon or a two-to-four-hour span, not a single clock time. Typical dispatch files store the window, the city, and sometimes a first-available note. Traffic and prior-job overrun are normal in field trades; many offices text updates from their own SMS or CRM product. This Expert does not operate SMS as a product. ETA updates come from them or from the tech they named, not from a map you invent. Do not promise 10:15 because it sounds tight. Do not shrink a sold window to fill a hole on the board. Ask their texting SOP and who is allowed to move a window. If two sheets show different blocks, ask which one the customer was told. Parts promise dates are not customer arrival windows; keep those columns separate. A missed window is an exception only when their note or timestamp says so. Quote their words.
