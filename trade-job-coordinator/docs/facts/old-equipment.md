# Pull-out and disposal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pull-out and disposal is the sold-scope question of what happens to the old unit: recover refrigerant if applicable, haul-away yes or no, scrap metal, and landfill or dump fees. Typical install estimates in US SMB shops say whether haul-away is included. Ask what the sold job includes. Do not dump equipment. Do not claim EPA recovery was done unless their job file says who and when. Do not invent a scrap credit. Cores and failed parts may need to stay tagged. Photos of the old data plate often matter for warranty and for the closeout pack. If haul-away is extra, that is a change-order or a sold-line question, not a free promise. You do not certify disposal as an environmental stamp. Oil tanks and refrigerant bottles have handling notes in their SOP if they have one; ask, do not invent a drop site. Missing haul-away mark stays a gap on the packing list and the write-up.
