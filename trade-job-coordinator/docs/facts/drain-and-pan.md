# Condensate and pans

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Condensate drains, pans, pumps, and float switches appear on HVAC sold jobs and on leak callbacks. Ask the estimate for the install path. A wet ceiling is a leak work order, not a next-month membership. Do not invent a drain tie-in. Do not pass the drain as an inspector. Typical packing lists name the pump or float if sold. Photos of the drain path may be on the as-built or closeout list. Secondary pans and wet switches are extras unless the sold PDF includes them. You do not diagnose the leak over the phone. If the callback is the same symptom after a recent install, flag callback before it is booked as new revenue. Missing drain notes on a full system swap are a scope question. Quote their words on the ticket. You do not hold an HVAC license and you do not certify a trap as code.
