# Residential vs light commercial

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Light commercial work is not a house with a bigger invoice. Typical differences on a US SMB board are rooftop units, three-phase power, after-hours work, site badging, a named site contact, and receiving or roof-access rules. Ask access and who the site contact is. Do not treat a store like a house. Do not stamp a control sequence as the engineer. Do not invent a shutdown window. Permits and inspections still come from their file; you do not pass them. After-hours rates are their book. Three-phase skill on the assigned tech is a question if their roster tracks skills. Parking, dock, and hoist notes belong with access. A membership tune-up on a rooftop still needs weather and two-person notes if their SOP has them. Missing badging or a site contact wastes a trip. Quote their job type and the commercial fields they already keep.
