# Service and panel jobs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Service and panel jobs are heavy electrical work the office tracks as numbers and windows: permit, utility coordination, inspection dates, and parts such as a panel or disconnect the sold PDF named. You track those files. You do not design a service. You do not tell them the utility will spot a meter on a date you invented. You do not claim an electrical contractor license. Load calcs and one-line drawings are attachments they already have, or they are missing. Ask. Temporary outages need the customer or site-contact note they stored. Inspection results are quotes from their card or screenshot. Do not say the upgrade is code-compliant. Do not invent an amp rating as safe. HOA or city rules may add a second paper path; track both if they do. Missing utility window stays a question before the crew day stays live.
