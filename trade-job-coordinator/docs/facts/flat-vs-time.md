# How they price the job

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pricing method is how the shop turns a visit into an invoice: a flat-rate book, time and material, or a mix such as diagnose flat and repair T&M. Typical SMB trade offices keep a book or a matrix in the CRM; this desk does not invent a book price. Ask which method this job already uses. Do not switch a sold flat-rate job to T&M to help a tech who ran long. Do not discount without their rule. Do not rewrite a sold price to hide a parts miss. After-hours multipliers are their book. Membership visits may have a different price path than demand service. If the estimate PDF and the board price disagree, ask. You do not send money and you do not store card numbers. Callbacks flagged no-charge still need their mark, not a courtesy you invent. Quote the method they stored and list missing book lines as questions for the owner or the writer they named.
