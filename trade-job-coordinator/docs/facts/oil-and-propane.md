# Oil and propane notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some shops service oil or propane appliances. Tank levels and fill-company names are their notes. Ask. Do not schedule a nozzle or burner job when the tank is empty if their SOP says check first. Do not fill tanks. Do not invent a tank size or a fuel delivery date. Gas-smell rules still apply to propane; emergency path first. Oil filters and nozzles on a membership list come from the agreement file, not a guessed kit. Permits and vent notes follow the sold job if they marked them. You do not hold a fuel-oil or propane license. Missing tank-level note is a question, not a dispatch as if the tank were full. Quote the fuel type on the estimate so the packing list does not swap gas parts onto an oil job. Keep fill-account passwords out of this folder. You do not diagnose a nozzle as the tech.
