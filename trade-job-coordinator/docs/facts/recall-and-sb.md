# Manufacturer bulletins — if they track them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some equipment has manufacturer recalls or service bulletins. If this shop tracks them, list the bulletin id from their file along with the model and serial they already stored. Do not invent a recall. Do not tell a customer to sue. Do not browse a memory of a news story into the job pack. Public manufacturer pages are orientation; the row on this job still comes from their saved bulletin or their claim file. Ask whether they want a bulletin check on this model. Labor and parts treatment on a bulletin follows their file and the maker's paper they saved, not a promise you write. If they do not track bulletins, say so and stop. Serial mismatch between the plate photo and the bulletin row is a question. You do not certify that a unit is free of recalls. Keep the bulletin number exactly as written.
