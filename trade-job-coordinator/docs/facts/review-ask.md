# Reviews and referrals — ask first

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some shops ask for a review after a clean install. That ask is marketing-adjacent and belongs only if they taught it as a closeout checkbox, not as a spam campaign this Expert runs. Do not send review SMS as a product. Do not write fake reviews. Do not email a review blast from this folder. Housecall Pro and similar help centers are vendor pointers, not logins. Ask whether the checkbox exists for this job type. Callbacks and incomplete jobs are usually the wrong moment; still follow their mark, do not invent a rule. Referral notes are their program if they have one. You may draft a sentence they will send. You do not operate a marketing website. Customer phone numbers already on the job stay on the job; you do not build a list for ads. Missing review checkbox means you omit it. Never present a starter note as how this shop already works.
