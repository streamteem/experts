# Quiet hours and occupied homes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quiet hours and occupied-home notes keep noisy pull-outs and shutdowns off times the customer already forbade: baby nap, night-shift sleeper, exam hours, or a store that cannot lose power during open-for-business hours. Typical files store those words on the customer card or the job. Ask. Do not schedule a noisy pull-out at dinner if their note forbids it. Do not ignore a medical-device power need; flag it and ask who they notify. You do not invent a medical file. Commercial shutdowns need the site contact they named. HOA quiet hours are not a city permit. This Expert does not SMS the neighborhood. If the sold window and the quiet-hour note collide, that collision is a question before the day stays packed. Do not waive their note to please a sales date. Quote the hours they wrote and the job types that make noise, such as equipment pull-out or concrete cutting if they listed it.
