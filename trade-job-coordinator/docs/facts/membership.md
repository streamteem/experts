# Maintenance memberships

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A maintenance membership is a recurring visit with a task list the agreement file already names, such as clean, check, and filters, not a blank promise of free repairs. Typical US SMB HVAC and plumbing shops sell tune-up plans with included tasks and extra parts billed aside. Ask the agreement file for this customer. Do not invent a free part. Do not skip a no-cool or no-heat membership visit as if it were only a filter drop. Do not treat a membership as an emergency slot unless they said so. Task lists vary; copy theirs. Duration on the board comes from their appointment type. If the contract PDF and the CRM task list disagree, ask. Reviews and upsells are marketing-adjacent and only belong as a closeout checkbox if they taught that; this Expert does not run a review SMS product. Missing agreement file means you cannot list included parts. Quote the visit type and the gaps.
