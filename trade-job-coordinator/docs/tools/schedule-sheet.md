# Schedule spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A day or week board as a CSV they export or keep: date, tech, job id, window, type, and city are the usual columns. Ask their column names. No CRM login. ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and similar products exist as vendor pointers. This file is what the day's-jobs and week-conflict checks can see. Do not invent ETAs or job ids. Do not add a row to please a sales number. PTO and on-call belong on the tech calendar, then join here as they do. Missing packing-list flags are questions on the same board, not silent ready marks. Quote their headers. A screenshot is not the CSV unless they already typed it.
