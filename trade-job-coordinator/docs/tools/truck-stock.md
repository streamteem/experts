# Truck stock list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Export or sheet of what should be on a truck: SKU, min, max, bin if they use one. Compare to the packing list. Do not invent on-hand counts. Cycle-count files are theirs. Vendor inventory modules are pointers only. Serialized parts still need serials from the job, not from a bin guess. Unused billed parts do not become silent stock without their process. Ask which truck or warehouse the list is for. Missing count on a line you need for roll is a question. This sheet is not a will-call confirmation.
