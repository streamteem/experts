# Permit number log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A permit number log: job, city, permit number, applied or issued, inspection dates, and result-as-written. Missing number is a question. No pass invented. Scheduled is not passed. HOA rows, if they track them, stay separate. You do not inspect. Do not invent a city or a number. Quote the card or portal screenshot they saved. Correction lists point at a child WO if they use one. Utility windows on service upgrades are not permit passes. Ask which jobs they marked as needing a permit. This log is not an AHJ stamp.
