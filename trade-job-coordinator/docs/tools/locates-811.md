# Locate ticket file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ticket number, valid dates, job id, site. For trench and ground-rod jobs. Do not invent a clear. Do not invent a ticket. Call 811 pages are orientation. Expired tickets are a question before the dig day stays live. Paint photos are not a legal clear. Locates do not replace before-cover photos. You do not call as excavator of record unless they only asked for draft fields. Missing ticket on a job they marked as dig stays a gap. This file is not a utility map you drew.
