# Supply house (pointer)

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Supply websites exist. No login here. They paste order confirmations and invoices as files. Ask which house for this job: many shops use more than one counter. Typical pasted fields are PO, SKU, qty, promised date, and will-call versus delivery. Do not pay the house. Do not change a quoted price. Do not invent an ETA. House account names are labels only. Core and warranty RMAs may ride the same account; keep their codes. If the confirmation and the sold PDF disagree on model, stop. This pointer is not a Ferguson or Johnstone session. Missing confirmation on a special order is a board question.
