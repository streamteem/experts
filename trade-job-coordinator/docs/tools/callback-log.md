# Callback log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A callback log: original job, return job, days since, symptom in their words, and bill or no-charge as they marked. Used to keep callbacks off the new-sale pile. Ask their window definition. Do not admit legal fault. Do not invent no-charge. Same symptom inside their window is a flag even if they later bill it. Labor and parts warranty clocks differ; this log does not extend them. Missing bill mark is a question. Quote their type code. A membership visit is not automatically a callback row.
