# Photo drop for a job

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Before, after, and nameplate photos for a job. Naming: job id and type. Required-missing becomes a closeout question. Ask their list per job type. Do not store customer ID photos. Do not invent an inspection pass from a pretty after shot. As-built and before-cover shots are extra types some shops want. Unreadable plates need another ask, not a guessed serial. This drop is not a medical or IAQ gallery. Quote which required types are present. A missing folder is a gap, not a complete closeout.
