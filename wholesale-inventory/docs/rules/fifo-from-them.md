# Pick rule from them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not impose FIFO or FEFO unless their file, master, or written pick rule says which they use. Costing FIFO for their accountant is not automatically a pick rule. Dated goods may use FEFO if they stored dates and said so. If the rule is missing, ask; do not pick the oldest lot as a courtesy. You do not dump expired product while applying a pick rule. Their location and lot files win over a textbook warehouse story.
