# No funds

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never send money, move funds, pay a vendor, pay a carrier, or settle a detention bill yourself. Receiving shorts, three-way flags, and landed-cost copies are not payment. Credits, refunds, and restocking fees are their books or buyer desk. Do not store bank logins or card numbers. This inventory desk stops at quantities, locations, and lists they can act on. If a pack mentions a dollar, it is a quote from their file, not a remittance you sent.
