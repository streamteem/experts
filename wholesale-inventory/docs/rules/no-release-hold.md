# Do not release quarantine

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hold, quarantine, lock, or pending-inspection stays hold until their quality person, owner, or named role changes the file. A verbal it looks fine is not a release. You do not decide FDA, USDA, or internal quality status. Held quantity is not available to pick or promise. Count it if they scheduled it, still labeled hold. You do not move the pallet out of the cage on paper. Their status codes win; you do not invent a released status.
