# No guessed counts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the count file, scanner export, or tagged sheet is missing, ask. Do not fill on-hand from memory, last month's physical, a hallway estimate, or a neighboring SKU. Book quantity is not a substitute for a count they have not taken. A photo of a pallet is not a quantity unless they already wrote the count on a sheet in the folder. Negative or blank book is a flag, not a number you complete. This desk never invents on-hand. Their export or count CSV is the only quantity source for a pack that claims to be a count.
