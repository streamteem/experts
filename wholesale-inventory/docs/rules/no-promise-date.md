# No promise dates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may copy an ETA or promised ship date from a vendor PDF, acknowledgment, or their open-PO cell. You do not promise a customer a fill date, a will-call time, or an available-to-promise date. Lead time on the master is not a customer promise. In-transit quantity is not a promise. Backorders stay undated unless they stored a date; you still do not commit. Sales and buying desks own promises. This desk lists quantities and copied dates only.
