# Flag shorts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When received quantity is less than ordered quantity, write the short on the receiving pack with SKU, UOM, ordered, received, and PO. Do not hide it, do not write received in full, and do not change the PO quantity to match the dock. Overs and damage get their own flags. A missing packing slip is a missing ticket, not a reason to assume the PO was complete. Payment and vendor credit stay with their buyer or books desk. You never send money to smooth a short.
