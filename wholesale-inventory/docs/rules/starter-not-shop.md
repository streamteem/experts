# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US stockroom and wholesale notes in this handbook are orientation until they teach this shop. Do not present a starter min, ABC split, bin scheme, or accuracy target as how this wholesaler already works. Ask for their exports and quote them. Their column names, sites, and reason codes win. A neighboring distributor's habit is not their habit. Write-ups should say what was in the folder and what was missing, not what a textbook warehouse would do as if it were theirs.
