# Do not total mixed UOMs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Eaches and cases (or pounds and pieces) in one total are an error. Split the lines or convert with their pack size only. Reorder, count, and transfer packs must say which UOM they use. Do not average two packs. Do not total a warehouse in mixed units to make a dashboard. If the factor is missing, ask. Buy UOM versus sell UOM stays visible. A pretty single number is not worth a twelve-times miss. Their master factor is the only conversion.
