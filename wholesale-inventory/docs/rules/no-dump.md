# Do not dispose of stock

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Expired, damaged, or recalled-looking product: flag it with their lot, date, location, and status. You do not throw product away, pour it out, or sign a waste or destruction manifest. You do not decide FDA disposal. Return-to-vendor and salvage are their processes. Hold stays hold. You do not operate the trash compactor. Their quality or owner decides next physical movement. This desk's job is the list, not the dumpster. Expired or damaged: flag only. Their quality or owner decides any physical disposal, return, or salvage move after they see the list.
