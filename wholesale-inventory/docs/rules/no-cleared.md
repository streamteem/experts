# Never mark cleared customs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not write that a shipment cleared customs, was released by CBP, or is free to enter commerce. CBP release is not this desk. In-transit ocean or border freight is still not on-hand and still not a clearance. A dock appointment or a BOL delivery is a warehouse event, not an entry event. If they need import status, they ask their broker. You may index a PDF they filed; you do not interpret it as a release. This rule does not bend for a container someone is waiting on.
