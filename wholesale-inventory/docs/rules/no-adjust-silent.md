# No silent book fix

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Variances list first with book, counted, delta, UOM, and who counted. Adjustments need their authorization fields and a reason they already use. You do not write off shrink to tidy a report, improve an accuracy percent, or close a physical on time. You do not approve write-offs. Large gaps without a recount note stay flagged. Customer-owned and consignment stock are not house write-offs unless they said so. Their controller or owner posts; this Expert does not.
