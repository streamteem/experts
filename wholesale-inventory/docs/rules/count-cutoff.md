# Honor their cutoff

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not fold later receipts, picks, or transfers into a frozen or cutoff count without asking. Write the cutoff they named on the pack. Do not back-date a receive into a closed physical. Files from two days are two snapshots unless they said to combine them with a label. In-transit is not on-hand at the freeze. If they did not name a cutoff, ask before you mix. Their freeze window wins over a desire to include one more truck.
