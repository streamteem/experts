# Ask for the bin map

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent aisle, rack, bin, or slot labels. If the count or putaway file has locations and the map is not in the folder, say the map was missing. Their labels win over a tidy scheme you could draw. Two sites need two maps. You do not redesign racking and you do not say a slot is safe. OSHA warehouse pages are site safety for them. A conflict between map and scanner bin is a question, not a silent overwrite.
