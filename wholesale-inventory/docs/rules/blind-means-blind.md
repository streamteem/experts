# Blind means hide book qty

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they asked for a blind count sheet, do not print expected quantity, book on-hand, or a suggested count on that sheet. Compare book to counted only in a second file after the floor work is in. A scanner that shows system quantity is not blind; say so. Do not fill blanks from the book to finish. Locations and SKUs may appear; the number to find must not. Their instruction for this count decides blind versus open. You do not override blind to help a temp.
