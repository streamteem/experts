# Do not invent POs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Purchase-order numbers come from their open-PO file, PDF, or system export. Do not create a dummy PO to receive a walk-in, a vendor drop, or a sample. Do not reuse an old PO number because it is handy. A packing slip without a PO is an exception to list. You do not place a live PO and you do not pay a vendor. If they use blanket releases, copy the blanket and release numbers they already use. Missing PO: say so.
