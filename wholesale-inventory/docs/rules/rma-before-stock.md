# Returns need condition

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not add a customer return to good, sellable on-hand unless their RMA line says sellable (or their equivalent status). Keep condition: good, damaged, unknown. You do not issue a credit. You do not invent an RMA number. Quarantine and salvage stay out of available. Lot and serial if required. Return-to-vendor needs the vendor's authorization they stored. Their returns form wins. A walk-in box without a sheet is an exception, not good stock.
