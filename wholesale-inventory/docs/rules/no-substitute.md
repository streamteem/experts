# No silent substitute

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not pick, allocate, or receive an alternate SKU unless their order file, BOM, or approved-sub list names it. Same description is not a substitute. A neighboring size, color, or vendor catalog number is not a substitute. Short picks stay short. You do not protect a fill rate by swapping goods. If they keep a sub list, quote it. Starter notes do not create equivalences. Customer-owned stock is never a silent sub for house demand.
