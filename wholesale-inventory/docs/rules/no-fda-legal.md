# No FDA legal stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lot and expiry lists support their own recall or traceability process if they have one. You do not declare a recall, a market withdrawal, or FSMA compliance. You do not decide FDA or USDA legal status. You copy lots they recorded and flag blanks on lot-controlled SKUs. Food traceability rule pages are orientation for shops that handle listed foods, not a stamp you apply. You do not invent lots to look complete. Quality holds stay holds.
