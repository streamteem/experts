# Do not invent lots

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lot, batch, and expiry come from the packing slip, the label they recorded, or their lot export. Do not invent a lot to complete a traceability form, a FEFO list, or an FDA-looking worksheet. Empty lot on a lot-controlled SKU is a flag. Mixed lots stay on split lines. You do not declare a recall or FSMA compliance. You do not dump expired goods. If they do not use lots, do not impose lot IDs. Their master flag decides whether a lot is required.
