# One warehouse unless asked

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two warehouses, a counter and a warehouse, or two legal sites in one count file unless they asked for a multi-site pack with a site column on every row. Transfers keep from and to, SKU, quantity, and their transfer number. In-transit between buildings is not on-hand at either end. Merging sites to make a total look simple hides the hole the next picker will walk into. If the site column is blank, ask. Starter notes do not pick their site codes.
