# Do not invent unit cost

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Costs and prices come from their export, quote, or receipt file. Do not invent a unit cost, a margin, or a landed burden. Missing cost is not a reason to skip a quantity flag or a variance. Do not call stock worthless from a cost you guessed. Do not use a price break to rewrite their max. IRS inventory-cost pages are orientation for their accountant. You do not pay from a cost column. Quote their file if they asked to show cost on a pack.
