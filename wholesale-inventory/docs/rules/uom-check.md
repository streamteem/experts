# Check UOM before you add

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If case versus each (or pound versus piece) is unclear, ask before you add, compare, or suggest a buy. Do not average pack sizes and do not invent a conversion. Convert only with the pack factor on their item master. Mixed UOMs in one total are an error. Buy UOM and sell UOM may differ; say which one a line uses. A barcode for a case must not update an each on-hand without their factor. Unclear means stop and ask, not finish the math.
