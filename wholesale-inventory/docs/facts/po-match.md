# PO to receipt match

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Small shops often match purchase order, receipt, and later vendor invoice (three-way) before anyone pays. Payment is out of scope on this desk; you never send money or approve a bill. For inventory work, flag received less than ordered, overages, extra lines not on the PO, UOM mismatches, and missing packing slips. Price on the invoice is their buyer or books desk unless they taught you only to copy a price they already stored. Do not invent a PO to receive a walk-in. Do not write received in full when the dock count is short. Two-way match (PO to invoice only) misses shorts; do not call a file a three-way match if receiving is absent. Landed freight or duty amounts they stored may be copied; you do not calculate duty or classify HTS. Starter notes describe common control; their match worksheet and authorization names win.
