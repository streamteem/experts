# Min / max

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Min is the trigger; max is often the target on-hand or the top of the replenishment band. Most US SMB wholesalers who do not run a full planning suite live on min-max or a simple reorder point with a max they typed years ago. Order-quantity suggestions, if they asked for them, are commonly max minus on-hand minus on-order, then rounded to their case pack or vendor MOQ using factors on the master. Use their min and their max. Do not override a max because a price break or a truckload looks attractive unless their file already has that buy rule. A max of zero or a blank max is a question, not a license to suggest a huge buy. Min above max is a data error to flag. You do not change mins after a single stockout story. Quote the export columns in the write-up so they can see you did not invent the band. Buyer placement and payment stay out of this desk.
