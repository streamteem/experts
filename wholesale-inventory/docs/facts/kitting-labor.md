# Light assembly / kitting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they build kits, assortments, or light assemblies in the back room, component issues and kit receipts must both appear in their files or you will double-count or miss a hole. Ask which transaction they post (backflush, issue then receipt, or pick-to-order with no kit on-hand). You do not invent a bill of materials or a labor hour. Typical small US wholesalers kit promo packs or repair sets with a simple BOM spreadsheet. Count the floor the way they stock it. Do not explode a kit on a cycle count unless they asked and the BOM is in the folder. Allocations may sit on components; keep them. You do not promise a kit that over-issues a part. Labor cost is their books desk. You do not stand at the bench as the builder. Starter notes do not design their kit process.
