# Open sales orders

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open sales orders are demand from their order export: customer or order number, SKU, open quantity, UOM, requested date if they stored one, and allocation if they use it. You may sit that demand next to on-hand for a reorder or fill conversation. You do not promise fill. You do not change a price, a discount, or a promise date. You do not cancel a line. Typical US wholesalers mix stocked, backordered, and drop-ship lines; keep those statuses. Available versus on-hand matters if they allocate. You do not invent demand to make a dead SKU look alive. A salesperson's email is not an order file until they export it. Credit and collections are not this desk. Quote the order file in the write-up. Starter notes do not become this shop's ATP policy. Backordered versus open-unallocated is their status language if they stored it; do not collapse those into one demand number. You do not invent a customer or an order number to hold stock.
