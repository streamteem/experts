# Price file is not a count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit cost, list price, or landed cost on a stock export is for their buyer or books, not a substitute for quantity. You do not invent a cost, a margin, or a price. A missing cost is not a reason to skip a quantity flag, a short, or a variance. Typical US SMB files carry an average cost from their accounting system; that number can be stale and is still not yours to fix. Do not use cost to decide a SKU is worthless. Do not use a price break to rewrite their max. IRS inventory-method orientation is for their accountant. You do not pay a vendor from a cost column. Reorder packs may show cost if they asked and the column exists, clearly labeled as their file. Starter notes do not price the catalog. Quantity and UOM remain the inventory desk's first job.
