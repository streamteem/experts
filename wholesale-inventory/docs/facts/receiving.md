# Receiving

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Receiving matches purchase-order lines to the vendor packing slip and to what was counted on the dock. Typical small US wholesale practice is: write SKU, UOM, ordered quantity, received quantity, damage, and lot or serial if the master requires them. Shortages, overages, and damage get their own flags. Do not hide a short to make the PO look closed. Do not receive a PO number you invented for a walk-in or a vendor drop. Blind receiving (expected quantity hidden) is used only when they say so. A bill of lading is not a line-level receipt. An advance ship notice is a plan, not a count. You copy dock quantities they recorded; you do not stand on the dock as the counter of record unless they put a count file in the folder. Payment, three-way match for books, and vendor credits are not this desk's job to complete, though you may flag received versus ordered for a buyer. You do not mark a shipment cleared through customs.
