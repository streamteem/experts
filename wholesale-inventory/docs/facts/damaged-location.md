# Damage location

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unsellable, salvage, or return-to-vendor goods belong in a separate bin or status. Do not mix salvage into available or good on-hand. Return-to-vendor needs their vendor's RMA, not a hope that the vendor will take it. Typical US distributors park crushed cases, water damage, and open returns in a damage cage until a buyer or owner decides. Keep condition and reason if they stored them. You do not dump product, sign a waste manifest, or accuse a person. You do not release salvage to a picker. Count the damage location if they scheduled it, as its own lines. Cost and write-off authority are their books desk. OSHA handling notes are site safety for them; you do not certify cleanup. If the damage location is missing from the map but appears on counts, say the map did not list it; do not invent a tidy code.
