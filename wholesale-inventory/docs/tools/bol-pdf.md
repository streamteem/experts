# Bill of lading PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Carrier bill of lading for PRO, trailer, seal if they wrote it, and high-level carton or pallet counts. Not a line-level SKU count and not a customs release. Do not write that a shipment cleared. Line quantity still comes from pack list plus dock count. Missing BOL: say so if they wanted it; you may still match PO to slip to dock. You do not sign as the carrier or run the dock. OSHA dock notes are site safety for them. Copy what is readable; do not invent a PRO.
