# Item master export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Canonical export of SKU, description, base UOM, pack size, barcodes, lot or serial flags, min and max if they store them, preferred vendor, owner or consignment flags, hazmat or SDS pointer, and kit flag. Conversions come from here, not from a receiving guess or a photo of a carton. Do not add a SKU. Do not type a guessed GTIN. Two masters that disagree: quote both and ask. This file is the lock for UOM math, lot required, and buy-versus-sell units. Starter field lists are not their catalog.
