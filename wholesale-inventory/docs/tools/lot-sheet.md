# Lot and expiry sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lot or batch, expiry or best-by if they use dates, SKU, quantity, UOM, and location. Required when their master says lot-controlled. Do not invent a lot or a date. Flag blanks on lot-controlled SKUs. Mixed lots stay split. FEFO or FIFO only if they said which. You do not declare a recall or dump expired goods. FDA FSMA pages are orientation for shops that need them. Their lot export is the traceability file for this desk, not a compliance stamp.
