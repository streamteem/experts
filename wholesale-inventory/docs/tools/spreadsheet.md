# Stock spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stock and working CSVs typically need sku and a quantity column, plus whatever they actually export: on-hand, available, min, max, location, UOM, owner, lot. Their column names win; map them in the write-up rather than renaming their world. Label whether a quantity is book, counted, on-order, or allocated. Do not invent on-hand to fill a blank. Mixed UOMs do not belong in one total. This file is the usual source for reorder and variance compares. Never store passwords in a sheet. Quote the filename and cutoff they used.
