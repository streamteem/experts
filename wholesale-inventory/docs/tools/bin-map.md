# Bin map

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Aisle, rack, shelf, and slot labels they use, as a PDF, CSV, or photo of a posted map. You index putaway and count lines to those labels. Do not redraw the warehouse as truth and do not invent a missing bin. If bins appear on a count and the map is absent, say so. One site per map. OSHA warehouse orientation is site safety; you do not certify racks or operate a lift. Conflict between map and live scan is a question. Their labels win.
