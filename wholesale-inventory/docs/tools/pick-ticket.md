# Pick ticket

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Order export or printed pick CSV with order number, SKU, quantity, UOM, location if they pick by bin, and lot or serial if required. Write shorts. No silent substitute. Quantity cannot exceed the on-hand or available file they provided. Holds and damage locations are not pick faces. You do not promise a ship date or change a price. Drop-ship lines stay off the warehouse pick. Their wave or single-order format wins. Missing on-hand: say stock was not confirmed.
