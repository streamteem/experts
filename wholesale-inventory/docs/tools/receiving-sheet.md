# Receiving sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dock worksheet or CSV with PO, SKU, UOM, ordered, received, damage, overage, lot or serial if used, and location if they put away on the same sheet. Shorts stay visible. Do not invent a PO. Blind receiving hides ordered quantity only when they asked. Packing-slip and dock count both belong in the story; ASN is a plan. You do not mark customs cleared. You do not pay. Their sheet columns win. Missing slip or missing count: say which file is absent.
