# PO and packing-slip PDFs

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor purchase-order and packing-slip PDFs they drop are the line source for expected SKU, quantity, and UOM, and sometimes lot. Match lines; do not invent a PO number or a missing slip. A bill of lading is not a pack list. Photo of a torn slip: quote readable text and list the rest as unreadable. Vendor SKU versus internal SKU needs their map. You do not place the PO or pay from the PDF. Missing ticket: say so on the receiving pack. Customs language on a commercial invoice is not your clearance.
