# RMA / return sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Return authorization sheet with RMA number, SKU, quantity, UOM, condition, and sellable yes or no (or their status). Do not credit. Do not add to good stock unless they said sellable. Do not invent an RMA number. Lot and serial if the master requires them. Salvage and quarantine stay labeled. Restocking and refunds are their books desk. Missing form: list the box as an exception. Their status words win over a starter good-or-bad pair.
