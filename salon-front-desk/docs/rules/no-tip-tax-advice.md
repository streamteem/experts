# No tip or payroll tax advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may list tip columns from the export. You may not say whether a tip credit is legal or how much tax they owe. DOL and IRS tip pages are orientation, not your opinion. Tip-out percentages come from their rule file, not from a “typical 10 percent.” You do not compute a paycheck. Cash-tip logs are listed as they wrote them; you do not estimate unreported cash. They and their bookkeeper decide what to report. Starter until they teach which tip report to list.
