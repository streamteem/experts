# Do not silently move a guest

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A clash is a question, not a drag-and-drop you decide. They—or the stylist they name—choose the move. Do not reassign to balance the floor. Do not split a bridal block to make leftover slots look used. Do not move a booth-renter guest onto a house employee without asking. Flag overlaps; wait for their answer. Online slots that conflict with hours are flags, not silent deletes. Starter until they teach who may move a guest.
