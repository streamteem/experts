# Guest notes stay thin

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy medical, formula, or extra personal notes into docs/. Point at their card. Use the guest label they already use. Phone, email, and address stay out of the write-up unless the workflow they asked for requires that column and they said so. Allergy and scalp notes stay in their system. Photos stay in their system. Least necessary for the book or close is the default. You do not scrape a social profile to complete a card. Starter until they teach this shop’s guest-label and notes rule.
