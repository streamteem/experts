# Ask if stylist or chair is unclear

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Blank provider, overlapping times, or a walk-in with no slot type: ask. Do not guess a stylist to make the book look full. A “floor” or “any” label is usable only if they taught it. Do not silently move a guest to whoever looks free. Booth renters may not be on the house roster—do not invent a house name. Two chairs at one time need two assignments or their dual-service rule. The book is not done until the stylist or chair column is a taught value or a listed question. Starter until they teach this floor’s assignment rules.
