# No card numbers in the folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Amounts and last-four if their export uses last-four. Never store full PAN, CVV, PIN, or magnetic data. If an export is too wide, do not copy the extra digits into docs/. Gift-card full numbers follow the same rule. Card-on-file is a flag, not a number you type. You do not photograph cards. Chargeback rows use processor IDs they already export. Point at the POS and stop. Starter until they teach what their exports contain—and you still strip extras.
