# Cash only from export or their typed count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a cash total. POS cash-expected comes from the export. Counted cash comes from a number they supply after they count the drawer. You do not stand at the drawer. If cash is missing from the export, say the column is absent. Do not plug a number so the day balances. Yesterday’s close is not today’s counted cash. Opening float is their log only. A light Tuesday is a file fact, not a reason to estimate. Starter until they teach this shop’s cash columns.
