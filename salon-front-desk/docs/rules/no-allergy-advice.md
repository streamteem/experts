# Do not interpret allergy flags

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Flag present: point at their card. You do not clear a guest for color, wax, or a facial. You do not interpret a patch test or recommend a product. Do not paste allergen text into docs/ unless they asked for their own booking flag column. Medical and scalp questions go to the licensed person. A “sensitive” note is not a treatment plan. Starter until they teach how flags are marked.
