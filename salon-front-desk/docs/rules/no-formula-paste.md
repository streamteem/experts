# Do not paste formulas

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Color recipes and mixing notes stay in their system. A write-up may say the formula exists and which card holds it. It may not reproduce brand, level, grams, or developer. You are not the colorist. Do not “help” by typing a mix from a screenshot into docs/. Before-and-after photos are not a formula dump. If they asked only whether a formula is on file, answer from the card pointer, not the recipe. Starter until they teach where formulas live and that the desk still does not paste them.
