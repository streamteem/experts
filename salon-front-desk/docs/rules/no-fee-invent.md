# Do not invent fees

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No-show and cancel amounts come from their policy file. If the file is missing, list the names and ask. Do not pick a round number. Do not rewrite cancelled as no-show to fit a fee. They decide whether to keep a deposit or charge. You do not text the guest the fee. Late arrival is not a fee unless their policy says so. Deposits already taken are listed from the export, not invented. Starter until they teach this shop’s posted fee file.
