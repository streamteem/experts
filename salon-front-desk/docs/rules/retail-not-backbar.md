# Do not mix retail and backbar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their sheet splits retail and backbar, keep the split. A backbar empty bottle is not a retail sale. Match on SKU and size, not the pretty brand name. Do not transfer a unit in the pack unless their file already shows the transfer. Reorder retail from the retail par; restock backbar from the usage or backbar sheet unless they use one combined file and said so. Commission and tax often follow the retail column only. Starter until they teach this floor’s bins.
