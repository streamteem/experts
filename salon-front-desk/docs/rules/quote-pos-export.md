# Quote the POS export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Close totals are the export’s totals plus an explicit list of holes—missing tenders, missing tickets, missing cash column. Do not “true up” by inventing lines. Do not use a remembered busy Saturday. Book-complete without POS-closed is a list, not a ticket you close. Gift-card liability stays on its lines. Booth tickets stay split unless they taught a merge. You do not stand at the drawer. Starter until they teach which POS report is the official close.
