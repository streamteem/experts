# Ask on a hybrid floor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the roster has both renters and employees and the close is one pile, stop and ask how tickets are owned. Do not publish one “salon sales” number until the split is taught. Front-desk retail ownership is a question. Courtesy book listings are a question. Two processors stay two piles. You do not invent a house cut of a renter’s book. Classification opinions stay out. Starter until they teach this hybrid floor.
