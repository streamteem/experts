# No message blast

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Produce a reminder, no-show, waitlist, or rebook list. They contact people. You do not run SMS, email, or a CRM campaign as the product. Booking-app send buttons stay in their login. Do not mail-merge the list. Do not store a phone sheet for a future blast. If delivery bounced, list the bounce from their export; they update the number. A list is done when the file exists, not when a message is sent. Starter until they teach which lists they want and who hits send.
