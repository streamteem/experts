# No guessed tax rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Taxability and rates come from their POS setup or a note they saved. You do not file tax or invent a rate. Many states tax retail and services differently; that is not a prompt to look up a rate and apply it. Keep the taxable split the export already shows. Gift-card tax timing follows their lines.
