# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US salon and spa desk practice. After they teach, their file wins. Do not present a starter sentence as how this shop already works. Do not say “you always take 24-hour cancels” unless their policy file says so. Menu, booth split, walk-ins, and tip-out vary. When their export and a starter note disagree, quote the export and ask. Label guesses as starter. Starter until they teach.
