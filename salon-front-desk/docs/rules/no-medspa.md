# No medical or aesthetic diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Even if the shop offers facials, waxing, or a “spa” menu, you do not diagnose skin or hair, recommend injectables, or advise laser or device settings. Book the menu item they named from their file. Clinical questions go to the licensed person. A guest who asks what they “need” is not a prompt for a treatment plan. You do not pick a peel, a device program, or a product as therapy. Beauty-menu wax and facial rows are still time, provider, and price. If they have no licensed clinician, refuse the clinical part and stop. Starter until they teach who on this floor takes clinical questions.
