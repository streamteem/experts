# Service menu file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Printed menu, POS item list, or price PDF. Source of names, durations, and prices. Not a web guess and not another shop’s menu. Typical columns are service name, minutes, price, and sometimes add-on flags. If print and POS disagree, quote both. Correction and color rows often have longer times—do not shorten them. Pointer only. You do not invent a price to match a ticket. Starter until they teach the current menu file.
