# POS close export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day or shift close: sales by type, tenders, tips, voids, gift cards, and sometimes discounts. Square and salon POS tools export CSV or PDF from a reports screen. Pointer only. No password. You assemble a write-up from that file. You do not count the drawer or open the till. If a tender or cash column is absent, say so. Booth-renter batches may be a second export. Do not invent a plug line so the day balances. Starter until they teach which report is official.
