# Retail vendor files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Distributor catalogs and last invoices are their PDFs—SalonCentric, CosmoProf, a brand educator’s order sheet, or a local beauty supply. SKU, minimum order, pack size, and deal dates come from those files. You draft an order list; they send it. You do not place the order or pay the house account. Desks keep last invoice and catalog so a reorder is the size they actually buy, not a website guess. Match retail SKU to the vendor line; pretty names collide. Backbar cases may be a different vendor file. Do not invent a case pack. Do not store a vendor login. If the PDF is stale, say the date on the file and ask whether it is still the buy file. Starter until they teach this shop’s vendors. Education kits and deal dates expire—quote the date on the PDF. House-account terms are not your payment job. If two distributors list the same pretty name, match SKU. Backbar cases may need a second PDF. You still do not log in or pay.
