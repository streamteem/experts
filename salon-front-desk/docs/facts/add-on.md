# Add-on services

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gloss, trim, brow, toner, treatment, or paraffin add-ons change duration and price. Take add-ons from the ticket or menu file, not from a guess that “color guests always get a gloss.” Desks keep add-ons as separate lines so the book’s minutes and the POS dollars match. If checkout added a service the morning book did not have, list the mismatch; do not back-fill the morning book as if it had been there all day. Typical files are the POS ticket export and the menu item list. Upsell talk is theirs at the chair; your pack only records what was sold or booked. Do not add a complimentary add-on in the write-up to soothe a complaint. If an add-on has no menu name, ask—do not invent a SKU. Starter until they teach which add-ons this floor books versus which are sold only at checkout.
