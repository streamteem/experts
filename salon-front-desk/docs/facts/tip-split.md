# Tips and tip-out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Card tips usually appear on the POS export; cash tips may be a separate log the stylists write. Tip-out to assistants, shampoo, or a pool, if they use one, comes from their rule file. You can list tip columns the export already has. You do not give FLSA tip-credit or tax advice. They and their bookkeeper decide what to report. IRS tip topics and DOL tipped-employee sheets are orientation only—you do not apply them. Desks keep tip lists so Friday cash-out is not a fight from memory. Typical fields are provider, card tip, cash tip if logged, and tip-out if their file has a formula. Do not invent a pool percentage. Do not move a tip from one name to another to “fix” fairness. Do not store a guest’s card number next to a tip line. Starter until they teach this shop’s tip-out rule, if any.
