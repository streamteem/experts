# Waitlist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A waitlist is a list of people who wanted a time that was gone. Produce the list from their export. They call or text. Do not treat a waitlist row as a confirmed appointment, and do not message the list as a product. Desks keep a waitlist so a cancellation can be offered in the order they already use. Typical columns are requested date or window, service, preferred stylist, and a guest label. Some apps auto-offer; your pack still does not send. A waitlist person who “said they would take anything” is still not on the book until their process confirms. Keep the waitlist file separate from confirmed appointments. Do not invent a priority score. Do not drop a waitlist name onto an open chair to look helpful. Starter until they teach how this floor orders and contacts the waitlist.
