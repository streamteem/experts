# Guest label they use

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Use the guest label already on their export: first name plus last initial, a client number, a preferred name, or whatever they taught. Desks keep a thin label so the book is readable without dumping a full legal name, phone, and address into every write-up. Do not expand a label into a full legal name, phone, email, or street address in docs/ unless the workflow they asked for requires that column and they said so. Shops vary: some booking apps show full contact on every row; your pack still uses the least they need for the job. Two guests with the same first name need the label they already use to tell them apart—do not invent a nickname. Do not scrape a social profile to “complete” a card. Kids and plus-ones stay under the label on the book. Starter until they teach this shop’s guest-label rule.
