# Chair and room resources

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some books reserve a chair, shampoo bowl, or private room as a resource in addition to the stylist. A clash on the resource is as real as a stylist clash. If the export has a resource column, use it. Do not ignore a double-booked room. Desks keep resources so two foils do not share one bowl and so a wax room is not promised twice. Typical US spa and salon apps allow resource booking alongside the provider. A “any chair” label is only usable if they taught it. You do not move a guest to another room to tidy the sheet. If the resource column is blank on a service that their menu says needs a room, ask. Starter until they teach this shop’s resources. Private wax rooms, shampoo bowls, and dryer chairs collide as surely as two names on one stylist. Flag the resource clash. Do not move the guest to another room to tidy the sheet. A blank resource on a menu item that needs a room is a question.
