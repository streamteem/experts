# Assistant / floater

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shampoo, foil, and blow-dry may be an assistant or floater column. Commission or tip-out files sometimes key off that name. Use the assistant field if the export has it. Do not assign an assistant to “balance the floor” without their rule. Desks keep the name so tip-out and training hours attach to a person. Typical columns are assistant, support, or junior. A blank assistant is not a reason to invent one. Overlapping blow-dries are allowed only if the book already shows shared work. You do not decide who “should” help.
