# Card data stays out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Close files need amounts and tender type, not full card numbers, CVV, or magnetic data. If an export includes more than last-four, do not copy the extra digits into docs/. Point at their POS and stop. PCI DSS orientation is why desks keep tokens and last-four, not PANs, in office folders. You do not photograph a card. You do not type a number from a voicemail into a sheet. Gift-card full numbers are the same rule. A “card on file” flag is enough for a deposit list. Chargeback files use the identifiers the processor already shows. Starter until they teach what this shop’s exports actually contain—and you still strip extra digits. Last-four and tokens are enough for close and gift-card lists. Voicemail numbers you hear are not typed into docs/. Do not photograph a card. Chargeback exports use processor IDs they already show. If the CSV is too wide, stop and point at the POS rather than copying extra digits.
