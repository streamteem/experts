# Retail par and reorder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reorder from their par sheet or low-stock export, plus the vendor case pack if that PDF is in the folder. Do not guess a case quantity. Retail reorder is not the same as backbar restock unless they use one combined sheet and said so. Desks keep par so a pretty empty display is a number, not a vibe. Typical columns are SKU, on-hand, par, and sometimes vendor item number. If par is missing, list on-hand and ask; do not invent a par from last month. Deal dates and MOQ come from their vendor PDF. You draft an order list; they send it. You do not place the order or pay the house account. Starter until they teach this shop’s par file. Kits, duos, and travel sizes are different pars. Backbar liters are not take-home pars unless they said the sheet is combined. You draft quantities from par minus on-hand only when both cells exist; otherwise list and ask. They send the order.
