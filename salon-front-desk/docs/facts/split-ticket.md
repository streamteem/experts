# Split tickets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Couples and families may split tenders or put retail on one ticket and service on another. Keep the ticket IDs the export shows. Do not merge tickets to make the day look like one payment. Desks keep split IDs so gift cards, tips, and tax lines stay attached to the payment that actually happened. Typical cases are two cards, cash plus card, or one person paying another’s service. You do not reassign a tip to tidy the split. You do not store full card numbers. If the export shows a split but the summary does not, say the summary is incomplete.
