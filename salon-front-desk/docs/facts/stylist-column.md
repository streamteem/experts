# Stylist / provider column

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The book is not done if the stylist or chair column is blank on a confirmed service. Ask. A “floor,” “any,” or “next available” label is only usable if they taught that as a real assignment rule. Desks keep a named provider so commission, tips, and the guest’s request stay attached to the right person. Booth renters may not appear on the salon employee roster; do not invent a house stylist for their guest. Typical columns are stylist, assistant, and sometimes chair or room. A request for “whoever does blondes” is not an assignment until a name is on the row. If two stylists share a guest—colorist plus cutter—use the columns the export already has; do not collapse them into one name to tidy the sheet. You do not move a guest to balance the floor. Starter until they teach this shop’s assignment labels.
