# Opening float

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Opening float is a number they set—the cash that should be in the drawer at open. Record it only from their log. Do not assume yesterday’s close cash is today’s float. Desks keep the float so a morning variance is against a known start, not against a hope. Typical files are a drawer log or a close envelope note. You do not count the drawer. You do not change the float in the pack to make last night’s overage disappear. If the log is blank, say the float is not on file. Card tips and gift-card sales are not float. Starter until they teach this shop’s float log. Some shops reset to a fixed float; some leave last night’s cash. Only their log says which. You do not count. You do not change the float in the pack to hide an overage. Card and gift-card activity are not float. If the log is blank, say so.
