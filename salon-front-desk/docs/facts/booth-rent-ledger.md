# Booth rent ledger

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Booth rent—weekly or monthly—is usually a separate ledger from service sales. If they want a rent-due list, use their rent sheet. You do not invoice rent as if you were the landlord’s billing product, and you do not decide whether a renter is “really” an employee. Desks keep the ledger so Friday rent is not mixed into Saturday retail. Typical columns are provider, period, amount due, amount received as they recorded, and a note they wrote. You do not collect rent or move money. A late rent flag in their sheet is a list item; you do not lock a chair. Hybrid floors may charge booth plus a desk fee—quote their sheet only. Starter until they teach this shop’s rent file. Weekly versus monthly periods stay as the sheet’s period. Partial weeks and deposits on the chair are only what they recorded. You do not lock a station or send a demand letter. Late flags are list items, not legal findings.
