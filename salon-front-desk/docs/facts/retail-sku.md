# Retail SKU and on-hand

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Retail counts use their SKU, size, and on-hand from a count sheet or POS inventory export. Par or reorder points come from that sheet if present. Do not guess a bottle count from last week’s memory. If backbar and retail share a brand name, match on SKU, not on the pretty name. Desks keep SKU-level counts so a 300 ml take-home is not confused with a backbar liter and so commission and tax attach to the right item. Typical files are a Square or Vagaro inventory CSV, a handwritten count photo in work/, or a SalonCentric-style item list they saved. Units matter: each, duo, and kit are different rows. If on-hand is blank, say the cell is blank. Do not invent a par. A zero on the take-home display is a count they recorded, not a reason for you to place an order. Starter until they teach this shop’s retail SKU file.
