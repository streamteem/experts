# Late guest

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Grace minutes and when a late guest is treated as a no-show come from their policy. List actual arrival versus start if the export has it. Do not decide to cancel the remaining service to punish lateness. Desks keep grace so the next guest is not silently crushed and so the desk does not invent a punishment. Typical files are the policy card and a status or arrival timestamp on the book export. Some shops shorten the service; that is the stylist’s or manager’s call, not a line you write as done. You do not text the guest a warning as the product. A late bridal party still follows the event sheet—ask before splitting the block. Starter until they teach this shop’s grace minutes. Arrival timestamps, if exported, are listed next to start. Shortening the remaining service is the stylist’s or manager’s call, not a line you mark done. You do not punish and you do not text a warning as the product. Weather waivers are theirs to grant.
