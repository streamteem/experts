# Cancellation window

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical posted windows are twenty-four hours or forty-eight hours; this shop’s number is the one in their file. Same-day cancels and no-shows may be coded differently in the software. Use their status labels. Do not rewrite a “cancelled” row as a no-show to make the fee story tidier. Desks keep the window so the floor can refill a chair and so guests hear one rule. Typical files are the posted policy PDF and the booking status export. Some shops waive the window for weather or illness; that waiver is theirs to grant, not yours to assume. Online cancel buttons may use a different clock than the desk’s verbal rule—quote both if they disagree and ask. You do not text the guest a countdown. If the window file is missing, list the status as exported and ask. Starter until they teach this shop’s cancel clock.
