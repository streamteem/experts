# Card disputes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their processor export flags a dispute, list the ticket ID and amount from that file. They and the processor handle the case. Do not email the guest about a chargeback, and do not store a full PAN. Desks keep a dispute list so a missing Tuesday sale has a reason in the processor file, not a guess. Typical fields are date, amount, ticket or last-four, and status as the processor labeled it. You do not refund as the product. You do not coach them on representment language as counsel. PCI practice: amounts and identifiers they already export, never magnetic data. A friendly “billing question” call is theirs. Starter until they teach where this shop’s dispute export lives. Ticket ID, amount, and processor status are enough. Do not email the guest. Do not store a PAN. Representment language is theirs and the processor’s. A friendly billing call is theirs. List the flag; stop. PCI still applies to any extra digits on the export.
