# Consult vs service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A consult, patch test, or quote visit may be a different book type than the paid service. Do not convert a consult row into a full color on the book. If they require a patch-test wait, that wait comes from their policy file, not from a guessed hour count. You still do not interpret the test. Desks keep the type so a fifteen-minute consult does not become a three-hour correction without a new slot. Typical labels are consult, quote, patch, and service. You book the named type they chose. A “just looking at the color” walk-in is still not a service until they book it. You do not clear a guest for color. Starter until they teach this shop’s consult types and any wait they posted. Quote visits, patch tests, and paid services keep their book types. You do not convert a consult into a full color to fill a hole. Patch-test waits come from their policy file. You do not interpret the test or clear the guest. They book the named type.
