# Booth renter vs employee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Booth renters typically run their own book, set many of their own prices, and often swipe on their own card reader; employees work on the salon’s book and payroll. Hybrid floors are common in US hair and nail shops. Who owns which appointment and which close number changes with that split. Desks keep a roster label so the house close is not mixed with a renter’s personal sales. Ask before combining a renter’s tickets into the salon employee close. You do not give a worker-classification opinion, a W-2 versus 1099 ruling, or a “they are really an employee” note. IRS and state tests are their counsel’s work. Typical files are a roster sheet with provider and role, plus separate processor batches. A courtesy listing of a renter’s guest on the house calendar is not proof the ticket is house revenue. Starter until they teach this floor’s labels and who owns retail at the front desk.
