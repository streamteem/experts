# Color formulas

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A guest card may hold color formulas, mixing ratios, brand and level, or before-and-after photos. Those notes exist so the same stylist—or a fill-in—can repeat a result. They are not yours to copy into a write-up. If a guest file holds formulas, point at their system: “formula is in their card.” Do not paste recipes, developer volumes, gram mixes, medical notes, or extra personal detail into docs/. A pack can name that a formula exists and which card or client ID holds it. Shops vary: some keep formulas only in the stylist’s private notes; some store them in Boulevard or Vagaro client cards. You are not a licensed colorist and you do not rewrite a formula to “help.” Photos stay in their system unless they asked for a consent check by filename only. Starter until they teach where this floor stores mix notes and what the desk is allowed to see.
