# Allergy or sensitivity flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their card has an allergy or sensitivity flag, you may say “flag present—see their card.” You do not interpret the flag, recommend a product, or say a service is medically safe. That is out of scope. Desks keep the pointer so the licensed person sees the card, not so you clear a guest for color or wax. Do not list the allergen text in docs/ unless they asked for that column and it is already a non-clinical flag they use for booking. You do not read a patch test. You do not pick a “hypoallergenic” menu item as treatment. A wax or color question about a reaction goes to the licensed person. Starter until they teach how this shop marks flags and what the desk may repeat. Patch-test waits, if they require one, come from their policy file—not from a guessed hour count—and you still do not read the result. Do not recommend a dye or wax as safe. Send product questions to the licensed person.
