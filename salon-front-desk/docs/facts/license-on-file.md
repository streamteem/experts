# Licenses on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stylist and shop licenses are their documents. You may list expiration dates if they keep a tracker. You do not practice on a license, renew a license, or say the floor is cleared by the board. Desks keep a tracker so a lapsed card is a date on a list, not a legal opinion. Typical columns are provider, license type as they labeled it, number if they store it, and expiration. You do not call the board. You do not work under their cosmetology license. A missing card in the folder is “not in the tracker,” not “unlicensed” as a finding. Booth renters often hold their own licenses—list only what their file shows. Starter until they teach this shop’s license tracker. Shop license and personal licenses may be two rows. Expiration is a date from the tracker, not a finding that the board cleared the floor. You do not practice, renew, or call the board. Missing from the folder is “not on the tracker.”
