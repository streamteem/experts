# Deposits at booking

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some shops take a deposit or a card on file for long services, new guests, bridal parties, or Saturday color. Amounts and which services require them come from their policy or booking export. You list who paid a deposit and what the export shows remaining. You do not charge a card, capture a card, or keep a card number in this folder. Desks keep deposit rows so a no-show conversation has a file behind it. Typical fields are amount, date taken, method as a tender type, and whether it applies to a future ticket. Square and salon apps often show a hold or a paid deposit separately from the final ticket. Do not invent a remaining balance. Do not treat a card-on-file flag as permission for you to run a fee. PCI practice: amounts and last-four if they export last-four, never a full PAN. Starter until they teach this shop’s deposit rules.
