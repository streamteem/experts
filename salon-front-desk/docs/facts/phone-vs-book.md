# Phone list is not the book

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Voicemail and Instagram requests are intake, not confirmed appointments. Log them on a request list if they asked. The book changes only when their process says the slot is confirmed. You do not text the request list as a marketing blast. Desks keep intake separate so a DM is not a Saturday chair. Typical fields are received time, requested service or window, guest label, and channel. Pending online widget rows are also not the book until confirmed. Do not write a request onto the day’s book to fill a gap. They contact the person. Starter until they teach this shop’s request-to-confirm path. DMs, voicemail, and walk-up “are you free” stay on the request list until their process confirms a row. You do not text the request list as a blast. Pending widget rows are the same rule. Filling a gap is not a reason to write a request onto the day’s book.
