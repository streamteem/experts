# Double-book risk

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Two confirmed services on the same stylist at the same start, or two guests assigned to one chair resource, is a clash. Flag it. Ask which one moves. Do not silently reassign to “whoever is free.” Desks keep clash flags so a Saturday color and a cut do not land in one chair. Assistants and overlapping blow-dries are allowed only if their book already shows those as shared or assistant columns. Some floors double-book on purpose during process time; that is a taught rule, not a default you assume. A family that wants two chairs at once is two resources, not one stylist unless their book already shows a dual service. Online widgets sometimes allow overlaps the desk never intended—flag those against the hours and resource columns. You do not pick the guest who “matters less.” Starter until they teach this floor’s overlap rules.
