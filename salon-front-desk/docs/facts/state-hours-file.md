# Posted hours and holidays

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Book only inside the hours and holiday list they keep. Do not assume the shop is open Sunday or the day after a holiday. If online booking shows a slot the hours file says is closed, flag the conflict. Desks keep hours so a widget cannot sell a chair on a day the floor is dark. Typical files are a weekly hours sheet plus a holiday list. Booth renters may keep different personal hours—ask before treating the house hours as theirs. Early colorists and late nail desks may differ by station; use the file they gave, and ask if the book has a provider-level hours column. You do not invent a holiday close.
