# No-show and cancel

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cancellation windows, deposits, and no-show fees live in a policy file or the booking software’s posted rule, not in a round number you pick to sound professional. Desks keep the policy so long color blocks and Saturday slots are protected and so guests see the same rule the desk applies. You list who cancelled or did not arrive from their book export, using their status labels. Typical files are a printed card, a Vagaro or Boulevard policy page they saved, or a note in the booking settings. They decide whether to keep a deposit or charge a fee. Same-day cancels and no-shows may be coded differently; do not rewrite a cancelled row as a no-show to make the fee story tidier. Do not invent a dollar amount, do not text the guest about the fee, and do not treat a late arrival as a no-show unless their policy file says so. If the policy file is missing, list the names and ask. Starter until they teach this shop’s posted rule.
