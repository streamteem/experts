# They count the drawer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Opening float and closing cash are physical counts they do. Your close pack can show POS cash-expected from the export next to a blank they fill after the count. Never write a counted-cash number you did not receive as a file or a number they typed. Desks keep the blank so a missing twenty is their count, not your invention. Typical practice is a close envelope or a drawer log with opener, closer, and variance they compute. You do not stand at the drawer, do not “help” by estimating cash from a busy book, and do not force the day to balance. If the export has no cash-expected column, say so. Yesterday’s close cash is not automatically today’s float. Starter until they teach this shop’s drawer log. Over and short are their subtraction after they count. You may show POS cash-expected beside a blank. Do not move a tip jar into the drawer total. Do not treat Venmo as cash unless their export already coded it that way.
