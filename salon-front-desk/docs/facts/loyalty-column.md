# Loyalty points

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Loyalty balances live in their software. You may list a points column if they exported it. You do not invent a promotion or message the list as a campaign. Desks keep the column so a “I have points” conversation has a file, not a remembered punch card. Typical fields are guest label, points or visit count, and expiry if they store it. Do not add bonus points in the pack to fix a complaint. Do not blast a “double points weekend.” A membership inclusion is not loyalty unless their export treats it that way. If the column is absent, say so. Starter until they teach this shop’s loyalty export. Punch cards photographed into work/ are a file if they said so; you still do not add bonus punches. Expiry dates come from the export. Do not invent a promotion weekend. Membership inclusions are a different file unless they treated them as points.
