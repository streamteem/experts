# Kids and guardian policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Whether a minor can sit without a guardian, and which services are offered, comes from their posted policy. You list the rule from that file. You do not invent a consent form or give medical advice about a child’s hair or skin. Desks keep the policy so a drop-off is their rule, not a guess. Typical files are a lobby card or a booking note. Chemical services on minors may be restricted in their policy—quote it; do not add a medical reason. Photo consent for kids follows their file. You do not diagnose a scalp.
