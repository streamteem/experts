# End-of-day checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical close includes a POS export, a drawer count they perform, a retail glance, tomorrow’s book on screen or printed, and sometimes a sanitation or door-check they initial. Your pack is the file side. Their checklist file, if any, tells you which reports they expect attached. Desks keep a checklist so a closer does not skip gift-card activity or tomorrow’s first color. Do not invent a cash total to finish the list. Do not mark a physical task done unless their file says they did it. If the checklist names a report you do not have, list it as missing. Opening tasks are a different list. Starter until they teach this shop’s end-of-day sheet. Tomorrow’s first color, gift-card activity, and a named exception report are typical attachments if their checklist lists them. Mark a physical lock or trash task only if their file says they did it. Missing named reports are listed as missing, not invented.
