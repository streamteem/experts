# Close-out from POS export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day close numbers come from their POS or booking-software export, not from a remembered busy day. Typical US salon closes list service sales, retail sales, tips, gift-card sales and redemptions, voids, comps, and tender—cash, card, and other. You assemble a write-up from that file so the owner or bookkeeper can see the day without you standing at the till. Square, Vagaro Payments, Boulevard, and similar tools export CSV or PDF from a reports screen. You do not count the drawer, open the till, or invent a cash total because the export looks light. If cash is missing from the export, say the column is absent. Gift-card liability and service revenue are often different lines; keep them as the export shows. Booth-renter tickets may belong on a separate pile—ask before combining. Do not “true up” a short day by adding a plug line. Starter until they teach which report is the official close for this shop.
