# In-house CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The in-house CSV is who is already in a room: room number, guest label, type, departure date, stayover versus due-out. You do not invent occupancy. Status clashes with the room-status sheet stay quoted. No folio PAN belongs on this sheet. This tool feeds house-count, due-out, and breakfast-count work when they asked. Stayover versus due-out must stay their codes. Room moves show the current room if the export already moved them. Breakfast-count and departure workflows read this file after they drop it.
