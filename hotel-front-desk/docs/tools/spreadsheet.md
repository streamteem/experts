# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build from their exports: arrivals, house count, exceptions. Columns stay their names plus flags you labeled as flags. You do not fill money columns from memory. Checks look for file-exists or named columns on work files. This tool is the usual pack table before the write-up. Work CSVs live under work/; write-ups under out/. Do not paste PAN or passwords into cells. This is the usual last tool on a pack after the source files are listed and checked.
