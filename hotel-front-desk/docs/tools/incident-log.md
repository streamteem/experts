# Incident log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The incident log is their dated operational notes: noise, maintenance, alleged theft, walk, skipper. You copy what they wrote. No legal conclusion, no invented comp, no PAN. Min-necessary only. This tool supports exception write-ups when an incident explains a room move or a folio flag. They handle the guest. Date, room, code, and open-versus-closed if they use those columns. Walk, skipper, and room-move write-ups may cite a row already there. You add no legal conclusion and no extra identity scan.
