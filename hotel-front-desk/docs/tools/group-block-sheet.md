# Group block sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The group sheet holds group name, nights, types, contracted qty, pickup, cutoff, rate code from their file, and routing. You do not invent wash or a group rate. Blank cutoff is an ask. This tool feeds block-versus-pickup packs. They release rooms. Checks may look for group and pickup columns they named on the work file. Pickup should be reconcilable to arrivals with that group code. Wash and cutoff stay blank when the sheet is blank. BEO links are optional notes only if a BEO exists. They maintain the master.
