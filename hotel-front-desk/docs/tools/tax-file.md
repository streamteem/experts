# Tax file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The tax file is their posted tax names and percents and exemption notes. You compare folio lines to it. You do not invent occupancy tax. Certificates live beside it as present or missing. This tool feeds tax-exempt completeness and folio-exception packs. They maintain the PMS tax setup. Names, percents, and exemption types as they wrote them. Folio-exception and tax-exempt workflows compare posted lines to this sheet. You do not invent an occupancy-tax percent from a comptroller memory.
