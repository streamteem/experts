# Group blocks only from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Contracted rooms, rates, cutoff, wash, and routing come from their group sheet or PMS group master. Do not invent a group rate, attrition percent, or rebate. Pickup versus block is a comparison, not a release you perform. Rooming-list names must already be on their list. They cut and they wash. Pickup, wash, cutoff, and routing are four different columns — do not blend them. Extra rooms above the block need their overbook or extra-rooms note. You do not email the planner a cutoff warning as this product unless they taught that send.
