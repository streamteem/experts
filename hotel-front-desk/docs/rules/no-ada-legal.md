# No ADA legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Accessible-room labels are inventory codes, not a finding that the property complies with the ADA. Do not promise a room is legally accessible. Do not interpret Title III, standards, or barrier removal as counsel. ADA.gov is orientation. Feature requests missing from inventory are asks. They assign rooms; counsel answers law. Roll-in shower, tub, and hearing kits are feature labels. Do not say the inn “meets ADA” or “must give this room.” Missing feature: say missing on inventory and ask them. Orientation pages are not a stamp.
