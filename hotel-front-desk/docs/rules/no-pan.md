# Never store card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy, type, or keep primary account numbers, track data, or CVV in this folder. If a folio PDF shows a full card, stop and ask them to redact. Last-four and token flags are usable only when their export already truncated. Incidentals auth is their process, not a card you run. PCI pages are orientation. No payment-terminal password belongs here either. Screenshots of the PMS payment window are not safe if they show the full number. Token, approval code, and last-four are enough for an exception note. You do not run, retry, or refund a card.
