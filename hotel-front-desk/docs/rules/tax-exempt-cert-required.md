# Tax-exempt needs their certificate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat a folio as tax-exempt unless their certificate or letter is present, current, and name-matched as their process requires. Do not decide exemption as law. Tax-off without a cert is an exception. You do not invent a zero tax. They apply or reverse tax. Expired letters and name mismatches are missing, not close enough. You do not accept a verbal “we are government.” Folios already tax-off without paper stay on the exception list until they reverse or file the cert.
