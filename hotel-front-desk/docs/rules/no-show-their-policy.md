# No-show fees from their policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No-show charges follow their cancellation and guarantee policy already on the reservation or policy PDF. Do not invent a fee or a tax on the fee. Do not run the card. Guaranteed versus 6:00 p.m. hold stays as they coded it. Group no-shows use the block file when that file speaks. Cancelled is not no-show. Hold-until-six and guaranteed first-night are different. Prepaid OTA no-shows follow the reservation as stored, not a house flexible policy you prefer. You list; they post the fee if the policy says a fee exists.
