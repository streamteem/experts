# Minimum necessary guest data

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy only the guest labels, confirmation numbers, and flags needed for the list they asked for. Do not dump extra ID images, full SSN, medical notes, or card PAN into docs. Incident logs stay operational. Photo ID at the desk is their check-in process, not a scan you file here. Less is enough. Confirmation, room type, and status usually suffice for a day list. Leave driver’s-license images and medical notes out of docs. Folio PDFs get redacted if they show PAN. Incident write-ups stay short and operational.
