# No comp without their code

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Complimentary and house-use exist only with their existing rate code and the authorizer their file names. Do not invent a free night to close a complaint in the pack. “Comp asked” on an incident log without a post is a missing-post flag, not a dollar you write. Tax on comps follows their tax file. Owner, employee, and make-good comps are different codes if they split them. A noisy-night note is not a dollar. Flash ADR treatment of comps follows their report, not a formula you invent.
