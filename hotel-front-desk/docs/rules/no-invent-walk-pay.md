# Do not invent walk pay

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Walk payments, taxi, and complimentary nights at another hotel come only from their walk SOP. No SOP: ask. Do not pick a walk hotel from a map. Do not invent a dollar. Do not mark a guest walked unless their file says so. They authorize the walk. You list walk risk and their hotel list. Sister-property lists beat a maps search. If the walk SOP is silent on taxis or meals, those dollars stay blank asks. Do not delete the original reservation row; mark it walked after they say so.
