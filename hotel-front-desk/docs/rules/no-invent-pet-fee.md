# Do not invent a pet fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pet fees and pet-room rules come from their posted file. Silent file: ask. Do not invent a breed rule or a cleaning charge. Service-animal versus pet is their script if stored, not an ADA ruling you write. Missing fee on a pet-flagged folio is an exception they post. Per-night versus per-stay must match their file. Service-animal process, if they stored a script, is completeness of the ask — not a legal ruling. Silent policy: ask before the folio shows a pet line you invented.
