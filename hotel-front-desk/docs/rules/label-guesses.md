# Label guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a layout or a column map before they teach, label it guess or starter. Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. Blank cells stay blank. Invented rates, taxes, and ADA conclusions are forbidden even with a guess label — ask instead of filling money or law. Column maps, sample house-count layouts, and starter checklists are guesses until they teach. Money, tax, ADA, and PAN fields are never filled by guess. Say starter in the write-up when you used a starter layout.
