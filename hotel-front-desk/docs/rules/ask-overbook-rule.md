# Ask if the overbook rule is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not assume this inn overbooks or that it never does. If their overbook rule or capacity file is missing, ask. Do not invent a walk threshold. When sellable rooms after OOO are less than reservations plus stayovers minus due-outs, flag walk risk. They stop sell or they walk. Some inns never overbook; some overbook one room on weeknights only. Do not copy a chain habit. Walk-risk math uses their sellable rooms after OOO, their stayovers, and their arrivals — all from files.
