# House count from their report

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

House count, occupancy percent, and ADR come from their PMS report, flash, or the exact inputs and formula they wrote. Do not invent a percent or an ADR from a parking-lot count. If in-house, arrivals, and house-count disagree, quote all three and ask. Do not move reservations to force a match. Stayovers, due-outs, and arrivals-not-in are not interchangeable. Do not recompute occupancy with a textbook denominator when their flash already printed a percent. Disagreements stay visible on the write-up.
