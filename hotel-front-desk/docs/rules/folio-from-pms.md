# Folios from their PMS file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Folio lines, balances, and tenders come from the PMS PDF or export they dropped. Do not invent a charge, a balance, or a settlement. Do not post or void in the system as this product. Exceptions stay listed. City-ledger and splits stay as routed. They adjust folios. Printed PDF and on-screen export should match; if they do not, quote both. You do not create a folio. Adjustments, voids, and transfers stay their postings. Exception packs name the line, the date, and the disagreeing file.
