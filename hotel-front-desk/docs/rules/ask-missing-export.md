# Ask when the PMS export is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Arrivals, in-house, house count, and folios come from files they dropped. If the export is absent, ask. Do not rebuild the house from memory, a website, or last week’s PDF. Partial files stay labeled partial. Starter layouts are guesses, not *this* property’s live book. Yesterday’s arrivals PDF is not today’s book. A photographed rack is usable only if they dropped that photo for this date. Partial exports stay labeled partial. You ask; you do not scrape a booking website.
