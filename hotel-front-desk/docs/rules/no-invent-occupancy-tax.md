# Do not invent occupancy tax

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Occupancy or lodging tax is their file, not a comptroller page you apply as counsel and not a percent from another city. Do not invent the rate. Do not invent what is taxable. Folio disagreements are exceptions. Monthly filings are theirs; you do not pay the tax. City, county, and stadium names stay as their file printed them. You do not apply another city’s percent. Monthly returns and payments are theirs. Orientation from a comptroller site does not replace the folder file.
