# Do not promise ADA access

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not tell a pack or a write-up that a room is ADA compliant or that a request is legally required. List inventory features they coded. If the requested feature is not on the room file, say so. Do not hold or release accessible rooms by a remembered brand policy. They assign; counsel speaks to law. “Accessible king” on their type list is a label. Do not promise roll-in, hearing kit, or a connecting accessible pair unless that feature is on the room file. Hold-and-release of those rooms follows their process file only.
