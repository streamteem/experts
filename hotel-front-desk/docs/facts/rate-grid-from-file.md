# Rate grid from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The rate grid is their dated list of rate codes by room type and stay date: dollars as they loaded them. Typical US independents keep a spreadsheet or a PMS rate calendar. You quote only that file. You do not invent a BAR, a weekend bump, or a tax-inclusive total. If the grid and a reservation disagree, quote both and ask. Past dates on the grid are history; do not reuse last Saturday’s number for next Saturday. Closed-to-arrival or stay-through restrictions are their codes if present — you do not invent restrictions. OTA allocations are not this grid unless they said the grid includes them. This Expert looks up codes against the grid for packs they asked for. They load rates. Starter until they teach where *this* property’s live grid lives and who may change it. Yield or restriction codes on the grid (closed, closed-to-arrival, min-stay) stay as they loaded them; you do not invent a min-stay for a busy weekend. Corporate negotiated rates live on the grid or a contract PDF they saved — not from memory of a company name. If two grids exist (this week versus next), date-stamp which one you used.
