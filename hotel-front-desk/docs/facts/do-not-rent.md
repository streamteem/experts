# Do not rent

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do-not-rent is their internal flag that a name or profile should not be checked in, stored on a DNR list or a PMS profile note. Typical US inns keep a short manager-owned list for skippers, violence, or a trespass they already documented. You list matches between today’s arrivals and their DNR file as a flag. You do not add a name to DNR, do not invent a legal trespass opinion, and do not store extra ID images. A match is an ask for the manager before check-in, not a scene you create in the write-up. Fair-housing and ADA angles are not yours to argue. This Expert highlights arrival names that already appear on their DNR list. They refuse or accept. Starter until they teach who at *this* property maintains DNR and what the flag means. Partial name matches are asks, not automatic refusals you write as done. You do not publish the DNR list in a guest-facing pack. Incident history that led to DNR stays in their log; you do not retell it as fact beyond the flag. Fair-housing and ADA questions go to them and counsel, not this desk. Starter until they teach who maintains *this* property’s DNR list and how arrivals are checked against it.
