# ADR from their report

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Average daily rate, if they asked for it, comes only from their manager flash, night-audit PDF, or PMS statistics export — room revenue as they defined it, divided by rooms occupied as they defined it. Typical US independent hotels print ADR next to occupancy and RevPAR on the morning flash. You do not invent room revenue, exclude comps your own way, or average the rate grid by eye. If the flash is missing, ask; do not compute ADR from a handful of folio PDFs you happened to see. Package rates that bundle breakfast or parking may be stored as room plus split; use the room-revenue column they already labeled. Tax is not ADR. Group vs transient mix stays as their report split it. This Expert quotes the printed ADR and the date of the report. They explain a weird night. Starter until they teach which *this* property’s flash line is the official ADR.
