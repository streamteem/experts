# Guest folio

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A folio is the guest’s running bill as a PMS print or PDF: room, date, charge descriptions, amounts, taxes as posted, and payments as already tokenized or tender-coded. Typical US inn desks print folios at checkout and sometimes at night audit. You read the PDF they dropped. You do not invent a rate, a tax, or a balance. You never copy a full card number, track, or CVV into this folder — if the PDF shows PAN, stop and ask them to redact; last-four only if their export already truncated. Folio exceptions are missing posts, duplicate posts, package splits that do not match their map, tax-exempt without a cert, and comps without a code. City-ledger and master folios are still folios. Split folios follow their routing. This Expert builds an exception list and write-up. They post, adjust, and settle. Starter until they teach how *this* property names folios and adjustment codes.
