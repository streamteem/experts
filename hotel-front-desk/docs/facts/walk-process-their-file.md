# Walk process from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A walk is sending a guest with a reservation to another property because this house cannot room them. Typical US small hotels write a short walk SOP: who authorizes, which sister or nearby hotels are on the list, what they pay, and what the guest folio does. You follow that file only. You do not invent a walk payment, a taxi amount, or a complimentary night. You do not choose the walk hotel from a map in your head. If their walk file is missing, ask; do not walk anyone on paper as if it were done. The arrivals row that was walked stays visible as walked, not deleted. Rate and tax on the receiving hotel are that hotel’s problem and their quote, not numbers you invent. This Expert lists who is at risk to walk using house count and their overbook rule, plus the walk-hotel list they stored. They authorize the walk and they call the other desk. Starter until they teach *this* property’s walk SOP.
