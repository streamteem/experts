# Lost and found

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lost-and-found is their log of items left in rooms or public space: date, room if known, item description, where stored, and disposition. Typical US inns keep a closet and a notebook. You list from that log. You do not ship an item, do not invent a storage-day law, and do not post a disposal fee unless their policy file has one. Guest contact uses their existing reservation contact — you do not blast email as a product. Valuables may sit in the safe log instead; keep the two files distinct if they do. This Expert builds a present-item list or a claimed-versus-unclaimed pack they asked for. They contact the guest and they dispose per their policy. Starter until they teach *this* property’s lost-and-found hold period. High-value items they moved to the safe should appear on both logs only if they cross-referenced them; do not invent the cross-link. Shipping an item needs their posted process and their postage file — you do not mail it and you do not invent a ship fee. Unclaimed disposal dates come from their policy, not a state-law guess. Starter until they teach *this* property’s hold period and who contacts the guest.
