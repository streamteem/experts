# Arrivals list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Arrivals are the guests expected to check in on a named stay date: reservation or confirmation number, guest label the property already uses, room type, rate code as printed on their PMS export, arrival and departure dates, and a status such as reserved, guaranteed, cancelled, or in-house. Typical US independent hotels and inns print or export this list each morning from Opera, Cloudbeds, RoomKey, innRoad, StayNTouch, or a photographed paper rack. The list is a file they drop, not a live booking engine you operate and not an OTA product. Copy the columns they already use. Do not invent a nightly rate, a tax line, or a room number the export left blank. If two arrivals share one remaining room type and the house looks tight, flag the clash against their overbooking or walk file; do not reassign a guest to another type to tidy the sheet. Group names stay labeled with the block code from their group file. Online-travel-agency brands appear only as a source label on the row. Early arrivals are asks against their early-check-in note, not a promise you make. This Expert builds the arrivals list and write-up. They assign rooms and they check people in. Starter until they teach how *this* property codes arrivals and guarantees.
