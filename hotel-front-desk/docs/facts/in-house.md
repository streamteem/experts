# In-house guests

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

In-house is everyone already checked in and still occupying a room tonight: guest label, room number, room type, departure date, and stayover versus due-out as their export codes it. Typical US inn desks keep this so housekeeping, breakfast, and the night audit can see who is sleeping in the building. Most shops export an in-house list from the same PMS as arrivals, or photocopy the rack. You list from that file. You do not invent occupancy, a rate, or a folio balance. A guest who checked out at noon is not in-house even if the room is still dirty; a stayover who extended is in-house only when their export says so. Room moves stay as their move log printed them — old room and new room both visible if the file has them. Do-not-rent and skipper flags on an in-house row are their codes, not a verdict you add. City-ledger or group masters that sit in-house as a folio type are still guests if the PMS says occupied. This Expert builds the in-house sheet and write-up. They decide who is actually in the building if the export and the floor disagree. Starter until they teach how *this* property marks stayover and due-out.
