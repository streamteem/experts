# Connecting rooms

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Connecting or adjoining rooms are physical pairs on their room map or type file, not a hope that two kings are near each other. Typical US inns mark connecting doors in the PMS so families can request them. You assign or list connecting only when their inventory says those two rooms connect. You do not promise a connection that the map does not show. Adjoining (near) is not connecting (door). If the only connecting pair is OOO or stayover, say unavailable from file; do not invent another pair. Accessible connecting rooms stay their inventory labels, not an ADA opinion. This Expert lists connecting requests on arrivals versus available pairs on the room-status sheet. They assign the pair. Starter until they teach *this* property’s connecting-room map. Some inns sell “near each other” as a comment, not a connecting door; keep that comment as a request, not a map fact. If the connecting pair is due-out today, readiness still follows the status sheet after checkout, not hope. You do not move a stayover to free a connecting pair unless they asked. Starter until they teach *this* property’s connecting pairs and who may break a pair.
