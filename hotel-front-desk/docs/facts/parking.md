# Parking

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parking — self-park, lot, garage, or valet — is an add-on or a package inclusion only as their rate grid, package sheet, or posted parking file says. Typical US inns either include parking or post a nightly lot fee. You do not invent a parking price, a valet tip, or an in-and-out rule. If parking is in the package, do not also list it as a missing incidental unless their folio map says it should post and did not. Oversized-vehicle and EV spaces are inventory notes if they wrote them, not promises you invent. Street parking is not your product. This Expert lists who needs parking from arrivals comments versus who was posted. They sell the stall and they post. Starter until they teach *this* property’s parking fees and inclusions. Valet tickets and hang-tags they already numbered stay on the list as their numbers, not as a new valet product you invent. Overnight versus day-rate, if they split those on the file, stay split. If the lot is full on their count sheet, say full from that sheet and ask; do not send a guest to a street space as if the inn guaranteed it. Starter until they teach *this* property’s lot map and who posts parking.
