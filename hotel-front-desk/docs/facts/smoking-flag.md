# Smoking flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Smoking versus non-smoking is their room flag and their posted fee for violation if they have one. Typical US inns are 100 percent non-smoking with a deep-clean fee on the policy sheet, or they still have a few smoking rooms. You copy the flag from the room file. You do not invent a smoking fee or a municipal smoking-law opinion. A guest who reserved smoking when the house has none is a relocate or refuse-to-honor question they decide. Incident logs that say “smoke in room” are routing notes; the fee posts only from their policy file. Connecting a smoking room to a non-smoking room is their map, not a health opinion. This Expert lists smoking flags on assigned rooms versus reservation requests. They assign and they post fees. Starter until they teach *this* property’s smoking inventory and violation fee. Deep-clean fees, ozone notes, and “out of service until inspected” belong on the status or incident file if they wrote them — you do not invent the dollar or the out-of-service days. A walk-in who wants smoking when the flag file is all non-smoking is an ask, not a secret smoking room you invent. Starter until they teach *this* property’s smoking inventory and who posts the violation fee.
