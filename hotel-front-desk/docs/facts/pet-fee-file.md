# Pet fee from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pet fees, pet rooms, and pet-deposit rules come only from their posted policy or rate-grid add-on file. Typical US inns charge a flat pet fee per stay or per night, or they refuse pets except service animals — and service-animal questions are process, not an ADA legal opinion you write. You do not invent a pet fee, a breed rule, or a cleaning charge. If the arrivals row says pet and the folio has no fee, flag against their file; they post. If their file is silent, ask. Service-animal versus pet is their front-desk script if they stored one; you do not rule on the ADA. Room assignment to a “pet-friendly” room follows their inventory flag. This Expert lists pet flags versus posted fees. They assign rooms and they post fees. Starter until they teach *this* property’s pet and service-animal process.
