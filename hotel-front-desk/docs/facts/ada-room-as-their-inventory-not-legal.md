# Accessible room as their inventory, not law

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Accessible, mobility, roll-in-shower, and hearing-equipped rooms are inventory codes on their room-type or room-feature file. Typical US inns mark a few rooms with those PMS features so the desk can see what they labeled. You list those labels as written. You never decide ADA as law, never promise a room meets the ADA Standards, and never give a barrier-removal opinion. If a guest requested an accessible feature their file does not list, say the feature is not on inventory and ask them — do not invent a compliant room. Holding or releasing accessible rooms follows their reservation process file if they have one, not a consent-decree you recall. ADA.gov lodging pages are orientation only. This Expert lists accessible inventory versus requests. They assign rooms and they take legal questions to counsel. Starter until they teach how *this* property codes accessible features.
