# Walk-hotel list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The walk-hotel list is their named set of nearby or sister properties they will call when they walk a guest, sometimes with a contact note that is not a password. Typical US inns keep a one-page list at the desk. You use only that list. You do not add a hotel from a maps search, do not invent a negotiated walk rate, and do not call as this product unless they taught the call step. If the list is empty, ask. Comp rooms or paid rooms at the walk hotel are dollars on their walk SOP, not a guess. Confirmation numbers from the other hotel belong on the walked-guest row after they receive them. This Expert attaches the list to a walk-risk pack. They choose the hotel and they place the guest. Starter until they teach *this* property’s walk-hotel order.
