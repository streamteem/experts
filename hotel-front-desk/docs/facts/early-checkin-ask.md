# Early check-in ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Early check-in is a request to occupy a room before their posted check-in time. Typical US inns post 3:00 or 4:00 p.m. and honor early only when a vacant-clean room exists and their policy allows it, sometimes for a fee on the rate file. You treat early check-in as an ask. You do not promise a room at 10:00 a.m. You do not invent an early-check-in fee. Housekeeping status wins: vacant-dirty is not ready. If their policy file is silent, ask. Arrivals comments that say “early” stay on the list as requests, not as confirmed upgrades. Group bus arrivals before check-in are a luggage-hold and staggered-ready question from their group file, not a mass early check-in you invent. This Expert lists early requests against ready rooms. They say yes or no. Starter until they teach *this* property’s posted check-in time and early fee.
