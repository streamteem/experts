# Block wash

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wash is their planned or actual release of unsold group rooms back to the house, sometimes on a schedule before cutoff, sometimes as a percent they wrote. Typical US inns either wash by phone with the planner or let the PMS auto-release on cutoff. You quote wash only from their group sheet, contract clause they saved, or PMS history. You do not invent an attrition percent or a wash curve from another hotel. If they have no wash file, say so and ask — do not “help” the house by releasing rooms in a pack. Actual no-shows inside a group are no-shows under their policy, not extra wash you apply. Attrition fees, if any, are dollars on their contract file, not a formula you invent, and not a bill you send. This Expert lists contracted, picked up, washed, and remaining as their columns. They wash. Starter until they teach how *this* property washes a block.
