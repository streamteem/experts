# No-show

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A no-show is an arrival that did not check in by their cutoff time and was coded no-show in the PMS or on the night-audit PDF. Typical US inns bill a no-show under the cancellation and guarantee policy already on the reservation or the policy PDF. You list no-shows from that file. You do not invent a no-show charge, a tax on the charge, or a card settlement. You do not run the card. Guaranteed versus hold-until-6:00 stays as they coded the reservation. Groups may have their own no-show rule on the block file — use that file, not the transient policy, when the row is a group. Cancelled before arrival is not a no-show. This Expert builds the no-show list and folio-exception notes. They post the fee and they settle. Starter until they teach *this* property’s no-show cutoff and posting code.
