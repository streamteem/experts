# Comp as their code

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Complimentary and house-use stays exist only when their PMS already has a comp or house-use rate code and an authorization name their file requires. Typical US small hotels comp a noisy-night make-good or an owner room; those are coded, not whispered. You do not invent a comp, a percent off, or a free night to tidy a complaint in the pack. If the incident log says “comp asked” and no code was posted, flag the missing post — do not write the dollar. Tax treatment of comps follows their tax file, not a guess. Owner and employee rates are different codes if they split them; keep the label they used. This Expert lists comp codes already on the folio or the flash. They authorize comps. Starter until they teach who at *this* property may comp and which code they use.
