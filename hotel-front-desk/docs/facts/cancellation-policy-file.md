# Cancellation policy file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cancellation windows, penalties, and free-cancel hours come only from their policy PDF, rate-code rules, or the reservation as stored. Typical US inns use 24-hour or 48-hour cancel on flexible rates and tighter rules on prepaid or event nights. You do not invent a cancel fee or a “standard 24-hour” if their file says something else. OTA cancel rules are the source label’s problem as already stored on the reservation — you do not re-trade the OTA as a product. Prepaid non-refundable stays stay labeled from their file. This Expert lists cancellations already on the export and whether a fee should exist per their policy versus whether it posted. They cancel in the PMS. Starter until they teach *this* property’s cancel policies by rate code. Prepaid, non-refundable, and flexible codes can live on one night’s book; keep the policy that matches the reservation’s rate code, not a house-wide habit. Group cancel windows sit on the block file when that file speaks. You do not email a cancel confirmation as a product unless they taught that send step. Starter until they teach *this* property’s cancel rules by rate code and channel.
