# Tax file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The tax file is their posted schedule of which tax names and percents the PMS should apply: state sales, local lodging, stadium, or whatever they loaded. Typical US hotels keep a one-page tax sheet plus the PMS tax setup. You copy those names and percents as written. You do not invent a tax, a surcharge, or a “usual city rate.” If the folio tax does not match the tax file, that is an exception — do not silently recalculate a new amount. Inclusive versus exclusive display follows their file. Package splits may tax room differently from food; use their map. This Expert compares folio tax lines to the tax file. They load taxes in the PMS. Starter until they teach *this* property’s tax names and who maintains the file. Resort or facility fees, if they load them as a tax-like item, still come from this file or the rate grid — you do not invent a junk-fee amount. FTC fee-transparency pages are orientation; they do not set this inn’s dollars. Inclusive versus exclusive stay as labeled. If the PMS tax setup PDF and the one-page sheet disagree, quote both and ask. Starter until they teach who maintains *this* property’s tax file.
