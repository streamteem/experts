# Group block

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A group block is a hold of rooms by type and night for a named group, with a cutoff date, a rate code from their file, and a pickup count as the PMS or the group sheet shows. Typical US hotels and inns keep a one-page block sheet plus the PMS group master. You list contracted rooms, picked-up rooms, remaining, and cutoff as written. You do not invent a group rate, a rebate, or a wash percent. Pickup that exceeds the block is an ask against their overbook or extra-rooms rule. Pickup far below the block near cutoff is a wash question they decide — you do not release rooms yourself. Rooming lists they dropped become arrivals rows only with the names they provided; do not invent attendees. Folio routing (master vs individual) follows their group file. This Expert compares block versus pickup and writes the exception pack. They cut, wash, or extend the block. Starter until they teach how *this* property numbers groups and cutoffs.
