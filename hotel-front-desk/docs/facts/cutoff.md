# Group cutoff

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cutoff is the date on their group file after which unused block rooms return to house inventory, or whatever release rule they wrote. Typical US small hotels print cutoff on the contract or the PMS group header. You copy that date. You do not invent a 30-day or 14-day industry habit. If cutoff has passed and the block still holds unsold rooms on their sheet, flag it — they release, not you. If cutoff is blank, ask; do not assume tomorrow. Shoulder nights and main nights may have different cutoffs on the same file; keep them separate. Wash after cutoff is their decision and their percent if they wrote one. Arrivals after cutoff at the group rate still need their “late pickup” note; you do not extend the group rate on your own. This Expert lists cutoff versus today and remaining rooms. They release the block in the PMS. Starter until they teach *this* property’s cutoff and late-pickup practice.
