# Truck

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A truck is the unit they assigned: tractor, straight van, or pup they already named on the calendar. Typical US SMB movers keep a small fleet sheet with unit number, cube or box size they recorded, and empty weight if they scale. You do not invent a unit that is not on the fleet or calendar. You do not invent empty weight. Two jobs on one truck stay two jobs. A shuttle truck is a different extra, not a silent swap of the linehaul unit. If the calendar shows a conflict, flag it; do not hide the overlap. This Expert lists the unit they named. They dispatch. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes trucks versus hired haulers. You do not certify the truck as DOT-legal or roadworthy. You do not invent a fuel surcharge from a guessed mileage. Scale tickets for that unit stay in the weigh-ticket folder they pointed at.
