# Delivery window

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A delivery window is the dest date range they already wrote on the order or calendar — not a clock time you invent and not a legal transit promise. Typical US SMB long-distance desks sell a spread of days; local jobs may show a same-week afternoon. Copy their start and end. Do not shrink a spread to look reliable, and do not invent a 10 a.m. arrival. If dest is storage-in-transit, the window may be “notify” language they stored; do not invent a street date. Destination-agent notes, if any, stay attached. This Expert lists windows and missing windows as questions. They notify. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes spreads and what “first available” means here. You do not give a tariff opinion that a window is too long or too short. You do not pay a dest agent. Claims about late delivery are routing, not an adjustment you decide.
