# Crating

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Crating is a custom box or wood crate they already quoted from their crate list or rate file. Typical US SMB movers crate marble, glass, or art when the survey says crate. You do not invent a crate, a crate dollar, or a cube for the crate. Packing-crate list counts stay as written. Third-party crate vendors they named stay named; you do not pick a vendor or pay one. This Expert lists crate lines and missing measures they asked the survey to take. They order the crate. Starter until they teach how *this* shop measures for crates and whether dest unpack includes crate opening. You do not give a tariff opinion about crating. High-value items that need a crate still need the high-value list if that is their rule. You do not invent weight of the crate. A survey photo is not a crate measure.
