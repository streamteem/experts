# Cancellation file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cancellation and postponement rules live on their policy PDF or jobber note. Typical US SMB offices write hours-notice and a fee or deposit-keep line. Copy it. You do not invent a cancel fee, and you do not decide that a customer is in breach. Calendar changes they already wrote stay as reschedule versus cancel using their words. This Expert lists canceled jobs and missing policy pages. They call the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes weather versus customer cancel. You do not give a tariff or contract-law opinion. You do not keep a card “in case they cancel.” Crew-day lists should drop canceled jobs they marked, not silently keep a truck on the street. Deposits still follow the deposit policy file, not a new number. Missing policy PDF: say missing; do not invent hours-notice from another shop.
