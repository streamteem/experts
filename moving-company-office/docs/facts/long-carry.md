# Long carry

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Long carry is extra distance from truck to door that their file already measured or checked. Typical US SMB movers write footage, “over 75 feet,” or a yes/no on the survey. You do not invent footage from satellite view or a remembered city block. Origin carry and dest carry stay separate. Shuttle is a different extra; do not relabel a long carry as a shuttle or the reverse. If footage is blank, ask. Rate money comes from their rate file only. A parking-permit note is not a carry measurement. This Expert lists the carry field they stored. They measure again if the crew later writes a different number — you do not silently overwrite the estimate with a crew guess unless they said the crew sheet wins. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s carry threshold and unit. You do not give a tariff opinion that a carry must be billed. You do not invent weight to justify a carry line.
