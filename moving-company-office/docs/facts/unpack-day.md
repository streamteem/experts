# Unpack day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unpack day is the dest labor date they booked, which may be the delivery day or a later ticket. Typical US SMB calendars show a second row for unpack. You do not invent an unpack day to close a window, and you do not promise a clock hour. If unpack was not sold, do not add a day. Debris may share the day or not, as their file says. Dest-agent unpack stays labeled if they coded it. This Expert lists unpack days and missing ones on sold unpack jobs. They schedule. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s dest follow-up. You do not pay the dest crew. You do not invent wait time on unpack. Claims found on unpack day still go to the claims log with photos they stored, not a field cash you invent. A delivery window is not an unpack day unless they merged the rows.
