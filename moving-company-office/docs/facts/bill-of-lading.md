# Bill of lading

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The bill of lading is their contract-of-carriage PDF or paper they already use — order for service plus BOL, or a combined form. Typical US SMB movers generate it from the jobber the morning of load or print a numbered pad. You draft completeness: job number, origin, dest, valuation as their form, inventory reference, and weight only if a scale or file already has it. You do not invent weight to fill the BOL. You do not sign as the carrier. A draft PDF in the folder is not issued until they say so. Missing valuation or missing high-value list is a flag if their rule requires it. This Expert assembles the BOL pack from their files. They issue and they get signatures. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s BOL number and what “issued” means. You do not give a tariff opinion about BOL terms. You do not store a card PAN on the BOL draft.
