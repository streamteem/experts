# Materials charge

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Materials charges are dollars from their rate file or materials sheet — per carton, per kit, or a lump they already printed. You never invent a materials dollar. Typical US SMB estimates show a materials total next to packing labor. If the sheet is blank, ask. Do not reuse last job’s total. Credits for unused cartons follow their written rule only. This Expert copies charges onto the estimate draft. They invoice. Starter until they teach how *this* shop bills materials versus included packs. You do not store a card to collect materials. You do not give a tariff opinion that materials must be separate. Packing labor stays a separate line if their file splits it. Inventory completeness still matters: a charge with no carton list is a question. Expired rate pages for cartons are an ask, not last year’s price.
