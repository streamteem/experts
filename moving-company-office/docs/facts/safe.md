# Safe

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A safe is a specialty piece they already listed, sometimes with a weight they recorded from a plate or a prior move — still never a weight you invent. Typical US SMB surveys write make, approximate size they measured, and whether a third-party safe crew is required. Copy those fields. You do not invent a pound figure from a catalog memory. Stairs, hoist, and crew size stay as their file. This Expert lists the safe and missing plate photos they asked for. They hire the specialty vendor if that is their note. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s safe rule and whether gun safes need an extra form they already use. You do not pay the vendor. You do not give a legal opinion about firearms in the safe. Valuation still follows their form. You do not open or inventory the inside of a safe.
