# Origin

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Origin is the pickup address and access they already filed: street, unit, building name, stairs or elevator note, and any COA or union flag they wrote. Typical US SMB movers copy origin from the survey, the estimate header, or a customer email PDF. Do not invent a unit number, a loading-dock hour, or a stair count. Do not clean a messy address into a prettier one if that hides the apartment they wrote. Parking-permit and elevator-reservation asks stay questions until their note says booked. Gate codes and lockbox combinations do not belong in this folder. A second origin or a split pickup is a separate row if they coded it that way. This Expert lists origin as filed. They confirm access with the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop stores origin versus billing address. You do not promise a load hour the calendar file does not show. You do not invent long-carry footage from a map in your head.
