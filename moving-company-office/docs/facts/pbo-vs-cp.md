# PBO vs CP

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Packed-by-owner versus carrier-packed is a carton or room code on their inventory. Typical US SMB sheets mark PBO, CP, or PBC. Copy the code. You do not relabel PBO as CP to sell packing, and you do not relabel CP as PBO to hide a materials line. Packing labor and materials still come from their files. Claims notes often treat PBO differently on their form; you copy that form language and still do not adjust the claim. Mixed rooms stay mixed. This Expert lists counts by code when their sheet has codes. They pack. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks cartons and whether a wardrobe is always CP. You do not invent a carton count. You do not give a tariff opinion that PBO is excluded. Debris pickup after unpack is a separate extra if their file has it.
