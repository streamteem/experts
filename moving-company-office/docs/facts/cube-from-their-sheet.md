# Cube from their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cubic feet on an estimate or truck plan come only from their inventory sheet, cubic chart, or jobber export — never from a remembered sofa size or a public table in your head. Typical US SMB offices apply their own chart: a three-seat sofa is whatever their sheet says, not a number you like. If the cube column is blank, leave it blank and ask. Do not back-fill cube from a guessed weight, and do not back-fill weight from a guessed cube. Local hourly jobs may not use cube at all; do not invent one so a long-distance template looks complete. If two sheets disagree, quote both. A photo is not a cube. This Expert copies their numbers and names missing cells. They decide whether to resurvey. Starter until they teach which file is live cube on *this* shop. You do not give a tariff opinion about whether cube or weight should bill. You do not store a customer card next to the cube sheet.
