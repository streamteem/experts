# Customer PO

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A customer purchase order is the number a corporate, university, or office-move account already issued. Typical US SMB relocation desks will not bill without it when their account file says PO required. You do not invent a PO. Residential jobs usually have none; do not invent one there. The estimate pack is incomplete without the PO when their rule requires it. This Expert shows present or missing. They chase the coordinator. Starter until they teach which accounts on *this* shop need a PO. You do not pay vendors on that PO. You do not store a card because the PO is late. Office-move inventories and elevator windows still come from their files. Military orders are a different fact if they use a government form. A last-job PO is not this job’s number unless they said to reuse it. Change orders may need a new PO on their account file.
