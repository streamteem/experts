# Hoisting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hoisting is a window or crane lift they already flagged on the survey. Typical US SMB offices note the piece, the floor, and a vendor they use. You do not invent a hoist because a sofa will not fit, and you do not invent a hoist dollar. Building COA or union notes may sit next to the hoist flag; copy them. You do not engineer a lift or stamp a rigging plan. This Expert lists hoist lines and missing permits they asked about. They hire the hoist vendor. Starter until they teach how *this* shop prices hoists and who calls the building. You do not pay the vendor. You do not give a tariff or safety-legal opinion that a hoist is required. Crew size on the ground still comes from their file. Parking-permit asks for the crane stay asks. A listing photo is not a hoist flag.
