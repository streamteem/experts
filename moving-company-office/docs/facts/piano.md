# Piano

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A piano is a specialty item only when their survey or estimate already names type — upright, baby grand, grand — and any stair or hoist note. Typical US SMB offices have a piano line on the rate file or a third-party piano crew they already use. You do not invent a piano that is not on the inventory, and you do not invent a piano rate. Stairs and hoist stay as their notes, not as a guess from a listing photo. Crew size for the piano comes from their file. This Expert copies the piano line and flags missing type. They book the specialty crew if that is their process. Starter until they teach how *this* shop prices pianos and when they refer out. You do not pay that crew. You do not give a tariff opinion that a piano must be an accessorial. You do not invent weight for the piano.
