# Inventory stickers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Inventory stickers or tags are the piece-count system they use on load day: numbered labels that should match the inventory sheet. Typical US SMB crews run a sticker roll or a jobber scan. You list sticker ranges they recorded and flag gaps they already noted. You do not invent a sticker number for a missing piece, and you do not invent a piece count to match a guessed weight. High-value stickers, if they use a separate series, stay separate. Photos of damaged stickers belong in the claims or exception folder they named. This Expert compares sticker log to inventory rows when both files exist. They recount. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers tags and whether PBO cartons get stickers. You do not adjust a claim from a missing sticker. You do not invent cube from a sticker total.
