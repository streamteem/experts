# High-value list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The high-value list is their form or sheet of items they treat as high value — art, jewelry notes, antiques — with descriptions they already wrote. Typical US SMB movers require the list when their rule or form says so. You do not invent an item or a dollar value. You do not omit a line that is on their list to tidy a claim. If their rule says the list is required and the folder has none, the pack is incomplete: say so. Valuation on the main form is not a substitute for the list if they split those papers. This Expert checks presence and blank fields. They collect the list from the customer. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s dollar threshold and form name. You do not give a legal opinion about what must be listed. You do not store photos of cards or IDs beyond min-necessary job photos they already dropped.
