# Origin agent

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An origin agent is the local office they recorded as booking or packing the shipment when this shop is not the only door. Typical US SMB van-line or agent shops store agent name, code, and a booking number on the job. You list that name as filed. You do not invent an agent to make an interstate label look complete, and you do not appoint a new agent in the write-up. If this shop is the origin, say so from their file; do not guess. Pack dates and inventory still come from the files they dropped, even if another agent surveyed. This Expert copies agent fields and flags blanks. They keep the agency relationship. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes booking versus hauling agents. You do not pay the origin agent. You do not give a tariff opinion about agent splits. Crew notes from the origin stay attached, not rewritten as a dest plan.
