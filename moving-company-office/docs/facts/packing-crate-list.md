# Packing and crate list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The packing-crate list is their count of cartons, wardrobes, dish packs, and crates to pull from the cage. Typical US SMB warehouses keep a pick list next to the estimate. You copy quantities from that list or from the inventory carton tally they named as live. You do not invent a count to empty the cage, and you do not invent a materials charge. Crating lines that need custom measure stay questions if measure is blank. This Expert drafts the pull list. They pull stock. Starter until they teach which sheet wins when inventory and crate list disagree. You do not pay a crate vendor. PBO cartons they will not supply stay off the pull list if they coded PBO. Cube still comes only from their sheet, not from a guessed carton mix. Missing list: ask, do not copy last week’s cage pull.
