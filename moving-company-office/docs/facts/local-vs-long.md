# Local vs long

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Local versus long-distance is how they coded the job on the estimate or calendar — often local hourly versus weight-or-cube linehaul — not a legal interstate determination. Typical US SMB shops use a job type or a mileage they already wrote. Copy their label. Do not recode a local job as interstate because a map in your head crosses a state line, and do not recode a long job as local to skip a scale. Rate path follows their file: hourly sheet versus weight sheet. Missing type: ask. This Expert keeps the label they stored. They decide the product. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s cutoff and whether a commercial zone note exists on the file. You do not give a tariff or DOT opinion that the job is interstate. Weight still comes only from scale or file. Cube still comes only from their sheet.
