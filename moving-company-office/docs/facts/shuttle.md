# Shuttle

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A shuttle is a smaller vehicle they already flagged because the linehaul truck cannot reach the door. Typical US SMB surveys mark shuttle at origin, dest, or both. You do not invent a shuttle from a satellite photo or a narrow-street rumor. Shuttle is not the same as long carry. Rate dollars come from their rate file only. If the flag is blank but notes say “tight street,” ask; do not add the extra. This Expert copies shuttle fields and questions. They order the shuttle truck. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s shuttle trigger and which unit they use. You do not invent empty weight for the shuttle. You do not give a tariff opinion that a shuttle is mandatory. Crew size for the shuttle still comes from their file. Parking-permit asks stay asks. Origin shuttle and dest shuttle stay separate rows if they split them.
