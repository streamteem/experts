# Weight from scale or file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shipment weight comes only from a scale ticket they stored or a weight field they already wrote on their file. You never invent pounds. Typical US SMB long-distance desks use certified-scale tickets: empty truck, loaded truck, and a net they recorded. Local hourly jobs often have no weight; do not invent one to finish a template. A survey “about 8,000 lb” note is not a scale ticket unless they said to treat that file as the weight. Do not convert cube to weight with a density you remember. If tickets and the estimate disagree, list both and let the scale ticket win when their rule says so. Missing ticket: say missing. This Expert packs present weights and questions. They send the truck to the scale. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files tare, gross, and net. You do not certify a scale or give a DOT weighing opinion. You do not invent a fuel surcharge from a guessed weight.
