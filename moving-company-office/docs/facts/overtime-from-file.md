# Overtime from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Overtime, after-hours, or weekend premiums come only from their rate file and the hours they already wrote on the ticket. You never invent an OT rate or OT hours. Typical US SMB local movers print a clock cutoff on the rate sheet. Copy it. If the ticket has no times, ask; do not assume the crew ran late. Union-building notes are not an OT rate unless their file prices that. This Expert lists OT lines as written. They approve. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s cutoff and whether pack days use a different clock. You do not pay OT. You do not give a wage-and-hour legal opinion. Crew size still comes from their plan; you do not add people on paper to avoid OT you are not allowed to compute as a wage. Wait time stays a different line if they split it on the ticket.
