# Load day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Load day is the pickup date they booked, sometimes with a window they already sold. Typical US SMB offices write a date and “first” or “anytime” on the job. You do not invent a load day to make the truck calendar look full. You do not promise an arrival hour unless their file has one. Pack day is not load day unless they merged them. If the estimate says a spread and the calendar shows a single date, quote both and ask which is live. Weather or customer delays they recorded stay as their note, not a new date you pick. This Expert lists load day as filed. They confirm with the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop uses firsts versus windows. You do not give a DOT hours opinion that the crew can or cannot start. You do not invent weight the morning of load to finish a bill of lading draft.
