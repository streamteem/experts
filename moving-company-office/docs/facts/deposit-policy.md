# Deposit policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A deposit is the booking amount and due-when language on their policy PDF or estimate footer. Typical US SMB movers take a deposit to hold a first. You copy the percent or dollar they printed. You do not invent a deposit, and you do not take a card. You never store PAN, CVV, or a full account number. A deposit is not a payment you run. Cancellation file may say whether a deposit is kept; copy that file, do not decide. This Expert lists deposit due and whether their log says received — without card data. They collect. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s deposit and what “hold the date” means. You do not give a contract-law opinion about forfeits. Military or customer-PO jobs may have a different deposit note on their file; use that note. A blank deposit field stays blank and becomes an ask.
