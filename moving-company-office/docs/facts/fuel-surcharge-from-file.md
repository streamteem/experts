# Fuel surcharge from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fuel surcharge is a percent or a per-job dollar already on their rate file or a dated fuel memo they stored. You never invent a fuel number, a DOE average, or a percent from memory. Typical US SMB long-distance desks paste a weekly fuel PDF next to the rate book; local hourly may include fuel in the hour. Copy their method. If the memo is missing or past the date they wrote, ask. Do not compute fuel from a guessed mileage or an invented weight. This Expert copies the file amount onto the estimate draft. They publish the surcharge. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates fuel memos. You do not give a tariff opinion that a surcharge is lawful. You do not store a fuel-card PAN. Scale tickets do not become a fuel formula unless their file says so.
