# Destination

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Destination is the delivery address and access they already filed. Typical US SMB offices keep dest on the same estimate header as origin, plus a delivery window if they sold one. Stairs, elevator, long carry, and COA notes at dest are separate from origin; do not copy origin access onto dest because the cities look similar. Do not invent a dest unit, a dock hour, or a shuttle need. If dest is “TBD” or storage-in-transit, say so and do not invent a street to make the truck calendar look finished. Split deliveries stay split if they coded them. This Expert lists dest and missing access as questions. They notify the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes dest windows and whether a dest agent owns the last mile. You do not promise a delivery clock time without their file. You do not give a tariff opinion about dest accessorials. Gate codes stay out of the pack.
