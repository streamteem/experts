# Truck empty weight

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Truck empty weight, or tare, is the pounds on a scale ticket or the fleet field they already recorded for that unit. You never invent tare. Typical US SMB long-distance desks weigh empty before load. A painted empty-weight stencil is not a ticket unless they said to use the fleet file. If tare is missing, say missing; do not borrow another truck’s tare. Loaded gross minus invented tare is still invented net — do not do that. This Expert copies tare from the ticket or fleet file they named. They scale the unit. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files tare versus fuel-and-driver-on-board notes. You do not certify a scale. You do not give a DOT opinion about what must be on the truck when tare is taken. Net weight still cannot be invented. A shuttle’s tare is a different unit if they scaled it separately.
