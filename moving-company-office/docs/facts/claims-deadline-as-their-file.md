# Claims deadline as their file, not legal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Any claims-deadline words are copied from their bill of lading, valuation form, or claims-policy PDF — not issued as a legal bar date and not extended by you. Typical US SMB forms print a notice period. Quote the sentence and the document name. You do not tell a customer they are too late as a lawyer, and you do not tell them they still have time as a lawyer. FMCSA pages are orientation, not a ruling on this file. If the form is missing, say missing; do not insert a number from memory. This Expert routes the claim with the quoted line visible. They decide.
