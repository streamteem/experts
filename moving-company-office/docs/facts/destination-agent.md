# Destination agent

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A destination agent is the local office they recorded for delivery, unpack, or dest accessorials when this shop does not finish the last mile. Typical US SMB agent networks store dest-agent name and a registration or haul number. Copy it. You do not invent a dest agent because the city is far, and you do not promise that agent a delivery hour. Delivery-window lists should still show their window, not the agent’s rumor. Claims at dest still route through their claims log. This Expert lists dest-agent fields and missing ones. They assign the agent. Starter until they teach how *this* shop hands a registration. You do not pay the dest agent. You do not give a DOT or tariff opinion about who must deliver. Shuttle or wait time at dest still need their dest file, not a guess that the agent’s street is tight.
