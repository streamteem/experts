# Cubic chart in their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cubic chart is their lookup of piece to cubic feet — the sheet they trained estimators on. Typical US SMB offices keep a PDF or a jobber table. You apply only that chart. You do not use a public table, a remembered AMSA chart, or another shop’s numbers. If a piece is not on their chart, ask; do not invent a cube. Weight is still not computed from cube unless their file has a density they already wrote — and even then you copy their factor, you do not invent one. Local hourly jobs may ignore the chart; do not force cube onto them. This Expert cites the chart file name on the inventory pack. They update the chart. Starter until they teach which revision is live on *this* shop. You do not give a tariff opinion that cube billing is required. Missing chart: ask, do not download a replacement.
