# Debris

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Debris is a return trip or same-day pickup of empty cartons they already sold or noted. Typical US SMB offices write “debris yes” on the estimate or add a dest ticket. You do not invent a debris day or a haul fee. You do not invent a dump charge. If unpack is sold without debris, do not add debris to look helpful. Building rules about cardboard may appear on their COA note; copy the note, do not invent a city rule. This Expert lists debris tickets and missing ones. They schedule. Starter until they teach how *this* shop bundles debris with unpack. You do not pay the dump or the crew. You do not give an environmental-law opinion about cardboard. Materials counts still come from their materials sheet, not from a hoped-for pickup volume. A debris day that fights the dest window they filed stays a question, not a new date you pick.
