# Scale ticket wins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When their rule says the scale ticket wins, the printed net on that slip beats a survey guess, a cube conversion, and a remembered last-move weight. Typical US SMB offices keep the slip with the job. You copy the ticket. You never invent a competing weight. If the estimate had a survey pound and the ticket differs, show both and keep the ticket as the weight field when they said ticket wins. Missing ticket: the weight field stays blank or “awaiting scale,” never a filler number. This Expert builds the weigh-ticket pack. They bill from the ticket if that is their process. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s ticket-wins rule and what happens if a customer disputes the scale. You do not give a tariff or DOT legal opinion about reweigh rights. You do not invent fuel or valuation from a pre-ticket guess once a ticket exists.
