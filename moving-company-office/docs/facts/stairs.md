# Stairs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stairs are an accessorial only when their survey, ticket, or rate file already records a count or a yes. Typical US SMB offices write flights, floors, or “walk-up” on the estimate. You do not invent a flight count from a listing photo or a map pin. Origin stairs and destination stairs are separate lines if they split them. Elevator present does not automatically zero stairs unless their note says the elevator is reserved and used. If the count is blank but a photo shows steps, ask; do not number the steps yourself. Rate dollars for stairs come from their rate file, not a guessed per-flight fee. This Expert copies their stair field and flags blanks. They resurvey if needed. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts flights and whether a landing breaks a flight. You do not give a tariff legal opinion about stair charges. You do not promise the crew can carry a piece that their notes already flagged as a hoist.
