# Crew size from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

How many people go on a truck or a pack day comes from their crew plan, rate file, or job note — never from a guessed “four-man crew” habit. Typical US SMB offices write a headcount on the estimate or the day board. You do not add a person to look safe, and you do not drop a person to save a wage you are not allowed to invent. Names come from the roster they stored. A missing roster is an ask, not a made-up nickname. Piano, safe, or hoist notes may change headcount only if their file says so. This Expert copies planned size and flags jobs with no number. They assign. Starter until they teach how *this* shop sizes local hourly versus long-distance crews. You do not pay the crew. You do not certify that a headcount meets a DOT or union rule. Overtime hours still come from their overtime file, not from stretching a short crew on paper.
