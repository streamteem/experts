# Wait time

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wait time or detention is extra time they already recorded on a ticket or rate line — elevator late, COA hold, customer not home. Typical US SMB local jobs write hours on the timesheet; long-distance dest may have a wait code. You do not invent wait hours to fill a day, and you do not invent an overtime dollar. Start and stop times come from their ticket. A calendar window that the customer missed is still their note, not a wait you create. This Expert lists wait lines they stored. They approve billing. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s grace minutes and who initials wait. You do not pay the crew those hours. You do not give a tariff legal opinion about detention. You do not promise a delivery hour that would avoid wait when the file has no hour.
