# Inventory

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The inventory is the item list for the job: rooms, pieces, cartons, and notes they already wrote. Typical US SMB movers keep a survey sheet, a jobber inventory, or a paper pad the crew later stickers. Each row usually has a description, a quantity, and sometimes a cube or weight column they filled from their chart or a prior scale. You list what they recorded. You do not add a sofa to fill a hole, and you do not invent cubic feet or pounds for a blank row. Packed-by-owner versus carrier-packed stays as they coded it. High-value items belong on their high-value list if that is their rule, not hidden inside a furniture row. A missing room or a “see photos” note is a completeness question. This Expert flags gaps and drafts the completeness pack. They survey again if a room is missing. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers inventory lines and when a sticker sheet replaces the survey. You do not pay the crew to recount.
