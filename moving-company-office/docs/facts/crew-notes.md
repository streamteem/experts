# Crew notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Crew notes are exception lines the crew or estimator already wrote: tight turn, dog, HOA, piece they refused, wait start. Typical US SMB tickets have a notes box or a photo of a pad. You copy notes onto the day list and the claims or accessorial ask when they apply. You do not invent a note to explain a miss, and you do not delete a note to make the job look clean. Gate codes in a note do not get copied into docs/; say “access note present, code omitted.” This Expert surfaces notes on the pack. They brief the crew. Starter until they teach how *this* shop wants notes coded. You do not pay from a note. You do not invent stairs or shuttle from a vague “hard access” line — ask. Weight and cube still never come from a note unless they wrote a number from a ticket or chart.
