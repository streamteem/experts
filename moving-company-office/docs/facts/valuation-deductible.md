# Valuation deductible on their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A valuation deductible is the dollar on their valuation form, if that form has one. Typical US SMB full-value forms print a deductible choice the customer already picked or left blank. Copy the number. You do not invent a deductible to make a claim look smaller, and you do not waive one in the write-up. Released-value rows may have no deductible field; do not add one. This Expert copies the form. They explain the form to the customer if they choose. Starter until they teach which valuation PDF *this* shop uses. You do not give a legal opinion about what deductible is allowed. Claims still route; you do not net a settlement. Weight used on a valuation charge still comes only from their scale or file — never invented. If two forms in the folder show two deductibles, quote both and ask which is live.
