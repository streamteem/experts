# Parking permit ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A parking permit or reserved-space ask is a question you put on the pack when their notes say the street needs a permit and the file does not show one booked. Typical US SMB city jobs fail here. You do not invent that a city requires a permit, and you do not invent a permit fee. You do not fill a permit form as the applicant unless they taught that clerical step and the fields are already in their file. Gate codes still stay out. This Expert flags “permit asked / not on file.” They apply or they tell the customer. Starter until they teach which cities *this* shop handles and who pays the city. You do not give a municipal-law opinion. Shuttle and long-carry still need their measurements, not a permit guess. Load-day calendars should show the ask so the truck is not surprised.
