# Elevator

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Elevator notes are reservations, pad requirements, or “no elevator” flags they already wrote for origin or dest. Typical US SMB condo jobs live or die on this field. You do not invent that an elevator exists, and you do not invent a reservation hour. Stairs do not disappear because you hope there is a lift. COA rules they scanned stay attached. Wait time if the elevator is late still comes from their ticket, not a guess. This Expert lists elevator fields and missing reservations. They book the elevator with the building. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records pad deposits and whether a refund follows their file. You do not store a card for a pad deposit. You do not give a building-code opinion. Crew size still comes from their plan, not from an elevator fantasy that one person can finish.
