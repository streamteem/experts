# Packing labor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Packing labor is crew time or a packed-by-carrier line from their rate file and ticket — not a wage you invent and not a promise you dispatch. Typical US SMB movers sell packing as hours, as a per-carton rate, or as a room package they already printed. Copy their unit. Do not invent hours so a day list looks staffed. Pack day and load day may be different calendar rows; do not collapse them. Unpack labor is a separate fact. Crew size for packing comes from their file, not from a hope that two packers finish a house. This Expert lists packing lines they stored. They assign the pack crew. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s pack-versus-load split and overtime rule. You do not pay packers. You do not give a tariff opinion that packing must be carrier-packed. High-value items they want packed still need the high-value list if that is their rule.
