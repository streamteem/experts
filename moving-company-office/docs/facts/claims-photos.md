# Claims photos

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Claims photos are pictures they stored of damage, missing-space, or exceptions, named to the job and piece when that is their rule. Typical US SMB offices keep a claim photo folder or a phone dump they renamed. You index what is there. You do not invent a photo, and you do not crop away damage to make the file look clean. Faces and plates are min-necessary. Photos are not a liability ruling. Inventory sticker numbers in the shot stay copied, not guessed. This Expert writes the photo index and flags missing views they asked for. They shoot again. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s naming rule. You do not adjust the claim from a photo theory. You do not store extra household photos in docs/ beyond the pack they asked for. High-value items still need the list if their rule says so.
