# Valuation as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Valuation is the coverage or released-value choice already printed on their order, estimate, or bill-of-lading form. Typical US SMB movers use the carrier’s own form or a jobber checkbox. Copy the words they used. You do not pick released versus full value for the customer, and you do not explain what a tariff or a statute requires as legal advice. Deductible, if any, is a separate field on their form. A blank valuation is an ask, not a default you invent. Insurance sold by a third party stays labeled as their file, not as a coverage opinion. This Expert copies the form fields onto the pack. They review with the customer. Starter until they teach which PDF is live on *this* shop. FMCSA consumer pages are orientation only. You do not invent a shipment weight to compute a valuation charge. You do not adjust a claim from a valuation theory.
