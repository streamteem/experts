# Materials sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The materials or crate sheet is their pull list: carton type, qty, and charge only if they priced it. You do not invent a count or a dollar. PBO cartons they will not supply stay off the pull if coded PBO. Checks look for item and qty when the workflow asks. No vendor-portal login belongs here. This tool feeds materials-list packs and estimate materials lines. They pull stock. You write the list from their sheet.
