# Inventory sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The inventory sheet is their item list: rooms, pieces, carton codes, and cube only if they filled it. Typical US SMB surveys export from the jobber or a workbook. You do not add a piece and you do not invent cube or weight. PBO versus CP stays as coded. High-value rows may live here or on a separate list. Checks expect job, item, and qty when the workflow says so. This tool feeds completeness, materials, and estimate packs. They resurvey gaps.
