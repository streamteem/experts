# Crew roster

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The crew roster is who can be assigned: name, crew or truck, and role they already wrote. Typical US SMB offices keep a short sheet or a board photo. You do not invent a person or a wage. Headcount on a job still needs their job plan. No payroll login belongs here. Checks look for crew and name columns on the day-list file. This tool feeds crew-day packs. They assign. You list who is on the roster they dropped.
