# Job / estimate workbook

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is their export or daybook: typically job, customer, origin, dest, date, and status. Typical US SMB desks export from SmartMoving, MoveitPro, or a numbered workbook. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a job number, a weight, or a rate. Export only; no login stored. Checks look for job and date or the columns the step names. This tool is the usual pack writer for lists and write-ups they asked for.
