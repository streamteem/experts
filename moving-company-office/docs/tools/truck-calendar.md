# Truck calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The truck calendar is unit, date, and job they already booked. Typical US SMB fleets use a workbook or the jobber dispatch board exported to CSV. You do not invent a unit or a free day. Conflicts stay visible. Empty weight on this sheet is not a scale ticket unless they said so. No ELD password belongs here. Checks look for truck and date when the workflow asks. This tool feeds truck-calendar and load-day collision packs. They dispatch.
