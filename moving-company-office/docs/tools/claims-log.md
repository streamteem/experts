# Claims log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The claims log is their workbook of claim number, job, status, and who owns the row. You list and route. You do not set a settlement or invent a missing piece. Deadline words stay quoted from their form, not issued as law. No insurer-portal password belongs here. Checks look for claim and job when the workflow says so. This tool feeds claims-routing packs. They adjust. You assemble the file list and write-up.
