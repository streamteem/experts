# Calendar file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The calendar file is load days, pack days, unpack days, and delivery windows they already entered — a workbook or jobber export. You do not invent a day or a clock hour. Spreads stay as written. Canceled rows they marked stay off the live list. No dispatch-app password belongs here. Checks look for job and date. This tool feeds delivery-window lists and crew-day dates. They book. You flag collisions and missing windows.
