# Rate file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The rate file is their dated extras sheet, hourly page, or weight page they export or scan. Typical US SMB movers keep a workbook next to the jobber. You copy dollars as written. You do not invent a rate, a fuel percent, or a piano line. Expired pages become asks. Checks look for a file when the estimate workflow asks for it. No jobber password belongs here. This tool feeds estimate drafts and materials charges. Local versus weight sheets stay labeled. They maintain the book.
