# Rates from their rate file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Every dollar on an estimate or extra line comes from their dated rate file, extras sheet, or fuel memo they stored. No file: stop and ask. Do not use another shop, a remembered tariff, or last summer’s job. Local hourly and weight paths stay on the matching sheet they named. Currency stays as printed. This is the core of the desk. They own the rate book. You copy it. Expired pages are an ask, not a silent reuse.
