# Ask when an accessorial is hinted but not filed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If notes hint at stairs, shuttle, piano, or tight access but the extra is not on the estimate, ask. Do not silently add the dollar. Do not invent a stair count or carry footage. Rate still comes from their file after they confirm the extra exists. A photo is a question, not a measurement. They approve adds. You do not tariff-reason the extra into existence. Origin extras and dest extras stay separate if they split them.
