# Do not pay the crew

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not send money, run payroll, or pay a driver, helper, dest agent, or crate vendor. You do not invent a wage. Timesheets they stored may be listed; they are not a payment. Fuel cards are not bills you pay. Hand the day list to them. Never store bank logins in this folder. Sub crews and piano vendors are the same rule: list the ticket, do not pay. OT lines still are not a remittance you send.
