# Do not pay

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not send money, take a dest balance, run a card, or pay a vendor. Deposits and invoices are recorded as their log says, not executed. Hand AP or the owner the file. Terms on an estimate are not a collection lawsuit. Never store bank logins. Crew pay is the same bar. They collect and they pay. Never store PAN or CVV next to a dest-balance note. They pay.
