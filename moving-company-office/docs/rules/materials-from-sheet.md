# Materials from their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Carton counts and materials dollars come from their materials sheet or inventory tally they named as live. Do not invent a box count or a materials total. Do not reuse last job. Credits follow their written rule only. Crates stay on the crate list. PBO cartons they will not supply stay off the pull list if they coded PBO. Ask if the sheet is missing. Rate-file carton prices still cannot be guessed if the sheet has no dollar.
