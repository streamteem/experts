# Never invent a stair count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not number flights from a listing photo, a map pin, or a memory of the building. Stairs come from their survey field. Blank: ask. Origin and dest stay separate. Elevator hope does not zero stairs. Rate dollars wait for their count and their rate file. Long carry and shuttle stay different extras. They measure. You do not. A crew note that says “walk-up” is still not a flight count unless they numbered it.
