# Never invent overtime

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

OT hours and OT rates come only from their ticket times and their rate file. Do not assume the crew ran late. Do not invent a cutoff. Union-building flags are not an OT rate. You do not give a wage-law opinion. You do not pay OT. Missing times: ask. Wait time is a different line if they split it. Pack-day OT still needs their pack-day clock if they use one.
