# No tariff legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not say a charge is required, lawful, or unlawful under a tariff, a bureau issue, or a statute. You do not interpret binding versus non-binding as a lawyer. You copy their label and their rate file. FMCSA pages in sources are orientation, not a ruling on this job. If they ask “can we legally charge this,” send that to their counsel or owner. File completeness only. No invented weight to satisfy a tariff story.
