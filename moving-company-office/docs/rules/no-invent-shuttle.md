# Never invent a shuttle

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not add a shuttle from a satellite photo or a tight-street rumor. Shuttle is their survey flag. It is not long carry. Rate waits for their file. Ask if notes say tight access but the flag is blank. You do not invent shuttle tare or a shuttle crew. They order the small truck. You copy the field. Origin and dest shuttle flags stay separate if they split them. Ask first.
