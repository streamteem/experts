# Ask if binding type is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the estimate or order has no binding, non-binding, or not-to-exceed label, stop and ask. Do not guess. Do not relabel to calm a total. Their file is the only source. You do not opine which type the law requires. A later change they stored is a before/after, not a silent overwrite. They pick the product they sell. You do not invent a weight to force a binding-looking total. Ask first.
