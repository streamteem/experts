# Inventory completeness stays visible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Missing rooms, blank cube columns, missing high-value list when their rule requires it, and “see photos” with no photo stay on the pack as questions. Do not invent lines so the inventory looks finished. Do not hide a gap in a comment. PBO versus CP codes stay as written. They resurvey. You write the hole list. A completeness pack is not a scale ticket and not an estimate total. Blank cube stays blank; you never invent cubic feet.
