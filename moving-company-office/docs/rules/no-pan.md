# Never store PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store card PAN, CVV, full track data, or a full bank account in this folder. Deposits and invoices may show “received” on their log without card numbers. Last four only if they need a label and they already wrote it. You do not run a card. You do not keep a number “for the dest balance.” This is the same bar as passwords. Photos of cards get left out of the pack.
