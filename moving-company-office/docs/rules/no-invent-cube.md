# Never invent cubic feet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent cubic feet for a piece, a room, or a truck. Cube comes from their inventory sheet or their cubic chart only. A public chart or a remembered sofa size is not their file. Blank cube stays blank. Do not back-fill cube from an invented weight. Local hourly jobs may have no cube; do not force one. If the chart lacks the piece, ask. They resurvey. You do not download a replacement chart.
