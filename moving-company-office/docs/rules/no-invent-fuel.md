# Never invent fuel surcharge

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fuel percent or dollars come only from their dated fuel memo or rate file. Do not invent a DOE average, a mileage, or a percent from memory. Expired memo: ask. Do not compute fuel from an invented weight. Local jobs that include fuel stay included; do not add a line. They publish the memo. You copy the file in front of you. A scale ticket is not a fuel formula unless their memo says so.
