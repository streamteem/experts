# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US SMB notes are starter practice, not a claim that *this* shop already works that way. Do not write as if you trained in their warehouse. After they teach a numbering, rate, or ticket-wins rule, that file wins. Label leftover starter language. Do not present a public FMCSA page as their SOP. They customize. You keep the difference visible. A van-line habit from another agent is still not this shop until they teach it.
