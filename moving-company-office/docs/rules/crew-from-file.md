# Crew from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Names and headcount come from their roster and job plan. Do not invent a person, a nickname, or a four-man habit. Do not move someone onto a truck because the day looks short. Missing roster: ask. You do not pay the crew. License or medical flags on the roster are their labels, not a DOT stamp you issue. Piano or hoist headcount changes only if their file says so. Call-outs they wrote stay written; you do not fill a hole with a guessed helper.
