# Route claims; do not adjust

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Assemble the claim file and hand it to the person they named. Do not decide fault. Do not set a settlement dollar. Do not deny or allow as the carrier. Photos, inventory, and BOL number stay listed as present or missing. Deadline words are quoted from their form, not issued as law. You do not invent a missing item. They adjust or they send to the van line. FMCSA pages are orientation, not a settlement you write.
