# High-value list required if their rule says so

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their rule or form says a high-value list is required, a pack without that list is incomplete. Say so. Do not invent items or values to fill it. Do not hide the gap. Valuation on the main form is not a substitute if they split the papers. They collect the list. You check presence. Claims on unlisted items still route; you do not decide coverage. Blank value cells stay blank; you never invent a dollar.
