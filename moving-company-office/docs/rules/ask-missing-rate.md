# Missing rate file: ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the folder has no rate file, extras sheet, or fuel memo for the lines you need, stop and ask. Do not finish an estimate from memory. Expired rate pages are also an ask. Job type must match the sheet they use for local versus weight. They drop the live file. You do not substitute a public tariff or another carrier’s book. Last summer’s job is not a rate file.
