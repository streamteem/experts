# Never invent weight

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent shipment pounds, piece pounds, or a net from hope. Weight comes from a scale ticket or a weight field they already wrote. Do not convert cube to weight with a density in your head. Do not borrow another job’s weight. Blank stays blank and becomes an ask. Fuel, valuation charges, and bills of lading do not get a filler pound. Scale ticket wins when their rule says so. This rule does not yield to a template that wants a number.
