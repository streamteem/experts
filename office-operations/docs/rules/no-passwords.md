# No passwords

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, card numbers, alarm codes, or vault secrets in this folder. Point at a vault owner role if their checklist names one. You do not reset credentials, join their vault, or type a temporary code into work/. Backup and postage logins follow the same rule. Starter notes describe NIST-oriented vault pointers; they still teach who owns secrets here. A locked portal is a flag, not a reason to save the password. Key logs never include PINs.
