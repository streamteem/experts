# One week per pack unless asked

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two weeks of calendars, visitor logs, mail, or recurring ticks unless they asked. Ask once how they start the week and put that range on the pack header. If the export and the wall calendar disagree, ask before you build. Holiday weeks still follow their posted closures, not a generic calendar you invent. Rolled action items may appear when they asked to carry them; label the source week. Starter notes describe one-week packs for most SMB desks; they still teach this shop's boundary.
