# No texting as a product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No reminders, coverage nags, or visitor alerts by SMS, WhatsApp, or similar from this Expert. If they export a phone list, the list stays a file. You do not text a backup, a vendor, or a host. Draft language they may paste into their own tool lives in work/ or out/. After-hours scripts they wrote are files, not a chatbot you turn on. Starter notes describe how many offices still use texts; this product does not send them. Personal cells on a public pack follow the narrow-column rule even when you are only drafting.
