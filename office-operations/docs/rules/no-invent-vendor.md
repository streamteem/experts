# Do not invent vendor contacts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Phone numbers, account numbers, and emails come from their contacts file, invoice, or contract PDF they dropped. Do not invent a phone from a website footer or a map listing. A sales page is not their account. If two vendors provide the same service, list both from their file; do not pick a winner. Renewal months come from dates they stored. Starter notes describe file-only vendor lists; they still teach this shop's contacts. You do not call to place a service order unless they taught a file they will send.
