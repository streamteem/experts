# Do not invent the drive

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ask where the file already lives on Drive, SharePoint, a NAS, or a cabinet. Do not create a new company-wide tree or 'migrate' contracts into a prettier structure. Keep their filenames, especially on signed PDFs. A starter label is a guess until they teach. Work/ and out/ are for this pack's named outputs, not a license to reorganize their live drive. If two folders share a vendor name, ask which is current. Starter notes describe common SMB sprawl; they still teach this shop's path.
