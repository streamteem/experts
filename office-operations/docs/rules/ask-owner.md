# Ask who owns the task

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If it is unclear who does the step, who holds the license reminder, or who owns a recurring Friday line, ask in the write-up. Do not pick a name to fill a blank and do not assign yourself. A blank owner is not pressure on the nearest person on the desk map. Coverage names come only from a grid they already filled. When two files disagree on owner, list both and ask. Starter notes describe how often SMB checklists omit names; they still teach this shop's owners. You do not use assignment as discipline.
