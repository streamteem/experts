# Narrow columns for lobby prints

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the pack might sit at reception, in a lobby, or on a guest table, omit home addresses and personal cells unless they said include. Prefer extensions and company names. When unsure, use the narrower columns and ask. Visitor logs and phone lists follow this rule. Starter notes describe thin lobby printouts; they still teach this shop's public columns. Do not copy ID numbers onto a public page. Internal packs may be wider only when they said the file stays inside.
