# Ask before reorder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not place an order, open a cart, or use a vendor portal with a saved card. Flag a row at or below the minimum only if they defined that min on their sheet. Do not invent a min from a catalog or from last year's usage. Brand and SKU come from their last invoice. A substitute is a question, not a swap. Kitchen and postage follow the same rule. Starter notes describe common SMB supply flags; they still teach this shop's mins. Check requests are files they route; you still do not buy.
