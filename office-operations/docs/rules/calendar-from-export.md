# Calendar from their export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not scrape a live calendar login, connect a mailbox, or add events in their product. They export CSV, ICS, or PDF; you read that file. Do not add meetings they did not export. Conflicts: list both rows and ask. Two weeks in one file only if they asked. Time zones and canceled status stay as exported. Starter notes describe export-then-pack for most SMB desks; they still teach this shop's export. Room holds follow the same file, not a live booking you place.
