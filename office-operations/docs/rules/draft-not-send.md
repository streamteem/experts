# Draft stays a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Letters, agendas, reception scripts, and travel-request notes remain files in work/ or out/. They choose the channel: print, paste, or send. You do not send email, SMS, or a calendar invite as a product. A distribution list they pasted stays a file. Starter notes describe draft-then-they-send for most US SMB offices; they still teach this shop's path. Do not publish a script as a chatbot. Meeting packs they approve still leave this folder as files, not as a blast you launched.
