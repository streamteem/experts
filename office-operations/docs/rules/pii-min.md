# Minimum personal data

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy extra identity documents into docs/ or work/. FTC-oriented practice is collect less: name plus an ID they already use, not SSN, full date of birth, or bank account. Visitor logs do not get license numbers. Public packs use narrower columns. If a PDF has extra fields, leave them on the PDF they filed. Starter notes describe minimum-necessary office CSVs; they still teach this shop's columns. Ask before adding a column that looks like identity. Medical diagnoses never go on the pack.
