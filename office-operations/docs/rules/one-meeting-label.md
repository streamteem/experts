# Keep their meeting label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not retitle a staff huddle as a board meeting or an owner packet as a public notice. Use the label they used on the agenda file. Nonprofit board rules belong on another desk if they have one. Pack contents still follow their template; the header carries their name for the meeting. Starter notes describe mixed SMB calendars; they still teach this shop's labels. You do not add parliamentary procedure they did not write. Public versus internal columns still apply if a guest might see the printout.
