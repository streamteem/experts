# Do not reclassify workers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Roster labels stay. Tax classification is their advisor's job. You never move a person from employee to contractor or the reverse. If the directory and the vendor list disagree, list both labels and ask. Do not apply an IRS test in the write-up. 1099 and W-9 flags are presence of paper, not a classification you chose. Starter notes point at SBA hire pages as orientation; those pages are not your call. You do not set pay or hire.
