# No customer breach notice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not notify customers, staff as IT, or regulators about a suspected account or data incident. Stop and hand the note to the role they named. Do not reset passwords, forward phish as a blast, or dump a mailbox into this folder. SBA and NIST pages treat response as the organization's job. Starter notes describe a hand-off; they still teach who this shop calls. A failed backup is a flag, not an incident you announce. Keep their words; do not diagnose the attack.
