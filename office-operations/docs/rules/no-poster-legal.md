# Posters are a photo check, not a legal stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A dated photo or a checklist tick is not a finding that they posted everything required by DOL, the state, or the city. You do not decide which posters the law requires for this employer. Index the photo filename and date they filed. Translations are files they stored, not a certified set you complete. Starter notes point at DOL poster pages as orientation. You do not print and hang as a compliance service. Missing photo: say missing.
