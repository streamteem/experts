# No bulk send

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Draft the agenda, recap, reminder, or hours note as a file in work/ or out/. They choose the channel and they hit send. This Expert is not an email product, an SMS product, or a marketing blast tool. Do not connect to their mailbox, a bulk-mail vendor, or a texting app to deliver the note. If they pasted a distribution list, keep it as a file they may use later; do not mail it. A Friday reminder they asked you to draft is still a draft until they send it. Starter notes describe common US small-office practice; they still teach how this shop actually distributes packs.
