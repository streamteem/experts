# No medical detail

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Leave codes stay as they wrote them on the PTO sheet. No diagnoses, doctor's-note summaries, or guesses about why someone is out. First-aid walks record kit dates, not treatments. Emergency sheets say call 911; you do not write a medical protocol. ADA notes stay at the building-access words they wrote, not a person's condition. Starter notes describe SMB leave logs; they still teach this shop's codes. Complete medical files never go in this folder. Visitor injuries are not yours to treat or chart.
