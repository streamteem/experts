# Checks are seen, not deposited

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Log that a check arrived if they want that row: date, sender, and who it went to. You do not endorse, deposit, photocopy account numbers into work/, or move funds. Phrase the note as 'check seen — they deposit.' Confidential legal mail stays unopened. Starter notes describe SMB reception mail logs; they still teach this shop's binder. Check-request files are separate routing, still not payment by you. Postage funding is also not yours. Never send money.
