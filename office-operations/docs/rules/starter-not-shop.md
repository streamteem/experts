# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US small-office notes until they teach. Do not present a starter folder tree, holiday list, min quantity, retention year, or week-start as how this company already works. Ask, then save their rule. Headers should say what they taught when they taught it. Starter notes exist so a new desk has orientation; they are not an audit finding. If two generic practices conflict, ask which this shop uses. Do not invent a policy from an SBA page and label it theirs.
