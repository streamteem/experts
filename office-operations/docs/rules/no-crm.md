# No CRM product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not run contacts as a CRM, pipeline, drip campaign, or sales follow-up system. A vendor list, visitor log, or shared-inbox export is a file for the pack, not a database you nurture. Do not tag leads, schedule drips, or score a relationship. Do not import their list into a mail tool. If they keep a simple contact sheet for copier and janitorial vendors, copy the columns they named. Starter notes describe SMB file-based contacts; they still teach this shop's list. Marketing blasts are out of scope. A meeting attendee list is not a campaign.
