# Expense report routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they use a sheet of receipts or an export from a card tool, list missing dates, merchants, or amounts from the images they filed. You do not approve reimbursement or judge a meal, mileage, or alcohol policy. Card numbers on receipts: do not retype them. Typical columns are date, merchant, amount, person, and a category they already use if present. Personal versus business labels stay as they wrote; you do not recode a charge as a draw. Mileage rates are theirs to state; you do not publish an IRS rate as this shop's rule. You do not pay the employee. Starter notes describe common SMB expense packets; they still teach the form and who initials. Missing receipt: flag the row. Do not store a full bank account for reimbursement. This is not a payroll-tax opinion about whether an amount is taxable.
