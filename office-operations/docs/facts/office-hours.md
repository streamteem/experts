# Posted hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Posted hours and holiday closures come from their posted list, website copy they filed, or a door sign photo they dated. Do not invent a holiday calendar from federal or state holidays. State holiday versus they-are-open: ask. Typical fields are weekday hours, lunch if they close, and named closure dates they wrote. After-hours access for cleaners or owners belongs on their access note, not mixed into a public hours line unless they said so. You do not change the voicemail greeting as a phone product. Weather closures they recorded are dates they named, not a forecast you add. Starter notes describe how most US SMB offices post hours; they still teach this shop's list. A visitor pack should use the same hours they posted. Do not assume Monday start for the week header until they confirm week start. Remote or hybrid days are a separate log if they keep one.
