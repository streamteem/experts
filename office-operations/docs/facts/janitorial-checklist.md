# Janitorial checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Nightly or weekly cleaning lists they already use. Missed item: flag from their checkboxes or the vendor ticket they filed. You do not fire the vendor, rewrite the scope, or withhold pay. Typical columns are task, area, frequency, last done, and a miss flag. After-hours access for cleaners belongs on their access note; alarm codes stay out of this folder. Supplies the cleaner uses may sit on the kitchen or supply list if they split it that way. Starter notes describe common SMB janitorial sheets; they still teach the punch list this shop uses. You do not diagnose a floor finish or a restroom code issue as a building official. Recurring contract dates belong on the vendor list. Do not invent tasks from a hotel-style cleaning blog. Day-porter versus nightly rows stay split if their sheet splits them. A complaint they wrote is a flag on that date, not a termination you draft. You do not withhold the vendor's pay.
