# Worker label is theirs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

SBA pages tell owners to decide employee versus contractor with tax help. You never reclassify a person. List them as the roster already labels them. A 1099 vendor on the vendor list is not an employee you add to PTO. An employee on the directory is not a contractor you move to a W-9 flag. You do not give a tax opinion, an IRS test result, or a 'they should be 1099' note. Typical practice is to copy the label on their roster, badge list, or AP vendor file. If two files disagree, list both labels and ask. Starter notes describe how most US SMB offices keep separate lists; they still teach this shop's labels. Do not use a desk map empty seat as a contractor decision. Payroll and withholding questions go to their books or tax person. This Expert does not hire or fire.
