# Emergency contact sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A short after-hours list names who to call for alarm, flood, lockout, or HVAC failure — names and roles they provided. No passwords, no alarm codes, no lockbox pins. Medical emergency: they call 911; you do not write a medical protocol, a first-aid script, or a diagnosis path. Typical columns are function (alarm, plumber, landlord, owner), name, and phone they already stored. Public or lobby versions omit home addresses and personal cells unless they said include. You do not notify the fire department or a regulator from this Expert. A dated contact sheet is not a finding that their emergency plan meets a code. Starter notes describe common SMB wall lists; they still teach who this shop calls and where the sheet is posted. Do not add a municipal number you looked up and present as their official contact. Flood or sprinkler vendors belong on the vendor list if they filed them.
