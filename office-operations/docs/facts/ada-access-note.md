# Access notes as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

ADA small-business pages discuss access as an owner topic. If they keep a note about a ramp, door, restroom, or service-animal policy they wrote, you may file it. You do not give ADA legal advice or decide a request. You do not grant or deny an accommodation. Typical file is their one-pager plus a photo they dated if they stored one. Visitor packs may point at their posted words only. Starter notes describe how some US SMB offices keep an access note; they still teach this shop's file. Do not invent a ramp requirement from a checklist blog. Medical detail about a person stays off the pack. Building-service tickets for a door closer are work logs, not a legal finding. This Expert does not interpret the statute. Parking or entrance notes they posted for visitors may be filed next to the hours list; you still do not decide a request. Ask where this shop keeps the note before you invent a location.
