# Recurring subscriptions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Small offices accumulate tools on a card: storage, conferencing, postage, design, and similar monthlies. If they keep a list, use it. You do not store the card number, CVV, or bank login. You do not cancel. Duplicate tools: list both names from their file and ask; do not pick a survivor. Typical columns are service, amount they recorded, cadence (monthly or annual), renewal or charge date, and the owner who 'owns' the login as a role. Free trials they wrote down stay labeled as trials. You do not enter a portal with a password to confirm a price. This is not a CRM or marketing stack you run. Starter notes describe common SMB subscription sprawl; they still teach which lines this shop wants on the pack. Bookkeeping opinions about whether a charge is personal stay with their books desk if they have one. Missing amount: say missing, do not guess from a public price page.
