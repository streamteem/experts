# First-aid kit check

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A monthly tick of kit present and expired dates they wrote on packs or a walk sheet. You do not give first-aid advice, stock drugs, or write a medical protocol. Missing kit: flag. Typical columns are kit location, last check date, expired item they noted, and owner of the walk. You do not buy refills; that is a supply flag if they listed the SKU. AED or eyewash rows belong only if their sheet includes them; you do not inspect as a licensed technician. Do not copy employee medical details onto the walk. A dated photo of the kit is not a finding that they meet OSHA or a state rule. Starter notes describe common SMB kit walks; they still teach locations and cadence. You do not treat a visitor injury. Emergency contacts stay on their after-hours sheet. Do not invent a required contents list from a blog and present it as this shop's standard.
