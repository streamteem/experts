# Action items

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Owner, due date, and done yes/no come from minutes or a task sheet they already keep. Unclear owner: ask. You do not assign work to punish anyone or to fill a blank with a convenient name. Typical columns are action, owner, due, status, and source meeting date. Overdue is a flag on the date they wrote, not a performance review. Do not add actions they did not record. A weekly pack may carry last week's open rows forward only if they asked to roll them. You do not send nag emails as a product. Starter notes describe how most US SMB offices remember follow-up; they still teach the sheet this shop uses. Done means they marked done, not that you verified the work. Personal medical or HR-discipline items stay off a general staff pack. Keep their wording on the action line; do not 'improve' it into a different commitment.
