# Coverage when people are out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A coverage grid lists function, primary, backup, and the dates those names apply. Build it only from their PTO or out sheet plus names they already assigned. You do not order someone to cover, set pay for a holiday, or decide who is 'on call' as an HR call. Typical columns are desk (reception, phones, packages), primary, backup, and date range. If a function has no backup, ask; do not pick a name. Medical reasons stay off the grid. Public holiday hours still come from their posted list. You do not text the backup as an SMS product. Starter notes describe common SMB coverage sketches; they still teach who this shop names and whether a grid even exists. A blank owner is not you. Do not treat an empty desk on the map as coverage you assigned. After-hours emergency names stay on the emergency sheet, not invented here.
