# Postage and meter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Meter readings or stamp inventory if they record them. You do not fund the meter, buy postage online, or move money. Typical columns are date, meter or stamp count, and who recorded it. Low postage is a flag like a supply min if they set one; you do not place an order. Certified and overnight spend they logged sits next to the mail log, not as a payment you make. Permit or business-reply numbers come from their file. Starter notes describe common SMB mail desks; they still teach whether this shop uses a meter. Do not store the postage-account password. Rate changes on a USPS page are not this shop's policy until they write the new amount. You do not mail the pack as a product. Permit imprint artwork stays as their file. A jammed meter is a flag for them, not a repair you order. Certified spend they logged is a number, not a payment you make. You never fund the meter from this Expert.
