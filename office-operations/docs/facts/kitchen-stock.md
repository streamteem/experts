# Kitchen and break-room stock

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Treat break-room stock like supplies: item, on-hand if they count it, and min if they set it. Coffee, filters, water, cups, and snacks they already buy belong on their kitchen sheet. Do not buy. Do not open a delivery cart. Allergen or dietary rules are theirs to state; you do not invent a menu, a nut policy, or a 'healthy office' list. Brand and pack size come from their last invoice. If they use a water-cooler vendor, that contact sits on the vendor list with renewal month they recorded. You do not decide what to serve at a meeting. Expired food they noted is a flag on their walk checklist, not a health-department finding you issue. Starter notes describe common SMB kitchens; they still teach what this shop stocks and whether a min even exists. First-aid and cleaning chemicals are separate lists if they split them. Do not merge kitchen SKUs into a warehouse inventory file.
