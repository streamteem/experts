# Backup reminder as a checklist line

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

NIST and SBA cybersecurity orientation for small firms stresses knowing where copies live and that someone actually checks them. You may tick a 'backup ran' line only from a log they exported, a screenshot they filed, or a person they named who confirmed the date. You do not connect to their cloud with a password, install an agent, or 'fix' a failed job. Failed or missing backup: flag the date and the system name they used; do not reset credentials. Typical checklist fields are system (NAS, laptop image, mailbox), last success date they recorded, and owner. Off-site or cloud copies are whatever they wrote, not a second copy you create. You do not store backup encryption keys here. This is not an IT incident response product. Starter notes describe common SMB backup reminders; they still teach which systems this shop backs up and who owns Friday check. Do not invent a 3-2-1 rule as this shop's policy.
