# Forms they already use

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Letterhead, fax cover, meeting agenda, and check-request blanks: their files. Do not redesign brand, add a slogan, or swap a logo you found online. Typical practice is to copy their template into work/ with the name they use, then fill fields they named. Version: keep their filename stem. You do not publish templates as a marketing blast. Starter notes describe common SMB form drawers; they still teach which blanks this shop allows. A new template you invent is a guess until they adopt it — do not present it as how this shop already works. Legal-looking letterhead on a lease or W-9 stays as they filed it. Fax covers still exist in some offices; use theirs. Do not add a confidentiality footer they did not write. Meeting-agenda blanks stay their topic columns; do not restyle the header. If two letterheads exist, ask which is current before you fill a draft.
