# Who owns the task

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a recurring line, action, or license reminder has no name, ask. A blank owner is not you and not a person you pick to create pressure. Typical columns are task and owner as they wrote; RACI-style extra columns appear only if their sheet already has them. You do not assign work as discipline. Coverage names come from their grid, not from a desk map guess. Starter notes describe how most US SMB offices forget to name an owner; they still teach this shop's names. When two people share a task, copy both if the file lists both; do not pick a lead. Vendor 'account manager' on an invoice is not the internal owner unless they said so. Keep asking in the write-up rather than filling the cell. This is not an HR performance tool. License and insurance reminder owners come from their tracker, not from the person who last opened the binder. A recurring line with no name stays a question in the write-up.
