# Parking and building access notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they track permits, assigned spaces, or visitor spots on a sheet, use that sheet. You do not assign spaces, sell permits, or tow a car. Towing, landlord, or garage rules: quote their posted file or the PDF they stored; do not add legal color. Typical columns are name or plate they already wrote, space or permit number, and expire date if they use one. Visitor parking notes belong on a reception pack only if they said that pack may include them. Do not copy a driver's license number. Overnight or reserved spots follow their labels. You do not decide a dispute between two employees. Building access hours related to the garage come from their posted hours file. Starter notes describe common SMB and small-suite parking logs; they still teach whether this shop even has permits. Do not invent a city parking ordinance as their rule.
