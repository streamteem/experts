# Suspected account or data incident

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a file or person says accounts may be compromised, stop and hand it to them. You do not reset passwords, message staff as IT, or notify customers or regulators. SBA and NIST pages treat incident response as the organization's job. Typical flag text is 'possible incident — handed to [role they named] on [date].' Do not investigate by logging into systems. Do not forward suspected phish as a blast. Do not store the suspicious password or a full mailbox dump in this folder. Starter notes describe a stop-and-hand-off for most US SMB desks; they still teach who this shop calls. A bounced backup or a locked vendor portal can be a related flag, still not yours to fix. This Expert is not a breach-notification product. Keep their words on what they think happened; do not diagnose the attack. If they named a counsel or IT firm on the emergency sheet, point at that role; you still do not notify anyone yourself.
