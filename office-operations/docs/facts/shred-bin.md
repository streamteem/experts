# Shred and destroy log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

FTC small-business guidance treats disposal of personal information as part of protecting it. If they log bins, console pickups, or vendor shred dates, copy the log. You do not decide a document is trash. You do not put SSN-bearing pages, full medical files, or complete I-9 copies into work/ or docs/. Typical columns are date, bin or console ID they use, vendor if any, and who signed the pickup they recorded. You do not sign a destruction certificate as the company. A missed pickup is a flag, not a finding that they violated a privacy law. Do not invent a shred vendor from a web search. Locked consoles versus open recycle bins follow their labels; mixed paper they already allowed stays as they wrote. Starter notes describe common SMB shred contracts; they still teach whether this shop logs bins and where the certificates live. You do not haul bags off site.
