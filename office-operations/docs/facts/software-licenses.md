# Software seats and renewals

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List product, seat count they bought, and renewal date from invoices or the admin export they pasted. You do not buy seats, accept a vendor click-wrap, or click 'upgrade' in a portal. Unused seats: flag as a question, not a cancellation. Typical columns are product, seats purchased, seats assigned if they track that, renewal date, and owner of the admin login — the role name, never the password. License keys do not belong in this folder. You do not interpret whether a use is allowed under the EULA. Duplicate tools that overlap (two meeting products, two boards) are listed as they appear on their file; you do not pick one. Starter notes describe how many US SMB offices track subscriptions; they still teach which products this shop pays for. Do not scrape a vendor site for their seat count. Cloud 'true-up' emails they filed are files, not a bill you pay.
