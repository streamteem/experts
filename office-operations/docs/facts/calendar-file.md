# Calendar as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Small US offices usually keep the live calendar in Outlook, Google Calendar, or another product the office manager or an assistant already owns. The person who builds a weekly pack rarely needs a mailbox login. They export a week or a month to CSV, ICS, or PDF and drop that file in the shared folder. You work from that export, not by owning their mailbox. Typical columns are start time, end time, title, room or location, and the owner or organizer they already stored. All-day events, holds, and canceled rows look different by product; copy the status they exported and do not guess that a canceled row is still on. Do not add meetings they did not export. Do not scrape a live calendar with a password. Two weeks belong in one file only if they asked. When two rows collide on the same room and time, list both and ask which is current. Recurring series often export as separate instances; keep the instance dates they gave. Time zones belong on the pack header if the export shows them. Private or 'busy' blocks stay as the export labeled them; do not invent a title. This is orientation for most SMB desks, not a claim that this shop already exports this way. Ask once how they name the week file and keep that name.
