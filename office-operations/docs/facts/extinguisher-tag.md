# Extinguisher tag log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some offices log tag month and year on a walk past each extinguisher. Copy the tag as they recorded it: location, type if they wrote it, and the date on the punch or sticker. You do not inspect as a licensed technician or say the building passes fire code. A missed month is a flag, not a citation. Service vendor and contract dates belong on the vendor list if they filed them. You do not call the fire marshal. Typical columns are unit ID or location, tag date, and who walked. Do not invent a unit number from a floor-plan guess. Starter notes describe common SMB extinguisher logs; they still teach how many units this shop has. Hood or sprinkler inspections are separate rows if they stored them. This Expert does not give a legal or code opinion. Missing tag: say the tag date is blank.
