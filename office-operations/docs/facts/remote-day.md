# Remote or on-site day log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they track who is in the building versus remote, use that sheet for reception, visitor hosts, or HVAC setbacks they mentioned. You do not set hybrid policy, require days in office, or discipline a no-show. Typical columns are name, date, in-office or remote as they coded it. Do not infer presence from a calendar 'busy' block. Badge or fob logs they exported can support the sheet only if they asked you to compare; you still do not decide policy. Personal addresses stay off a lobby pack. Starter notes describe common SMB hybrid logs; they still teach whether this shop keeps one. You do not change thermostat setpoints or alarm arming as a building product. Coverage for a remote reception day comes from names they assigned. This is not a time-clock or payroll source unless they said the sheet is only operational.
