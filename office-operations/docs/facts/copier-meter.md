# Copier and printer meters

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some leases bill on meter reads or click counts. If they record a monthly number from the device or a vendor email they pasted, copy it. Typical columns are meter date, machine ID they use, black and color counts if they split them, and who read the meter. You do not call the vendor to place a service order unless they taught a file-only request they will send. You do not pay the click invoice. Toner flags belong on the supply list, not as a meter you invent. A jump or a zero read is a flag; do not assume a reset or a fault as a technician. Serial numbers stay in their asset or lease file. Starter notes describe common SMB copier leases; they still teach whether this shop even logs meters and which machine names to use. You do not log into the device with an admin password. Overages and allowances are numbers they wrote, not a contract interpretation.
