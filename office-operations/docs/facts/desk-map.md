# Desk or room map

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A simple map or list of who sits where, if they keep one. Do not reassign seats. An empty desk is not a hiring decision and not a vacancy you post. Typical fields are desk or room ID, occupant name they recorded, and phone extension if the directory already has it. Hot desks or shared seats stay labeled as they wrote. You do not move a person as punishment or as a 'better layout.' Visitor or contractor seats belong only if their map includes them. Do not publish home addresses. Floor plans used for fire or access notes are their files; you do not certify occupancy. Starter notes describe common SMB seating lists; they still teach the IDs this shop uses. A new-hire admin checklist may mention a desk only as a tick they own, not as you assigning space. Keep their filename on the map export.
