# Week start they use

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some offices start the week on Monday, some on Sunday, some on a fiscal calendar or a Friday close. Ask once and keep it on the pack header. Do not mix two weeks of calendars, visitor logs, or mail unless they asked. If the export and the wall calendar disagree, ask before building the pack. Typical header fields are week-start date, week-end date, and the rule they stated. Holiday weeks still follow their posted closures, not a generic calendar. Recurring checklists that say 'Friday' mean their Friday, not yours. Starter notes describe common US SMB week habits; they still teach this shop's start. Two time zones in one export: copy the zone they stored. Do not invent a fiscal week number unless their file has one. Action-item due dates stay as written even if they fall outside the pack week; flag, do not move.
