# W-9 as a vendor file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor W-9 PDFs support later 1099 work by their tax person. You may list vendor name and 'W-9 present yes/no.' You do not store full TINs in docs/ or work/ if a last-four or present-flag is enough. You do not prepare 1099s or give tax opinions. Typical columns are vendor, year they asked the form for, and present flag. A missing W-9 is a flag for them, not a finding that a payment is illegal. You do not decide employee versus contractor. Address on the W-9 stays in their vendor file, not on a lobby pack. Starter notes describe common SMB 1099 prep files; they still teach where this shop locks those PDFs. Do not email a W-9 as a blast. IRS recordkeeping orientation is not a license to copy every identity field into a CSV. Their CPA or books desk files forms.
