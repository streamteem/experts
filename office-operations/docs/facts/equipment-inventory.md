# Office equipment list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Chairs, laptops, monitors, phones, and similar office assets often have a tag, a serial if they recorded it, and who holds the item. You do not assign a laptop or decide who gets a new chair. Serials stay in their asset file, not in a public write-up if they said the pack might be printed for a lobby. Typical columns are tag, description, serial or asset ID they use, location or holder, and purchase or warranty date if they stored it. You do not buy replacements. Lost or missing items are flags from their last count, not an accusation. Do not invent a serial from a photo unless they already typed it. Personal devices they allow under a BYOD note stay labeled as they wrote; you do not enroll a device. Starter notes describe common SMB asset lists; they still teach which items this shop tracks. Warranty claims are theirs to file. Do not store admin passwords for the laptop next to the serial.
