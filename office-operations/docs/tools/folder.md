# Shared folder pointer

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A shared-folder pointer is a path they wrote to Drive, SharePoint, a NAS, or a cabinet label. No passwords, no vault secrets, no 'I created a new company tree.' You read and write the pack files the work map names under work/ and out/; you do not reorganize their live drive. If two paths exist, ask which is current. Keep their filenames on signed PDFs. Starter notes describe how SMB offices point at a folder; they still teach this shop's path. Shortcuts and alias folders stay as they labeled them. Personal and medical cabinets are out of a general pack.
