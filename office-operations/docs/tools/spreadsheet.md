# Office spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The office spreadsheet is a CSV they named for supplies, PTO presence, tasks, licenses, or a coverage grid. Use the columns they already use; do not add identity fields they did not ask for. No macros that phone home, no live workbook login, and no password inside the file. Typical work/ outputs follow the work map names. You flag blanks and collisions; you do not invent mins, owners, or dates. Starter notes describe CSV packs for most US SMB desks; they still teach this shop's columns. Checks in the workflow look for named headers, not a pretty layout. This is not a CRM or books product.
