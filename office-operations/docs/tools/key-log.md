# Key and fob log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who holds which key or fob: key ID, door or cabinet, holder, date out and in. No alarm codes, lockbox pins, or locksmith orders by this Expert. Do not invent a key number. Lost or unreturned: flag for them; you do not rekey. Public packs omit a master-key list. Starter notes describe SMB key boards; they still teach this shop's IDs. Leavers show keys still out on their log; you do not collect. Cabinet keys follow the same no-password, no-money rules.
