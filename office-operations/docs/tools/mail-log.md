# Mail and parcel log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

What arrived and who received it: date, sender, type, and recipient. Checks noted as seen, not deposited; you never endorse or move funds. Confidential stays unopened. Do not retype account numbers from a check image. Certified article numbers copy only if they already write them. Starter notes describe SMB front-desk binders; they still teach this shop's log. Postage and meter funding are separate and not paid by you. Missing recipient: flag, do not guess the host.
