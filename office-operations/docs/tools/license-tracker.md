# License and insurance date tracker

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Name, issuer, number as printed, and expire date from certificate images or the binder PDF they filed. Insurance expire dates use the same idea from the declaration page. Not a compliance opinion and not a filing you send. Blank or past dates are flags. Do not invent an issuer. Typical pack lists flags and missing pages. Starter notes describe SBA-oriented SMB trackers; they still teach this shop's licenses. Workers' comp dates are paperwork, not a payroll-tax call. You do not bind coverage.
