# Calendar export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

They export a week or month to CSV, ICS, or PDF. You read that file. It is not a live mailbox and not a booking product you control. Typical columns are start, end, title, room, and owner. Copy canceled and all-day status as exported. Do not add meetings. Conflicts: list both and ask. One week per pack unless they asked. Time zones stay on the header if present. Starter notes describe export-then-pack for most SMB offices; they still teach this shop's export name. Room holds come from the same file, not a live room panel you book.
