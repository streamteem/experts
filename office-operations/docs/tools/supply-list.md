# Supply list sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Item, on-hand if they counted it, min if they set it, and vendor SKU from their last buy. Flag at or below min. Do not buy, open a cart, or invent a min. Kitchen and postage rows follow the same sheet if they keep one list, or a sibling sheet they named. Brand and size come from the invoice they filed. Office stock is not warehouse inventory unless they taught that merge. Starter notes describe SMB closets; they still teach this shop's items. Substitute SKUs are questions. Checks look for item and qty headers on the work file.
