# Agenda and minutes files

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their agenda template plus last open actions from minutes or a task sheet. Columns they named: topic, owner, and often a time box or attachment filename. They approve distribution; you do not send the invite or blast a recap. Keep their meeting label (staff, owner, board). Unclear owner: ask. Do not invent decisions. Public packs omit personal phones if the printout might sit out. Starter notes describe SMB huddle packs; they still teach this shop's template. Work files stay CSV plus a write-up they can paste.
