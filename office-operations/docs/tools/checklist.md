# Recurring checklist

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Named recurring lines with owner and last-done date they recorded. Only lines they named: backup tick, shred pickup, meter, kit walk. Missing owner: ask, do not assign. You do not tick from memory. A missed week is a flag, not a compliance stamp. Starter notes describe SMB Friday sheets; they still teach this shop's lines. Backup ticks need their log or a named person. Do not add a generic 'what offices should do' row. Cadence stays as they wrote it.
