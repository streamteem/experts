# Visitor log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Date, visitor name or company, host, and times in or out if they record them. No ID numbers, license photos, or badge system. Ask if a visitor week is in scope. Public or lobby packs omit personal cells and home addresses. You do not decide who may enter. Unsigned-out rows are flags. Starter notes describe SMB reception logs; they still teach this shop's form. Contractors stay on this log unless they taught a separate trades sheet. Delivery exclusions follow their rule.
