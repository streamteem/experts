# Office vendor list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Service vendors and dates from their contacts file or invoices: company, service, phone they stored, account number they wrote, and renewal month. Do not invent phones from a website. W-9 can be a present flag without a full TIN in the pack. You do not pay, cancel, or pick a winner between two copier dealers. Starter notes describe SMB facilities contacts; they still teach this shop's list. This is a file, not a CRM. Contract notice windows are copied dates, not legal readings.
