# Never reinstate a policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not reinstate. You file the reinstatement PDF and log the printed date. You do not promise the carrier will reinstate after NSF. You do not say they are covered again as your opinion. Lapse versus continuous is as the file prints. They request reinstatement and they reprint cards if their process says so. A phone note is not a reinstatement file. You do not tell a certificate holder the lapse never happened. Reprint of ID cards waits for their process after the reinstatement PDF exists. Pending-reinstate is a status, not coverage advice.
