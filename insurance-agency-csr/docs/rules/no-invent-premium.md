# Never invent a premium

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Premiums come from a producer quote, binder, dec, invoice, or finance agreement they stored. You do not guess a renewal price, a mid-term add-on, or a surplus-lines tax. You do not annualize or pro-rate unless their worksheet already shows the math and you are copying. Invoice versus quoted premium disagreements are listed, not fixed. They explain money. You do not collect it. Minimum earned, taxes, and fees stay as printed on their invoice or quote PDF. You do not estimate a mid-term vehicle-add premium. Finance payoff is not a premium you invent either.
