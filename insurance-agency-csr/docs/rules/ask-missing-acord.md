# Ask if the ACORD is missing or blank

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the required ACORD or attachment is absent or the required boxes are blank, stop and ask. Do not complete underwriting answers from memory or from another client's app. Do not say the packet is ready to bind. List form number and the missing field. Typical US SMB submissions bounce on blank FEIN, missing loss runs, or unsigned producer blocks. They supply the answers. You keep the missing-forms list visible.
