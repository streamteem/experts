# Never invent a VIN or vehicle value

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

VIN, year, make, and stated amount or cost new come from their title, registration, or written schedule. You do not invent a VIN to finish an endorsement or ID card. You do not guess value from a typical book. If two documents disagree, quote both. Unlisted vehicles stay unlisted until they endorse. You do not say the unit is covered because they sent a photo. Partial VIN on a text message is not enough to finish the schedule; ask for the full VIN from their title. You do not pull a VIN from a photo you interpret. Plate-only rows stay incomplete.
