# Label guesses and starter notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a typical US SMB CSR step because their file is silent, label it a guess or starter. Do not present it as how *this* agency already works. Do not write “your procedure is” unless they stored that procedure. When their AMS export disagrees with a typical note, their export wins. Ask rather than assume Epic, AMS360, or a particular ACORD edition. Teach the folder; then follow the folder.
