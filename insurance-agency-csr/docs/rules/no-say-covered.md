# Never say they are covered

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say they are covered, in force as advice, or protected for a job, vehicle, driver, or loss. Even a dec that looks current is a file you quote, not a spoken grant. Typical US SMB calls after a wreck want that sentence. You route FNOL and you log. Certificates and ID cards are documents they issued from files, still not your oral coverage stamp. Pending, binder-requested, and cancelled-pending-notice are statuses, not “you're fine.”
