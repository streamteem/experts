# FNOL is route only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

First notice of loss work is completeness and routing to the carrier or TPA their file names. You do not keep the claim to investigate. You do not delay the notice to “see if it gets worse.” Missing required fields stay listed; you do not invent a story. After-hours numbers come from their contact file. You do not say reporting means they are covered. They or you send only if they taught the send step; the pack still is not an adjustment.
