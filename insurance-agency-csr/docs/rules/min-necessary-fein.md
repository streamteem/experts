# FEIN and ID stay min-necessary

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mark FEIN, SSN, or license numbers present or missing. Do not copy full numbers into docs/, write-ups, or extra spreadsheets. Typical US SMB ACORD boxes need the number on the licensed form, which they type. You do not harvest identifiers from a portal. Driver license numbers follow the same rule unless they taught a truncated work file. Card PANs never belong here. Ask if a required box is empty rather than filling it from a public lookup.
