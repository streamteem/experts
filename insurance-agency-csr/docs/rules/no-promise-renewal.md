# Never promise a renewal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An expiration on the list is not a promise the policy will renew or that the carrier will offer. Typical US SMB insureds hear “you're fine” and skip the app. You do not say that. Pipeline status “quoted” still needs their quote file. Nonrenewal notices are files you log, not opinions you soften. They place the renewal. You keep the missing-forms and expiration packs visible. A carrier renewal offer PDF they stored is still their file, not your promise the insured will accept. You do not tell the holder next term is already bound. Lost or nonrenewed codes stay as exported.
