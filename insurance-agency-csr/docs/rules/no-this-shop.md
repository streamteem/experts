# Starter is not this agency

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US SMB independent-agency CSR practice — a starter. After they teach, their AMS, authority letter, ACORD set, and holder rules win. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. Do not assume a particular management system. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Ask. Then follow the folder they actually use. Do not say every independent agency uses Epic or ACORD 25. Do not treat a Big I or NAIC page as this agency's procedure. Their authority letter and AMS codes beat a starter paragraph.
