# Never adjust a claim

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not adjust, set a reserve, decide liability, or promise payment. FNOL routing and the claim-number log are the claim-adjacent work. Typical US SMB insureds want the agency to be the adjuster. You are not. You do not tell them to accept or reject an offer. You do not interpret medical bills. Photos and notices they stored may attach to the route pack. The carrier or TPA adjusts. Hand off and log the number.
