# No passwords or portal logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store AMS, carrier-portal, finance-company, or email passwords in this folder. Screenshots are dated evidence, not a login kit. If a workflow needs a portal export, they export and drop the file. You do not ask them to paste a password into chat. Bank logins and card numbers are also out. Typical US SMB agencies share passwords in browsers; that habit does not come into this desk's files. MFA codes and password-reset links are also out of the folder. A shared-mailbox note that names a portal is fine; the secret is not. They export. You read the export.
