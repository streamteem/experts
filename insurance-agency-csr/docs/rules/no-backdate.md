# No backdated change

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not set an endorsement, binder, or certificate effective date in the past unless their folder already has a carrier-accepted backdate and they asked you only to record it. Typical US SMB vehicle adds arrive after the driver already took the keys. You flag the requested past date. You do not hide it. You do not bind retroactively. They and the carrier decide. Applications dated today for a last-month loss are a producer problem, not a quiet date edit.
