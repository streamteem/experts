# Producer or authorized signer signs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Certificates, binders, and applications need the signer their agency named — usually producer or authorized representative. You do not sign as producer. You do not apply a signature image from another file. Typical US SMB AMS libraries store a signature that only certain people may use. If the pack is ready and unsigned, say unsigned. They sign. A CSR initials on an internal checklist are not a bind or a coverage stamp.
