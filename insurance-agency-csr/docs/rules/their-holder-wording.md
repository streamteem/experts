# Holder wording stays theirs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Certificate-holder name, address, job description, and special wording come from the request they stored or their holder list. You do not clean up legal names. You do not add “and all additional insureds required by contract” unless that exact phrase is on their approved wording file. Mismatch between holder ask and dec is a flag, not a courtesy edit. They approve exceptions. Repeat holders stay as the library shows. Job number, contract date, and project name stay as the request printed them. You do not expand a holder to include affiliates. If their library has approved alternate wording, use only that file, not a remembered phrase.
