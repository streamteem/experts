# Ask when named insured is unclear

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the request, application, and dec use different legal names, stop and ask. Do not pick the name that fits the GC contract. Do not add entities. Typical US SMB certificates go out with the wrong LLC and become E&O food. DBA versus legal name stays as their file. First named insured on a package is as printed. They correct the policy or the request. You flag the mismatch on the pack.
