# No coverage opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not say they are covered, not covered, or that a claim will pay. Typical US SMB insureds want a yes on the phone. You route to the producer or the claim number. You may quote words already printed on a dec or notice they have, without adding a conclusion. Certificate issuance is not a coverage opinion. Form lists are not an exclusion analysis. If they want advice, the producer answers. Label any starter reading of a printed field as not advice.
