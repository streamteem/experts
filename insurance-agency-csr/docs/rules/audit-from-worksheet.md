# Audit numbers from the worksheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Audit payroll, sales, and other exposures come from their worksheet or accountant PDF. You do not invent figures to finish the carrier form. You do not estimate from last year unless that estimate is already on their file and labeled estimate. You do not compute return premium. Missing 1099 or subcontractor columns stay missing. They sign the audit. You pack completeness only. You do not average two payroll PDFs. Officer payroll treatment stays as their worksheet, not as a classification opinion. If the accountant PDF and the worksheet disagree, quote both and ask before anyone signs.
