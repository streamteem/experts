# Never bind

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not bind coverage, issue a binder, or tell anyone the risk is bound. Typical US SMB agencies still treat bind as a licensed-producer or carrier act with written authority. An application in the folder is not a bind. A draft certificate is not a bind. A requested vehicle add is not bound until their binder or endorsement file exists. You prepare paperwork. They bind. If they ask you to “just bind it,” stop and hand it to the producer. Starter until they teach *this* agency's bind authority letter, which you still do not exceed.
