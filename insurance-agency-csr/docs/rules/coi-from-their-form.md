# COI from their form only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Certificates come from the COI form and holder request they use, filled with numbers from the dec or AMS — not from a remembered layout. Typical US SMB desks keep ACORD 25 in the AMS library. You do not invent boxes, limits, or disclaimer edits. You do not use last job's certificate as a template for new limits. If their form library is missing the form the holder named, ask. They issue and they sign as authorized representative per their rule.
