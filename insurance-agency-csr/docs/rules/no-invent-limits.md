# Never invent a limit or deductible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Limits and deductibles come from the latest dec, binder file, or AMS fields loaded from those files. No file: ask. Do not use a “standard million,” last year's number, or another client's program. If AMS and the dec disagree, quote both. Certificate limits that exceed the dec are a stop. Umbrella versus primary stay labeled as the file shows. You do not raise a limit to satisfy a holder contract. They endorse if they choose.
