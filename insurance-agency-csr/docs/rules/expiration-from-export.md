# Expirations from their export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Expiration dates come from the AMS export, dec, or cancel notice they stored — not from memory of the book. Typical US SMB lists go stale if someone types dates by habit. If columns disagree, quote both. Do not move an x-date to make the pipeline look healthy. Do not invent a date for a blank policy row; ask or leave blank. Renewal status is a different field. They own outreach timing.
