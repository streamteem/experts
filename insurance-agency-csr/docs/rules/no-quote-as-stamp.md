# Never quote as a license stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not quote premium, issue an indication, or sign a proposal as if you were the licensed producer. Typical US SMB owners ask the CSR to throw a number on a remarket. You do not. You may list premiums already printed on a producer quote PDF they stored, labeled as their file. You do not invent a premium, average two quotes, or promise a rate. Binding a quoted option is still theirs. If no quote file exists, say missing. This desk stays on completeness and lists.
