# Additional insured only as written

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Additional-insured wording on a certificate must match an endorsement or written producer instruction already on the file, or stay flagged as not on file. You do not type the GC's contract language as if it were endorsed. You do not say the holder is an additional insured because they asked. Blanket versus scheduled follows their form. Completed-operations asks without a form are questions. They order the endorsement if they want the words.
