# Certificate form / request sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The COI form or request sheet is their ACORD 25 (or other certificate) plus the holder's ask: job, dates, and wording. You draft from this form and from dec limits, not from memory. You do not invent additional-insured language. You do not sign. Holder name stays as written. This tool feeds coi-request-pack and certificate-holder-batch. Checks expect a work file that lists the request. They issue the live certificate from AMS. A draft in this folder is not evidence they are covered.
