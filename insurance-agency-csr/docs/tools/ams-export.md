# AMS policy export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The AMS export is their book dump from Applied Epic, AMS360, HawkSoft, EZLynx, or a similar system: client, policy number, named insured, line, carrier, effective and expiration dates, and status as they coded it. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a policy number or a status. Export is their job; no AMS password belongs in this folder. Checks look for policy and date columns when a workflow asks. This tool feeds renewal-pipeline and missing-dec packs. Issued versus pending stays as they coded it.
