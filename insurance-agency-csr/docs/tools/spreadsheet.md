# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the pack's list: expirations, missing forms, holders, or endorsement requests with the columns that workflow needs. Typical US SMB CSR desks live in workbooks next to AMS. You do not fill blank limits or premiums. You do not hide a missing dec behind a comment. Checks look for stated columns on work CSVs or for the written pack in out/. This tool builds expiration-list, acord-completeness, renewal-pipeline, and endorsement-request write-ups they can act on.
