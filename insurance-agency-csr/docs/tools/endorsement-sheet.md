# Endorsement / change request sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The endorsement sheet lists requested mid-term changes: vehicle, driver, location, effective date asked, and supporting-doc present or missing. You do not invent a VIN or a backdate. You do not mark the change issued until an endorsement PDF or AMS issued code exists. This tool feeds vehicle-add-paperwork and endorsement-request-list packs. Checks look for a work CSV of requests. They submit to the carrier. The sheet is paperwork, not a bind.
