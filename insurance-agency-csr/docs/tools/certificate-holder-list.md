# Certificate holder list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The holder list is their library of certificate-holder legal names, addresses, and repeat wording per client or job. You copy names exactly. You do not invent a holder or tidy Inc. versus LLC. Additional-insured flags on this list are still not endorsements unless the policy file agrees. This tool feeds coi-request-pack and certificate-holder-batch. Checks look for holder columns when asked. They maintain the library. A batch is drafts and a write-up, not issued certificates unless they said they already issued.
