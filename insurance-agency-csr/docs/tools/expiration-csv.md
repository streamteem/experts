# Expiration list CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The expiration CSV is a dated slice of the book: policy, named insured, expiration date, and often producer and line. Typical US SMB CSRs pull thirty-sixty-ninety day views. You do not type dates from memory. Blank expirations stay blank and become asks. Status filters stay as they exported them. Checks look for policy, named_insured, and exp_date when the workflow says so. This tool is the source for expiration-list and the date side of renewal-pipeline packs. They use the list; you do not bind renewals from it.
