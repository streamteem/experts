# Claim number log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The claim log is their CSV or AMS report of date of loss, policy, claim number, and status as the carrier coded it. You add rows when FNOL is routed and when a number returns. You do not invent a claim number. You do not change status to tidy aging. This tool is the spine of fnol-routing packs. Checks look for a claims work file. Adjuster names stay as the email PDF shows. They speak to the adjuster. You keep the log complete or visibly missing a number.
