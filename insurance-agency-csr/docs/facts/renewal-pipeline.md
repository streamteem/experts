# Renewal pipeline

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A renewal pipeline is their working list of accounts approaching expiration, with producer, CSR, line, and status as they coded it: not-started, apps out, quoted, ordered, issued, or lost. Typical US SMB agencies keep this in AMS activities or a shared sheet. The pipeline is a completeness and follow-up board, not a quote and not a bind. You copy status from their export. You do not invent a renewal premium, a carrier offer, or a “they will renew” outcome. A missing application or missing dec is a missing-forms row, not a coverage hole you diagnose. Remarketing rows stay labeled remarket, not quoted, until a licensed producer puts a quote file in the folder. You do not promise the insured a renewal. You do not bind the renewal. They chase and they place. Starter until they teach how *this* agency stages renewals and which codes mean waiting on the carrier versus waiting on the insured.
