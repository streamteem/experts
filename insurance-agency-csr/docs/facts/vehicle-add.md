# Vehicle add paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vehicle add is the paperwork to put a unit on an auto or garage schedule: year, make, model, VIN, cost new or stated amount as they provided, garage location, and use as their form asks. Typical US SMB commercial accounts add trucks weekly; personal lines add a household car. You copy the VIN exactly from their title, registration, or written note. You do not invent a VIN, plate, or value. You do not say the vehicle is covered because they emailed a photo. ID cards wait for issue or their binder file. Lienholder or loss-payee names come from their note, not from a guessed bank. If VIN on the title and the email disagree, quote both and ask. Effective date is the date they requested, flagged if it is in the past. They bind or the carrier endorses. Starter until they teach *this* agency's vehicle-add checklist and same-day card rule.
