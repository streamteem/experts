# Mortgagee clause

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A mortgagee or loss-payee clause is the lender or lienholder wording as their file or the lender letter prints it: name, address, loan number if they stored it. Typical US SMB property and auto desks update these at refinance. You copy the clause exactly. You do not invent a loan number or a bank name from a remembered close. You do not say the lender is covered. ISAOA / ATIMA and similar suffixes stay only if they wrote them. Two lenders on one building stay two rows if the file shows two. Deleting a mortgagee is an endorsement request, not a silent erase because the insured said they paid off the loan with no payoff letter. Auto loss payees follow the vehicle schedule. They order the change. Starter until they teach how *this* agency formats mortgagee clauses and which proof they require to remove one.
