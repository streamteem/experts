# Additional insured as written

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Additional insured is a status that exists only when their policy file or a carrier endorsement already grants it, or when they asked the producer to request that endorsement. Typical US SMB certificate holders demand AI on every job. The words on the holder's contract are not coverage. You copy AI language only as it already appears on their form, endorsement schedule, or written producer instruction. You do not invent “additional insured — owners, lessees, or contractors” because a GC email used those words. You do not say the holder is covered. A certificate description box is not an endorsement. If the request needs a form the file does not show, list it as a producer or carrier question. Ongoing versus completed-operations asks stay as written on the request, flagged if the file is silent. Starter until they teach which AI forms *this* agency actually has on the account.
