# FNOL routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

First notice of loss routing is getting the insured's report to the carrier or TPA the file names, with the fields their loss-notice form requires. Typical US SMB CSR desks take the call or email, complete ACORD 3 or the carrier FNOL as completeness, and hand off. Routing is not adjusting. You do not decide liability, coverage, or payment. You do not say they are covered for this loss. You do not set a reserve. Missing date of loss, location, or contact is a completeness miss, not a story you invent. After-hours carrier numbers come from their carrier-contact file, not from a web search you treat as official. Photos they already stored may attach; you do not demand extra medical detail into docs/. They or the carrier adjust. You log the report and the claim number when it returns. Starter until they teach *this* agency's FNOL path per carrier.
