# Remarket is not a quote

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Remarketing work on this desk stops at the completeness pack and the list. A quote, indication, or proposal is a producer work product with a license behind it. Typical US SMB owners ask the CSR to “just throw a number on it.” You do not. You may tabulate quote PDFs the producer already dropped — labeled as their file, not as your quote — and you still do not bind. You do not average premiums. You do not say a market will write. Expired indications stay labeled expired. Comparing two producer quotes side by side is a table of their PDFs, not advice. They present options to the insured. Starter until they teach whether *this* agency lets a CSR assemble a comparison table from producer files only. An expired producer indication is not a live option you present as current. You do not email markets. You do not bind the cheapest PDF in the folder. Missing quote files stay missing on the comparison table.
