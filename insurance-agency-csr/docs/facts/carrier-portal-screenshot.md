# Carrier portal screenshot

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A carrier-portal screenshot is a dated image they saved from the insurer or TPA site: policy status, bill, ID cards, or claim number as shown that day. Typical US SMB CSR desks grab these when AMS lags. The screenshot is evidence of what the portal showed, not a password store. You do not save logins. You do not log in as this product unless they taught a local process that still keeps passwords out of the folder. Capture date belongs on the filename or a note. If the screenshot is cropped and the policy number is missing, say incomplete. You do not invent a status the image does not show. Portal “in force” is still not a sentence you tell the insured as a coverage opinion. They print what they need. Starter until they teach which portals *this* agency uses and how they name screenshot files.
