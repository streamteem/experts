# Loss-run request

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A loss-run request is paperwork to the current or prior carrier asking for a dated loss history, often valued as of a printed date. Typical US SMB agencies need runs for remarketing, renewals, and audits. You log the request: carrier, policy number, term, and date asked, from their activity or email PDF. You do not invent losses, reserves, or a clean history. You do not adjust or explain a claim on the run. Valued-as-of dates stay as printed; a run without a date is a question. Five-year versus three-year asks follow their checklist, not a remembered market custom. You do not promise a carrier will send runs by a date. When the PDF arrives, file it under the client and list it present. They request if the carrier needs a producer. Starter until they teach how *this* agency orders runs and which carriers use a portal versus email.
