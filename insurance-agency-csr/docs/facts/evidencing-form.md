# Evidence of property / evidencing form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Evidencing forms are the property-side certificates their agency uses — often ACORD 24 or 27 — to show a mortgagee or other interest the property policy as printed, not a liability COI reused because it was handy. Typical US SMB commercial and homeowners desks issue these at loan close or lease start. The form is information from the dec. You do not invent a building limit, deductible, or valuation type. You do not say the building is covered. Mortgagee clause wording comes from their clause file or the lender letter, copied exactly when they asked you to match it. If the lender wants a form the file does not support, flag. You do not bind a property policy to finish the loan. They issue the evidence. Starter until they teach which evidencing form *this* agency prints per line and who signs.
