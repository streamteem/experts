# Remarketing list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A remarketing list is the accounts they want shopped at renewal or mid-term: client, expiration, line, reason as they coded it, and current carrier. Typical US SMB agencies remarket for price, loss history, or carrier exit. The list is a pipeline, not a quote. You do not invent a target premium. You do not pick markets. You do not bind. Completeness for remarket is apps, loss runs, and decs present or missing. AOR issues stay flagged if they noted another broker. You do not tell the incumbent carrier they are losing the account. They market. You keep the list and the missing-forms column. Starter until they teach how *this* agency chooses remarket candidates and which documents the producer wants in the pack. Incumbent carrier and requested markets stay in their columns if they have them; you do not add a market you like. Loss-run valued dates on the completeness side still come from the PDF. They decide who is shopped.
