# DBA / trade name

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A DBA or trade name is an other-name field on their application or dec, present or missing. Typical US SMB contractors operate under a shop name that is not the LLC. You copy the DBA as written. You do not invent a DBA so a certificate matches a GC contract. You do not treat a DBA as a separate named insured unless their file already lists it that way. If the website name and the dec disagree, the dec wins until they change the policy. Certificates may show DBA only if their form and file support it. You do not give a legal opinion that the DBA is properly registered. They add the name through endorsement if they choose. Starter until they teach how *this* agency prints DBA on certificates and apps. A logo or invoice header is not a DBA field. You do not invent punctuation. If they want the shop name on the certificate, that is an endorsement or DBA-add question for the producer, not a courtesy typeover.
