# Binder as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A binder is temporary written evidence that someone licensed already bound coverage, with dates and limits as that file shows. Typical US SMB agencies still issue or receive binders around new business and mid-term adds. You may list a binder only when they stored the binder PDF or AMS binder record. You do not issue a binder. You do not bind. You do not say the risk is bound because an application sits in the folder. Expired binder dates stay labeled expired. If binder and later dec disagree, quote both; the dec usually replaces the binder once issued, but you do not declare coverage. Limits on a binder come from that file, never from habit. They or the producer bind. You file and flag missing replacement decs. Starter until they teach how *this* agency numbers binders and how long they keep one on the activity list.
