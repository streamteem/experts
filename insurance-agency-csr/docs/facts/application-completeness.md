# Application completeness

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Application completeness is a checklist against the application packet they named: ACORD or carrier app, supplemental questionnaires, loss runs if they require them, and driver or location schedules. Typical US SMB CSR desks assemble the packet so the producer can submit. Completeness is not underwriting and not a quote. You mark present, missing, or unreadable. You do not invent a class code, payroll, sales figure, VIN, or date of birth to finish the form. You do not copy extra identification numbers into docs/. If two apps disagree on named insured or DBA, quote both and ask. A signed app in the folder is still not a bind. Carrier-specific supplements sit only if they stored the blank or the completed file. You pack the missing-forms list. They obtain signatures and they submit. Starter until they teach *this* agency's required attachments per line.
