# Driver add paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A driver add is the paperwork to list a person on a personal or commercial auto file: name as they wrote it, license state, and the dates or numbers their application already stores — not extra identity data you copy into docs/. Typical US SMB agencies also track MVR order dates as a file present-or-missing, not as a driving verdict you announce. You do not invent a license number. You do not say the driver is covered. You do not decide acceptability or surcharge. Excluded-driver forms stay as their form if they use one. Household versus employee drivers follow their app, not a guess. If two spellings of the name appear, ask. Date they want the driver added stays as requested and is flagged if backdated. They or underwriting accept. You pack the missing-driver-info list. Starter until they teach *this* agency's driver checklist and what they refuse to store in the folder.
