# Certificate holder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The certificate holder is the third party named in the holder box — landlord, GC, owner, or vendor — with the exact legal name and address they provided. Typical US SMB agencies keep a holder list per client so repeat jobs do not reinvent the name. Holder is not additional insured. Naming a holder does not grant rights and does not mean they are covered. You type the holder as written on the request or their saved list. You do not “clean up” LLC versus Inc. You do not invent a job number. If the request PDF and the email use different legal names, quote both and ask. Blanket holders or “and all subsidiaries” wording follows their form rules, not a courtesy expansion you add. They approve odd wording. Starter until they teach how *this* agency stores holders and whether AMS has a holder library.
