# Declarations page

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The declarations page is the carrier's printed summary of named insured, term, limits, deductibles, forms, and schedules as issued. Typical US SMB CSR desks treat the latest dec as the source for certificate numbers and for “what the file says,” not as a script to tell a client they are covered. You copy limits and deductibles only from this PDF or from AMS fields they already loaded from it. You do not invent a limit. If AMS and the dec disagree, quote both and ask. Missing decs on an in-force policy number are a missing-file row. Renewals need the new-term dec, not last year's. Package policies may have several decs; keep line labels. You do not interpret exclusions from the form list as a coverage opinion. They obtain the dec from the carrier if it is missing. Starter until they teach where *this* agency files decs and which revision wins.
