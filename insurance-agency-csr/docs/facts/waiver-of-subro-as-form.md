# Waiver of subrogation as a form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A waiver of subrogation is a form or endorsement on their file, not a sentence you type onto a certificate to satisfy a contract. Typical US SMB construction and service accounts see waiver asks on every bid. You note whether their endorsement schedule or AMS already shows a waiver form for that line, and you copy the form number if printed. You do not invent a waiver. You do not pursue subrogation or waive the carrier's rights. If the holder wants a blanket waiver and the file shows scheduled only, quote both and ask. Workers-comp waivers and general-liability waivers are different files when they have them. Premium for a waiver is a producer or audit question, not a number you guess. The certificate may evidence a waiver that already exists; it does not create one. Starter until they teach how *this* agency records waivers and who orders the form.
