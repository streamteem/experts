# Auto ID cards

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Auto identification cards are the state or ACORD cards their AMS prints from the vehicle schedule and policy term on file. Typical US SMB personal and commercial auto desks mail or email cards after issue, vehicle add, or renewal. The card is evidence the agency printed from their file, not a coverage opinion you speak. You list VIN, vehicle, named insured, and term as the card or schedule shows. You do not invent a VIN, a plate, or an effective date. You do not say they are covered to drive. Temporary cards after a binder follow the binder file if they have one. Digital-card portals are their process; no portal password belongs in this folder. Missing cards on an issued policy are a paperwork miss, not a bind you perform. They issue cards. You pack the missing-card list. Starter until they teach *this* agency's card print and delivery steps.
