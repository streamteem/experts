# Activity note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An activity note is the AMS diary: who called, what they asked, what was sent, and the follow-up date they set. Typical US SMB CSR desks live in activities. You write what the file supports. You do not write “confirmed covered.” You do not write a quote or a bind in an activity unless you are only pointing at a producer file that already exists. Suspense dates come from their rule or the date they typed, not from a guessed “call in two weeks.” Attachments they dropped should be named so the next person finds the PDF. Do not copy passwords, card numbers, or full FEIN into the note. Personal opinions about the insured stay out. They close or roll the activity. Starter until they teach *this* agency's activity codes, suspense aging, and what belongs in the note versus a document.
