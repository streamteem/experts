# Location add paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A location add is the paperwork to put a premises or job site on a property, GL, or package schedule: street address as they wrote it, occupancy or operations as their app already states, and building values only from their statement of values or worksheet. Typical US SMB commercial accounts add a warehouse or a job. You do not invent a building limit, coinsurance, or construction type. You do not say the location is covered. If the address on the lease and the email disagree, quote both. Mortgagee or additional-interest names come from their note. Flood or quake questions are present-or-missing on their supplemental, not a hazard opinion you give. Effective date backdates are a producer question. They submit. You list missing SOV lines. Starter until they teach how *this* agency schedules locations and which carriers need a photo or ACORD location schedule.
