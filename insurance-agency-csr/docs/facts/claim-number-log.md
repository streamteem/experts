# Claim number log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A claim-number log is their list of carrier or TPA claim numbers, date of loss, policy number, and status as the carrier coded it — open, closed, subrogation, or payment issued — copied from emails or portal screenshots they saved. Typical US SMB agencies keep this so the next call does not invent a number. You copy the number exactly. You do not invent a claim number to tidy the log. You do not change status to make aging look clean. You do not adjust, negotiate, or tell the insured what will pay. A screenshot without a number is “number not yet assigned.” Duplicate reports of the same loss stay linked if they said so; do not merge two dates of loss because they sound similar. You update the log. They speak to the adjuster. Starter until they teach how *this* agency stores claim numbers and which statuses they use.
