# Endorsement request

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An endorsement request is mid-term change paperwork: add or delete a vehicle, driver, location, or coverage part, with the effective date they asked and the documents their carrier wants. Typical US SMB CSR desks collect the request and the supporting schedule, then the producer or CSR submits per agency rule. A request is not a bind and not a statement the change is already on the policy. You list what they asked versus what the current dec shows. You do not invent a VIN, address, or limit. You do not backdate unless their file already has a carrier-accepted backdate and they asked you only to record it. Missing ACORD change forms stay missing. Issued endorsement pages go in the dec-page folder when they arrive. They submit and they confirm issue. Starter until they teach who at *this* agency may request endorsements and which changes need underwriting.
