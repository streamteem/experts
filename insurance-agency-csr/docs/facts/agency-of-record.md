# Agency of record

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Agency of record, or broker of record, is their letter or carrier acknowledgment that this agency may service the policy. Typical US SMB shops win and lose AOR on commercial accounts. You file the dated letter and log the carrier's accept date if they stored it. You do not invent an AOR. You do not tell another agency they are off the account. Pending AOR is not a bind and not a right to issue certificates from another agency's dec you do not have. Loss runs and decs after AOR still come from the carrier files they receive. You do not backdate an AOR. They send the letter. Starter until they teach how *this* agency tracks AOR and whether they service only after the carrier confirms. A signed AOR sitting in email is still pending until they store it and, if their rule requires, the carrier acknowledges. You do not start certificates on a newly won account with no decs. Lost-AOR dates come from their letter, not from rumor.
