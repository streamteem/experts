# Reinstatement as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A reinstatement is a dated carrier or finance-company file that says the cancellation did not stay in effect, with a reinstatement date as printed. Typical US SMB agencies see these after NSF or late pay. You file the PDF and log the date. You do not reinstate. You do not say they are covered again as your opinion — you may quote the reinstatement document they stored. If cancel notice and reinstatement disagree on dates, quote both and ask. A producer phone note that “it will reinstate” is not a reinstatement file. Lapse days, if printed, stay as printed; you do not invent a no-lapse statement. ID cards or certificates after a lapse wait for their process. They request reinstatement if the carrier needs it. Starter until they teach how *this* agency treats lapse versus continuous and what they reprint after reinstate.
