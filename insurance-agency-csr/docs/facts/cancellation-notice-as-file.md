# Cancellation notice as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cancellation notice is a dated carrier or finance-company PDF: named insured, policy number, cancellation date, and reason as printed. Typical US SMB desks see nonpay, underwriting, and insured-request notices. You file the PDF and log the dates. You do not cancel the policy. You do not tell the insured they are cancelled unless you are only quoting the notice they already have, and even then you do not add a coverage opinion. Flat versus pro-rata is as printed, not as you calculate. Notice date and cancellation date are different fields; keep both. A threatened cancel in an activity note is not a notice. Reinstatement is a different file. You do not invent a cancel date to tidy AMS. They talk to the insured and the carrier. Starter until they teach how *this* agency logs notices and which statuses they use after a cancel.
