# Payment status as their code

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Payment status is the AMS or carrier-bill code they already store: current, past due, NSF, installment, or cancelled for nonpay — copied as a code, not rewritten as a coverage sentence. Typical US SMB books mix direct bill and agency bill. You do not collect premium. You do not send money. You do not store card numbers. You do not say they are covered because a code says current. If AMS and a carrier screenshot disagree, quote both. Agency-bill invoices they dropped are files for the producer or bookkeeper, not a bill you pay. Finance-company status is a different file when they have one. You list the code and the as-of date of the export. They call the insured about money. Starter until they teach *this* agency's bill types and which codes trigger a CSR activity versus a producer call.
