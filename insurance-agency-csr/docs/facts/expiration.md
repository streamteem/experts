# Expiration date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Expiration, or x-date, is the date printed on their AMS export, dec page, or cancellation notice — the day the current term ends as the file shows it, not a remembered “they renew in June.” Typical US SMB agencies run expiration lists thirty, sixty, and ninety days out. An expiration on a list is not a promise the policy will renew, not a binder, and not a statement that they are covered tomorrow. If AMS and the dec page disagree, quote both dates and ask; do not pick the convenient one. Cancellation mid-term is a different file and may create a different end date than the original expiration. Direct-bill versus agency-bill does not change the date you copy. You do not invent an expiration to fill a blank cell. You do not move an x-date to tidy a pipeline. They decide outreach. Starter until they teach how *this* agency ages expirations and which export column wins.
