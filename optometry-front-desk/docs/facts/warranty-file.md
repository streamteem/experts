# Warranty file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Warranty at a typical US optical is their written policy plus any vendor warranty PDF they stored: what is covered, how many months, whether progressive non-adapt is included, and whether a redo fee is waived. You quote only that file. You do not invent a one-year scratch warranty because another shop offers one. You do not extend a warranty. You do not decide a pair qualifies — that is staff plus their policy. If the warranty file is missing, say missing. Pair the warranty row to the original job date as their sheet stores it; do not compute remaining months unless they asked and the file has start and length. Vision-plan replacement pairs are not a warranty you invent; those sentences come from a dated eligibility print. You still do not interpret the Rx to say the pair “should have worked.” Least PHI on the warranty log. Starter / guess until they teach which warranty PDF *this* optical treats as live for frames versus lenses.
