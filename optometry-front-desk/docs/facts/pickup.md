# Pickup

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pickup or dispense at a typical US optical is a logged event: job or tray, chart label, date and time they recorded, who released the pair as their process names (often an optician), and whether a dispense checklist they use is present. You list completeness. You do not dispense as the optician. You do not adjust the pair. You do not say the Rx is correct. You do not release a pair that their pickup list does not show as ready. Guardian or authorized-pickup names come from their file; you do not decide who may take a minor’s glasses if the list is blank — ask. Outstanding-balance flags stay as their sheet shows; you do not waive a balance to release a pair. Warranty and care cards they already print are present or missing. Distinct from a pharmacy pickup and from a dental denture seat. Prefer chart and job number. Starter / guess until they teach who at *this* optical may mark a pair picked up.
