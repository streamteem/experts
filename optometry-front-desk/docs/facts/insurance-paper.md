# Insurance paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US optometry check-in keeps a front-and-back image of the vision card, the medical card, or both. The image is paperwork: payer name as printed, member ID as printed, group number if shown, and whether the scan is dated this visit or older. You may list those labels they asked for. You do not invent a member ID from memory. You do not type a full SSN if the older card still uses SSN as the ID — say the image uses that pattern and ask how they want the tracking sheet labeled; still do not paste the full number into docs/. Payment-card PAN is not an insurance member ID; never store PAN or CVV. Eligibility is a different file: a card image is not a remaining-allowance fact. Vision-plan cards (VSP, EyeMed, Spectera, Davis, and others they stored) stay labeled as vision; medical cards stay labeled as medical — do not merge them into one “insurance” invent. If only one side is in the folder, say which side is missing. Subscriber versus patient name mismatches are questions, not a coverage opinion. Prefer chart number on the index row. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office names and dates card images.
