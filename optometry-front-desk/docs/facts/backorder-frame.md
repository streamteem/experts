# Backorder frame

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A backorder frame is a board or vendor SKU they already marked unavailable, plus any ETA their vendor file or POS shows. You copy that flag and the ETA as written. You do not invent an arrival week. You do not swap a lookalike SKU to keep the sale. You do not promise the vision plan will wait. If ETA is blank, say blank. Special-order is a different path when they coded it that way — do not relabel a backorder as special-order to tidy the board. Patient-notified about the delay is their notify process; you list, they contact. Price still comes from their fee file, not a rush fee you invent. Distinct from a wholesale inventory backorder desk as a product — this is the optical ticket flag. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* optical marks backorder versus discontinued.
