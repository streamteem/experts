# Contact-training flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Contact-lens training on a typical US book is a flag or appointment type they already created for insertion and removal teaching they staff. Copy the flag. You do not teach insertion. You do not fit the lens. You do not say the patient is ready to take lenses home. You do not prescribe a care solution. If the flag is set and no trainer resource is on the book, flag that gap for staff. Trial-ack and care-booklet pages their process requires are present or missing. You do not invent a training duration. New-wearer versus experienced-wearer labels stay as they stored them — you do not decide experience from a story. Distinct from a medical nurse-visit teaching flag. Prefer chart number. Extra slit-lamp notes stay out of docs/. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office books contact training versus CL evaluation.
