# Order and lab status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Status on a typical US optical job is the word their lab portal, EHR, or tray log already stored: submitted, in production, shipped, received, edged, ready, notified, picked up, redo, or canceled as they coded it. You copy that word and the timestamp if present. You do not invent that a job shipped because it is past a remembered due date. You do not mark picked-up to clear the board. If lab-status export and tray log disagree, quote both and ask. Promise or due dates stay as stored; a late flag is a comparison they asked for against those dates, not a new promise you make. You do not call the lab as a product. Passwords to lab portals stay out. Least PHI on a status board that may sit at the window. Distinct from medical claim status and from dental case-at-lab. Starter / guess until they teach which status report *this* optical drops and which words mean ready for notify.
