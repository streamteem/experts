# Dilation as their schedule flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dilation on a typical US optometry book is a scheduling flag or visit type they already stored so the floor knows extra time, a driver note, or a pretest path. Copy the flag as the export shows. You do not decide the patient needs dilation. You do not say dilation is safe or that they can drive. You do not prescribe drops. If their process requires a driver-present note and the field is blank, say blank and ask — do not invent that a family member will drive. A dilated-exam type versus an undilated type stay as booked; you do not convert the type to tidy the day. Medical versus vision split still follows their process, not a dilation you order. Extra clinical indication notes stay out of docs/. Distinct from a medical clinic procedure-consent pack. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office flags dilation on the day list.
