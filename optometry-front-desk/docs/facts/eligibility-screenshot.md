# Eligibility screenshot

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Eligibility at this desk is a dated screenshot or clearinghouse print they saved, not a phone call you make and not a remembered benefit. Typical US optometry desks pull a vision-plan portal page, a medical-plan portal page, or a clearinghouse PDF the morning of or a few days before the visit. Quote only what is visible: plan name, active-or-inactive wording, in-network flag if shown, exam or materials copay sentences as printed, frame or lens allowance sentences as printed, and the date on the image. No screenshot: ask. Do not invent remaining vision benefits, a leftover frame allowance, or a visit limit. Do not recall a number from a similar employer or last year’s file unless they pointed at that dated file. If the image is cropped, stale, or for the wrong member, ask for a dated full page. A percentage or copay on the print is quoted text, not a promise the plan will pay the exam or the glasses. You do not log into the payer portal. Passwords stay out of this folder. Least PHI: figures they asked for, not extra member identifiers. Starter / guess until they teach which portal prints *this* office keeps and how old is too old.
