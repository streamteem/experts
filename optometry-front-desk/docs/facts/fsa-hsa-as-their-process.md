# FSA and HSA as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Flexible-spending and health-savings paperwork at a typical US optometry desk is their process file: itemized receipt they already generate, letter-of-medical-necessity template they already use if any, and whether they print a superbill they already coded. You assemble completeness of those named pages. You do not write a medical-necessity letter as the doctor. You do not diagnose so a pair becomes eligible. You do not invent that a plan or an FSA administrator will reimburse. You do not store the full SSN from an FSA card. You do not take the FSA card number into this folder. If they have no LMN template and someone asks for one, ask staff — do not draft clinical language. Receipt line items come from their POS or fee file, not a guessed usual charge. Distinct from a medical clinic sliding-fee packet. Starter / guess until they teach which receipt and letter files *this* office prints for FSA or HSA.
