# Day-close tender

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-close tender is their end-of-day list of what was collected: cash, check, card-batch total as their merchant or EHR report shows, exam copays, and optical retail tenders they named. Typical US optometry desks also split doctor collections from optical collections if their close report does. You copy amounts and tender types from their close report or drawer sheet. You do not take cards. You do not store PAN or check account numbers. You do not send a deposit or move money. Short-or-over versus their expected total is a flag from their math, not a silent fix. Insurance posting is not tender. Vision-plan write-offs stay on the adjustment report, not mixed into cash as if collected. One clinic date and one location per close pack unless they asked otherwise. If the close file is missing, ask; do not invent a batch total from copay guesses. Distinct from a salon POS close only in the exam-versus-optical split you keep. Starter / guess until they teach which close report *this* office runs.
