# Outside lab

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An outside or wholesale lab is the named vendor on their order: lab account as they wrote it, ship-to, and which lens types they already route there. You copy the destination from the ticket. You do not pick a cheaper lab. You do not invent a turnaround. Status still comes from their lab-status export. Invoice match is a separate fact. You do not pay the lab. You do not log into the lab portal. If two labs appear on one ticket, quote both and ask. Frame-to-follow versus complete-pair send-outs stay as they coded. In-house edging jobs stay off this list unless their routing file says the job still ships uncut first. Promise dates stay as the ticket or lab-status file stored them, not a remembered three-day habit. Distinct from a medical reference lab and from a dental off-site ceramic lab. Prefer job number. Starter / guess until they teach which outside labs *this* optical uses and which file lists the routing rule.
