# Special-order frame or lens

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A special order is a frame or lens they already coded as not-on-board: vendor, SKU as quoted, deposit if their process requires one as posted, and promised vendor date as stored. You list those fields. You do not pick the special-order frame. You do not invent a deposit percent. You do not promise a custom lens will match a remembered design. Lens specials still need Rx-present and lens-type-from-file. Cancel-after-order rules come from their written file, not a kindness you invent. Backorder of a board SKU is a different fact — do not relabel a delayed board piece as a special order to tidy the ticket. Vision-plan specials still need a dated eligibility sentence if they asked about coverage — you still do not adjudicate. Prefer SKU and chart label. You do not take a card for the deposit into this folder. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* optical opens a special-order ticket versus a board sale.
