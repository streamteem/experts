# Redo reason

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Redo reason is the coded why on their remake row: lab error, doctor Rx change, patient change, defect, non-adapt, or another word they already use. You copy that code. You do not invent a reason so the remake is free. You do not decide the lab was at fault. You do not interpret the old Rx versus the new Rx. If reason is blank, ask; do not send the redo. Warranty-file still decides whether their policy even speaks to that code — you quote the file, you do not extend it. Complaint sentences the patient gave stay off the work sheet when the code exists; do not paste a headache story into docs/. One reason per redo row unless they use a combo code they stored. Distinct from a print-shop reprint reason only in the optical codes you keep. Starter / guess until they teach *this* optical’s reason list and who may set the code.
