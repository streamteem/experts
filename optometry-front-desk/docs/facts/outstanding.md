# Outstanding balance flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An outstanding-balance flag on a day list, pickup list, or registration row is a copied ledger fact: they have a balance on the account as of the export, sometimes split exam versus optical. Typical US offices show it so staff can follow their collection script before a dispense. You copy the flag or the amount their export shows. You do not invent a collect-at-check-in amount beyond what they posted. You do not waive. You do not refuse an exam as a clinical decision. You do not hold a pair as your decision if their process is silent — flag the balance and ask. You do not send a collections letter. Insurance pending versus patient balance stay split if their file splits them. Family guarantor totals stay at the export’s level. Do not re-age a row. Card numbers stay out. Prefer chart or account number. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office wants balances shown on the day list versus the pickup list.
