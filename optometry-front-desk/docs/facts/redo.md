# Redo ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A redo or remake is a second lab ticket they already opened against a prior job: original job number, new job number if assigned, and a reason code from their list (lab error, doctor change, non-adapt, defect, patient change — as they coded it). You list those fields. You do not decide the lenses were wrong. You do not interpret the old or new Rx. You do not invent a reason so the lab will not charge. Warranty versus billable redo comes only from their warranty file and the reason they stored. You do not promise a free remake. Missing reason: ask; do not send a silent redo. Frame reuse versus new frame stays as their ticket shows. Distinct from a dental remake of a crown. Do not copy extra complaint narrative into docs/ when their reason code will do. Prefer job numbers. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* optical codes redos and who may open one.
