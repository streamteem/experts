# No-show

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No-show on a typical US optometry export is a status they already coded, sometimes split from late cancel and from broken. Copy their word and the timestamp. Do not infer a no-show from a blank confirmation or from an unopened tray. Do not invent a no-show fee or a dismissal rule. Fees come from their written policy file only. Do not refuse future booking as your decision. Do not diagnose why the patient missed. Optical pickup no-shows and exam no-shows stay on the books they used if the office splits those columns. A wait-list fill is their choice. One clinic date unless they asked for a range and the file has dates. Prefer chart number. They contact; you do not message as a product. Distinct from a gym class no-show and from a dental broken column only in the optical types you keep. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office codes no-show versus broken versus cancel.
