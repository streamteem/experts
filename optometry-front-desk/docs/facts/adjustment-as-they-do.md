# Adjustment as they do it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An adjustment visit on a typical US optical book is a slot or walk-in type they already coded so an optician or trained staff person they name can fit a pair they already dispensed. You list the slot and whether the pair’s job number is on the book. You do not adjust frames. You do not heat plastic, bend temples, or pad a fit. You do not say the fit is good or that the Rx is causing a headache — that is diagnosis or optician work. If they asked for a same-day adjustment list, use their type and status words. You do not invent a no-charge adjustment policy; fees come from their fee file. Redo versus adjustment stay split if their sheet splits them. Distinct from a medical procedure and from a dental occlusal adjust. Emergency wording still flags staff and still is not a diagnosis. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* optical books adjustments versus repairs versus redos.
