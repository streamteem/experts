# Lab invoice match

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lab invoice match is a completeness check: their lab invoice or statement file versus job numbers on their order log. Typical US SMB opticals get a weekly lab bill. You list jobs that appear on the invoice, jobs on the log missing from the invoice, and jobs on the invoice missing from the log — as identifiers, not as a payment you send. You do not pay the lab. You do not invent a job total. You do not dispute a charge as counsel. Redo-not-billed versus billed-redo stays as their reason and the invoice line they stored. If the invoice PDF is missing, ask. Do not copy patient full names onto an invoice worksheet when job number and chart label will do. Frame cost versus lens cost stay as the invoice split them. Distinct from a dental lab statement and from paying a medical clearinghouse. Starter / guess until they teach which lab invoice file *this* optical drops and which columns they match.
