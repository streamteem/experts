# Spectacle Rx expiration as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Spectacle prescription expiration is the date on their Rx print or EHR Rx field, not a clinical judgment you make. Typical US offices store an exam date and sometimes a separate expire date. Copy both if they exist; if they disagree, quote both and ask. You do not invent a two-year rule. You do not refuse an order as the doctor. You do not interpret the Rx to say it is outdated because the numbers look high. FTC Eyeglass Rule pages are about release of a copy, not a duration you set. Outside-Rx expire dates stay as printed on that image. An expired-on-their-file flag on a frame order is a question for staff, not a remake you invent. CL expire stays a separate fact. Least PHI. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office dates spectacle Rx expiration versus last comprehensive exam.
