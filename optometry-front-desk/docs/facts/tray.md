# Tray

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A tray is their physical or barcode location for a finished or in-progress pair: tray number, job number, patient chart label they allow, and status such as at-lab, in-house-edge, ready, notified, or picked-up as they coded. Typical US opticals keep a tray log in the EHR or a workbook. You list tray versus job match. You do not move a pair to “ready” because the box looked done. You do not invent a tray number. If tray and job disagree, quote both and ask. Ready-for-pickup still needs their notify process before you treat the patient as called. Do not copy extra Rx values onto a tray list. One location per list unless they asked. Lost-tray rows stay on their incident process. Distinct from a dental lab pan and from a medical specimen tray. Prefer tray number plus chart label. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* optical numbers trays and which status words they use.
