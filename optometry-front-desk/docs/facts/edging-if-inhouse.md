# Edging if they do it in-house

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

In-house edging is a status and queue they already keep when the office finishes lenses on site: job number, tray, edge-complete timestamp, and inspector or optician initials as they store them. You list the queue from their export or whiteboard photo they dropped. You do not run the edger. You do not invent a cut time. You do not say the edge is good. If they have no in-house edge path, say the file is absent — do not invent a finish shop. Uncuts that still go to an outside lab stay on the outside-lab fact. Safety notes and machine passwords stay out of this folder. Missing tray on an edged job: ask. Distinct from a manufacturing scheduler and from a dental in-house mill. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* optical edges on site and which status words mean ready for tray.
