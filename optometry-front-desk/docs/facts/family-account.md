# Family account

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A family or household account on a typical US optometry ledger groups several patients under one guarantor or subscriber. Copy the account label they use and which chart IDs sit under it. You do not merge two households to tidy insurance. You do not invent that a vision-plan family maximum is shared — that sentence comes only from a dated eligibility print. Balances stay at the level their export uses: patient versus guarantor. Do not waive a child’s pair because a parent’s exam copay posted. Minors still need their guardian process. Prefer account or chart numbers over stacked names and birth dates on a huddle sheet. If two exports disagree on who is on the account, quote both and ask. Distinct from a dental family plan with frequency limits you would not invent. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office prints family versus individual rows.
