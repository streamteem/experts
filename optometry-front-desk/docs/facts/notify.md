# Notify as their list, not a message product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Notify is their list of jobs that are ready for the patient to hear about: chart label, tray or job number, preferred contact as stored, and whether they already marked notified. You produce the list. They call, text, or portal-message from their own tools. We do not sell messaging. You do not invent that someone was reached. You do not send the ready notice. Extra phones the list does not need stay out of the write-up. Ready status must come from their status or tray file — do not notify from a guessed ship date. Do not include extra Rx or clinical notes on the notify list. Minors follow their guardian contact fields. Broken notify attempts stay as they coded them if the sheet has that column. Distinct from a medical recall blast. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office marks ready versus notified versus left-message.
