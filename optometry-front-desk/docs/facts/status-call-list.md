# Status call list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A status-call list is their workbook or export of jobs patients or staff asked about, or jobs past the promise date they stored. Columns are usually job number, chart label, last status as the lab or tray file shows, promise date as stored, and whether a call is still needed. You build that list from their files. You do not call the patient as a product. You do not call the lab as a product. You do not invent a new promise date to calm a row. If status and promise disagree, quote both. Do not copy extra complaint narrative into docs/. Ready jobs belong on notify, not as a second invented call. Backorder and special-order ETAs stay as their vendor file wrote them; do not replace an ETA with a kindness date. Prefer job number. Distinct from a medical referral-status chase. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* optical wants late jobs listed versus ready-notify jobs.
