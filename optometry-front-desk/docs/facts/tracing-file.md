# Tracing file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A tracing file is the named digital artifact they stored for a job: filename, job number, frame SKU match, and date the tracer wrote. Typical US labs accept a tracer export or a barcode that points at a stored trace. You list whether that file is in the folder they pointed to and whether the job number matches the ticket. You do not invent geometry. You do not substitute another job’s trace. You do not open a lab portal to pull a trace unless they taught that export step and the file landed here. Missing file on a send-out that their process says is remote-trace: ask. Patient-own frames may have a different filename pattern they already use — copy it. You still do not interpret the Rx. Prefer job number on the index. Starter / guess until they teach where *this* optical drops tracer exports and how they name them.
