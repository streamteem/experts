# Office spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wait-list, redo log, tray log, lab-invoice match, or day-close workbooks if they live outside the EHR. Their columns win. Export to CSV for checks when you can. Do not add a no-show-fee column, a Rx-power column, or a CPT column they do not have. Do not invent statuses. Prefer chart or job number over stacked identifiers. Card numbers, full SSNs, and refraction values stay out. If two sheets disagree with an EHR export, quote both and ask. Starter / guess until they teach which workbooks *this* office actually uses.
