# Frame and lens order sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their optical ticket workbook or EHR job export: chart label, frame SKU, lens type as already stored, treatments they checked, PD and add only as on file, lab destination, job number, status. Completeness only. Pointer, not a lab portal. You do not interpret the Rx to finish a blank ticket. You do not invent a frame price — that lives on the fee file. Missing Rx, missing PD, or missing trace stay questions. Redo rows need their reason code. Prefer job number plus chart label. Starter / guess until they teach which columns *this* optical requires before a ticket may go to the lab.
