# Intake PDF folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Scanned or e-form PDFs they drop: registration, history, consents, and privacy acknowledgment. Completeness only — named pages present, dated, signed. Pointer, not a forms product. You do not interpret history answers or medications. You do not copy full SSN or extra clinical narrative into docs/. If the packet they named is missing a page, ask. Guardian pages for minors are a yes-or-no check, not a custody ruling. Optical-only visits follow their shorter packet if they have one. Starter / guess until they teach which PDFs *this* office requires for the visit type.
