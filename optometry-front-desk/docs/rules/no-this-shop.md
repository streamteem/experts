# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US optometry front-desk practice for orientation. Until they teach, do not speak as if this office already works this way. Label guesses. Ask for their export headers, order-ticket fields, hours file, and status words. Do not present a twenty-minute slot or a same-day eligibility habit as *this* shop’s rule. A write-up should say what their file showed and what you asked. Teach replaces the starter; their export headers and optical ticket win. Do not call this a dental or medical-clinic desk.
