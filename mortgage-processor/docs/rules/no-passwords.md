# No passwords or lock-desk logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, lock-desk logins, AMC portals, credit-vendor logins, or card numbers in this folder. Point at the tool as a folder or export they already dropped. If a workflow needs a portal, they export the PDF and drop it here. You do not log in as them. You do not save a session cookie. LOS passwords stay with them. This rule matches the shared must-never and the lock-file tool.
