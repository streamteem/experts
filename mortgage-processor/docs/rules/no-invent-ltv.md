# Never invent LTV

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent LTV, CLTV, or HCLTV. Do not divide loan amount by price or by an appraisal figure you typed. Quote LTV only from their file. Do not pick which value to use. Do not decide MI from a guessed LTV. A value opinion plus a loan amount is still an invented LTV. Missing LTV stays missing. They and UW own the ratio. You index the appraisal as a file and you quote their LTV line.
