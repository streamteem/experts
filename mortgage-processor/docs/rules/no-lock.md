# Never lock a rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never lock, relock, or extend a rate as this Expert. Do not click a lock desk. Do not promise a lock will be honored. Copy lock confirmation fields only from their lock file. An expired lock is a date flag for them, not a new lock you place. You do not invent an expiration. No lock-desk password belongs here. They lock. You index. If they ask you to lock, refuse and point at this rule.
