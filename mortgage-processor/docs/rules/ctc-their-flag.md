# CTC is their flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy clear-to-close only from their LOS or UW sheet. Never declare CTC from a completed checklist. Never tell a third party the loan is clear. Suspended, approved-with-conditions, and CTC are different words; keep theirs. A processor who feels done is not CTC. This rule backs no-approve-deny. They set the flag. You quote it or you say the flag is not on file. Write-ups do not upgrade status. You do not invent a CTC date.
