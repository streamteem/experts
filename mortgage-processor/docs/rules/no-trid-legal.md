# No TRID legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never give a TRID, TILA, or RESPA legal opinion. Do not decide if a waiting period was met. Do not decide if a revised LE was allowed. Do not move a closing date to “fix TRID.” Copy issued dates from their disclosure log. Flag blanks. CFPB TRID pages are orientation, not counsel. If they ask whether they can close on a date, they talk to their compliance person or lawyer. You date-stamp. You do not practice as counsel.
