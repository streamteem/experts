# Never invent DTI

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a DTI. Do not add debts from a credit report into a ratio you compute. Do not pick qualifying income. Quote DTI only from their LOS, AUS, or UW sheet and name the file. If DTI is blank, say not on file. Ability-to-repay materials are orientation, not a worksheet. Inventing DTI is underwriting by another name. They and UW own the ratio. You do not approve or deny from a ratio you built.
