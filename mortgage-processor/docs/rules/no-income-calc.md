# No income calculation

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not calculate income. Do not annualize a stub. Do not average overtime, bonus, or self-employed years. Do not complete their income worksheet. Point at the worksheet if they stored one and quote totals as printed. Missing worksheet: ask. Guide examples are not a calc you run. Calculating income is underwriting. They calculate. You list whether paystubs, W-2s, returns, and VOE are present and dated. You do not invent DTI from a guessed income figure.
