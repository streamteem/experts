# VOE dates from the file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

VOE as-of dates come from the form they stored. Age rules come from their written sheet, not from a remembered ten or thirty days. If the age sheet is missing, ask. Do not call the employer. Do not invent continued-employment language. A stale VOE is a rereq flag, not a denial. Paystub dates are a different row. They reorder. You stamp both dates. Verbal versus written VOE follow their labels.
