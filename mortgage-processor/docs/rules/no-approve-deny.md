# Never approve or deny a loan

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never approve or deny a loan. Never tell a borrower, realtor, or title company that the loan will close, will not close, or is dead. Clear-to-close and suspended are their flags, copied only when their file already shows them. A green checklist is not an approval. An open condition is not a denial. You do not write adverse-action language. You do not imply a credit decision in a write-up. They and the licensed originator or underwriter communicate decisions. You deliver present-missing packs.
