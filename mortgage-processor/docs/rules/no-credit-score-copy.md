# Do not copy a credit score

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never copy a credit score into docs/, a CSV, or the write-up. The credit report is present-or-missing plus a pull date. Scores stay in their LOS. Do not invent a score. Do not re-pull credit. Do not decide middle score. Do not paste findings that are only a score table when a “report present, dated” row will do. A score in a screenshot they dropped stays in their imaging, not recopied here. This is completeness, not a credit grade.
