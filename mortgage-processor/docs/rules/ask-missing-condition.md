# Ask when the condition list is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they ask for a conditions checklist and no conditions sheet or LOS export is in the folder, ask. Do not invent conditions from a remembered product overlay. Do not build a Fannie-style list from memory. You may index documents that are already in the folder as a present list, still labeled as not-their-condition-list. A missing UW list is a missing-file row. They export or they write the list. You wait. Rush does not let you invent UW items.
