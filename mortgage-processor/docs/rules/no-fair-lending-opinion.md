# No fair-lending legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never give a fair-lending, ECOA, or HMDA legal opinion. Do not decide if a file was steered. Do not decide occupancy or product choice as a discrimination call. Do not write an adverse-action reason. You may list missing forms they named. You do not interpret why a file was suspended. HUD fair-housing pages, if used for orientation elsewhere, are not a stamp you apply. They and counsel own fair-lending. You keep the folder complete and the write-up factual.
