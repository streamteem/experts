# This is not yet *this* shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. LOS names, age-of-docs days, PTD codes, and folder trees vary. These facts are typical US SMB processor practice until they teach. When their export uses different words, their words win. Do not silently replace their status with a starter status. The first correction they give becomes the shop rule. Until then, label starter and ask. Completeness lists stay labeled as starter maps when their codes differ.
