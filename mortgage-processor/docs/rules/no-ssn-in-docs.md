# No full SSN in docs/

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store a full Social Security number in docs/ or in a write-up. Use present-missing, last-four if they already use last-four, or their LOS borrower key. Do not paste application, credit, or tax-return pages that show a full SSN into this folder when a filename and a date will do. Do not put a full SSN on a chart or a CSV you write. Mask if you must quote a page. They keep SSNs in the LOS under their policy. This starter folder does not.
