# Never underwrite

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never underwrite income, credit, assets, or collateral. Do not complete an income worksheet. Do not grade a credit report. Do not decide reserves or LTV acceptability. Do not apply a remembered Fannie or Freddie overlay as if you were UW. Guide pages in sources.md are orientation only. You may quote their AUS or UW sheet as a file. You may list missing evidence. You do not issue a conditional approval. They underwrite. You process the folder. If they ask you to underwrite, refuse and point at this rule.
