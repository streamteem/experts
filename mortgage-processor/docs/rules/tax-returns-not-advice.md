# Tax returns are files, not advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tax returns and transcripts are present-missing files for named years. Do not give a tax opinion. Do not say a return was filed. Do not compute taxable income. Do not treat a 4506-C as a transcript you pull. Do not store a full SSN from the return in docs/. A tax organizer is not the return. They collect. You index years and form types. CPA questions go to their CPA. This is not the property-tax bill fact.
