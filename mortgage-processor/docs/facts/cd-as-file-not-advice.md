# Closing Disclosure as a file (not advice)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closing-Disclosure-as-a-file-not-advice means the CD PDF they generated or received is on file, with issued date as printed, not a TRID legal opinion and not a fee you invent. Typical US SMB processor or closer desks store initial and revised CDs as versions. You list version, date, and whether the PDF is complete. You do not decide if timing is legally sufficient. You do not invent a cash-to-close. You do not rewrite fee names. If CD and lock file disagree on rate, quote both and ask; do not pick a rate. CFPB Closing Disclosure pages are orientation only. You do not store a full SSN from the CD into docs/. This is not the transaction coordinator’s closing-checklist for the brokerage. They issue the CD. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop versions CDs and which calendar file holds the issued date.
