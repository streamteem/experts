# Conditions prior to docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Conditions-prior-to-doc are the items their sheet says must be cleared before closing documents are drawn, listed as open or cleared only as they coded. Typical US SMB processor desks split PTD from PTF. You copy PTD rows and the evidence files they pointed at. You do not draw docs. You do not tell the closer to print because you think PTD is done. You do not invent a PTD item. Docs-out is their flag, not yours. A UW condition labeled PTD stays PTD until they recode it. Completeness of PTD evidence is still not an approval. You do not approve or deny because PTD looks empty or full. You do not invent a rate on a docs request. They draw docs. You list open PTD. Starter until they teach how *this* shop exports PTD versus PTF and which status means docs may be requested.
