# Insurance binder present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Insurance-binder-present means a homeowners or hazard binder or declarations page they stored is on file with named insured, address, and effective dates as printed, not that you bind coverage. Typical US SMB processor desks need a binder that covers closing and often a mortgagee clause as their checklist wrote it. You list present versus missing, effective and expiration dates, and whether the mortgagee line matches the wording they stored. You do not invent a coverage amount. You do not say they are covered. You do not bind. A quote is not a binder unless they labeled it as the accepted evidence. Flood insurance, if their sheet requires it, is a separate row from HOI. You do not store a policy login. They and the agent obtain the binder. You mark the row. Starter until they teach how *this* shop treats binder versus full policy and which mortgagee clause text they require.
