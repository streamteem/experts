# VOE expiration date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

VOE-expire is the date stamp on the verification-of-employment file compared to their written age rule, not a stability decision. Typical US SMB processor desks treat verbal and written VOEs as stale after a number of days their investor or house sheet prints. You copy the VOE date and the rule date if they stored the rule. You do not invent a thirty-day or ten-day rule from memory. If the rule file is missing, ask. A VOE that is past their printed age is a rereq or refresh flag, not a denial you write. Paystub dates and VOE dates are different rows; do not collapse them. You do not call the employer to refresh as this product. They reorder. You date-stamp. Starter until they teach how *this* shop’s age sheet treats verbal versus written VOE and whether a current stub resets the VOE row.
