# HOA documents if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

HOA-docs-if-any are the association papers the loan checklist named when the property is in an HOA or condo: budget, questionnaire, master policy, or resale items they asked the processor to store. Typical US SMB processor desks chase lender-required HOA packets, which is not the same job as a transaction coordinator chasing a buyer resale packet for the brokerage file. You list each named item as present or missing and the date on the association letter. You do not invent a transfer fee. You do not give a project-approval opinion. You do not say the condo is warrantable. A marketing flyer is not the questionnaire. If the file is a detached house with no HOA, say none on their sheet rather than inventing docs. Master-policy versus HOI-binder stay separate rows. They order the packet. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names condo versus HOA document sets on the conditions list.
