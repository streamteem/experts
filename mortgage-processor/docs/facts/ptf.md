# PTF (prior to funding)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

PTF is their code for prior-to-funding conditions that remain after docs, not a funding decision you make. Typical US SMB processor desks use PTF for collateral, insurance, or trailing items the funder watches. You list PTF items and evidence. You do not fund. You do not send a wire. You do not invent a PTF item. You do not mark funded. A trailing document they expect after close stays trailing if they coded it that way, not silently moved to PTF. Open PTF is not a denial you write. You do not tell title they can disburse. You do not invent a funding date. You do not mark CTC from an empty PTF list. They and the funder own funding. You pack the PTF list. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits PTF from trailing and which status the funder treats as clear to fund.
