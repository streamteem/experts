# VOD as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A verification of deposit is a stored form or bank PDF they filed, not a balance you look up and not a funds-to-close decision. Typical US SMB processor desks request a VOD when statements are stale, when their investor sheet says so, or when the conditions list names it. You list present versus missing, the as-of date on the form, and institution name as printed. You do not invent an available balance. You do not copy the full account number into docs/. You do not decide whether the funds are acceptable. A VOD and a statement packet can both be required; do not drop one because the other arrived. A portal screenshot they did not label as the VOD stays a question. This is not an asset underwrite. They order the VOD. You index the file. Starter until they teach how *this* shop treats VOD versus stacked statements on the same condition.
