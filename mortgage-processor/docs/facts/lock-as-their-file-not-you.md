# Lock as their file (not you)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lock-as-their-file-not-you means a lock confirmation they stored shows product, rate as printed, and expiration as printed, and you never lock or relock. Typical US SMB processor desks file the lock from the lock desk or investor portal. You list present versus missing, lock date, expiration, and rate exactly as printed. You do not invent a rate. You do not extend a lock. You do not tell anyone they are locked because a conversation happened. An expired lock on their file is a date-stamp flag for them, not a new lock you place. Lock and LE can disagree; quote both. No lock-desk password belongs here. This is not a pricing desk. A float is not a lock unless they filed a confirmation. They lock. You index the confirmation. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names lock confirmations and who is allowed to lock.
