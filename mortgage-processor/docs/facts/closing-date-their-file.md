# Closing date from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closing-date-their-file means the closing or disbursement date comes from their calendar, CD, lock, or title scheduler file, not from a remembered typical thirty days and not from a realtor text you did not file. Typical US SMB processor desks keep a target close in the LOS. You copy that date onto the date-stamp pack. You do not invent a close date to make a lock look safe. You do not move the date to “fix” TRID. If contract close, lock expiration, and LOS close disagree, quote all three and ask. You do not run the brokerage option or inspection calendar; that is the transaction coordinator. Wet-sign versus eNote is their process fact, not a date you pick. They set the date. You stamp it. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names estimated versus confirmed closing and who may change it.
