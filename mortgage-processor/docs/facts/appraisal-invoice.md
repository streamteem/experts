# Appraisal invoice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An appraisal invoice is the AMC or appraiser bill they stored, with amount and paid-or-due as they coded it, not a payment you send. Typical US SMB processor desks file the invoice next to the appraisal PDF and sometimes a paid receipt. You list present versus missing and the amount as printed. You do not pay the appraiser. You do not invent a fee. You do not move funds. A paid stamp they recorded is a file fact; you do not mark paid from memory. Invoice amount and CD appraisal-fee line can disagree; quote both. No AMC portal password belongs here. This is not a value opinion. You do not treat a missing invoice as a missing appraisal report; they are separate rows. They pay. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records paid versus collected-from-borrower appraisal fees on the conditions list.
