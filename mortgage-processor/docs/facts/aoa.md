# Acknowledgment of appraisal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An acknowledgment of appraisal (AOA) is the borrower receipt or delivery proof they stored that the appraisal was provided, dated as stamped, not a value the borrower accepted as correct. Typical US SMB processor desks keep a signed AOA or an e-delivery log from the LOS. You list present versus missing and the date. You do not decide if delivery timing was legally enough. You do not invent a signature. You do not treat a “looks fine” email as the AOA unless they filed it as the acknowledgment. Missing AOA when their sheet requires it is a missing-docs row, not a denial. This is not an underwrite and not a TRID legal opinion. They deliver the report. You index the receipt. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records e-delivery versus wet AOA and which date they put on the calendar.
