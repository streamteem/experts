# HOI binder document

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An HOI binder document is the specific binder or dec-page PDF they filed as the insurance evidence row, distinct from the broader HOI topic. Typical US SMB processor desks want a binder that shows effective date on or before closing as their sheet wrote it. You list the PDF name, dates, and named insured as printed. You do not treat an expired binder as current. You do not invent a renewal. You do not bind. A binder that names the wrong mortgagee is a mismatch flag, not a coverage you rewrite. Quote versus binder stay labeled. If they use a full policy in place of a binder, say so on the row rather than inventing a binder. They and the agent correct names. You mark present, dated, or missing. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files binder versus declarations and which date they compare to closing.
