# Title commitment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A title commitment is the title-company PDF they received, indexed with commitment date and file number as printed, not a title opinion you write. Typical US SMB processor desks file the commitment, exception schedules, and later updates in the title folder. You list present versus missing and version dates. You do not clear exceptions. You do not say title is insurable. You do not hold earnest money. Schedule headings may be routed to a missing-response list if they asked. A proforma stays proforma. This is routing inside the loan file, distinct from a transaction coordinator managing broker objections. American Land Title Association pages are orientation only. Missing schedules they named stay missing. You do not republish wire instructions found in the title packet. They and title or UW own clearance. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files commitment versus policy and which update replaces the prior PDF.
