# W-2 present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

W-2-present is a completeness row for wage statements they said this loan needs, usually one or two years matching the application employers. Typical US SMB processor desks match W-2 employer names to the application and to VOE as a file, and they flag a year their sheet named that did not arrive. You list year, employer as printed, and whether the PDF is cut off. You do not annualize wages. You do not decide overtime or bonus treatment. You do not invent a W-2 because a paystub exists. A 1099 is not a W-2; keep types labeled. You do not copy a full SSN from the W-2 into docs/. A transcript they stored that includes W-2 data is still their file, not a score you compute. This is not an income underwrite. They collect W-2s. You index years and names. Starter until they teach how *this* shop matches W-2s to employers and which years this product’s sheet requires.
