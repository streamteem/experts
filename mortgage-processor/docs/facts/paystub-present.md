# Paystub present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Paystub-present means the YTD stubs they named are in the income folder, with period-end dates readable, not that income is acceptable. Typical US SMB processor desks want the most recent consecutive stubs their sheet lists, often thirty days of pay. You list employer, period dates, and whether YTD is visible. You do not calculate monthly income from the stub. You do not annualize. You do not decide whether a stub is too old; you copy the period-end date onto the date-stamp pack and compare it to their written age rule if they stored one. Missing their age rule: ask, do not invent thirty days. A handwritten pay record counts only if they filed it as the stub substitute. You do not store a full SSN from the stub into docs/. This is not an underwrite. They chase current stubs. You mark present, dated, or missing. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts consecutive stubs and which YTD fields their sheet requires.
