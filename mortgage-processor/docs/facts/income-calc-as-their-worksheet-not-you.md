# Income calc as their worksheet (not you)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Income-calc-as-their-worksheet-not-you means any monthly-income figure on the pack comes from a worksheet they already stored or from the LOS income screen they exported, not from a calc you run. Typical US SMB processor desks have an income worksheet the underwriter or a designated processor completed. You may list that the worksheet is present and the total as printed. You do not annualize stubs. You do not average bonuses. You do not decide overtime. You do not invent a method. Missing worksheet: ask, do not build one. This is the hard line against underwriting. Fannie and Freddie guide pages are orientation, not a worksheet you fill. You do not invent DTI from a guessed income. They calculate. You index the sheet. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop is allowed to complete the income worksheet and which export you may quote.
