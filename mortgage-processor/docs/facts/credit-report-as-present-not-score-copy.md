# Credit report as present (do not copy the score)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Credit-report-as-present-not-score-copy means the tri-merge or other credit file they pulled is on the loan as present, with pull date as stamped, and you do not copy scores into docs/ or the write-up. Typical US SMB processor desks need a report in the LOS; the underwriter reads it. You list present versus missing and the date. You do not re-pull credit. You do not invent a score. You do not paste FICO or Vantage numbers onto a checklist in this folder. You do not decide tradelines or derogatory treatment. A frozen-file note they stored is a routing flag, not a denial. Soft-pull marketing prints are not the loan credit report unless they said so. This is not an underwrite of credit. They pull. You mark present and dated. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names the credit vendor and which date they treat as the live pull.
