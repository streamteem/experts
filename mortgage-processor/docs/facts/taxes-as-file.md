# Property taxes as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Taxes-as-file means property-tax bills, county printouts, or title tax figures they stored are on the loan as present, not a tax opinion and not an escrow analysis you invent. Typical US SMB processor desks file the latest tax bill to support the CD or an escrow worksheet they already ran. You list tax year, amount as printed, and parcel as printed. You do not invent an annual tax. You do not decide homestead or exemption treatment as counsel. You do not give a property-tax legal opinion. A title commitment tax line is a file quote, still not your number. You do not store a full SSN from a tax bill if one appears. This is not the 1040 tax-return fact. They collect the bill. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop treats county website prints versus paid-tax receipts on the conditions list.
