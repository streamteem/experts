# Loan Estimate as a file (not advice)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Loan-Estimate-as-a-file-not-advice means the LE PDF they issued is on file, with issued date as printed, not a shopping opinion and not a rate you invent. Typical US SMB processor desks store the initial LE and any revised LEs as versions next to intent-to-proceed. You list version, date, and product name as printed. You do not decide if a changed-circumstance LE was legally allowed. You do not invent fees or a rate. You do not lock from the LE. If no LE is in the folder, say missing; do not draft one. CFPB Loan Estimate pages are orientation only. You do not store a full SSN from the LE into docs/. Intent-to-proceed is a separate fact. They issue the LE. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names initial versus revised LEs and which LOS disclosure log is the date source.
