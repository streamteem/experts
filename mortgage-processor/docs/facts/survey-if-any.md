# Survey if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Survey-if-any is the survey, plat, or survey affidavit they stored when the loan or title checklist named one, not a boundary opinion and not the transaction coordinator’s survey-objection calendar. Typical US SMB processor desks file a new survey, an existing survey the title company accepted, or an affidavit they chose. You list present versus missing and the survey date. You do not interpret encroachments. You do not say a fence is over the line. You do not invent a survey requirement for a product that their sheet marks survey-not-required. A county GIS printout is not a survey unless they labeled it as the accepted stand-in. Missing survey when their sheet requires one is a missing-docs row, not a denial. They order the survey. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop treats refinance versus purchase survey rules and which title notes waive the survey row.
