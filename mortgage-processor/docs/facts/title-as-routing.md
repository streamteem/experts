# Title as routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Title-as-routing means you move title PDFs they received into the loan folder and list them against conditions, not that you clear title or hold escrow. Typical US SMB processor desks receive a commitment, later updates, invoices, and wire instructions they must not republish. You record document names, file numbers as printed, and dates. You do not say title is clean. You do not clear exceptions. You do not choose a title company. You do not send money. Exception headings may be copied onto a missing-response list if they asked, still as routing, not as a title opinion. This is distinct from a transaction coordinator’s title-objection calendar for the broker. A proforma labeled draft stays draft. They and counsel or the underwriter own objections. You route. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names title companies on the file and which PDFs belong in the title folder versus the CD packet.
