# Gift letter present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gift-letter-present is a completeness row for the gift letter PDF they require when the file uses gift funds, plus any donor evidence their sheet named. Typical US SMB processor desks keep a signed letter with donor name, amount, and relationship as printed, then a paper trail of transfer. You list the letter as present or missing and whether the amount on the letter matches the amount they coded on the asset sheet. You do not invent a gift letter. You do not decide whether the gift is an acceptable source. You do not write the letter for the donor. A text that says “mom is gifting” is not the letter. You do not store a full account number from the donor statement into docs/. This is not an underwrite of donor capacity. The gift-paper-trail fact covers the transfer evidence. They collect the letter. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop’s gift form looks and which donor docs sit on the same condition.
