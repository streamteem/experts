# Appraisal conditions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Appraisal conditions are repair, completion, or recert items written on the appraisal or on the underwriter’s condition list they stored, tracked as open or cleared only as they coded them. Typical US SMB processor desks list “subject to” repairs, final inspection, and invoice paid as separate rows. You copy the condition text. You do not decide the repair is done because a borrower texted a photo unless they filed that photo as the clearing document their sheet names. You do not invent a repair list from habit. You do not give a value opinion when a completion report arrives; you mark the completion PDF present. A 1004D or equivalent is a file if they stored it. This is not an underwrite of collateral. They order the inspection. You list open appraisal conditions. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes subject-to versus as-is and which photos count as clearing evidence.
