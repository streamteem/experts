# LTV as their file (not you)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

LTV-as-their-file-not-you means loan-to-value, CLTV, or HCLTV appears only as printed on their LOS, AUS, or UW sheet, never as a ratio you compute from price and loan amount. Typical US SMB processor desks see LTV on findings next to the appraisal file. You may quote their number and name the file. You do not invent an appraised value. You do not pick price versus appraisal to compute LTV. You do not decide MI is required as a product decision; MI-as-file is their flag. You do not give a value opinion. Missing LTV: say not on file. A CMA is not an LTV source. You do not invent a rate from an LTV tier. They and UW own the ratio. You quote. Starter until they teach which screen at *this* shop is the LTV source and how they label CLTV versus HCLTV.
