# DTI as their file (not you)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

DTI-as-their-file-not-you means debt-to-income appears on the pack only as printed on their LOS, AUS, or UW sheet, never as a ratio you compute. Typical US SMB processor desks see front-end and back-end DTI on a findings PDF. You may quote those numbers as “their file says” and name the PDF. You do not add debts. You do not omit debts. You do not invent a qualifying income. You do not decide the DTI is acceptable. Ability-to-repay pages are orientation, not a credit decision. Missing DTI on their sheet: say not on file, do not calculate. This is not an approve or deny. You do not invent a compensating factor. You do not store a credit score next to a DTI quote. They and UW own the ratio. You quote. Starter until they teach which LOS screen or findings PDF is the DTI source at *this* shop.
