# VOE as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A verification of employment is a stored form, vendor PDF, or employer letter they filed, not a phone call you make and not an income figure you compute. Typical US SMB processor desks use The Work Number, a written VOE, or a verbal VOE form they already completed and saved. You list present versus missing, the date on the form, employer name as printed, and whether their sheet also wants a current paystub. You do not call the employer as this product. You do not invent a hire date or a probability of continued employment. You do not treat a LinkedIn page as a VOE. A VOE that their sheet marks expired is a date-stamp flag, not a denial you write. This is not underwriting stability of income. They order the VOE. You file the date and the present-missing row. Starter until they teach how *this* shop orders VOEs and which vendor PDFs count as the live file.
