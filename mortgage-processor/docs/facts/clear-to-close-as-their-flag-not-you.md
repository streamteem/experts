# Clear to close as their flag (not yours)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Clear-to-close-as-their-flag-not-you means CTC, approved, or similar words appear on the pack only when their LOS or UW sheet already shows that flag. Typical US SMB processor desks wait for an underwriter or a designated closer to set CTC. You do not declare CTC because every document row is green on your checklist. You do not tell the borrower they are approved. You do not tell the realtor the loan will fund. A conditions checklist with no open PTD items is still not CTC unless they flagged it. Suspended or incomplete files stay those words. This is the brightest line on the desk: completeness is not a lending decision. You do not invent a CTC date. You do not upgrade approved-with-conditions into CTC. They set the flag. You quote it. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop is allowed to set CTC and which LOS status string you may copy.
