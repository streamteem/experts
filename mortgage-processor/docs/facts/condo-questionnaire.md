# Condo questionnaire

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A condo questionnaire is the project form the lender or investor sheet asked them to store, completed by the association or management company as they filed it. Typical US SMB processor desks chase HOA questionnaires for condo and sometimes PUD loans. You list present versus missing, date, and whether all pages their form numbered are there. You do not decide warrantability. You do not invent reserve-percent answers. You do not fill blanks from a remembered project. A questionnaire for a different phase or building stays a mismatch question. This is not a project-approval underwrite and not the brokerage HOA resale packet. Master insurance on the questionnaire is still not you binding coverage. Blank required fields on their form stay listed as blank, not filled by you. They send the form. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names the condo questionnaire versus the full HOA packet.
