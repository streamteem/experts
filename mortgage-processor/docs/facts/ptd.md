# PTD (prior to docs)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

PTD is their code for prior-to-docs conditions, a subset of the conditions list, not a credit decision. Typical US SMB processor desks use PTD for items the closer needs before generating the package: updated stubs, HOI binder, CD issued as their process, and UW PTD rows. You list PTD items and evidence present-missing. You do not approve PTD. You do not deny for open PTD. You do not invent the code. If their LOS uses different letters, use theirs and label this starter name. PTD and prior-to-doc are the same family; keep one working list keyed as they key it. Docs drawn with open PTD is a flag they own. You do not waive a PTD row. They clear or waive in the LOS. You pack the list. Starter until they teach how *this* shop’s PTD codes read and who may waive a PTD row.
