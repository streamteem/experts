# Entity documents if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Entity-docs-if-any are articles, operating agreements, resolutions, or good-standing prints they stored when the borrower is an entity, indexed as present or missing, not an entity-law opinion. Typical US SMB processor desks see LLC or trust purchases on investment files. You list each named document on their entity checklist. You do not decide who may sign. You do not invent a resolution. You do not say the entity is in good standing as counsel. A draft operating agreement is a draft. You do not store a full EIN in docs/ if a present-and-last-four row will do and their sheet allows it. Personal-borrower files should say none rather than inventing entity docs. Missing a named resolution is a missing-docs row, not a denial you write. They collect organizational papers. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop’s entity checklist looks and which secretary-of-state prints they accept.
