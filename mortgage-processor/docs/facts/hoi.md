# Homeowners insurance (HOI)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

HOI on this desk is the hazard-insurance evidence they stored for the subject property: binder, dec page, or invoice, with amounts and dates as printed, not a coverage opinion. Typical US SMB processor desks need HOI before CTC as their sheet says. You list carrier as printed, effective dates, and whether a mortgagee clause is visible. You do not invent a dwelling limit. You do not say the coverage is enough. You do not bind. Deductible as printed can be copied onto the checklist if their sheet asks; do not invent one. HOI is the policy topic; hoi-binder is the specific binder document when they split those rows. Flood and HOI stay separate. A quote is not HOI evidence unless they labeled it that way. They obtain insurance. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names HOI versus master condo policy and which amount fields their sheet wants copied.
