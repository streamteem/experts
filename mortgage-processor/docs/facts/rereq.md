# Rerequest (rereq)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A rereq is a document they asked for again because the first file was missing, stale by their date rule, or unreadable, tracked as a new present-missing row. Typical US SMB processor desks rereq stubs, VOE, and statements when dates age out. You list what was rereqed, the reason as they wrote it, and whether the refresh arrived. You do not invent a rereq. You do not invent an age rule. You do not treat a rereq as a denial. You do not calculate new income from the refresh. Old and new versions stay versioned; do not delete the stale PDF if their process keeps history. You do not store a full SSN from the refresh in docs/. They request the refresh. You date-stamp both. Starter until they teach how *this* shop logs rereqs in the LOS versus a side workbook.
