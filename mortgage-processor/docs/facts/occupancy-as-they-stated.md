# Occupancy as they stated

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Occupancy-as-they-stated means primary, second home, or investment is copied from the application or their occupancy form, not inferred from mail, utilities, or a visit. Typical US SMB processor desks take occupancy from the URLA. You list what they stated. You do not decide occupancy fraud. You do not change occupancy to make a product fit. You do not give a fair-lending opinion about occupancy. If application and contract occupancy disagree, quote both and ask. A landlord statement they stored is a file, not your occupancy call. This is not an underwrite of intent to occupy. You do not invent occupancy from the tax bill mailing address. You do not invent a rate from an occupancy type. They and UW own occupancy. You copy the stated value. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records occupancy changes and which form wins when two files disagree.
