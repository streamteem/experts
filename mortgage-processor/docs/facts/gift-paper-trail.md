# Gift paper trail

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A gift paper trail is the donor-to-borrower transfer evidence they stored in addition to the gift letter: withdrawal, deposit, or gift-check images as they filed them. Typical US SMB processor desks want a trail that matches the letter amount. You list each piece as present or missing and dates as stamped. You do not invent a trail. You do not decide the gift is acceptable. You do not store full account numbers in docs/. A Venmo screenshot counts only if they filed it as trail evidence. Letter without trail is a missing-docs row when their sheet requires both. This is not an asset underwrite. Amount mismatches between letter and trail stay quoted, not silently fixed. You do not invent a donor relationship. They collect the trail. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop chains letter, donor statement, and borrower deposit on the same condition.
