# Wet sign versus eNote as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wet-sign-versus-eNote-as-their-process means the closing package type is whatever their closer, title, or investor process file says for this loan, not a method you choose. Typical US SMB processor desks flag hybrid, wet, or eClose on the file. You copy that flag. You do not decide whether eNotes are legally allowed. You do not invent a notary. You do not send the package. A borrower preference email is not the process unless they filed it as the chosen method. MERS or eNote IDs stay as printed if present; you do not invent one. Package-type mismatches with investor overlays they stored stay listed as questions. You do not pick wet sign to “be safer.” They and the closer own the method. You index the flag. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes wet, hybrid, and eClose and which title companies they use for each.
