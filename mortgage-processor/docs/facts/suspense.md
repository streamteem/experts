# Suspense

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Suspense is their underwriting or LOS status when the file is incomplete or waiting, copied as they coded it, not a denial you issue. Typical US SMB processor desks see suspense with a numbered list of rereqs. You list the suspense code, date, and the items on their suspense sheet. You do not unsuspend. You do not approve. You do not tell the borrower the loan is dead or alive. You do not invent suspense reasons. A processor hold is not suspense unless they used that word. When they later change status, quote the new flag; do not keep suspense on the pack as if you decided. This is file status, not fair-lending advice. They and UW change status. You list. Starter until they teach how *this* shop’s suspense codes read and which comments are allowed on a working list.
