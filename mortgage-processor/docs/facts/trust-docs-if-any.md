# Trust documents if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Trust-docs-if-any are the certificate of trust, trust agreement pages they asked for, or trustee ID they stored when title or the application is in a trust. Typical US SMB processor desks file a certificate of trust instead of the full instrument when their sheet allows it. You list present versus missing and the date. You do not give a trust-law opinion. You do not decide whether the trustee may convey. You do not invent required pages from a remembered state custom. A will is not a trust. Revocable versus irrevocable labels stay as they printed them, not as your legal call. You do not store extra beneficiary SSNs in docs/. They and counsel own the trust. You index the loan-file copies. Starter until they teach how *this* shop treats certificate-of-trust versus full trust and which title notes close the row.
