# Intent to proceed as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Intent-to-proceed-as-a-file means their signed or dated ITP, or the LOS note they treat as ITP, is present, not that you decide TRID timing. Typical US SMB processor desks collect a signed intent after the LE. You list present versus missing and the date on the form or note. You do not invent an intent from a phone call you did not file. You do not decide whether the intent is legally valid. You do not issue an LE. A pre-qual they signed is not ITP unless they labeled it as the intent document. Missing ITP when their sheet requires it is a missing-docs row. CFPB TRID pages are orientation only. You do not store a full SSN from the intent form in docs/. They collect intent. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records ITP in the LOS versus a wet-signed PDF.
