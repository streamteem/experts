# Underwriter conditions as their list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Underwriter conditions as their list means you copy the UW worksheet or LOS UW conditions they stored, word for word as statused, and you do not rewrite them into a credit decision. Typical US SMB processor desks receive a conditional approval or a suspense with numbered items. You index those items onto the conditions checklist. You do not approve. You do not deny. You do not decide an item is satisfied because you personally think the PDF is enough; satisfaction is their code or the UW’s later note. You do not invent an overlay. A processor condition and a UW condition can both exist; keep the source labeled. This is not you underwriting. Fair-lending and TRID legal questions stay out of scope. They send the list. You track documents against it. Starter until they teach how *this* shop separates processor conditions from UW conditions in the LOS.
