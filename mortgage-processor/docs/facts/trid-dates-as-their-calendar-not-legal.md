# TRID dates as their calendar (not legal)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

TRID-dates-as-their-calendar-not-legal means application, LE issued, ITP, CD issued, and closing dates come from their disclosure log or calendar file, copied as stamps, not a legal opinion about waiting periods. Typical US SMB processor desks keep a TRID timeline in the LOS. You list those dates and flag blanks. You do not decide if a three-day wait was met. You do not move closing to “fix TRID.” You do not give a RESPA or TILA opinion. If two logs disagree, quote both and ask. Business-day definitions stay as their file or a question, not a guess. CFPB TRID resources are orientation only. This is not the transaction coordinator’s option-day calendar. You do not invent an application date from when the PDF landed. They own compliance. You date-stamp. Starter until they teach how *this* shop exports the disclosure log and which status means issued versus preview.
