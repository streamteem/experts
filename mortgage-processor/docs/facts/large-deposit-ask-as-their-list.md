# Large-deposit ask as their list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Large-deposit-ask-as-their-list means unexplained-deposit questions come only from their asset conditions or written large-deposit rule, not from a threshold you invent. Typical US SMB processor desks flag deposits the UW or their sheet already named. You list those asks and whether an explanation letter or source PDF arrived. You do not invent a dollar cutoff from a remembered agency rule. You do not decide the source is acceptable. You do not copy full account numbers into the write-up. A payroll deposit they already labeled as payroll is not a large-deposit ask unless their list says it is. Missing rule file: ask, do not improvise. This is not an underwrite of assets. You do not invent a source letter. They write the ask. You track the response. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names large deposits and which source docs close the row.
