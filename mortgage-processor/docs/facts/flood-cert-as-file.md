# Flood certificate as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A flood certificate as a file is the flood-zone determination PDF they stored, with zone and cert date as printed, not a flood-insurance legal opinion and not a map you draw. Typical US SMB processor desks file a life-of-loan cert from their vendor. You list present versus missing, zone as printed, and whether their sheet also wants a flood policy. You do not decide if the property is in a special flood hazard area as a legal call beyond quoting the cert. You do not invent a zone from a remembered street. FEMA map pages are orientation; their cert wins. You do not say they must or must not buy flood insurance as counsel. Missing cert is a missing-docs row. They order the cert. You index. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names the flood vendor and which zone values on the cert trigger a flood-policy row on their sheet.
