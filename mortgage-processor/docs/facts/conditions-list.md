# Conditions list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A conditions list is their sheet of prior-to-doc, prior-to-funding, and any suspense items for this loan, with owner and status as they coded them. Typical US SMB processor desks export conditions from the LOS or keep a workbook keyed by loan number. You list each condition text as they wrote it, present-missing evidence, and dates on the evidence. You do not add a condition because a remembered overlay usually wants it. You do not delete a condition because the file feels close. You do not mark cleared unless their process says the stored document clears that row. Completeness is not an approval. An empty open-conditions column is not clear-to-close unless they flagged CTC. This is the core of the desk. They and the underwriter own the list. You maintain the pack. Starter until they teach how *this* shop exports conditions and which status words mean open versus cleared.
