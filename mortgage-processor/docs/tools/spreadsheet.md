# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV you build for a conditions checklist, missing-docs list, or suspense list. Typical US SMB processors live in workbooks even when an LOS exists. You do not invent keys. You do not fill blank dates, rates, DTI, or LTV. Checks often look for loan and status columns. No password belongs in the workbook. Full SSNs and credit scores do not belong in the CSV. This tool is the usual pack writer after a source export is in work/.
