# Income folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The income folder holds paystubs, W-2s, tax-return PDFs, and their income worksheet if they stored one. You list types, years, and period dates. You do not calculate income. You do not invent a document type. You do not store a full SSN from those PDFs in docs/. No payroll-portal password belongs here. This tool supports missing-docs for income conditions. Worksheet totals may be quoted as printed; you do not fill the worksheet.
