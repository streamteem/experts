# Conditions sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The conditions sheet is their LOS export or workbook of open and cleared conditions: loan number, condition text, PTD/PTF/suspense code as they coded it, owner, and status. Typical US SMB processor desks export from Encompass or keep a numbered workbook. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a condition. You do not mark cleared without their code. No LOS password belongs here. This tool starts conditions-checklist and suspense-list packs. Checks look for loan and condition when asked.
