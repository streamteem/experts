# Processor calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The processor calendar is their disclosure log, lock expirations, VOE age, and target closing dates as they exported them. Typical US SMB desks keep this in the LOS or a sheet. You copy dates as stamped. You do not invent a close date. You do not decide TRID waits as counsel. You do not lock from a date. No calendar-login password belongs here. This tool feeds date-stamps and any pack that quotes issued or expiration dates. Missing calendar: ask.
