# VOE folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The VOE folder holds written VOEs, vendor PDFs, and verbal VOE forms they already completed and saved. You list present, as-of date, and employer as printed. You do not call the employer. You do not invent a hire date. You do not calculate income from the VOE. No verification-vendor password belongs here. This tool supports date-stamps and missing-docs when employment conditions are open. Stale dates follow their age sheet, not a guessed number of days.
