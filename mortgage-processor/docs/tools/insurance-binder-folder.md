# Insurance binder folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The insurance-binder folder holds HOI binders, dec pages, master condo policies, and flood policies they stored. You list present, effective dates, and mortgagee line as printed. You do not bind. You do not invent a limit. You do not say they are covered. Quote versus binder stay labeled. No carrier-portal password belongs here. This tool starts insurance-binder-present. Flood and HOI stay separate rows when both files exist. Expired binders stay expired on the date-stamp pack.
