# Purchase contract PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The contract PDF is the executed purchase agreement plus amendments they stored in the loan file. You list present, price as printed, and amendment dates. You do not invent a closing date. You do not run option or earnest-money calendars; that is the transaction coordinator. You do not change the contract. If price and their loan-amount field disagree, quote both. This tool supports missing-docs and date-stamps on purchase files. Refinance files may have no contract; say none if that is true.
