# SIS student export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The student-information export is their roster truth: student_id, name as they allow, grade, homeroom, and status. Typical US small schools export from PowerSchool, FACTS, Alma, or a workbook. You join other lists on their ID. You do not invent a student. You do not store the SIS password. Draft versus withdrawn status stays as they coded it. This tool feeds attendance joins, transcript-request checks, waitlist-versus-roster, and records-request completeness. Withdrawn and no-show statuses stay on the export so you do not treat them as active roster. No login is stored here. Checks often look for a student column. Homeroom and grade help attendance and waitlist joins.
