# Waitlist sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The waitlist CSV is their order, grade, and date added. You do not reorder. You do not promise a seat. Sibling or parish flags appear only as they wrote them. This tool is the source for the waitlist pack. Openings they marked on the sheet stay as marked; you do not invent an opening. Order columns stay stable. Openings they marked are the only openings. Deposits as recorded, not guessed. Feeds the waitlist workflow. You do not call families from this sheet as the product.
