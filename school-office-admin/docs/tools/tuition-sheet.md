# Tuition sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The tuition workbook or billing export holds rates, invoices, discounts, and their payment codes. You copy amounts as written. You do not invent a rate and you do not collect. Aid columns come from their aid file if joined. This tool is the source for the tuition-from-file pack. No card numbers belong here. Term, month, and incidental fees stay in their columns. No PAN. Aid is only what they already joined. Exceptions — blank rate, hold, NSF as their code — go to the tuition pack.
