# Pickup list file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The pickup list is names authorized at the door, bus, or aftercare. You compare notes and early-dismissal rows to this file. You do not add a name. You do not release a child. Min necessary on printed door sheets. This tool is the source for the pickup-list workflow. Custody mismatches stay flags. Aftercare and bus variants stay on the sheet they named. You do not add rows. Early-dismissal notes compare here. Min necessary if the CSV will be printed for the door.
