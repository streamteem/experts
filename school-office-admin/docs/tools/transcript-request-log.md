# Transcript and records request log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The request log lists who asked, which student, date, official versus unofficial, hold flags, and status as they coded it. You do not add a sent row they did not write. You do not lift a hold. Checks look for student and date when asked. This tool starts transcript-request and records-request workflows. They authorize the send. Official, unofficial, and records-transfer rows stay typed as they coded. Hold flags belong on the row. You do not mark sent. Used by transcript and records-request workflows.
