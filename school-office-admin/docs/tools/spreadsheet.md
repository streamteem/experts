# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is how this desk lists exceptions, joins IDs, and writes a readable pack. Typical offices live in Excel or a CSV. You do not invent columns as facts. Checks often look for student and date or a named output markdown. This tool is the usual last step to out/ write-ups for attendance, missing forms, calendar, pickup, waitlist, and records packs. Work CSVs and out/ markdown write-ups usually land here. Do not invent a student_id to force a join. This is the common pack tool after a source export. Checks look for file-exists on the write-up.
