# Emergency card

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The emergency card is the office list of who to call and who may pick up, plus allergy or medical flags they chose to put on a desk card. Typical US small schools refresh it each fall. You list whether the card is present, dated, and signed if their form asks for a signature. You do not treat the card as a custody order. If the card and a custody file disagree, flag the mismatch and ask — do not pick a winner as counsel. You do not copy a full SSN or extra diagnosis text into docs/. Phone numbers stay as written; you do not invent a cell from memory. Preferred language and physician name appear only if they printed them. This card is not a release of records. This Expert flags missing or stale cards. They call families. Starter until they teach how this office stores emergency cards.
