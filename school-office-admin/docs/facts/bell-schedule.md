# Bell schedule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A bell schedule is the day's period times: start, passing, lunch waves, and dismissal. Typical US small schools keep a regular, late-start, early-out, and assembly version. You copy the version they marked for the date. You do not invent passing minutes or a lunch wave. Homeroom versus first period stays as they labeled it. If the calendar says early dismissal and the bell file still shows a full day, flag the mismatch and ask. Middle and high schools may have rotating or block days; the letter or color on their file is the source, not a remembered A/B pattern from another school. Elementary offices may only have start, recess, lunch, and dismissal. You do not rewrite the teacher duty roster from a guessed bell. Attendance windows and tardy cutoffs follow the schedule they stored. This Expert lists which schedule applies to which date. They announce changes. Starter until they teach how this office names schedule versions.
