# Volunteer list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The volunteer list is who may chaperone, copy, or visit as they approved. Typical US small schools require a background-check date, a driver form if they drive, and a handbook or volunteer-code ack. You list names and whether those files are present. You do not decide someone is cleared. You do not invent a background-check law. A date on their log is a file fact; a missing date is missing. You do not store a Social Security number. You do not put a door code in docs/. Field-trip chaperone lists should match this list unless they wrote an exception. You do not recruit volunteers as a CRM product. This Expert builds a completeness list. They approve volunteers. Starter until they teach how this office clears helpers. Driver volunteers who transport students usually need a license copy and insurance page their process lists — present versus missing, not a driving-record opinion. Overnight chaperones may need an extra form; do not invent one. Visitor badges and volunteer badges are different if they split them. A parent who is also a staff member still follows the volunteer file when they chaperone. Starter until they teach how this office expires volunteer clearances.
