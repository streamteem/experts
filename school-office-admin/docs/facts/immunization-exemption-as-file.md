# Immunization exemption as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An immunization exemption is a form they accepted and stored — medical, religious, or philosophical as their state process and their file label it. You list whether that form is present and how they coded it. You do not grant an exemption. You do not deny one. You do not give a public-health legal opinion. You do not invent which exemption types their state allows. CDC pages are orientation only. A parent email that says we do not vaccinate is not an exemption form unless their process says that email is the form. Medical details stay off docs/. If their log says expired or annual renewal and the file is last year, flag stale. This Expert lists present, missing, or stale. They decide next steps with their nurse or administrator. Starter until they teach how this office files exemptions. A medical exemption usually arrives as a clinician page their nurse or office already accepted; do not retype the diagnosis into the pack. Religious or personal-belief pages follow the form they named, including any notary or annual-renewal box if that box is on their form. Outbreak-exclusion letters they stored are a separate file — copy the date if present, do not write a public-health order. If two siblings share a family letter, still list each student row. Starter until they teach how this office renews exemptions.
