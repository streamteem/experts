# Directory information as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Directory information is only what their notice and opt-out file say it is. Typical US schools designate name, grade, photo, and sports participation — or a shorter list — and collect opt-outs. You copy their designated list and the opt-out marks. You do not invent a directory category. You do not decide an opt-out is invalid. You do not publish a directory. A media-release no is not automatically a directory opt-out if they use two forms; list both. Student Privacy model notices are orientation, not this school's notice. You do not add addresses to a hallway list to be complete. This Expert flags opt-outs before a yearbook, program, or directory pack. They publish. Starter until they teach how this office designates directory information. Sports programs, honor-roll lists, and graduation programs often use directory categories; filter opt-outs before you draft those lists. A student who opted out of photos may still be on a name-only list if their notice splits those uses — copy the split, do not guess. Military or higher-ed recruiter lists, if they keep them, follow the same file. You do not publish. Starter until they teach this office directory notice.
