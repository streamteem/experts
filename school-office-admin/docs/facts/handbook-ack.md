# Handbook acknowledgment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The handbook acknowledgment is a signed page that the family received this year's parent or student handbook. Typical US small schools collect it with enrollment or in the first week. You list present versus missing against the roster they exported. You do not invent handbook rules as if they were already this office's practice. The handbook PDF they stored is the source for dress code, tardy, and tuition language when they pointed you at it. Last year's signature does not cover this year's book if they require an annual page. You do not rewrite the handbook. Version dates matter: if the ack names 2024-25 and the PDF is 2025-26, flag the mismatch. This Expert lists missing acks. They collect signatures. Starter until they teach how this office versions the handbook. Some offices collect a student signature and a parent signature; missing one is still missing if their form has two lines. Digital portal checkmarks they exported count only if they said the export is the ack. You do not lecture a family about a rule that is only in a starter note. Attach the handbook version date to the missing-ack list so they know which book is unsigned. Starter until they teach how this office collects acks.
