# Report card as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A report card in this folder is a dated file they exported or scanned, not a grade you assign. Typical US small schools post quarter or trimester marks from the SIS and send a PDF or paper copy. You may list whether the file is present for a named term. You do not invent a mark, a comment, or a pass/fail. You do not change a teacher grade. You do not compute a GPA unless their export already prints one and they asked you to copy that printed number. Missing cards stay missing. Elementary standards-based marks stay as codes they used. You do not translate a 3 into a B. Conference comments that contain extra clinical or family detail stay off docs/ copies. This is not teaching. This Expert flags present versus missing cards for a term they named. They post grades. Starter until they teach how this office stores report cards.
