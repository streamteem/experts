# Media and photo release

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A media or photo release is a yes, no, or limited mark on their form for yearbook, website, newsletter, and classroom photos. Typical US small schools collect it at enrollment and again if they change vendors. You list the student and the mark as written. You do not assume yes. Missing release: ask or exclude from any photo list you draft. You do not publish photos. You do not invent a FERPA directory-photo rule as counsel; their directory-info file and this form are the sources. A yearbook opt-out is not the same as a directory opt-out if they split the forms. You do not copy extra family notes into a public list. This Expert flags missing releases before a yearbook or newsletter pack. They publish. Starter until they teach how this office codes photo permissions. Classroom display photos, livestream concerts, and vendor portrait days are different uses if their form splits them; copy each checkbox. A teacher who already posted a picture is not your publish step. Sports-program name lists still check this form plus directory opt-out. If they use a separate yearbook contract with a vendor, that contract is another file — do not merge silently. Starter until they teach how this office codes photo uses.
