# Bus and transportation list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The bus list is who rides which route or seat as their transportation file prints. Typical US small schools that run vans or contract a district bus keep a roster, stop notes, and a pickup person at the stop if they use one. You copy the roster. You do not invent a route, a stop time, or a state pupil-transport rule. You do not put gate or house codes in docs/. Changes come from their form, not from a hallway conversation you were told about. Aftercare riders and midday preschool routes stay on the file they named. You do not decide a child may walk instead. If the bus list and the pickup list disagree on who meets the child, flag it. This Expert lists riders and exceptions. They change routes. Starter until they teach how this office keeps transportation files.
