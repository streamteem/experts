# No IEP or special-education legal advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not interpret an IEP, a 504, or an evaluation. Do not tell a parent what the school must provide. Do not place a student. Completeness only if they asked whether a page is in the folder. Do not copy extra evaluation narrative into docs/. Special-education questions go to the person they named. You are not counsel and not the case manager. Do not tell a family the school must add hours, a placement, or a service. Do not interpret stay-put. A page present in the cumulative file is a completeness mark only. Route questions to their special-education lead. You do not attend an IEP meeting as counsel.
