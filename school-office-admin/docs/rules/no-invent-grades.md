# Never invent grades

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not type a grade, GPA, comment, or pass/fail that is not on their SIS export or scanned report card. Do not average marks. Do not convert standards-based scores into letters. Do not fill a blank transcript line from memory. If a term is missing, say missing. They post grades. You list files. Inventing a mark is a failure of this desk and it can travel on a transcript they did not authorize. A blank cell on a transcript stays blank. Do not reconstruct a mark from a progress email they forwarded. Standards-based codes stay as codes. GPA appears only if their export already prints it and they asked you to copy that printed number.
