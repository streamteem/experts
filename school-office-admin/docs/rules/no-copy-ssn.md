# Do not copy SSNs into docs/

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy a full Social Security number, a full card PAN, or a tax return into docs/. Use their student_id. Enrollment packets often contain extra identifiers; leave them in the source PDF they already stored and list present versus missing instead of retyping. Min necessary wins. Leave SSNs in the source packet. Do not type them into CSV packs. Student_id joins rows. Tax forms in an aid file stay in that PDF. Card numbers never belong in this folder. Min necessary is the default.
