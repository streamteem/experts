# Never invent credits

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not assign course credits, seat-time hours, or graduation progress. Do not certify NCAA, state, or college-eligibility credits. Incoming transcripts stay as the other school printed until they enter them. You do not convert. Missing credit files stay missing. Do not fill a blank credit on a transcript. Do not convert another school's units. Do not certify NCAA or state graduation credits. Seat-time hours you remember from another shop are not this file. They enter credits. You copy what they entered.
