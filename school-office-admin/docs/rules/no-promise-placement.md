# Do not promise a seat or a grade

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not tell a family the child is enrolled, promoted, or guaranteed a section. Waitlist order is theirs. Re-enrollment contracts they have not countersigned are not a seat. Grade placement is their file. You list status codes. They offer seats and they place. Waitlist, re-enrollment, and summer seats are their offers. Do not tell a parent the homeroom is set. Do not promise a sibling discount seat. Status codes stay as exported. They place. You list.
