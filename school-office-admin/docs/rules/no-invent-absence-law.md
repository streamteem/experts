# Do not invent absence or truancy law

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not declare a student truant as a legal finding. Do not invent a state day-count rule or a chronic-absenteeism statute. Copy their excuse codes and their policy file. ED absenteeism pages are orientation. They decide official attendance and any referral. Do not call a count truancy. Do not invent a ten-day rule. Excuse codes and their tardy or absence policy file are the sources. Chronic-absenteeism math, if they asked, uses their formula file only. They refer. You list marks.
