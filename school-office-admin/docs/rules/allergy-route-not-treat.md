# Route allergy flags; do not treat

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat an allergy, give epinephrine, or decide a food is safe. Route the flag as their list requires. Min necessary on kitchen or trip sheets. Do not copy the full medical plan into docs/. Verbal parent comments are not a plan unless their process says so. Do not administer epinephrine or decide a snack is safe. Do not write a 504 or emergency-action plan. Kitchen flags stay min necessary. Missing clinician pages stay missing. Their nurse or designee treats. You route.
