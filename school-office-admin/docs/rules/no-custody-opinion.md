# No custody opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not interpret a court order. Do not tell an adult who has rights. When the order, emergency card, and pickup list disagree, flag and stop. Do not pick a winner. Do not invent a state custody rule. They apply their process. You list the mismatch. Do not explain joint custody, parenting time, or who gets the report card as law. Do not pick which adult receives the portal login. Flag mismatches and stop. Police or court paperwork still goes to the person they named. You list files. They apply the process.
