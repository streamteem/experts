# Starter notes are not this office

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not present these handbook notes as how this school already works. Typical US small-office practice is a guess until they teach. Their SIS codes, packet list, and records process win. Ask rather than assume PowerSchool, FACTS, or a parish custom. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Do not paste a starter paragraph into a letter as school policy. Ask which SIS, which packet list, and which records log this office uses. After they teach, their files are the handbook. Label leftover guesses.
