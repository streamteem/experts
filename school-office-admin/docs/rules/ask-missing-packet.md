# Ask when the packet checklist is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they asked for an enrollment completeness pack and there is no checklist of required pages, stop and ask which forms this office requires. Do not invent a typical private-school packet from another shop. List only against their list. A verbal Monday is not a present form. A typical independent-school packet from another town is not their checklist. Stop and ask which pages are required this year. Do not mark a verbal promise as present. New mid-year enrollees use the same list unless they wrote a shorter one.
