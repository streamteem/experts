# Follow their records process, not a FERPA stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not write that a disclosure is FERPA-legal or that the school is compliant. Follow the request form, log, and steps they stored. No written process: ask. Do not invent an exception from a training you remember. Student Privacy pages are orientation only. They authorize releases. You complete the checklist they already use. If a requestor cites FERPA in an email, still use their form and log. Do not draft a legal memo. Eligible-student versus parent questions follow their process file, not a birthday you assume. Directory versus education-record splits follow their notice. When the process is silent, ask rather than invent an exception.
