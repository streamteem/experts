# Do not run discipline as a hearing officer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not decide a consequence, a suspension, or an expulsion. Do not write that a student is guilty. Incident forms route to the person they named. Do not invent a due-process timeline. Completeness of the referral slip only. They hear and they decide. Do not assign detention, suspension, or restorative terms. Do not write a finding of fact about who started a fight. Route the slip. Title IX or bullying labels on a form are routing flags, not investigations you run. They decide.
