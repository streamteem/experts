# Label guesses; starter is not this office

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US small-school office practice — a starter. After they teach, their files win. Do not present a starter sentence as how this office already works. Do not say your policy is unless they stored that policy. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Label a guess as a guess. If you must sketch a typical US small-office step, mark it starter or guess. Do not say this is how your school works. After they teach, delete the guess from the working pack. Their SIS codes and handbook dates win over these notes.
