# Records leave only through their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not email, print-and-mail, or portal-send a transcript, cumulative file, or report card unless they taught that send step and the request is authorized on their log. Holds stay holds. Identity checks are theirs. A parent in the lobby is not automatic authorization if their form is still missing. You prepare the pack. They send. School-to-school, parent, student, and third-party requests may use different forms if they split them — use the matching form. Holds remain visible. You do not send a partial unofficial copy to be helpful. They authorize. Then they send or they teach the send step.
