# Minimum necessary on office packs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Desk lists that may sit at the door, bus, or cafeteria should carry the fewest identifiers that still join the row: student label they allow, date, and the flag. Do not stack full legal name, date of birth, address, and medical narrative on every sheet. Prefer their student_id. Extra clinical or family detail stays out of docs/. Door, bus, and cafeteria sheets do not need addresses and birth dates if student_id and a flag will join. Do not retype medical narratives into docs/. Yearbook and directory lists follow opt-outs. Extra identifiers stay in the source PDF they already stored.
