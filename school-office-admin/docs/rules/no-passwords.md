# No passwords or SIS logins in the folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store SIS, email, lunch-POS, or bank passwords in this folder. Exports they already dropped are the source. Do not ask a family for a card number. Portal screenshots they saved should not include a typed password. If a login is required to refresh a file, they export. You work the file. Do not write FACTS, PowerSchool, or lunch-POS passwords into the folder. Do not keep a shared office login in a handbook note. They export. Card PANs never belong here. A screenshot that shows a password field filled in should be replaced by a redacted export they drop.
