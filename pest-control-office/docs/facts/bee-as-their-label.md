# Bee as their label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bee, wasp, or hornet is a ticket label they already used, sometimes with a height or location note they wrote. Typical US pest offices treat this as specialty or add-on work. Copy their label. Do not diagnose a species. Do not invent a foam or dust mix. Do not give a wildlife or pollinator legal opinion. If they do not service bees, say so from their menu file; do not invent a hive removal. Photos they named help the estimate from the price file after they want a number. Access and ladder notes stay as they wrote them, not a safety stamp. A nest height they left blank stays blank. You list the ticket and the price-file line if present. They treat or they refer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits bees from general pest and who may take a rooftop stop.
