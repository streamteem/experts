# License number on file present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

License number on file is a present-or-missing check: their PDF, card photo, or roster field already shows a number and a date they recorded. Typical US pest offices keep commercial applicator and business licenses in a binder. You may list present, expired-as-they-dated, or missing. You do not stamp a license. You do not say a tech is “certified to apply” as this product. EPA certification pages are orientation only. A landscaping applicator flag is not this file. CEU dates are a separate file if they split them. Do not invent a license number. A card they marked expired stays expired on the pack. Business and applicator cards stay the rows they used. You show the hole. They renew. Starter until they teach which *this* shop licenses live on the roster versus a PDF folder and who may run a route when the card is missing.
