# Crawl space as a site note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Crawl is a location flag or ticket note for work under the house: access door they mentioned, moisture note they already wrote, and termite or rodent labels they used. Typical US termite and exclusion jobs live here. Copy their words. Do not diagnose moisture, fungus, or structure. Do not invent a vapor-barrier spec. Access asks stay visible; no codes in docs/. WDO graphs and moisture notes, if present, stay attached as their files. Landscaping drainage engineering is out of scope. A flooded crawl they already noted is a skip or a hold they call, not a cancel you issue from weather memory. Pet flags still apply under the house. You list the crawl flag and missing access. They enter and they write findings. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes crawl treatments versus a walk-around exterior and who may skip a flooded crawl.
