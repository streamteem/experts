# Inspection graph

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An inspection graph is their marked diagram or checklist sketch from a visit that is not necessarily a full WDO package: rodent signs they marked, harborages they labeled in their words, or a commercial walk. Typical US shops attach it to the ticket or drop a photo of a clipboard. Copy presence and their labels. Do not add findings. Do not diagnose the structure. Device maps and termite graphs may be separate files; keep the names they used. If the graph has no date or account, ask. Photos named to the graph help completeness; they are not a species ID you write. A blank graph on a sold inspection stays a hole, not a chance to draw marks from a photo memory. You index the file. They inspect. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits a quick graph versus a WDO graph and whether the handheld drawing is the record of record.
