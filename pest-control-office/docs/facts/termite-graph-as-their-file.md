# Termite graph as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A termite graph is their diagram of the structure as they already drew it: walls, porches, and marks they placed for evidence or treatment areas. Typical US termite desks attach the graph to the WDO form or the treatment ticket. Copy their file name and whether the graph is present. Do not redraw the building. Do not add damage marks or treatment zones you invented. Do not diagnose subterranean versus drywood as a finding; keep the words and boxes they used. If the graph and the form address disagree, ask. Photos they named may support the graph; photos are not a new diagnosis. Pre-treat graphs for new construction are a different file if they split them. This is not a landscape site map. You index the graph. They draw and they treat. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names graphs and whether a handheld sketch counts as the file.
