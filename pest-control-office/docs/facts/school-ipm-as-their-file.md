# School IPM as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

School IPM is the plan, log, or notification packet they already stored for a school account: product list they approved, thresholds they wrote, and forms they use. Typical US districts require this packet before or after a visit. Copy presence and the words they filed. You do not write an IPM plan. You do not invent a product list. You do not say the school is “in compliance.” EPA school pages are orientation, not a stamp. If the account is a school and the IPM file is missing, ask. Chemical-log rows for that site should match products on their IPM list when they require a match; mismatches stay questions, not silent edits. You check completeness. They and the school own the plan. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files IPM versus the commercial logbook and who may add a product to the packet.
