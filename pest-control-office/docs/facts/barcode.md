# Barcode on a device

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A barcode or tag number is the id they already stuck on a station, trap, or logbook page. Typical US shops print codes that match the bait-station map. Copy the id as written. Do not invent a barcode to make a new station look official. Do not reuse another account’s code. If the scan export and the map use different ids, quote both and ask. This is not a landscaping plant tag. Inventory chemical barcodes, if they use them, are a different file; do not mix station tags with product SKUs unless they said the same system holds both. A replaced station keeps the new id they wrote, not the old one you remember. Missing tags stay missing. You list ids. They print and they stick. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers tags after a station is replaced.
