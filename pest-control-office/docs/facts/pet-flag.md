# Pet flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pet flag is their note that an account has a pet that matters to the stop: dogs, cats, birds, or “pets inside” as they wrote it. Typical US pest offices put a column on the route because indoor sprays, baits, and reentry notes change how they run the visit. Copy the flag. Ask if a tech note mentioned a pet and the route has no flag. Do not invent a species or a temperament. Do not write a veterinary or toxicity legal opinion. Reentry as their label still comes from their label file, not from a pet you guessed. Skip lists may already hold the stop until pets are secured; the skip wins. You copy the flag. They decide to skip or to require the customer to secure animals. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks pets versus a separate dog-bite flag and who calls the customer.
