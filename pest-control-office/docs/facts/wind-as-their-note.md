# Wind as their note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wind is a field-condition note they already wrote on an exterior spray ticket or chemical log: a word, a number they recorded, or “held for wind.” Typical US pest offices hold perimeter sprays when the tech or owner calls it. You do not cancel a route from a weather app. You do not invent a wind speed so the log looks complete. After they write a hold, list leftover exterior stops. Interior and device-only stops may still run if they said so; do not assume. This is not a landscaping rain-day mow board. Lightning or heat holds they wrote stay their holds, not an OSHA legal opinion. A held spray does not get a fake log row. You list their note. They decide the truck stays in. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records wind and who may release a held spray.
