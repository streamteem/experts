# Trap log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A trap log is their record of mechanical devices: trap id or location, date checked, catch as they coded it, and reset or missing. Typical US rodent and wildlife desks keep this next to the bait-station map. Copy their codes. Do not invent a catch count to make the week look busy. Do not diagnose a species from a photo. Device count should reconcile to the map; mismatches stay questions. Photos they named belong with the row. Pet flags still apply. This is not a chemical log unless they also logged a pesticide. Empty rows stay empty; do not write a catch so the commercial logbook looks active. Wildlife-legal questions stay theirs. You tabulate the log. They check traps. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes empty versus catch versus damaged and whether a scan export is the log.
