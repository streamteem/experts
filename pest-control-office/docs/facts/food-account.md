# Food account

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A food account is a site they coded as food handling: restaurant, cafeteria, grocery, or commissary as they wrote it. Typical US pest offices keep extra files here: commercial logbook pages, device maps, and sometimes a customer IPM spec. FDA Food Code pages are orientation for why the paperwork exists, not an inspector stamp you issue. Copy their account type and required files. Do not invent a logbook page. Do not diagnose a contamination event. Do not invent a mix because a health inspection is “soon.” Invoice lines still come from closed tickets. Interior kitchen versus dock exterior stays as they coded it. Device maps stay attached when they stored them. You list present-or-missing logbook, map, and ticket. They service the account. Starter until they teach which *this* shop food sites need a signed logbook every visit and who keeps the book on site.
