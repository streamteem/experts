# Pre-treat

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pre-treat is a new-construction termite ticket they sold: lot, builder, stage they named (soil, slab, final), and the product file they already attached. Typical US termite desks schedule these against a builder calendar, not a monthly residential route. Copy their stage and lot. Do not invent a mix, rate, or EPA number. Do not diagnose soil or structure. Graphs and chemical-log rows belong if they stored them. Weather or pour delays they wrote stay their delays; you do not cancel from radar. This is not a landscaping start-up week. Builder PO missing when their rule requires one stays a hold, not a guessed PO number. Invoice lines follow the closed pre-treat ticket and their price file. You pack the lot list. They treat. Starter until they teach how *this* shop stages soil versus final and who calls the superintendent.
