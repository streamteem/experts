# Warranty file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pest warranties are only what their contract, proposal fine print, or warranty PDF says: months, retreats included, exclusions, and what “activity” means as they wrote it. Typical US general-pest and termite shops print a short warranty; many one-time jobs print none. Copy their file. Do not promise eradication, a pest-free house, or that termites will not return. Do not invent a one-year retreat to close a callback. Termite bonds, if they sell them, are a separate file if they split them — still their words, not your bond opinion. Commercial specs may point to a different warranty than the residential menu; quote the file that matches the account. You list the written terms. They decide a retreat. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates a warranty clock and who signs a courtesy return. Replacement of a bait station is not a plant-survival warranty; keep pest language only.
