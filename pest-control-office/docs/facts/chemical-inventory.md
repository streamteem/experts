# Chemical inventory

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Chemical inventory is their on-hand file of pest products: product name as they key it, quantity, unit, and sometimes lot or expiration they already recorded. Typical US shops count the locker or the truck kit. Copy on-hand from their count or export. Never invent a quantity. Never invent an EPA number to fill a blank; that comes from the label file. Mix cards point at inventory names they already use. This is not a landscaping mulch yard list. Reorder flags follow mins they wrote, not a guessed par. Expired lots they dated stay visible; do not hide them so the locker looks clean. You do not invent a mix from a leftover jug. You list counts and holes. They buy. You do not pay the vendor. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts the shop versus the truck and who may transfer product between trucks.
