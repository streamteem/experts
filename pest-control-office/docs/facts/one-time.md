# One-time service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

One-time work lives on a ticket that ends: a spot treatment, a real-estate WDO, a bed-bug job, or an exclusion they sold once. Typical US shops blur this when a “one-time” quietly repeats. Use their coding. Do not put one-time hours on the monthly truck without a ticket. Do not invent a recurring line to make revenue look stable. If the customer asks to repeat, that is a new sold item they must accept. Chemical logs and photos still attach when they stored them. Warranty language, if any, comes from their file — often none on one-time. Follow-ups they already sold on a one-time stay follow-ups, not a silent monthly. You keep the buckets separate. They decide to convert a one-time to a route. Starter until they teach how *this* shop flags a repeating extra so it does not live forever on the specialty tech.
