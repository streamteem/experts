# Skip list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The skip list is accounts or dates they already marked do-not-service: vacation holds, non-pay holds they coded, construction, no-access, or a customer pause. Typical US pest route shops keep skips on the account row or a short sheet the dispatcher reads every morning. The skip list wins over the printed route. Do not send a tech to a skipped stop to fill the day. Do not invent a skip to hide a missed account. When the skip ends, their date or note ends it — not a guess that two weeks is enough. Billing during a skip follows their contract file, not a courtesy credit you invent. Wind holds are a different list unless they merged them; keep the labels they used. You apply skips to the day pack. They add or lift a skip. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes vacation versus collections versus no-access holds.
