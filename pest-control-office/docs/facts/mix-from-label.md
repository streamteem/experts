# Mix from their label or mix card

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A mix is only what their label file or written mix card already says: product, water, and rate as they wrote it. Typical US pest shops keep a short card per product or a page in the label binder. Copy that card. Do not invent a tank mix. Do not combine two products because you remember a “common pair.” Do not improve a dilution from an EPA page you read; those pages are orientation. If the ticket says treat and no mix card or log rate exists, ask. This is the same bar as no applicator stamp and no invented EPA number. Landscaping fertilizer blends are not this card. A superseded card they marked void stays void. You list the written mix or the hole. They mix. Starter until they teach how *this* shop versions mix cards and who initials a change.
