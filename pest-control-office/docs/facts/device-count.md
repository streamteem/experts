# Device count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Device count is how many bait stations, traps, monitors, or ILTs they already listed on the map, ticket, or scan export. Typical US commercial rodent programs live on this number. Copy the count from their file. Do not invent a station to match a contract memory. If the map says twelve and the scan says eleven, quote both and ask. Missing devices they coded as damaged or missing stay visible; do not hide a hole so the account looks serviced. Barcode or scan files, if they use them, are the count source they named. Interior versus exterior splits stay in the columns they use; do not merge them to make one pretty total. This is not a landscaping plant count. You tabulate. They place and they replace. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts interior versus exterior devices and what “serviced” means on a scan.
