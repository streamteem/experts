# Bed bug as their label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bed bug is a ticket label they already used, often with a unit number, a follow-up date they wrote, and a heat-treat flag if they sell heat. Typical US shops run a different process than monthly general pest: inspection graph, prep sheet they already use, and sometimes a hotel or apartment packet. Copy their label and their process files. Do not diagnose. Do not invent a spray mix or an EPA number. CDC bed-bug pages are orientation only. Do not promise eradication. Follow-up tickets they opened stay visible. This is not a landscaping ticket. Adjacent units they did not sell stay off the pack unless they wrote a neighbor ticket. You pack the files they stored. They inspect and they treat. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers units on a multi-family account and whether heat and chemical are separate tickets.
