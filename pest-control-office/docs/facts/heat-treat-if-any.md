# Heat treat if they sell it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Heat treat is a ticket and equipment note only if they already sell it: unit, date, target label they wrote (often bed bug), and a prep checklist they stored. If they have no heat file, say they have no heat desk in this folder — do not invent a heat job. Copy their checklist completeness. Do not stamp that heat “killed everything.” Do not invent a temperature log. If they also logged a chemical, that log still must not get an invented mix. Equipment-down they marked stays visible. Missing prep items they require stay holes; do not mark the job ready. Do not promise eradication after a heat visit they closed. Equipment they marked down stays down. You list the ticket and missing prep items. They run the equipment. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even owns heat and how they name room-by-room photos.
