# Wildlife as their label, not legal advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wildlife is a ticket label they already used: raccoon, squirrel, bird, bat, or “wildlife” as written. Typical US pest shops that also run wildlife keep a separate ticket and often an exclusion scope. Copy their label. You do not give wildlife legal advice, do not say a species is legal to trap, and do not invent a permit. USDA APHIS pages are orientation only. Exclusion tickets, attic notes, and one-way door products they named stay as their files. If they have no wildlife desk in this folder, say none — do not invent a wildlife route. Photos they named are not a species stamp you issue. You list the ticket and questions. They decide the work and any permit they already know. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even runs wildlife and who holds any permit they care about.
