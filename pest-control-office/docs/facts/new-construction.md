# New construction

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

New construction is an account type or ticket family for builder lots, not a finished house on the monthly route. Typical US pest offices track lot number, community, builder PO, and stage. Copy their keys. Do not invent a lot number or a PO. Do not treat a model home as a residential monthly stop unless they coded it that way. Pre-treat tickets, final-grade treatments, and warranty starts they dated stay separate if they split them. Access on an active job site is an ask; no gate codes in docs/. A cancelled lot they marked stays off the pour-week list. Chemical logs still may not get an invented mix. You list lots and missing tickets. They schedule around pours. Starter until they teach how *this* shop keys a lot versus a street address and when the account converts to a homeowner warranty.
