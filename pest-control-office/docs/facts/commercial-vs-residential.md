# Commercial versus residential

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Commercial and residential pest stops use different paperwork even when the same tech hits both. Typical US shops invoice houses on a recurring route and invoice commercial sites against a contract, a monthly lump, a logbook, or a customer PO. Keep the type they coded. Do not apply a residential callback text process to a site that requires a signed commercial logbook or a school IPM packet. Time windows, device maps, and food-account rules often differ; copy the file for that type. A homeowners association can behave like commercial even when the lots look like houses; use their coding. You do not invent a PO or a lump-sum price. You do not treat this as a landscaping commercial campus. You label the pack. They bill. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits commercial techs versus neighborhood routes and which accounts always need a logbook page.
