# Rate as their log, not invented

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Application rate is the dilution, ounces per gallon, ounces per thousand square feet, or bait placement amount they already wrote on the log, label file, or mix sheet. Typical US pest shops copy rate from the label they keep on file or from a written mix card the owner approved. Use their number and unit. Do not invent a rate because “that’s the usual tank.” Do not convert units to look neater unless they asked for math on numbers they already listed. Missing rate on a spray ticket is a completeness hole, not a chance to prescribe. This is the same bar as no invented mix and no applicator stamp. Landscaping fertilizer pounds per thousand is not this field. You list the logged rate or you ask. They mix and they apply. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes rate on the handheld versus the paper log and who may change a mix card.
