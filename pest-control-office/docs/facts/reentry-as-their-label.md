# Reentry as their label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reentry is the wait they already wrote from the product label file or the ticket note: a time, a “when dry,” or a printed REI they copied onto the log. Typical US indoor and school jobs need this on the pack so the office does not invent a number. Copy their words. Do not invent a reentry interval. Do not turn a pet or child flag into a homemade wait. EPA label pages are orientation, not a number you pull from memory. If they applied and the log has no reentry note while their process requires one, ask. Interior versus exterior may carry different written waits on their card; keep that split as written. You list the written interval. They post and they tell the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes reentry on commercial logbooks versus residential tickets.
