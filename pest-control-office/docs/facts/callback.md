# Callback

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A callback is a return visit they opened because activity, a miss, or a customer complaint was logged — not a new sold one-time unless they recoded it. Typical US pest offices track “still seeing ants,” a missed room, or a bait station the customer says failed. List the original ticket, the complaint words they recorded, and photos if present. Do not diagnose why the pest is still there. Do not promise eradication this time. Warranty versus courtesy versus billable return follows their warranty file. Hours on a callback come from their sheet, not invented time to soothe the account. Skip, pet, and access flags still apply. You list open callbacks. They schedule and they talk to the customer. This is not a landscaping quality callback about clippings. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes courtesy versus warranty returns and how they close a callback ticket.
