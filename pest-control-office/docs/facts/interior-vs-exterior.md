# Interior versus exterior

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Interior versus exterior is how they coded where the service happened or where devices sit: inside, outside, both, or a finer split they already use (kitchen, dock, perimeter). Typical US pest tickets and chemical logs keep this split because labels, reentry notes, and commercial logbooks treat indoor and outdoor work differently. Copy their code. Do not assume a “general pest” stop was exterior only. Do not invent an interior treatment so a school or food account looks complete. Wind notes they wrote matter more on exterior sprays; still you do not invent a wind reading. Reentry as their label applies when they logged an indoor application. Landscaping “front versus back yard” is not this split. If ticket and log disagree on interior versus exterior, quote both and ask. You label the pack. They choose where to work. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes crawl, attic, and perimeter versus true interior rooms.
