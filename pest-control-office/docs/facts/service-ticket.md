# Service ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A service ticket is the office unit for one pest-control visit: account, address as they file it, service type, date or route day, tech if assigned, and the target-pest label they already wrote. Typical US structural pest shops print tickets from a route app, a workbook, or a paper pad the tech photographs at the shop. Recurring general-pest stops, one-time treatments, callbacks, WDO inspections, and skips are different ticket kinds; do not collapse them because the street looks the same. Ticket number, account name, and site stay copied, not cleaned into a prettier code. This is not a landscaping job ticket and not a mow stop. A ticket is not an invoice, not a chemical log, and not an applicator license. Hours, products, and device counts belong on the ticket only when their sheet or tech note already has them. Missing tickets stay missing: ask rather than invent a stop so the day list looks full. Extra work the tech saw on site is not a new ticket until they authorize it. This Expert lists tickets they stored. They dispatch and they close. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers tickets and what “done” means on a stop.
