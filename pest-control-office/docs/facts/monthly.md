# Monthly service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Monthly is a frequency they assigned, common on commercial food, rodent, and some residential accounts. Copy the cycle from the account row. Do not invent a monthly stop because the site “looks commercial.” Do not drop a monthly account from the route to make room for one-time work. Device scans and logbook pages, if their process requires them every month, stay completeness checks. A landscaping weekly mow is not a pest monthly. If last-service and next-due disagree with “monthly,” quote both and ask. Five-week months still use their next-due, not a guessed fifth visit. Skips they wrote still win over a printed monthly. You place monthly stops on the route day they already use. They sell the frequency. Starter until they teach how *this* shop handles a month with five weeks and who may skip a monthly without a skip row.
