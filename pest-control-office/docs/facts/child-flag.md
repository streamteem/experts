# Child flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A child flag is their note that children are expected on site: a house with kids, a daycare, or a play area they marked. Typical US pest offices keep this next to pet and school flags because product choice and timing are their licensed person’s call, not yours. Copy the flag. Do not invent a child-safe mix. Do not recommend a product. School and daycare accounts may also have an IPM file; that file stays attached. Reentry as their label is still copied, not invented. You do not write a pediatric or legal opinion. If a ticket mentions kids and the account has no flag, ask. Time windows they already wrote stay on the day list. You list the flag. They schedule and they apply. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes daycare versus residential kids and whether a time window is required.
