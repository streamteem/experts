# Moisture as their note, not a diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Moisture notes are words or meter readings they already wrote on a ticket, WDO form, or crawl note: “damp corner,” a number they recorded, or a photo they named. Typical US termite and crawl jobs collect these as observations, not as a building-science report. Copy their note. Do not diagnose a leak, a grade problem, or a fungus as the cause. Do not stamp the structure. Do not invent a moisture reading so the form looks complete. Drainage and landscaping grade questions are out of this desk; you do not size a drain. If the customer asked “why is it wet,” that stays a question for them, not a finding you write. You list the note and whether a photo exists. They decide next work. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records meter readings versus plain words and who may open an exclusion or plumbing referral.
