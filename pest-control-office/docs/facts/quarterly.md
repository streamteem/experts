# Quarterly service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quarterly is a frequency they assigned: four visits a year, often with a next-due they printed. Typical US residential and light-commercial pest books use quarterly as the default sold plan. Copy the cycle and the next-due from their sheet. Do not move a stop to monthly to fill a tech day. Do not invent a quarter-end date from a calendar memory. Skips do not silently convert quarterly to “whenever.” Chemical logs for quarterly stops still need their completeness fields when a product was applied. Landscaping seasonal quarters are not this field. A sold quarterly that their sheet shows last serviced six months ago is a due question, not a silent extra stop you add. You list who is due this quarter from their file. They change the plan. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks Q1–Q4 versus a rolling ninety days and who may pull a visit early.
