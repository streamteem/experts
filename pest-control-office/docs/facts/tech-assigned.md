# Tech assigned

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tech assigned is who their roster or ticket puts on a stop: name or employee id, sometimes a license-number-on-file flag they already recorded. Typical US pest offices keep a short sheet, a whiteboard photo, or an export from the same system that prints routes. Do not invent a person or a nickname. Do not move someone onto a route because the day looks short. A license or applicator flag on the roster is a label they wrote, not a stamp you issue and not permission for this Expert to recommend a chemical. Helpers and trainees they listed stay labeled as they coded them. Call-outs come from their note, not from a guess that someone “usually” stays home Mondays. A roster is not a timesheet and not a payroll file. You assemble who is listed. They assign and they pay. If the folder has no roster, say so and ask. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names techs and who may run a restricted-use product as their file already says.
