# Bait as their labeled product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bait is a product or placement they already named: station bait, gel, or granule as written on the ticket, inventory, or chemical log. Typical US pest shops treat bait as a logged product with an EPA number from their label file when it is a pesticide. Copy the name they used. Do not invent a bait product, a placement amount, or an EPA number. Do not recommend a bait because “mice like it.” Trap-only accounts stay trap-only unless they recoded them. Pet and child flags matter on the account; you copy the flags, you do not write a toxicity opinion. Food accounts may forbid some products on their IPM or contract file; quote that file. You list what they logged. They place bait. Starter until they teach how *this* shop keys bait SKUs to the inventory sheet and who may switch a product.
