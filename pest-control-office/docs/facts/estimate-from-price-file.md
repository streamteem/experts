# Estimate from their price file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An estimate draft is line items priced from the price file, menu, or dated quote they stored — not from a neighbor’s job or a number in your head. Typical US pest shops keep a unit-price book: monthly general pest, quarterly, one-time, WDO inspection, exclusion by opening as they already published, heat-treat if they sell it. Copy unit, quantity they listed, and unit price as written. Extended amounts are math on those inputs, not a new price. If quantity is missing, ask; do not pace the house from a satellite guess. If the price file is silent on a line, ask. Do not invent a chemical or a mix as a priced line you recommended. A draft is not a signed contract and not an invoice. They send the proposal. Starter until they teach how *this* shop measures and which book is live.
