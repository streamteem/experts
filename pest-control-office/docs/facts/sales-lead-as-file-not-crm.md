# Sales lead as a file, not a CRM product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A sales lead is a file they dropped: a web form export, a callback sheet, or a photo of a notepad. Typical US pest offices want an estimate from the price file next. You list the lead and missing fields they use (address, target label, commercial versus residential). You do not run a CRM or email/SMS product. You do not invent a target pest so the estimate looks complete. You do not promise eradication in a draft. Price comes from their price file after they want a number. This is not a landscaping enhancement lead. A lead without an address stays a hole, not a guessed house. Pet and child flags they already wrote stay on the pack. They call and they sell. You pack questions. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers leads versus tickets and when a lead becomes an account.
