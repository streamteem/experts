# Device scan

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A device scan is their barcode or NFC export from stations they already tagged: station id, time, and sometimes a service code they chose on the wand. Typical US commercial rodent programs use this as the proof the tech walked the map. Copy the export. Do not invent a scan to hide a missed station. Device count is a reconcile against the bait-station map; mismatches stay questions. If they have no scan file, say none — do not invent barcodes. Photos are not a scan. A failed tag they already noted stays a miss, not a guessed service. Interior versus exterior counts stay split if they split them. Do not invent an EPA number on a bait scan. You tabulate misses. They reservice. Starter until they teach how *this* shop treats a failed tag and whether a paper map still wins when the wand dies.
