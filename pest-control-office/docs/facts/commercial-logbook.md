# Commercial logbook

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A commercial logbook is the on-site or scanned book they already keep for a commercial account: date, tech, target label, devices, and product rows as their form prints them. Typical US food and warehouse accounts expect a page each visit. You list whether this visit’s page is present in the folder or marked complete on their export. Do not invent a page. Do not invent a mix or EPA number to finish a page. Chemical-log completeness and the logbook may be the same rows or two files; keep their split. School IPM packets are a different file if they stored both. A photo of last month’s page is not this visit’s page. You check completeness. They write the page. Starter until they teach how *this* shop photographs the book versus a handheld signature and who leaves a copy on site.
