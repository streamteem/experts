# Follow-up visit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A follow-up is a planned return they already put on the ticket or calendar: bed-bug second visit, termite post-treat, or a “check stations in two weeks” they wrote. Typical US pest offices treat follow-ups as part of the sold job, not as a complaint callback, unless they recoded them. Copy the date and the reason they stored. Do not invent a follow-up to look thorough. Do not invent a mix for the return. Warranty follow-ups still quote the warranty file. Skip and access flags still apply. A missed follow-up date stays a hole on the calendar pack, not a silent skip. Pet, child, dog, and access flags they wrote still apply on the return visit. You list due follow-ups from their calendar and tickets. They schedule. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes follow-up versus callback versus the next monthly.
