# Bait-station map as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A bait-station map is their sketch, site plan, or numbered list of devices: station id, location words they wrote, interior versus exterior, and sometimes a barcode. Typical US rodent and commercial accounts keep a PDF or a photo of a floor plan with dots. Copy their numbers and labels. Do not invent a station to make the count look even. Do not redraw the building as a structural diagnosis. Device count on the ticket should match the map they stored; if not, quote both and ask. Missing maps on an account that their process requires stay missing. This is not a landscape bed map and not an irrigation zone sketch. Photos of stations they named belong with the map. You index the file and the count. They place and they service devices. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers stations and whether a barcode scan replaces a paper map.
