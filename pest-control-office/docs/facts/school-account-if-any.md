# School account if they have one

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A school account is a site they coded as a school, daycare, or district building. If they have no school file, say they have no school desk in this folder — do not invent a campus route. Typical US shops that service schools keep time windows, an IPM file, and sometimes a notification process they already wrote. Copy those files. EPA school-IPM pages are orientation only. Do not invent a product or a mix for a classroom. Do not stamp IPM compliance. Child flags and reentry-as-their-label still apply. Access and holiday calendars they stored stay on the day list; no codes in docs/. A weekend or after-bell window they wrote beats a weekday memory. You pack the school stops they listed. They schedule around the bell. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even takes schools and which buildings share one ticket.
