# WDO as their form, not an inspector stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A wood-destroying organism (WDO) or wood-destroying insect report is completeness on the form they already use: property, date, graph present or missing, findings boxes they checked, and signatures they require. Typical US pest offices fill a state or company form for real-estate or annual termite inspections. You list whether required fields and attachments are present. You do not stamp the inspection, do not diagnose the structure, and do not say the house is “clear” or “infested” as the inspector. Moisture and damage notes stay as their words on the form. NPMA and state form pages are orientation only. If they have no WDO form in the folder, say none — do not invent findings to finish a file. This is not a landscaping tree inspection. You pack present-or-missing. They inspect and they sign. Starter until they teach which *this* shop form is live and who may sign.
