# CEU date as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A CEU or recertification date is copied from the certificate, roster, or state printout they stored. Typical US applicator programs need credits before a card expires. You list the date they recorded and whether a file is present. You do not invent hours. You do not say the person met the law. You do not pick courses. If the license-on-file date and the CEU file disagree, quote both and ask. This is not a landscaping safety tailgate. Missing certificates stay missing; do not total credits from memory. Calendar reminders they already wrote may sit next to the date. You do not stamp recertification as complete or legal. Hours they left blank stay blank. You pack present-or-missing. They register and they sit the class. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tracks CEUs per tech versus a company-level file and who owns the calendar reminder.
