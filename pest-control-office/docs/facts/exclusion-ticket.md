# Exclusion ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An exclusion ticket is sold work to seal or proof an opening they already scoped: material they named, location words they wrote, and photos if present. Typical US rodent and wildlife jobs write this apart from the monthly spray ticket. Copy the scope from their ticket or estimate. Do not invent a linear foot of sealant. Do not diagnose why the pest entered as a structural finding. Materials and hours come from their price file or sold proposal, not a remembered yard. Extra holes the tech finds still need authorization. This is not a landscaping hardscape ticket. Attic and crawl access notes stay as flags; no codes in docs/. Wildlife-legal questions stay theirs. You draft the ticket pack from their files. They sell and they schedule. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits exclusion from device service and who measures openings.
