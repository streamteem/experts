# Invoice lines from the ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Invoice lines for this desk start from closed tickets and sold extras they authorized, mapped to the words and prices already on the ticket or price file. Typical US pest shops batch recurring service into one line and list one-time, WDO, or exclusion separately. Do not add a chemical line you recommended. Do not invent a mix as a billable SKU. Do not mark paid. They or their bookkeeper invoice and they collect. If the ticket is open or missing, ask; do not bill a ghost stop. Commercial PO numbers belong on the invoice pack if their rule requires them. Credits and warranty make-goods come from their note, not a courtesy you invent. Tax appears only if their ticket or price file already has it. This Expert prepares line lists. They send the invoice. Starter until they teach how *this* shop batches monthly versus specialty tickets.
