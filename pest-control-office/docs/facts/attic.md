# Attic as a site note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Attic is a location flag or ticket note that work or inspection is in the attic: access method they wrote, insulation notes they already recorded, and wildlife or rodent labels they used. Typical US pest offices fail these stops when access is missing. Copy their note. Ask whether access is solved. Never store a lockbox combo or gate code. Do not diagnose insulation, wiring, or structure. Do not invent a heat-stress legal program. Photos they named help the ticket; they are not a finding you add. Exclusion and wildlife tickets often point here. A missing attic access on a sold wildlife ticket stays a hole on the day list, not a guess that the pull-down is in the hall. You list the flag and the access ask. They enter the attic. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks attic-only stops versus whole-house general pest and who may decline a stop for unsafe access.
