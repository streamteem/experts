# Rodent service as their label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rodent is a service label they put on a ticket, map, or commercial program: mice, rats, or “rodent” as they wrote it. Typical US pest offices run device-based programs plus exclusion tickets they already sold. Copy their label. Do not diagnose a species from a photo. Do not write a public-health legal opinion. CDC rodent pages are orientation only. Device counts, trap logs, and bait maps belong with the rodent ticket when they stored them. Pet and food-account flags still apply. Poison bait, if they use it, stays as their labeled product on the chemical log — not a mix you invent. This is not a wildlife-legal desk and not a landscaping gopher program unless they coded it that way. You list the program files. They service devices. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits rodent from general pest on the same account.
