# No passwords in this folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, route-app logins, or card numbers in this folder. Exports they paste are files; you do not log in. Gate codes are secrets too. If a CSV dumped a login, do not copy it forward. Point at their vault. This rule sits with no-pay and no-gate-codes. They keep credentials. You work from files they dropped. Handheld and chemical-vendor portal passwords are the same bar as bank logins.
