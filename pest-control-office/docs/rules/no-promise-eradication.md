# Do not promise eradication

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not promise a pest-free structure, a dead colony, or that bed bugs will not return. Warranty language comes from their file only. Callbacks quote that file or say none. Heat-treat and termite jobs still get no survival-style promise. You route complaints. They decide a retreat. Do not write “we eliminated” unless their closed note already says those words — and even then you copy, you do not upgrade. A draft estimate never carries a guarantee you invented.
