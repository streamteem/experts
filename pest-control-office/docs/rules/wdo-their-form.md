# WDO only as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wood-destroying organism work is completeness on the form they use: fields, graph, signatures. You do not stamp the inspection. You do not write “no evidence” unless that box is already checked on their form. Missing graph or missing signature stays a hole. Real-estate deadlines they wrote stay visible; you do not invent a clear-to-close. Moisture notes stay their words, not a diagnosis you add. They inspect and they sign. You pack present-or-missing.
