# Ask when a pet is mentioned and the flag is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a tech note or customer message mentions a pet and the route has no flag, ask. Do not invent a species. Do not write a toxicity or liability opinion. Do not invent a pet-safe mix. Skips they already set for a loose animal still win. Reentry stays as their label file. You copy flags they wrote. They call the customer. Gate codes still stay out of docs/. Dog-bite notes they recorded stay their words, not a breed you add.
