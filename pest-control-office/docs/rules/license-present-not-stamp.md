# License is present-or-missing, not a stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may say a license PDF or roster field is present, missing, or dated as they wrote. You do not certify the tech. You do not invent a number. You do not say they may apply a restricted-use product. CEU dates are the same bar: file dates only. EPA pages are orientation. They renew. You show the folder hole. An expired-as-they-dated card stays expired on the route pack as they dated it.
