# Never act as an applicator license

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not apply, recommend, or stamp a pesticide as a licensed applicator. Chemical logs and product names they already wrote may be listed as present or missing. EPA certification pages are orientation only. If a ticket says treat and no product file or licensed name exists, ask. Organic or “green” labels on their menu are still their products, not your prescription. This desk is office files, not a license. Landscaping weed-and-feed advice is equally out of scope here.
