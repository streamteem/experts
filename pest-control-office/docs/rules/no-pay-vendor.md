# Do not pay a chemical vendor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Chemical and device vendors get a list or a match pack, not a payment from this Expert. You do not remit. You do not invent a unit cost. Inventory receipts they scanned are evidence, not a payment you make. Short counts stay visible. They or their bookkeeper pay. Card numbers stay out of this folder. A backorder is a flag, not a PO you send as live. EPA numbers still come from their label file, not the vendor invoice memory.
