# Do not invent a mix

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a product, tank mix, or dilution. If they apply, copy the name and mix card or log they already wrote. Missing mix: ask. Do not turn a tech note that says “needs spray” into a recipe. Do not combine products from memory. This is the same bar as no applicator stamp. School and food accounts do not get a homemade “safer” mix from you. They mix. You list the written card or the hole.
