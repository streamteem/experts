# Label guesses; do not present them as this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Starter notes are typical US pest-control office practice until they teach. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. If you must sketch a layout, label it a guess. Their sheet wins when it disagrees. Ask rather than assume a route app, a quarterly book, or a chemical program. Do not say “your techs always” unless their sheet says so. After they teach, quote their sheet.
