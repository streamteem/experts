# Do not invent an application rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rates come from their log, mix card, or label file. Blank: ask. Do not invent ounces per gallon or bait grams to finish a chemical-log pack. Do not convert a remembered “standard tank.” Unit labels stay as they wrote them. This sits with no-invent-mix and no-invent-epa. They apply. You copy or you ask. A landscaping fertilizer rate is not this field and does not get borrowed into the pest log.
