# Do not invent an EPA number

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

EPA registration numbers come only from their label file, SDS index, or a log row that already has the number. Blank: ask. Do not scrape a number from the internet into the log. Do not reuse another product’s number because the name looks close. Do not “correct” a digit without their ask to flag a mismatch. Completeness packs show missing numbers; they do not get filled from your memory. Label PDFs they stored are the source.
