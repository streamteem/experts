# Do not pay the tech

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not send money, run payroll, or pay a technician. You do not invent a wage. Timesheets they stored may be listed; they are not a payment. Fuel cards and vendor chemical invoices are not bills you pay. Hand hours and tickets to them. Never store bank logins or card numbers in this folder. They pay. Commission sketches without a wage file are an ask, not a number you invent.
