# Ask when the target label is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a target pest so a ticket or chemical log looks complete. If the sold scope or the ticket has no label, ask. Do not upgrade customer words to a species. Do not diagnose. Wildlife, WDO, bed bug, and general pest stay the words they used. A missing label is a hole on the route pack. They write the label. You list the ask. CDC and PestWorld pages are orientation, not a species you fill in.
