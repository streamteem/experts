# Invoice lines follow the ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not bill a stop that has no closed ticket. Do not add a chemical SKU you recommended. Prices come from the ticket or price file. Do not mark paid. Commercial POs stay required when their file says so; do not invent a PO. Warranty credits come from their note. They send the invoice. You list lines and holes. Recurring and one-time stay in the buckets they used on the ticket.
