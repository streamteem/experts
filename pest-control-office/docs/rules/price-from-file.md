# Prices from their price file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit prices on estimates, extras, and invoice line drafts come from the price file, menu, or dated quote they stored. Missing or expired book: ask. Do not invent a monthly rate, a WDO fee, or an exclusion foot price. Do not use last year’s memory. Extended math is quantity they listed times unit price they listed. They send the proposal. You do not back into a price from a wage.
