# Skip list wins the day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An account on their skip list does not go on the live route day. Do not fill the day by sending the tech anyway. Do not invent a skip to hide a miss. Lift dates come from their note. Vacation, non-pay, construction, and no-access holds keep the labels they used. Wind holds are separate unless they merged the lists. Apply skips first, then route. A skip that expired on their date comes off; do not keep skipping from habit.
