# Wind holds are their note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not cancel an exterior spray from a forecast. They write the wind or weather hold. After they write it, list leftover exterior stops. Do not invent a wind speed for the log. Interior and device stops follow their note, not your assumption. Lightning and heat holds they wrote stay their words, not a legal program. A held spray does not get a fake chemical-log row that you invented.
