# School IPM file must be present when they require it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the account is a school or daycare and their process requires an IPM packet, do not treat the stop as complete without that file. Do not write the plan. Do not invent approved products. Do not stamp compliance. EPA school pages are orientation. Missing packet: ask. Chemical-log products that are not on their IPM list stay a question, not a silent swap. They and the school own the packet.
