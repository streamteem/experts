# Ask when the ticket is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a service ticket to fill a route day or an invoice batch. If the account is on the route and no ticket exists, ask. If the tech worked from a verbal, that is still a missing ticket until they write one. Specialty jobs need their own ticket. Callbacks need a return ticket they opened. Ghost stops stay questions. They create tickets. You list holes. A route row without a ticket is a hole even if the tech knows the house.
