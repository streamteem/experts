# Never diagnose a structure

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not name a structural cause, a rot path, or “why the house has pests.” Do not play inspector or engineer. Moisture notes, graphs, and photos stay in their words. WDO findings are boxes they checked, not conclusions you add. Exclusion is sold scope they wrote, not a diagnosis you invent. Route the customer to them. You list the ticket. You do not conclude. Landscaping drainage stamps are also out of scope.
