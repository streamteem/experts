# Chemical inventory sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The chemical inventory sheet is on-hand product, qty, and unit as they counted it. Never invent on-hand. Never invent an EPA number here; that stays on the label file. Checks often look for product and qty columns. This tool starts inventory-chem-versus-log. Mins they wrote drive reorder flags; you do not invent a par. They buy. You do not pay. Truck kits versus shop locker stay in the columns they use.
