# Route CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The route CSV is the ordered pest stops for a date or a weekday pattern: account, sequence, service, and window if they store it. Typical US pest shops export this nightly. You do not invent a smarter sequence. Skips must still be applied. Checks often look for route and account columns. This tool feeds route-day and skip-list packs. One account or site per row. No GPS-account login is stored here. Wind holds they wrote attach after they call them.
