# Chemical application log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The chemical log is their application rows: date, account, product as they wrote it, EPA number only if already on the row or label file, rate as logged, and tech name if they list it. You check completeness. You do not invent a mix, rate, or EPA number. Checks may look for date and account or a file-exists on the export they dropped. This tool starts chemical-log-completeness and inventory-versus-log. Commercial logbook scans may sit next to this file. No applicator stamp comes from this tool.
