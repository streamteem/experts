# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build for a pack: route day, log holes, callbacks, device counts, or estimate lines. Columns stay their words plus the check columns the workflow names. You do not invent prices, mixes, EPA numbers, or tickets into blank cells. One account per row. This tool is the usual last step that writes out/ write-ups and work/ CSVs the checks can see. Export from their files; no login is stored in this folder.
