# Service ticket sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The ticket sheet is their export or workbook of pest stops: ticket id, account, service, date, target label if they store it, and status. Typical US shops export from a route app or keep a numbered sheet. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a ticket or a target pest. Checks often look for ticket and account columns. This tool feeds route-day, callback, estimate, WDO, and invoice packs. Extra-work rows without auth stay questions. No login is stored here.
