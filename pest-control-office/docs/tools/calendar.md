# Calendar file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The calendar is their dated plan for route days, follow-ups, pre-treat pours, and CEU reminders they already wrote. You pack due lists from this file plus tickets. You do not invent a bed-bug follow-up week. Weather or wind they call can slide a row after they write it. Checks may be a file-exists on the calendar they dropped. This tool supports follow-up and skip questions. No mailbox login is stored here.
