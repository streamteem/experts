# Callback list file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The callback list is their export or sheet of return visits: ticket, account, reason in their words, and status. You do not invent a complaint. You do not promise eradication. Warranty versus courtesy versus billable stays as they coded it. Checks often look for ticket and account columns. This tool starts the callback-list workflow. Skip and pet flags still apply when you pack the day. No customer-email product lives here.
