# Bait-station map PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The bait-map PDF or image is their device layout: station ids and location words they wrote. You index ids and counts. You do not invent a station or redraw the building as a diagnosis. Checks often look for a file-exists on the map or an index CSV you write. This tool feeds bait-map-index and device-count. Barcode scans, if any, reconcile to this map. No lockbox codes belong in the index.
