# Customer PO on the work order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The customer purchase order or sales-order number on the work order is the commercial trace for make-to-order work. Typical US SMB plants copy it from the order entry file onto the job header and the traveler. You copy it as written. You do not invent a customer PO. If the job has none and the item looks make-to-order, ask. Line numbers and customer item numbers stay as labeled. Promise dates on the sales order and due dates on the job may differ; quote both. Partial-ship rules often live on that order. This is not a collections desk and not a price desk. Starter until they teach how *this* shop links jobs to orders and what to do with stock jobs that later get reserved. Blanket customer releases that create jobs still carry the release number their order file used. You do not invent a release. If two jobs share one customer PO, list both. Price and tax on that PO are not this desk.
