# ECO as a file, not an approval

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An engineering change order or notice is their dated file: what item changed, from-rev and to-rev, effectivity, and whether open jobs must stop, finish, or rework. Typical US SMB plants keep an ECO log in a workbook or a PDF folder from the customer or from their engineer. You list pending and released ECOs against open work orders. You do not approve an ECO. You do not mark a job safe to run through a pending change. You do not invent effectivity dates. If the log says “hold WIP” and dispatch still shows the job, the hold stays visible. If the log is silent on open jobs, ask; do not assume they can finish the old rev. Implementing a change on the floor is their call after whoever owns engineering signs. This desk prepares the pending-ECO pack. They stamp. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers ECOs and who may release them.
