# Overtime is an ask, not an approval

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Overtime, extra shift, or a weekend crew is extra capacity they may choose. Typical US SMB plants write a short ask: which center, which day, which jobs, and hours beyond the shift file. You draft the ask from the load versus calendar. You do not approve overtime. You do not invent hours. You do not invent a wage. A hot-list code is not overtime approval. If they already approved a dated overtime note in the folder, you may quote that note as their note. Finite load that only fits with unapproved overtime stays labeled does-not-fit. Starter until they teach who may authorize overtime at *this* shop and how it appears on the calendar. A load chart that only closes if Saturday is staffed is an ask pack, not a calendar edit. You do not invent which people come in. Paid hours and wages are not this desk. Quote their existing overtime note only when that dated file is in the folder.
