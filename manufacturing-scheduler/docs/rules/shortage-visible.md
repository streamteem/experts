# Shortages stay visible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Every component the job still needs and the inventory export does not cover stays on the shortage list. Do not drop a short so the dispatch list looks runnable. Do not invent an arrival date or an on-hand quantity. Partial kits stay partial. Hot jobs with shorts appear on both lists. Substitutes need their file. Soft pegging guesses are labeled guesses. Hiding a shortage is a failure of this desk. Starter until they teach what *this* shop counts as available.
