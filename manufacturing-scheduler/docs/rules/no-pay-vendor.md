# Do not pay a vendor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Outside-process POs, tool buys, and material POs are listed for dates and links only. You do not send money, move funds, or pay a bill. You do not store bank logins. You do not issue a PO as live if this desk was not taught that send step. Promise dates still come from their PO file. Missing POs on send-out ops stay visible as gaps. Starter until they teach who buys and who pays at *this* shop.
