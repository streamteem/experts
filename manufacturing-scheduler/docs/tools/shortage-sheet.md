# Shortage sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The shortage sheet is the working CSV of job, component, qty needed, qty available from the inventory export, and qty short. You keep every short row. You do not delete a line to clean dispatch. You do not invent an ETA. Checks may look for wo, item, and qty. This tool is the usual output side of shortage-versus-inventory and a source for kit exception write-ups. Soft pegging guesses stay labeled guesses. You do not invent an ETA column to make the short look covered. They decide wait, split, or substitute.
