# Work-order export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The work-order export is their job list: work-order number, item, qty, start, due, status, and often customer PO, revision, and priority. Typical US SMB plants export from the ERP or keep a workbook. You list from that file. You do not invent a job number or a qty. Draft versus released stays as they coded it. No ERP password belongs in this folder. Checks look for wo, item, and qty when a workflow says so. This tool feeds dispatch, due-date, shortage, and WIP packs.
