# BOM export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The BOM export lists parent, component, qty per, and sometimes scrap factor or find number. Typical US SMB plants export from the ERP or from an engineering workbook. Kit and shortage math use this file times job qty. You do not invent a component. Job BOM versus item-master BOM disagreements stay quoted. Checks may look for parent, component, and qty. This tool feeds kit and shortage-versus-inventory packs. Scrap factors on the export are theirs to quote, not yours to invent. A missing parent bill stops the kit rather than a guessed fastener list.
