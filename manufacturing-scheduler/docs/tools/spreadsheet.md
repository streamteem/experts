# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the desk’s CSV or workbook for dispatch, due-date sorts, and write-up tables. Columns come from their exports, not from invented cycle times. You do not store passwords. Checks look for the columns a workflow names. Draft lists stay draft. This tool is the usual pack surface after WO, calendar, and center files are in work/. Cycle-time columns stay blank when the router left them blank. You do not fill hours from a typical industry table. Export dates belong in the write-up.
