# ECO log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The ECO log is their workbook or PDF index of change numbers, items, from-rev and to-rev, status, and effectivity. You list pending changes against open jobs. You do not approve an ECO. You do not invent effectivity. Hold-WIP notes stay visible on dispatch. This tool feeds the pending-ECO pack. If the log is empty, say empty; do not assume the floor is clear. Released versus pending stay as they coded. You do not stamp approval. Open jobs that the log says to hold stay held on dispatch.
