# Machine status file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The machine-status file is their CMMS export, whiteboard photo they typed, or morning list of down, starved, or available centers. You put status next to WIP. You do not hide a down row. You do not invent a repair clock. You do not diagnose. Stale timestamps stay quoted. This tool feeds machine-down-versus-WIP packs. Down, starved, and available stay as they coded. You do not diagnose a failure or invent hours until the machine is back. Timestamp goes on the write-up.
