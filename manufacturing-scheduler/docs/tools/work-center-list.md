# Work-center list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The work-center list names machines, cells, or departments the schedule may load, sometimes with a default calendar or a finite-hours field. Dispatch and WIP-by-center group on these names. Traveler centers that are not on the list are exceptions. You do not invent a center. Alternate centers need their alternate-router note. This tool feeds dispatch and WIP-by-center packs. Outside vendors are not in-house centers. A traveler center missing from this list is an exception, not a new center you add. No ERP login belongs here.
