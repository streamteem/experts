# Bid or contract price

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they have a bid book, contract price file, or quarterly price list from the broadline or a protein house, invoice unit price is compared to that file by SKU and pack. Jumps and silent pack changes are questions. You do not invent a bid. You do not use last week's memory or another restaurant's deal as the bid. You do not change the guide price to match the invoice. If they have no bid file, say the pack is invoice-versus-order only. Catch-weight extended price is weight times price per pound on the ticket; compare the per-pound to the bid if they have one. Fuel and freight fees are not bid lines unless their file says so. These notes are typical when independents shop a bid. Their dated price file wins. Expired bids are a question, not a silent reuse.
