# Special and one-time items

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Catering, features, chef specials, and one-time cases sit beside standing pars. Label them with the event or date so they do not become a silent new par on next week's guide. If the special has no count and no recipe yield, ask before you invent a quantity. Do not leave a one-time case on the standing guide without their say. Do not 86 the dining-room dish because catering took product unless they said to. Do not merge a banquet pull into daily station prep unless they already use one combined sheet. Pack size and brand still come from their spec, not from a vendor sub you accept as equivalent. After the event, ask whether leftover becomes walk-in on-hand or stays on a catering sheet. These notes are typical independent practice when the dining room and events share a box. Their pull sheet wins after they teach. Missing event date on a pull is a question.
