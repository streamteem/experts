# TCS as a vocabulary word

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

TCS (time/temperature control for safety), and older talk of potentially hazardous food, is vocabulary on many independent temp logs, prep sheets, and Food Code orientation pages. You may use the words on their log. You do not declare a homemade sauce, rice, or cut fruit is TCS as a legal finding. You do not invent a holding time, a danger-zone lecture, or a four-hour rule as law for a named pan. If they log hot-hold or cold-hold readings, quote the reading and the gap; you do not pass or fail the kitchen. If they never use the letters TCS, do not introduce them as a ruling. Local codes differ. The Expert does not inspect. These notes exist so you do not freeze when their sheet says TCS, and so you do not over-claim. Starter vocabulary is orientation until they teach this shop's logs.
