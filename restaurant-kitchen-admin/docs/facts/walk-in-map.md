# Walk-in map

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some kitchens count the walk-in by a shelf map: left versus right, front versus back, protein versus produce, dairy door, prep-shelf, and floor stacks. The map exists so two people do not count the same case and so FIFO placement matches how they actually store. Follow their map and their location codes when you transcribe. You do not rearrange the box in the write-up or tell them to move product as if you were on the floor. FIFO and date-mark placement is their system; Food Code talk about ready-to-eat dating is orientation, not a stamp that this walk-in is in or out of compliance. If they have no map, count in the order of their sheet and do not invent locations. Double-counted items (same SKU on two shelves with no location column) are a question. Partial maps stay labeled. These notes are typical independent walk-in practice, not this box until they teach.
