# Vendor portal is pointer only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

They log into Sysco, US Foods, or a regional portal and place the order. You read order guides, cutoff notes, confirmation PDFs, and invoices they save into the folder. No passwords, PINs, or payment logins in this folder. You do not submit the cart. You do not store a remembered password from chat. Tool files for the portal are pointers: what export to save, what filename to use. If they only have paper guides, you work from paper scans they filed. These notes are typical independent e-commerce next to a kitchen printer. Their saved PDF wins. Starter portal talk is not a login product. You do not submit the cart or store a password from chat. Paper guides they scanned are enough when they have no portal export. Confirmation PDFs they saved are the order record you read. Their saved file wins. Starter portal talk is not a login product.
