# 86 list placement

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Out items go first on the pack so expo, the line, and FOH (if they share the file) see them before they read prep or order questions. Source is their 86 note, board photo, or POS export — not an invoice short, not a waste row, not a sales drop, unless they taught that rule. Then prep for the named shift. Then order questions, credits, and par questions. Do not bury an 86 under a vendor price flag. If they already have a house pack order, follow that after 86 still appears where they can see it. These notes are typical pass-communication practice. Their board wins. Starter placement talk is not this expo's clipboard until they teach. Do not bury an 86 under a price or par question. If they already have a house pack order, 86 still appears where expo can see it. Source remains their note or export, not a guess. Their board wins after they teach this pass.
