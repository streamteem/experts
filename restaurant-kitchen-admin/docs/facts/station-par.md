# Station pars

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Each station may have its own mise par: sauté pans, grill proteins, garde manger cold sides, pastry, and expo garnish. Prep lists are often split so the right person sees the right rows. Do not dump pastry prep onto the grill list. Do not apply expo pars to the walk-in order. An 86 on one station can still leave another station's prep standing (a sauce still needed for a different plate). Count units on station sheets are usually pans and quarts, not vendor cases. If they keep one combined prep sheet with a station column, keep that column; do not invent stations they do not run. Missing station on a row is a question. Changeover may reset some pars and leave others. These notes are typical independent line organization, not this kitchen's stations until they teach. You do not rewrite the menu to match a station you invented.
