# Vendor minimums and fees

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Minimum order dollars or cases, fuel surcharges, freight, and small-order fees come from the guide, the vendor PDF they saved, or the invoice footer — not from memory. Flag a draft order that may miss a minimum as a question. You do not add filler SKUs to hit a minimum unless they asked. You do not pick a random dry-goods case to 'make the truck.' If they have two houses on one day, do not combine them to invent a minimum save unless they asked and the files stay labeled by vendor. Late add-ons after cutoff are their call. These notes are typical independent ordering friction. Their fee schedule wins. Starter talk about minimums is orientation, not this account. Do not invent a minimum from another restaurant's account. If the footer fee is new versus last invoice, list it as a question. Cutoff and minimum are different constraints; missing either file is an ask, not a guess.
