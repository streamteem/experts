# Linen and paper goods

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Linen, bar towels, chef coats, napkins, and many paper or disposable goods often ride a separate vendor with its own par, delivery day, and invoice. Pars still apply. Do not mix linen into protein or produce counts. Shortages, missing bundles, and price jumps are questions like any other invoice. You do not invent a rental-contract legal reading. Staff-meal napkins and FOH linen may be different pars if they split them; keep their split. These notes are typical independent side-vendor practice. Their linen guide wins. Starter talk is orientation until they teach whether paper is on the broadline or a paper house. Do not invent a bundle count from the dining-room photo. Rental versus owned linen follows their file, not a legal reading you write. Paper on the broadline stays on that guide if that is how they split it. Their linen guide wins after they teach.
