# On-hand is counted

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Theoretical inventory from sales, a POS usage report, last week's close, or a recipe times covers is not a walk-in count. Do not treat POS theoretical as counted on-hand unless they taught that exception in writing for this shop. Do not fill a blank count cell from the order guide's last qty. Do not guess a rail from a ticket photo. Partial counts stay labeled. Missing count file: ask. Food-cost theoretical versus actual still needs an actual count file to talk about actual. These notes are a hard desk habit for independents who otherwise order from memory. Their sheet wins. Starter 'do not guess' is the rule until they teach a documented exception.
