# Delivery day and cutoff

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Orders belong to one delivery date unless they asked to combine trucks and the date column is clear. Cutoff times are theirs: a produce house may cut mid-afternoon for next-morning delivery; a broadline may cut the night before. A late order is a question for them, not something you submit in a portal. Do not mix two trucks on one CSV without a date and vendor column. Do not apply Tuesday pars to a Thursday truck. Lead time on specialty or protein (two-day butcher, Friday-only fish) comes from their vendor note or the guide, not from a remembered industry habit. Holidays and weather closures are on their calendar if they filed one; you do not invent a closed day. Standing pars live on the master guide; the quantity column on the order file is for that delivery only. Starter cutoff talk is orientation. Their posted cutoff wins.
