# Shift and daypart pars

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

AM, PM, brunch, late-night, and close often have different pars, different 86 lists, and different prep owners in a typical independent. Weekend sheets often differ from weekday. You ask which shift the count, prep, and order belong to, and you print that name on the pack. Do not apply Friday dinner pars to a Monday lunch list. Do not merge AM and PM prep onto one sheet unless they already run a combined file and said to keep it that way. Changeover leftover (lunch mise that becomes dinner on-hand) is their rule; ask whether PM par includes AM leftover before you compute a second full batch. Catering or patio-open shifts are separate if they keep them. A starter note about dayparts is orientation. Their posted schedule and their sheet headers win. Missing shift on a count or 86 photo is a question, not a default you pick because most shops count at close.
