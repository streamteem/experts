# Line and lowboy counts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Station pars in lowboys, reach-ins, garnish rails, and expo drawers are often separate from walk-in pars. A full walk-in and an empty rail is a prep or restock problem, not a receiving short. Typical independents count the line at open or at changeover so AM leftover and PM build are visible. You count only the stations they listed. Do not assume sauté and grill share a par. Do not dump pastry lowboy counts onto the hot line. Units are usually sixth pans, quarts, and each, not vendor cases. If they never count the rail and only count the box, say the pack is walk-in only. Mise that turns daily (herbs, portioned proteins, sauce backups) belongs on the station sheet they use. You do not guess rail on-hand from a ticket photo. Starter talk about line versus box is orientation until they teach this line.
