# Sauce and stock batches

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sauce, stock, and soup batches follow their recipe yield and their hotel-pan or cambro par. Date marks on pans come from their label system, not from a seven-day figure you apply as law. Do not invent a batch the count does not support. Do not 86 a sauce except from their 86 note. If yield is four quarts and par is six, ask how they round; do not invent a half batch unless the card allows it. Secret recipes stay unpublished. Allergen lines on the sauce card are quoted as written. These notes are typical independent sauce-room practice. Their card and count win. Food Code TCS talk is orientation only. You do not declare the sauce TCS as a legal finding. Do not invent a half batch to make the math neat unless the card allows it. Do not apply a seven-day hold as law to the pan. Secret recipes stay unpublished. If count does not support the batch, ask. Their card and count win after they teach.
