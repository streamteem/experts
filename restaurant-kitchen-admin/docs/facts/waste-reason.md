# Waste reasons they use

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Use the reason codes on their waste sheet or app: spoil, burn, drop, expire, over-prep, misfire, trim, walk-out, or whatever they printed. Do not invent a code, and do not write theft or 'employee issue' as a reason you guessed. Date-mark expire and cook error are different rows when they treat them that way; collapsing them hides the par question. If reason is blank, ask before you summarize a week. Compost hauler tickets, grease-trap tickets, and oil-pickup slips are vendor files; they are not waste-log substitutes unless they taught that mapping. Staff meal and donation, if logged separately, stay on those logs, not relabeled as spoil. Quantity units follow the log (each, pan, pound). You do not convert to food-cost dollars unless they gave a price file and asked for that arithmetic. Patterns across reasons are questions for the chef or GM. Starter reason lists are orientation, not this shop's codes.
