# Mise and garnish pars

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Herbs, sauces, portioned proteins, and expo garnish often have small pars that turn every shift. Waste on garnish is commonly over-prep. Use their garnish or mise par, not a dining-room photo and not a menu description. Count units are usually each, sixth pan, and squeeze bottle. Do not set a mise par from a cover forecast unless they said that is the math. 86 on a garnish can 86 a plate; still take that from their 86 note, not from an empty rail you assume. Station mise is not walk-in backup; keep the sheets separate if they do. These notes are typical independent line mise. Their par card wins. Starter talk is orientation until they teach this expo. Do not set herb pars from a menu photo or a cover guess. Over-prep on garnish is a par question only when their waste log shows it. Walk-in backup herbs are not the rail par unless they said so. Their mise card wins after they teach this expo.
