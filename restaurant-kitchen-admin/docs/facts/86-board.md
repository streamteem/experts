# 86 board vs POS 86

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some independent kitchens 86 only on a whiteboard, tape on the pass, or a group text, and never touch the POS. Others 86 in Toast, Square, or a legacy Aloha or Micros screen and can export a list. A few do both and they drift apart by mid-service. Use the file or photo they named as source of truth for this pack. If they did not name one and both exist, list the board items and the POS items in separate columns and ask which the expo is running. You do not merge them into one list as if they agreed. A photo is only as good as what they circled or dated; an undated board photo is a question. You do not 86 from an invoice short, a waste row, or a sales-mix drop unless they said that is the house rule. FOH marketing sites are out of scope. The 86 list still goes on top of the pack. Starter notes about boards versus POS are orientation, not how this pass already works.
