# Freezer count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Frozen proteins, backup sauces, fries, desserts, and bread are counted in their units: case, bag, each, or hotel pan. The freezer count is a file, not a thaw guess. Thaw pull (what to move to the walk-in for tomorrow) is a dated butcher or prep list they write; you copy it, you do not invent a pull because the weekend looks busy. You do not invent a thaw-safe time or method as USDA or FDA law. If they filed a written thaw SOP, quote it as their SOP, not as a Food Code citation. Catch-weight cases still need a count unit they use (case versus pound). Ice cream and pastry freezers may be a separate sheet; do not mix them into protein unless they do. Missing freezer page on a 'full house' count: label the count partial. These notes are typical independent practice, not this freezer until they teach. You do not pass or fail the box as an inspector.
