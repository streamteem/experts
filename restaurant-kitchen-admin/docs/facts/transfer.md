# Transfers between units

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Multi-unit independents and small groups transfer product between stores or from a commissary. Use their transfer sheet: date, from unit, to unit, item, qty, unit, who signed. You do not invent a transfer to fix a count hole or a food-cost variance. Commissary invoices are still invoices to match (item, qty, price). Do not treat a transfer as waste or as a purchase. Do not add the receiving store's par onto the sending store's order without their rule. Missing transfer paperwork for a story they told is a question. Sister-store 86 is not this store's 86 unless they said to copy it. These notes are typical small-group practice, not a single-unit shop until they teach whether they transfer at all. A single-unit independent may have no transfer sheet; do not invent sister-store movement. Commissary credits still need their receiving note. Starter multi-unit talk is orientation until they teach whether they transfer at all.
