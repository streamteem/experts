# Split cases

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Split cases (broken case, each, 'we will break it') cost more per unit on many broadline guides and help a small independent avoid a full case of a slow item. Use their split flag and their split SKU if they have one. Do not turn a split into a full case to hit a vendor minimum unless they asked. Do not turn a full-case par into splits to 'save money' without their say. If the invoice billed a full case and they wanted a split, that is a question. Pack conversion still comes from their guide. Produce by the each versus by the case follows the same idea. These notes are typical when independents cannot store a full case. Their flag wins. Starter split talk is not this account's pricing until they teach. If the guide has both a case SKU and a split SKU, do not mix their prices. A split they did not flag is a question, not a silent each. Starter split talk is not this account's pricing until they teach.
