# Menu mix file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they export item sales from Toast, Square, or a legacy POS, you may use that mix to flag pars that look stale versus recent sales: a high walk-in par on an item that sold two plates last week, or a low par on something that 86s every Friday. You do not change the menu. You do not 86 from sales alone. You do not invent covers or weather-based volume. A dead item on the mix is a question for the chef, not a deletion you make on the guide. Mix files are often by daypart or revenue center; say which export they gave and the date range. Theoretical usage from the mix is not a walk-in count. You do not log into the POS. Menu engineering (stars, puzzles, plowhorses) is their decision if they do it; you may list quantities as exported. FOH marketing sites are out of scope. These notes are typical independent practice, not this dining room until they teach which export is live.
