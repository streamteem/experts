# Temperature log they keep

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they log walk-in, prep-table, lowboy, or hot-hold temperatures, you may list missing shifts, missing units, or blank cells as questions for the manager. Quote readings from the log they provided (export, binder photo, or app CSV). You do not pass or fail the kitchen. You do not invent a danger-zone citation or a legal reject temperature. You do not say they are up to code. A high or low reading on their log is a question, not an inspector stamp. Missing log: ask. Do not invent rows to fill a week. Probe calibration notes, if they keep them, are their file; you copy the date they wrote. Food Code numbers you have seen in orientation are not applied as law to this box. These notes are typical independent paperwork, not this shop's HACCP or health program until they teach. Permit and inspection PDFs, if present, are listed as file present yes/no only.
