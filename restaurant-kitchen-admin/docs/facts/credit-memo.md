# Credits and shorts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Credits for shorts, mispicks, damaged cases, and quality rejects need their receiving note (or marked invoice) plus the invoice line: item, qty, price, reason they wrote. You draft a credit-question list; they submit it in the portal or by phone. You do not invent a credit amount, a pickup authorization, or a 'we always get 10 percent.' You do not mark the invoice paid or unpaid as AP. You do not send money. If they have no receiving note, say the draft is invoice-only and ask. Catch-weight credits need the weight they disputed, not an average. Substitution they accepted is not a credit unless they said so. These notes are typical kitchen-to-vendor practice. Their process wins after they teach. Do not treat a verbal 'they will credit us' as a memo. Keep the draft labeled as questions until they submit it. Starter credit talk is typical kitchen-to-vendor practice, not this house's portal steps until they teach.
