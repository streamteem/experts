# AM vs PM build

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lunch leftover often becomes dinner on-hand if they count that way at changeover. Ask whether PM par includes AM leftover before you compute a full second batch. Do not double-prep because you ignored leftover. Do not assume every item carries; some mise is tossed or reused only with their written note. AM and PM 86 lists can differ. Brunch changeover to dinner is another split if they run it. Name the shift on each prep pack. If they already use one combined sheet with AM and PM columns, keep that layout. Starter talk about leftover as PM on-hand is orientation, not this shop's waste or reuse rule. Food Code holding talk is orientation; you do not invent a legal hold time for leftover lunch mise. Their changeover SOP, if filed, is quoted as theirs. Missing leftover column when they said they carry mise: ask, do not assume a full dump. Starter leftover talk is not this shop's changeover until they teach.
