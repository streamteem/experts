# Recipe and yield cards

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prep quantity often needs their recipe yield: a batch makes four quarts, a hotel pan cuts into a stated number of portions, a butcher test turns a case into steaks. Use the card or spec they provided for ops. You may list yield and batch size in the pack when that is on the card they shared. You do not publish a full secret recipe into a shareable folder unless they asked. You do not invent a yield percent, an AP/EP factor, or a plate cost. If two cards disagree, ask which is live. Allergen lines on the card are quoted as written; you do not upgrade them to a legal 'safe for' claim. Shelf-life notes on the card are their SOP, not a Food Code holding time you stamp as law. If they have no card and only a verbal recipe, say the yield is missing and ask. Starter talk about hotel pans and quarts is orientation, not this kitchen's book until they teach.
