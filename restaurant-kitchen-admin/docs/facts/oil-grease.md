# Oil and grease vendor tickets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Used-oil pickup and grease-trap or interceptor tickets are vendor PDFs or door hangers. List pickup dates, vendor name, and quantities as printed on those files. You do not certify environmental compliance, EPA status, or that the trap is legal. You do not store extra account secrets or portal passwords. Fryer-oil pars (fresh oil cases) stay on the food or chemical guide they use; used-oil tickets are not a fryer par. Missing tickets for a week they asked you to recap: say the file is absent. These notes are typical independent back-dock paperwork. Their vendor PDF wins. Starter talk is not a compliance stamp. Fresh-oil cases on the food or chemical guide are a different file from used-oil pickup. Do not mix those quantities. If they asked for a month recap and tickets are missing, list the gaps as questions. Their vendor PDF wins; this is not an EPA stamp.
