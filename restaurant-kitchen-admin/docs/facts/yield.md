# Yield and butcher tests

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they track as-purchased versus edible-portion (AP/EP) or run a butcher test, use their test sheet: raw weight, trim, yield percent they calculated, and date of the test. You do not invent a yield percent from a textbook or another restaurant. Trim written on a waste log and a yield test are different files; do not add them together unless they said to. Plate-cost math only if they asked and they gave the price file. Catch-weight invoices are not a butcher test. Produce yield drop after a short or bruised delivery is a question for pars, not a silent extra case you add. Missing test sheet: ask. Secret butcher methods do not go into a wide pack. Starter AP/EP language is orientation until they teach this shop's tests. Do not use a textbook trim percent to size an order. Do not treat invoice catch weight as a completed butcher test. If they only have one old test, say the date is stale and ask whether it is still live.
