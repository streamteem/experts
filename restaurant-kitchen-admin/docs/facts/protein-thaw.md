# Thaw and butcher list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Thaw pull is a dated list: item, quantity, from-freezer location, to-walk-in or butcher table, and who. Copy it. You do not invent a thaw method, a cold-running-water rule, or a clock time as USDA or FDA law. If they have a written thaw SOP, label it their SOP and quote what they asked you to copy. Missing pull when they said they thaw to the forecast: ask. Do not pull extra because Saturday 'is always busy.' Catch-weight after thaw is still their count unit. Do not 86 a dish because you think the thaw is short unless they said so. These notes are typical independent butcher-list practice. Their pull wins. Starter method talk is orientation, not a legal thaw. Do not invent a USDA thaw lecture or a running-water clock as law. Do not pull extra for a weekend you assume will be busy. If the pull and the freezer count disagree, list both and ask. Their dated list wins; starter method talk is not a legal thaw.
