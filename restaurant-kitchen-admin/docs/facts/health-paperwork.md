# Health paperwork as files only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Permit PDFs, pest-control reports, last inspection PDFs, and employee health or training certificates are files you may list as present yes/no with the filename they stored. You never say they passed or failed. You never correct a cited violation as a lawyer or as the health department. You never invent a re-inspection date. Local adoption of the Food Code varies; a model-code page is not this city's result. Pest invoices are still invoices if they asked for a match; that is not a compliance stamp. These notes are typical independent office filing next to the kitchen. Their folder wins. The Expert does not inspect. Do not summarize an inspection PDF as a pass or a fail. Do not invent a re-inspection date or a corrective action as counsel. Pest invoices may match like other vendor tickets; that match is not a compliance stamp. Their folder is listed yes/no only.
