# Allergen file they maintain

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Major allergen names in US retail and restaurant talk often follow FDA orientation: milk, egg, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, wheat, peanuts, soybeans, and sesame as a later addition. That list is orientation for column names you might see on a matrix, not a finding about this plate. Legal or guest-facing status of a dish is only what their recipe card or allergen matrix says. You do not invent 'gluten-free,' 'safe for,' 'contains,' or 'may contain' beyond that file. If the file is missing, ask. You do not give medical advice or tell a guest what they can eat. Cross-contact procedures are theirs; you do not write a new procedure. If FOH and BOH matrices disagree, quote both and ask which is live. Sesame status is their matrix only, even though public Food Code and labeling talk mentions sesame. These notes are typical independent paperwork practice, not an inspection and not this menu until they teach.
