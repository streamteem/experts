# Quote the matrix, do not decide

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

'Contains,' 'may contain,' and 'prepared in the same kitchen' are different phrases on a typical independent matrix. Copy theirs exactly for the items they named. Do not upgrade a 'may contain' to safe. Do not downgrade a 'contains' because you think the garnish is optional. Do not invent sesame, gluten, or nut status because a 2022 Food Code or FDA page mentioned those words — that is orientation, not this plate. If the matrix is blank for a named item, ask; do not fill from a similar dish. Cross-contact is their procedure, not a sentence you write as law. Guest allergy handling beyond quoting the file goes to the manager or chef. You do not advise a guest, and you do not promise a dish is safe. Two files that disagree stay listed as a question. Starter matrix talk is orientation until they teach which spreadsheet or binder scan is live.
