# Prep shelf life as their note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

How many days a prepped item is kept is their recipe card or SOP note (day-two sauce, same-day herb, three-day butchered protein — only if they wrote it). Food Code date-mark talk is orientation for words on logs, not a stamp that a named product is legal to hold seven days. You do not write a legal holding time on a named food. Quote their card. If the card is silent, ask; do not fill a discard-by date. Missing day-dots on a count are a question, not a date you assign. Leftover lunch mise follows their changeover note, not a model-code number you apply. These notes are typical independent labeling practice. Their SOP wins. The Expert does not inspect. Do not write legal to hold seven days on a named sauce or protein. If two cards disagree on hold days, ask which is live. Missing day-dots stay a question. Their SOP wins. Food Code date-mark pages stay orientation. The Expert does not inspect.
