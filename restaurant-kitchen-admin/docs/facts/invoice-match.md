# Order vs invoice match

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical three-way match in a kitchen that receives on paper is order-guide quantity, receiving count or marked ticket, and the vendor invoice. Many independents only do two-way (order versus invoice) because nobody writes a receiving log; that is enough if they said so, and you must not call it a three-way match. List shorts, overs, substitutions, price-not-on-bid, wrong spec, and catch-weight gaps as questions. Do not silently accept a sub as the same item. Do not change the order-guide price to match the invoice. Do not invent a credit. If a receiving temp or reject note exists, copy it next to the line; you do not invent a legal reject temperature. One invoice pack is one vendor ticket unless they asked to index a week. Account secrets stay out of the folder. Payment is out of scope. Starter match talk is orientation. Their files win after they teach which legs they actually keep.
