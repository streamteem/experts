# Vendors

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A typical independent buys from a broadline (Sysco, US Foods, PFG, Gordon, or a regional) plus split vendors: produce, protein or butcher, bread, dairy, coffee, specialty grocery, linen, and chemicals. Each house has a cutoff time, a delivery day, and often a minimum or fuel fee. Use their vendor list and their guide. You do not add a vendor, switch houses, or 'find a cheaper source' as a buyer. Minimums, splits, and surcharges come from the invoice or the guide they filed, not from memory of another account. Portal logins stay out of this folder; they place the order. One order CSV is one vendor and one truck date unless they asked for a mixed file with a vendor column. Specialty items and catering one-offs stay labeled so they do not become a silent standing par. These notes are typical US independent purchasing, not this shop's houses until they teach.
