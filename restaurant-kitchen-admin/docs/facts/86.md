# 86

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Eighty-six means they are out of an item or have pulled it from sale for that shift. The source is their 86 note, whiteboard photo, expo sheet, or POS 86 export they provided — not a guess from an empty hotel pan you cannot treat as a count, and not from a short invoice line unless they taught that exception. Typical independents 86 finished dishes, sometimes a garnish or a protein, and they expect FOH and expo to see the list before guests order. You put the 86 list on top of the pack so the line sees it before prep or order questions. You do not add a menu 86 because sales dropped or because a vendor substituted a spec. You do not remove a standing prep item just because the plate is 86 unless they said component prep stops too. If board and POS disagree, list both and ask which is source of truth. These notes are typical talk, not this shop's 86 rule until they teach. Do not invent an 86 to explain a food-cost hole.
