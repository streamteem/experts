# Produce quality notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Produce invoices and receiving notes may say short, bruised, substituted variety, or ripe versus green. Quote the note. You do not invent a USDA grade or a 'should have rejected' legal call. Prep yield may drop after a bad case; that is a question for pars or a credit draft, not a silent extra case on the next truck. Count produce in their unit (case, each, pound, bunch). Do not mix the produce house into the broadline CSV without a vendor column. Specialty herbs with daily pars still need their count. These notes are typical independent produce receiving. Their ticket wins. Starter grade talk is orientation, not this house's spec. Do not invent a USDA grade or a must-reject finding. A substituted variety is a question, not an automatic accept. Do not add a silent extra case because yield dropped. Count in their unit. Their ticket and receiving note win after they teach.
