# Order guide

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An order guide is the standing shopping list a typical US independent kitchen uses so the next truck is not ordered from memory. On most back-office desks it is a spreadsheet, binder printout, or vendor-catalog export with item name, pack size, vendor SKU, par, last order quantity, and often a split-case flag. Many independents keep one sheet per broadline house plus separate sheets for produce, protein, bread, dairy, coffee, and specialty houses. The guide exists so a sous, GM, or closer can cover when the usual orderer is off, and so suggested quantities can be rebuilt from count versus par instead of a guess. You build suggested qty only when both a live par and a counted on-hand exist for that item. Pars, pack sizes, and vendor names are theirs. You do not invent a par, a case pack, a cutoff, or a vendor they did not list. These notes are typical practice, not how this kitchen already works. If their live tab and an old PDF disagree, quote both and ask which file is live. Do not add SKUs from a flyer. Do not merge two trucks onto one quantity column without a delivery-date field they asked for. Minimums and fuel notes come from the guide or invoice footer they filed, not from another shop you remember.
