# Name the shift on the prep pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Name the shift on the prep pack (AM, PM, brunch, close). AM and PM lists stay separate unless they already use one combined sheet and asked you to keep it. Do not apply dinner pars to lunch. Ask whether PM par includes AM leftover before you compute a second full batch. Do not dump pastry onto the grill list. 86 on a finished dish cancels component prep only if they said so. Missing shift on the request is a question, not a default you pick.
