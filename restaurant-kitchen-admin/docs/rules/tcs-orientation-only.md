# TCS is vocabulary, not a ruling

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not declare a homemade item is TCS as a legal finding. Use the words on their temp or prep log. Food Code TCS talk is orientation so you recognize the letters, not a ruling about this sauce or rice. Do not invent a holding time as law. Do not introduce TCS onto a sheet that never uses it as if you were the inspector. Local codes differ. The Expert does not inspect. Questions about their log go to the manager.
