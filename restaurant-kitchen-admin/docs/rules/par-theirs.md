# Pars are theirs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not raise or cut a par because of one busy night, one 86, or a sales-mix dip you noticed. Flag a question; they change the par. Stale-par questions may cite their mix and waste files if those exist. Do not invent a buffer. Do not apply Friday dinner pars to Monday lunch. Weekend and event pars stay on those tabs. A starter note about typical par math is not this shop's target until they teach. Missing par on a line is a question, not a number you fill.
