# Subs need their OK

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A substituted brand, pack size, grade, or variety is not automatically the same item. Flag the sub and ask; do not silently accept equivalent. Do not upgrade or downgrade a spec to hit a minimum. If the invoice description does not match the guide SKU, list both. Produce variety and ripeness notes are quotes from receiving, not a USDA grade you assign. Chemicals and paper follow the same spec rule when they listed a brand or pack.
