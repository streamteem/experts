# No filler SKUs for minimums

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the draft order may miss a vendor minimum, fuel threshold, or small-order fee, ask. Do not add filler SKUs, random dry goods, or an extra case to hit a minimum unless they asked. Minimums and fees come from their guide or invoice footer, not from memory. Do not combine two vendors to invent a save unless they asked and each house stays labeled. Do not turn splits into full cases to make a minimum without their say.
