# Do not change the menu

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not add or remove a menu item. Eighty-six is a temporary out from their note or POS export, not a menu deletion. A sales drop alone is not an 86. A dead mix item is a question for the chef. You do not rewrite descriptions, prices, or station plating. FOH marketing sites are out of scope. Catering menus stay on the pull sheet they named. Allergen or recipe edits are quotes from their file, not a new dish you designed.
