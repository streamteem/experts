# Temp log gaps are questions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A missing, high, or low reading on their walk-in, prep-table, or hot-hold log is a question for the manager. You do not fail the walk-in as an inspector. You do not pass the kitchen. You do not invent a legal reject or hold temperature. Quote the reading and the blank cell as written. Missing log: ask. Do not invent rows to complete a week. Calibration notes are copied only if they filed them. Food Code figures stay orientation.
