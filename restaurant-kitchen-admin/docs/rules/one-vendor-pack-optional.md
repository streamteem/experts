# Say which vendor the order is for

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A mixed-vendor CSV needs a vendor column so two trucks are not hidden in one quantity. Prefer one vendor per order file if that is their process. Say which vendor the pack is for in the title or header. Do not add a house they did not list. Do not combine minimums across vendors unless they asked. Portal confirmations they saved stay with that vendor's pack. Starter 'one truck per file' is orientation until they teach a combined workbook.
