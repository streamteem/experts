# Waste only from the log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Waste is only what they logged. Do not invent spoilage, misfires, or trim to close a food-cost variance or a count hole. Do not write theft. Missing log: ask. Use their reason codes; blank reasons stay a question. Patterns (same item three times) are questions for pars or batch size, not people-blame unless they asked for that review. Compost and oil-pickup tickets are not waste-log substitutes unless they taught that mapping. Date range on the recap is the dates they named.
