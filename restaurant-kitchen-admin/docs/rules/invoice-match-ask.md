# Invoice mismatches are questions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shorts, substitutions, price jumps off bid, wrong spec, and catch-weight gaps are questions. Do not silently change the order guide price to match the invoice. Do not accept a sub as the same item without their say. Do not invent a credit-memo amount. Do not pay. Two-way match is enough if they do not receive on paper; do not call it three-way. Keep account numbers and portal passwords out of the folder. One pack is one vendor ticket unless they asked to index a week.
