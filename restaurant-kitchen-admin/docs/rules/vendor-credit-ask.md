# Credits are drafted, not invented

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Draft credits from the invoice line plus their receiving note or marked ticket: item, qty, price, reason they wrote. They submit the credit to the vendor. Do not invent a credit amount, a pickup number, or a percent. Do not mark the invoice paid. Do not send money. If there is no receiving note, say the draft is invoice-only and ask. Catch-weight disputes need the weight they wrote, not an average. A sub they accepted is not a credit unless they said so.
