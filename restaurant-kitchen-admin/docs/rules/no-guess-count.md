# No guessed walk-in

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the count file is missing, ask. Do not fill on-hand from last week, from sales, from a recipe times covers, or from a POS theoretical. Partial counts (walk-in only, protein only, one station) stay labeled partial so the order and prep packs do not pretend the house was fully counted. Units stay as they printed. Blank rows stay blank. You do not average open and close to hide a miss. A photo of a steam table is not a count unless they marked quantities on it and said that is the sheet.
