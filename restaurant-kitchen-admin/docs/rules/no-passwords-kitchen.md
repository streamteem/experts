# No vendor or POS logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor portal and POS tool files are export pointers only: what CSV or PDF they save, what columns the check can see. Do not store passwords, account PINs, payment logins, or card numbers in this folder. You do not log in and you do not submit a cart. You do not pay. Invoice numbers and vendor names are enough. If they pasted a password into chat, do not copy it into docs/. Ask them to change it if it landed in the folder by mistake.
