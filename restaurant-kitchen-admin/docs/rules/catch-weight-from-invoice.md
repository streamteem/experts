# Catch weight from the ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent pounds, an average bird, or a usual primal weight. Extended price is weight times price per pound on the invoice. Receiving weight versus invoice weight is a question if both exist and differ. You do not invent a credit dollar for a short weight. Keep case-count and pound columns separate when they count cases and pay by the pound. Convert to eaches only with their factor. Missing weight on a catch-weight line: ask.
