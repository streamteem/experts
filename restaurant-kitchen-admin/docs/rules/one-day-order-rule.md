# One delivery day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two delivery dates on one order CSV unless they asked and the date column is clear. One typical file is one vendor and one truck date. Standing pars may live on a master guide; the quantity you fill is for that delivery only. Do not carry last truck's quantity forward. Do not hide produce Tuesday and broadline Thursday in one number. A late order after their cutoff is a question, not a cart you submit. Label catering and one-time lines so they do not become next week's standing par.
