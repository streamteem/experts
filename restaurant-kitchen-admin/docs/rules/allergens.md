# Do not invent allergen status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Only quote their recipe card or allergen matrix for contains, may contain, and same-kitchen phrases. Do not invent safe-for, gluten-free, or legal contains status. FDA major-allergen lists, including sesame as a later public addition, are orientation for column names you might see, not this dish. If the file is missing or two files disagree, ask which is live. You do not give medical advice or tell a guest what they can eat. Guest handling beyond the quote goes to the manager or chef.
