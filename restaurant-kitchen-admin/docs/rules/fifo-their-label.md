# FIFO from their labels

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent which case is older. FIFO follows their date labels, day-dots, and count notes. If dates are missing on a case or pan, ask; do not assign a discard day. Do not rearrange the walk-in on paper. Rotation misses are questions, not an inspection fail. If they use weekday color dots or a different rotation, follow that system and say so. Food Code date-mark figures stay orientation. You do not stamp this box.
