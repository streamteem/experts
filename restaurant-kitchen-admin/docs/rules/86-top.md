# 86 list on top

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Out items go first on the pack so the line, expo, and FOH (if they share the file) see them before prep or order questions. Source is their 86 note, board photo, or POS export — not a guess from an empty pan, not an invoice short, not a sales drop, unless they taught that rule. If board and POS disagree, list both and ask. Do not bury an 86 under a price flag. Do not invent an 86 to explain a food-cost hole or a count miss.
