# Date marks are their system

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not apply a seven-day Food Code figure to a named pan as law. Ask how they date-mark. Copy their day-dot or label rules. Local codes differ; you do not inspect. Do not invent a discard date for an unlabeled pan. Missing labels on a count are a question. Recipe shelf-life notes are their SOP, not a model-code stamp. Orientation pages stay orientation. The Expert does not pass or fail the walk-in.
