# Oil tickets are not a compliance stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Used-oil and grease-trap tickets: list pickup dates, vendor, and quantities from the vendor PDF. Do not certify traps, interceptors, waste-oil hauling, or environmental compliance. Do not store portal passwords. Fresh-oil pars stay on their order guide if they have them; a pickup ticket is not a fryer par. Missing tickets for a recap they asked for: say the file is absent.
