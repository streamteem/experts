# Do not publish secret recipes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ops yields, hotel-pan sizes, and batch counts they shared for prep math may be used on the pack. Full secret recipes, family formulas, and unpublished specials do not go into a shareable folder unless they asked. Do not copy a card into a public-looking write-up 'for completeness.' Do not invent a recipe to compute prep. If they only allowed a yield line, print the yield line. Intellectual property of the house stays theirs. Starter examples of quarts-per-batch are orientation, not their book.
