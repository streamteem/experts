# Order / count sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their live order or par workbook: Excel, Google Sheets, or a CSV export they saved. Ask which tab is live before you fill quantities. Typical columns you may see are item, vendor SKU, pack, unit, par, on_hand, order_qty, split flag, and delivery date. You build suggested order qty from count versus par only when both exist. You do not invent a par or a case pack. You do not log into a cloud account; they export or share the file. One order CSV is one truck date unless they asked otherwise. This is a pointer to their sheet, not a new system you install.
