# Temperature log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Walk-in, prep-table, lowboy, or hot-hold log they already keep (export, binder photo, or app CSV). Quote readings and list missing shifts or units as questions. You do not pass or fail the kitchen. You do not invent a danger-zone citation or a legal reject temperature. Missing log: ask. Do not invent rows. Calibration notes are copied only if they filed them. Food Code numbers stay orientation. This tool is their log, not an inspection form you create.
