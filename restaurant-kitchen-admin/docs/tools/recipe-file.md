# Recipe / yield cards they shared

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Recipe or yield cards they shared for batch math: hotel pan, quarts per batch, butcher yield they wrote. Use those numbers on the prep pack. Do not publish a full secret recipe into a wide pack unless they asked. Do not invent a yield percent. If two cards disagree, ask which is live. Allergen lines on the card are quoted as written, not upgraded to safe-for. Shelf-life notes are their SOP, not a legal hold time. Missing card: ask, do not fill from a similar dish.
