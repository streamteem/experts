# Count sheet / walk-in map

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The walk-in or station count sheet they walk with, including a shelf map if they have one. Keep their units (each, case, pan, quart, pound). Do not invent rows or quantities. Partial counts stay labeled partial. Open versus close timestamps stay as written; ask which count feeds the order. Theoretical POS on-hand is not this sheet unless they taught that. You do not guess from sales. Follow their map so cases are not double-counted. This is their clipboard file, not a count you invent.
