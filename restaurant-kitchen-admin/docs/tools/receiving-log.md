# Receiving log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Receiving log or marked invoice: incoming shorts, temps they recorded, damage notes, and who signed. Feeds the credit-question list with the invoice PDF. You do not invent a legal reject temperature or a USDA grade fail. You do not reject the truck in the write-up. They send the credit. If they never log receiving and only keep invoices, say the pack is invoice-only. Catch-weight scale notes stay next to the line. This is their dock file, not a compliance stamp.
