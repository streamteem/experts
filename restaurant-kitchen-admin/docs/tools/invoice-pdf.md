# Vendor invoice PDF or CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor invoice PDFs or CSVs they saved from email or the portal. Quote item or SKU, quantity, pack, unit price, catch weight, and extended price. Match to the order and to receiving notes if those exist. Shorts, subs, and price jumps are questions. You do not pay, invent a credit amount, or mark the ticket paid. Keep account secrets out. One pack is one ticket unless they asked to index a week. Footer fees are copied from that invoice. Alcohol lines still do not get license advice.
