# Waste log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Waste sheet, binder, or app export they already keep. Use their reason codes (spoil, misfire, over-prep, expire, drop) and their units. Date range is the dates they named. Do not invent rows to explain a food-cost variance. Do not write theft. Patterns are questions for pars. Compost or oil tickets are not this log unless they taught that mapping. Missing log: ask. Staff meal and donation stay on those files if separate. This is their file, not a new app you add.
