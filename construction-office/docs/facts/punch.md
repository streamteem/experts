# Punch list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closeout punch items typically name room or grid, the defect, who owns the fix, and done yes or no with a date. CSI-style punch forms exist; many shops use a spreadsheet, a punch app export, or a marked PDF. Do not mark an item complete unless their sheet says so. Do not invent rooms, unit numbers, or deficiencies from a photo you captioned. Owner punch, contractor pre-punch, and inspection punch are often separate lists; keep the list they named. Reopen versus still open is their status if they use it. A photo can support an item they already listed; a photo alone is not a new item you add. Do not assign a sub as owner unless their column already names that company. Dates you compare for aging come from their due or created column, not from a guess. Substantial-completion punch remaining after a certificate still comes from their list. Their columns win, including informal handwritten cards they asked you to type.
