# OAC or progress meeting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Owner-architect-contractor meetings usually have an agenda, last-week actions, RFI and change status, and schedule talk. Industry notes often say change and RFI status should be discussed regularly. Minutes they approve are the record. You draft from their notes only if they asked. You do not speak for the owner, assign new work, or promise a date. Some remodelers have a short weekly call with a notepad; some GCs use a standing template. Use their template. Attendee lists come from their sign-in or the draft they marked. Do not add an attendee you inferred from an email. Action items stay in the meeting-action file with their owners and dates. You do not send the minutes to the distribution list; they send. If last week's minutes are unsigned, say unsigned if they track approval. This pack is a file set, not the meeting itself. Never invent a decision that is not in their notes.
