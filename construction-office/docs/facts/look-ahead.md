# Look-ahead crew plan

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A short look-ahead lists who is planned where in the next few days or weeks so the office and trades can see the next window. It is a plan, not a promise that the work happened and not a staffing order you issue. Separate planned versus actual. Do not staff a job from this Expert, call a sub to mobilize, or change crew sizes. Their window length, often one or three weeks, wins. Activities come from their schedule export or from a superintendent sheet they already use. Do not add an activity because it is common at this phase. If the look-ahead and the longer schedule disagree, quote both and ask which is current for the pack. Weather and inspections they listed as constraints are copied, not managed. You do not claim a critical path. A look-ahead pack should keep their activity names even when they are informal. Actual work stays on the daily log.
