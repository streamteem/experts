# Schedule update file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many general contractors keep a look-ahead of one or three weeks plus a longer update so the office and trades share one dated plan. The export or PDF they provide is the source. You list activity names and dates as shown. You do not re-sequence the job, claim a critical path, or say an activity is late as a contract conclusion. If two updates disagree, quote both dates and ask which is current. Some remodelers use a whiteboard photo; some GCs export from scheduling software. Either is a file if they pointed to it. Baseline versus update labels stay in their words. You do not add logic ties or float opinions. A look-ahead is a plan, not proof the work happened; keep planned versus actual in separate columns when they have both. Weather and manpower notes belong on the daily log, not as schedule edits you make. Missing activity IDs: copy the name they printed. This desk does not own the project schedule.
