# Areas, floors, and phases

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Larger jobs split logs by building, floor, or phase so manpower and punch do not mix two areas into one story. Keep their area codes, even when they are ugly abbreviations. Do not merge two phases into one daily file unless they asked. Do not rename Building A to a prettier label. A remodeler with one house usually has no area split; do not invent wings. Some GCs use grid lines, unit numbers, or lot numbers in a subdivision. Use their key. If a delivery or punch item has no area and their template requires one, say blank and ask. Photos should keep the same codes when they index by area. Schedule activities may use a different code set; do not silently translate. You do not decide which phase is the critical one. Their map or legend, if they filed it, is the decoder. One job number can still have many area rows. Do not mix two job numbers to make a campus look unified.
