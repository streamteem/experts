# Closeout document list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical closeout checklists include operation-and-maintenance manuals, as-builts, warranties, attic-stock counts, keys, extra paint, and training sign-in. Use their list, not a generic internet closeout package. Do not mark a manual received unless the file is in the folder they named. As-builts you index are not stamped record drawings unless they said so. Some remodelers close with a short punch and a warranty packet; some GCs have a specification-section matrix. Keep their matrix. Extra materials counted on a sheet are copied, not recounted by you as the estimator. Training dates come from their sign-in. You do not declare closeout complete or start warranties. Missing items stay missing with an ask. Keys and access devices are listed as counts they wrote; do not store codes in this folder. Owner training videos, if they filed them, are filenames. This pack is presence and absence, not a legal handover.
