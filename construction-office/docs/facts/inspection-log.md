# Inspections

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Building, fire, and special inspections are often logged with type, inspector, result, and a report number so the office can see what passed or failed. Copy the result from their card, portal printout, or PDF. You do not pass or fail work. A failed inspection stays failed until their file shows a reinspection. Special inspections, municipal inspections, and owner quality walks are different lists if they split them; keep the split. Do not invent a result from a photo of a sticker that is unreadable. Permit-card initials, if they photograph the card, are copied as they appear. You do not schedule the inspector or say the work is ready. Some remodelers only have a passed text from the superintendent; if that is their file, quote it and still do not upgrade it to an official result. Missing report number: say missing. This desk does not speak for the building department.
