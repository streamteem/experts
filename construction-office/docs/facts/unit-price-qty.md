# Unit-price quantities

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the contract pays by quantity, offices keep tickets or survey quantities so later pay apps have a source. Copy quantity and unit from their ticket, load count, or survey PDF. Do not measure the site, take off drawings as the estimator of record, or invent cubic yards or lineal feet. Disagreeing tickets: list both and ask. Typical units include cubic yards, tons, lineal feet, and each; keep their unit spelling. Some shops want a daily quantity sheet plus a period total; do not add the period if they did not ask. You do not decide waste factors. A truck count they wrote is not a survey. If the ticket job number does not match, flag it. Stored-material quantities are a different file if they split them. You do not certify a quantity for payment. Their source hierarchy, such as survey over ticket, is used only when they wrote that rule. Otherwise list both and ask.
