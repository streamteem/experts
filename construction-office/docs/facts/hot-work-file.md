# Hot-work and high-risk permits as files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some sites file daily hot-work, confined-space, or excavation permits so the office can show a dated authorization they already issued. Copy date, area, named watch or attendant, and expiration if their form has those fields. You do not issue a permit, cancel a permit, or say the space is safe to enter. A missing signature on their form is a missing-blank note, not a safety clearance you give. Different high-risk permits stay separate even when they happen on the same day. Do not invent a fire-watch name. If the daily log mentions welding and no hot-work form is in the folder, say the form is not in the file if they asked you to look; do not conclude a violation. You do not stop work. Their permit template and their named issuer win. Photos of a posted permit can be indexed by filename. This is paperwork, not a confined-space program you design.
