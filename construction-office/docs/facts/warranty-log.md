# Warranty log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List product, installer, and warranty start only from their closeout sheet or manufacturer PDF. You do not start a warranty, decide a claim, or tell an owner the clock began. Missing start date: ask. Substantial-completion dates on a certificate are not automatically the warranty start unless their sheet says so. Manufacturer versus installer warranties are different rows if they split them. Do not invent a duration from a typical one-year custom. Extended warranties, if a PDF is in the folder, are indexed with the filename. You do not contact the manufacturer. A punch item that is also a warranty callback stays on both lists only if they already linked them. Do not promise a repair. Serial numbers come from their sheet or the O&M file, not from a guess. This is an index, not claims handling. Their columns, including void or not received, stay as written.
