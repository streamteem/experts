# Toolbox or stretch-and-flex note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many contractors note the day's safety talk topic and who led it so the project file shows a dated toolbox or stretch-and-flex entry. That note is not OSHA legal advice, not a training certificate, and not proof that a person is a competent person. Copy the topic, date, leader, and attendee list only from their form or sign-in sheet. Do not write that a crew is trained, certified, or competent because a talk happened. New-hire site orientation, if they track it, is a separate roster they own; do not merge it into the daily talk line. Topics vary by shop and by trade on site that day. If the form is blank, say so. Do not invent a topic from a poster on the wall or from last week's talk. You do not pick tomorrow's topic, grade the talk, or say the program meets a standard. If they attach a one-page outline, index the filename; do not rewrite the outline as if you taught it.
