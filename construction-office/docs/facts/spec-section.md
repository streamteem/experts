# Spec section numbers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US projects number specifications with CSI MasterFormat, for example Division 03 concrete or Division 26 electrical. Use the section number on their submittal, RFI, or table of contents. Do not invent a section to file a product because the name sounds like the right division. If they use an older numbering style, a custom office code, or no numbers at all, follow their sheet. Product data often prints a section on the transmittal; copy that number even if you would have filed it elsewhere. Substitutions, if they track them, still keep the specified section as they wrote it. Do not rewrite a specification. A brochure without a section number is listed with the filename and an ask, not a guessed 08 71 00. Closeout manuals are often indexed by the same section numbers; keep that consistency only when their closeout list uses it. Their project manual wins over a public MasterFormat example in this folder.
