# Photo log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dated photos support the daily story, a delivery, or a punch item when the office can find them later. Typical index fields are date, location or grid, filename, and a one-line caption from them. Do not invent captions that claim defects, completion, or safety violations. Avoid faces and license plates if they asked for a public pack. Do not copy driver licenses or medical details into this folder. A photo is not a daily log by itself and is not an inspection pass. Some shops want a CSV index; some only want a dated folder. Use their rule. Duplicate shots can be listed once if they said to dedupe by filename, not by your taste. You do not fly a drone or take new pictures. EXIF dates, if they asked you to read them, are quoted as metadata, not as the official date when their caption differs; ask which wins. Crop if they asked. Their job number belongs in the index when they use that column.
