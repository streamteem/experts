# As-built markups

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Field redlines show what was installed. They are not a professional-engineer record drawing and they are not a sealed survey. File what they scanned, including messy clouds and incomplete notes. Do not redraw, clean, or seal. If a clouded area has no note, say the markup is incomplete. Issued-for-construction sheets with field marks are still markups until they labeled a set as record drawings. Some shops keep trade as-builts separately; do not merge electrical and plumbing into one invented set. You do not resolve a conflict between the redline and a shop drawing. Dates on the scan are copied. A contractor as-built is not an architect's record set unless they stored that later set and named it. Never say the as-built is code-compliant or stamped. Their filename and revision note win. Photos of a wall are not as-builts unless they asked you to index photos as backup to a listed markup.
