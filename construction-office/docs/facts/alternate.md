# Alternates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bid alternates accepted or rejected belong in their contract, bid form, or later change order. List status from that file. Do not assume an alternate was taken because it appears on a drawing cloud or in a submittal. Add-alternate and deduct-alternate labels stay in their words. Some shops convert accepted alternates into change-order numbers; some fold them into the original contract sum. Copy the path they used. You do not price an alternate. If the drawing set still shows both options, list the sheet revision and the contract status and ask which governs the pack. You do not tell the field which option to build. Rejected alternates can still appear in old files; do not delete them. Their bid-form line numbers, if present, are the keys. This is a status index, not a scope opinion. Missing acceptance paper: say the status file is not in the folder.
