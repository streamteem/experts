# Action items from minutes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Progress minutes often end with who, what, and when. Extract only numbered actions they recorded. Do not assign new owners, invent due dates, or add an action you think was implied. Overdue is a date compare against their due column they can correct; you do not scold a party. If last week's action is missing from this week's draft, say it is not on the new list rather than secretly carrying it. Owner, architect, and contractor actions stay in their columns. You do not send reminders to people. Some shops number actions across the job; some restart each meeting. Keep their numbers. A discussion note is not an action unless they numbered it. This file feeds the progress-meeting pack and should quote the minutes filename. Never speak for the owner in the action text. If two versions of minutes disagree on an owner, quote both and ask which is approved.
