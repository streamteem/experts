# Retainage on a pay app

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many contracts hold a percent of billed work until closeout or a stated date. Copy retainage held, previously held, and released amounts only from their pay-application file or stored schedule of values. Do not calculate a release, change the percent, or say funds are due. Payment is out of scope for this desk. Some jobs reduce retainage after a milestone; list the percent they printed, not a custom you assume is typical. Subcontractor retainage may differ from owner retainage; keep the columns they used. Do not tell a sub retainage will be paid this cycle. If the continuation sheet is missing the retainage line, say which page is absent. You do not give prompt-payment or lien advice about held funds. A spreadsheet they use instead of a standard form is still the source if they pointed to it. Never move money or preview a check. Flag blanks rather than filling a percent from another job.
