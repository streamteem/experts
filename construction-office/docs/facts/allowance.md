# Allowances

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Allowances are buckets in the contract for later owner choices such as finishes or fixtures. Log draws, remaining amounts, and related change or pay-app lines only from their change-order or pay-application file. You do not rebalance an allowance, move money between allowances, or say a selection is covered. If they have an allowance log, use those columns. A showroom selection email is not a draw until they filed the priced change or the pay-app line they use. Do not invent an original allowance amount from memory of a typical kitchen. Over-and-under is their math on their sheet; if the sheet is missing, say missing rather than computing. You do not tell the owner a selection is affordable. Alternate versus allowance is a distinction you keep if their file uses both words. This desk does not pay a vendor from an allowance. Quote the description they printed, even when it is vague, and ask rather than filling a product name.
