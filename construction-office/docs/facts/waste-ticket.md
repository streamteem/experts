# Haul and dump tickets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dump tickets and manifests, when they keep them, have date, quantity, and facility so haul can be matched to the job. Index from the PDF or photo of the ticket. You do not classify hazardous waste, sign a manifest, or say a load is nonhazardous. Missing facility name: say missing. Some remodelers keep a monthly dumpster invoice instead of per-load tickets; use what they filed. Do not invent tons from a photo of a truck. Hazardous, universal, and construction-debris labels stay in their words if they used them; you do not pick a waste code. If the ticket job number does not match, flag it. You do not schedule a haul. Scale tickets and landfill receipts are different pages; list what is present. This desk does not give environmental-law advice. Their project number belongs on the index when they use that column. Never complete an e-manifest.
