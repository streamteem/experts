# RFI log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An RFI is a written question to design or owner when the documents are unclear or conflict. Typical log columns are number, date sent, specification or drawing reference, question in one line, who owns the answer, due date, date returned, and whether cost or time is flagged. You log status from their software export or spreadsheet. You do not answer as the architect, pick a detail, or tell the field which product to install. CSI and AIA-oriented practice treat an RFI as clarification, not a change document by itself. Numbering schemes vary: some shops use a single sequence, some prefix by trade or spec section. Use their numbers; do not renumber to look tidy. If the question text in the export is truncated, say so and point at the PDF. Overdue versus their due column is a date compare they can correct, not a claim. Do not invent a due date from a custom of five days. Closed, void, and superseded statuses stay in their words.
