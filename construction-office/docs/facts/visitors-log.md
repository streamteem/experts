# Visitors and inspectors

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some sites log owner, architect, inspector, vendor, and walk-through guests so the office can later say who was on site and why. Typical columns are name or company, time in and out, purpose, and sometimes a badge number. This is a file they keep for access questions, incident follow-up, and meeting proof. You do not invent a visitor, a time, or a purpose to fill a gap in the story. If they have no visitor sheet, do not create a badge program, a sign-in policy, or a privacy notice. Inspectors often also appear on the inspection log; copy each form as it stands rather than merging them into one invented list. Owner and architect visits may also show up in progress-meeting minutes. If the visitor line is blank, say blank. Do not add a person you saw in a photo caption they did not name. Avoid extra personal data they did not already put on the sheet. Their template wins, including informal notebook scans.
