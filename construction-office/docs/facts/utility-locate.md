# Utility locate ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they file a locate ticket number and expiration, copy those fields so the office can see whether a ticket is still in the folder and still dated. You do not call 811, refresh a ticket, or say the ground is clear. White-line or wait-status notes they wrote are copied, not interpreted as a clearance. Expired date: flag and ask. Some states use different one-call names; use the name on their printout. A daily-log mention of digging is not a locate ticket. Photos of paint marks can be indexed; do not caption them as proof every utility was marked. You do not choose a trench route. If two tickets overlap, list both numbers. Private-utility locates, if they stored a separate report, stay separate from the public ticket. This is not a safe-dig opinion. Their excavation permit, if any, is a different file. Never invent a ticket number to fill a blank on the daily log.
