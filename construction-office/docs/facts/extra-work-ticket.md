# Extra work / T&M ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Time-and-material tickets typically show names, hours, materials, and a signature line so extra work has a same-day paper trail. Missing signature: say so. A signed ticket is still not a change order unless their rule says it is. Do not add hours, crew names, or material quantities. Shops differ on whether a ticket can be billed before a change-order number exists; ask their extra-work rule rather than assuming. Owner or architect signature versus superintendent only is copied as shown, not judged. Do not turn a verbal note into a ticket. If the ticket job number is blank, ask. Photos they attached are filenames. You do not price labor rates or markups. A ticket that also appears on the potential-change register should keep both numbers if they wrote both. This desk does not tell the field to keep working on extra scope. Their form, including informal duplicate books, wins.
