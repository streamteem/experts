# Differing site condition note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the field writes that conditions differ from the documents, log the note and the date so the office can find the remark later. You do not decide if it is a Type I or Type II condition or whether money is owed. Ask them where the photo or survey file lives, and list those filenames if they point to them. Do not diagnose rock, water, or hidden structure as the geotechnical person. A daily-log sentence, a potential-change item, and an RFI can all mention the same event; copy each as filed rather than merging them into one legal theory. Do not invent quantities of rock or unsuitables. Unit-price tickets, if they have them, stay on the quantity file. You do not waive notice or tell the field to proceed with extra excavation. If their form has a differing-site checkbox, copy it. This is not contract-law advice. Their contract clause name, if they wrote it on the note, is quoted, not interpreted.
