# Stored materials note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pay applications sometimes bill materials stored off site or in a locked yard. Typical backup is a photo, bill of sale, or insurance note they filed. List what is in the folder. You do not certify title or insurance, and you do not say stored materials are billable. Missing bill of sale: say missing. Off-site address, if they recorded one, is copied; you do not visit. Some shops never bill stored materials; do not add the column. Quantities on the continuation sheet and quantities on the backup must be quoted as printed; if they differ, list both and ask. You do not move materials between jobs on paper. A delivery ticket to the site is not automatically stored-material backup. Insurance certificates for a warehouse, if present, are indexed, not approved. This desk does not pay the vendor. Their stored-materials exhibit name wins. Never invent a storage location to complete a pay-app story.
