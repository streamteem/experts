# Backcharge file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A backcharge is a cost they intend to send to another party for damage, cleanup, or work another trade did not finish. Log only what their ticket or backcharge form says: date, party, description, and amounts they printed. You do not decide fault, send the bill, or deduct from a sub's pay. Missing backup photos or tickets: say what is absent. Some shops require notice to the other party; if their form has a notice date, copy it, and if it is blank, flag blank. Do not invent hours. A daily-log remark about a mess is not a backcharge until they used their backcharge form or asked you to list that remark as a draft they will finish. You do not negotiate. Payment files stay lists. Their numbering wins. Never present a starter note as how this shop already prices backcharges. If two parties' notes disagree, quote both files and stop.
