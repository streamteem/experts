# Certified payroll as a file they keep

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Public or prevailing-wage jobs may store weekly certified-payroll PDFs. You may list week-ending and whether the file is present. You do not complete certified payroll, decide wage class, or give Davis-Bacon advice. That is their payroll person or advisor. Presence of a weekly PDF is a file check, not a compliance statement. Missing week: say missing. Do not invent hours or classifications to fill a form. Some shops keep these in a payroll folder off the project drive; only index what they pointed to. You do not send the packet to an agency. Owner or agency correspondence about wages, if they filed it, is a separate letter you may list by date, not answer. This desk does not compare rates to a determination. Their project and contract labels on the PDF are copied. Never store payroll logins. Keep employee personal data to the minimum the task needs; do not copy full Social Security numbers into docs.
