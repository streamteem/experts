# Delay and impact log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Offices often keep a dated list of events that stopped or slowed work: weather, late answers, other-trade stacking, utility wait, or owner hold. Record what they wrote, including start and end if those fields exist. Do not invent a delay claim, a concurrency opinion, or a time extension. Notice deadlines are in their contract; you may flag notice date blank when their register has that column empty. A rain note on the daily log is not automatically a line on this register unless they copied it there. Some shops never keep a delay log and only have daily remarks; do not create a claim log they did not ask for. If they asked for a list of events already written on dailies, quote those lines and still do not title the pack as a claim. Photos and RFIs they linked are filenames, not proof you argue. You do not write a notice letter as a lawyer. Their status words, including disputed, stay as written.
