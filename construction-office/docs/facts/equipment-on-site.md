# Equipment on site

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many daily reports list owned or rented machines, hours, and idle or standby time so later rental bills and delay talks have a same-day source. Typical lines include a machine name or number, vendor if rented, hours, and whether the unit worked or sat. Copy names, numbers, and hours from their sheet or from a rental ticket they already filed. Do not guess a crane, pump, or lift was on site because the look-ahead said it might arrive. If a rental ticket exists, point to the ticket number rather than rewriting the vendor invoice or changing hours. Idle time they wrote is not a claim you draft. Some remodelers skip equipment entirely on small interiors; some GCs split owned fleet from rentals. Keep their split. A photo of a machine is not hours. If two tickets disagree, list both numbers and ask which hours belong on the log. You do not decide whether standby is billable.
