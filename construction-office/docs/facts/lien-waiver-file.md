# Lien waiver as a filed PDF

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Offices often collect conditional or unconditional waivers with each pay cycle so the file shows who signed through which date. List who signed, date, through-date, and conditional versus unconditional only from the PDF. You do not give lien-law advice, say a waiver is valid, or say a deadline passed. Do not draft a waiver or pick a state form. Progress versus final is their label if the form uses it. A waiver for a different job number is not this job's file unless they said it is. Missing notary block, if their form has one, is a missing-blank note, not a legal defect you pronounce. Do not collect signatures. If a sub sent a waiver that does not match the pay-app through-date, quote both dates and ask. You do not tell anyone a lien cannot be filed. This is an index of papers they stored, not a title search. Keep extras such as affidavits in the names they used.
