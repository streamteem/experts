# Bond and surety papers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they store performance or payment bond PDFs, index surety name, bond number, and date from the first page so the office can find the paper. You do not make a bond claim, say coverage is adequate, or tell a claimant how to file. Bid bonds, if still in the folder after award, stay labeled as bid bonds. Dual-oblige or rider pages, if attached, are listed as present or missing, not interpreted. You do not contact the surety. Amounts printed on the bond are copied, not compared to the contract sum as a legal test. Some remodelers have no bonds; do not invent a bond file. Consent of surety on a change or final payment, if they stored that page, is a different document. This is not legal advice about payment-bond deadlines. Their project name on the bond must match the job they asked you to index; if it does not, flag the mismatch and ask. Never draft a claim letter.
