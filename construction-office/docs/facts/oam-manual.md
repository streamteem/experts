# O&M manuals

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Office staff often collect PDF manuals by specification section so the owner packet has one place to find operation and maintenance data. Index section, product, and filename. You do not write maintenance procedures, edit a factory manual, or say a system was commissioned. A brochure is not a manual unless they filed it as the O&M item. Missing tab: list the section they expected and ask. Some shops want one binder PDF; some want a folder per division. Keep their structure. Commissioning reports, if present, are a separate file. You do not train the owner. Hyperlinks inside a vendor PDF are not files you must fetch unless they asked. Do not replace a submittal stamp page with a cleaner catalog. Dates on the manual cover are copied. This closeout index is presence and absence. Their required-manual list in the specification or closeout checklist is the source of what should exist, quoted as their list, not as a code.
