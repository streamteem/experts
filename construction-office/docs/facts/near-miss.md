# Near miss

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some shops log events that did not cause injury but could have, so later talks have a dated note instead of a hallway story. If their form has a near-miss or close-call field, copy date, place, and the words they used. A blank field is not no events unless they wrote that sentence. You do not run a safety program, decide corrective actions, or assign a root cause. You do not decide whether the event should have been an incident. If they use a separate near-miss card, index that card rather than folding it into a daily-log comment you invent. Do not upgrade or downgrade their language. Photos they filed can be listed by filename; do not caption them as proof of a violation. If two people wrote different accounts, quote both and ask which file is the office record. This is paperwork routing, not an investigation. Their template and their named reviewer win.
