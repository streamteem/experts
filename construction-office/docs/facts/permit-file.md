# Permit folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List permit numbers, issue and expiration dates, and the job address from documents they stored so the office can see what paper is on the wall and in the folder. You do not apply for permits, pull a permit, or say a permit covers extra work or a different address. Expired date: flag and ask. Trade permits and the master building permit are often separate; keep each number. Some jurisdictions use portal printouts instead of a paper card; a screenshot they filed is still a file. Do not invent a permit number from a job address search you ran. Renewals, if they stored a new page, are a new date, not an edit you make on the old PDF. Inspection cards may live with the permit; index both. You do not decide whether work is illegal to continue. Their job address on the permit must be copied as printed even if it differs from the marketing name. This is an index, not a code opinion.
