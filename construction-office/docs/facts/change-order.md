# Change order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Extra or deleted work that changes price or time is typically captured on a numbered change order after agreement. ConsensusDocs and AIA families both publish change forms; their contract names the form and the approval path. Log number, description, status such as pricing, approved, or void, and amounts only from their file. Do not create a change-order number, and do not say work is billable because someone wrote a field ticket. A signed change order is a document they stored; you do not decide it is enforceable. Zero-dollar changes that only add time still need their form if they used one. Some remodelers use a simple email plus a revised quote; if that is their file, index it as they labeled it, not as an AIA G701 you invent. Do not add markup or recompute the contract sum. If the register and the PDF disagree, quote both and ask. Payment of a change is out of scope for this desk.
