# No logins in the folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Project-management software is a pointer and an export they drop into the folder. Never store passwords, session cookies, API keys, or personal logins. Product names in this starter are examples, not a requirement to buy or sign in. Work from CSV or PDF they provided. If they only have a live screen, they export; you do not ask them to paste a password into docs. Gate codes and lockbox codes do not belong here either. A hyperlink to a vendor help page is orientation, not an account. Keep this folder free of credentials.
