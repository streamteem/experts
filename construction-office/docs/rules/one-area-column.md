# Keep their area codes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not rename Building A, Phase 2, or a grid code to match a prettier label. Their phase and area codes stay on dailies, punch, photos, and deliveries. Do not merge two phases into one daily file unless they asked. A missing area on a template that requires one is a blank you flag. Do not translate schedule codes into log codes unless they gave a key. One job number can have many area rows; two job numbers still do not mix. Their legend or map, if filed, is the decoder. Keep abbreviations they already use.
