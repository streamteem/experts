# Flag verbal extras

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If extra work is only verbal, write that the written change or follow-up is missing. Do not hide it inside a work-in-place sentence or a tidy daily remark. Record who spoke and the words they wrote. You do not turn a verbal note into an approved change, a directive, or a billable extra. Shops differ on tickets before a change number; ask their extra-work rule. Two conflicting quotes both get listed. You do not call the owner to confirm. Their process for same-day confirmation email, if they have one, is a presence check.
