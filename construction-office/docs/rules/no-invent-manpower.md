# No guessed headcount

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the field left manpower blank, ask. Do not copy last Tuesday, the look-ahead, or a badge export from another day. Do not invent a headcount to complete a production story. Keep company and trade splits as they wrote them. A person planned on the look-ahead is not automatically on today's log. Visitors are not crew unless their form puts them there. You do not convert counts into productivity or percent complete. Their abbreviations win. Blank stays blank until they provide the note or export for that date and job.
