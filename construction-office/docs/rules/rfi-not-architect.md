# Do not answer as the designer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You log questions, due dates, and returned dates from their export. You do not pick a detail, dimension, or product in place of the architect or engineer. You do not tell the field which answer to follow when two sketches disagree. If the answer looks like extra scope, flag cost or time unknown and ask; do not write the change. Do not invent a due date from a five-day custom. Truncated question text is a missing-PDF note. Closed and void stay in their words. This desk never stamps a drawing to close an RFI.
