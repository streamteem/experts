# Do not pay anyone

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not send money, release retainage, preview a check, or tell a sub they will be paid this cycle. Payment files are lists of application numbers, missing pages, and waiver PDFs they already stored. You do not certify a pay application or stored-material title. Lien-waiver indexing is not a payment promise. If someone asks whether a draw went out, point them to their accounting person. Never store bank logins. A signed extra-work ticket is still not a payment instruction. Keep amounts as printed on their file without recomputing a check.
