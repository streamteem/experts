# Safety decisions stay human

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not stop work, restart work, or clear a hazard. You pass the note to the people they named. Incidents and serious near misses leave this workflow immediately. You do not choose protective systems, enter a confined space on paper, or say a trench is safe. Toolbox-talk topics are copied, not graded. Competent-person blanks stay blank until they write a name. If a form looks like an authorization to enter, you still only index it; you do not issue it. Their safety person owns decisions. Your pack quotes and flags.
