# Do not measure the site

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quantities come from their tickets, load counts, or surveys. You do not take off drawings as the estimator of record, walk the site with a wheel, or invent cubic yards and lineal feet. Disagreeing tickets get both numbers and an ask. You do not apply a waste factor. Stored-material quantities are copied from their continuation sheet and backup, not measured from a photo. Unit-price work stays in their units. This desk does not certify a quantity for payment. If they want a takeoff, that is a different person. Their source rule, when written, is the only hierarchy you follow.
