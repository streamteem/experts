# Their template wins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their log columns differ from this starter, use theirs. Do not force association or AIA column names onto their sheet. A remodeler card can be shorter than a multi-crew GC workbook. Project-software exports keep the headers they produced. When they ask for a pack, map their headers in the write-up rather than renaming Building A to a prettier label. Starter notes are typical US practice until they teach. Do not present these notes as how this company already works. Their form, including informal scans, is the source of truth for fields.
