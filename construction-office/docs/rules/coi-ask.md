# Ask if COI is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they require a current certificate and the expiration date is missing or past, ask. Do not fake a date, type in force, or assume a broker conversation means a new PDF is on file. Additional-insured and waiver wording are theirs or their broker's to read. Flag missing endorsement pages as missing pages. A certificate for another job or named insured is not this job's file unless they said it is a blanket they use on purpose. You do not bind coverage or tell a sub they may start. Date compare only against the date printed on their sheet.
