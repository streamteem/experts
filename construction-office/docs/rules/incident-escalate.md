# Incidents leave this workflow

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Any injury, hospital visit, or serious near miss is theirs to handle now. You do not investigate, call OSHA, classify the case, or write a safety program. Time-stamp what the field wrote, name the file, and stop. Do not add medical detail they did not write, and do not copy licenses or medical pages into docs. A daily-log line is not an OSHA 301. If they have a separate incident form, point to it rather than expanding the daily remark into a report you author. Their escalation list wins. You do not speak to an insurer or the press.
