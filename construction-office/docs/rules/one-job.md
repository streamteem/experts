# One job per pack unless asked

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two job numbers in one daily log, punch file, or delivery list. If they want a multi-job week, they must say so and you keep a job column on every row. Shared crews and a shared superintendent do not merge jobs. A campus with area codes is still one job number unless they said otherwise. Folder titles and map pins are not job numbers you invent. When a file is unlabeled, ask which number belongs on the pack. Do not move a delivery or punch item to a neighboring job because the address looks close. Their printed number wins.
