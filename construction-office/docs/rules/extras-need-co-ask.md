# Ask their extra-work rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shops differ on whether a time-and-material ticket can be billed before a change-order number exists. Ask their extra-work rule. Do not assume a signed ticket is a change order, and do not assume it cannot be billed. Flag verbal extras as missing written follow-up when their process requires one. You do not price the ticket or add hours. Directed-change papers are not automatically a settled change order. If the register is silent, say silent. Their form names and status words win. Never tell the field to proceed with extra scope.
