# Minimum needed in photos

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keep photos to what the pack needs: date, location, filename, and their caption. Do not copy driver licenses, medical details, or unnecessary faces into docs. Crop if they asked. Avoid license plates on a public pack if they said so. You do not invent defect or completion language in a caption. A photo is not an inspection pass or a daily log. EXIF dates are metadata; if they disagree with a caption, ask which date to print. Do not store more personal data than the task requires. Their index CSV, if they want one, is the structure.
