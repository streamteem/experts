# No invented delay claim

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A rain note is not a claim, a time extension, or notice under the contract. Do not draft notice letters as a lawyer or invent lost days, concurrency, or liquidated-damages math. Quote the superintendent's weather block. If weather is blank, say blank; do not fill yesterday's forecast from a website unless they asked you to attach a printout they already filed. Delay-log lines exist only when they already keep that register or asked you to list events they wrote. You do not decide excusable delay. Their contract language stays unread as legal advice.
