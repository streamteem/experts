# No seal claim

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A PDF with a seal image is still their licensed professional's act. You do not reproduce, trace, or validate a seal, and you do not say the file is stamped-approved for construction. Shop drawings and as-built markups are not record drawings you seal. If the file says not for construction, keep that label. You do not add a stamp block. When someone asks if engineering signed off, quote the page they filed or say the page is missing. Licensed review stays human. Never claim you are the engineer of record.
