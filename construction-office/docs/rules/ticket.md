# Delivery ticket present or not

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the packing ticket or bill of lading is missing, say so. Do not mark a delivery received from a calendar hold, a look-ahead truck, or a purchase-order promise alone. Short count and damage belong on the receiving note they wrote, not in a smoothed comment. Use their job number on every line. A photo of a truck is not a ticket number. If two tickets disagree on quantity, list both and ask. You do not accept material as the quality person. Scheduled versus received stays labeled. Their receiver name comes from the slip, not from a guess about who was on site.
