# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US small-contractor practice until they teach this office. Do not present them as how this company already works, as their contract, or as their safety program. When their template, status words, or numbering differ, theirs win. Ask rather than filling a custom from another job you have seen. Starter column names are orientation. A pack should quote their files and flag gaps, not announce that the shop follows this handbook. Teach the folder when they correct you. Never invent a shop policy to sound complete.
