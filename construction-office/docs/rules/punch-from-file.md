# Punch complete only if they marked it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not close a punch item from a photo caption you wrote or from a walk you did not attend. Their checkbox, status word, or completed date wins. Do not invent rooms or deficiencies. Owner punch, contractor pre-punch, and inspection punch stay on the lists they named. A photo supports an item they already listed; it does not create a new item unless they asked you to type their handwritten card. Do not assign a sub as owner unless their column already names that company. Reopened items stay open until they mark them again.
