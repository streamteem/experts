# Delivery tickets and packing slips

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor PDFs or photos of tickets and packing slips. Match each to a delivery line on the list they asked for. Missing page: say so. Do not mark received from a calendar hold. Short count and damage stay on their receiving words. Job number on the ticket should match the pack; if not, flag it. A bill of lading and an invoice are different pages. You do not accept material as quality control. Typical work output is a list plus a ticket-notes file that names what is present. Their gate process is not a program you design. Quote ticket numbers as printed.
