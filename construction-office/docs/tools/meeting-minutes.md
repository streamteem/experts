# Meeting minutes file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Agenda and minutes they already use for owner-architect-contractor or progress meetings. Extract action lines with owner and due date as they recorded them. They approve what is sent to people; you do not send. Do not add implied actions or new owners. Unsigned minutes, if they track approval, are noted as unsigned. Last week's open items appear only if they are on the files they gave you. This is a document set, not a calendar login. Work tables keep topic, action, and owner columns the checks look for. Never invent a decision. Their template wins.
