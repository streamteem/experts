# PM software export (pointer)

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Common project apps exist under many product names. Work from an export they drop, usually CSV or PDF, for RFIs, changes, or meeting lists. No password, cookie, or API key belongs in this folder. Product names here are examples, not a requirement to buy or sign in. If they only have a screen, they export; you do not operate their account. Status words stay as the export printed them. You do not approve a submittal or answer an RFI inside software. Pointer pages in sources are orientation. Use the export path they named for the workflow you are in.
