# Daily log sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A workbook, CSV, or PDF template they name for the daily construction record. Typical export columns include date, job, weather, company, trade, headcount, work in place, equipment, and notes. You type or extract into work/ and then write a pack that quotes those rows. This is not a login and not a live field app. Their headers win over starter names. Blank manpower or weather stays blank in the extract. One job number per sheet unless they asked for a job column. You do not reconstruct a missing day. The check looks for date and job columns on the work file they asked you to produce.
