# Sub and COI roster

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A sheet of company, trade, contact, and certificate dates if they track them. Insurance status is a date compare against the printed expiration, not a legal call that coverage exists. You do not invent a license or an approved flag. A company on last week's daily is not automatically on this roster. Endorsement pages, if they store them, are a presence note. This is not a subcontract and not a payment list unless they already have those columns. Work files keep their headers. Flag missing or past dates; do not type a new date. Their roster path wins.
