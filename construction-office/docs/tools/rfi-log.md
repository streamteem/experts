# RFI register

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A spreadsheet or project-software export with number, date, reference, question line, status, due, and returned. You age against their due column and list open versus returned. You do not write the design answer, pick a detail, or invent a due date. Truncated text means you point at the PDF they filed. Cost or time flags are copied, not decided. Numbering stays theirs. This tool is a file, not a login. The work extract should keep their status words so the pack can quote them. Overdue is a date compare they can correct, not a claim you author.
