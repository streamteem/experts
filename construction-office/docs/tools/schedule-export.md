# Schedule or look-ahead export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A PDF or CSV of activities and dates, often a look-ahead plus or minus a longer update. You list names and dates as shown. You do not re-schedule the job, add logic, or claim a critical path. Planned versus actual stays labeled. If two exports disagree, quote both and ask which is current. Whiteboard photos they filed can be a source if they pointed to them. This is not scheduling software you log into. Work extracts keep activity and date columns for the check. You do not staff from the look-ahead. Their window length and activity names win.
