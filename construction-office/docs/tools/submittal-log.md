# Submittal register

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A log of product data, samples, and shop drawings by specification section and status. Status is copied from their stamp or export, not decided. Typical columns include number, section, description, sub, dates, reviewer, and return. You do not approve, reject, or change for review to approved. Resubmittal suffixes stay as they used them. Missing section numbers are asks, not guessed MasterFormat codes. Closeout submittals stay split if they split them. This is a register file, not a reviewer seat. Aging uses their date columns. The pack names the export they provided.
