# Photo folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dated images with a simple index they want, often a CSV of date, location, filename, and caption. Captions come from them, not invented defect language. Crop or omit faces and plates if they asked. Do not copy licenses or medical details into docs. A photo supports a daily, delivery, punch, or closeout line; it does not replace those files. EXIF versus caption dates: ask which to print when they differ. This is a folder pointer, not a drone program. Duplicate filenames stay as stored. Their job number belongs on the index when they use that column.
