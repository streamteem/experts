# Punch list sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A spreadsheet, app export, or card with room or grid, item, owner, done, and date. Their columns win, including informal handwritten lists they asked you to type. You do not mark complete from a photo caption. Do not invent rooms. Closeout checklists that reuse this tool still only record presence they asked for, not a stamp. Owner versus contractor lists stay separate if they split them. Work CSV keeps room and item when that is the check. You do not assign a new owner. Aging uses their dates. The pack quotes their status words only.
