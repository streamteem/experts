# Do not pay the crew

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not send money, run payroll, or pay a sub. You do not invent a crew wage. Timesheets they stored may be listed; they are not a payment. Fuel cards and vendor dumps are not bills you pay. Hand hours and tickets to them. Never store bank logins or card numbers in this folder. They pay. Sub crews are the same rule: list the ticket, do not pay, do not invent a day rate. Fuel and dump tickets are not remittances you send.
