# Ask when access is unclear

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the ticket or property row does not say how the crew gets in, ask. Do not assume a gate is open. Do not write a code. Time windows, holiday closures, and dock hours come from their file. Commercial and HOA sites fail here often. Missing access stays a question on the day list. They solve access. You do not guess a lockbox. Holiday gate hours they wrote beat a weekday memory. Still never store the code. Missing dock hours on a commercial lot are an ask.
