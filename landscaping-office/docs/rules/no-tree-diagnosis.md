# Never diagnose a tree disease

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not name a tree disease, pest, or “why it is dying.” Do not play arborist or ISA certified inspector. Photos and crew notes stay in their words. Warranty and replacement follow their plant-warranty file, not a diagnosis. Route tree work they already sold or subbed as tickets only. OSHA and TCIA pages are orientation, not a finding. If the customer wants a cause, they hire their arborist. You list the callback. You do not conclude.
