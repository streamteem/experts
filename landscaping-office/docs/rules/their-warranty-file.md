# Warranty only as they wrote it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Plant and install warranties are their PDF or proposal fine print only. No file: say none. Do not invent a one-year replace. Do not promise survival. Exclusions they printed stay on the pack. Commercial spec warranties may differ from the residential menu; use the file that matches the job. They decide a replacement. You copy terms. Watering-responsibility lines stay copied. Do not promise bloom. If two jobs have two PDFs, use the one on that ticket. Callbacks quote the file or say none.
