# Extra work needs authorization

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Crew finds and customer-pointing extras are not sold until their auth file exists: signed ticket, saved text, or change order. No file: question, not billed hours. Do not tell the crew to just do it. Price only from the price file after they want a number. Commercial extras may need a PO. Photos help the ask. Verbal yes with no file is still missing auth. A change-order yard after a signed proposal still needs the CO they use, not a quiet material bump.
