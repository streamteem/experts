# Do not invent a snow rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Snow prices, triggers, and per-push or per-season numbers come from their snow contract or price file. No snow file: say they have no snow desk in this folder. Do not invent a rate because the forecast is white. Ice-melt product stays as they labeled it, not a new mix. You pack their event list after they call the storm. They dispatch. Trigger depth and per-season versus per-push stay as written. No file means no snow pack, not a guessed push price. They call the event.
