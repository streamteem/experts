# Prices from their price file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit prices on estimates, extras, and invoice line drafts come from the price file, menu, or dated quote they stored. Missing or expired book: ask. Do not invent a yard of mulch, a lighting fixture, or a trip charge. Do not use last year’s memory. Extended math is quantity they listed times unit price they listed. They send the proposal. You do not back into a price from a wage.
