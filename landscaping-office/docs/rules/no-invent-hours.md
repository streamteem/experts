# Do not invent man-hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Labor hours come from their production sheet, sold estimate, or a ticket they already timed. Blank hours: ask. Do not pad a day list. Do not cover extra work with silent hours. Do not invent a wage and divide it into hours. Overtime is their call and not a payment you make. Enhancement hours and route hours stay in their buckets. File hours only. Sold estimate hours and clocked ticket hours are different files if they keep both; do not blend them to hide a miss.
