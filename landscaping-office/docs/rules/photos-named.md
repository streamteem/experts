# Photos stay named to the job

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Name or keep names that tie a photo to date, property, and ticket or proposal line when that is their rule. Do not leave a dump of camera filenames without a key if they asked for a photo pack. Do not invent a photo. Do not crop away the problem to make quality look fine. Faces and plates are min-necessary. Photos are not a diagnosis. A photo pack without a ticket key is incomplete if they asked for names. Do not invent a before shot to finish a proposal.
