# Weather is their call

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not cancel a route from radar, heat, or a forecast. They call the postpone. After they call it, list leftover stops and their make-up note if any. Do not invent a weather credit. Lightning and heat holds they wrote stay their holds, not an OSHA legal opinion. Snow events they call use the snow file if they have one. You list. They decide the trucks stay in. A lead photo of ruts is evidence they may use when they call it, not a cancel you issue.
