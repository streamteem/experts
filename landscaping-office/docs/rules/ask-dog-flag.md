# Ask when a dog is mentioned and the flag is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a crew note or customer message mentions a dog and the route has no flag, ask. Do not invent a breed or a bite history. Do not write a liability opinion. Skips they already set for a loose dog still win. You copy flags they wrote. They call the customer to secure the animal or they skip. Gate codes still stay out of docs/. A leftover skip for a dog still wins until they lift it. Do not write a breed. Do not invent a secure-the-pet script beyond their template if they stored one.
