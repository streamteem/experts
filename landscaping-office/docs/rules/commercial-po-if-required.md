# Ask for the commercial PO when their rule requires it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their property file or rule says a customer PO is required, do not treat the enhancement or monthly pack as complete without that number. Do not invent a PO. Residential jobs usually have none; do not invent one there either. Change orders may need a new PO. Show present or missing. They chase the site manager. A last-month PO is not this month’s number unless they said reuse. Change orders may need a new PO. Ask rather than invent.
