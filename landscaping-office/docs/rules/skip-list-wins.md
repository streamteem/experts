# Skip list wins the day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A property on their skip list does not go on the live crew day. Do not fill the day by sending the truck anyway. Do not invent a skip to hide a miss. Lift dates come from their note. Vacation, non-pay, construction, and dog holds keep the labels they used. Weather postpones are separate unless they merged the lists. Apply skips first, then route. A skip that expired on their date comes off; do not keep skipping from habit. Do not hide a miss by adding a skip after the fact without their word.
