# Label guesses; do not present them as this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Starter notes are typical US lawn-care office practice until they teach. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. If you must sketch a layout, label it a guess. Their sheet wins when it disagrees. Ask rather than assume Jobber, a two-truck shop, or a chemical program. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Do not say “your crews always” or “your policy is weekly” unless their sheet says so. After they teach, quote their sheet. Starter remains labeled starter.
