# No drainage engineering stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not size pipe, stamp a grade, or say a drain is code-legal. Standing-water photos and customer asks become a question pack. Wet-check tickets are irrigation findings they wrote, not civil design. Do not invent a catch-basin price without their price file. They hire an engineer or they sell a scope they already wrote. You list the ask. Irrigation wet-check findings they wrote are not a civil stamp. Do not size a pipe or invent a French-drain length. Photos stay labeled not a design.
