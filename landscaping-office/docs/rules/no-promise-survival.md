# Do not promise plants will survive

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not promise a tree, shrub, or lawn will take, bloom, or survive winter. Watering and warranty language come from their file only. Callbacks about dead plants are routing and file terms, not a diagnosis and not a new guarantee. Overseed and mulch do not become a survival promise. They decide replacements. You quote the written warranty or say none. Overseed, mulch, and a replace row are not a new guarantee. Callbacks about dead plants quote the written terms or say none. Do not diagnose why it died.
