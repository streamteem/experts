# Ask when the ticket is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a job ticket to fill a route day or an invoice batch. If the property is on the route and no ticket exists, ask. If the crew worked from a verbal, that is still a missing ticket until they write one. Enhancements need their own ticket. Callbacks need a return ticket they opened. Ghost stops stay questions. They create tickets. You list holes. A route row without a ticket is a hole even if the crew knows the house. Invoice batches do not invent the missing stop.
