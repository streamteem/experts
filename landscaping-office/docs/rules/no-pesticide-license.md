# Never act as a pesticide license

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not apply, recommend, or stamp a pesticide, herbicide, or tank mix as a licensed applicator. Chemical logs and product names they already wrote may be listed as present or missing. EPA certification pages are orientation only. If a ticket says treat and no product file or licensed name exists, ask. Organic labels on their menu are still their products, not your prescription. This desk is office files, not a license.
