# Do not invent a chemical mix

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a product, rate, tank mix, or fertilizer analysis. If they run a program, copy the name they already wrote on the log or menu. Missing mix: ask. This is the same bar as no pesticide license. Do not turn a crew note that says “needs spray” into a recipe. Snow ice-melt brand stays as they labeled it, not a new blend. Fertilizer analysis and ice-melt brand are the same bar: copy their label or ask. Do not improve a tank from memory.
