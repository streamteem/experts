# One property per row

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Crew-day, route, skip, and material packs stay readable: one property or one ticket per row unless they asked for a roll-up and you still keep a detail line. Do not merge two addresses because they share a customer. Do not hide a skip inside a combined stop. Commercial campuses use the site rows they already split. Sequence stays their sequence. A campus with three buildings they already split stays three rows. Materials for two tickets on one trailer still show two property keys.
