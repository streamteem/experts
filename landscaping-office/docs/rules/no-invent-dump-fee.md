# Do not invent a dump fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dump and transfer fees come from their price file, vendor ticket, or contract include-note. Blank: ask. Do not guess a per-load dollar. Do not assume the city site is free. If the contract says included, do not add a line. Weight tickets they scanned are evidence, not a payment you make. Hazardous-disposal law is not your opinion. Mixed dirt versus green waste may be different tickets on their vendor file; copy the type they used. You do not pay the dump.
