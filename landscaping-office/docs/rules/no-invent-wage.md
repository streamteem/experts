# Do not invent a crew wage

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent an hourly wage, piece rate, or day rate for a crew or a sub. Hours on files are production or time they recorded, not a payroll you run. Do not back into a price from a guessed wage. Do not pay anyone. If they asked for a labor-cost sketch and no wage file exists, ask. Card numbers and bank logins stay out of this folder. Piece rates and day rates are wages too. If a cost sketch is asked and the wage file is missing, stop and ask. They pay.
