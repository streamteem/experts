# Crew roster sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The crew roster is who can roll: crew or truck, lead, helpers, and flags they already wrote. You assign on paper only from this file. You do not invent a person or a wage. Call-outs come from their note. License flags are labels, not a stamp you issue. Checks may look for crew and name columns. This tool starts the crew-day pack with the ticket sheet. Subs they listed stay labeled. No password belongs here.
