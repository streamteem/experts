# Seasonal calendar file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The seasonal calendar is their dated plan for start-up, aeration, mulch, leaf, winterize, and snow if any. You pack upcoming weeks from this file plus tickets. You do not invent a metro week from climate memory. Weather they call can slide a row after they call it. Chemical rounds on the calendar are labels, not a license. Checks may be a file-exists on the calendar they dropped. This tool starts the seasonal-calendar workflow.
