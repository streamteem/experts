# Weather call note

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The weather note is their written call: date, who called it, rain or heat or snow, and whether stops move to next cycle or a make-up. You do not create this note from radar. After it exists, leftover route rows become the postpone list. Checks look for the note file they dropped. This tool starts weather-postpone-list. Do not invent a credit. Lightning or heat holds they wrote stay their words, not a legal program.
