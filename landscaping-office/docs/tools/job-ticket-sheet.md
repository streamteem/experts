# Job ticket sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The job-ticket sheet is their export or workbook of stops: ticket id, property, service, date, and status if they code it. Typical US shops export from a route app or keep a numbered sheet. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a ticket. Checks often look for ticket, property, and service columns. Closed versus open stays as they coded it. This tool feeds crew-day, callback, enhancement, and invoice-line packs. Extra-work rows without auth stay questions. No login is stored here.
