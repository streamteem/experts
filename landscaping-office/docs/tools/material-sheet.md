# Material sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The material sheet is what to pull: SKU or description, qty, unit, and ticket or property. Quantities come from the sold proposal or their takeoff, not a round guess. You do not add a chemical mix. Checks often look for sku and qty. This tool feeds material-pull and trailer lists. Substitutes stay questions. Label quantities by ticket when two jobs share a load. They buy. You list. Empty qty cells stay empty. This sheet is not a chemical log and not a PO you send to the yard supplier.
