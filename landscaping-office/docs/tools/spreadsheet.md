# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build for a pack: crew day, route leftover, estimate lines, or material pull. Columns stay their words plus the check columns the workflow names. You do not invent prices, hours, or tickets into blank cells. One property per row. This tool is the usual last step that writes out/ write-ups and work/ CSVs the checks can see. Export from their files; no login is stored in this folder.
