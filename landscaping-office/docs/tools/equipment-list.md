# Equipment list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The equipment list is units, assigned crew if they track it, and down or ready status. You do not assign a down unit and you do not diagnose a failure. Fuel-log pointers stay as they wrote them. This tool supports crew-day and trailer-load questions. Checks may look for unit and status columns. Rentals they listed are their rows, not a rate you invent. No telematics password belongs here. Down versus ready stays as they coded it. You do not invent a shop-return date or a rental dollar.
