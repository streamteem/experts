# Route CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The route CSV is the ordered stops for a date or a weekday pattern: property, sequence, service, and window if they store it. Typical US mow shops export this nightly. You do not invent a smarter sequence. Skips must still be applied. Checks often look for route and property columns. This tool feeds route packs and weather leftover lists after they call a postpone. One property per row. No GPS-account login is stored here.
