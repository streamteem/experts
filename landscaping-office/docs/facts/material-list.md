# Material list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A material list is what the crew or the yard is supposed to load for a ticket or a day: SKU or description they use, quantity, unit, and which job. Typical US shops pull mulch by the yard, soil, edging, plants by size, lamp fixtures, and sundries from a sheet the estimator or account manager already filled. Quantities come from that sheet or from the sold proposal, not from a pretty round number you chose. Substitutes stay questions. Do not add a pesticide or fertilizer mix as if this desk holds a license. Dump or dumpster needs are a fee ask, not a material you invent a price for. One job per pull-list section unless they asked for a combined trailer load; then still label each quantity by ticket. You assemble the list. They buy and they load. Starter until they teach how *this* shop stages the yard versus buying same-day.
