# Crew roster

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The crew roster is who can be assigned on a given day: crew name or truck, lead, helpers, and sometimes a language or license flag they already recorded. Typical US landscape offices keep a short sheet, a whiteboard photo, or an export from the same system that prints routes. Do not invent a person, a nickname, or a wage. Do not move someone onto a crew because the route looks short. License or applicator flags on the roster are labels they wrote, not a stamp you issue and not permission for this Expert to recommend a chemical. Sub-crews or day labor they listed stay labeled as they coded them. Call-outs and no-shows come from their note, not from a guess that someone “usually” stays home Mondays. A roster is not a timesheet and not a payroll file. You assemble who is listed. They assign and they pay. If the folder has no roster, say so and ask. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names crews and who may lead a truck.
