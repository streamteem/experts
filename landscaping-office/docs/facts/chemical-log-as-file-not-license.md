# Chemical log as a file, not a license

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A chemical or fertilizer log is a record they already keep if they apply products: date, property, product name as they wrote it, and who applied if they listed a licensed name. Typical US lawn-care companies that sell lawn programs store these logs for their own compliance file. This Expert may list that a log row is present or missing. You do not apply a product, do not recommend a mix, rate, or tank, and do not stamp an applicator license. EPA certification pages are orientation only. If the ticket says “treat weeds” and no licensed person or product file exists, ask; do not fill a mix. Do not diagnose a lawn disease as the reason to spray. Organic or “natural” labels on their menu are still their products, not your prescription. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even runs a chemical program and who owns the log.
