# Dog flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A dog flag is their note that a property has a dog that matters to the crew: inside on mow day, backyard only, bite history they recorded, or “do not enter if loose.” Typical US lawn-care injuries and callbacks include dog issues; offices put a column on the route. Ask if the flag is missing and the crew or customer mentioned a dog. Do not invent a breed or a temperament. Do not write a veterinary or liability legal opinion. The skip list may already hold the stop until the dog is in; the skip wins. Photos of a loose dog they stored support a postpone they call, not a confrontation script you invent. Gate and dog flags often travel together; still no codes in docs/. You copy the flag. They decide to skip or to require the customer to secure the animal. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks dogs and who calls the customer.
