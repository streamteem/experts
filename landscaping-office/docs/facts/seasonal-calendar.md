# Seasonal calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The seasonal calendar is their file of when recurring extras are supposed to hit: spring start-up, aeration and overseed windows, mulch weeks, leaf rounds, winterization, and snow if they offer it. Typical US shops in the Midwest and Northeast live on this calendar; Sun Belt shops still date start-up and monsoon or hurricane holds. Dates come from their calendar file, not from a climate memory or a magazine week. A calendar row is a plan, not a promise that the crew finished. Weather they call can slide a week; you list the slide after they call it. Chemical or fertilizer rounds on the calendar are schedule labels they wrote — not a mix you recommend and not a license. If the calendar and the route disagree, quote both. You pack the upcoming weeks from their file. They decide to pull a week forward. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates leaf and aeration in their metro.
