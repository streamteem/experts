# Weather postpone

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A weather postpone is their call that a route day or a stop will not run because of rain, lightning, heat, snow, or ground that is too wet to mow without rutting. Typical US lawn-care offices decide before the trucks leave or mid-day from the lead’s photo. You do not declare the metro “too wet” from a radar app and cancel the board. After they call it, list the postponed properties, the original date, and whether their note says move to next cycle or add a make-up day. Lightning and heat holds that they wrote on a safety file stay as their hold, not an OSHA legal opinion. Snow is a different route if they have one. Customers still need their message; you draft a list, they send. Do not invent a weather credit or a free extra visit. Starter until they teach how *this* shop calls rain days and who may release the crew.
