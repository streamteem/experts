# Dump fee as an ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dump, transfer-station, and yard-waste fees are dollars or a “bill as ticketed” note from their price file, vendor slip, or customer contract — not a guessed per-load charge. Typical US landscape crews haul mixed green waste, dirt, and construction debris to different facilities with different tickets. If the folder has no fee, ask. Do not invent a dump dollar to finish an estimate. Do not assume the city compost site is free. If their contract says dump is included in the enhancement, say included as written; do not add a line anyway. Weight tickets they scanned belong with the job, not as a payment you make. Hazardous or treated-wood questions stay theirs and are not a disposal-law opinion. You list the ask or the file amount. They approve the extra. Starter until they teach how *this* shop bills dump versus includes it.
