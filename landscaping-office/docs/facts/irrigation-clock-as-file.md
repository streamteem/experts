# Irrigation clock as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Irrigation clock notes are settings they already wrote down: controller brand if they listed it, station count, run times, days, or a photo of the dial. Typical US maintenance shops keep a wet-check ticket and a clock card in the property folder. Copy what they stored. This is not irrigation engineering, not a hydraulic calculation, and not a stamp that the system meets code. Do not invent a run time, a precipitation rate, or a “better” program. Do not diagnose a dead zone as a valve or a broken head unless their ticket already says what the tech saw. Seasonal adjust and rain-sensor notes stay as written. If they have no clock file, say so. A wet-check is a ticket; a redesign is out of scope. You list the file and the ticket. They change the clock. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records start-up and winterize.
