# Estimate from their price file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An estimate draft is line items priced from the price file, menu, or dated quote they stored — not from a neighbor’s job or a number in your head. Typical US lawn-care shops keep a unit-price book: per thousand square feet of mow, per yard of mulch, per hour of base labor they already published, per shrub, per lighting fixture. Copy unit, quantity they measured or listed, and unit price as written. Extended amounts are math on those inputs, not a new price. If quantity is missing, ask; do not pace the yard from a satellite guess. If the price file is silent on a line, ask. Dump fees, tax, and trip charges appear only if their file has them. A draft is not a signed contract and not an invoice. They send the proposal. You do not invent a wage to back into a price. Starter until they teach how *this* shop measures and which book is live.
