# Leaf cleanup

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Leaf cleanup is a seasonal service they sold or put on the calendar: rounds, full clean, or “blow only” as they coded it. Typical US shops in deciduous metros run two or three rounds; some sell per visit. Scope comes from their ticket or HOA spec — beds, lawn, street, and whether leaves leave the site. Dump fees are an ask if not in the file. Do not invent a round count. Do not promise the oaks will be done before a storm. Weather they call can slide a round. One-time storm cleanup is not the same as the seasonal package unless they said so. You list properties due from the calendar and the route. They schedule the leaf truck. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts rounds and what “clean” means on an HOA. Street-leaf rules in an HOA spec stay copied. Do not invent a dump load count. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts a round as done.
