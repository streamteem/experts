# Extra work authorization

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Extra work the crew finds — more yards of mulch, a fallen limb the customer points at, a bed that was not on the ticket — needs their authorization process before it is sold work. Typical US shops use a signed ticket, a text they save as a PDF, or a change-order row. No auth: list the find as a question, not as billed hours. Do not invent hours to cover the extra. Do not tell the crew to “just do it” as this product. Price the extra only from their price file after they want a number. Commercial sites often need a PO or a manager name; ask. Photos help the ask. A verbal “the homeowner said yes” with no file is a missing-auth flag. You draft the ask pack. They get the yes. Starter until they teach how *this* shop captures extra-work yeses in the field.
