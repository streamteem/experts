# Fuel log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A fuel log is their record of gallons, truck or can, date, and sometimes a yard pump versus a carded station. Typical US landscape fleets track this for cost and for missing cans. Copy the rows they stored. You do not pay a fuel bill, do not store a card number, and do not invent gallons to close a week. Missing receipts stay missing. Equipment-down that they blamed on fuel or water in the tank is their note, not a mechanic diagnosis you write as fact. Crew-day packs may list which truck needs fuel from their morning note. This is not a payment desk. Starter until they teach how *this* shop pumps at the yard versus cards on the road. Yard-pump versus carded gallons stay in the columns they use. A missing card receipt is a hole, not a guessed gallon. You do not store a PIN. Starter until they teach how *this* shop reconciles cans at night and who may buy fuel on the road.
