# Frequency on the route

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Frequency is how often a recurring property is supposed to appear on the route: weekly, every other week, monthly beds, seasonal only, or a custom cycle they wrote. Typical US lawn-care books set frequency at sale or renewal and print it on the property row. Do not change weekly to biweekly to make a wet week fit. Do not invent a cycle because the neighborhood “looks like a weekly street.” Skips and weather postpones are exceptions on top of frequency, not a silent rewrite of the contract cycle. If their sheet shows last-visit date and next-due, copy those; do not compute a next-due from a remembered rule of thumb. Commercial sites may run a different frequency than nearby houses on the same truck. One-time work has no frequency. You list what the file says. They change frequency with the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks off-week properties and who may move a stop.
