# Mulch install

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mulch install is an enhancement or seasonal ticket: product they named, yards or bags, beds on the site map, and edging if sold. Typical US shops price by the yard delivered plus install hours from their sheet. Quantities come from their takeoff or sold proposal. Do not invent yards from a driveway glance. Color and grind come from the file; do not swap dyed versus hardwood to finish a load list. Dump of old mulch is a dump-fee ask if they remove it. Warranty on plants nearby is their plant file, not a promise mulch will save a tree. HOA color specs win when on file. You draft the material pull and the ticket. They buy the yards. Starter until they teach how *this* shop measures beds and whether they install on the mow truck. Old-mulch removal, if not on the sold ticket, is extra-work auth. Do not invent a color to match a photo memory. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop installs on the mow truck or a separate crew.
