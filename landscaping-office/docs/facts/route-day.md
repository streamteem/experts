# Route day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A route day is the ordered list of properties one crew is meant to hit on a calendar date, plus the service type on each stop. Typical US mow shops group by neighborhood, HOA, or commercial park so drive time stays short; enhancement trucks may run a different sequence the same morning. Sequence comes from their route file, not from a map you invent to look efficient. Time windows, dog flags, and skip rows stay attached to the stop they wrote. Weather postpones change the day only when they call it; you list the leftover stops, you do not declare the route dead. A route day is not a GPS product and not a promise that every stop finished. If two files disagree on order, quote both and ask. One property per row. You draft the day list from their files. They change the order and they send the crew. Starter until they teach how *this* shop builds Monday versus Friday routes.
