# Man-hours from their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Labor hours on an estimate or a ticket come from their production sheet, sold hours, or a closed ticket the crew already wrote — not from a remembered “that yard is two hours.” Typical US landscape offices keep a man-hour book by service and size band they built over seasons. Copy their hours. Do not invent hours to make the day look full or to cover extra work without auth. Do not back into hours from a wage you also must not invent. If the sheet is blank, ask. Crew-day totals are sums of assigned tickets, not a target you pad. Overtime is their call and not a payment you make. Enhancement hours and route hours stay in the buckets they use. You list file hours. They set production. Starter until they teach how *this* shop estimates hours versus clocks actuals.
