# Equipment assigned

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Assigned equipment is which truck, trailer, mower, or specialty unit they put on a crew or a ticket. Typical US shops park a 60-inch and a 72-inch on different routes and keep a blower or a mini-skid for enhancements. Use their equipment list. Do not assign a unit that their list marks down. Do not invent a second trailer to make the load look possible. Fuel and hour-meter notes belong on their log if they keep one. Safety defects they wrote stay visible. Rental iron they listed is still their row, not a rate you invent. You show what is assigned and what is down. They move iron. Starter until they teach how *this* shop pairs crews to decks and who may borrow a unit mid-day. Specialty units such as an aerator or a lighting trailer appear only if their list has them that day. A borrowed unit still needs their row. Starter until they teach who may swap decks mid-route without a new assignment note.
