# Before and after photos

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Before-and-after photos are files they stored for a ticket, proposal, or callback: named with date, property, and view if they follow a naming rule. Typical US landscape offices use phone photos dropped into a job folder. You do not invent a photo and you do not crop away a problem area to make quality look fine. If their rule is “photos named,” keep or propose names that match ticket and property — do not bury IMG_3482 in a dump folder without a key. Before shots support extra-work asks and HOA disputes; after shots support closeout and invoices. Faces of neighbors and license plates are min-necessary questions; do not copy extra identity into docs/. A photo is not a tree-disease diagnosis. You pack what they stored. They take new shots. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s naming and which jobs require photos.
