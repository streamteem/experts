# Snow route if they have one

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A snow route is a separate stop list some US shops run in winter: plow, shovel, ice product they already named, trigger depth, and commercial lots versus walks. If they have no snow file, say they have no snow desk in this folder — do not invent a route because the forecast looks white. Rates, triggers, and salt or sand products come from their snow contract or price file, not from a remembered per-push number. Do not invent a snow rate. Ice-melt brand and mix, if chemical, stay as their labeled product — not a pesticide-license act and not a new mix you recommend. Time windows and lot maps come from their file. Equipment-down on a plow truck is a flag from their equipment list. You pack the list they stored after they call the event. They dispatch. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even sells snow.
