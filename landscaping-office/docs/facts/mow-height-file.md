# Mow height on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mow height is the deck setting they recorded for a property or a turf type: inches or a hole number on the deck, sometimes different for front versus back. Typical US lawn-care offices keep this on the property row, a route note, or an HOA spec sheet. Use their number. Do not invent a height because Kentucky bluegrass “is usually three inches” or because last summer’s drought felt short. Do not tell the crew to scalp or raise a deck as agronomy advice. If the file is blank, ask. If HOA spec and property note disagree, quote both and ask which wins. A height change after a complaint is their call and should show on the ticket, not as a silent default you apply to the whole route. This Expert copies the file. They teach the crew. Starter until they teach where *this* shop stores heights and who may change them.
