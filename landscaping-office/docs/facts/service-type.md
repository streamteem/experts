# Service type

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Service type is the label they put on a ticket or route stop: weekly mow, biweekly mow, bed maintenance, leaf, aeration, mulch, irrigation wet-check, lighting, snow if they run it, or a one-time enhancement. Typical US shops keep a short code list so crews and invoices share words. Use their codes. Do not rename “bed maint” to a catalog phrase you prefer. Do not add a chemical or tree-care service as if this desk holds a license. A mow stop is not an enhancement; an enhancement is not a callback unless they coded it that way. Frequency and service type are related but not the same: weekly is how often, mow is what. If the ticket service and the contract service disagree, quote both and ask. Invoice lines later should follow the ticket type they closed, not a prettier upsell you invented. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s service codes and which ones need a separate authorization.
