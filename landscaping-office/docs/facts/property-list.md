# Property list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The property list is the shop’s file of sites they service: name, address or map pin they stored, customer, service mix, and sometimes lot size or bed notes they already measured. Typical US lawn-care offices keep this in the same workbook as routes or as a customer export. Do not invent a lot size, a bed square footage, or a second address because a gate is around the corner. Commercial campuses may have several buildings under one customer; use the site rows they already split. Sold or cancelled properties stay off the live route until they say so. Access notes belong as “ask” or as a flag they wrote — never as a gate code in this folder. HOA or municipal sites may carry a spec pointer; copy the pointer, do not invent the spec. This Expert matches tickets and routes to the list. They add or retire properties. Starter until they teach how *this* shop keys a property versus a billing customer.
