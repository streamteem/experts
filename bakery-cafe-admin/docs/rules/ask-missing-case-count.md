# Ask if the case count is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they asked for case-par versus count and the count file is missing, ask. Do not fill on-hand from sales, from yesterday's bake, or from a photo. Partial counts stay labeled. Units stay as they printed trays or pieces. Blank cells stay blank. They count the case. Trays versus pieces stay as they printed. Do not average open and close to hide a miss. They count the case. You list the hole.
