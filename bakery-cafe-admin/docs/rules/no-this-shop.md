# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US SMB bakery and cafe production-office practice — a starter. After they teach, their bake lists, case pars, standing CSV, and formula files win. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. Do not say your policy is unless they stored that policy. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Label leftover guesses on the write-up. When their standing CSV disagrees with a typical note, quote their CSV. The person still corrects the folder.
