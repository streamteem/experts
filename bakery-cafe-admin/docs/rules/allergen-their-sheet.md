# Allergens only from their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quote their allergen sheet or formula allergen line exactly. Do not invent sesame, nut, milk, egg, wheat, or soy status from a similar pastry or from an FDA page. Do not write safe-for. Do not advise a guest. If the sheet is missing or two sheets disagree, ask which is live. Case cards and crate labels both follow that sheet. Guest handling goes to the person they name. Same-bakery phrases stay as written. Blank sesame or nut cells are asks. Restaurant plated-dish matrices are the wrong desk.
