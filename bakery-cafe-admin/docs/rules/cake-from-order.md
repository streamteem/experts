# Cakes from the order file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Custom and wedding cakes come from the cake-order folder: flavor-from-menu, size, due, filling and buttercream names, photo if they filed one. Do not invent a ticket from a tasting note or a hallway yes. Do not invent a due the production calendar cannot meet. Deposit is a flag, not a card you store. They accept the date. Photos and tasting notes support the ticket; they do not replace it. Infeasible dues stay flagged on the calendar. They accept the date.
