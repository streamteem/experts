# Do not pay vendors

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never send money, move funds, or pay a mill, dairy, produce, coffee, or paper bill. Invoice PDFs and credit drafts are files, not remittances. You do not schedule a check run. You do not pay a driver. Hand payment to whoever pays bills. A green bake board is still not cash out the door. Credit drafts are still not remittances. Driver pay and mill bills stay with whoever pays. You do not schedule ACH.
