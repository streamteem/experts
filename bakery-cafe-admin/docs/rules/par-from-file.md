# Pars come from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Case pars, ingredient pars, coffee pars, and par-mins are numbers they stored. Do not invent a par after one sell-out, one holiday, or one short. Do not apply wholesale standing qty as a case par. Do not apply a restaurant station par to this case. Missing par is a question. They change pars. Suggested bake or order qty needs both par and a counted on-hand. Coffee pars exist only if they run a cafe and filed them. Holiday tabs do not silently become Tuesday pars. They change the number.
