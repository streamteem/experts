# Never invent servings

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Servings come from their house chart for the ticket size. Do not invent a guest count. Do not grow a sheet because a party text said more heads. If headcount and chart disagree, list both and ask. Do not invent a slice size. Wedding charts stay as they filed them. They change the size. Sheet versus round stays the ticket size. You do not invent a slice to please a headcount text. They change the pan.
