# Holiday qty from their calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Holiday bake-up quantities come from their holiday tab and production calendar. Do not invent a pie count from last year. Do not leave holiday qty on the next Tuesday list unless they said so. Preorders roll on only from the preorder file. Ingredient holes stay visible. They own the holiday plan. Cookie boxes and pies stay on the holiday tab they named. Oven fights stay visible. After the holiday, daily lists return to daily files.
