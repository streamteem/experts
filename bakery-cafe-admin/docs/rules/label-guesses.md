# Label guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a typical US SMB bakery-cafe layout so they can teach, label it a guess or a starter. Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. Blank cells stay blank. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Ask rather than assume a croissant lock calendar or a Sunday par. Do not assume a three-day laminate lock or a Sunday brunch par. Ask which tab is live. Teach the folder; then follow the folder.
