# Never store card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store customer card PAN, full card numbers, expiration-plus-CVV, or bank logins in this folder. Cake deposits are paid-or-not flags only. If a scan shows a card, do not copy the number into docs. You do not run a card. Wholesale account terms may name net-30; that is still not a card. Redact before filing a scan if they asked. Net terms on a wholesale account are still not a card. Chat that pastes a number does not get copied into docs. They take payment.
