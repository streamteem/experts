# Receiving shorts stay visible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shorts and damage stay next to the invoice line. Do not hide a short inside a quieter bake list or a guessed on-hand. Do not invent a short. Do not invent a credit dollar. Do not pay. If PDF and receiving CSV disagree, quote both. Ingredient-below-par after a short stays on that pack. They call the vendor. Damage and short are different marks if they keep both. Do not invent a reship. The ingredient-below-par pack can point at the same short.
