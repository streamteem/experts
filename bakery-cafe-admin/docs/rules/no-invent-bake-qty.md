# Never invent a bake quantity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bake-list quantities come from their plan, from case par minus count when both exist, and from standing plus extras. Do not invent a tray count to look busy or to use leftover dough. Do not invent a holiday bump. If files disagree, quote both. They set the bake. Standing plus case par can exceed yield; that is a hole, not a quiet extra mix. They set the bake. You flag the fight.
