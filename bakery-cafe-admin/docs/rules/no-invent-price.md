# Never invent a price

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unit prices, deposits, and credit dollars come from their ticket, menu, or invoice PDF. Do not invent a cake price, a wholesale unit price, or a credit amount. Do not invent a tax. Do not take a payment. Amounts on a remembered last wedding stay out. If price is blank, ask. They price. You copy. Wholesale unit prices stay on their invoice or standing price file. Cake deposits are flags, not a percent you remember. They price.
