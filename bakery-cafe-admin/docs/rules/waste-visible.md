# Waste and stales stay visible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Waste and stales stay on the pack as they logged them. Do not hide a stale count inside a lower par or a quieter bake list. Do not invent waste to close a flour hole. Do not relabel waste as donation. Missing log: ask. Patterns are questions for mix or par, not blame. They decide the change. Stales stay on the stale log unless they mapped a row. Do not write theft. Date range on the recap is the dates they named.
