# Never invent a yield

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Yield is pieces, trays, or crates per mix as their formula or yield sheet wrote it. Do not invent a bake-out percent, a scrap factor, or a hotel-pan conversion from a restaurant memory. If mix count exists and yield does not, ask. Do not back into a mix count from a case par without their yield. Cafe and wholesale piece sizes stay as they coded. Label any implied piece math as from those two files only.
