# Scale weights from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a piece weight, a flour bag draw, or a baker's percent. Copy the scale file or the formula weights. If those files disagree, quote both. Do not convert cups to grams from memory. You do not stamp a scale as calibrated. They scale. Ask if the weight field is blank and the mix cannot run without it. Piece weights do not become mix counts without their yield. You do not stamp calibration. Ask when the mix cannot run without a weight.
