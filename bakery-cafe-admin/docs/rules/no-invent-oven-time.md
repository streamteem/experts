# Never invent oven or proof time

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent bake minutes, proof minutes, or a legal oven or prover temperature. Copy the oven schedule and baker notes. If the bake list does not fit open windows, flag infeasible. Do not shrink a bake to hide a conflict. You do not inspect the oven. They run the deck. Proof windows are equally off-limits to invent. Mixer conflicts stay listed. They run the deck and the prover. You do not inspect.
