# Lots only if they use them

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy lot codes only from receiving, formula, or label files they stored. Do not invent a lot to look traceable. Do not say they passed a traceback. If they do not use lots, do not add a live lot column. Baker notes that mention a lot are still copied, not created. Mill lots on a flour bag enter only from receiving they wrote. You do not invent traceback. If they skip lots, say so.
