# Do not hide stales

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stale counts stay on the stale pack. Do not hide them inside waste, inside a par cut, or inside a quieter next-day bake. Do not invent stales from an empty tray photo. Next-day bake drops by stale qty only if they taught that rule. Wholesale returns are a different file. Day-old discount notes are still not a hidden cut to par. Wholesale returns stay on that file. They decide the next bake.
