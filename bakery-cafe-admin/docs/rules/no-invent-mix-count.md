# Never invent a mix count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mix count comes from the bake list or a baker note they filed. Do not invent batches to even a case or to hit standing. Do not invent a half-mix. If case par needs a yield to become mixes and yield is missing, ask. Shared cafe/wholesale mixes need their split. They set the mix. Baker notes can change a mix only if they wrote the new number. Half-mixes stay labeled. They set the mix.
