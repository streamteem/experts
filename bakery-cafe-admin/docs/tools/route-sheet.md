# Route sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their wholesale or cake route list: stop order, account, crate or box count, window, driver if they roster one. You do not invent a stop or a drive time. You do not store gate codes. Used when a wholesale-day pack needs stops; otherwise the standing CSV may be enough if they said pack-only. Returns the driver brings back stay on their return file, not invented here. No gate codes. Driver names come from their roster. They drive.
