# Bake list sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their bake-list workbook or export: item, formula pointer, mix count, shift, cafe versus wholesale, and the date. Typical US SMB shops use a bakery system or a dated sheet. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a mix or a formula. Draft versus live stays as they coded it. Checks look for the file they named. This tool starts bake-list-day and holiday-bake-up packs. No system password belongs here.
