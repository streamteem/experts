# Nut status from their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Nut status is peanut or tree-nut columns on their allergen sheet and any baker note about a nut topping — not a legal nut-free stamp and not a restaurant 'may contain' you invent. Typical US SMB shops run almond croissant some days and keep a nut drawer. You quote the sheet for the named item. You do not invent almond status because a photo looks like frangipane. You do not invent a nut-free process. If the cake ticket asks for walnut and the menu flavor is pecan, quote both. Same-bakery phrases stay as they wrote them. Guest medical questions go to the person they name. Starter until they teach *this* shop's nut sheet. Almond croissant and a walnut cake are different rows. You do not invent nut-free because a photo looks plain. Starter until they teach *this* shop's nut columns and whether toppings get their own line.
