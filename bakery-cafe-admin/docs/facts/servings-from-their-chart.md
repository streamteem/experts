# Servings from their chart

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Servings are the guest count their house chart assigns to a named size — not a number you invent from a party and not a restaurant banquet guarantee. Typical US SMB bakeries keep a one-page chart: six-inch to N, half-sheet to N. You copy the chart value for the ticket size. You do not invent servings. You do not invent a slice size. If the guest wrote a headcount that does not match the chart, list both and ask; do not silently grow the cake. Wedding servings follow the same chart unless they filed a wedding-specific chart. You do not invent a price per serving. Starter until they teach *this* shop's chart. A text that says fifty guests does not change the chart by itself. List both and ask. You do not invent a slice height. Starter until they teach *this* shop's chart and whether weddings use a second page.
