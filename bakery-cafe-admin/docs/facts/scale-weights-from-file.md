# Scale weights from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Scale weights are grams or pounds on their formula file or scale sheet — piece weight, mix flour weight, roll-in butter — not a cup measure you invent and not a restaurant portion scoop. Typical US SMB shops scale dough and log a check weight on laminated locks. You copy weights from that file. You never invent a baker's percent. You never invent a piece weight to hit a case count. If the scale file and the formula disagree, quote both. Mix-count math uses their weights only. This is not an inspector calibration stamp. They scale. Starter until they teach *this* shop's scale sheet. Piece weight and mix flour weight are different fields if they keep both. You never invent a baker's percent from a book. Starter until they teach *this* shop's scale sheet and when a check weight is required.
