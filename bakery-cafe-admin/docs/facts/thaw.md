# Thaw

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Thaw is their written pull from freezer or retarder onto the bake list — not a restaurant steak thaw you invent and not a legal thaw temperature. Typical US SMB shops write thaw-day next to laminated and cookie pucks. You copy that date from the production calendar or baker notes. You do not invent a thaw window. You do not invent a piece count after thaw. If freeze qty and thaw qty disagree, quote both and ask. Proof may follow thaw on their schedule; still do not invent proof time. Eighty-six wholesale because thaw is late is their call, not yours. This is bake-plan, not line tickets. Starter until they teach *this* shop's thaw rules. Retarder overnight and same-day thaw are different notes if they keep both. Do not invent a piece count after thaw. Starter until they teach *this* shop's thaw rules and who writes the pull.
