# Wholesale standing order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A wholesale standing order is the repeating crate or tray list a cafe, market, or restaurant account takes on named days — not a restaurant produce standing, not a one-time catering pull, and not a price you invent. Typical US SMB wholesale bakeries keep a standing CSV: account, item, qty, pack, days of week, and a start or pause date. You copy qty from that file for the wholesale day they named. You do not invent a standing line because the account ordered extras last Friday. You do not change qty because you think the cafe case is short. A standing change needs their change note. Holiday and closure dates follow their calendar, not a remembered market schedule. Returns and credits stay on those files, not a quiet cut to standing. This desk packs wholesale from standing plus the day's exceptions. They call the account. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes standing.
