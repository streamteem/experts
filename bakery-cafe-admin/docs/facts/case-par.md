# Case par

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A case par is how many pieces or trays this shop wants in the pastry case or bread wall for a named daypart — a house target, not a restaurant station mise par and not a health-department figure. Typical US SMB cafe bakeries keep a case-par sheet with item, daypart, weekday versus weekend, and sometimes a holiday column. Morning pastry pars often differ from afternoon; Sunday brunch pars may live on a separate tab. You copy the par from their sheet. You do not invent a par because the case looked empty in a photo. You do not raise Saturday croissant par from a remembered rush. Missing par on a live case item is a question, not a number you fill. Cafe par and wholesale standing are different files; do not apply a wholesale crate count to the case. Eighty-six on the case is their 86-case note, not a silent par cut. Starter case-par talk is not how *this* shop already stocks the case until they teach.
