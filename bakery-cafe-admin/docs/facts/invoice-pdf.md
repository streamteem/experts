# Inbound invoice PDF

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An inbound invoice PDF is the vendor bill they dropped in the invoice folder — flour, butter, dairy, produce — not a payment you make and not a restaurant three-way match you invent. Typical US SMB shops save PDFs by date and vendor. You list lines against the receiving CSV: qty, shorts, price only as printed. You do not invent a price. You do not change their par file to match a new price without asking. You do not store account passwords visible on a portal screenshot; ask them to redact. One pack is one vendor ticket unless they asked to index a week. They pay. This Expert matches files. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files invoices. A portal screenshot is not a password you copy into docs. Price jumps are questions, not a silent par edit. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names invoice PDFs and whether they file credits in the same folder.
