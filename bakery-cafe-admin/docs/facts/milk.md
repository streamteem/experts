# Milk (cafe and bake)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Milk is both a bake dairy and, if they run a cafe, an espresso par — separate lines if they split them. Typical US SMB bakery-cafes receive whole milk for pastry cream and a cafe case of whole and oat. You copy names, pars, and counts from their files. You do not invent an alt-milk formula. You do not invent allergen-free status for oat or almond drinks; quote their sheet. Receiving shorts stay visible. Date marks are their system, not a legal hold you stamp. Do not merge bake-cream and cafe-pitcher counts without their say. You do not pay the dairy vendor. Starter until they teach *this* shop's milk lines. Oat and whole milk can be cafe-only lines. You do not invent allergen-free status. Bake milk for pastry cream stays on the dairy par if they split it. Starter until they teach *this* shop's milk lines and who counts the pitchers.
