# Freeze

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Freeze notes are their written plan for locking laminated dough, cookie pucks, or baked trays — not a restaurant protein thaw chart and not a holding time you invent as law. Typical US SMB bakeries freeze croissant locks two days ahead on the production calendar. You copy freeze dates and item names from that calendar or baker notes. You do not invent a freeze time or a safe-frozen claim. You do not invent a yield after freeze. Thaw is a separate fact; do not skip it on the bake list if they thaw before bake. Case par still uses thawed on-hand only if they counted it that way. You do not stamp the freezer. Starter until they teach *this* shop's freeze list. Cookie pucks and croissant locks can share a freezer; still copy the item they named. You do not invent a safe-frozen claim. Starter until they teach *this* shop's freeze list and how it lands on the bake calendar.
