# Preorder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A preorder is a customer or account hold for a dated item — pies, cookie boxes, or a menu cake — not a wholesale standing line and not a restaurant reservation cover count. Typical US SMB shops cut off preorders on a calendar date and roll the list onto a bake list. You copy name, item, qty, due, and flavor-from-menu from the preorder file. You do not invent a qty. You do not invent a pickup time their calendar cannot meet. Deposit flags stay as they wrote them; you do not store PAN. If preorder qty plus case par plus standing exceeds yield on file, flag the hole. Custom cakes may also be preorders; keep the cake ticket id. They call the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop takes preorders. A hallway yes is not a preorder. Pickup windows come from their calendar, not a remembered Saturday rush. Starter until they teach how *this* shop takes pie and cookie-box preorders and when they roll onto the bake list.
