# Wholesale day

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A wholesale day is the named date they pack and send standing plus extras — not a restaurant broadline truck day and not a cafe case refill. Typical US SMB shops pack Tuesday–Saturday routes, skip a market on its closed day, and print a pick list from standing filtered to that weekday. You use their calendar and standing CSV together. You do not invent a delivery day. You do not pack Monday's standing onto Tuesday's crate without their note. Route, driver, and box counts come from the route sheet if they have one. Cafe-versus-wholesale split on a shared bake list stays labeled so the case is not stripped for a route. Eighty-six wholesale is their 86-wholesale note, not a silent drop. Preorders and wedding pickups are not wholesale standing unless they coded them that way. Starter wholesale-day talk is orientation until they teach *this* shop's pack days.
