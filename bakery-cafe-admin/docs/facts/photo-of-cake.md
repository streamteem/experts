# Photo of cake (their file)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A photo-of-cake is a reference image they filed with the ticket — customer inspiration or a house photo — not a license to invent a formula and not a copyright opinion. Typical US SMB shops drop a JPG in the cake-order folder. You list that the file is present and whether the ticket text matches the obvious size or flavor notes they wrote. You do not invent decoration steps from the photo. You do not say the photo is cleared to copy a branded character. You do not change servings because the photo looks tall. If photo and ticket disagree, quote both. They approve the design. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files photos. Inspiration photos are not a copyright clearance and not a formula. If the photo shows four tiers and the ticket says two, quote both. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files photos and who approves a mismatch.
