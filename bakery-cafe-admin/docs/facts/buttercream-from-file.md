# Buttercream from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Buttercream is a named icing on their formula file or cake-order ticket — American, Swiss, or a house name they stored — not a restaurant frosting you invent and not a butter percent you guess. Typical US SMB shops keep one or two house buttercreams and a chocolate variant. You copy the name from the ticket and the formula pointer. You never invent a buttercream formula. You never invent a color-mix recipe. Allergen milk and egg quote their sheet. Yield of iced cakes comes from their servings chart and the ticket size, not a remembered smear rate. Scale weights for a batch come from the formula file. Starter until they teach *this* shop's icing names. Chocolate and vanilla house icings are different formula pointers if they stored both. You never invent a Swiss-meringue method. Starter until they teach *this* shop's icing names and which ticket field points at the file.
