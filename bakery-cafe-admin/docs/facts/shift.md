# Shift

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A shift on this desk is AM bake, overnight mix, or PM cake — named on the bake list and calendar, not a restaurant AM/PM line changeover as the whole job. Typical US SMB bakeries mix laminated overnight and bake at open; cake decorating may be a day shift. You name the shift on every pack. You do not apply AM case par to a PM bake without their file. You do not invent an overnight window. Baker notes stay with the shift they wrote. Wholesale pack may be a third window on the route day. Starter shift talk is orientation until they teach *this* shop's start times. Overnight laminate mix and AM bake are different shifts if they split them. Name the shift on every pack.
