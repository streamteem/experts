# Vendor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vendor is a mill, dairy, produce house, coffee roaster, or paper supplier they listed on the par or invoice — not a restaurant broadline portal as the product and not a login you store. Typical US SMB bakeries split flour, dairy, and produce across houses. You copy vendor names from their files. You do not add a house from a flyer. You do not submit a cart. You do not pay. Portal tool notes are export pointers only. Minimums come from their guide or invoice footer, not memory. Cafe coffee vendors stay on coffee-par if they filed them. Starter until they teach *this* shop's vendor list. Mill, dairy, and produce houses stay separate if they listed them that way. You do not add a flyer house. Starter until they teach *this* shop's vendor list and which export they save from each portal.
