# Proof

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Proof is their scheduled rise — retarder overnight or a prover window — written on the oven schedule, bake list, or baker notes. It is not a restaurant dough you invent and not a legal holding time. Typical US SMB shops write a proof start and a bake window for laminated and some breads. You copy those windows. You do not invent a proof minute count or a temperature as law. You do not invent a mix that needs a different proof. If the prover is down on their calendar, flag the conflict; they reschedule. Cafe versus wholesale split still follows the bake list after proof. This Expert lists the window. They judge the dough. Starter until they teach *this* shop's proof notes. Overnight retarder and a morning prover window are not a legal hold you stamp.
