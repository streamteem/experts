# Custom cake

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A custom cake is an order that is not a standing menu slice — design notes, a photo they filed, and a due date on the production calendar. Typical US SMB shops require a written ticket plus a deposit flag before they hold the date. You list the board from the cake-order folder: due, size, flavor-from-menu, filling and buttercream names from their file, and missing fields. You never invent a formula for a new flavor. You never invent servings, a price, or an allergen claim. If the photo and the ticket disagree, quote both and ask. Decoration notes are theirs; you do not upgrade a sketch into a promised design. Gluten or nut requests stay as their process and their sheet, not a legal free-from stamp. This is not a restaurant pastry-station 86. They accept or refuse the date. Starter until they teach *this* shop's custom board.
