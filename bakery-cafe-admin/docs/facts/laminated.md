# Laminated dough

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Laminated dough is croissant, danish, and puff they sheet and fold on a production calendar — not a restaurant puff-pastry thaw-and-bake note you invent and not a butter percent you guess. Typical US SMB shops lock a three-day laminate plan: mix, fold, rest, roll, cut, proof, bake. The bake list shows cut counts and bake-day; the formula file holds butter and dough weights if they stored them. You copy lock-in dates from their production calendar. You do not invent a fold count, a rest time, or a butter block weight. You do not invent a yield of croissant from scrap. Freeze and thaw of locks follow their freeze/thaw notes. Cafe versus wholesale split on croissant is their allocation. Allergen lines (butter, milk, egg) quote their sheet. This is bake-plan, not line tickets. They run the sheeter. Starter until they teach *this* shop's laminate calendar.
