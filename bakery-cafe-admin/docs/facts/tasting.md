# Cake tasting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A tasting is a scheduled sample flight they booked — flavors from their menu, a date on the calendar, and notes they wrote after — not a formula you invent and not a restaurant chef's tasting menu. Typical US SMB shops cut tasting slices from a house set or from a small mix on the bake list. You copy the booking and the flavor list they stored. You do not invent a new flavor for the flight. You do not invent a price or a deposit rule. Allergen cards at the tasting quote their sheet. Photo-of-cake they show is their file. A tasting choice becomes a cake order only when they write the ticket. You do not treat a verbal favorite as a live wedding order. Starter until they teach how *this* shop runs tastings.
