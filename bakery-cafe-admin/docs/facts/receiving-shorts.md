# Receiving shorts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A receiving short is a qty on the invoice they did not get, marked on the receiving CSV or the PDF — not a guessed walk-in hole and not an inspector reject. Typical US SMB bakeries short flour bags, butter cases, and berry flats. You list the short next to the invoice line. You do not hide a short inside a lower bake list. You do not invent a short to explain a mix miss. Ingredient-below-par after a short stays visible. You do not invent a credit dollar. You do not pay. If the PDF and the receiving CSV disagree, quote both. They call the vendor. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks shorts. A missing flour bag is not a guessed mixer miss. Keep the short next to the PDF line. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks shorts and when they ask for a credit versus a reship.
