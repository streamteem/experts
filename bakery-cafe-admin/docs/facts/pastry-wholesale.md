# Wholesale pastry

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wholesale pastry is the croissant, muffin, and sweet crate on a standing order — not the cafe pastry case and not a restaurant pastry-station 86. Typical US SMB shops pack pastry at a cut time on the wholesale day, often earlier than case refill. You copy item and qty from standing plus extras they filed. You do not invent a pastry assortment. You do not strip the case to fill a crate unless they asked. Labels and boxes follow those files. Returns-wholesale stay on the return file. Cafe-versus-wholesale split on a shared mix stays labeled. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pastry pack list. Morning pastry crates can pack before the case is filled; still do not strip the case unless they asked. Returns stay on the return file. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pastry pack time and which items never go wholesale.
