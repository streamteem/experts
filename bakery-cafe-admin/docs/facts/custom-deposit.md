# Custom deposit (flag only)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A custom deposit is a paid-or-not flag they wrote on a cake or wedding ticket — not a card you run and not a restaurant banquet guarantee you invent. Typical US SMB shops require a deposit before they hold a weekend date. You copy the flag and the amount only if they stored the amount as a number on the ticket, not from a remembered percent. You never store card PAN, CVV, or a payment login. You never take a payment. You never invent a price. If the flag is blank and their process file says deposit required, ask. Refund rules are theirs; you do not give a legal refund opinion. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks deposits. Paid-or-not is a flag, not a card run. You never invent a percent. If their process says deposit required and the flag is blank, ask. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks deposits on cake and wedding tickets.
