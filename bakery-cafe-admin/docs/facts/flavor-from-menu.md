# Flavor from their menu

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Flavor is a name from their menu or formula list — chocolate, vanilla, lemon they already sell — not a new flavor you invent and not a restaurant special you write. Typical US SMB shops refuse off-menu flavors unless the owner files a new formula. You copy the flavor string from the cake order, preorder, or bake list. You do not invent a flavor to please a photo. You do not invent a formula for a named off-menu request; flag missing formula. Allergen lines follow the named flavor on their sheet. Filling and buttercream are separate names from their file. Starter until they teach *this* shop's live flavor list. Off-menu requests stay asks until they file a formula. You do not invent a seasonal flavor from last year. Starter until they teach *this* shop's live flavor list and who may add a name.
