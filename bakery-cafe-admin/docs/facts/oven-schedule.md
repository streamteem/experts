# Oven schedule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An oven schedule is which deck or rack oven they assigned and the bake windows on a named shift — not a restaurant ticket-time and not a bake minute you invent. Typical US SMB shops block laminated first, then bread, then cake; some write steam or vent notes. You copy the schedule they stored. You do not invent an oven time, a temperature as law, or a capacity to make a holiday list fit. If the bake list does not fit the open windows, flag infeasible. Mixer and proof windows are other files; do not hide a fight by shrinking bake time. They run the oven. This desk is production office, not the inspector. Starter until they teach *this* shop's oven names and blocks. Deck versus rack assignment stays as they wrote it. Do not invent steam or vent minutes. If holiday trays do not fit, flag infeasible.
