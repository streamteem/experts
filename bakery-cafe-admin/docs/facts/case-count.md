# Case count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A case count is how many pieces or trays they counted in the pastry case, bread wall, or back-bar at a named time — not a restaurant walk-in count and not a theoretical leftover from yesterday's bake. Typical US SMB shops count at open, at mid-morning refill, or at close; some count trays, some count pieces. You copy the count from their case-count sheet or from the count columns on the case-par workbook. You do not guess on-hand from a phone photo, from sales, or from the last bake list. Partial counts stay labeled. If par exists and count exists, suggested bake or pull is par minus count only when both are on their files for the same unit. Blank cells stay blank. Stales pulled off the case are not still on-hand unless they said so. This Expert lists count versus par. They count the case. Starter until they teach when *this* shop counts.
