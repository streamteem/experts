# Bread

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bread on this desk is hearth, pan, and sandwich loaves they bake on a named schedule — not a restaurant bread order from a commissary and not a formula you invent. Typical US SMB shops bake a daily baguette, a few loaves, and a weekend focaccia; some keep a sourdough starter as their file. Bake-list rows name the formula, mix count, score or pan type if they record it, and cafe versus wholesale. You copy those rows. You do not invent a hydration, a steam time, or a loaf weight. Scale weights come from their scale file or formula. Proof and oven windows come from their oven schedule or baker notes, not a remembered hour. Starter-if-they-use is a preferment file, not a wild recipe you write. Eighty-six bread on the wall is 86-case. Wholesale sandwich loaves follow standing. Starter bread talk is orientation until they teach *this* shop's loaf list.
