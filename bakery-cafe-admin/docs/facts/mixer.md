# Mixer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The mixer is the named spiral or planetary and the mix windows on their shift plan — not a restaurant robot-coupe mise and not a mix minute you invent. Typical US SMB shops write mixer, formula, and mix count on the bake list so two doughs do not land on one bowl. You copy assignment from their file. You do not invent a mix time or a load size. You do not invent a formula to fit a smaller bowl. If mix windows and oven windows conflict, list both. Baker notes about a mixer fault are questions, not a repair you schedule as a vendor payment. They mix. Starter until they teach *this* shop's mixer names. Spiral and planetary are different bowls if they named both. Do not invent a load size to fit leftover flour. Starter until they teach *this* shop's mixer names and how two doughs share a morning.
