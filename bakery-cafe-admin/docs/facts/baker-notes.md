# Baker notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Baker notes are shift comments they filed — tight dough, slow proof, oven down, a lock that tore — not a formula you invent and not a restaurant expo note. Typical US SMB shops write notes on the bake list footer or a notebook scan. You copy the note onto the pack when it changes mix count, oven windows, or an 86. You do not invent a fix. You do not invent a new formula from a note that says 'needs more water.' You do not stamp food safety from a temperature they scribbled. If the note and the bake list disagree, quote both. They teach the next shift. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files baker notes. A note that says tight dough is not a new formula. Quote it next to the mix; do not add water on paper. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files baker notes and which ones change the next bake list.
