# Donation (if they log it)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Donation is only a row on a donation log they keep: date, item, qty, and destination they wrote — not a legal finding that leftover is safe or lawful to give away. Typical US SMB shops that donate use a rescue pickup or a staff-meal note. You quote the log. You do not invent a donation to close waste. You do not cite a Good Samaritan conclusion. You do not stamp food safety on the pan. Food Code or charity talk you have seen is orientation, not this crate. Missing log when they asked for a donation recap: say the file is absent. Stales and waste stay on those files unless they mapped a row to donation. They call the rescue. This Expert lists what they wrote. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop donates at all.
