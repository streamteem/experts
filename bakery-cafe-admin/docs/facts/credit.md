# Credit (draft only)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A credit on this desk is a drafted question from a receiving short, a damaged case, or a wholesale return they logged — not a payment you send and not a restaurant bid-price fight you invent as a dollar. Typical US SMB shops draft vendor credits from the invoice PDF plus the receiving CSV, and account credits from the wholesale invoice plus the return file. You list item, qty, and the price only if it is on their file. You do not invent a credit amount. You do not mark an invoice paid. You do not store PAN. They submit the credit. Starter until they teach *this* shop's credit process. Vendor credits and account credits are different drafts. You still do not send money or invent an amount. Starter until they teach *this* shop's credit process and who submits the memo.
