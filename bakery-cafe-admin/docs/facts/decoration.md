# Decoration notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Decoration notes are the finish they wrote on a cake ticket or baker note — piping, drip, fresh fruit they named — not a formula you invent and not a restaurant pastry-station garnish. Typical US SMB shops attach a sketch or a photo-of-cake they filed. You copy the note. You do not upgrade a sketch into a promised sculpted design. You do not invent a buttercream color formula. Dietary flowers or fondant they named stay as the ticket; allergen quotes stay on their sheet. Time on the production calendar is their block, not a decorating hour you invent. They decorate. Starter until they teach *this* shop's finish codes. Fresh fruit they named is still a ticket line, not a garnish you add from a photo. You do not invent a color formula. Starter until they teach *this* shop's finish codes and who signs the cake as done.
