# Cafe versus wholesale split

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cafe versus wholesale is how they split one bake or one mix between the pastry case and wholesale crates. Typical US SMB bakery-cafes bake one croissant dough and portion trays to the case and crates to accounts; some bake separate mixes. The split lives on the bake list, the standing file, or a house allocation sheet — not in a restaurant expo ticket. You copy the split they wrote. You do not steal from the case to fill a wholesale short, or the reverse, unless they asked. You do not invent an allocation percent. If case par and standing together exceed the mix yield on file, flag the hole; do not invent an extra mix. Holiday bake-ups can change the split; use the holiday tab they named. This is bake-plan and case, not line tickets. They decide who gets the last tray. Starter until they teach *this* shop's split rule.
