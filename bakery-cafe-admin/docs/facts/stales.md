# Stales

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stales are leftover case or bread they pulled as unsold — not restaurant leftover mise and not a donation legal status. Typical US SMB bakery-cafes log stales at close: item, qty, and sometimes a next-day discount or compost note. You copy the stale log. You do not invent a stale count from an empty tray photo. You do not hide stales inside waste or inside a lower case par. You do not say leftover is legal to donate. Next-day bake can drop by stale qty only if they taught that rule in writing. Wholesale returns are a different file. Eighty-six case after a sell-out is not a stale. This pack stays visible so they can teach a par change. Starter stale talk is orientation until they teach how *this* shop treats day-old. Day-old bread and unsold croissant are still stales only if they wrote the row. Do not invent a discount price for the day-old shelf. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop discounts, composts, or only logs the pull.
