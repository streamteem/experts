# Pastry case

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The pastry case is the retail display they stock from the bake list and case par — viennoiserie, slices, cookies, and house pastry — not a restaurant pastry station and not a health inspection of the glass. Typical US SMB cafe bakeries name case sections (morning pastry, afternoon cake, cookie wall) on the par sheet. You list items, pars, counts, and 86-case notes from their files. You do not invent a case assortment from a remembered bakery. You do not stamp the case as holding-temperature legal. Labels on the case are their labeling file, not an FDA stamp you write. Allergen cards in the case quote their allergen sheet only. Stales pulled at close leave the count if they logged the pull. Wholesale crates are not the case. Coffee and milk pars sit next to the case only if they run a cafe and filed those pars. Starter case talk is not *this* shop's case map until they teach.
