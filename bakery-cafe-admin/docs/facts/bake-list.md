# Bake list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A bake list is the production office's plan for what comes out of the oven on a named day or shift — not a restaurant line ticket, not a dining-room 86 board, and not a health-department stamp. Typical US SMB bakeries and cafe production desks keep it as a workbook, a bakery-system export, or a printed sheet the mixer and oven lead mark. Fields you usually see are item name, formula name or number from their formula file, mix or dough count, bake window, cafe-case versus wholesale crate, and the shift. The list exists so the morning baker is not guessing from yesterday's leftover trays and so case pars and wholesale standing orders can both pull from one plan. You copy item names and quantities from the bake list they saved or from the standing and case files they pointed at. You do not invent a formula to fill a blank item. You do not invent a mix count from a remembered Saturday. If the formula pointer is missing, ask. Cafe versus wholesale split stays as they coded it. This Expert lists the day's plan and flags holes. They mix, they bake, they pull. Starter practice until they teach how *this* shop numbers and issues bake lists.
