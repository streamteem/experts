# Par minimum

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A par-min is the minimum on-hand they wrote for an ingredient or a case item — their number, not a vendor minimum you invent and not a restaurant weekend buffer you guess. Typical US SMB sheets have par and sometimes min and max. You use their columns. You do not invent a min after one short. You do not invent a vendor order-minimum filler. Suggested order is from par or min versus count only when those cells exist. Below-par packs list the hole. They change the min. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop uses min, par, or both. Min and par can both exist; use their columns. Do not invent a vendor order-minimum filler to look tidy. Below-par packs list the hole and wait. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop reorders at min, at par, or only when they say so.
