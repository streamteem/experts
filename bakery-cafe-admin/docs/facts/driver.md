# Driver

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The driver is the person they named on the route sheet for a wholesale or cake run — not a password, not a vehicle VIN you invent, and not a restaurant courier app. Typical US SMB bakeries roster one or two drivers on named days. You copy the name from the route sheet or calendar. You do not invent a driver. You do not store license numbers, insurance PDFs with extra PII, or app logins. Box counts and POD notes come from the route file they dropped. Returns the driver brings back stay on the returns-wholesale file. You do not pay the driver. If the route sheet has no driver and they asked for a pack, flag missing. Starter until they teach how *this* shop assigns drivers. Two drivers on one Saturday still need names from the roster they filed. You do not store a license photo. Starter until they teach how *this* shop assigns drivers and what POD they collect.
