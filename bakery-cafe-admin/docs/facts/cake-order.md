# Cake order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A cake order is their written ticket for a custom or menu cake — flavor from their menu, size, date, name on the cake if they take it, and pickup or delivery — not a restaurant dessert ticket and not a formula you invent. Typical US SMB bakeries keep cake-order forms in a folder or bakery-system export: due date, servings from their chart, sheet versus round, filling and buttercream from their file, and a deposit flag. You copy the ticket they stored. You do not invent a flavor off-menu. You do not invent servings. You do not invent a price. Photo-of-cake they filed is a reference, not a license to change the formula. Allergen and dietary notes quote their sheet and the ticket. Wedding cakes and tastings are separate facts when they use those files. This Expert boards orders. They bake and decorate. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes cake tickets.
