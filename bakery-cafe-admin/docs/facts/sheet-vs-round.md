# Sheet versus round

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sheet versus round is the pan shape and size on the cake ticket — quarter sheet, half sheet, six-inch round — not a serving count you invent and not a restaurant hotel-pan cut. Typical US SMB shops write size on the form and look up servings on their chart. You copy the size string. You do not convert a round to a sheet to fit the oven. You do not invent a pan they do not list. Wedding tiers stay as they wrote. Bake-list mix for cake layers uses their formula and their size, not a remembered batter weight. If size and servings-from-their-chart disagree with a guest note, quote both. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pan list. Quarter-sheet and six-inch round are different bake-list rows if they batter them separately. You do not convert shapes to fit a deck. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pan list and how it maps to the serving chart.
