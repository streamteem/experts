# Filling from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A filling is the named jam, pastry cream, ganache, or fruit they stored on the formula file or cake ticket — not a restaurant sauce and not a filling you invent. Typical US SMB shops keep a short filling list on the menu and a formula card for pastry cream if they cook it. You copy the name. You never invent a filling formula or a cook time as law. You never invent a shelf-life as a Food Code stamp. Allergen and dietary notes quote their sheet. Cake-order filling versus danish filling can be different files; keep the pointer they wrote. Waste of leftover pastry cream is only what they logged. Starter until they teach *this* shop's filling list. Pastry cream and jam can live on different cards. You never invent a cook time as a Food Code hold. Starter until they teach *this* shop's filling list and whether leftover cream is wasted or reused by their note.
