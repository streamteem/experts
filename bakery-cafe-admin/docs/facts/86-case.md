# Eighty-six the case

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Eighty-six case is their note that a pastry-case or bread-wall item is out — not a restaurant line 86 for a plated dish and not a guessed empty tray. Typical US SMB cafe bakeries write 86 on a case board or a POS note they export. You copy that note. You do not 86 from a photo, from a stale log, or from a wholesale crate short unless they taught that rule. You do not cut the case par silently. A mid-day 86 can still leave the wholesale pack standing. Allergen cards come down only if they said to. This Expert lists 86-case on top of the case pack. They tell the counter.
