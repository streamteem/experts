# Coffee par (if they run a cafe)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Coffee par is a cafe-side par for beans, brew, or espresso milk they filed — only if they run a cafe. It is not a restaurant coffee standing from a broadline house as the whole desk and not a drink recipe you invent. Typical US SMB bakery-cafes keep a small cafe-par tab next to pastry-case par. You copy those lines. You do not invent a bean SKU or a drink formula. You do not invent a caffeine or temperature claim. Milk for espresso stays on the milk fact and the cafe par. If they have no cafe, say coffee par is out of scope. Wholesale coffee beans are a vendor line only if they filed it. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop's cafe pars live in this folder. Bean bags and brew-coffee bags can be different pars. You do not invent a drink recipe. If they have no cafe, say so. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop's cafe pars live next to the pastry-case sheet.
