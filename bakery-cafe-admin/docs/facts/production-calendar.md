# Production calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The production calendar is their dated plan for mixes, locks, holiday bake-ups, cake dues, and wholesale pack days — not a restaurant reservation book and not a due you invent. Typical US SMB shops keep a wall calendar or a workbook with freeze, thaw, bake, and decorate rows. You copy dates from that file. You do not invent a bake day to make a custom cake fit. You do not invent oven capacity. Cake tickets, preorders, and standing extras must fit the calendar they stored; flag infeasible. Holiday tabs override daily lists only if they said so. This Expert lists conflicts. They own the calendar. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates production. Lock days, cake dues, and wholesale pack days can share one workbook. You do not invent a bake day to make a wedding fit. Starter until they teach how *this* shop dates production and who may move a block.
