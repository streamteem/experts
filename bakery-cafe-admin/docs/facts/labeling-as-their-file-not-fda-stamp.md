# Labeling as their file, not an FDA stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Labels on packed bread, wholesale crates, and case cards are text from their labeling file or allergen sheet — not an FDA-compliant stamp you issue and not a restaurant date-dot you invent as law. Typical US SMB shops print ingredient lists they stored, a packed-on date they chose, and sometimes a lot if they use lots. You copy that file onto a completeness list: missing ingredient line, missing allergen line, missing date they require. You do not write a Nutrition Facts panel. You do not invent an ingredient list from a remembered formula. You do not say the label meets FDA rules. Unpackaged case items may use a card; still quote their card file. Wholesale labels follow the account's required fields only if they stored that spec. This Expert flags holes. They print and stick. Starter until they teach how *this* shop labels.
