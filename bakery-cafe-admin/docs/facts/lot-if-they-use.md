# Lot (if they use lots)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A lot is a code they assign to a mix, a bag of flour, or a packed crate if they use lots — not a number you invent and not an inspector trace you stamp. Typical US SMB shops that lot write the mix date plus a letter, or copy a mill lot off a flour bag onto the receiving CSV. You copy lots only from their receiving, formula, or label file. You do not invent a lot to make a recall drill look complete. You do not say they are traceable as a legal finding. If they do not use lots, say so and do not add a lot column as if it were live. Baker notes may mention a lot; still copy, do not create. Starter lot talk is orientation until they teach whether *this* shop lots at all.
