# Flour

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Flour is a par-ingredient on this desk: bread, pastry, whole-wheat, or a house blend they named — not a restaurant AP flour bin you invent and not a formula. Typical US SMB bakeries receive 50-lb bags, keep a bag count, and write a par-min on the ingredient sheet. You copy on-hand from their count or receiving, and par from their par-ingredient file. You do not invent a bag count. You do not invent a protein percent or a mill brand to substitute. If the bake list will consume more bags than on-hand plus expected receiving, flag the hole; do not invent a formula that uses less flour. Lot if they use lots stays on the bag note they wrote. Allergen wheat is their sheet. You do not stamp gluten-free because a bag says a different mill. They order flour. Starter until they teach *this* shop's flour names and pars.
