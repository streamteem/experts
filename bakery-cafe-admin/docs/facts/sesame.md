# Sesame from their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sesame status is their allergen sheet for bagels, everything topping, tahini, or a bun they named — not an FDA sesame ruling you apply to this tray. Public sesame-labeling pages are orientation for a column name you might see. Typical US SMB shops that bake bagels keep sesame on the matrix. You quote contains or may-contain exactly. You do not invent sesame because a 2023 labeling page mentioned bakeries. You do not invent a sesame-free process. Unpackaged case items still follow their card file, not a packaged-food stamp you write. If sesame is blank on a named item, ask. Starter until they teach *this* shop's sesame column.
