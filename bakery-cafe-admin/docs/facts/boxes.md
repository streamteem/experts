# Boxes and packs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Boxes are the crates, bakery boxes, and cake boxes they pack to — counts from the wholesale-day list or cake ticket, not a restaurant to-go clamshell par you invent. Typical US SMB shops keep a box SKU on the ingredient or paper par and a pack size on standing (six-pack, dozen, half-sheet). You copy pack size from standing or the cake order. You do not invent a pack to fill leftover pastry. You do not invent a box count. Labels on boxes follow their labeling file. Short packed boxes stay short on the pick list. Ingredient-below-par on boxes is a paper-goods question, not a bake-list change. They pack. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pack sizes. Half-dozen pastry packs and wedding cake boxes are different SKUs if they stock both. Do not invent a pack to use leftover danish. Starter until they teach *this* shop's box list and which standing lines use which pack.
