# Banquet check signature

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who may sign the banquet check is their process: planner, on-site contact, or a named master-account signer. Typical US captains will not close the check without that name. You list whether a signed PDF is in the folder and whether the signer name matches the contract contact if they require that match. You do not sign. You do not store a card authorization PAN. Unsigned checks stay on the exception list. A signature is not a liquor-license act and not a fire stamp. Disputed lines belong on the exception write-up with both BEO and check amounts. They collect the signature. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s signer rule. A digital signature PDF is present only if they saved it. You do not forge initials. Disputed tax lines stay disputed until they say otherwise. The signer is not automatically the liquor-license holder. They collect the name their process requires.
