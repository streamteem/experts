# Reset / turn

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A reset or turn is changing the same room from one function to the next — breakfast classroom to lunch rounds, ceremony theater to reception rounds. Typical US SMB hotels and halls put reset time, crew, and the next BEO number on the day sheet. Reset is labor and inventory, not a free instant change. If two BEOs share a room and the gap is shorter than their written turn time, flag the conflict; do not invent a turn time from another hotel. Do not hide a same-day flip. Linen color, table count, and AV that change between functions need both BEOs, not one blended sheet. Outdoor-to-indoor rain moves are resets too if they wrote a rain plan. You list the gap and both setups. They add labor or move a function. You do not promise a twenty-minute miracle the labor file does not support. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s turn windows.
