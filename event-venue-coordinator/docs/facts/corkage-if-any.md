# Corkage (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Corkage, wine-on-site, and outside-alcohol rules come from their banquet menu or alcohol-policy file. Typical US venues either forbid outside alcohol, allow wine with a printed corkage per bottle, or allow a licensed caterer only. You copy the file. You do not invent a corkage fee. You do not stamp a guest as allowed to pour. You do not serve as the liquor license for BYOB. If the policy is silent, ask — do not assume hotel-brand corkage you remember. Cake alcohol infusions and champagne toasts still follow the same file. Service-charge on corkage is only if printed. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s outside-alcohol rule. Per-bottle versus per-table corkage stays as printed. You do not waive corkage for a wine sponsor. Outside beer and liquor follow the same policy file as wine unless they split them. Unlabeled bottles at load-in are a captain question, not a fee you invent.
