# Chef and kitchen count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Kitchen labor counts (chef, cooks, dishwashers) come from their labor guide or the BEO staffing block — not from a food-cost model you invent. Typical US banquet kitchens staff from guarantee and menu style (plated versus buffet versus stations). You copy their ratio file if they have one. You do not invent a chef count. You do not invent a food cost or a plate-up minute. Union kitchen calls, if any, follow the union file. Vendor meals are a different count. If the labor guide is missing, ask; do not staff from another hotel’s book. You list the file’s number next to guarantee. They schedule the crew. Starter until they teach *this* kitchen’s staffing guide. Station dinners and action-chef counts follow the same guide if they wrote those rows. You do not pull a cook from another event’s BEO to paper over a hole. Overtime for a late tasting is the labor file, not a guessed wage.
