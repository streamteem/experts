# Last call (from their file)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Last-call and bar-close times come from their house policy, BEO, or liquor-service rules they stored — not from a remembered ABC clock. Typical US halls close the bar thirty to sixty minutes before curfew or before contracted end. You copy the clock. You do not serve as the liquor license and you do not extend service. You do not invent a last-call time to squeeze another package hour. Outdoor versus indoor last call may differ. Noise curfew and last call are different files; list both if both exist. A bartender count does not imply legal service. They run the bar. You print what they wrote. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s bar-close rule. Champagne toasts after printed last call are extras only if their file allows a toast exception. You do not pour. A planner who wants a later bar must clear their license holder, not this desk. Indoor last call does not silently move outdoors.
