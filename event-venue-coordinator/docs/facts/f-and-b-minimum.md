# Food and beverage minimum

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An F&B minimum is a dollar or cover floor in their contract or rate file for that date, room, and meal period. Typical US halls and hotels print a Saturday-night minimum that differs from a Tuesday lunch. You copy the number and the what-counts notes (food only, food plus beverage, service charge in or out) exactly as written. You never invent a minimum to “protect Saturday.” You never invent a food cost or a contribution margin. If the BEO estimated spend is below the contract minimum, flag the gap using their figures; do not raise menu prices to close it. If no minimum is on file, say none — do not borrow last year’s Saturday number. Room rental versus minimum-or-rental language stays as the contract printed it. They discount or they hold the floor. You list. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s minimum grid.
