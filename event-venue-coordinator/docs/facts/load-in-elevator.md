# Load-in elevator

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Freight or service-elevator reservations, exclusive times, and pad rules come from their load-in calendar or engineering sheet. Typical US hotels share one freight car among groups, room service, and housekeeping. You copy reserved windows. You do not invent an exclusive hour. You do not put a guest-elevator pad plan in the write-up as a promise. Overlap between two vendors is a conflict list. Weight and size limits, if on their sheet, are copied — you do not engineer a load. Dock-to-elevator path notes stay as facilities wrote them. You do not store access codes. Starter until they teach how *this* building books the freight car. Passenger-elevator use for freight is only if their engineering note allows it. You do not promise pads and blankets. A wedding dress arriving by car is not a freight reservation unless they put it on the calendar. Oversize scenery that fails their size note is a flag.
