# Bartender count (file, not a license)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bartender and barback counts come from their banquet labor file or BEO — never as a liquor-license determination. Typical US venues write one bartender per so many guests on a house sheet. You copy that sheet when the guest or bar-type inputs are present. You do not invent a ratio. You do not serve as the license, do not stamp TIPS or alcohol-server cards, and do not decide who may pour. Cash versus hosted versus consumption bar is a BEO label, not a license type. Last call is their file, not your legal last-call. Missing labor file: ask. Client-hired bartenders still need their COI and house rules if the packet says so. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s bar staffing sheet. Consumption bars and cash bars may use different rows on the same labor sheet; pick the matching row. You do not certify TIPS cards. A client mixologist is still a vendor COI and house-rule question, not a license you grant.
