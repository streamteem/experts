# Cake-cutting fee (from file)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cake-cutting, dessert-plating, and outside-bakery fees come from their menu or wedding-package file. Typical US halls include cutting on some packages and charge a per-slice fee on others. You copy the printed fee and what it includes (plates, forks, the toast pour). You do not invent a cutting fee. You do not invent a leftover-cake policy; leftovers follow the leftover-food file. Bakery load-in and refrigeration are timeline questions. Allergy flags on a cake are labels, not a diagnosis. A tasting cake is not the reception cake guarantee. If no fee is on file, say none — do not borrow a country-club number. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s cake line. Sheet cakes versus plated dessert swaps follow the menu file, not a courtesy cut. You do not invent a plating fee for a grocery-store cake. Bakery COI is only if their vendor checklist asks. Leftover cake still follows the leftover-food policy.
