# Hold cutoff / release date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A hold cutoff is the date the tentative option expires if it does not go definite. Typical US venues print a release date on the hold sheet or send a release letter they save as PDF. You list holds with cutoff on or before the pack date as due-to-release. You do not release the space yourself. You do not invent a cutoff from a remembered two-week rule. If cutoff is blank, ask. Second-option holds may have a different clock; keep option rank from their sheet. A cutoff is not a room-block cutoff; that is a sleeping-room date. You do not upgrade a hold to definite to avoid the cutoff. They call the planner. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s release letters. A courtesy reminder they asked you to draft is still a draft; they send it. Holds past cutoff that they extended need the new date on the sheet. You do not keep a dead option on the definite list to make the week look full.
