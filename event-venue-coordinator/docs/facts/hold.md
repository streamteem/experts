# Hold / tentative

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A hold is a tentative claim on a room and date that is not yet a signed definite. Typical US SMB venues use first-option and second-option language, a release or cutoff date, and a status such as tentative, penciled, or hold. The hold sheet should show room, date or date range, option rank if they use one, who requested it, and when the hold expires if they wrote that. A hold is not revenue and not a BEO. Do not treat a hold as definite. Do not invent a cutoff if the sheet is blank — ask. Two holds on the same room-date is a conflict they resolve; you list both rows and the option order from their file. First-option does not become definite because the calendar is getting full. You do not release another planner’s hold to make a new inquiry fit. They release, they upgrade to definite, or they let the cutoff run. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s option and release rule.
