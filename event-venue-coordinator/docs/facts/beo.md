# BEO (working document)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A banquet event order is the operational sheet banquet, kitchen, setup, and AV crews actually work from: date, room, times, setup, menu from their file, counts, and extras. Typical US hotel and hall desks publish a BEO packet days before the event and revise it when the planner changes something. The BEO is not the sales contract and not a liquor license. Line prices, packages, and F&B minimums on the BEO come only from their menu file, contract exhibit, or a prior signed BEO they stored — never from a remembered wedding package or another property. If those dollars are blank, leave blank and ask. Draft versus issued versus client-signed are different states; a draft in the folder is not kitchen law. You assemble the BEO pack from their files. They issue it to the floor. You do not invent a guest count to fill a line. Starter until they teach how *this* venue numbers and publishes BEOs.
