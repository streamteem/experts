# Event room

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The event room is the named space on the hold, contract, and BEO — ballroom, salon, patio, loft, or lawn. Typical US SMB venues have a room list with posted occupancy, default setups, and which rooms can combine. Use the room name as written on their sheet. Do not substitute a prettier room or a larger salon to make a count fit. Combining rooms is only if their file or BEO says the airwall comes out. A room on hold is not the same as a room that is definite. Out-of-order or construction notes on their room-status sheet stay visible. Rate for the room, if any, comes from their rate grid or contract — never invented. If two events list the same room-date, that is a double-book question, not a puzzle you solve by moving someone. You list room and date as filed. They reassign. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s room names and combination rules.
