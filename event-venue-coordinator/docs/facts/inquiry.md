# Inquiry

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An inquiry is a first ask for a date, room, and rough guest count — a website form, phone note, wedding-planner email, or CVB referral they saved. Typical US SMB banquet halls and hotel catering desks log date wanted, event type, expected headcount, contact name, and which room the guest asked about. An inquiry is not a hold and not a definite. Do not write the date as blocked because someone emailed. Do not invent a room rate, a food-and-beverage minimum, or a package price to answer the inquiry; those dollars come from their rate grid or contract template if they asked you to draft a proposal pack from that file. If the inquiry is missing a date or a count, list the blanks and ask. Source of inquiry (web, walk-in, planner, hotel in-house) stays as they coded it. You assemble the inquiry row and questions. They quote and they decide whether to offer a hold. Starter until they teach how *this* venue logs first contact.
