# Dance floor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dance-floor size and placement come from the BEO or diagram, not from a remembered wedding rule of thumb. Typical US halls own a sectional floor (for example 12-by-12 or 18-by-18) or rent one. You copy the size they wrote. You do not invent a square footage from guest count. A floor that eats two table positions should show on the diagram; if the diagram omits it and the BEO includes it, quote both. Indoor versus outdoor floor is a weather and surface question they own. Load-in of a rental floor uses the load-in calendar. You do not promise the floor meets a fire-aisle layout. Labor to install comes from their labor file. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s floor inventory and who installs it. Outdoor floors on grass or patio pavers need their surface note if they stored one; you do not declare the ground safe. A ceremony aisle runner is not a dance floor. Missing size on a wedding BEO that mentions dancing is an ask.
