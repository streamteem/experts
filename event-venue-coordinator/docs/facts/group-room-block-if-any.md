# Group room block (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A group sleeping-room block is a hotel file attached to some banquet events: peak nights, contracted rooms, rate from their group contract, and a cutoff date. Typical US SMB hotels attach blocks to weddings and corporate meetings; standalone halls often have none. If they have no block file, say none. You do not invent a group rate. You do not invent a contracted room count. Pickup versus contract is a later list. Attrition on rooms uses their contract worksheet only. The banquet F&B minimum is not the room block. You list block, cutoff, and rate as printed. They manage the hotel reservation system. Starter until they teach whether *this* venue even has rooms. Shoulder nights and peak nights stay in the columns they used. You do not invent a rebate. A wedding block at a nearby hotel that is not their property is only in the pack if they stored that file; you do not build a hotel product.
