# Room-block cutoff

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Room-block cutoff is the last date guests may book inside the group rate and the date unused rooms may return to house inventory, as the group contract printed it. Typical US hotels use twenty-one or thirty days. You copy that date. You do not invent a cutoff. You do not wash the block yourself. Hold-cutoff on event space is a different clock; keep the two dates labeled. After cutoff, their file says whether the rate continues or not — you do not promise a late reservation. Pickup reports near cutoff belong in the group-pickup pack. They release rooms. Starter until they teach *this* hotel’s wash practice. A planner asking to push cutoff is a question for them, not a date you edit. After wash, pickup reports should show the new contracted number if they changed it. You do not promise walk-in guests the group rate.
