# Union (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Union jurisdiction, minimum calls, and steward notes appear only if they stored a house union file, rider, or hotel engineering/AV agreement. Typical US city hotels have banquet or stagehand rules; many suburban halls have none. If they have no file, say none — do not invent a four-hour call. You do not interpret a collective-bargaining agreement as counsel. You do not invent a scale wage. Load-in that triggers a call is a flag against their written trigger, not against a memory of another building. You list the call as their file states it. They book the call. Starter until they teach whether *this* venue is union and which crafts. AV, banquet, and engineering crafts stay separate if their file separates them. You do not trade a houseperson for a stagehand on paper. Minimum-call hours that exceed the event clock are still the written call, not a shortened guess.
