# Event timeline

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The timeline or run-of-show is the clock: load-in, ceremony, cocktail, announced dinner, speeches, last dance, load-out. Typical US wedding coordinators send a minute-by-minute PDF; corporate days use an agenda with break times. You copy times onto the day calendar and the BEO pack. You do not invent a ceremony start to make photos fit. Conflicts (ceremony still on the lawn while cocktail BEO has already flipped that lawn) stay visible. Curfew, last call, and load-out must be reconcilable to the last line; if they fight, quote both files. You do not run the show. They approve the clock. Starter until they teach how *this* venue stores timelines versus BEO time blocks. Photo-block and first-look times belong on the calendar if they wrote them, even when they are not food functions. You do not invent a first-dance minute. A timeline PDF that disagrees with BEO start times is a quote-both ask, not a silent blend.
