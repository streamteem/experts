# Incidentals and extras

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Incidentals are day-of extras: more wine, extra cheese, broken glassware fees, parking overage, or a last-minute room — only as posted on their check or captain notes. Typical US captains write extras on a duplicate pad that becomes part of the banquet check. You list extras that appear on their file. You do not invent an incidental charge. You do not add a damage fee from a photo you interpreted; damage is their incident note. You do not run a card for extras. If the BEO forbids extras without a signed change, and the check shows extras with no change slip, flag it. They authorize extras. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s day-of extra process. Broken-glass and linen-damage notes need their incident or captain slip; a photo alone is not a dollar you invent. Last-minute room flips are overtime or reset questions from those files. You do not add a surprise fee to teach the planner a lesson.
