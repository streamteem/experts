# Guarantee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The guarantee is the count the kitchen and billing will use, due by a date on their contract or BEO — often seventy-two hours or a named business-day rule they wrote. Typical US banquet desks cook and set from guarantee, then bill the higher of guarantee or actual if that is their file. You do not invent the due date from a remembered hotel rule. Missing guarantee date: ask. Do not treat an email “we think 120” as the guarantee unless they said that email is the guarantee file. A guarantee above posted occupancy is a flag, not a silent set. You do not lower a guarantee to make the minimum look met. Actual walk-in count after the event belongs on the banquet check if they record it; it does not rewrite history on the BEO without a revision. They collect the number. You list due date, number, and source file. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s guarantee clock.
