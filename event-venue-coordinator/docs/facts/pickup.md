# Block pickup

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pickup is how many sleeping rooms in the block are reserved as of a date, from their PMS export or pickup sheet — not from a guessed fill. Typical US hotel catering desks pull pickup twice a week toward cutoff. You copy reserved, contracted, and remaining if those columns exist. You do not invent a pickup number to make attrition look safe. Complimentary rooms and staff rooms stay as they coded them. Pickup is not banquet guarantee. A low pickup is a question for them and the planner, not a quiet shrink of the banquet count. You do not release rooms. They wash or they hold. Starter until they teach how *this* hotel exports pickup. Same-day pickup dumps they exported beat a week-old PDF. You do not smooth a dip to make the chart look healthy. Rooms reserved outside the block at the same rate still follow how they coded them on the export, not a guess.
