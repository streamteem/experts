# Definite / signed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Definite means they marked the booking signed, contracted, or confirmed in their file — not that you decided the deal is done. Typical US SMB desks move a hold to definite when a signed contract or a required deposit note is in the folder. Status words stay as they coded them: definite, confirmed, contracted. Do not upgrade a hold because the couple sounded sure on a call you did not hear. Do not invent a deposit received. If the contract PDF is missing but the sheet says definite, quote both and ask which wins. Definite events still need a BEO later; definite is booking status, not an event order. Room and date on the definite sheet should match the contract if both exist. A cancelled definite is a cancel, not a silent delete. You list definites and missing contract or deposit files. They sign and they take money. Starter until they teach how *this* venue codes definite.
