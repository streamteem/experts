# Loading dock

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The loading dock or receiving bay is a timed slot on the load-in calendar: truck, vendor, in and out. Typical US halls have one bay and a tight alley. You list reservations from their sheet. You do not invent a dock time. You do not promise a tractor-trailer will fit. Union dock rules stay in the union file. Valet or guest cars in the dock apron are a conflict. Overnight truck parking is only if their file allows it. You do not pay a driver or a detention fee. Missing dock fields on a heavy production day are an ask. They assign the bay. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s dock hours and shared-use rule. Shared-use with hotel receiving or restaurant produce trucks stays on the conflict list when their dock log shows both. You do not invent a second bay. After-hours dock fees come from their labor or dock file. You do not sign a bill of lading as the venue.
