# Leftover food policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

What happens to leftover banquet food — client takeaway, donation, staff meal, or discard — comes only from their leftover or food-safety policy file. Typical US venues forbid take-home on some items and allow boxed cake on others. You copy the file. You do not invent a donation plan. You do not stamp a health-department ruling. CDC and FDA pages are orientation, not this kitchen’s inspection. Vendor meals are a planned count, not leftovers. You do not promise leftover survival or safe holding times as law. If they have no leftover file, say so and ask. They box or they discard. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s leftover rule. Donation-partner names appear only if their file lists a partner. You do not call a food bank as this product. Staff meal from leftovers is still their policy, not a perk you grant. Boxed client cake is not a blanket take-home for dinner leftovers.
