# Load-out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Load-out or strike is when vendors must be off the floor and out the dock after the event. Typical US SMB venues print a load-out window on the BEO or timeline so the next setup can start and so overnight storage is not assumed. Load-out is not leftover-food policy and not a banquet-check signature. If load-out overlaps the next event’s load-in, flag it. Do not invent a strike time so the day looks tidy. Items left after the window are a storage or lost-and-found question they own. Union or after-hours labor for late strike comes from their labor file, not a guessed overtime dollar. You list the written window and any next-room conflict. They extend the window or they charge from their file. You do not keep vendor gear on a promise you invented. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s strike rule.
