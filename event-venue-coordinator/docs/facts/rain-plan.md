# Rain plan

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A rain plan is the indoor backup room, tent sidewall, or delay rule they wrote for an outdoor ceremony or reception. Typical US wedding files name a decision time (for example morning-of at 7 a.m.) and who calls it. You copy that file. You do not invent a backup ballroom that is already definite for someone else. You do not promise the weather. If the backup room is on hold for the same client, say so; if it is not held, flag that the rain plan has no space. Reset labor to flip the backup room comes from their labor file. You do not move another group to make rain space. They call the weather and they hold the backup. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s rain-call process. Decision-time weather screenshots they saved are files, not a forecast you make. A tent-only rain plan still needs the tent vendor on the load-in calendar. You do not promise the indoor room is beautiful enough; you only list whether it is held.
