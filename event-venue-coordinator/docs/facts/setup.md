# Room setup

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Setup is how the room is built: rounds of ten, crescent, classroom, theater, u-shape, cocktail high-tops, or a custom diagram they saved. Typical US banquet desks pull chair and table counts from the guarantee or the diagram, not from the original inquiry. Banquet rounds, classroom tables, and theater chairs are different labor and different inventory. Do not invent a chair count from a remembered “eight per table.” If the BEO says rounds of ten and the diagram shows eights, quote both and ask. Setup start time on the BEO is when the crew may enter, not when guests arrive. Head table, buffet lines, DJ space, and dance floor eat seats; those footprints come from the diagram, not a guess. You do not promise the room will hold a count the posted occupancy or their inventory file does not support. You list setup as written. They change the diagram. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s default sets.
