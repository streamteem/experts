# Banquet event order packet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The banquet event order packet is the distributed set: cover or resume pointer, one BEO per function, diagrams, and sometimes AV or linen attachments. Typical US hotel catering desks issue the packet to kitchen, banquet captain, setup, AV, and the client for sign-off. Packet completeness is a file check: every definite function on the day should have a BEO page, or you name which function is missing. The packet is not a liquor license and not a fire stamp. Client-signed versus internal-only stays as their process. A sales contract in the folder is not a substitute for a missing BEO page. Multi-day groups may have one packet per day or one book; follow their naming. You assemble pages they already drafted or you list missing pages. They send the packet to the floor. Starter until they teach how *this* venue compiles the book.
