# Guest count (expected)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Guest count on an inquiry or early BEO is expected headcount, not the guarantee and not a fire-occupancy stamp. Typical US planners change the number several times. Keep expected, guaranteed, and actual as separate columns when their files split them. Do not overwrite expected with a hope that more RSVPs will come. Do not use expected as the kitchen produce number if they require a guarantee. Do not invent a count because the room “looks like it holds 180.” Posted occupancy is a posted number, not your estimate, and not law you declare. If expected exceeds posted occupancy on their door sheet, flag it as a question for them — you do not approve a variance. Setup chair counts should trace to guarantee when the guarantee exists, else to expected with that label. Starter until they teach how *this* venue names expected versus guaranteed.
