# Vendor meal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor-meal counts and menus (photographer, DJ, planner, band) come from the BEO or package file. Typical US wedding packages include a set number of vendor plates at a printed price or included. You copy that line. You do not invent a vendor-meal price or a complimentary count. Vendor meals are not the guest guarantee; keep them in their own column if the sheet does. Service time for vendor meals is on the timeline so they eat before the dance, if they wrote that. Allergy flags are labels. You do not feed anyone as this product. Missing vendor-meal lines on a vendor-heavy wedding are an ask. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s vendor-plate rule. Band plus photographer plus planner can exceed a package allotment; extra plates need the printed price. You do not feed vendors off the guest buffet unless the file says that is how they plate. Service time that collides with speeches is a timeline flag.
