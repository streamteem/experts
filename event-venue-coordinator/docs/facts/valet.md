# Valet / parking

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Valet, self-park, and overflow lots are operations lines from their parking or BEO file. Typical US clubs and urban halls contract a valet company for Saturday nights and use a lot map for overflow. You copy hours, vendor, and any posted stall count they stored. You do not invent a stall count or a valet price. You do not promise ADA parking as a legal determination; you can list stalls they marked on their lot map as inventory. Street-permit or tow notes stay as their file. Load-in trucks versus guest valet is a dock conflict if both use the same curb. You list the plan as written. They add a second lot. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s parking split. Overflow church or garage lots appear only if their parking file names them. You do not invent a shuttle. Posted stall counts are inventory, not a zoning opinion. Valet COI follows the renter-COI folder if their checklist includes the valet company.
