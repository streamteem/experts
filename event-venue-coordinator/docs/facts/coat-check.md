# Coat check

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Coat check is a seasonal or contracted service on the BEO: hours, attendant count from their labor file, paid versus complimentary as their policy. Typical US northern halls run it November through March and skip it in July unless the BEO says otherwise. You do not invent an attendant count or a per-coat fee. Lost-item process is their incident or coat-check policy file, not a claim you settle. Tickets and racks are inventory if they track them. You do not store guest card numbers for a paid coat check. If the BEO is silent and their seasonal policy file exists, quote the policy and ask whether this event uses it. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s coat-check season and fees. Complimentary versus paid is their posted policy, not a courtesy you invent for a VIP. Staffing an extra attendant is a labor-file question. You do not settle a lost-coat claim or store a guest card for the fee.
