# Deposit schedule (from file)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Deposit amounts, due dates, and remaining balance come from their contract payment schedule — not from a guessed percent. Typical US halls take a retainer to go definite, a second payment at thirty days, and a guarantee-week balance. You list due dates and amounts as printed. You do not run a card, store PAN, or mark paid unless their payment export says so. You do not invent a remaining balance. A missing payment is a flag for them, not a collection lawsuit you write. Refund and cancel dollars follow their cancel-policy file if they asked for that pack; you still do not send money. Starter until they teach how *this* venue records deposits versus banquet checks. Second-deposit and final-balance dates stay on the calendar even when the first retainer already posted. You do not apply a payment to the wrong event. NSF or failed-draft codes come from their export, not a collections letter you write.
