# Tasting (from their file)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tasting eligibility, guest count at the tasting, menu, and fees come from their tasting policy or wedding-package file. Typical US venues include one tasting for packages above a printed minimum and charge for extra seats. You copy that file. You do not invent a complimentary tasting. You do not invent a tasting menu price. Chef labor for tastings is their schedule, not a promise you make. Allergen notes at tasting are flags on the tasting BEO, not a diagnosis. A tasting is its own small BEO or ticket if they use one; it is not the reception guarantee. You list who is booked and what the policy allows. They cook. Starter until they teach *this* venue’s tasting rules. Extra tasting guests above the package allotment need the printed per-person fee. You do not book the chef’s Saturday without their calendar file. A no-show tasting is their cancel note, not a fee you invent.
