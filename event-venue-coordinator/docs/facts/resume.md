# Group resume

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A resume or event specification guide is the multi-day cover: VIP notes, billing, room-block pointer, and a list of functions that then have BEOs. Typical US hotels write a resume for in-house groups and skip it for a one-room Saturday wedding. You do not invent VIP amenities or a welcome amenity price. The resume is not a substitute for missing BEO pages; it should point to them. Billing instructions (master versus individual) stay as written and are not a card you run. You assemble the resume from their draft or list missing sections. They issue it to the hotel desks. Starter until they teach when *this* venue writes a resume. Quiet-hours and newspaper flags on a resume are house notes, not press you write. Missing billing instructions are asks. You do not invent a suite drop or a welcome amenity. Function lists on the resume that lack BEO pages stay on the BEO-missing list.
