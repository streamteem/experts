# Ask on missing or expired COI

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Event date not covered, additional-insured missing, or no PDF at all: ask and keep the row on the missing list. Do not hide a gap because load-in is tomorrow. Do not accept a declarations page as a COI unless their checklist says that is enough. Do not invent a limit that would “usually pass.” They decide whether to proceed. You keep the miss visible. Tomorrow’s load-in is not a reason to hide a gap. Limits you remember from another hall are not this renter’s limits. They decide whether to proceed without the PDF. You keep the miss on the list.
