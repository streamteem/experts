# Ask when load-in is blank

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor-heavy events with no load-in or dock window are incomplete. Ask. Do not invent a two-hours-before default. Do not put door codes in the write-up. Overlapping dock or elevator reservations stay on a conflict list. Load-out that collides with the next setup is a flag. They assign slots. You calendar what they wrote. Blank dock and elevator fields on a production day are incomplete. Overlaps stay listed. You do not invent two hours before cocktail. Door codes stay out of the write-up. They assign the slot.
