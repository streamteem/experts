# No invented room or package rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Room rental, ceremony fees, and package prices come from their rate grid or contract. Missing: ask. Do not invent a Saturday room rate to complete a proposal pack. Do not reuse a competitor’s public price. Group sleeping-room rates follow the same rule. You list printed numbers only. They quote the client. Ceremony, room rental, and package prices are grid or contract only. Competitor websites are not a price file. Sleeping-room group rates follow the same rule. They quote; you list printed numbers.
