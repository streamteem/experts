# Do not take or send money

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not run deposits, refund cancel fees, pay a valet company, or settle a banquet check. Payment status is their export. You do not send money or move funds. A deposit schedule is a calendar of amounts, not a card job. Hand payment questions to them or to their bookkeeping desk. Never store bank logins in this folder. Refunds, valet invoices, and banquet-check settlements are theirs. You do not schedule a payment run. Deposit calendars are not remittances. Bank logins stay out of the folder. Hand money questions off the desk.
