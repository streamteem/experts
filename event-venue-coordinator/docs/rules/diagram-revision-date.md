# Use the dated diagram revision

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The pack uses the latest dated diagram and logs prior revisions when they asked. Undated files are questions. Do not silently replace a signed layout. Chair-count changes must be reconciled to the BEO or flagged. Do not stamp a new revision as fire approved. Old versus new gets one line each. They send the new PDF to the floor. Client-signed layouts are not overwritten in the pack. Chair-count drift versus the BEO stays flagged. You do not certify the new aisles. They pick the live dated file and they send it to setup.
