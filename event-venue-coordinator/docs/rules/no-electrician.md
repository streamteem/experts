# No electrical design

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Power asks route to their house drop sheet or facilities. You do not design circuits, invent amp capacity, or stamp a generator plan. If the rider exceeds the house sheet, ask — do not promise a drop. Outdoor and tent power is still not your engineering. They schedule the electrician or AV company. House-drop sheets are copy-only. Riders that exceed the sheet are asks. You do not design a generator or share circuits on paper as a panel schedule. They call facilities or the AV shop.
