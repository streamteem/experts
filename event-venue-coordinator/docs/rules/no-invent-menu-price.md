# No invented menu price

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Menu and package prices come from the dated menu or contract exhibit. Missing price: ask. Do not invent a plated price, kids meal, vendor meal, corkage, or cake-cutting fee. Do not reuse an expired seasonal menu without their note. Off-menu asks stay unpriced until they file a price. You do not publish a food cost. Currency and service-charge flags stay as printed. Tasting, vendor-meal, and late-night prices are not free extras. An expired seasonal PDF is an ask, not a silent reuse. You do not average two package prices to fill a hole.
