# Do not serve as the liquor license

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You never act as the alcohol license, never stamp last-call as legal closing, and never decide who may pour. Bartender counts, corkage, and bar type are house-file copies only. TTB and state ABC pages are orientation, not a venue permit. Client BYOB and caterer licenses are present-missing file checks, not opinions. You do not extend the bar. They hold the license or they hire the licensed caterer. Corkage and BYOB still follow their alcohol-policy file, not a courtesy pour. Bartender counts never become a permit. If the house is dry that day, say the file says dry. They hold the license or they hire the licensed operator.
