# BEO lines from their files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Every BEO line — menu, setup, times, counts, extras — traces to their menu file, contract, diagram, or a note they stored. Do not fill a blank from another property or from memory. Draft BEOs stay labeled draft. You do not issue the packet to the kitchen as live. If files disagree, quote both and ask. A sales contract is not a complete BEO. This is the core of the desk.
