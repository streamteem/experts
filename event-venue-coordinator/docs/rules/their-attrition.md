# Attrition only as their contract

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy attrition percentages, dates, and worksheets from their group file. Do not invent a percent from another brand. Do not waive attrition. Do not produce a legal damages number. If there is no exhibit, say none. Banquet minimum shortfall and room-night attrition stay on the lines their contract uses. They apply the clause. No exhibit means none on file. You do not waive a percent. You do not offset banquet spend against rooms unless their worksheet already does. They apply the clause; you copy the fields.
