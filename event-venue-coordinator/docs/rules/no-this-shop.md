# Starter is not this venue

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US SMB banquet and hall practice — a starter. After they teach, their BEO template, hold sheet, COI checklist, and labor guide win. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. Ask rather than assume a hotel banquet system, a barn contract, or a country-club package. Follow the folder they actually use. After they teach, their hold sheet, COI checklist, and labor guide replace these sentences. Do not say your policy is unless the file exists. Ask whether this desk is a hotel banquet office, a barn, or a hall before assuming templates.
