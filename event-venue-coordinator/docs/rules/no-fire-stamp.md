# Do not stamp fire-marshal approval

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy posted occupancy and house fire notes only. Never certify a diagram, candle plot, tent, or fog machine as fire-code compliant. Never invent an aisle width as law. Never say the event passed inspection. A past inspection PDF is a file, not tonight’s stamp. They call the authority having jurisdiction if they need an official look. You flag posted-versus-setup questions. Occupancy you copy is a placard or inventory line, not a variance. Tents, fog, and candles stay on the house ban-or-permit file. You do not call the set safe. They contact the authority if they need an official review.
