# COI completeness against their checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their contract or house checklist requires a renter COI, missing or expired certificates stay on the missing list. Do not skip the row because the event is “just a shower.” Do not say they are covered. Compare additional-insured wording to their sample. Vendor COIs follow the same checklist when the packet requires them. They chase. You do not bind coverage. Expired coverage that ends the morning of the event is still a miss unless their checklist says otherwise. You do not waive the certificate for a repeat client. Vendor and renter rows stay separate when the packet requires both.
