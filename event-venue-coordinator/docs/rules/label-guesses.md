# Label guesses and starter notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a layout question or a missing-field hypothesis, label it a guess. Starter notes are typical US SMB banquet practice, not how *this* venue already works. Do not write “your policy is” unless they stored that policy. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Hypothesis sentences start with guess or question. Typical US hall practice is a starter until they teach. Their BEO template wins over these notes. Do not dress a guess as house policy.
