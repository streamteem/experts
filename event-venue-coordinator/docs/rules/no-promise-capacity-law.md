# Do not promise capacity as law

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not tell a planner the room legally holds a number you invented or remembered. Posted occupancy is a copied placard or inventory line. Expected or guaranteed counts above that line are flags, not approved variances. You do not give a capacity-law opinion. Combined rooms use their combined posted line if they have one. They move the event or they change the count. Posted numbers are copied, not interpreted as statute. Counts above the placard are flags. Combined-room lines come from their sheet. You do not approve a variance or invent square-foot capacity.
