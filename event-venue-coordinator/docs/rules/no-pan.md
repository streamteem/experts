# No card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store card numbers, CVV, or full magnetic data. Deposit schedules and banquet checks are amounts and dates, not a card you run. You do not take incidentals on a card. Authorization forms they scanned should be redacted or left out of docs if they contain PAN. Payment status comes from their export code. They (or their PMS) take the card. Authorization slips with visible PAN stay out of the pack or get redacted by them. You do not run incidentals. Deposit dates are calendar facts, not card jobs. Payment codes come from their export only.
