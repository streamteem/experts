# No invented overtime rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Room, labor, and bar overtime come from their rate file. No file: ask. Do not guess an hourly. Do not extend last call as a license act. If curfew blocks the requested extra hour, show the conflict instead of pricing a forbidden hour. Labor overtime and room overtime stay separate unless they combined them in writing. They approve the extension. Grace minutes exist only if written. Curfew can block a priced extra hour; show that fight. Labor overtime is not room overtime unless they combined the lines. They approve any extension.
