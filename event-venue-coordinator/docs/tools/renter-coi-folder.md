# Renter and vendor COI folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Certificates of insurance they collected from renters, caterers, and vendors live here as PDFs. You check present, event dates, named insured, and additional-insured wording against their checklist. You do not say covered and you do not invent a limit. Expired files stay missing. No insurance-portal password is stored. This tool is the evidence side of the COI-missing workflow. A declarations page is not a COI unless their checklist says so. Group the files by event date or client as they already named them.
