# AV list file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The AV list is their house or rental equipment sheet, sometimes a third-party quote they stored. You copy items and printed prices from that file. You do not invent a projector model or a labor hour. Power lines on the list are asks, not circuit designs. Client-owned AV still needs vendor name and load-in if their process requires it. No AV-portal login is stored. Use this file when a BEO pack needs equipment completeness or when load-in depends on production. Missing list plus a BEO that says “full AV” is a question.
