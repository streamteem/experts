# Hold / tentative sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The hold sheet is their working CSV or export of tentative options: date, room, option rank, contact, and cutoff if they track it. You list holds and conflicts. You do not upgrade a row to definite. You do not invent a release date. Two rows on one room-date stay both visible. Checks often look for date, room, and status columns. No PMS login is stored here. This tool is the hold side of hold-versus-definite and the source for cutoff-due lists. Empty cutoff cells become asks, not guessed clocks.
