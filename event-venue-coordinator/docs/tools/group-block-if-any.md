# Group room-block sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The group-block sheet or PMS pickup export holds contracted rooms, reserved pickup, rate as printed, and room cutoff date. If they have no sleeping rooms, the file may be empty — say none, do not invent a block. You do not invent a group rate or a pickup number. You do not wash rooms. This tool feeds the group-pickup workflow. Checks may look for date and rooms columns. No PMS password belongs in the folder. Banquet minimums stay on the banquet files, not this sheet.
