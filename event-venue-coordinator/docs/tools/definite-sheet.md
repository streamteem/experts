# Definite / contracted sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The definite sheet lists signed or confirmed events: date, room, client, status as they coded it, and pointers to contract or BEO. You do not add a definite without their status. You do not invent a deposit received. Use this file as the event list for COI-missing packs and as the other side of hold-versus-definite. Checks may look for date, room, and status. Cancelled rows stay labeled cancelled if they still appear. Export only; no password in the folder.
