# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is how this desk builds hold-versus-definite lists, cutoff lists, COI-missing rows, and write-up tables. Typical columns include date, room, status, client, and flags. You do not invent rates or counts in blank cells. Export from their files; no login stored. Checks look for the columns the workflow names. This tool is the usual pack step after PDFs and sheets are pulled. Keep hold and definite as they coded them. One event per row unless they asked for a function-level list.
