# Membership export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their membership CSV or PDF: member label, plan or type, status, join date, dues as printed, cycle if they bill, and flags such as freeze or pending cancel. Typical US SMB clubs export from ABC Financial, ClubReady, Mindbody, Daxko, PushPress, Glofox, or Mariana Tek. You list from that file. You do not invent dues or a plan. Pointer only. No password. Checks often look for member_id and status. This tool feeds freeze-and-cancel, missing-waiver joins, failed-draft context, and guest-host checks. Starter until they teach this club’s export path.
