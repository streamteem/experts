# No card PAN or bank numbers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Amounts and last-four if their export already uses last-four. Never store full PAN, CVV, PIN, magnetic data, routing, or full bank account numbers. If a scan of an EFT form is too wide, do not copy the extra digits into docs/. Card-on-file is a flag. You do not photograph cards. PCI overview pages are orientation: extra digits still stay out. Point at their billing system and stop. Starter until they teach what their exports contain — and you still strip extras.
