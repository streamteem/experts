# Do not invent freeze length

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Freeze length and whether days are consecutive come from their policy file or the dated freeze row. Do not invent thirty days. Do not invent a medical-freeze length from a note you must not read as a clinician. Missing end date: ask. They set the dates. Do not extend a freeze to tidy a failed-draft list. Starter until they teach this club’s freeze lengths. Prepaid and month-to-month freeze lengths stay on the matching policy file.
