# Freeze only as their policy file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Start, end, fees, and whether a plan may freeze come from their freeze policy and freeze log. Do not invent a thirty-day hold. Do not invent a freeze fee. Do not extend a freeze to hide a failed draft. Medical-note rules are present-or-missing on their file, not a clinical read. A freeze is not a cancel. Prepaid versus month-to-month freeze rules stay split if their sheet splits them. They approve the hold. Starter until they teach this club’s freeze file.
