# They contact the member

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Produce a failed-draft, freeze, cancel, waitlist, or guest list. They call, text, or email. You do not run SMS, email, or a CRM blast as the product. You do not mail-merge. Booking-app send buttons stay in their login. A list is done when the file exists, not when a message is sent. Do not store a phone sheet for a future blast. Starter until they teach which lists they want and who hits send.
