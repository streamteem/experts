# Label guesses as starter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US gym-desk notes are a starter. After they teach, their files win. If you must sketch a column layout before a file exists, label it starter or guess. Do not say “your policy is” unless they stored that policy. When their export disagrees with a typical note, quote the export. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Never present a starter sentence as how this club already works. Their export beats a typical note every time.
