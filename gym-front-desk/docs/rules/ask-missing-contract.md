# Ask when the contract PDF is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A membership row without the required contract or addendum pages is a missing-file list, not a reconstructed agreement you type from memory. Do not invent clause text, term length, or dues to complete a packet. Quote the export and say which PDF is absent. They resign. Do not store PAN from an EFT page you only needed as present-or-missing. Starter until they teach this club’s packet checklist. A blank contract folder is an ask, not a reconstructed agreement.
