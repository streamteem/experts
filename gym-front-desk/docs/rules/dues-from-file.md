# Dues only from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dues, enrollment fees, add-on amenity prices, and promo dollars come from their dated price file, contract PDF, or billing export. Missing file: ask. Do not invent a rate from another club or from last year. If contract and export disagree, quote both. Do not average. Do not raise a founding or student row to the walk-in rate without their file. Tax on dues stays as printed. They change prices. Starter until they teach this club’s live dues file.
