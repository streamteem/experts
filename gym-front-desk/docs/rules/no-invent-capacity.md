# Do not invent class capacity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Capacity is the number on their schedule or room file. Do not guess from mats you remember. Do not invent a fire-code legal limit. Blank capacity: ask. Holiday short classes do not change capacity unless they edited the file. They set the number. Do not overbook to hide a waitlist. Starter until they teach this studio’s capacity column. Bike or reformer counts they stored beat a guessed room size every time.
