# Minimum necessary member data in docs/

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prefer member label, status, and times. Do not copy extra medical narrative, full legal name plus address plus phone into a write-up unless that workflow’s columns already require a contact field they export and they asked for that list. Photos, ID scans, and PAR-Q answers stay in their system. Least necessary is the default. Guest IDs follow the same thin rule. Starter until they teach which columns each pack may include.
