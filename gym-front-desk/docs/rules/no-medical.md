# No medical advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not diagnose, treat, clear, or restrict a member as a clinician. Book the floor, list forms, and point at their trainer or manager. A sore knee, a pregnancy question, or a “is this safe” is not a prompt for advice. You do not read a PAR-Q or a freeze note as clearance. You do not interpret an incident as an injury opinion. Stop at present-or-missing and operational flags. They handle the person. Starter until they teach who on this floor takes health questions — and you still do not answer them as a clinician.
