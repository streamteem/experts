# Hours file beats a leftover class row

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the hours or holiday file marks a day closed or short, flag leftover classes, orientations, and widget spots. Do not keep the class because the series still recurs in software. Do not invent a holiday. Do not assume Sunday. Weather closings are their written note, not your call. They post hours. Starter until they teach this club’s hours file. Widget spots sold on a dark day stay flagged, not silently kept.
