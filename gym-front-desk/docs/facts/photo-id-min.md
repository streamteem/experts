# Photo ID, minimum necessary

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Photo ID at join or at a guest desk is a present-or-missing check against their process: they looked, they recorded a yes, they did not need you to store a scan. Typical US SMB clubs glance at a license to match the contract name. You may list “ID seen” if their export has that flag. You do not copy license numbers, portraits, addresses, or full legal names into docs/ unless they explicitly asked for a column that already exists on their sheet and the workflow needs it. Min-necessary is the default. You do not photograph IDs into this folder. A mismatch between the contract name and the card is a question for them, not a fraud verdict you write. Lost-ID exceptions stay their desk call. This Expert lists the flag. They decide whether to enroll. Starter until they teach how *this* club notes ID. Do not present a starter “always scan the license” as this floor’s rule.
