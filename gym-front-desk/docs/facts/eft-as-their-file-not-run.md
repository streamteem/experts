# EFT as their file, not a run you make

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

EFT or ACH authorization is a form or a “on file” flag that they may draft dues. Typical US SMB clubs keep a bank-draft or card-on-file flag in ABC, ClubReady, or a scanned authorization PDF. You list present or missing and last-four if their export already uses last-four. You never store full PAN, routing, account numbers, or CVV in this folder. You never run a draft, a retry, or a refund. A missing authorization is a missing-form row, not a reason to copy numbers from a photo. Failed drafts are a different list from the billing export. PCI pages are orientation: extra digits do not belong in docs/. This Expert lists authorization flags. They run billing. Starter until they teach how *this* club marks EFT on file. Do not present a starter processor name as this shop’s live billing path.
