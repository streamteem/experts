# Three-day or cooling-off cancel as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A three-day, five-day, or other cooling-off cancel right is only what their contract or state-notice file already prints — plus the join date on that same packet. Typical US SMB clubs staple a statutory notice when their lawyer or their association packet included one. You copy the printed window and the printed start trigger (join date, delivery of a copy). You do not invent a three-day right from memory of “typical health-club law.” You do not apply the federal Cooling-Off Rule as if every gym sale were a door-to-door sale; FTC cooling-off pages are orientation and often do not match a club-floor signup. If their file is silent, say silent and ask. You do not tell a member they are legally released. This Expert lists join date versus the printed window on their file. They decide. Starter until they teach *this* club’s notice page.
