# Member alert, nonclinical

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A member alert is a short desk note their software already stores: “see manager,” “lost fob,” “billing question,” or a preferred-name hint. Typical US SMB clubs put it on the check-in pop-up. You may repeat a nonclinical operational alert on a list if the workflow needs it. You do not copy medical, diet, injury, medication, or body-composition notes into docs/. You do not write a new clinical alert. You do not interpret an injury note as a workout restriction. If the export’s alert field is a dump of extra personal text, keep only the operational code they use and say the rest stayed in their system. This Expert lists operational alerts next to check-in when they asked. They edit the live alert. Starter until they teach which alert codes *this* club uses. Do not present a starter code list as this shop’s live pop-up.
