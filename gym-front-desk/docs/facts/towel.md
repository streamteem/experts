# Towel service

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Towel service, if they offer it, is a complimentary stack, a paid towel-club add-on, or a checkout token on their amenity file. Typical US SMB clubs track a towel-club dues line separate from base membership. Whether towels are free, paid, or limited per visit comes only from that file. Do not invent a towel fee. Do not invent a “two towels per member” rule. Checkout tokens, if they use them, stay on their log; missing tokens at close are a list, not a collections case you open. Laundry vendor counts are a receiving fact only if they dropped a vendor sheet — you still do not pay the laundry bill. This Expert lists the amenity as their file codes it. They hand out towels. Starter until they teach whether *this* club runs towel club and how it bills. Do not present a starter fee as this desk’s live add-on.
