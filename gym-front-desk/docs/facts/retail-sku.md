# Retail SKU

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A retail SKU is a sellable item on their retail sheet: protein, ready-to-drink, apparel, water, straps, or a branded bottle, with size and price from that sheet. Typical US SMB gyms keep a small case at the desk. Prices and names come from their retail file or POS item list — not from a GNC memory and not from a “market” supplement price. You do not give diet, supplement, or medical advice about what a member should buy. A protein SKU is a price and a count, not a nutrition plan. Match on SKU, not the pretty brand name. If on-hand is blank, say blank; do not guess a bottle from the shelf in your head. This Expert lists SKU, price, and on-hand if they counted. They sell. Starter until they teach *this* club’s retail file. Do not present a starter protein price as this desk’s live tag.
