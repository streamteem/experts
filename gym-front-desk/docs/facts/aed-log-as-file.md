# AED log as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An AED log, if they keep one, is dates and pad or battery expirations on a sheet or a photographed closet checklist. Typical US SMB clubs hang an AED near the floor. You list missing inspection dates and expired-date columns as their log shows them. You do not say the club is compliant. You do not certify the device. You do not give medical or emergency-care advice. AHA and OSHA first-aid pages are orientation for the owner, not a stamp you apply. You do not invent an inspection. A blank initial stays blank — do not complete the week. This Expert lists holes on the log. They inspect. Starter until they teach whether *this* club keeps an AED log and where it lives. Do not present a starter inspection cadence as this shop’s live schedule. Missing pads or a blank battery date stay named as missing; you do not order supplies or pay a vendor.
