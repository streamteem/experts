# Corporate membership

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A corporate or employer membership is a plan tied to a company code on their corporate file: employer name as they spelled it, discount or billed-to-company flag, and eligibility proof they already require (email domain, letter). Typical US SMB clubs invoice some employers and draft others. Rates and who qualifies come only from that file — not from a remembered “all hospitals get 10 percent.” You do not invent a corporate rate. You do not add an employer to the list. If eligibility proof is missing, say missing. A terminated employee on their file may convert to a consumer plan; that conversion is their rule, not a guess. This Expert lists corporate rows and missing proof. They bill the company or the member. Starter until they teach *this* club’s employer list. Do not present a starter discount as this shop’s live corporate deal.
