# Failed draft

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A failed draft is a row on their billing export: member, amount as the export printed it, fail date, reason code as they stored it (NSF, expired card, closed account), and retry or not. Typical US SMB clubs retry on a schedule they already configured. You list from that export. You do not invent a decline reason. You do not invent the amount. You do not retry the draft. You do not call the member as a collections product; they contact. Do not copy full PAN from a processor email into docs/. A failed draft is not a collections lawsuit and not a legal demand you write. Access flags after a fail stay their code. This Expert builds the failed-draft list. They retry or they freeze access. Starter until they teach how *this* club codes fails and retries. Do not present a starter retry day as this shop’s live schedule.
