# Lost fob

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A lost-fob row is their log that a credential was reported missing: member label, date, old token if they store it, replacement issued or not, and any replacement fee on their fee file. Typical US SMB clubs deactivate the old fob in the access system and encode a new one. You list from that log. You do not invent a replacement fee. You do not deactivate the live door as this product. You do not charge the card. A stolen-fob story is still a log row, not a police report you file. If the fee file is silent, ask. Do not copy a full unexpired token string into the write-up if their log already has an internal id. This Expert lists lost versus replaced. They kill the old fob. Starter until they teach *this* club’s replacement fee and deactivation step. Do not present a starter ten-dollar fee as this desk’s live charge.
