# Day close

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day close is the desk’s end-of-day pack: membership drafts they already ran or failed, retail and day-pass tenders from the POS export, and any cash they typed after they counted. Typical US SMB gyms close in ClubReady, ABC, Square, or a photographed Z-tape they drop in the folder. Totals come from that export. You do not invent a cash-drawer total. You do not stand at the till. You do not mark EFT as collected unless the export says so. Missing tender columns stay named as missing. Retail stays split from dues if their report splits them. Gift-card and package liability lines are not today’s dues. This Expert writes the close from the file. They count cash. They run drafts. Starter until they teach which report is *this* club’s official close. Do not present a starter tender list as this shop’s live Z-tape.
