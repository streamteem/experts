# PT session count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

PT session count is remaining versus purchased on their training export or punch sheet: package id, purchased, used, remaining, last session date if they store it, and expiration if printed. Typical US SMB clubs decrement when the trainer marks the session in ClubReady, Mindbody, or a paper punch card they scanned into the folder. You copy those numbers. You do not invent a remaining count so a trainer’s book looks even. You do not add a complimentary session. A no-show that they coded as used stays used; a no-show they coded as still available stays available — their code wins. Do not interpret a leftover pack as a medical need for more training. This Expert builds a remaining-session list. They decide comps and extensions. Starter until they teach how *this* club decrements sessions. Do not present a starter “no-show burns a session” as this shop’s rule unless their file says so.
