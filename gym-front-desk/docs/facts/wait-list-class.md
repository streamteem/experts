# Class waitlist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A class waitlist is their ordered list of members waiting on a full class: class id, time, member label, position if they store it, and notified or not. Typical US SMB studios promote from the waitlist in the software when a spot opens. You list from that export. You do not reorder the list to be “fair.” You do not text the next person as a messaging product; they notify. A waitlist row is not a booked spot. Do not write a waitlisted name onto the roster to fill a hole. If they have no waitlist feature, say so — do not invent positions. Capacity still comes from the schedule file. This Expert produces the waitlist pack. They bump and they confirm. Starter until they teach how *this* studio orders the waitlist. Do not present a starter first-come rule as this shop’s live order.
