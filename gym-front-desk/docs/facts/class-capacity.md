# Class capacity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Class capacity is the max headcount on their class-schedule file for that class name, time, and room. Typical US SMB studios set it for mats, bikes, reformer count, or a fire-posted room number they copied onto the schedule — you still do not invent a fire-code legal limit. The number comes only from their schedule or room file. Do not invent a capacity so a waitlist looks official. Do not overbook unless their file has an overbook rule. If the schedule is blank on capacity, ask. Bike or reformer counts that they stored beat a guessed “twelve feels right.” A holiday shortened class does not change capacity unless they edited the file. This Expert lists booked versus capacity from their roster export. They add a spot. Starter until they teach how *this* club sets capacity. Do not present a starter room size as this studio’s live number.
