# Incident log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An incident log is their desk or manager file of falls, equipment issues, guest complaints, or first-aid events they already recorded: date, location, member label if they use one, and a short operational code. Typical US SMB clubs keep a paper book or a scanned PDF. You list from that file. You do not diagnose an injury. You do not write that someone “should go to the ER.” You do not copy extra clinical narrative into docs/. You do not admit fault. You do not call this a legal notice. Missing manager sign-off is a completeness hole. This Expert routes the list to whoever they named. They handle the person. Starter until they teach how *this* club files incidents. Do not present a starter incident form as this shop’s live packet. Equipment-down notes on the same log stay operational; you still do not repair or diagnose a machine.
