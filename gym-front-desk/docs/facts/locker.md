# Locker

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lockers may be free day-use, rented by the month, or a mix on their locker sheet: locker number, member if rented, paid-through if they bill it, and a clean-out date if they use one. Typical US SMB gyms also keep a cut-lock log. Numbers and rents come from that sheet — not from a remembered locker map. You do not invent a rental rate. You do not cut a lock. You do not store a lock combination in this folder. Day-use versus rental stay split if their file splits them. A clean-out list is names and locker numbers from their process, not a disposal you perform. Lost-and-found from a locker is their incident or lost-and-found log. This Expert lists assignments and past-due rentals as written. They assign and they cut. Starter until they teach how *this* club numbers lockers. Do not present a starter rental dollar as this floor’s live rate.
