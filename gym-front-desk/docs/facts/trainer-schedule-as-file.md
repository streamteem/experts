# Trainer schedule as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The trainer schedule is their file of who is on the floor or in a session block: trainer name as the software spells it, start, end, member or “floor,” and location if they use rooms. Typical US SMB clubs keep it in Mindbody, ABC, or a photographed whiteboard. You list from that file. You do not write a training program. You do not move a client to another trainer to balance the book. You do not invent a session that is not on the file. Clashes with a class the same trainer teaches are flags, not silent moves. Session decrements still come from the PT count export, not from guessing that a calendar block burned a punch. This Expert lists the day’s trainer book. They train. Starter until they teach how *this* club books trainers versus group. Do not present a starter block length as this floor’s live session.
