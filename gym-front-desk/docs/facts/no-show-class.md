# Class no-show

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A class no-show or late cancel is a status on their class roster export: booked, canceled inside the window, or no-show. Typical US SMB studios attach a strike, a fee, or a burned credit — those consequences come only from their class policy file. You list the names and the status as coded. You do not invent a no-show fee. You do not rewrite late-cancel as no-show to fit a fee. You do not charge the card. They decide whether to keep a credit. A PT no-show is a different file if they split training and group. This Expert builds the no-show list. They contact the member if they choose. Starter until they teach *this* studio’s late-cancel window and fee file. Do not present a starter twelve-hour rule as this shop’s posted policy. A waitlist promotion that never confirmed is not a no-show unless their export already coded it that way.
