# Member photo, minimum necessary

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A member photo is a face-on-file flag many clubs use at the desk to match the person to the roster. Typical US SMB clubs snap it in the membership system. You may list “photo on file” or “missing photo” from their export. You do not copy the image into this folder. You do not store extra portraits. You do not run a recognition product. A missing photo is a checklist hole, not a reason to deny access unless their access file already says photo-required. Guest photos follow the same min-necessary rule: flag, not a file dump. This Expert lists missing photos if they asked. They take the picture. Starter until they teach whether *this* club requires a photo. Do not present a starter “always photograph” as this lobby’s live rule. A blurry or outdated photo flag on their export stays their words; you do not retake or store a replacement image here.
