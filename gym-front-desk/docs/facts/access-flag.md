# Access flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An access flag is their software’s door or desk code: paid, past due, frozen, canceled, expired, pending waiver, lost fob, or a nonclinical alert they already typed. Typical US SMB clubs use the flag so a scan can deny without the desk inventing a reason. Flags come only from their access export — never from a guessed balance or a collections rumor. If a member checked in and the flag says deny, that is an exception row, not a reason to rewrite the flag. If the flag says allow and the freeze log says frozen, quote both and ask. You do not turn access on or off in the live door system as this product. You do not invent a “collections hold.” Clinical or diet notes do not belong in an access flag you copy into docs/. This Expert lists flags next to check-ins. They change the live flag. Starter until they teach how *this* club codes access. Do not present a starter code list as this shop’s vocabulary.
