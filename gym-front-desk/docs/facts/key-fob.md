# Key fob or access credential

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A key fob, keytag, barcode card, or phone-pass is the credential their access file ties to a member row. Typical US SMB clubs encode fobs at the desk and also use an app QR. You list fob number or token id as their export already shows it — not a number you invent to complete a row. Do not store a photo of a barcode that is effectively a secret door key in docs/ if a member label plus “fob on file” is enough. Lost and replacement are a different fact. Access still follows the access flag; a valid fob on a frozen member is an exception, not a reason to invent a new flag. You do not program the live door panel as this product unless they taught that step and still you store no door-system password. Starter until they teach how *this* club numbers fobs. Do not present a starter brand of lock as this lobby’s live hardware.
