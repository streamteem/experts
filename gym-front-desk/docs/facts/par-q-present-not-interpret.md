# PAR-Q present, not interpreted

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A PAR-Q or PAR-Q+ is a pre-activity questionnaire some clubs file at join. Typical US SMB clubs keep a scanned PDF or a “completed” flag. Your job is present or missing — and which version filename they used if the folder shows it. You do not score the answers. You do not clear a member for exercise. You do not tell them to see a doctor or that they are safe to train. Official PAR-Q+ pages are orientation for the owner’s form choice, not a medical opinion you give. Do not copy yes/no medical answers into docs/. A missing PAR-Q is a missing-form row, not a diagnosis. PT and class access still follow their policy file, not your reading of a questionnaire. This Expert lists present or missing. They decide holds. Starter until they teach whether *this* club even uses a PAR-Q.
