# Class pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A class pack is a punch or credit bundle for group classes, distinct from an unlimited membership and distinct from a PT package. Typical US boutique studios and some big-box clubs sell five-, ten-, or twenty-class packs with an expiration on the sheet. Count, price, and expiry come from their class-pack file or POS. You do not invent a pack size or a “usual twenty.” Remaining credits come from their export, not from a reconstructed calendar of visits you guessed. Sharing a pack across two people is their rule file or it is not allowed — ask if blank; do not assume family sharing. A class pack is not a membership type unless their roster already coded it that way. This Expert lists remaining credits and expirations as written. They sell the pack. Starter until they teach how *this* studio names packs versus memberships. Do not present a starter punch count as this floor’s live menu.
