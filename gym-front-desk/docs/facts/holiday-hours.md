# Holiday and special hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Holiday and special hours are their hours file: closed days, short days, and whether classes or child watch still run. Typical US SMB clubs post Thanksgiving, Christmas, and weather closings on a sheet or a lobby PDF. That file beats a leftover class series and beats a widget that sold a spot on a dark day. You do not invent a holiday. You do not assume the club is closed on a Sunday or on a federal holiday. Weather closings are their call already written or a note they dropped — you do not decide snow. 24-hour access clubs still have desk-hour exceptions on the same file if they use one. This Expert flags check-ins, classes, and orientations that fall outside posted hours. They post hours. Starter until they teach *this* club’s hours file. Do not present a starter holiday list as this shop’s live closings.
