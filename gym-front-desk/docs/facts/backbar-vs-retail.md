# Backbar versus retail

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Backbar is product the floor uses in a class, a PT session, or a complimentary towel-counter cup — not a retail sale. Typical US SMB clubs that sell shakes also mix a staff or class sample from a different bin. If their sheet splits retail and backbar, keep the split. A backbar empty tub is not a sold unit. Do not transfer a unit in the pack unless their file already shows the transfer. Commission and tax, if they track them, often follow the retail column only — copy their split, do not invent a commission. Shake-bar recipes stay out of docs/; you are not writing a diet. This Expert keeps the two piles apart on the close. They restock. Starter until they teach how *this* club bins retail versus backbar. Do not present a starter split as this shop’s live inventory rule.
