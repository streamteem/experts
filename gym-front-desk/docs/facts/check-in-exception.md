# Check-in exception

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A check-in exception is a denied scan, a manual override, a forgotten fob, or a name that is not on the roster. Typical US SMB clubs export exceptions from the door log or the desk writes them on a sheet. You list time, member or guest label, flag at the time if the export has it, and what the desk did if they recorded it. You do not invent an override. You do not clear the exception. A guest treated as a member, a frozen member who walked, and a past-due deny that was waved through are different rows if their codes differ — keep their words. You do not change the live access flag to make yesterday’s exception disappear. This Expert builds the exception list. They decide the override policy. Starter until they teach how *this* club logs exceptions. Do not present a starter override as this lobby’s live habit.
