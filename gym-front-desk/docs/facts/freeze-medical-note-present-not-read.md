# Freeze medical note present, not read

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A medical freeze, if their policy uses one, is a note or letter they stored as a file: present or missing, date on the letter if the filename or a log column shows a date, and the freeze row it supports. Typical US SMB clubs ask for a clinician note before a long medical hold. You do not read the note as a diagnosis. You do not copy the diagnosis, medication list, or extra clinical sentences into docs/. You do not decide whether the condition “counts.” You do not invent how many days a medical freeze lasts — their policy file states the length or it is an ask. A missing note is a missing-form row. This Expert lists present or missing against the freeze log. They accept the note. Starter until they teach how *this* club files medical-freeze proof. Do not present a starter doctor letter as this shop’s live requirement unless their file says so.
