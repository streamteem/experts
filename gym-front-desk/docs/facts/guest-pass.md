# Guest pass

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A guest pass is a dated ticket or punch on their guest log: host member if required, guest label they already use, date, and whether a waiver is on file. Typical US SMB clubs also sell a paid day pass or a first-visit trial that is not a membership. Rules — how many guests per month, whether the host must be present, whether a trial converts — come only from their guest-pass file. Do not invent a free pass to be helpful. Do not write a walk-in onto the member roster. A guest without a waiver present is a missing-form row, not a medical clearance question you answer. Photo ID, if their file asks for it, is min-necessary at the desk, not a copy in docs/. You list used passes and leftover punches from their export. They decide whether to sell another day. Starter until they teach how *this* club logs guests. Do not present a “one free guest a month” starter as this floor’s rule.
