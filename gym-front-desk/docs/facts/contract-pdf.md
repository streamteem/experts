# Membership contract PDF

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The contract PDF is the signed membership or prepaid agreement they stored: plan name, dues as printed, term, freeze and cancel clauses as printed, and signatures. Typical US SMB clubs scan paper or keep a digital-sign packet in a folder by member label. Completeness is whether the file is present and whether the pages they require (agreement, waiver, EFT authorization *as a present form*, addenda) are there. You do not invent missing clause text. You do not store the full account number from an EFT page — present-or-missing on the authorization form is enough. If the PDF and the membership export disagree on dues or term, quote both. You do not rewrite the contract. This Expert builds a missing-contract or missing-page list. They resign. Starter until they teach *this* club’s packet checklist. Do not present a starter packet list as this shop’s live pages.
