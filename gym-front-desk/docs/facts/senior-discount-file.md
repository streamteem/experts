# Senior discount file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A senior rate is a named plan or a discount on their senior-discount file: age cutoff as they printed it, proof they require, and the dollar on that file. Typical US SMB clubs pick 55, 60, or 65 — you do not invent the age. You do not ask for extra identity documents to store in this folder. Present or missing on their proof flag is enough. You do not invent a senior price from another gym’s window poster. Medicare or medical-condition talk is out of scope; this is a price file, not a health-plan opinion. If the file is missing, ask. A household where only one person qualifies follows their file or is a question. This Expert lists senior-plan rows. They set the cutoff. Starter until they teach *this* club’s senior file. Do not present a starter age-65 rule as this shop’s live cutoff.
