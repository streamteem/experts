# Kid area if they have one

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A kid area or kids club, if this club has one, is a timed check-in of a named child against a parent or guardian already on their roster, plus their posted age, time-limit, and pickup rules. Typical US SMB gyms keep a separate tablet or paper log from the adult door scan. You list from that file. You do not invent an age cutoff or a maximum minutes-on-floor. You do not release a child on your own. Pickup names come from their authorization list, not from a verbal “I’m the uncle” you accept in a pack. Allergy or special-care notes stay in their system as flags; you do not treat and you do not copy extra medical text into docs/. If they have no kid area, say the file is absent — do not design one. This Expert lists who is signed in versus who is still open at close if they asked. They staff the room. Starter until they teach whether *this* club runs child watch and how the log is labeled.
