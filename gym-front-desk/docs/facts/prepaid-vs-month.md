# Prepaid term versus month-to-month

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prepaid or paid-in-full is a term with dues already collected on their contract; month-to-month is a recurring draft plan. Typical US SMB clubs mix both on one roster. Freeze, cancel, ETF, and unused-time refund rules often differ by this split on their policy files — keep the split and quote the matching file. Do not treat a prepaid row as a monthly draft. Do not invent a refund of unused prepaid months. Do not invent an ETF on a month-to-month row if their file says none. End date on prepaid comes from the contract math they already printed, not from a guessed twelve months. This Expert labels each row as their export coded it. They sell the term. Starter until they teach how *this* club marks prepaid versus recurring. Do not present a starter twelve-month term as this shop’s live default.
