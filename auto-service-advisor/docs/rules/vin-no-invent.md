# VIN from their RO

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy VIN from their RO, saved photo, or shop field. Do not invent, complete, or correct a VIN. Do not paste extra owner personal data into docs. A public decode is only after they supplied the VIN and is build data, not a diagnosis. If export and scan disagree, ask. Independent and dealer parts errors start with a bad VIN; you do not silently pick one. Plate is not a VIN. Missing VIN when their process requires it is a question, not a decoder you run on a guess.
