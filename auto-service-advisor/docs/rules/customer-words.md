# Keep customer words

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not replace noise on left turns with a made-up diagnosis or a part name. Conditions stay with the concern: when, speed, gear, hot or cold, as they said. If you tidy spelling, keep the meaning. Independent and dealer write-ups both fail when the advisor cleans the sentence into a cause. After the tech writes a cause, the original customer words stay. A check engine concern stays check engine until a scan file exists. Ask for missing conditions rather than inventing them. Typical US desk practice until they teach required concern fields.
