# Use their labor source

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two labor guides in one estimate. Ask which file is source of truth for this shop. Factory time, Mitchell, ALLDATA, Motor, and a posted menu are different sources. Independent and dealer rates and operations differ; use the sheet they named. Missing hours are questions, not memory. Menu packages stay on the menu. You do not invent book time or a shop multiplier. Clock time does not replace billed time. Parts prices still come from their parts file, not the guide's list price you assume.
