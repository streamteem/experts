# One RO per write-up pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A single-RO write-up does not mix two tickets unless they asked for a multi-RO list. Open-RO lists still keep one row per ticket. Do not merge two numbers because the VIN matches. If two tickets look like the same car, ask which is live. Independent and dealer boards both break when rows are combined. History may cite a prior RO; that citation is a field, not a second ticket stuffed into cause. Keep parts and auth on the number they named.
