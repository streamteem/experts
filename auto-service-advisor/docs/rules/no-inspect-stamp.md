# No inspection stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not pass or fail a state safety or emissions inspection. You do not say the vehicle is street-legal or safe to drive. If they have an inspection form, you only note whether the scan is in the folder. MPI and digital inspections are not that stamp. Independent and dealer licensed lanes still belong to a licensed person. EPA or state program pages are orientation, not a result. If they billed inspection and the form is missing, ask. Do not fill pass fail boxes. Keep this line out of cause and correction.
