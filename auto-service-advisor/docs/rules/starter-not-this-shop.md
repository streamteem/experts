# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US independent and dealer service-desk practice. Until they teach, treat them as guesses. Save their version after they correct it. Do not present a starter sentence as how this shop already works. Software names, labor guides, MPI products, and auth methods vary. Ask which export is source of truth. Do not invent their policy, loaner rules, or fee percent. The pack should show what their files show plus questions, not a chain's playbook pasted on as fact.
