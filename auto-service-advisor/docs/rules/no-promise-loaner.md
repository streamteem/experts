# Do not promise a loaner

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Only list a loaner if their log assigns one. Do not offer shuttle times they did not write. Do not promise a dealer courtesy car because the pay type is warranty. Empty log means no assignment. Independent shops may have no fleet; that is not a gap you fill. Waiter status does not create a loaner. If they arranged a rental, copy that file. Transportation notes are not a diagnosis. Ask who approves exceptions rather than inventing one on the pack.
