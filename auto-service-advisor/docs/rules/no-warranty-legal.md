# No warranty coverage opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may list claim number in file or warranty pay type on RO. You may not say the manufacturer or a service contract will pay. You do not give a legal warranty opinion. Shop policy length is quoted from their sheet, not invented as 12/12. Independent and dealer coverage screens still require their file. Comebacks do not become approved claims in this pack. Policy adjustments are manager amounts only. OEM versus aftermarket is a quote label, not a coverage ruling you make.
