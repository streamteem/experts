# Parts status from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On order, in stock, backorder, and ETA come only from vendor confirmations or the parts person's file. No guessed ETA. You do not log into the portal. Special-order and core notes copy from that same paper. Independent jobbers and dealer depots use different status words; use theirs. If the write-up needs parts status and there is no confirmation, ask. Do not assume the part is in the building. Do not switch OEM and aftermarket to fix a delay as a safety ruling.
