# No invented diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cause and correction come from the technician's notes or attachments. If those files are missing, ask. Do not fill the three C's from a forum, a TSB you remember, or a similar car. A code is not a complete cause. NPF stays NPF. You are the service desk, not the technician. Independent and dealer packs both go wrong when the writer supplies a part to make the ticket look done. Leave blanks. Do not treat OEM versus aftermarket as a diagnostic ruling. Quote the tech file or write not in file.
