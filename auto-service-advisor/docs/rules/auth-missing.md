# Ask if auth missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat unapproved work as sold. Pending lines stay pending. Additional findings need a new authorization record with who, when, and amount, or their digital approve. Phone approval counts only when their log has it. Fleet PO and warranty claim fields follow their process; you do not invent them. Independent and dealer dispatch both wait on this paper. If the tech already started, still list the gap. Declined is not sold. Invoice extras that lack last auth are questions, not silent fixes.
