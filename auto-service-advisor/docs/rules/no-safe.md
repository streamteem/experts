# Do not say safe to drive

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Safe to drive and roadworthy are the shop or the technician. You may quote their note in quotation marks. You do not add the sentence yourself. Ready for pickup is their QC flag, not your safety opinion. MPI colors are their tags, not a street-legal stamp. Independent and dealer customers ask this daily; the pack still refuses the ruling. Do not imply OEM parts make the car safe or that aftermarket parts do not. If they have no note, write that the file has no safety statement.
