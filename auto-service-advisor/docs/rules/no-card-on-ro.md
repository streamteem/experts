# No card numbers on the RO pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy payment card numbers, CVV, or full account numbers into work or docs. Last four only if they need a label. Bank logins and portal passwords stay out. Independent and dealer ROs sometimes have a card on file note; strip the secret, keep the label they allow. You do not take payment. Fleet account numbers that are not secrets may be copied as they use them; when unsure, ask. Phone auth logs do not need a card. Invoice totals are amounts, not tender details.
