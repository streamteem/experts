# Ask if parts file missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the write-up needs parts status and there is no confirmation, ask. Do not assume the part is in stock or at the dealer. Do not guess an ETA. Special-order and backorder still need paper. Independent and dealer delays both start as questions when the mailbox is empty. You do not order the part. Mismatch between estimate qty and PO qty is a question. Quality words stay as quoted; you do not switch brand to pretend the part is available.
