# No invented book time

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hours and part prices come from their labor guide, menu, or price file. If the guide is not in the folder, ask. Do not use a time you remember from another shop. Do not mix two guides on one estimate. Clock hours are not billed hours. Menu packages override guide time only when that menu is their source. Independent Mitchell or ALLDATA and dealer factory time are different files; use the one they named. Mark missing operations as questions. You do not invent a multiplier.
