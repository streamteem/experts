# Do not overwrite the concern after diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

After a cause is known, still keep the original customer words. Cause is a separate field. Do not rename the concern to match a TSB title, a part, or a cleaner shop phrase. Independent and dealer invoices that overwrite the complaint hide why the car came in and break comeback matching. Conditions stay. If they want a short shop summary, that is another line they already use, not a replacement you invent. NPF keeps the same concern. Ask before any rewrite they request.
