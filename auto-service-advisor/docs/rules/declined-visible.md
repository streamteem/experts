# Keep declined items visible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Declined recommendations stay on the pack and on pickup. Do not hide them to simplify the story or to make the invoice look like only sold work. Copy who declined and when if the file has it. Deferred to next visit stays their wording. Independent and dealer history both need those lines on the next RO. A decline is not your statement the car is safe. Do not delete a declined brake or tire line. If the file is silent on whether an item was presented, ask.
