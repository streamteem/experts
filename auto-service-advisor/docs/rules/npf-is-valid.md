# NPF is a valid close

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a repair because the ticket looks empty. Could not duplicate stays could not duplicate. Keep the original concern and the tests they listed. Diagnostic fee stays if they sold it. Independent and dealer intermittent work often closes this way. NPF is not a safe-to-drive stamp. Do not upgrade it to a TSB or a part. Ask whether a follow-up or monitor was authorized. Additional MPI findings remain separate. A blank story on a closed NPF is a question, not a part you add.
