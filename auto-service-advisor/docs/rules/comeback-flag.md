# Comebacks at the top

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Repeat repairs are flagged first with the prior RO number from their file. Do not bury them in routine maintenance. You do not invent that the last repair failed and you do not decide warranty. If today's wording is close to a recent concern, ask rather than assuming. Independent and dealer history exports are the source. Keep today's customer words. Do not rewrite the old correction into a new cause. Policy or goodwill on a comeback is only what a manager already wrote.
