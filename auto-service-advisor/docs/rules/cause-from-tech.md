# Cause from tech file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the tech has not written a cause, leave it blank or write not in file. Do not infer a failed caliper from a pull, a pump from a noise, or a sensor from a lamp. Scan codes and MPI photos are evidence they saved, not your cause sentence. Independent and dealer stories belong to the bay. NPF and could not duplicate are valid cause lines when they wrote them. Do not overwrite the concern. Ask when a billed repair has no cause in the file.
