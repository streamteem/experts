# Photos only from their inspection

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Describe only photos attached to their MPI or RO. Do not invent wear from a verbal it is rusty. Captions are not diagnosis unless the technician wrote one. Red tags stay their tags. Independent DVI and dealer photo requirements both count; missing required photos are questions. Do not import stock images. Do not describe the other side of the car. Photos are not a state inspection and not a safe-to-drive ruling. If they billed a line that requires a photo and none is filed, ask.
