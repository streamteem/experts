# Dispatch stays inside auth

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Assignment notes do not add jobs. Related recommendations are listed separately until approved. Dispatch stays inside authorized lines, including diagnostic-only tickets. Independent and dealer boards both creep when the writer tells the tech to also do the MPI reds. Waiting auth is not assigned work. Parts holds do not expand scope. You do not assign a technician unless they asked for a roster draft. Quote their sold lines in the assignment, nothing extra.
