# Tire DOT and road-hazard notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they require DOT numbers on tire lines, copy from the RO or the tire sheet they filed. Road-hazard or manufacturer tire warranty is their program; quote the form. You do not certify a tire as legal tread. MPI tread depths come only from their form. Independent and dealer counters both sell tires from a matrix; do not invent a size or load rating. Do not treat OEM versus aftermarket tires as a safety ruling you make; use the line they quoted. Photos of wear belong only if attached. A declined tire stays declined on the pack. If they store a road-hazard contract number, copy it. DOT and depths exist so later claims and rotations have a file, not so you stamp the car street-legal. Starter practice until they teach this shop's tire worksheet and whether advisors or techs write DOT.
