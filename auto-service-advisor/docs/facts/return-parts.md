# Unused and returned parts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unused parts should be returned or moved to inventory per their process. If the RO billed a part that the return file shows credited, flag it. Do not invent a restock fee. Independent and dealer parts rooms both return special-order leftovers under vendor rules; copy those rules from the confirmation, not from memory. A return is not a diagnosis that the part was wrong, and it is not your decision to switch quality brands. If the tech never installed the part, the pickup invoice should not silently keep it; ask. Cores coming back are a credit path, not a new sale. Keep RO number, part number, and qty from their return paper. The file exists so close-out and vendor statements match. Typical US desk practice until they teach who processes returns and whether the advisor or parts person updates the RO.
