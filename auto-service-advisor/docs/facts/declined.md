# Declined and deferred items

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Declined or deferred recommendations typically stay on the RO or inspection so the next visit can see them. Copy the decline notation from their file, including who declined, when, and the line. Do not delete declined lines to make the ticket look clean. Do not treat a declined brake job as completed. Independent and dealer desks both keep this history because comebacks and later estimates depend on what was already offered. A decline is not your opinion that the car is safe to drive. If they deferred to next oil service, copy that wording; do not invent a due date. Present declined items on pickup packs so the invoice and the MPI still match. You do not re-sell in this product as a marketing message. If the file is silent on a recommended line, ask whether it was presented. Typical US desk practice until they teach how this shop codes declined versus not presented.
