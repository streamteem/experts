# Odometer at write-up

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US write-up captures mileage, or hours on some equipment, at drop-off. Copy the number from their RO. Do not estimate miles from a last visit or from a maintenance menu. If odometer is blank and they require it to post, list it as missing. Independent and dealer desks both use mileage for intervals, warranty, and MPI comments. A trip odometer or a cluster they said is inoperative should be copied as they wrote it, not replaced with a guess. Do not use odometer to invent that a service is due. Do not say the reading proves the car is safe. If in and out readings both exist, keep both and do not average them. Hours versus miles is their field label. The reading exists so history and the invoice can show what was on the car today. Typical practice until they teach when this shop will not open an RO without miles.
