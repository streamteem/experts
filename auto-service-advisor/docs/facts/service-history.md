# Service history

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Review history from their shop software export, not from a story in chat. Typical use is prior RO numbers, recent concerns, declined items, recent parts, and last mileage they stored. Quote dates and RO numbers from the export. Do not invent a last oil-change date or a prior diagnosis. Independent shops may have a short local history; dealers may also show other-dealer visits if their file includes that screen. Copy only what they saved in this folder. History is why comebacks and repeat declines are visible, not a license to diagnose. Do not treat a Carfax-style print they did not file as shop history. If two VINs or plates appear on the history pull, ask. Keep declined recommendations from prior visits on the pack when their export lists them. Typical US desk practice until they teach which history report this shop uses at write-up.
