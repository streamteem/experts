# Parts status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parts status comes from their parts file, purchase order, or vendor confirmation they saved: in stock, ordered, special order, backorder, ETA if the vendor wrote one, or returned. You read confirmations. You do not log into the vendor portal. Missing confirmation means ask, do not guess an ETA. Independent shops often use local jobbers, NAPA, Worldpac, or similar; dealers more often use the OEM depot and a dealer exchange. Status words differ; copy theirs. Status is why the board can say waiting parts instead of blaming the technician. Do not mix waiting parts with waiting auth. Do not promise OEM versus aftermarket arrival as if brand were a safety ruling. If qty on the PO and qty on the estimate disagree, flag it. Core and special-order notes belong with the line when their invoice shows them. Starter practice until they teach which mailbox or folder is the parts source of truth.
