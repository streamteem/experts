# MPI colors and photos

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their form uses color or priority tags, copy those tags exactly as exported. Photos and videos belong only if they attached them to that inspection or RO. Do not describe wear you cannot see in their file. A photo caption is not a diagnosis unless the technician wrote one, and a red tag is not your statement that the car is unsafe to drive. Independent DVI tools and dealer factory inspections both color-code; the meaning of yellow varies by shop. Ask if their legend is not in the folder. Do not import a generic checklist to fill empty boxes. Do not upgrade a yellow to red to create urgency. Colors exist so the additional-findings call and the declined list stay consistent with the form. If a photo is missing on a billed alignment or tire line that their process requires, ask. Starter practice until they teach how this counter presents the inspection to the customer.
