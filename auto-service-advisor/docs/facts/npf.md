# No problem found

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No problem found or could not duplicate is a valid close when the technician wrote it, often after listed tests. Keep the original concern. Do not invent a part to fill the ticket. Ask whether a monitor, keep-it, or follow-up was authorized. Independent and dealer bays both close intermittent work this way. NPF is not a claim the car is safe to drive. Do not replace the concern with a cleaner diagnosis. Diagnostic time still belongs if they sold it. If they printed a scan or road-test note, quote those files. Additional findings from MPI remain separate recommendations. You do not upgrade NPF to a TSB. The close exists so the invoice and the next visit tell the truth. Typical US desk practice until they teach how this shop codes NPF and what they tell the customer from the file.
