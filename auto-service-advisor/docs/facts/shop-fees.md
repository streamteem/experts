# Shop fees and supplies

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shop supplies, environmental fees, and tax come from their posted rate or RO settings. Copy the labels and amounts from the estimate. Do not invent a shop-fee percentage. If a fee is on one RO and not another, ask which rule they use. Independent tickets often show a supplies percent and a waste or hazmat fee; dealer invoices may use different factory or state-looking labels. Use their words. Fees are not a diagnosis and not a legal opinion that waste handling is compliant. Do not add a fee because another shop charges it. If they waive fees on warranty or internal, copy that from the file. Tax is what their RO calculated, not a rate you apply. The lines exist so the customer-facing total matches the last approval. Starter practice until they teach this shop's fee table and which pay types include or exclude each fee.
