# Comeback and warranty callback

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A comeback, also called a repeat repair or warranty come-back, is the same concern returning after recent work. Flag it at the top of the pack. Tie it to the prior RO number if their history export has one. You do not decide warranty coverage. You do not invent that the prior repair failed. Independent and dealer desks both separate a new concern on the same car from a repeat of the last complaint; if the wording is close, ask rather than assuming. Comeback is why dispatch, parts, and policy may follow a different path, but those paths are theirs. Keep the original customer words from today and from the prior ticket. Do not bury the flag inside a routine oil-change write-up. Do not write that the last tech was wrong. If they have a comeback reason code, copy it. Typical US desk practice until they teach how this shop codes callbacks versus new work.
