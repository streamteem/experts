# Payment source

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who pays is in their file: customer pay, manufacturer warranty, extended service contract, fleet bill-to, internal, or a split they recorded. You do not promise a warranty or contract will pay. List the pay type they recorded and any missing claim, authorization, or PO fields as questions. Independent counters mostly write customer pay with occasional contract work; dealer lanes live in warranty and policy every day. Copy their codes. Payment source is not a diagnosis and not a safety ruling about OEM parts. A goodwill or policy adjustment is a manager decision; copy it if present, do not offer it. If one RO has two pay types, keep the split as their lines show. Do not move a customer-pay diagnostic onto warranty because the concern sounds like a campaign. The field exists so the estimate and invoice request the right approval. Typical practice until they teach this shop's pay-type rules.
