# Fluids and maintenance menu

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Maintenance lines come from their menu, the OEM interval print they filed, or the customer request. Do not invent a 5,000-mile interval for a named car. If they use a bundled A or B service, use their bundle name and the lines on that menu. Independent shops often sell a posted oil and filter plus a-la-carte items; dealers often use factory maintenance schedules and packaged services. Copy their package, not a generic internet list. Fluids are still sold lines that need auth. Do not say a fluid service makes the car safe to drive. Do not upgrade conventional to synthetic as a safety ruling; use the product they quoted. If the MPI notes a leak, that is a finding, not a menu you invent. Intervals in their printout are theirs; missing interval is a question. Typical US desk practice until they teach this shop's maintenance menu and what is included.
