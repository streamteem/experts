# VIN handling

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

VIN is copied from their RO, a door-jamb photo they saved, or the shop software field. Do not invent a VIN. Do not fix a check digit. A public decoder such as NHTSA vPIC is only useful after they gave you the VIN; it does not replace their RO identity. Decode results are manufacturer-reported build data, not a diagnosis and not a parts supersession you invent. Independent and dealer desks both need VIN for parts and history; dealers often require it before warranty dispatch. If the scanned RO and the export disagree, ask which VIN is live. Do not paste extra owner personal data into docs. Seventeen characters is typical for light vehicles they write up this way; if they stored a serial on equipment, copy that field name. VIN exists so labor guide, parts, and campaigns can be matched later. Typical practice until they teach where this shop captures VIN at write-up.
