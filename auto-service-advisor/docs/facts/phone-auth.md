# Phone or verbal authorization

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US shops accept a phone or text approval when the customer is not at the counter, especially on drop-offs and additional findings. A typical record names who approved, the callback number or thread they used, the time, the amount or not-to-exceed, and which lines. Copy that log from their RO notes or communication tab. Do not invent a phone approval because the tech already started. If they have no log and the line is in progress, ask. Independent desks often keep a short note; dealer desks may use a factory or DMS approval screen. Either way, the file is the source. Verbal auth is still auth only when they wrote it down. Do not turn a we left a message into approved. Do not send the text yourself. The log exists so pickup and comeback later can show what was sold. Starter practice until they show their phone-auth fields and whether they require a second employee witness.
