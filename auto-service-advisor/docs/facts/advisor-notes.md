# Advisor notes vs customer-facing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Advisor notes may include tone, negotiation, or family context that should not be pasted into a customer letter. The write-up quotes concern, authorization, parts, and technician findings. Keep extra personal remarks out of docs. Independent and dealer software both have an internal comments box; ask which notes are customer-facing. Do not rewrite a blunt tech story into marketing. Do not add your own opinion that OEM is safer than aftermarket. Do not copy payment card data from a note. If they stored a preferred callback window, that may belong on the pack; gossip does not. Notes exist so the next advisor can see the conversation, but this product still respects their split between internal and customer paper. If a note claims approval and the auth log is empty, ask for the log. Starter practice until they teach this shop's note policy.
