# Status board and WIP

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open ROs live on a job board: write-up, waiting authorization, waiting parts, in progress, quality check, ready. Export or screenshot they provide is the source. Do not move a ticket's status in the pack beyond what the board shows. Independent shops may use a TV board or a shop-app list; dealers use DMS dispatch boards with more hold codes. Copy their status word. A hold must say auth, parts, or tech time when their file does, and may list more than one. Status is not a diagnosis and not a ready-safe opinion. Promised time that conflicts with a parts hold is a question, not something you smooth over. Comebacks should still be visible on the row if their export flags them. The board exists so the open-RO pack matches the lane. Typical US desk practice until they teach this shop's status list and who may change a tile.
