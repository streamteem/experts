# Related and additional services

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Related services are technician or inspection recommendations tied to the concern or to the MPI. Present them as recommendations with their prices from the labor matrix and parts file, not as required law. Authorization is still required. Do not pile on a menu the tech did not mark. Independent writers often hear related as upsell; the pack still stays on marked items only. Dealer MPI may auto-list factory items; still copy only what their form shows as recommended. Related is not your diagnosis and not an OEM-versus-aftermarket safety ruling. Declined related lines stay visible. If a related line has no hours or part price in their files, mark a question. Keep the original concern first so related work is not mistaken for the write-up. You do not dispatch related lines in an assignment note. Typical practice until they teach this shop's related-service script and who may add a line.
