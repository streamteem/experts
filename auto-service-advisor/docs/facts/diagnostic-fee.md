# Diagnostic as a sold line

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Diagnosis is often a separate authorized line with a fee or a not-to-exceed. Typical US independent and dealer practice is to sell and authorize diagnostic time before the technician spends hours chasing a noise or a lamp. You do not skip diag and jump to a part. If their RO has no diag line and the concern is a symptom, ask whether diag is authorized. A code on a printout is not a complete cause and not permission to sell a part without their process. Warranty and factory tickets may use a different diagnostic labor operation; copy their code, do not invent coverage. Diag is not a state inspection and not a safe-to-drive opinion. If they apply diag toward repair when the customer approves the fix, quote that policy from their sheet only. The line exists so clock time has a sold home. Typical practice until they teach this shop's diag menu and when they waive it.
