# Parts purchase orders

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parts purchase orders tie a vendor line to an RO. Typical match is RO number, part number, quantity, cost, vendor, and status. List mismatches as questions. You do not order parts. You do not invent a cost. Independent POs go to jobbers; dealer POs go to the depot or an outside vendor. Copy the file they saved. A PO is not a diagnosis and not a ruling that the part must be OEM for safety. Core, special order, and backorder notes on the PO belong on the parts-status pack. If the estimate has a part the PO lacks, or the PO has extras, flag it. Credits and returns need their return document. The PO exists so the estimate, the parts file they already counted, and the invoice can be reconciled by them. Starter practice until they teach which PO export this shop files against each ticket.
