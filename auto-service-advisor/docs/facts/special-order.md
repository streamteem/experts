# Special-order parts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Special-order or dealer parts often need a deposit and may be not returnable. Copy those notes from the parts person or vendor confirmation. Flag restocking or no-return language they saved. Do not promise the part will arrive tomorrow. Independent jobbers and OEM depots both mark special order; the RO should show that status. Special order is not a safety ruling about OEM versus aftermarket; it is a supply note. Auth still needed before you treat the line as sold, especially when they require a deposit. If the customer later declines, ask what their restock file says rather than inventing a fee. ETA only from the confirmation. Do not invent a second vendor to beat the delay. The note exists so the board hold and the customer conversation match the vendor. Typical US desk practice until they teach this shop's deposit and non-return rules for special-order parts.
