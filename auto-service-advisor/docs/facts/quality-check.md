# Quality check and ready

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ready for pickup is their quality-check or service-manager mark, not your opinion the car is safe. Copy the ready flag. If QC notes list an open concern, keep it open in the pack. Independent shops may have a simple ready hook; dealers often require a QC checklist, road test, or factory close. Use their form. QC is not a state inspection stamp and not a street-legal certificate. Do not invent a QC pass. If they billed work and QC is unsigned, ask. Photos or printouts they require at QC should be present or listed missing. Declined items remain declined even when the car is ready. Do not add a correction the QC note does not show. The mark exists so the board and the pickup invoice agree the bay is done. Typical US desk practice until they teach who performs QC here and what must be attached before ready.
