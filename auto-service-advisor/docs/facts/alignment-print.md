# Alignment and printouts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Alignment before-and-after printouts, battery tests, and A/C gauge sheets are attachments they saved. Copy the machine's readings. Do not invent spec versus actual. If the printout is missing and the line is billed, ask. Independent racks and dealer racks both spit PDFs or photos; use those files. Printouts are measurements, not your statement the car is safe to drive and not a state inspection. Do not rewrite a red or out-of-spec highlight into a part replacement unless the tech wrote that correction. If they billed an alignment and only a before sheet exists, flag the missing after. Specs on the sheet are the machine's; do not replace them from memory. Do not pick OEM versus aftermarket parts from a print. The attachment exists so the write-up and invoice match the rack. Typical practice until they teach which prints this shop requires before a line can close.
