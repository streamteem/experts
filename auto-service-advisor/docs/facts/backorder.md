# Backorder communication

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Backorder is a vendor status. Quote the confirmation they filed. Tell the pack the customer-facing delay only from that file or their advisor note. Do not invent a second source or a better ETA. Independent and dealer parts rooms both live with backorders; dealer factory allocation notes still must be in the folder before you copy them. Backorder is a hold reason, not a diagnosis and not a reason to switch the customer to a different quality word as a safety ruling. If they offered an alternative part, copy that offer from their note with its price and quality label. Mix waiting parts with waiting auth only when both are in the file, and list both. If the confirmation expired, ask. The status exists so promised time can be revisited by them, not by your guess. Starter until they teach who updates the customer on backorders here.
