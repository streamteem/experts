# Customer follow-up

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Follow-up is their process: a call list, a CSI export, or a due list from shop software. You may list who is due from that file. You do not send the messages as a marketing product. Do not invent a satisfaction score. Independent shops may keep a simple callback sheet; dealers often have factory CSI lists. Copy columns they exported. Follow-up is not a diagnosis, not a promise the car is safe, and not a second sell of declined work unless they asked for a findings list from the file. If the export lacks a phone or a due date, say so. Do not pull extra personal data into docs. Comebacks on the list still need the prior RO from history, not a story. The list exists so their people can call from their own tools. Typical practice until they teach this shop's follow-up report and who owns it.
