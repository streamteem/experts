# TSB and campaign notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Technical service bulletins and campaigns appear only if their repair-info printout or OEM portal screenshot is in the file. You do not browse a paywalled manual into docs. You do not declare a recall is open. List TSB number in their printout or ask. Independent desks see TSBs when someone printed them from Mitchell, ALLDATA, or similar; dealer desks live in campaign screens. Either way, the saved page is the source. A bulletin is not a diagnosis you apply, not a promise of coverage, and not a safety ruling you add. Do not rename a customer concern to match a bulletin title. If they have a campaign number on the RO, copy it; if they do not, do not invent one from a public recall list. The printout exists so the tech and warranty clerk can see what this shop already pulled. Starter practice until they teach who checks campaigns at this counter.
