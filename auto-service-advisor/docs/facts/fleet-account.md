# Fleet and wholesale tickets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fleet and wholesale ROs often need a purchase order, unit number, and a bill-to that differs from the driver. Copy those fields. Do not start a pack without the PO if their process requires it. You do not invent a national-account rate. Independent shops may have a few local fleets; dealers may have manufacturer or rental accounts with extra screens. Use their export. Fleet auth may be a dispatcher call rather than a retail signature; still copy who when and amount or the PO limits they stored. Do not diagnose from a driver story. Do not promise OEM parts as a safety rule; quote their account parts policy if it is on file. Declined fleet items still stay on the ticket if they recorded them. The fields exist so billing and dispatch match the account. Starter until they teach this shop's fleet open checklist.
