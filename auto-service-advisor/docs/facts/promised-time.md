# Promised time and status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Completion expectation is what they wrote on the RO or status board: promised time, waiting, parts hold, with tech, quality check, or ready. Quote their status. Do not invent a ready time. If status and promised time conflict, ask. Independent boards are often a screen or a whiteboard photo; dealer DMS boards add factory hold codes. Copy their words. Promised time is not a diagnosis and not a promise you make to the customer in this product. Waiters and drop-offs have different clocks; do not move a drop-off to waiter in the pack. A parts ETA from a vendor file can explain a hold, but it does not become a new promise unless they wrote one. If they use a range, keep the range. The field exists so the open-RO list matches the board. Typical practice until they teach who may change promised time at this counter.
