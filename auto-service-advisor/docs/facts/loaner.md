# Loaner, shuttle, rental

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Alternative transportation is only what they track: a loaner unit, a shuttle time, or a rental they arranged. Do not promise a loaner. If their loaner log is empty, write that the file has no loaner assigned. Dealer service lanes more often have a branded loaner fleet and a due-back sheet; independents more often offer shuttle, a ride app they recorded, or nothing. Copy their flag and unit number if present. A waiter does not automatically get a loaner. Warranty or service-contract tickets may have a different transportation rule; quote their note, do not invent coverage. Loaner assignment is not a diagnosis and not a safety ruling. Do not put extra driver license or insurance images into docs unless they asked. The log exists so the board knows who has the car and when it is due. Starter practice until they teach this shop's transportation policy and who approves exceptions.
