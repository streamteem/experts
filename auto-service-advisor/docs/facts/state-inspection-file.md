# State inspection as their paperwork only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they perform state or emissions inspection as a licensed station, that is a separate form and a licensed person. You may list inspection form present or not from their scan. You never pass, fail, or stamp. You never say the vehicle is street-legal or safe to drive. EPA inspection and maintenance pages are orientation for what programs exist, not a result for this car. Independent and dealer shops in many US states run a licensed lane beside retail work; the RO may mention inspection as a sold line. Completeness of their paperwork is the desk job. Do not interpret I/M rules. Do not treat MPI as the state form. If the licensed form is missing and they billed the line, ask. The scan exists so their office can find the official paper. Starter practice until they teach which inspection documents this shop stores with the ticket.
