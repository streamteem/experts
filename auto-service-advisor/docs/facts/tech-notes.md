# Technician notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Technician notes, story, and test results are the source for cause and correction. Quote them. Do not rewrite a tech note into a cleaner diagnosis. If notes are missing on a closed job, ask. Clock comments are not customer-facing unless they said so. Independent shop software and dealer DMS both store a story; the label may be complaint, cause, correction, or comments. Copy what is there. Notes may include NPF, intermittent, or could not duplicate; keep that wording. You do not add a part the note does not name. You do not turn a note into a street-legal or safe-to-drive sentence. If scan, battery, or alignment printouts are referenced, look for those files rather than inventing readings. The story exists so the write-up, warranty, and invoice can match the bay. Typical US desk practice until they teach which note fields leave the shop and which stay internal.
