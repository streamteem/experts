# Parts quality labels

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shops often quote OEM, aftermarket, reman, or rebuilt at different prices. Use the quality word that is on their estimate or vendor invoice. Do not upgrade aftermarket to OEM in a write-up. Do not tell the customer that aftermarket is unsafe or that OEM is required for safety; that is not a ruling this desk product makes. If the customer asked for a specific brand and the file is silent, ask. Independent counters commonly present two prices; dealer counters may default to genuine and offer alternative only when their process allows it. Copy their presentation. Quality labels exist so auth and the invoice match what will be installed. Reman may carry a core; list that only from their sheet. If two lines on one RO use different quality words, keep both and do not tidy them into one. Typical US practice until they teach this shop's quoting script and part-source rules.
