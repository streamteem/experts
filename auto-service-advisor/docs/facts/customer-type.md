# Customer type

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

ASE-style desks often tag customer type: first visit, repeat, warranty, fleet, wholesale, or internal. Use their tag. Fleet and warranty tickets may need a PO or claim number before work starts. Do not invent a fleet account or a first-time coupon. Independent shops may only mark waiter versus customer; dealers often have more pay-type and customer-type codes in the DMS. Copy what they stored. Type is why promised time, loaner, and authorization path may differ, but you do not invent those differences. A repeat customer is not automatically a comeback; comeback is the same concern after recent work. Internal or employee vehicles still need a concern. Do not put extra personal marketing notes into the customer-facing pack. The tag exists so the board and the invoice use the right process. Starter until they teach this shop's customer-type list and which types block dispatch.
