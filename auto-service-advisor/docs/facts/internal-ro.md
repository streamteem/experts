# Internal or shop vehicle

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Internal tickets for a shop truck, loaner, or employee vehicle still need a concern and authorization from whoever they say approves internals. Do not skip the concern. Do not treat an internal as a license to invent diagnosis or to skip parts status. Independent and dealer shops both use internal pay types; the approval person is often a manager, not the driver. Copy their auth note. Internal is not a safety stamp and not a reason to hide declined items. Estimates still come from their labor matrix and parts file if they price internals. If they zero the ticket, copy that from the RO rather than inventing a discount. Comebacks on shop vehicles still get a prior RO flag when history shows one. The ticket exists so parts, clock, and warranty on the unit stay traceable. Typical practice until they teach who can open and approve internal work here.
