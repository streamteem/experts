# Policy adjustments

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Policy or goodwill adjustments are manager decisions on the RO. Copy the amount and who approved if the file shows it. You do not offer a discount. You do not invent a goodwill reason. Independent owners and dealer service managers both use policy to close a dispute or a comeback; the paper still has to be there. Policy is not a diagnosis, not a warranty legal opinion, and not a promise the manufacturer will pay. Do not move customer-pay lines to policy to make a total match. If they split a line, keep the split. Missing approver on a large adjustment is a question. Do not invent a comeback failure to justify the amount. The adjustment exists so the invoice and the auth story stay honest. Starter practice until they teach who may write policy at this shop and how it appears on the ticket.
