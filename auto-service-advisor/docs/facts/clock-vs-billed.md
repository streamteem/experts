# Clock hours vs billed hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Clock time is how long the technician was on the job. Billed time is the estimate line from their guide or menu. Typical packs keep them separate. Do not correct billed hours to match clock. Efficiency reports come from their software export, not your math as a verdict. Independent shops watch efficiency; dealers do too with factory time. Copy both columns if they asked for a pack that includes them. Clock versus billed is not a diagnosis and not proof of a comeback. If billed exceeds authorized hours, that is an auth question, not a silent fix. If clock is missing on a closed job, ask. Do not invent book time to fill a blank billed cell. The split exists so payroll, the estimate, and the customer approval stay distinct. Starter practice until they teach which columns this shop uses and who may change billed hours.
