# Parts and labor warranty language

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Warranty length is what their invoice or policy sheet says. Quote that sheet. Do not invent 12/12 or 24/24. Manufacturer warranty, extended service contract, and shop parts-and-labor warranty are different; use the word they used. You do not give a legal warranty opinion and you do not promise the manufacturer will pay. Independent shops usually hand a shop policy; dealers add factory coverage screens. Copy the file they saved. Warranty language is not a diagnosis and not a ruling that OEM parts are required for safety. Comebacks may use a different path; still do not decide coverage. If a part has a vendor warranty note on the invoice, quote that note. Missing policy sheet is a question. The handout exists so pickup and later callbacks have the same words. Starter practice until they teach which warranty documents this counter prints with the RO.
