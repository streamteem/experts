# Sublet work

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sublet is work sent out: an alignment rack they do not have, a machine shop, glass, towing they billed, or programming at another site. List the sublet vendor and amount from their PO or invoice. You do not invent a sublet price. Authorization still applies to the customer-facing line. Independent shops sublet more specialty work; dealers may sublet body, glass, or overflow. Copy their vendor name as filed. Sublet is not your diagnosis and not a safety stamp from the outside shop unless their file quotes that shop's note. If the sublet invoice and the RO amount disagree, flag it. Do not promise a sublet ETA that is not on the vendor paper. Cores and old parts on sublet follow their PO, not your memory. The line exists so the estimate and pickup invoice show work this bay did not perform. Typical practice until they teach who writes sublet POs here.
