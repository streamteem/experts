# Vehicle condition at drop-off

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US shops note existing damage, fuel level, and personal items at drop-off on a walk-around or condition form. Copy that form. Do not add damage that is not on it. Belongings lists stay minimal; do not copy extra personal detail into docs. Independent lots and dealer lanes both do this to separate incoming marks from later claims. A missing form is a question, not a blank you fill from a photo you assume. Condition notes are not a diagnosis and not a statement the car is safe to drive. Do not invent a fuel-enough-for-road-test remark. If they store a lot location or hang-tag number, copy it. Waiters may have a shorter walk-around; still use what they recorded. The form exists so pickup and comeback conversations have a file. Starter practice until they teach this shop's walk-around product and who signs it.
