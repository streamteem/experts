# Labor guide they use

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Book hours come from the labor guide or OEM information this shop already uses: Mitchell, ALLDATA, Motor, a factory labor system, or a posted menu. You do not invent a time from another shop's memory. If they have no guide export, ask which sheet is the source. Actual clock hours and billed hours may differ; use the column they named for the estimate. Dealer operations are often factory codes; independents often search by YMM and operation name. Do not mix two guides on one estimate. Do not treat guide time as a safety ruling or as proof a job is complete. Menu-priced maintenance may ignore guide hours; copy the menu if that is their source. Mark missing operations as questions. The matrix exists so estimates and auth talk in this shop's numbers. Starter practice until they teach which guide, which labor rate columns, and whether they use a multiplier.
