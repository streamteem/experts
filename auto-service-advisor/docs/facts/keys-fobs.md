# Keys, fobs, and valet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they log key count, fob count, or valet location, copy that log. Do not invent a spare key. Programming a fob is a sold line from their menu, not a courtesy you invent. Independent shops may use a hook board and a note; dealers often have a key control screen. Copy their count at drop-off. A missing key note is a question, not a walk-around you invent. Key logs are not a diagnosis. Do not put extra locksmith or owner identity documents into docs unless they asked. If they require a signed valet or overnight note, list present or missing from their form. Programming, immobilizer, or dealer-code work still needs auth and parts status from files. The log exists so pickup does not argue about what was left. Typical US desk practice until they teach this shop's key control and who may start programming.
