# Customer contact log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who was contacted, when, and what amount was quoted belongs in their RO communication log. Copy it. You do not send the text or email. If they said they approved and the log is empty, ask for the log; do not invent it. Independent counters often type a short called customer note; dealer DMS logs may be longer. Use what they saved. The log is the home of phone auth, additional-findings conversations, and backorder updates. It is not a diagnosis and not a marketing send. Do not copy full card numbers from a payment note. If two contacts disagree on the approved amount, list both and ask which is last. Missing callback number is a question when their process requires it. The log exists so pickup and comeback can show the conversation. Typical US desk practice until they teach this shop's required contact fields.
