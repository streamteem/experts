# Invoice at pickup

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical close is authorized work performed, declined items still listed, warranties they printed, and totals matching the last approval. You confirm invoice lines against auth and tech notes. You do not change a total. Discrepancy is a question. Independent and dealer pickup counters both catch extras, missing declined MPI lines, and parts that were returned but still billed. Copy the invoice PDF they filed. Do not invent a correction to make the math pretty. Do not say the invoice proves the car is safe. Policy adjustments belong only if a manager amount is on the RO. Payment card numbers do not go into the pack. If cause or correction is blank on a billed repair, ask. The pickup check exists so the customer is not surprised and the file stays complete. Typical US desk practice until they teach this shop's close checklist and who can edit an invoice.
