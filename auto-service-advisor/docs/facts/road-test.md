# Road test notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Concern verification and post-repair road tests are technician or advisor notes they recorded. Quote the conditions they tested: speed, gear, temperature, road type, if those words are there. Do not write verified safe. If they could not duplicate, that is the note. Independent and dealer tickets both use road-test lines for noises and drivability; some shops require a second person for QC. Copy their form. A road test is not a state inspection and not a street-legal stamp. Do not invent a test you assume they did because the job closed. If fuel was too low and they wrote that they could not test, keep that. Results feed cause and correction only as they wrote them. The note exists so NPF and comeback files show what was tried. Starter practice until they teach who road-tests here and what they write down.
