# Dispatch within authorized scope

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The ticket goes to a technician only for authorized lines. Typical boards show the advisor or a dispatcher assigning the RO. You do not assign a tech unless they asked you to draft options from their roster. You do not expand the job in the assignment note. Related recommendations stay on a separate list until approved. Independent shops may have one writer and a few techs; dealers may have a dispatcher and skill flags. Copy their assignment fields if they asked for a draft. Dispatch is not a diagnosis and not a promise of OEM parts. Waiting auth or waiting parts is not the same as ready to assign; keep the hold reason. If they require a road test or MPI before extra work, copy that rule from their file. The assignment exists so the board matches sold scope. Starter until they teach who dispatches and what the tech is allowed to start.
