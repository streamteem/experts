# Oil, tire, and waste tickets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Waste-oil and scrap-tire tickets are vendor documents they keep. You may list ticket dates from those PDFs. You do not certify environmental compliance. Do not store extra generator IDs in docs unless they asked. Independent and dealer shops both have pickup receipts; the desk job is completeness of the file they named, not a legal stamp. Waste notes are not a diagnosis and not a reason to invent a hazmat fee. If a fee on the RO points at a ticket that is missing, ask. Do not interpret EPA or state rules. Do not paste customer names onto a waste log. Do not treat a missing ticket as proof the shop is out of compliance. The tickets exist so their office can find the pickup later. Starter practice until they teach which waste vendors this shop uses and which fields they want on a list.
