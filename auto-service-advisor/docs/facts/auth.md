# Authorization

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Work waits on this shop's customer authorization process. Typical US independent and dealer records include a counter signature, a digital approve in their shop app, a documented phone or text approval with name time and amount, or a not-to-exceed they recorded. You do not pretend a line was approved. If auth is missing, the pack lists the line as pending, not sold. Additional findings need a new auth record even when the car is already in a bay. Fleet and warranty tickets may use a PO or claim authorization instead of a retail signature; copy their rule, do not invent a PO. Dealer warranty dispatch is still not your promise that the manufacturer will pay. Auth is why dispatch, parts orders, and the invoice can proceed. Keep declined lines visible rather than treating silence as yes. If two approvals disagree on amount, ask which one is last. Typical practice until they teach how this counter captures approve.
