# Holds: auth vs parts vs tech

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A ticket can wait on authorization, parts, or technician time. Use their hold reason. Mixing waiting parts with waiting auth hides the next action. If both are true, list both from the file. Independent and dealer boards both fail when a tile says waiting without a reason. A vendor ETA explains a parts hold only when their confirmation has one. An empty auth log explains an auth hold. Tech time is their roster or board, not your guess that a bay is free. Holds are not a diagnosis and not a safe-to-drive opinion. Do not clear a hold in the pack because you think the part arrived. Special-order and backorder stay parts holds until their status changes in the file. The reason exists so the open-RO list tells the advisor what to do next. Starter until they teach this shop's hold codes.
