# Loaner or shuttle log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Spreadsheet or software export of loaner units, who has one, and due back. Empty log means no assignment, not a promise. Dealer fleets and the few independents who keep a car both file something like this. Copy unit number and RO if present. Do not offer a loaner or shuttle time they did not write. Do not store extra driver license images unless they asked. Warranty pay type does not create a row. Use the log only to say assigned or not. Pointer to their transportation file, not a rental site login.
