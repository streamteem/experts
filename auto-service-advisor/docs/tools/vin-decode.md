# VIN decode print (after they supply VIN)

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Optional decode they already ran, or a public NHTSA vPIC result after the VIN is on their RO. Build data only: year, make, model, and other fields the decode printed. Not a diagnosis. Not a parts supersession. Do not decode a VIN they did not provide. Do not fix a check digit. Independent and dealer desks may attach this print to support YMM. If decode and RO disagree, ask. Do not paste extra owner data. Use only to quote build fields they wanted checked. Pointer, not a login to a paid decoder.
