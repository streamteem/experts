# RO or estimate PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Printed or digital RO or estimate they drop back into the folder. Use it to quote concern, authorization signature block, totals, pay type, and vehicle fields. Do not OCR a diagnosis that is not there. Independent prints and dealer DMS PDFs both count. Compare lines to the auth log and to tech notes at pickup. Do not change a total. Do not invent VIN or hours from a blurry scan; ask for a better save. Card numbers on a copy get stripped. The PDF is a source for write-up and invoice check, not a stamp that the car is safe.
