# Parts PO / vendor invoice file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Purchase orders and vendor invoices saved against the RO. Match part number and quantity to the estimate. Flag extras, shorts, cores, and credits. Independent jobber POs and dealer depot POs both count. You do not order and you do not invent cost. Status on the PO feeds the parts board. Special-order and backorder language copies from this file. Quality words stay as printed; you do not upgrade aftermarket to OEM. Missing PO when the line is sold is a question. Pointer to their PO export or scan, not a live purchasing system.
