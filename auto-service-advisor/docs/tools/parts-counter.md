# Parts vendor confirmations

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

They order. You read confirmations, invoices, or ETA emails they save as files. Typical fields are part number, quantity, cost, status, vendor, and quality word if present. Independent jobbers and dealer depots both send these papers. Not a portal login. Use them for parts-status and estimate packs. Missing confirmation means ask, not a guessed ETA. Do not switch OEM and aftermarket on the confirmation. Core and special-order notes copy from this paper. You do not place the order. Flag qty mismatches to the RO. Pointer only until they teach which inbox or folder is theirs.
