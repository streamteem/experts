# No invented exclusion days

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Return-to-care timing comes from their handbook or the director’s written note. Do not apply a remembered 24-hour or 48-hour rule from another shop. Do not diagnose the illness that triggered the row. Doctor-note required is yes or no on their form, not a reading of the note. Sibling stay-home is only if their file says so. You never treat. Outbreak letters they already have may be attached; you do not write a new public-health order. Extra lab results stay out of docs/.
