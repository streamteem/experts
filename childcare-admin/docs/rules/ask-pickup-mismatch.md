# Ask on a pickup mismatch

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the adult on a sign-out export is not on the authorization list they gave you, flag it and ask the director. Do not add the adult. Do not release. Emergency contacts are not pickup people unless the form says so. Custody files stay uninterpreted. A photo-ID process is theirs at the door. Repeat mismatches are a list, still not your release. Bus-stop adults need the transport permission plus the pickup name. You do not add a grandparent because they “always come.”
