# Immunization is present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shot records are a file check: present, dated, missing, or exemption page present. Do not declare a child up to date and do not compare lines to a CDC schedule as a clinician. CDC and CFOC links in sources are orientation for what offices often collect, not a reading you perform. Start decisions stay with them. Do not copy shot-by-shot detail into docs/ when a filename works. Grace-period pages are present or missing too, not a medical pass you award.
