# Ask on a missing packet page

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a page their packet names is not in the folder, say missing and ask. Do not mark complete because the family is “bringing it Monday.” Do not fill blanks for a parent. Sibling packets are separate. Start without a packet is their exception in writing, not your assumption. Photo release, handbook ack, and pickup pages are frequent misses — list each. Extra medical pages are present-or-missing only. Immunization and physical pages follow the same ask. You do not start the child to be helpful.
