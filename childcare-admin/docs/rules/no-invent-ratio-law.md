# Do not invent a ratio statute

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not quote a remembered state ratio, mixed-age table, or nap-time exception as law. Use only the chart they posted or exported. Caring for Our Children and TA network pages are orientation, not *this* license. You do not stamp the room compliant. Alerts are their math. Missing chart: ask. You do not move children to match a law you imagined. Nap-time or playground combined numbers apply only if their posted chart shows them. You do not staff the room.
