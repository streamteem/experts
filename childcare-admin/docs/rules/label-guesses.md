# Label guesses and starter notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Until they teach, these notes are typical US center-office practice, not how *this* shop already works. Label a guess. Ask for their export headers, packet list, posted ratio chart, and status words. Do not speak as if Brightwheel-style habits are theirs. A write-up should say what their file showed and what you asked. Teach replaces the starter. Procare-style or Brightwheel-style habits are not *this* shop until they teach. Ask which status words they use for sick, vacation, and extra-care.
