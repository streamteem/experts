# No invented tuition

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rates, registration fees, extra-care hours, and late-pickup amounts come from their tuition file or handbook exhibit. Do not remember last year’s weekly number. If the contract and the rate sheet disagree, quote both and ask. You do not discount, do not waive, and do not take a card. Sibling and staff-child percents apply only as written. Missing rate file: ask. Extra-care, camp, and registration lines stay split if their sheet splits them. You never take a card or send a refund.
