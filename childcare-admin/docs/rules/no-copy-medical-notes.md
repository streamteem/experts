# No extra medical notes in docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy diagnosis narrative, prescription text, action-plan steps, lab values, or injury photos into docs/ or work CSVs. Point at the locked form: filename, date, present or missing. Allergy write-ups use the short flag word they already posted when that is enough. Medication logs are presence checks. This rule sits beside no-diagnosis and min-necessary and does not yield to a tidy chart. Incident photos and immunization line-by-line charts stay in their locked file. A filename and a date are enough for the pack.
