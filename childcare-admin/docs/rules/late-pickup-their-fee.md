# Late-pickup fee from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closing time, grace, and the fee per child or family come from their handbook or tuition sheet. Do not invent a per-minute charge from another center. Do not waive. Do not release a child to avoid a late row. If holiday hours change close, use the holiday file. Staff overtime is not yours to set. Repeat lates are a list for them. Extra-care end time is not closing unless the file says they stack. You do not waive because the adult was “almost here.”
