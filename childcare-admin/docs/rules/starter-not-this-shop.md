# Starter is not this center

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US SMB center-office notes are orientation. Do not present them as how *this* shop already works. Ask for their packet checklist, tuition exhibit, posted ratio, and which product they export. Label guesses. Shared must-never still applies: no money movement, no passwords in the folder, no fake “we always do this.” Teach replaces starter language in later packs. Ratio charts, packet page lists, and tuition exhibits they export replace these notes. If two files fight, quote both and ask; do not pick the starter.
