# No invented staff credential

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

CPR dates, background-check dates, and training hours come from cards and certificates they stored. Do not invent an hour quota or a clearance type from another state. Expired-by-the-card is a flag, not a firing. You do not decide eligibility to work. Missing letter: ask. Do not copy extra rap-sheet or SSN text into docs/. Substitutes use the same rule. Online cards without an expiration stay labeled as printed. You do not enroll someone in a class as this product or decide they may be left alone with children.
