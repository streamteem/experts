# Minimum necessary identifiers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prefer child ID or the short label they allow on door lists. Do not stack full legal name, date of birth, address, and parent workplace on every row. Do not copy full SSN, insurance numbers, or extra medical notes into docs/. HHS minimum-necessary pages are orientation. Pickup lists need names they already authorized, not extra ID images. Write-ups that may sit at the front desk stay thin on extra identifiers.
