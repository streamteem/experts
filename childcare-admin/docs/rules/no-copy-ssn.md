# No SSN in docs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy a full Social Security number, full bank account, or card PAN into docs/ or work files. Year-end statements use the provider identifiers they already print. Subsidy case files stay in their locked folder; your list can say “auth on file” plus dates. Staff I-9 work is completeness, not a number dump. If a form is only complete with an SSN box, say the box is filled — do not retype the digits.
