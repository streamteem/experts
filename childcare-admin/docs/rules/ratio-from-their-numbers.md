# Ratio from their counts only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Use children present from their attendance, staff from their schedule or sign-in, and the posted number they wrote. Do not invent a missing headcount. Do not move children or staff on paper to make the math pretty. An alert is two numbers and a send to the director. You do not staff a room. If posted numbers are missing, ask — do not use another center’s chart. Early and late counts stay on the timestamp they asked, not a midday average you invent.
