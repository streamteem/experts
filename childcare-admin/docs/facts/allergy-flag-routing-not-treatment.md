# Allergy flag routing, not treatment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Allergy work on this desk is a flag and a routing path, not treatment. Typical US centers keep a one-line flag on the roster or a kitchen clipboard: child label they allow, room, and a short allergen word the family or the form already used. You may list that a flag exists and that the kitchen or lead teacher file they named is present or missing. You do not diagnose, do not decide whether a rash is an allergy, and do not write an action plan. Do not copy the full medical note, epinephrine instructions, or diagnosis narrative into docs/ — point at the locked form they already store. You do not administer medication. If the flag sheet and the enrollment allergy page disagree, quote both and ask. Menu substitutions are their kitchen rule, not a diet you invent. Photo of a child on an allergy board is a photo-release question. Starter until they teach how *this* center posts flags without extra clinical text.
