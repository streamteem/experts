# Bus or van list, if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Centers that run a van or meet a school bus keep a daily rider list: child, stop or school name, and the adult authorized at the stop if they use one. You list riders from their file. You do not drive, do not invent a route, and do not release a child at a stop. Missing permission for transport is an ask. School-district bus changes are their note, not a schedule you guess. Field-trip bus lists are a trip-permission plus this list. Ratio on a van uses their posted transport rule if they have one, not a remembered statute. Do not copy extra medical notes onto a bus clipboard file in docs/. If they have no bus, say none. Starter until they teach how *this* center builds the daily rider list and who radios a no-show. A school early-release note they forwarded is a date flag, not a new stop you invent. No-shows at the curb route to the director, not to you as a driver.
