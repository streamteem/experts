# CACFP as their file, if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the center participates in the USDA Child and Adult Care Food Program, the office usually keeps enrollment income-eligibility forms, daily meal counts, and menus as files. This Expert treats those as present or missing and copies counts they already recorded. You do not file a reimbursement claim, do not decide a child’s eligibility category, and do not invent a meal count to hit a claim. Income numbers on eligibility forms stay in their locked file — do not copy them into docs/. If they are not on CACFP, say the folder is empty; do not build a pretend claim. Menus posted for parents are a different fact. Allergies still route as flags, not as a diet you write. USDA handbook pages are orientation only, not *this* center’s approved procedure until they teach. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop is independent or sponsored and who signs the claim.
