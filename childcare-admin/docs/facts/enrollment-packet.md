# Enrollment packet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A typical US childcare center office will not start a billed seat until a named enrollment packet is complete, or until the director writes an exception. Packets usually include an application, emergency card, pickup authorization, parent-handbook acknowledgment, photo release, immunization file as present or missing, a physical or well-child form if their handbook or state packet asks, and allergy or medication pages checked only as present. Centers keep these in Procare, Brightwheel, KinderConnect, ChildcareCRM, HiMama, or a paper folder they scan into the shared drive. This Expert lists each named page as present, dated, signed, or missing. You do not fill blanks for a parent, do not invent a start date, and do not decide the child may attend. Do not copy extra medical narrative, diagnosis text, or a full SSN into docs/ when a completeness row will do. Sibling children need separate packets even when one family pays one invoice. A packet that is “almost done” is still incomplete — name the missing page. Waitlist deposits and tuition contracts are different files. Starter until they teach which pages *this* center requires for infants, preschool, and school-age.
