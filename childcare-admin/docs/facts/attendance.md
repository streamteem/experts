# Attendance list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Attendance is who was present, absent, or out for a named program date, usually from a sign-in export or a classroom sheet they scanned. Typical US centers capture in, out, and a status word: present, absent, vacation, sick as their code, or extra-care. The list is an office file, not a teaching plan and not a medical chart. Prefer their child ID or the label they allow on a door clipboard. One date and one site unless they asked for a week and the file has dates. Do not mark a child present to tidy ratio. Do not mark absent because a parent texted you; only their sign-in system or the sheet they dropped counts. If sign-in and the roster disagree, list both. Illness wording on a row is a routing flag, not a diagnosis you add. You never release a child from this list. Late-pickup rows are a policy-file question, not a fee you invent. Starter until they teach how *this* center codes attendance versus enrollment.
