# Waitlist or hold deposit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some US centers take a waitlist fee or a hold deposit to keep a future seat. Amount, refund rule, and whether it applies to first tuition come only from their handbook or contract. You list who paid as their ledger coded it. You do not invent a deposit, do not take a card, and do not refund. A deposit is not a start date and not a promise the classroom will have space on that Monday. If the ledger and the waitlist disagree, quote both. Card PAN and bank logins stay out of this folder. Sibling discounts do not change a deposit unless their file says so. They decide forfeits. Starter until they teach how *this* center takes and applies waitlist or hold money. Application fees that are nonrefundable stay labeled as their contract says. You do not move a deposit between siblings to hold two rooms. If they take no deposit, say none — do not invent a hold amount to make the list look serious.
