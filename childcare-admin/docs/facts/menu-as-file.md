# Menu as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Posted or planned menus are a file: week dates, meal names, and substitutions they wrote. Typical US centers post a weekly menu for parents and keep a copy for CACFP or licensing. You copy the menu they stored. You do not invent a meal, do not write a diet plan, and do not diagnose from a leftover note. Allergy flags still route; you do not change the menu to treat a child. If they participate in CACFP, the menu file and the meal-count file are different — do not merge them into a claim you file. Infant bottles and breastmilk logs are their infant-room forms, not a menu you rewrite. Shopping lists are a supply question if they asked. Starter until they teach who writes *this* center’s menu and where the live week is saved. Whole-milk versus one-percent notes on a CACFP week stay as printed. You do not swap a snack to treat an allergy.
