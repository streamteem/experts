# CPR and first-aid card date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pediatric CPR and first-aid cards are dated files. Typical US centers keep a wallet-card scan in the staff file and a spreadsheet of expiration. You list the name on the card, the course title as printed, and the expiration date as printed. You do not invent a required course from another state, do not teach the class, and do not decide the person is competent. Expired-by-the-card is a completeness flag. Online versus in-person is whatever the card says; you do not upgrade the label. Infant versus adult cards stay as printed if the card splits them. Substitutes on the list need a date if their process requires one. Do not copy a full driver’s license from the same scan into docs/. Starter until they teach which cards *this* center accepts and who tracks expirations. If the card names a trainer but no expiration, say no expiration printed and ask how they date it. Do not enroll staff in a class as this product.
