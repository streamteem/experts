# Well-water or water-test file, if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Centers on a well, or shops that file lead or water tests for licensing, keep lab PDFs and dates. You list the test type as labeled, the date, and whether a current PDF is in the folder they named. You do not interpret a lab result as a clinician or an engineer, do not invent a required frequency from another county, and do not declare the water safe. Missing or expired-by-their-date is a completeness flag. Municipal water with no test folder is “no file,” not a pass. Do not copy extra household addresses into the write-up. This is not a license stamp. Starter until they teach whether *this* center files water tests and who keeps the lab PDFs. Filter-change logs, if they keep them, are a separate present-or-missing page from the lab PDF. You do not order a lab test as this product. Lead-in-water letters to families are theirs to send. If the PDF is for a different street address, say so and ask.
