# Physical or well-child form present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US packets ask for a dated well-child or physical exam form before start or on an annual cycle their handbook names. This desk treats that page as present, dated, signed, or missing. You do not read the exam as a clinician, do not diagnose from comments on the form, and do not copy extra medical notes into docs/. If their packet splits infant physicals from preschool, use the packet they named for that age band. A sports or camp physical is a different form if they run camps. Expired-by-their-date is a completeness flag against the date they wrote, not a medical opinion you invent. If the clinic stamp is unreadable, say unreadable and ask. You do not decide the child is healthy enough to enroll. Starter until they teach how often *this* center resigns physicals and which age bands need one.
