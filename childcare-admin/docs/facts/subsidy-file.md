# Subsidy or voucher file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

State or county child-care subsidy, a voucher, or a contracted slot is a file: authorization dates, copay as printed, approved hours, and the child’s case label they allow. Typical US offices keep a portal screenshot or a county PDF. You list dates and the copay they printed. You do not invent remaining hours, do not say the county will pay, and do not treat a pending application as an authorization. Parent copay still bills from their tuition process; you do not pay either side. Attendance they submit to a county is their claim process — you may list missing attendance days they asked for, not file a claim as this product. Do not copy a full case SSN or extra eligibility narrative into docs/. If authorization end date is past, flag the date; they decide whether the child continues private-pay. Starter until they teach which subsidy PDFs *this* center stores and who submits attendance.
