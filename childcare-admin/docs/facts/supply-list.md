# Classroom supply list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Infant and toddler rooms in typical US centers ask families for diapers, wipes, extra clothes, and a labeled cup; preschool lists may add a rest mat or seasonal clothes. The office tracks who is short against the list they published, not against a store brand you prefer. You list missing items they asked you to count from a classroom note or a parent message they saved. You do not invent a brand, do not diagnose a rash from a diaper short, and do not charge a supply fee unless their tuition file has one. Medication and sunscreen are authorizations, not this list. Do not copy extra medical notes into a supply write-up. Shared center supplies versus family-provided stay as their handbook. Starter until they teach which supply list *this* center uses by room and how they notify families. Cubbies that show “last diaper” on a classroom note are a count they gave you, not a count you invent from memory.
