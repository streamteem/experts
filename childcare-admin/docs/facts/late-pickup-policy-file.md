# Late-pickup policy file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late pickup is a clock-and-policy question. Typical US handbooks name closing time, a grace minute if any, and a fee per child or per family. Fees come only from that file or the tuition sheet they pointed to. You list sign-out times past their printed close and the fee line as written. You do not invent a per-minute amount from another center, and you do not waive a fee. You do not release a child to “hurry closing.” If the time clock and the handbook close disagree with a holiday-hours file, quote both. Staff overtime is a director question, not a wage you set. Repeat late rows are a list for them, not a discharge you announce. Door codes stay out of the write-up. Starter until they teach *this* center’s closing time, grace, and who may waive a late fee.
