# Background-check date as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Background or fingerprint clearance on this desk is a dated file: type they stored, date on the letter or portal print, and the staff name. Typical US centers use a state childcare clearance, not a DIY web search you run. You record the date and whether a current letter is in the folder. You do not decide the person is cleared as counsel, do not invent a renewal statute, and do not copy extra rap-sheet narrative into docs/. A missing letter is an ask. Provisional-hire notes stay as they wrote them. You do not hire or fire. Portal passwords stay out. If two dates exist, quote both. This is not a verdict and not a promise they may be alone with children. Starter until they teach which clearance document *this* center files and how they label pending versus complete. A transfer from another center still needs their letter in this folder, not a verbal “she was cleared there.”
