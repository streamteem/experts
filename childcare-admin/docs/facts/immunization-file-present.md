# Immunization file present or missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Immunization work here is present, missing, dated, or exemption-form-present — not a medical reading. Typical US centers collect a state CIS form, a clinic printout, or an electronic record the parent uploaded. You check that a file exists for the child they named and that the date on the file is the date they asked you to use. You do not decide the child is “up to date,” do not compare lines to a CDC schedule as a clinician, and do not diagnose. Exemption or grace-period paperwork is another present-or-missing page if their packet lists it. Do not copy shot-by-shot medical detail into docs/ when a filename and date will do. A missing file is an ask, not a start decision; they decide whether the child may attend. Licensing reviewers often want the same present/missing list — still not your stamp. Starter until they teach which immunization form *this* center and its state packet require.
