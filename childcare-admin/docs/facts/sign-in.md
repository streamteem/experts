# Sign-in and sign-out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sign-in and sign-out are the timed marks that a named adult brought or collected a child. Typical US centers use a tablet kiosk, a PIN, a paper clipboard, or a staff-entered Brightwheel or Procare event. The office export usually shows child, time in, time out, and the adult name the system stored. You list gaps: missing in, missing out, or an adult name that is not on the pickup list they gave you. You do not check IDs at the door as this product, and you do not tell a waiting adult they may take a child. A PIN or door code that appears in an export is a secret — do not copy it into docs/. Staff-entered “parent forgot to tap” notes stay as their words. Split custody days are a pickup-list and custody-file question, not a sign-in you rewrite. If the kiosk date looks like yesterday, ask before you treat it as today. Starter until they teach how *this* center captures in and out.
