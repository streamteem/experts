# Tuition file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Private-pay tuition comes from their rate sheet, contract, or handbook exhibit — not from a remembered weekly number. Typical US centers price by age band, full-time versus part-time days, and sometimes a registration fee. You copy the rate that matches the child’s schedule on their contract. You do not invent a rate, a sibling percent, or a camp add-on. Billing exports from Procare, Brightwheel, or QuickBooks are status as they coded them: posted, paid, or past due — not a payment you make. Extra-care hours and late-pickup fees are separate lines if their file splits them. You never send money. Card numbers and bank logins stay out of this folder. If the contract and the rate sheet disagree, quote both and ask. Year-end statements are a different ask. Starter until they teach which rate file is live for *this* center and which classroom maps to which price.
