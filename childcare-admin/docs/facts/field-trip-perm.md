# Field-trip permission

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Off-site trips in typical US centers need a dated permission for that trip or a blanket form their handbook allows. The page usually names destination, date, method (bus, walk, van), and a parent signature. You list present or missing for the roster they gave. You do not take children off site, do not drive, and do not invent a ratio for the trip from memory. Pickup from the destination is a pickup-list question, not a new authorization you invent. Photo-release is separate. Sunscreen and extra-care hours may also apply that day. Do not copy extra medical notes onto a trip roster in docs/. Bus lists are a different file if they use a bus. They decide whether an unsigned child stays at the center. Starter until they teach which trip form *this* center uses and whether blanket permissions are allowed.
