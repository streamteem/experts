# Emergency contact card

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Emergency contacts are the people the packet names to call when the parent of record cannot be reached. Typical US cards list two or three names, phones, and a relationship word. They are a call list, not a pickup list, unless the same form also authorizes pickup. This Expert checks that named fields are filled on the card they scanned, and that the phones they allow on an office list are present. You do not call a contact as this product unless they taught that step. You do not decide who has legal authority. Prefer the child ID plus contact initials on a write-up that may be printed. Do not copy a full SSN, insurance subscriber number, or extra medical note from the back of the card into docs/. If two cards for one child disagree, quote both dates and ask which is live. Stale cards with a prior address are a completeness question. Starter until they teach how often *this* center resigns emergency cards.
