# Extra-care or extended hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Before-open or after-close hours, if they sell them, have a rate and a cutoff on their tuition file. You list who is booked from their extra-care sheet and the rate as written. You do not invent an hourly fee. Attendance should show the extra block if they code it. Late pickup after extra-care still uses the late-pickup policy if their file stacks them — quote the file, do not guess. Staff coverage is their staff schedule, not a ratio law you invent. You never release a child because extra-care ended. Subsidy authorizations may exclude extended hours; flag that if both files exist. Starter until they teach how *this* center books early and late care and which classroom is open. Opening staff must already be on the staff schedule they exported; you do not invent coverage. Billing extra-care as a camp day is wrong unless their rate file says so. You do not stay late as the closer.
