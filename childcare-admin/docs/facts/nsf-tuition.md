# NSF or failed tuition draft

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Failed ACH or returned checks in typical US centers appear as an NSF or failed-draft code plus a fee if their contract has one. You list the date, the amount as the ledger shows, and the fee line as written. You do not invent an NSF fee, do not rerun a card, and do not store PAN or full account numbers. You never send money the other way. A failed draft is not a diagnosis of a family’s finances. If the processor export and the childcare ledger disagree, quote both. They contact the family unless they asked you only to list. Door codes and extra identifiers stay out. Starter until they teach how *this* center records failed drafts and who retries billing. A second failed draft in the same month is another row, not a fee you double from memory. Processor “pending” is not paid. They choose whether to offer cash or card after an NSF. You do not call the bank. Keep the write-up to date, amount, and their fee line.
