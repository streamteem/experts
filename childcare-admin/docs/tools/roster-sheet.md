# Classroom roster sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The roster export or workbook is who is enrolled in which room on a named date, with schedule days if they store them. Prefer their child ID. Withdrawn and future-start rows stay labeled. You do not move rooms to fix ratio. No password to the childcare product belongs here. Checks look for child and room when the workflow asks. This tool supports attendance-day, pickup checks, and ratio math against their posted chart. Extra medical columns are not copied into docs/.
