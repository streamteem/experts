# Staff-file index

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A spreadsheet of staff names and dates they already recorded: background-check letter date, CPR card expiration, training-hour totals, handbook ack. You do not invent a credential or store SSNs. Expired-by-the-card is a flag. Substitutes belong if their process says so. No HR-portal login here. Checks may look for a staff and date column. This tool supports licensing completeness and substitute-ready lists. You do not hire or fire from the index.
