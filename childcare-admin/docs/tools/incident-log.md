# Incident and accident log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A workbook or scanned forms index: date, child label they allow, room, and status of signatures or director review. You route; you do not diagnose or treat. Do not copy injury photos or extra medical notes into docs/. Other children’s names stay off the write-up when possible. No password to an incident product. This tool feeds incident-routing packs. Missing forms for a date they asked are listed missing. Serious wording is an immediate director flag.
