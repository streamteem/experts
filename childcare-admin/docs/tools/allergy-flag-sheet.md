# Allergy flag sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

A one-line flag list they maintain for kitchen or leads: child label, room, allergen word they already used. Routing only. You do not treat and you do not copy the full care plan into docs/. Photo-on-board is a photo-release question. If the sheet and the enrollment allergy page disagree, quote both. No medical-portal login. This tool supports day lists when they asked for flags present-or-missing, not a diagnosis. Missing sheet: say missing.
