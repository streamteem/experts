# Tuition and rate sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rate exhibit, contract extract, or ledger export: child or family, schedule, rate, and status as they coded it. Fees come from this file only. You do not invent a weekly number or take a payment. Card PAN and bank logins stay out. Subsidy copays are a different file if they split them. Checks look for child and amount or rate when the workflow asks. This tool feeds tuition-from-file packs and late-fee lists as written. Year-end paid totals use the paid column they exported.
