# Attendance export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

They export daily attendance or sign-in from Procare, Brightwheel, KinderConnect, or a scanned clipboard sheet typed to CSV. Typical columns: child label they allow, room, status or in/out times, and date. No login is stored here. Confirm the date before you treat it as today. You do not mark present to tidy ratio. Checks often look for a child and status or time column. This tool feeds attendance-day and ratio-alert packs. Door PIN columns get dropped before the file stays in the folder.
