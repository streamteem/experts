# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build in work/ or out/ for a dispatch board, a below-min list, or a join. Columns come from their exports, not from invented intervals or hours. Typical US planners live in Excel beside the CMMS. Checks use csv-has-columns or file-exists as the step says. No cloud-sheet password belongs here. This tool is the usual pack-out surface for write-ups they will read.
