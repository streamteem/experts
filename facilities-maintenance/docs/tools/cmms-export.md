# CMMS export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The CMMS export is their work-order, asset, or PM dump from Fiix, UpKeep, Limble, eMaint, or a workbook they treat as the CMMS. You list rows; you do not invent tickets. Draft versus approved stays as they coded it. No portal password belongs here. This tool can start dispatch, backlog, and warranty packs when they use one file for several tables. Checks look for wo or asset columns when the step says so. Vendor help pages are pointers, not a login.
