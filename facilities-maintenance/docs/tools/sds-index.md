# SDS index

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The SDS index is their list of chemical PDFs: product name, file name, date if they store one. You list present or missing against crib items or tickets that name a chemical. You do not write an SDS. You do not stamp OSHA hazcom. No vendor-portal password belongs here. Use this folder when a pack must show SDS coverage. Missing index rows stay flags. They keep the binder. Product names on the crib must match the index or stay a mismatch flag. You do not download a live SDS unless they saved the file. Dates they store are dates, not a hazcom stamp.
