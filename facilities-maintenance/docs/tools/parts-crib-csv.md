# Parts-crib CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The parts-crib CSV is on-hand, min-qty, location, and SKU for the storeroom. Typical US plants export from the CMMS or a crib workbook. You compare on-hand to min. You do not invent a count or a min. Shortages stay visible. No vendor-portal password belongs here. This tool starts parts-below-min. Checks look for sku, on-hand, and min-qty when asked. Kit and spare flags stay as they coded them. Bin and stockroom columns stay if they exported them. You do not merge satellite cages into one on-hand. Units of measure stay as written. Zero on-hand is a real zero, not a missing row you drop.
