# Procedure PDF folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The procedure folder holds LOTO PDFs, permit-to-work forms, hot-work and confined-space forms, and job-plan attachments they dropped. You index present or missing against the ticket or asset. You do not write a new procedure. You do not stamp OSHA. No portal password belongs here. This tool starts loto-form-present and supports any pack that must show a form. Empty folder on a required asset is a flag. File names and dates are the index. You do not OCR a procedure into new isolation steps. Duplicate PDFs for one asset stay listed so they can pick the live one. Expired forms stay expired.
