# Plant calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The plant calendar is their production, holiday, shutdown-window, and silent-hours file. You read tickets against it. You do not invent a down day or a quiet hour. You do not rewrite the production schedule. No calendar-login password belongs here. This tool supports emergency-after-hours-list and any pack that claims a ticket fits a window. Holidays follow this file, not a guessed federal list. Shutdown, holiday, and silent-hours tabs stay labeled. You do not invent a second shift. This tool is not manufacturing-scheduler dispatch. Emergency waives use their emergency rule file if they pointed at it.
