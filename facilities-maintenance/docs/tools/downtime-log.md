# Downtime log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The downtime log is their start, end, asset, and downtime-code file from the CMMS or a supervisor workbook. You do not invent an end time. You do not hide a row. Open outages stay open. No production-system password belongs here. This tool starts the downtime-log workflow. Checks look for asset, start, and code when the step says so. Planned shutdown rows stay labeled if they labeled them. Time zones follow their file. You do not convert a shift comment into an end stamp. Codes not on their list stay questions. This tool does not rewrite the production calendar.
