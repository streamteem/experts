# Work-order CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The work-order CSV is the ticket table: wo, asset, location, priority, craft, status, type. Typical US plants export open and recently closed tickets. You do not invent a wo number or a diagnosis. Waiting-parts and emergency flags stay as coded. No CMMS password belongs here. This tool starts dispatch-list, backlog-by-craft, contractor-wo-list, emergency-after-hours-list, and loto-form-present. Checks often look for wo, asset, and priority. Closed tickets belong in the export only when they asked for a look-back. You do not reopen a ticket in the CMMS. Status and type stay their codes, not a tidy rewrite.
