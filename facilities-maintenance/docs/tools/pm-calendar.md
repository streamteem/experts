# PM calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The PM calendar is their next-due or job-plan export: asset, plan name, interval as stored, last-done, next-due or meter trigger. You do not invent an interval. Overdue is versus the date they asked you to use. No CMMS password belongs here. This tool starts the pm-due workflow. Checks look for asset and next-due when the step says so. Meter-based rows still need a meter column they stored. Last-done blanks stay blank. You do not back-fill a due date from a remembered interval. Job-plan names stay their names. Forecast rows they did not ask for stay off the pack.
