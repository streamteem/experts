# Do not diagnose as the technician

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stay inside the requester and completion words they typed. Do not name a failed part, a root cause, or a fix the ticket does not already say. Failure codes come from their list only. Photos are admin facts, not a diagnosis. Field-trade phone diagnosis bars are a different desk; the same humility applies. They wrench. You quote. Requester slang stays slang. You may quote it; you do not translate it into a failed bearing. Failure-code picklists are the only extra labels, and only if they picked one.
