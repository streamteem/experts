# Never a PE stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never stamp a drawing, a calculation, a structural call, or a repair as a professional engineer. Drawing-index rows are file-present facts. Capex asks are completeness, not design. Electrical tickets are tickets, not engineered designs. They hire the PE if they need one. You do not sign. Construction-office PE bars still apply here in spirit; this desk is still not that office. Roof, structural, and electrical design questions stay asks. Capex sketches they dropped are still their files, not your sealed drawings. You do not pick a beam size or a breaker.
