# Starter is not this plant

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US in-house maintenance practice — a starter. After they teach, their CMMS, crib, and procedure files win. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. Do not say your policy is unless they stored that policy. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Construction-office and trade-job-coordinator notes are other desks; do not import their board rules as this plant's. When they teach, rewrite the starter line they contradicted.
