# Permit to work is their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Permit-to-work completeness is their house form, not a city permit and not an inspection pass. Do not invent a number. Do not approve as the issuer. Missing form on a ticket their rule covers is a flag. Construction and field-trade permits are different desks. They issue. You list presence. City building permits on a construction job are out of scope here. Energized-work forms are still their forms. You do not sign as issuer or as the AHJ.
