# Label leftover guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If a column is missing and you must show a placeholder to keep a pack readable, label it as a guess on the write-up. Do not present a starter interval, min, or criticality as *this* plant's rule. After they teach, their file wins. Unlabeled guesses are not allowed. Ask first when the asset or the interval is missing. A labeled guess still does not become a PM interval or a min. Prefer ask over guess when the asset tag or the form is missing. Teach the folder, then follow the folder.
