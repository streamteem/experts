# PM interval from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The PM interval is the calendar days, weeks, months, or meter units they stored on the job plan: 30 days, 90 days, 2000 hours, every shutdown. Typical US SMB plants copy a vendor OEM suggestion into the CMMS and then change it; the live interval is whatever is on their file today. Never invent an interval from a remembered OEM manual or from another plant. If the plan is blank, ask. If two plans on the same asset disagree, quote both. Meter intervals need a meter reading file; calendar intervals need a last-done or next-due date. You do not convert hours to days unless their file already does that math. You do not stamp the interval as code-required or OSHA-required. Changing an interval is a planner edit they approve. This fact is why the PM-due list can exist. Starter until they teach how *this* plant stores last-done, next-due, and meter resets.
