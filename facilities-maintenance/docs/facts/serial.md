# Serial number

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A serial number is the nameplate or OEM serial they stored on the asset or on a spare. Typical US plants copy it at install and reuse it on warranty claims and parts-used rows. Use the serial on the file. Do not invent a serial from a blurry photo you cannot read. Do not swap a serial to make a warranty pack look complete. If the work order, the asset row, and the leftover-return disagree, quote all three. Field-trade install serials on customer equipment are a different desk. A model number is not a serial. Missing serial on a warranted asset is a flag. They read the nameplate. You copy the string they stored. Starter until they teach when *this* plant requires a serial at receive and at install. Core returns and rotables keep the same serial string they stored. Do not tidy a transcription; if two characters disagree, ask. Model plus date is not a serial.
