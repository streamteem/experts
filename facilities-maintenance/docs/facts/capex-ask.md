# Capex ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A capex ask is their request file to replace or add an asset: estimate they stored, asset, reason in their words, and a status such as draft, submitted, or approved. Typical US plants keep a workbook or a CMMS project request. You list the ask as they wrote it. You do not approve capital. You do not invent a budget number. You do not stamp the replacement as engineering-required. A repair work order that someone wishes were capital still stays a repair until they change the type. Quotes from vendors are files they dropped, not prices you invent. This is not a construction bid desk and not GoNoGo. They submit the ask. You assemble the completeness pack. Starter until they teach who at *this* plant may open a capex request and which quotes must sit in the folder. Competing quotes stay listed as separate files. You do not pick the winner.
