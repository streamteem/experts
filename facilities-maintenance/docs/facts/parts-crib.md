# Parts crib

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The parts crib is the in-house storeroom of maintenance spares: filters, belts, fuses, seals, kits they stock for assets they operate. Typical US plants export on-hand, location, and min-max from the CMMS or keep a crib workbook. This is not a field-trade truck stock and not a construction job-site laydown. You list SKUs and quantities from that export. You do not invent an on-hand count. You do not treat a vendor catalog as crib inventory. Reserved or kit-allocated rows stay labeled if they coded them. Cycle-count versus system qty disagreements stay listed. A stockroom aisle is not a second inventory unless they said so. You do not pay a vendor to refill the crib. Shortages against an open work order stay visible. They own the count. Starter until they teach how *this* plant issues parts to a work order and what a bin location means.
