# Stockroom

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The stockroom is the physical crib or satellite cage they named: main crib, line-side cage, contractor cage. Typical US plants put a stockroom code on each item location. Copy that code. Do not merge two rooms because they share a building. Do not invent a bin. Cycle counts are per stockroom if that is their file. Issuing from the wrong room is a question on the parts-used row. This is not a wholesale warehouse product and not a construction laydown yard. Access to the cage is a process; badge secrets stay out of docs/. They own the rooms. Starter until they teach how *this* plant transfers between stockrooms and which rooms a craft may enter. Line-side cages that crafts enter without a clerk still need their issue rule if they have one. Do not treat a toolbox as a stockroom unless they numbered it. Transfers in transit stay in transit. Satellite bins that share a SKU with main crib are two locations, not one count you merge.
