# Criticality from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Criticality is the A/B/C or production-critical flag they already stored on the asset. Typical US plants set it once in a workshop and then forget to update it; the live value is still whatever is on the file. Use that value. Never invent a criticality because the line was down last week. Never raise a min-qty or a PM interval from a guessed criticality. If two files disagree, quote both. Criticality is not an OSHA severity and not a PE risk study. Emergency priority on a ticket is not the same as asset criticality; keep both. They change the flag. You list it on PM-due and dispatch when they use it to sort. Starter until they teach who at *this* plant may edit criticality and whether spare mins follow it. A production-critical note in a comment is not the coded flag unless they said comments count. Do not sort the dispatch list by a guessed A when the field is blank.
