# Kit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A kit is a planned pick list they stored for a PM or a work-order job plan: filters, belts, gaskets, a counted bag. Typical US plants build kits in the CMMS so the crib can stage before the shutdown window. Use the kit file. Do not invent a line to be helpful. Do not substitute a close SKU. If the kit and the crib on-hand disagree, the shortage stays visible. A kit is not a BOM for making a product; manufacturing-scheduler owns production bills. Field-trade packing lists for a customer truck are a different desk. Leftover-return after the job still needs their return row. They maintain kits. You list picks and holes. Starter until they teach when *this* plant stages a kit and whether a short kit may still go to the floor. Partial kits stay partial. Do not mark the PM ready because most lines picked. Staging location comes from their stockroom code.
