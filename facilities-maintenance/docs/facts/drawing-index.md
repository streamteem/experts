# Drawing index

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A drawing index is their list of prints and as-builts: drawing number, revision, title, and a file name. Typical US plants keep a PDF folder and a spreadsheet index. You list whether the work order or the asset points at a drawing that is in the folder. You do not stamp a drawing as as-built. You do not revise a print as a PE. You do not invent a revision. If the asset row cites D-104 and the index has no D-104, flag missing. Construction submittal logs are a different desk. Field-trade install drawings for a customer are a different desk. A photo is not a drawing unless they filed it as one. They own the vault. Starter until they teach how *this* plant revisions prints and when a missing drawing blocks a ticket. Redlines they scanned are still their file, not a PE as-built. A missing revision letter is a blank, not a guessed A.
