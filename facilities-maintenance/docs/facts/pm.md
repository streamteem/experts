# Preventive maintenance

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preventive maintenance is the planned work they already scheduled against an asset: filter change, lube, inspection checklist, belt check. Typical US plants store PM job plans in the CMMS with a next-due date or a meter trigger. A PM work order is still a work order; it is not a production traveler and not a construction inspection. You list PMs that are due from their calendar file. You do not invent a PM that is not on the plan. You do not mark a PM complete because the asset looks fine in a photo. You do not write an OSHA-compliant PM. If the plan says quarterly and someone asks for monthly, that is a change they make on the plan, not a silent edit. Predictive or condition routes stay labeled as their file labels them. Project work is not a PM. They own the plan. Starter until they teach how *this* plant names PM job plans and what complete means on a PM ticket.
