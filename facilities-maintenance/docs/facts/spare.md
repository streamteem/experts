# Spare

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A spare is a crib item they stock against an asset or a class: a spare motor, a spare board, a filter they always keep. Typical US plants mark insurance spares or rotables on the item row. List the spare from the crib file and the asset link they stored. Do not invent a spare because the asset is critical. Do not treat a vendor catalog as a spare on the shelf. Rotable serials, if they track them, stay with the serial fact. Kits are not the same as a single spare unless they built a kit. You do not pay to create a spare. Below-min on a spare SKU still uses their min. They decide what to stock. Starter until they teach how *this* plant links spares to assets and how a rotable returns to the crib. Insurance spares that sit at the OEM are not crib on-hand unless they receipted them.
