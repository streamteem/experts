# Project versus repair

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Project versus repair is the split they already use: a corrective repair work order versus a small project or capital job they track with a different number or type. Typical US plants mark type as reactive, PM, project, or capex-ask. Copy their type. Do not flip a repair into a project to hide backlog hours. Do not flip a project into a repair to skip a capex ask. Construction change orders and field-trade sold jobs are not this split. A project may still need LOTO forms and a shutdown window. Hours and parts still come from their files. If type is blank, ask. They classify the work. You keep the label. Starter until they teach how *this* plant numbers projects versus tickets and when a repair must become a capex ask. A multi-week outage can still be typed repair if that is their code. Do not relabel it project to park hours off the backlog chart. Mixed bags stay mixed until they split the numbers.
