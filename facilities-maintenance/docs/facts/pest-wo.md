# Pest work order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pest work order is a ticket or a contractor service row they stored: location, pest as they named it, and the vendor if any. Typical US plants keep a monthly pest contractor and exception tickets for sightings. You copy their words. You do not diagnose an infestation. You do not apply pesticide as a license. You do not invent a treatment interval. SDS for chemicals they store still uses the SDS index. Food-plant versus office campus stays as their file. This is not a veterinary or medical opinion. They call the listed vendor. Starter until they teach which buildings *this* plant puts on the pest contract and what an exception ticket must include. Sighting photos follow the photo rule: name the file, do not diagnose. Contract service dates are dates, not a treatment you prescribe. Food-area versus warehouse zones stay their location codes. Do not invent a bait-station count.
