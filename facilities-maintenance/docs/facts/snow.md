# Snow and ice tickets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Snow tickets are work orders or contractor call logs they store for plow, salt, and ice events. Typical US plants keep a storm trigger in a weather note they wrote and a contractor name on the after-hours list. You list what they logged. You do not invent a trigger temperature. You do not invent a tonnage of salt. You do not pay the plow. A weather.com print they saved is a file; a live scrape is not this desk. Silent-hours do not erase a storm ticket. This is not a landscaping-office seasonal product. They call the vendor or the grounds craft. Starter until they teach *this* plant's storm trigger and which lots are in-house versus contractor. Pre-treat versus plow is their ticket type if they split them. Contractor arrival times come from the call log, not a promised SLA you invent. Ice melt qty is their issue or delivery ticket. Do not hide a storm row because the next morning was clear.
