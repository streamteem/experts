# Contractor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A contractor is an outside firm they hire for a craft they do not staff: elevator, fire sprinkler, chiller overhaul, roof patch, pest. Typical US facilities desks keep a contractor list with after-hours numbers they already published internally, COI dates as dates, and which assets or crafts the firm covers. You list who is on the ticket from their file. You do not hire the firm. You do not pay the invoice. You do not treat a contractor as holding your electrical license. Construction offices run subs on a GC job; this desk invites a specialist onto an in-house asset. Field trade coordinators dispatch their own techs to customers; you do not. Missing COI date is a missing date, not a legal finding. Scope on the work order stays their words. They call the contractor. Starter until they teach how *this* plant badges contractors and which tickets must stay in-house.
