# Leftover return

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A leftover return is the unused qty they posted back to the crib from a work order or a kit. Typical US plants require a return row so on-hand comes back. List the return from their file. Do not invent a return to tidy a variance. Do not assume leftovers were restocked because the job closed. Cycle-count fights after a missing return stay listed. Serials on rotables still need the serial fact. You do not pay a restocking fee. Field-trade parts-return to a supply house is a different desk. They restock. You show the return row or the hole. Starter until they teach when *this* plant requires leftover-return and who signs the crib back in. Damaged leftovers they coded scrap are not a return. Do not restock scrap on the pack. Partial-unit returns follow their unit of measure. If the kit issued a bag and they returned pieces, quote both qtys. A missing return on a staged kit is a crib hole until they post it.
