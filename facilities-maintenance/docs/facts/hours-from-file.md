# Hours from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Labor hours are estimated or actual hours they stored on the work order or the time sheet: planned hours, actual hours, overtime flag as their file. Typical US plants export hours from the CMMS or a time workbook. Use those numbers. Do not invent an estimate to load the week. Do not invent actuals because the ticket sat open for two days. Planned versus actual variances stay listed; you do not hide them. Hours are not a wage you set and not a payment you make. Production downtime hours and maintenance labor hours are not the same clock unless they said so. Contractor hours on their ticket stay labeled contractor. They enter time. You list it. Starter until they teach how *this* plant books wrench time, travel inside the campus, and waiting-parts time. Waiting-parts time is still hours only if they booked it that way. A blank actual at close is a question, not a guessed eight-hour day. Overtime flags stay their flags.
