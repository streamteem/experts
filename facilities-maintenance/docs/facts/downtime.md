# Downtime

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Downtime is the period their file says an asset or line could not run: start, end, duration, and often a downtime code. Typical US plants log downtime in the CMMS, a production sheet, or a supervisor workbook. Copy the times they wrote. Do not invent an end time to close a still-open outage. Do not hide a downtime row so the week looks healthy. Downtime is not an OSHA incident and not a construction weather delay. If production says down and maintenance has no work order, flag the gap. If a work order is open and downtime is blank, ask whether they want a downtime row. Partial derates stay labeled as their file labels them. Planned shutdown windows are not unplanned downtime unless they coded them that way. They own the log. You list it. Starter until they teach how *this* plant starts and stops a downtime clock and who may edit it.
