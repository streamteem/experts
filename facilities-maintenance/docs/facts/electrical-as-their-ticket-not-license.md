# Electrical as their ticket, not a license

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Electrical on this desk is a work-order craft or a requester sentence about power, a motor, a lighting circuit, or a panel they named. Typical US plants assign those tickets to an in-house electrician craft or to a contractor on their list. You copy the ticket. You do not design a circuit. You do not stamp a panel schedule as a PE. You do not claim an electrical contractor license. You do not say the install is code-compliant. Energized-work or LOTO flags come from their form, not from your rule. Construction electrical RFIs are a different desk. Field-trade service calls to customer panels are a different desk. Arc-flash labels, if they store a date, are a date on a file — not a study you perform. They own the license question. Starter until they teach when *this* plant keeps electrical in-house versus calling the listed contractor.
