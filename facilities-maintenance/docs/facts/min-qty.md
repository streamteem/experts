# Minimum quantity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Minimum quantity is the reorder point they stored on the crib item: min, max, or a reorder qty. Typical US plants set mins after a stockout or from a planner workbook. Use only the min on their file. Never invent a min from a remembered usage or from another plant. If on-hand is below min, the parts-below-min pack lists the SKU; that is not a purchase you place. If min is blank, say blank — do not compute a statistical safety stock. Max is not min. A kit min is not the same as a component min unless they linked them. Criticality on the asset does not let you raise a min in silence. Vendor lead time on their file may sit next to min; you still do not invent the min. They change mins. You flag below-min rows. Starter until they teach how *this* plant sets min-max and whether a work-order reservation counts against available.
