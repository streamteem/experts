# Crib cycle count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A crib cycle count is their dated count of a bin or SKU versus system on-hand. Typical US plants count A items often and C items rarely if they use that scheme; use their count calendar, not a guessed ABC. List variance as their count minus system qty. Do not invent a count. Do not adjust the system as the bookkeeper. Do not hide a variance so the below-min list looks clean. Blind count versus told count stays as they ran it. This is not a manufacturing finished-goods count. Photos of a bin are not a count unless they filed a count sheet. They count. You list exceptions. Starter until they teach how *this* plant posts adjustments and whether a work-order issue during the count is held. Recounts they ordered stay open until their second count posts. Do not average two counts to hide a fight. Frozen bins during count follow their freeze flag, not a guessed hold.
