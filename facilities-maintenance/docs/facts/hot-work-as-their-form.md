# Hot work as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hot work on this desk is whether their hot-work permit form is present for welding, cutting, or spark-producing work they named on the ticket. Typical US plants keep a dated permit with a location and a watch if their form has that box. You list present, expired, or missing from the file. You do not issue the permit. You do not stamp fire-code compliance. You do not invent a fire-watch hour. If the work-order craft is weld and their rule requires a form, a missing PDF is a flag. Construction hot-work on a GC site is a different desk. They isolate and they watch. You assemble completeness. Starter until they teach how *this* plant dates hot-work permits and whether a contractor brings a second form. Multi-day welds need a new date if their form expires daily. Do not extend a yesterday permit. Spark-producing grinding they named still follows the same form rule they wrote.
