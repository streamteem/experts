# Confined space as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Confined-space on this desk is whether their entry form or permit PDF is present for the ticket and whether the asset or location is on their confined-space list. Typical US plants keep a numbered form and a space inventory. You list present or missing. You do not decide the space is permit-required as the regulator. You do not write an entry procedure. You do not stamp OSHA. If their rule file says this asset needs a form and the folder is empty, flag it. Construction trench permits are a different desk. Attic or crawl notes on a field-trade house are a different desk. They run entry. You check the form. Starter until they teach which spaces *this* plant lists and who issues the form. Attendant and rescue names on their form are copied as written, not invented. A cancelled entry still needs the cancelled form if they filed it. You do not decide atmospheric testing passed.
