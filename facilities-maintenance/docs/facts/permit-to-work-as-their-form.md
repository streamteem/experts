# Permit to work as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A permit to work is their house form that authorizes a defined task in a defined window: hot work, confined space, energized work, or a general PTW if that is their packet. Typical US plants keep a numbered PDF or a CMMS permit module. You list whether the form they require is present, dated, and tied to the work order. You do not invent a permit number. You do not approve the permit as the issuer. You do not treat this as a city building permit for a field-trade job or a construction inspection result. Missing form on a ticket their rule says needs one is a flag. Expired dates stay expired if they date them. This is not an OSHA stamp and not a PE stamp. They issue and they close permits. You assemble the completeness list. Starter until they teach which crafts and tasks *this* plant puts on a permit-to-work form.
