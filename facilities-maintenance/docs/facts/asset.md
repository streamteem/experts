# Asset

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An asset is the named piece of equipment or building system the work order is about: AHU-3, Press 12, Dock 2, Elevator B. Typical US plants keep an asset number, description, location, and sometimes a serial on the CMMS row. Use the asset they already stored. Do not invent a tag to fill a blank request that only says the roof leaks. If the request names a room and the asset list has three units in that room, ask which one. A building or a campus is not a substitute for the asset when their process requires a tag. This is not a customer equipment tag on a field-trade callback and not a construction installed-item punch. Missing asset on a PM or a downtime row is a question, not a chance to guess from a photo. Parent versus child assets follow their hierarchy file. They create assets. You list the number they used. Starter until they teach how *this* plant tags units and when a space-only ticket is allowed.
