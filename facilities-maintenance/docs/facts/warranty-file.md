# Warranty file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A warranty file is their dated record that an asset or a part is still under OEM or installer warranty: start, end, terms pointer, and sometimes a claim number. Typical US plants store a PDF and a date on the asset row. You list warranty-end versus today from that file. You do not invent a warranty period from a remembered OEM year. You do not file a claim as the company. You do not extend a date. If the install date and the warranty start disagree, quote both. Parts used on a warranted asset still need their serial and the work-order link if they claim. Field-trade warranty labor on a customer job is a different desk. Construction manufacturer warranties on a new build are a different desk. Missing warranty on a new asset is an ask, not a guessed two years. They own the claim. Starter until they teach how *this* plant starts the warranty clock and who may file.
