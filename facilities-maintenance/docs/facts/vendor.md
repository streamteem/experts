# Vendor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vendor on this desk is the supplier or contractor name they already store for a part, a service, or an after-hours call: the filter house, the elevator firm, the OEM. Typical US plants keep vendor numbers on the crib item and on contractor work orders. Copy the name and number from their list. Do not invent a preferred vendor because a catalog looks cheaper. Do not pay the vendor. A manufacturer on the asset row is not automatically the parts vendor. Construction offices track subs on a job; this desk tracks MRO and service firms against assets they operate. Field trade shops buy for customer jobs; you buy nothing. If two vendors sit on one SKU, ask which the crib uses. COI dates, if they store them, are dates — not a legal opinion. They choose vendors. Starter until they teach how *this* plant codes OEM versus aftermarket and who may add a vendor.
