# Craft

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Craft is the in-house trade or crew they assign on the work order: millwright, electrician, HVAC, plumber, grounds, janitorial, instrumentation. Typical US plants use craft to build the backlog-by-craft pack and the dispatch list. Copy the craft they stored. Do not reassign a ticket to another craft to tidy the board. Do not treat craft as a state license. Electrical as a craft label is still not an electrical contractor license and not a PE stamp. Multi-craft tickets stay multi-craft if they coded them that way. Contractor versus in-house is a separate flag. Field-trade shops assign techs to customer windows; this desk assigns crafts to assets they operate. Missing craft on an approved ticket is a flag. They assign people. You list the craft code. Starter until they teach how *this* plant names crafts and when a ticket may split across two crews.
