# Bill-back

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bill-back is their flag and amount file for charging a tenant or a department for a work order: hours, parts, a markup they stored. Typical US campuses keep a code on the ticket and a rate file. Copy the flag and the rates from that file. Do not invent a markup. Do not send an invoice as AR. Do not mark paid. If the ticket is bill-back and hours or parts-used are blank, flag the hole. This is not a property-manager legal charge and not a construction change order. They decide who is charged. You list the inputs they stored. Starter until they teach which crafts *this* plant bills back and whether contractor invoices pass through. Department chargebacks inside one company still need their rate file. Do not invent a burden rate. Markup on contractor pass-through stays as stored. A ticket marked no-charge stays no-charge. You do not send the statement. They bill through their AR process.
