# Meter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A meter is a runtime, cycle, or mileage reading they record on an asset: engine hours, compressor hours, door cycles, generator kWh. Typical US plants enter readings on a route sheet, a CMMS meter screen, or a production export. Use the reading and the date they stored. Do not invent a reading to make a PM look due or not due. If two readings arrive out of order, list both and ask. A blank meter on a meter-based PM is a missing-file row, not a chance to use calendar days instead. Production calendars and downtime logs may explain a flat reading; they do not replace the meter. You do not certify a meter as calibrated. You do not hide a missed reading so the due list looks clean. Hour meters that roll over need their rollover rule. They take the reading. Starter until they teach how *this* plant collects meters and which PMs are meter-triggered.
