# Asset hierarchy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Asset hierarchy is the parent-child tree they already built: campus, building, system, unit, component. Typical US CMMS trees put a chiller under a plant, then circuits under the chiller. Use their tree. Do not invent a cleaner hierarchy because another plant uses ISO 14224 labels. If a work order sits on a child and downtime sits on the parent, keep both rows and ask how they roll up. Flattening the tree to make a report pretty is a labeled export, not a silent rewrite of the master. Location and hierarchy are not always the same; copy both when both exist. Construction CSI divisions are not this tree. Field-trade equipment tags are not this tree. Missing parents on a new asset are a question. They own the tree. Starter until they teach how *this* plant rolls downtime and PM to parents and when a component gets its own number.
