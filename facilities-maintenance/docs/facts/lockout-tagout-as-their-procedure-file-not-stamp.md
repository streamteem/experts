# Lockout-tagout as their procedure file, not a stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lockout-tagout on this desk is whether their machine-specific procedure PDF or form is present for the asset or the work order, and whether the ticket says LOTO is required in their words. Typical US plants store LOTO procedures in a binder scan or a CMMS attachment. You list present or missing. You do not write a new isolation procedure. You do not stamp the job OSHA-compliant. You do not say the lockout was performed correctly. OSHA pages in sources are orientation only. If the work order is on an asset that their rule file says needs a procedure and the folder is empty, the loto-form-present pack flags it. A generic plant-wide poster is not a machine-specific procedure unless they said it is. Construction lockout on a GC site is a different desk. They isolate energy. You check the file. Starter until they teach how *this* plant names procedure files and who may revise them.
