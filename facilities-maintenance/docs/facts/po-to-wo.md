# Purchase order tied to a work order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A purchase order tied to a work order is their AP or CMMS link: PO number, work-order number, SKU or service line, and status such as requested, issued, or received. Typical US plants write a PO when the crib is short or a contractor is hired for a ticket. You list the link they stored. You do not invent a PO number. You do not mark the PO paid. You do not receive goods in the accounting system. If the work order is waiting-parts and no PO row exists, flag the gap. If a PO exists with no work order, ask which ticket it belongs to — do not invent the link. This is not a construction subcontract and not a field-trade supply-house ticket you pay. Partial receipts stay partial. They issue POs. You show the join. Starter until they teach how *this* plant numbers POs and when a work order may close with an open PO.
