# Exception or OS&D log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pointer to their exception spreadsheet or TMS export for the day. Use their codes (late, refused, breakdown, NS) and timestamps. Breakdown rows go to the top of a pack. Do not invent a code or a reason from a call you remember. OS&D ticket numbers stay as filed; no liability language.
