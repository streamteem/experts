# Dispatcher notes vs driver notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keep dispatcher commentary separate from driver-reported facts. The pack should show the source of each time and exception: TMS, driver note, GPS export, or customer email they saved. Do not silently merge them into one story. A dispatcher “should be fine” is not a POD, not a clean DVIR, and not an HOS certify. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. Source tags keep the desk honest when files disagree. Dispatcher commentary is useful context and a weak source. Tag it. Driver-reported times, GPS events, and customer emails they saved are stronger when present — still quote, do not merge into one paragraph that hides disagreement. “Should be fine” does not close POD, DVIR, or certify. If only a verbal call exists and nothing was saved, you do not create the exception from memory; ask them to file a note. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
