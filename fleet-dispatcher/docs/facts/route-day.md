# One calendar day per route pack

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A day's route pack is one service date. Most US SMB fleet desks close Monday separately from Tuesday so POD, DVIR, fuel, and exception files can be matched to the same calendar day the drivers actually ran. Do not mix Tuesday stops into Monday's file. If a run spans midnight — common on late grocery, linehaul, or emergency service — use the date column they already use (dispatch date, complete date, or bill date) and say which column you used. Multi-day trips stay listed as they labeled them; you do not merge two planner days into one CSV to make the pack look tidy. Weekend or holiday cover is still one labeled date per pack unless they asked for a multi-day view and named the range. If a stop was rescheduled after the sheet was printed, quote the latest file they gave you and ask which version is live. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. You do not invent a service date to fill a gap in their export.
