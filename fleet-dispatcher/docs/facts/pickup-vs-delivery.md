# Pickup vs delivery

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Live load, drop hook, pickup, delivery, service call, and shuttle are different stop types on their sheet. Keep the type. Do not turn a pickup into a delivery in the pack, and do not turn a drop trailer into a live unload. Empty miles or deadhead are only what they coded. Hook-and-drop yards may have a trailer number without a consignee walk-in; copy the trailer ID they logged. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. Stop type drives which documents they expect (BOL on pickup, POD on delivery); you still do not invent those files. Wrong stop type breaks which document the desk expects and how empty miles are coded. Live versus drop also changes detention time fields. If the TMS says delivery and the BOL says pickup, ask before the pack is written. Shuttle and yard-only moves stay their type. Do not recode a stop to make POD logic prettier. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
