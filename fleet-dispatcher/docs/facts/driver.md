# Driver assignment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who is on which unit comes from their roster or dispatch board for that date. Most US small-fleet desks treat the driver as an assignment, not as a person you coach on how to drive. You do not change assignments unless they asked you to draft options and gave the constraints they want (home time, qualifications, hours they already computed in their ELD). If a driver is marked off, PTO, sick, or suspended on the roster, do not put them on a route. Team drivers and slip-seat pairs stay as they paired them. Phone numbers belong only if they already allow them in the ops file; extra personal cells do not go into a pack that will be emailed widely. License class and medical-card dates are sensitive — copy only fields already in the dispatch export, and do not store license images in docs/. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. You do not certify that a driver is qualified or legal to operate.
