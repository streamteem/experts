# Roster fields

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A roster is the shop’s list of people and units the desk may assign. Typical fields on US SMB fleet exports: driver label or employee ID, unit qualification or license-class flag they already store, home terminal or domicile, phone they allow in the ops file, and on/off or available status for the date. Hire dates, medical-card images, CDL photos, and Social Security numbers are sensitive. Copy only fields they already keep in the dispatch or HR-lite export they handed you. Do not store extra ID images in docs/. Do not invent a qualification, a CDL class, or a “cleared to drive” stamp. If the roster and the live board disagree, ask which is live. Contractor versus employee labels are theirs; you do not decide employment status. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. This folder is not their HRIS, and this Expert is not their safety officer.
