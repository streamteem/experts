# Site notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dock hours, gate instructions, appointment rules, PPE, and other site notes come from their customer or site file. Most US SMB desks keep a standing-notes column so drivers are not guessing at a new grocery DC or hospital dock. No secret codes, lockbox combinations, alarm passcodes, or gate PINs belong in docs/ or a shareable pack. If the only note is a code, write that an access note exists and is kept out of docs, and ask where they store it. Geofence names in a GPS export are labels they configured; you do not invent a fence or a lat/long. If site hours and today’s appointment disagree, quote both and ask. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. You do not publish personal cell numbers from a site contact unless they already use that number on the ops sheet.
