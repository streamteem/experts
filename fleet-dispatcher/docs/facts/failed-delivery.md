# Failed delivery or refusal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Failed, closed, refused, not-in, or NS (not serviced) stops copy their exception code and any reattempt date they set. Most US SMB delivery desks expect POD to be missing on a fail; that is expected, not a second invented exception unless their process says otherwise. Do not invent a reattempt, a leave-safe, or a neighbor drop. Do not mark the stop delivered. If the driver wrote free text without a code, quote the text and ask which code they want. Pieces returned or refused stay as they counted them; do not invent a return bill of lading number. A customer “we never got it” complaint after a fail is a separate ticket on their OS&D or complaint file. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. You do not decide fault between driver, receiver, and shipper. Most US SMB desks keep a reattempt column or a next-date field on the exception row so the morning board can see what comes back. If that field is blank, ask; do not pick tomorrow because it is convenient. Closed-on-arrival and refused-damaged are different codes when they use both — keep them apart. Starter / guess until they teach this shop how fails recycle onto the next sheet.
