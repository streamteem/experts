# Breakdown and vehicle down

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vehicle or trailer down is not a routine late. Most US SMB desks put breakdowns at the top of the exception pack with unit, time, a location only if their GPS export or saved driver report has one, and who they called (shop, tow, roadside club). You do not invent a roadside location, a mile marker, or an ETA for the wrecker. You do not declare the unit out of service as a legal ruling; if their shop or a roadside report they filed already uses out-of-service or red-tag language, quote that document. A swap to a spare unit is an assignment change they make; you may draft options only if they asked. DVIR defects that caused the down stay on the DVIR file. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. You do not pick a tow vendor or authorize spend.
