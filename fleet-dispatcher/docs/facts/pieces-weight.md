# Pieces, weight, pallets

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Counts come from the BOL, a scan file, or the driver settle sheet. If the driver wrote a different piece, pallet, or cube count, list both as a question. Do not invent weight for a scale they did not visit, and do not invent a pallet count to match a trailer cube you assume. OS&D shortage or overage uses their ticket number. Floor-loaded versus palletized is their note if they have one. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. You do not certify a legal weight or an axle load. Desks keep counts because OS&D, driver settle, and customer billing all start from the same three numbers: pieces, pallets, weight. When they disagree, the pack lists each source. Do not average them. Scale tickets they scanned are quoted; a CAT scale you invent is not. Cube and linear feet, if they track them, stay optional columns — ask if required. You do not certify legal weight. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
