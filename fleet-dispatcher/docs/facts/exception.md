# Exception

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An exception is a stop or unit event that did not go as planned: late, failed delivery, refusal, detention, breakdown, weather hold, or their own code. Flag it with the time and reason from their exception log, TMS export, or a driver note they saved — not from a phone call you reconstruct from memory. Do not invent a reason or a new code. Rank breakdown and safety defects above routine lates so the pack leads with what the shop must act on first. Missing POD when their process requires one is an exception question, not a silent omit. If GPS time and the route sheet disagree, list both as a question. OS&D and customer complaints stay on their claim ticket number; you do not admit liability. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. Exceptions are desk facts from files, not a finding that someone violated a regulation.
