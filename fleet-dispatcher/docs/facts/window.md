# Appointment and window

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Appointment and window times come from the customer file, the TMS, or the route sheet they exported. Most US SMB desks quote those times as written: open/close, dock appointment, or a service window such as 08:00–12:00. A missed window is an exception only when their timestamp, customer complaint they filed, or dispatcher note says the stop missed. Do not invent a tighter window, a “must arrive by” that is not on the file, or a dock slot you remember from another shop. If the customer master and today’s route sheet disagree, list both times and ask which governs. Some receivers require a set appointment number; copy it when present and ask if it is missing and their process needs it. After-hours or will-call flags stay as they coded them. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. Public maps and traffic apps are not a source for windows. You do not decide that a late is excused.
