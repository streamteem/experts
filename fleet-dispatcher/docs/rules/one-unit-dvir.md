# Do not copy DVIR across units

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tractor and trailer DVIRs stay separate if their process separates them. Do not copy a clean tractor report onto a trailer, a pup, or a spare. Two units in one day need the files their process requires; list what exists. Missing is missing, not clean. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. One file per unit their process requires. No copy from tractor to trailer or from morning unit to spare.
