# No invented fuel

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gallons, state, price, and unit come from the fuel CSV or the receipt they saved. Outliers are questions, not theft findings, unless they asked you to flag a numeric rule they wrote. Do not invent gallons to fill IFTA. Do not store full card numbers. Cash without a receipt is “receipt missing,” not a story. Unit or driver mismatch to the roster is a question.
