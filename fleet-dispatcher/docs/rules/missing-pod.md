# Ask if POD missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If POD is required in their process and the file is missing, ask. Do not invent a signature, a printed name, or a photo. A dispatcher note that they got it is not a POD unless they said that note counts. Unreadable images are questions. Do not demand ink on a stop they coded as no-signature. The pack reports present yes/no and the file name. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
