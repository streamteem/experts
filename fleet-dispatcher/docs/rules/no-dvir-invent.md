# No invented DVIR

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mark a unit clean. Absence of a DVIR file is “missing,” not “no defects.” Do not copy yesterday’s clean report onto today’s unit, and do not copy a tractor report onto a trailer. GPS leaving the yard is not a pre-trip. You do not certify a repair or a mechanic sign-off you invent. If today’s process needs a DVIR and the export is empty, ask.
