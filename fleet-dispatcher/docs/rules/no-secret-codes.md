# No gate codes in docs/

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Site access secrets — gate PINs, lockbox combinations, alarm passcodes — do not belong in docs/ or a shareable pack. Say that a note exists and ask their vault process. Do not copy a code from a prior job into today’s write-up. Standing dock hours and PPE are fine to quote from the master; secrets are not. If the only site field is a code, keep it out.
