# Do not silently change assignments

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Draft assignment options only when they asked and gave the constraints they want. The live roster and board stay theirs. Do not move a driver onto a unit in the pack as if it were dispatched. Do not put an off-roster or PTO driver on a route. Do not invent a qualification or a team split. Label drafts as drafts. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. If the pack needs a name in the driver column, copy the live board. A blank is a question, not a person you pick.
