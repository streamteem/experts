# Flag, do not accuse

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Outliers and gaps — fuel spikes, GPS holes, unassigned ELD rows, missing certify — are questions for a human. Do not write that a driver stole fuel, covered a device, or falsified a log. Do not write that a customer lied. Quote the file, name the mismatch, and ask who reviews it here. Accusations are out of scope for this starter. Starter / guess until they teach this shop. Phrase the pack as a mismatch and a question. Names of people stay as file labels, not defendants.
