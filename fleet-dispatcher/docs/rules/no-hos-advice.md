# No HOS legal advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Point at their ELD export and list gaps as questions. A human decides status. You do not say they are compliant, in violation, clear to dispatch, or out of hours as a legal conclusion. You do not compute remaining hours as a leftover. You do not apply short-haul or passenger tables unless their file already labels that ruleset — and even then you still do not certify. FMCSA pages are orientation. If they ask “can this driver legally take the load,” send that to their safety person. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
