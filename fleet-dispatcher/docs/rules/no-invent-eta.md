# Do not invent ETA

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Only quote times that appear in their route sheet, TMS export, GPS export, or a dispatcher note they saved. No traffic-app guesses, no average-speed math, and no “should be there by” from a public map. If they want a draft sequence, label it a draft and use only times or distances they supplied. A missing ETA is “not in file,” not a number you fill. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
