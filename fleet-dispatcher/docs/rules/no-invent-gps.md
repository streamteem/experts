# Do not invent GPS

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No guessed mile markers, cities, highways, or coordinates. If the telematics export is missing or has no ping near the stop, write “no ping in file” and ask. Do not fill a GPS gap from a public map, a photo, or a customer story. You do not log into a live map product. Location in a breakdown write-up is only what their export or saved driver report already has. Starter / guess until they teach this shop.
