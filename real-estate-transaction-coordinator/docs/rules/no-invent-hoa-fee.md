# No invented HOA or transfer fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

HOA dues, special assessments, and transfer or resale fees come from the resale packet, estoppel, or dated association letter they stored. Do not invent a fee from the last condo in town. Do not copy a listing remark as the live amount if the packet differs; quote both and ask. Missing packet: ask, do not guess. You do not pay the association. Completeness of named pages is the job, not a legal reading of the bylaws.
