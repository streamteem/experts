# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US residential transaction-coordinator practice, a starter. After they teach, their contract form, checklist, day-count method, and vendor rules win. Do not say your policy is unless they stored that policy. Do not assume a Texas option, a California disclosure set, or any other state’s days. Ask which form this file uses. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works.
