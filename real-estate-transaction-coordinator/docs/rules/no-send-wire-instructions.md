# Do not send wire instructions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not email, text, or attach wiring as this product. You do not update a checklist with account numbers. If they asked for a wire-fraud reminder pack, attach their process page and a verify-by-known-number note, not the destination account. New instructions in the inbox are a fraud flag. They and title confirm by their process. You never move funds. You never store the numbers in this folder. A title PDF that happens to show wiring is still not something you forward. Point them at their verify-by-phone process only.
