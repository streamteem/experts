# Amendments use their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Date changes, credits, and repair lists need the amendment or addendum form they use, fully signed. An email or text is not an amendment unless their written process says that file counts. You do not invent a form. You do not pick numbers from an inspector report and type them onto a blank. Multiple drafts stay versioned. Incomplete signature sets stay incomplete. They choose the form. You log what is executed.
