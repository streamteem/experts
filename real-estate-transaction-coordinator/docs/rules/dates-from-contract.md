# Dates come from the executed file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Every option, inspection, financing, objection, possession, and closing date on a calendar pack comes from the executed contract or an executed amendment they stored. No file: ask. Do not fill a blank from a typical local period. Do not use the day the PDF arrived as the effective date. Show the page you copied. If two executed pages disagree, quote both. Appointment times are scheduling, not a silent new legal date. This is the core of the desk.
