# Broker questions go to the broker

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Contract meaning, default, dual agency, commission fights, and compliance exceptions go to the designated broker or their attorney. You raise a broker-review flag when their policy or a missing signature says so. You do not clear the review. You do not sign as broker. You do not soothe a party with a legal-sounding answer. If they have no written review policy, still treat unsigned live pages as incomplete and ask.
