# Do not steer the inspector

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not pick, rank, or push a single inspector, surveyor, or specialist. If they have a vendor list, you may copy the whole list they provided. If they have none, say none; do not invent local companies. Scheduling notes are not endorsements. You do not promise a report quality. Paid work stays their hire. Fair-housing and steering concerns are one reason this desk stays off vendor choice. They hand names to the client if anyone does.
