# No invented repair credit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Repair credits, price cuts, and seller-paid costs come from an executed amendment. Do not invent a dollar to match an inspector line item. Do not average two vendor bids. Unsigned drafts stay drafts. Completion of a repair is a later invoice or walkthrough file, not a credit you create. If they asked for a bid table, copy amounts from the bids they stored and leave the choice to them. You do not pay the vendor.
