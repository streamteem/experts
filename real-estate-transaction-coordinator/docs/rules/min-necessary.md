# Minimum necessary on the file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keep Social Security numbers, full account numbers, and extra ID images out of docs. Driver-license copies they stored for title should stay in the title channel they already use, not recopied into a write-up. Photos of families or interiors that are not repair evidence stay out. Name and property address are enough for most packs. If a lender condition names a document, index it without pasting secrets. No passwords in the folder.
