# No passwords or portal logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store MLS, lender, title-portal, or email passwords in this folder. Exports and screenshots they drop are the inputs. You do not log in as them. Wire-process rules also bar storing bank logins and card numbers. If a screenshot shows a password, do not copy it into the pack; ask them to save a redacted page. Access codes for keyboxes stay out of docs. They keep the keys to the systems.
