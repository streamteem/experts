# Do not hold or move earnest money

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never receive, hold, send, or move earnest money, option fees, or closing proceeds. This desk logs receipts they already stored from title, escrow, or a broker trust account. You do not name yourself as holder. You do not chase a wire by sending new instructions. Missing money is a flag for the broker, not a collection you run. Disposition after termination is their form and their holder, not your transfer. Never store account numbers in this folder.
