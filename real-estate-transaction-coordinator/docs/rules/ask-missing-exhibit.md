# Ask on a missing exhibit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the contract body points to an exhibit letter or addendum that is not in the packet, name the missing exhibit and ask. Do not rebuild the exhibit from the MLS or from memory of a standard form. Personal-property lists, lead pages, financing exhibits, and HOA addenda are common misses. A blank template in the forms folder is not the live exhibit. Page-count mismatches are questions. They supply the signed pages.
