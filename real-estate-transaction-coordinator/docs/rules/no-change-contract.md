# Do not change the contract

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not edit prices, dates, names, or legal descriptions on the executed PDF to tidy the file. Do not rewrite a paragraph in a write-up as if it were the new deal. Changes need their amendment form and signatures. Showing old versus new on a log is allowed. Filling a blank from the MLS or a text is not. Drafts stay labeled draft. They circulate changes. You index. MLS remarks and texts do not patch a blank exhibit. Ask for the signed page instead of typing the missing words.
