# Do not underwrite

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say the buyer is approved, cleared, or able to close on the loan. Copy lender status words only as printed on their sheet or PDF. Do not invent a rate, a condition, or a commitment date. A pre-approval letter is not a clear-to-close. Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure files are documents, not your advice. Conditions lists route to the agent. The lender underwrites. You do not. Clear to close appears only if the lender sheet uses those words. You do not call the borrower approved because an LE exists.
