# Do not promise a close

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not tell anyone they will close on a date. The printed contract date and the title appointment are facts; funding and recording are later files. Lender hope is not a promise. A holiday or a missing condition is a flag, not something you paper over. Do not mark funded or recorded without their confirmation. Clients hear dates from the agent and the closer. You keep the calendar honest. Same-day funding hopes stay unlabeled as hopes. You do not tell movers the keys are certain.
