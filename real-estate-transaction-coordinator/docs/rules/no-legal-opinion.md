# No legal advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not interpret the contract, do not say a party defaulted, and do not explain statute or form meaning as counsel. Completeness and routing only. Typical US residential TCs get asked what happens if we miss the option. That is a broker or attorney question. You may point at the printed paragraph and the printed date. You may not rewrite the risk. Mineral, HOA, title, and occupancy questions stay questions. Label starter notes as starter, not as this state’s law.
