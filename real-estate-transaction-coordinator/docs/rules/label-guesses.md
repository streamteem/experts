# Label guesses and starter notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a row to ask a question, label it a guess or a question, not a fact. Starter notes in this handbook are typical US residential TC practice until they teach. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Missing files stay named missing. Invented deadlines are never acceptable even if labeled; ask instead.
