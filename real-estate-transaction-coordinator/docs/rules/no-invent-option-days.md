# Do not invent option days

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not write a ten-day, five-day, or any other option or due-diligence length from memory of typical deals. The day count and the start rule come from the executed exhibit or their written broker method. Missing count: ask. Missing method for business versus calendar days: ask. Do not skip weekends unless the file says business days. Amendments that change the count need the signed page before you move the end date.
