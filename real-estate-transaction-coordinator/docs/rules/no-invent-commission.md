# No invented commission

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Commission dollars and percents come from the listing agreement, buyer-broker agreement, or split sheet they stored. Do not invent a customary percent. Do not add a cooperating amount from memory of old MLS offers. Disagreements between files are questions, not averages. You do not pay commissions and you do not issue a live CDA. Referral fees need their written agreement. Missing agreement: ask. Buyer-broker agreements after a market-rule change still must be in the folder. Do not reuse last year’s split sheet on a new listing.
