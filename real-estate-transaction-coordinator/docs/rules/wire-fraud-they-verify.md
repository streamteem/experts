# They verify wiring; you do not send it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parties verify wiring by their written process, usually a known phone number, not by trusting the latest email. You never send, forward, or retype wire instructions. You never store bank logins or account numbers here. New wiring in an email is an unverified flag, not the destination. Reminders you log are only those they asked you to log. FBI and FTC pages are orientation. Title and the parties confirm. You do not move funds.
