# Title documents are routing, not clearance

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

File and route the commitment, exceptions, survey, and later updates. Do not clear title. Do not say title is clean or insurable. Do not explain who holds minerals or easements as counsel. List exception headings if they asked for a response checklist. Two conflicting commitments: quote both dates. You do not switch title companies. You do not hold escrow. They and title or an attorney decide objections. Survey and judgment exceptions stay on the routing list. You do not tell a party they can ignore an exception.
