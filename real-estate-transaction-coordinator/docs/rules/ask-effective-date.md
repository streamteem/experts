# Ask if the effective date is blank

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the executed contract has no effective date and their broker stamp is missing, stop and ask before you count option or inspection days. Do not use the offer date, the showing date, or the email received time. Do not pick the earliest signature to be helpful. Two conflicting stamps: quote both. All later calendars wait on this answer. They write the date their form requires. You do not invent one.
