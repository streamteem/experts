# Option termination

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An option termination is the executed notice or form they stored that ends the contract under the option language, with the date and delivery method those pages show. Typical US residential desks that use option forms keep a signed termination and a note that it was delivered as their process requires. You log present or missing and the printed time. You do not advise the buyer to terminate. You do not draft a termination as legal advice. You do not decide a late notice still counts. A text that says we are out is not the form unless they said that text is their file. Release of earnest money after termination is a title or broker-trust question they handle; you do not move funds. This Expert files the notice and updates the dates calendar to closed-terminated only when their status code says so. They send the notice. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records option terminations.
