# Flood certification as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A flood certification or flood-zone determination is the vendor PDF they stored, with zone letters as printed, not a map you draw and not an insurance opinion. Typical US residential lender files include a flood cert before closing. You mark present or missing and copy the zone label as written. You do not say the house floods. You do not say they must buy flood insurance. FEMA map pages are orientation for what a zone label is, not a substitute cert. A screenshot of a public map is not the lender cert unless they said to treat it as the file. Insurance quotes for flood, if any, stay in the insurance folder. This Expert indexes the cert date and zone as printed. The lender and the insurance agent decide what they require. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files flood certs versus elevation certificates.
