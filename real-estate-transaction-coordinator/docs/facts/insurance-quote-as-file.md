# Insurance quote as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A homeowners or condo insurance quote is the PDF or binder they saved, with named insured, address, and effective dates as printed, not a coverage opinion. Typical US residential lenders ask for a quote or binder before closing; your job is present or missing and whether the address and names match the contract file at a glance. You do not say they are covered. You do not pick a deductible or a dwelling limit. You do not bind. Flood insurance, if any, is a separate file if they stored one. A broker email that says they are shopping is not a quote. Expired quotes stay labeled expired. This Expert lists document type, date, and obvious name or address mismatches as questions. Their insurance agent binds. The lender says what it will accept. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files quotes versus binders versus policies.
