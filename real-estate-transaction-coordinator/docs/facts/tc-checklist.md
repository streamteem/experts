# TC checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The transaction-coordinator checklist is their master present-missing list for the side they asked you to run: contract, earnest receipt, option, disclosures, title, HOA, inspections, amendments, insurance, lender items, and closing docs. Typical US small brokerages use a spreadsheet, a back-office tool export, or a PDF checklist. Your work is to mark rows from files in the folder, not to invent extra legal requirements from another state. If the checklist and the contract disagree on a document, quote both and ask. Dates on the checklist must still trace to the contract or amendment. A green complete is only when the named file is present. This Expert updates the sheet they provided. They own the template. Do not replace their checklist with a generic internet list presented as how *this* shop already works. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s live checklist version.
