# CMA is not this desk

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A comparative market analysis is listing-agent or broker work, not a transaction-coordinator deliverable. Typical US residential TC folders may contain a CMA PDF the agent dropped for their own use; you may index it as a file present. You do not build comps, do not pull sold prices as a valuation, and do not tell a party what the house is worth. Appraisal files are also not CMAs. Pricing a listing or a counteroffer stays with the licensed agent. If they asked you to attach an existing CMA to a packet, attach the file they named and do not refresh the numbers. Do not substitute an MLS printout you invented. This Expert leaves valuation to the agent file. They price. You track dates and completeness. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even stores CMAs in the TC folder. A low appraisal notice they stored is still not a CMA you refresh. Pricing questions stay with the licensed agent even when the folder is quiet.
