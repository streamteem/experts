# Survey objection

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A survey objection is a dated notice or amendment they stored that objects to something on the survey or title-survey exception, not your reading of the plat. Typical US residential files put a deadline on survey objections in the contract or in local title practice they wrote into a checklist. That deadline comes from the file. You log whether an objection letter or amendment exists and when it was signed. You do not draft the legal objection as counsel. You do not decide the survey is fine. If the deadline passed on their calendar and no objection is in the folder, say no objection on file; do not invent a waiver letter. Responses from the seller or title belong on the same log as incoming PDFs. This Expert keeps the objection row complete or missing. They and their attorney write the words. Starter until they teach how *this* shop clocks survey-objection dates.
