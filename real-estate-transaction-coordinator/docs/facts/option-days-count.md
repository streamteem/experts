# Option days count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Option-day counting is arithmetic on the days printed on the executed option exhibit, starting from the effective date rule their form or broker file states, not a typical ten-day habit. Typical US residential forms say calendar days or business days; some start the day after effective date. That method comes from their state form PDF or a written broker note in the folder. If the method is not in the folder, ask. Do not count from memory. Do not skip weekends unless their file says business days. An amendment that adds days needs a new end-date row showing old versus new. Time-of-day endings stay on the row if printed. This Expert shows the printed day count, the method source, and the resulting end date only after that source exists. They confirm the math. You do not invent the method. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s day-count rule for the form they use.
