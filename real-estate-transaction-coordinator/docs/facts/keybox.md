# Keybox and access notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keybox or lockbox notes are listing-side access facts they wrote: box type, install or remove dates, and who still has a code, without putting live codes in this folder. Typical US residential listing files remind the TC to ask for removal after closing or termination. You calendar a removal task only if they asked. You never store gate codes, lockbox combinations, or alarm passwords in docs. Showing feedback is not this desk unless they dropped a feedback sheet to index. A missing key at walkthrough is a punch-list item they record. This Expert can list install and remove dates as they wrote them. They manage the box. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tracks boxes without writing secrets into the pack. Removal after funding waits on their recording or funding confirmation, not on the hoped-for closing day. Showing-service messages are not this desk unless they dropped a sheet to index.
