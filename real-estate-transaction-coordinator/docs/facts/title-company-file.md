# Title company file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The title-company file is the opener email, file number, closer name, commitment, and later settlement figures they stored for the named title or escrow company. Typical US residential contracts already name the company; your job is to match that name to the incoming PDFs. Do not switch title companies in a pack. Do not send a new opener as this product unless they taught that send step and the broker approved. Contact phone numbers come from their file or a known card, not from the latest email signature if their wire process says to distrust new numbers. You do not hold escrow. You do not disburse. Status words such as commitment issued or documents out come from their emails as labels. This Expert indexes file number and document list. They choose the company when the contract allows a choice. Starter until they teach how *this* shop opens and labels title files.
