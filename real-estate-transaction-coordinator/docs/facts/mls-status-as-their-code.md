# MLS status as their code

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

MLS status on this desk is a copy of the status code they exported or typed: active, pending, contingent, sold, withdrawn, or whatever their board uses. Typical US residential TC checklists remind the listing side to change status after an executed contract; you do not log into the MLS and you do not change the status. A screenshot they saved is the file. Do not invent a status from the fact that a contract PDF exists. Do not tell the public the home is pending. Coming-soon or hold codes stay as they wrote them. If their export and the listing agent’s note disagree, quote both and ask. No MLS password belongs in this folder. This Expert lists the code they provided and the date of the export. They or the listing agent update the MLS. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records status without giving you a login.
