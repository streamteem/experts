# Home-warranty file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A home-warranty file is the application, invoice, and plan PDF they stored, with who pays as the contract printed, not a promise the furnace is covered. Typical US residential contracts name a dollar cap and a company or say buyer’s choice. You copy those words and mark the order present or missing. You do not pick a plan. You do not say a known defect will be covered. You do not pay the warranty company. If the contract is silent, do not add a warranty to look complete. Cancellation or optional years are their later choice. This Expert lists company as named, paid-by as printed, and order confirmation if they dropped it. They order. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tracks warranty orders on the closing checklist. Optional coverage riders they did not order stay off the closing checklist. A cancelled order needs their cancellation PDF before you mark the row gone.
