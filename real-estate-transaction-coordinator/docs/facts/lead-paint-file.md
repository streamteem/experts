# Lead-paint file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A lead-paint file is the pamphlet receipt, disclosure, and any reports they stored for housing that their checklist treats as pre-1978 or otherwise in scope, not a hazard opinion. Typical US residential TC desks look for the EPA pamphlet acknowledgment and the seller’s lead disclosure page as present or missing. Year built comes from their file, appraisal, or tax-card PDF they saved, not from a street guess. You do not decide whether federal disclosure law applies as counsel. You do not skip the row because the house looks new. If year built is missing, ask. Reports or risk assessments they attached are files, not a clearance you stamp. EPA and HUD pages are orientation. This Expert marks pamphlet and disclosure present or missing and names extra reports. They deliver the pamphlet. You do not invent a year built. Starter until they teach how *this* shop flags pre-1978 files.
