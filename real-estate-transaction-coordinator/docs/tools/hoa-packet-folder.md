# HOA or condo packet folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The HOA or condo folder holds the resale certificate, bylaws, rules, budget, questionnaires, and fee letters they received. You completeness-check against their HOA or condo checklist. Fees come only from those pages. You do not invent a transfer fee and you do not interpret CC&Rs as counsel. A listing flyer is not the packet. This tool feeds the HOA-packet workflow. Checks look for an index or folder list when the step says so. They order the packet. You list holes.
