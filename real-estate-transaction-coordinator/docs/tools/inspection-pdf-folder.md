# Inspection PDF folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The inspection folder holds general, pest, sewer, and specialist reports they dropped, plus invoices if they track them. You list report type, date, and filename. You do not turn comments into a repair amendment. You do not steer the next inspector. Appointment notes are not contractual ends. This tool feeds the inspection-to-repair workflow before the amendment log is updated. Missing reports stay missing. They choose vendors. You index PDFs. Repair bids that arrived with a report stay labeled bids, not amendments, until a signed page exists.
