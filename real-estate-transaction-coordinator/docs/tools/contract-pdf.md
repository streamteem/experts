# Executed contract PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The executed purchase or sale PDF plus attached exhibits is the source for names, price, effective date, option language, closing date, possession, and exhibit letters. Typical US residential TC desks keep one live packet per side. You copy dates and flags as printed. You do not edit the PDF to tidy blanks. Unsigned or cut-off scans stay incomplete. This tool feeds dates-calendar, missing-exhibit, and amendment before-and-after packs. No portal login is stored here. If the live file is missing, ask; do not use a blank template.
