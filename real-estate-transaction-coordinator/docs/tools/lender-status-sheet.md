# Lender status sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The lender status sheet is their export or typed log: loan number if they stored it, status words as the lender wrote them, appraisal-ordered dates, and named outstanding conditions. You copy; you do not underwrite. You do not translate a friendly email into approved. No portal password. This tool supports dates-calendar financing rows and missing-docs packs that name lender items. Pre-approval versus commitment stays labeled as they labeled it. The lender owns the decision.
