# Dates calendar file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The dates calendar is their spreadsheet or export of contractual clocks: effective date, option end, inspection end, financing date, closing, and possession, each with a source page. Typical US small shops keep one tab per file. You write dates only from executed pages or their written day-count method. You do not invent typical days. Business versus calendar labels stay on the row. Appointment times sit in a separate column. Checks look for a calendar work file when the workflow says so. They watch the clock. You keep the sheet honest.
