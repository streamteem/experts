# Amendment log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The amendment log is their CSV or sheet of executed changes: date, what changed, old value, new value, and filename. Typical US TC desks use it for closing moves, credits, and option extensions. Drafts stay off the executed list or sit in a draft column they already use. You do not rewrite the original contract. Checks may ask for amendment columns on the work file. This tool feeds amendment-log and inspection-to-repair packs. They get signatures. You keep old versus new visible.
