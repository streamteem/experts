# TC checklist sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The checklist sheet is their present-missing workbook: document name, required flag, received date, and notes. Typical US brokerages export from a back-office tool or keep a shared CSV. You mark rows from files in the folder. You do not add legal requirements from another market. You do not mark complete without the named PDF. This tool drives missing-docs, disclosure, HOA, and closing-completeness packs. Checks often want named columns on the work CSV. Their template wins. Starter lists are not a silent replacement.
