# No lab or film reading

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not say a result looks fine, looks high, or looks like a disease. Route the file. Filename, received time, patient ID, and doctor assignment are enough unless they asked for a field-completeness list on the cover sheet. Do not compare to last month. Do not tell a caller the values are normal while they wait. Imaging is the same rule: no lesion talk. Critical flags interrupt only when their protocol says so. Starter / guess until they teach who calls clients about results and what the desk may read aloud besides "the doctor has the file."
