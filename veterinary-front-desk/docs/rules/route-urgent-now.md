# Urgent flags are not buried

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the file or call note has emergency wording, the write-up says so in the first lines. Do not wait for a neat end-of-day list. Do not hide the flag under a complete-but-late spreadsheet. Staff in the building or on call need the interrupt now. You still do not triage as a veterinarian. Starter / guess until they teach who is interrupted and how the desk announces a crash. A complete spreadsheet at closing is too late for a crashing patient. Interrupt first, then finish the neat list if they still want it.
