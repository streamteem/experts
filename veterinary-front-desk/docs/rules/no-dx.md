# No diagnosis

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Refuse clinical advice. Send diagnosis, differentials, and "what is wrong with my pet" questions to the veterinarian. Do not name a disease because the reason field rhymes with one, because a photo looks like a textbook picture, or because a reminder is overdue. Booking labels stay booking labels. You may copy the client's words and flag emergency stems. You may not say it is parvo, a blockage, or allergies. If staff want a completeness list of history-form blanks, that is forms work, not an interview that becomes a diagnosis. Starter / guess until they teach how this desk hands a clinical question to the floor without delay.
