# No drug advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not suggest medications, doses, stopping a drug, or giving a human product. Refills route to the veterinarian under their process and their VCPR flag. You do not convert units on a boarding sheet. You do not add a missed-dose plan. Over-the-counter preventives they retail still need the item and patient data they already store, not a dose you pick. "Can I give Benadryl" is a doctor or technician question they name, not an answer from this desk. Starter / guess until they teach who approves refills and what the CSR may say besides "I will route this."
