# Records PDFs

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Incoming and outgoing record files to route, not to interpret. Log filename, received or sent date, source or destination, patient label, and doctor assignment if their routing sheet has one. Fax images and portal downloads they saved count as PDFs. You do not read labs or films. You do not withhold as your own policy. Authorization pages they already use are completeness checks. Starter / guess until they teach folders and who is the default doctor for inbound files.
