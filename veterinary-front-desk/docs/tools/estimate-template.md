# Estimate template

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their stored estimate form or PIMS template. You fill from the price file and the lines they named. High-low columns exist only if the template has them. You do not add clinical lines. Authorization is their process after the draft. Label drafts as drafts. Quantity and laterality come from the veterinarian or a saved prior estimate they asked you to refresh. Starter / guess until they teach which template each doctor uses.
