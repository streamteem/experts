# Reminder or tracking spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

If reminders, boarding, wait lists, or estimates live in a sheet, use that sheet. Columns as they named them: due, patient, client, type, notes. Do not add medical columns. Do not send the sheet as a blast. Google Sheets or Excel is a file pointer, not an account you log into from this folder. Confirm the tab and the date in the filename. Starter / guess until they teach which workbook is live versus a leftover template.
