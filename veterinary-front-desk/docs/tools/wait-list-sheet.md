# Wait-list sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Short-notice or cancellation list they already keep: client, patient, type they asked for, preferred days, and sometimes doctor request. You sort the file. They call. You do not award a surgery slot when two names want the same opening. Emergency-wording rows are not wait-list puzzles. Species and type locks still apply. Starter / guess until they teach sort order and who contacts clients. Confirm the list date so yesterday's names are not today's. Species and type locks still apply when you sort. They call; you do not.
