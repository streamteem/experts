# Handling flag as they stored it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Caution, muzzle, fear-free, or "use back door" flags that they already stored can stay on the huddle list so handlers are ready. You do not add a behavior diagnosis. You do not decide the animal is safe or aggressive. You do not write a temperament essay from a lobby observation. A new bite or scratch report follows their incident process, not a flag you invent as a clinical label. Copy the stored words exactly. If two charts disagree, ask. Starter / guess until they teach which flags print on the day sheet, who may add one, and whether a flag expires after a calm visit. A new lobby growl is an incident or a question for staff, not a muzzle diagnosis you add to the chart. Copy stored flags exactly, including Fear Free or 'quiet room' if they use those words. Do not tell other clients why a pet used the side door. Ask who may retire a stale caution flag.
