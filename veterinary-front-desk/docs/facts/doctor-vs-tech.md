# Doctor versus technician column

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Who owns the slot matters for the floor in almost every US small-animal hospital. A technician appointment is a technician-owned visit for work their state and this clinic already allow: often vaccines under protocol, nail trims, suture removal, weight checks, or anal-gland expression as they book it. It is not a doctor diagnosis slot. Do not move a sick reason into a tech-only opening unless their rule file allows it and they asked you to draft that. Credentials stay as they labeled them: DVM, VMD, DVM relief, CVT, LVT, RVT, veterinary assistant. You do not decide that a technician may examine a vomiting dog. Doctor production reports later follow the same column; do not reallocate a tech invoice to a doctor. If two doctors share a day, keep the named column from the book. Starter / guess until they teach which reasons are tech-legal here and who may override a type.
