# Emergency flag to staff

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the appointment reason or call note says not breathing, toxin or bait, hit by car, collapse, seizure, bloated hard abdomen, bleeding that will not stop, or unable to urinate, as they typed those words, flag for staff now. Put those rows at the top of a day list or message log. You do not triage as a veterinarian, rank mild versus critical as a clinician, or give first-aid steps as treatment. You do not say the animal can wait at home. AVMA pet-owner emergency pages exist for owners; this desk still routes live animals to people in the building or to the after-hours number they post. Keep the client's words in the reason field. Do not replace them with a disease name. If the note is ambiguous but contains those emergency stems, still flag rather than bury the row in a routine sort. Starter / guess until they teach interrupt rules, who is on the floor, and whether a technician or doctor takes the first look.
