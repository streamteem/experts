# New-client forms completeness

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

New clients at most US clinics complete a registration form, how they heard about the hospital, patient identity, and often a privacy or photo-policy acknowledgment. Completeness is name, address, phones, email if they collect it, patient name and species, signatures, and dates from the PDF or tablet packet. You do not take a clinical history as the veterinarian: prior illnesses, current drugs, and allergy narratives belong on their history form for the doctor, not as advice you elicit. Authorization for another person to consent, if they use AVMA-style away-from-home forms, is a signature and named-agent check. Missing pages are a list, not a reason to invent answers. Driver license or photo ID, when they copy it, stays in their official file, not in this folder. Starter / guess until they teach which packet is current, whether e-sign counts, and who reviews incomplete forms before the first exam.
