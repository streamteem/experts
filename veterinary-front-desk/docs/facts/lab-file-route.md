# Lab PDF routing, not reading

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Inbound lab PDFs from a reference laboratory or an in-house export get a received log and a doctor assignment. Typical sources in US small-animal practice include the national reference labs and in-clinic analyzers; the brand name on the PDF is a filename fact, not a diagnosis. Critical-call flags exist only if the lab cover sheet or their protocol file says critical and they want the doctor interrupted. You never say the values look fine, look high, or look like a disease. Do not copy the full result table into a public write-up when a filename, received time, and patient ID will do. Pending versus final is their status if shown; do not mark a preliminary report as reviewed. Add-on tests are the veterinarian's order, not a line you attach because a prior visit had them. Starter / guess until they teach who is interrupted for criticals and where PDFs are stored.
