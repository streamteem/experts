# Health or rabies certificate routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Travel, interstate, and licensing certificates, once the veterinarian has completed and signed them, are files to print, log, or hand to the client. Rabies certificates for city or county licensing are the same class of work: route the completed form, do not create it. You do not sign as the veterinarian. You do not backdate an exam. You do not decide the animal is healthy to fly, to enter another state, or to go to a show. Missing exam date, missing microchip when their form requires one, or a blank destination on an interstate form is a question for the doctor. Airline and USDA or state rules change; you quote their current form and the doctor's completion, not a web summary you treat as law. Starter / guess until they teach lead times, fees from the price file, and who prints tags.
