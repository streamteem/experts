# Weight as their last number

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Last weight, when needed for an estimate line that their price file prices by weight band, comes from their patient record or the scale note they typed today. Units are as stored, pounds or kilograms; do not convert unless they asked and their file shows both. You do not estimate a weight from a breed average, a photo, or the client's guess on the phone. If weight is missing and the line needs it, ask. A stale weight with no date is a question, not a number you refresh from memory. Weight-check technician slots record a number they already entered; you list it, you do not decide the animal is overweight as a clinical finding. Anesthesia and drug calculations are not front-desk math. Keep the source of the number in the estimate notes when their template has a place for it. Starter / guess until they teach how old a weight may be before the floor wants a new scale reading.
