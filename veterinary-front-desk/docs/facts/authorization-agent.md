# Authorized agent on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Public AVMA client pages on care while the owner is away stress naming who may consent and any limits they wrote. Front-desk completeness is agent name, relationship, contacts, dates the authority covers, and the signed form they already use. Sitters, trainers, adult children, and barn managers appear often. You do not decide euthanasia authority, surgery consent, or financial responsibility beyond what the form states. If two agents conflict, or if a verbal okay arrives without their saved process, ask staff. A text that says "my sister can pick up" is not a completed authorization unless that is their written rule. Limits such as "no dentistry" or "call me before any estimate over X" are copied, not interpreted as medical orders. Starter / guess until they teach which form is current and whether a scanned card in the PIMS is enough.
