# Vaccine-due as their reminder export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Due dates on a reminder export are clinic protocol dates they stored in the PIMS: rabies, distemper-combination products, feline viral products, bordetella, leptospirosis, influenza, fecal, heartworm test, or whatever they configured. List them with patient, client, and the reminder name as exported. You do not decide a vaccine is due medically, which product to use, or whether a titer replaces a booster. Rabies certificate requests are a records-routing task from their certificate file after the veterinarian has completed the exam and the form, not a medical stamp you issue. Overdue versus due soon is their status word if the export has one; do not invent a grace period. Species mismatches on a reminder row are questions. Starter / guess until they teach which products they stock, which reminders they actually send, and how they handle declined vaccines as a stored status.
