# Pickup window as written

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Surgery and boarding pickups have times on the form or the book. List them on the huddle sheet so the floor and the client-facing desk agree. You do not promise an earlier discharge as a clinical recovery opinion. You do not hold an animal past the written window as your own judgment. Late pickups follow their boarding-fee or after-hours rule if they have one written; you do not invent a fee. Authorized pickup persons must match the form. If the doctor has not finished a surgery discharge, that is a staff flag, not a medical delay you explain. Starter / guess until they teach typical surgery pickup hours, boarding checkout time, and who calls when a time slips. Boarding late fees, if any, come from their policy file, not from a per-hour guess. Surgery pickup 'when the doctor calls' is a status, not a clock time you invent. Authorized pickup IDs must match the form before you list the animal as ready to leave.
