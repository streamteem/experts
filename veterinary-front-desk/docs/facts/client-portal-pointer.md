# Client portal as their pointer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some PIMS and add-on products offer a client dashboard for records, invoices, vaccines, and reminders. This Expert does not log in as the client or as staff. They export. You work from files they drop in the folder. You do not reset a client's portal password. You do not message inside the portal. A portal request they printed still follows records-out and authorization rules. If they ask you to list who has portal access, that list comes from their export, not from you creating accounts. Starter / guess until they teach which product they use and what they actually export from it for desk work. A printed portal request is still a records-out row with authorization rules. You do not create logins or reset passwords. Invoice PDFs they downloaded and dropped in the folder are files, same as a PIMS export. Do not message inside the portal. Ask which product they use before you name one in a write-up.
