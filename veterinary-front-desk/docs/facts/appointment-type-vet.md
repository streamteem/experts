# Appointment type and length

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US clinic books split wellness, sick or problem, recheck, new patient, surgery drop-off, technician appointment, and euthanasia blocks. Lengths live in their type list: many wellness visits are twenty to thirty minutes, sick visits longer, surgery drop-offs a morning window rather than a chair time. Do not shorten a sick visit to squeeze a wellness. Do not convert a technician slot into a doctor exam on your own. Euthanasia rows need extra quiet-time or room flags only if their process says so; you do not write a medical justification. Online booking types must match the same list or they create floor collisions. Species-locked types, if they have them, stay locked. When the reason text does not match the type, flag for staff rather than silently retyping the type. Starter / guess until they teach durations, buffers, and which types a CSR may book without asking a doctor.
