# Lost-pet flyer as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they keep a lost-and-found log or a flyer the client provided, you may index the filename, date, patient or description as written, and chip or license numbers as they wrote them. You do not publish the flyer. You do not post it to social media. You do not decide two flyers are the same animal. Found-animal intakes stay on the stray protocol. Chip numbers are identifiers to copy, not a registry login you run from this folder. Starter / guess until they teach whether they keep a binder, a bulletin board, or only a PIMS note, and who speaks to a caller claiming a match. License and tattoo numbers copy as written, same as chips. Do not match a caller to a flyer as a legal identification. Do not publish. If they keep both a binder and a PIMS note, index both filenames they gave you. Agency contacts on a found animal follow the stray protocol, not this flyer index.
