# Imaging files as filenames

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Radiograph, ultrasound, and other imaging files at the front desk are filenames and patient labels. Name the file and the patient. Do not describe lesions, fractures, or "looks normal." Specialist imaging CDs, cloud portals, or DICOM viewers they use are pointers; no password in this folder. Incoming images from another clinic follow the same records-in log: received date, source, patient, and which doctor should see them. You do not decide a retake is needed. You do not tell a client the films are clear. If their routing sheet sends orthopedic films to a surgeon and dental films to a dentist, copy that rule; do not invent a specialist. Thumbnails in a write-up are unnecessary when the filename is enough. Starter / guess until they teach storage folders and whether the client receives a disk or a portal link they already use.
