# Owner roster export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lot and owner labels for counts, notice lists they will send, and election tallies. Least extra contact data in the write-up. You do not decide legal ownership from a deed. Do not publish the full roster where phones and emails are not needed. Confirm the association name. One community per file unless they asked for a labeled multi-community index. Tenant rows stay labeled tenant if their export has them. Vote-weight and email columns only if the export has them and the task needs them; extra phones stay off public drafts.
