# Reserve study PDF

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The reserve-study PDF in their folder: report date, association name on the cover, and any printed number they asked you to copy into a board list. Not an engineering restamp, not a component inventory you rewrite, and not a transfer of cash. A missing or unexpectedly old report is a question. Funding-method language only if the report or their policy already states it. One association per index. Copy only numbers printed on the report they filed; do not interpolate a percent-funded figure from a chart you estimate.
