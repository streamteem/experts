# Vendor bid PDFs

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor proposal PDFs they received for one named project. Compare printed amounts, allowances, exclusions, and dates. Do not award, do not pick lowest responsible, and do not say a bid is too low as an engineer. Missing scope pages are questions. Keep add-alternates labeled. One project per comparison sheet unless they asked for an index with a project column on every row. A verbal quote in email is not a bid PDF until they save it; missing pages stay questions, and you still do not award.
