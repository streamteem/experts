# Vendor certificate-of-insurance folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Certificates of insurance they filed: expiration dates, named insured, additional-insured wording as printed, and coverage types their checklist listed. Not a coverage or adequacy opinion. Expired date, wrong community name, or a missing line they require is a flag for staff. Do not store producer logins. One vendor and one association per row unless they asked for a certificate-aging index with the community named. A binder or an expired certificate is a staff flag, not a finding that the vendor may start work, and you do not rewrite additional-insured language.
