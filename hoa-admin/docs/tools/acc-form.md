# Architectural application form

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The architectural application they actually use, plus required sketches, photos, and neighbor notes if their process requires them. Completeness of pages and fields against their form or adopted guidelines. Not an approval or denial by this Expert and not a committee stamp. Missing lot, unsigned owner line, or a missing exhibit is a question. One application per row in the completeness list. Do not apply another community’s color chart. A committee recommendation attached to the form is still not a stamp; list it as an exhibit present or missing.
