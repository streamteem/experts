# Common-area work-order export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open and closed common-area jobs from their software or workbook. Status, location, vendor, and dates come from their system, not your inspection or engineering judgment. Owner-versus-association responsibility is a quote from their matrix if they have one. Emergency flags use their vendor list, not a web search. One community per export unless they asked for a labeled portfolio. Closed jobs stay on the export if they asked for a period list; you do not reopen a ticket or inspect the site as an engineer.
