# Do not publish owner aging

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Aging, extra phones, emails, and full rosters stay in the work folder they use, not in a public minutes draft, website post, or widely forwarded packet. Follow their posting list. Approved minutes and budgets only if they asked and their list allows it. Use the least contact data needed in the write-up. Executive-session detail stays out of posted files. If they want a board packet exhibit, use lot and bucket totals they already export, not extra phones, and keep it off the website list.
