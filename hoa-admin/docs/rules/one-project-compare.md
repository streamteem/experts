# One project per bid comparison

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix roof and landscape numbers in one award table unless they asked for a vendor index with a project column on every row. Allowances and add-alternates stay labeled. Unsigned or different-scope bids are questions. One project per comparison write-up is the default. Do not fold a change order into a bid total unless their sheet already does. If two projects share a vendor, still keep two tables or a project column; do not combine roof and landscape into one award total.
