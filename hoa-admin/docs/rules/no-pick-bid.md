# Do not pick the winning bid

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Compare numbers, allowances, exclusions, and dates they provided on the bid PDFs for one project. Awarding a contract is a board motion. You do not pick a winner, a lowest responsible bidder, or a vendor to “avoid a lawsuit.” Too-low as an engineering judgment is out of scope. Missing scope pages and vendors off their insurance list are questions, not a ranking you announce. A comparison sheet is not an award recommendation; leave the winner cell blank unless they already recorded a board motion.
