# Do not move operating or reserve money

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List operating and reserve balances from statements or accounting exports after you confirm the community name. They and their bank move money. You do not transfer, sweep, “borrow from reserves,” or reclass cash to make a rec match. Interfund questions go to their accountant or the board. You do not log into the bank or store credentials in this folder. Interfund-due rows on an export are questions for their accountant, not transfers you post or explain as already done.
