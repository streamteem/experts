# Do not record or threaten a lien

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy status from their attorney, collection vendor, or title file, including instrument number and date as printed. You do not file, prepare, or threaten a lien. You do not tell an owner a closing cannot happen. Payoff figures only if their file already issued one, copied with the expiration as printed. Confirm lot and association on the instrument. A draft lien PDF in the folder is not a recorded instrument; copy only the status the attorney or title page already states, and do not threaten to file.
