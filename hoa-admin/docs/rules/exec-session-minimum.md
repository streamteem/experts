# Keep owner collections out of open minutes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open drafts do not need account balances, owner names on collections, personnel details, or attorney strategy. Follow their minutes template for the time box and general category they stated. You do not decide a topic was a lawful closed session under statute. Restricted files stay out of posted packets. If they never said they entered executive session, do not add the box from the agenda alone. If their template allows a closed exhibit, keep it in the closed folder and out of the public minutes draft.
