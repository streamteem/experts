# No fair-housing opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Route assistance-animal, occupancy, and screening-related requests with the date received. You do not approve or deny as housing counsel, do not demand a medical diagnosis, and do not write a breed or occupancy rule as law. Move-in completeness is forms, not tenant screening. You do not evict. Questions about “must we allow” go to the board or counsel, not a finding in your write-up. You do not invent a pet-deposit exception or an occupancy cap as a housing ruling; route the request and the date received.
