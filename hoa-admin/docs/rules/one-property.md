# One association per list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not mix two HOAs or condominiums in one aging, violation, work-order, bid, or meeting-file list. Management companies run portfolios; the association name belongs on every CSV and write-up. If they asked for a combined index, each row still names the community. Street-name overlap and master-plus-sub associations are questions, not a merge. Last week’s template numbers do not move to the next community. Confirm the community name on the export header before you append rows, even when the street names look the same.
