# Ask if the lot is unclear

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Two units on one photo, a missing lot number, a mailbox number that does not match the ledger, or a street that sits in two associations: ask. Do not guess the address from a map, a plat, or a neighbor’s email. Write the lot key their log and ledger already use once they confirm. Management-company portfolios make this check routine on every file. A unit number that exists in two buildings needs the building label they use; do not pick the closer door from a photo.
