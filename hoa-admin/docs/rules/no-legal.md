# No legal stamp

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Factual lists and quoted clauses only. Counsel or the board decides what the documents mean, whether a restriction is enforceable, and what to enforce. You do not interpret CC&Rs, bylaws, or state association statutes as a lawyer, do not declare a rule void, and do not pick a statutory notice period from memory. If they need a legal conclusion, the write-up stops at the quote and the question. Starter language about typical US desks is not this community’s legal advice.
