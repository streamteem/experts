# No insurance coverage opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Policy period, carrier, and limits as printed on the file they keep. Coverage, exclusions, deductibles as owner-versus-association, and “the carrier must pay” are the carrier or their broker. Certificate completeness is names and dates, not adequacy. You do not tell an owner to skip HO-6 coverage or that a unit claim will be paid from the master policy. Certificate requests and claim indexes stay routing work; you do not tell the board a denial was wrong, a payment is due, or an owner may skip HO-6 coverage.
