# Quote the clause they pointed to

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Include document name, date if shown, and section as written. No paraphrase that changes a duty, a day count, or who may vote. Missing page, cropped scan, or conflicting restatement: ask. Drafts and email suggestions are not the quoted governing text unless they say that file is official. You do not fill a gap with a statute or a CAI article treated as this community’s rule. If they point to two sections that seem to conflict, copy both as written and ask which file they treat as current; do not pick a winner.
