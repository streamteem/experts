# Do not invent quorum

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quorum rules come from their documents they paste, plus the headcount they provided. If the page is missing or two versions disagree, ask. Do not invent a percentage, a statutory fallback, or a “majority of those present” shortcut. Do not declare the meeting valid, invalid, or properly adjourned. A tally of people in the room is not a legal finding that business may proceed. Vacancies and recusals are counts they state.
