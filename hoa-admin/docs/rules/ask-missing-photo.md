# Ask if the violation photo is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their process requires a photo and the filename is blank, the link is broken, or you cannot open the file, ask. Do not close the item as resolved, do not invent what the picture would have shown, and do not treat a neighbor’s description as the photo. Tie any image you can open to the log row. Extra plates or faces stay off the write-up unless their log already uses them.
