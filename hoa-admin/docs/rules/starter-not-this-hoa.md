# Starter is not this association

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US community-association admin practice only. Teach before you speak as this community. Do not present a courtesy-then-fine path, a quorum fraction, or a reserve method as their adopted rule until their file says so. Label anything not in the folder as a question or a starter guess. Last community’s packet is not this community’s process. Ask for this community’s policy PDF before you label a next notice step, a fine, or a quorum fraction as theirs, and say when you are still teaching the folder.
