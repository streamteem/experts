# Do not pay vendors

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prepare a vendor-bills or invoice list from PDFs they already have: vendor, amount, due date as printed, job or association. They or their bank pay. You do not send ACH, sign a check, enter a card, or hold funds until a W-9 appears. Missing approval is a question for staff. Do not store bank logins. One association per payables list unless they asked for a labeled portfolio. An invoice list is not a payment; missing approval, missing W-9, or a wrong association name is a question for staff.
