# Do not certify an election

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Arithmetic from their roster and ballot files, labeled as a tally. Certification, inspector duties, and announcing a legal winner are human roles they name. You do not validate proxies as counsel or close nominations. Do not publish how a named lot voted. Missing signatures are questions. One meeting per tally unless they asked for a labeled multi-meeting index. Write “tally from files provided” on the product so no one can treat the count as an inspector’s certificate or a certified legal result.
