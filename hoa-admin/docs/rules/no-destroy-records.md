# Do not destroy records

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You may list what their retention schedule says for a document type. You do not shred, delete, or recycle boxes. Legal holds, open claims, and audits they named are theirs to apply and they override a routine destroy date on the schedule. A missing schedule is a question, not another community’s table you import. Confirm the association name on the schedule file. A director’s “we can toss that” is not a destroy order you carry out; list the schedule row and leave the box in place.
