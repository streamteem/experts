# Do not rule on proxy validity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Completeness yes or no against the form: signature, lot, assignee, meeting date if required, and other fields they print. Validity—revocation, fax, undated marks, power of attorney—is their inspector or counsel. You do not pick which of two proxies wins. Do not fill a blank assignee or change a directed-vote line. Duplicate lots on two proxies are a question, not a choice you make. An undated or incomplete proxy stays incomplete on the list; you do not cure it by calling the owner or filling a blank.
