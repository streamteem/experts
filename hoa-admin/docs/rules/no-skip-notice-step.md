# Do not skip their notice steps

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their adopted policy has courtesy, notice, hearing, then fine, list the current step and dates on the log. Do not jump to a hearing or a fine in the write-up as if it already happened. Do not mail the next letter as counsel. A missing policy file is a question, not a path you invent from another association. Returned-mail flags stay admin facts if they store them. A hearing date on a wish list is not a hearing that already occurred; keep the log status as they stored it.
