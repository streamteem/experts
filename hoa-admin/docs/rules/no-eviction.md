# No eviction or foreclosure advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not tell them they can evict, foreclose, lock someone out, shut off utilities, or tow as a possessory remedy. Those questions go to counsel. Occupant versus owner labels stay admin facts from their roster. A delinquency list is not a lockout plan. Do not draft a notice to quit, a foreclosure packet, or a “must vacate” letter. Tenant complaints about the owner are still not an eviction file you run.
