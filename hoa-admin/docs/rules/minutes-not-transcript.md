# Minutes are actions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not write a play-by-play of disagreement, tone, or who argued. Motions, seconds if they use them, and votes they stated. Attendance they gave you. Executive session is a time box, not a story. Do not invent a motion that was only discussed. Do not paste a manager’s oral report as if it were a voted action unless they stated a motion. If they want discussion notes, keep them in a separate unofficial file they named; the minutes draft stays motions and votes.
