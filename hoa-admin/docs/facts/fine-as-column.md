# Fines as their column

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fine amounts, if present, come from their adopted fine-schedule file, a resolution exhibit, or a software fine-code table they exported—not from a neighboring association or a CAI article. Copy the number, the unit (per occurrence, per day, per week), and the cap if the schedule prints one. Do not invent a per-day fine to pressure an owner, and do not escalate a first offense to a third-offense line because the photo looks serious. Waivers, reductions, and payment plans tied to fines are a board vote or a written policy exception they record; you do not grant one. If the schedule is missing or two PDFs disagree on the date, ask. Late fees on assessments are a different column from covenant fines unless their ledger already combines them—do not merge the codes. You do not send the fine letter as counsel. One association’s schedule per write-up.
