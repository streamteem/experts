# Lien status as their attorney or title file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their counsel, a collection vendor they already hired, or a title print says a lien is recorded or released, you may copy that status, the instrument or document number as printed, the recording date on the page, and the lot as the instrument names it. You do not record a lien, prepare a lien package, or threaten one. You do not tell an owner a sale cannot close or that a title company must collect. Release-tracking is presence of the recorded release they filed, not a title opinion you issue. Confirm the association and lot on the instrument. A payoff quote belongs only if their attorney or management file already issued one—you copy the number and expiration as printed. One lot per status row. A title email without an instrument number is a question, not a recorded lien, and a promised filing date from counsel is a status note only if they already wrote it.
