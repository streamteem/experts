# Proxy completeness as a form check

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Proxy completeness is a form check against the form they actually mailed or posted: signature or their accepted electronic mark, lot or unit, meeting date if the form requires it, and named assignee or “board” box as the form requires. Some US forms also need a date signed, a witness, or a secret-ballot envelope; copy those fields as present or missing. Validity fights—revocation, fax or email acceptance, power-of-attorney, undated marks—go to their inspector of election or counsel. You do not decide a faxed or emailed proxy is legal. Duplicate proxies for one lot are a question, not a pick you make. Do not fill in a blank assignee or change a directed-vote line. One proxy per row in the completeness list. Directed-vote lines and “assign to the board” boxes stay as the owner marked them; you do not convert a general proxy into a directed vote or the reverse.
