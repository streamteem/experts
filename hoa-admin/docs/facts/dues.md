# Dues aging from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Assessments and other charges age from their ledger or management-software export: lot or unit, owner label they store, current, 30, 60, 90+, and sometimes prepaid or credit balances. Typical US desks also carry late fees, collection costs, and special-assessment lines if those codes exist in the export. CAI financial-operations orientation expects a written collection policy the board adopted and uniform use of that policy; you list balances as exported and may label the next policy step only if that file is in the folder. You do not threaten collections, draft a demand as counsel, decide a lien, or report anyone to a credit bureau. Late-fee math only if their policy file states the number or formula and they asked you to apply it as arithmetic on the exported principal. Confirm the community name and period on the file. Prepaid lots are not delinquencies. One association per aging file.
