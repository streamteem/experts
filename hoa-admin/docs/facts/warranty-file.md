# Warranty or close-out file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Close-out folders typically hold manufacturer or contractor warranties, dated lien waivers they received, as-builts or shop drawings they were given, and sometimes a maintenance-manual PDF or attic-stock list. Presence is yes or no, plus issuer, date, and expiration if printed. Roof, paint, waterproofing, and equipment warranties often have different clocks; copy each as the page states. You do not certify workmanship, do not start a warranty claim as an engineer, and do not tell the board the vendor must return or replace. A missing waiver before a final payment they asked you to list is a flag for staff—you still do not pay. Confirm the association and project name on the documents. One project per close-out index. Warranty start dates that say “substantial completion” need the completion date they already filed; do not invent that date from the last invoice, and do not start a claim as the engineer.
