# Special assessment as their vote file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they have a board or membership vote file that levied a special assessment, you may list lots and amounts from the roster math they asked for, the purpose line as the resolution stated, due dates they printed, and whether the ledger already has a charge code. Typical US packets include the motion text, any member-meeting notice they filed, and a spreadsheet of per-lot shares if allocations are not equal—townhome and condo formulas sometimes differ from single-family equal shares. You do not recommend a special assessment, do not decide it was legally noticed, and do not invent an emergency exception because a roof is leaking. Payment-plan rows belong only if the same vote or a later logged policy created them. Refund or unused-balance questions go to their accountant. Confirm the association name on the resolution. One levy and one association per list.
