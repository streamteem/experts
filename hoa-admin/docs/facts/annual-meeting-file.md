# Annual membership meeting files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Annual membership meeting files typically include the notice they used, the proxy form they use, candidate bios or nominations they accepted, a roster or vote-weight list, and later the minutes and tally worksheets. Some US communities also file a mail-house proof or an electronic-voting vendor report. Count from files if asked and label the product a tally, not a certified result. You do not certify the election, close nominations as counsel, or decide a candidate was ineligible under the bylaws. Quorum math uses the sentence they pasted and the counts they provided. Floor nominations only if their file says they happened. Do not publish how a named lot voted. One annual meeting per pack. Notice proofs, proxy batches, and the roster used that night stay in the same pack; a missing notice file is a question, not a finding that the meeting was illegal.
