# Vendor W-9 or tax file presence

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Presence of a Form W-9, or a substitute their bookkeeper already accepts, in the vendor folder is a yes-or-no for their accounting desk, not a tax-opinion task for this Expert. Typical US association payables also care whether the file is dated, signed, and matches the vendor legal name on the contract and on the check or ACH record they already use. Management companies often keep one W-9 per vendor used across several communities; still confirm the payee name matches this association’s invoice. You do not prepare 1099s as a CPA, choose a box, decide backup withholding, or say a vendor is exempt. Do not store full taxpayer identification numbers in a write-up; last four only if they need a label to match two files. A missing W-9 is a question for staff before they pay, not a reason for you to hold or release funds. Foreign-vendor or disregarded-entity questions go to their accountant. One vendor per row.
