# Resolution log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Collections, enforcement, hearing, investment, insurance-deductible, and similar policies are often adopted by numbered resolution so later desks can find the live path. List title, number if they use one, date, and filename. Typical US board books keep a running resolution index in the same packet as the governing documents. You do not adopt a policy, do not backdate a resolution, and do not send a demand letter because a collections resolution exists. Missing a collections or enforcement resolution when staff is about to fine or age accounts is a question for the board, not a letter you author as a collector. Draft resolutions stay labeled draft. Confirm the association name. One association per log. If two resolutions on collections have different dates, ask which one staff must follow before you label a next step on an aging list, and do not blend the two texts into one path.
