# Meeting notice calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

How many days before a board meeting, special meeting, or annual membership meeting the agenda or notice must go out lives in their documents, an adopted policy, or a state-notice checklist they keep as a file. Typical US desks also track posting locations (mail room, website, doors) their rules name. List the target dates they asked you to compute only from that file and the meeting date they gave you. You do not certify statutory notice as counsel, do not pick a delivery method they did not use, and do not invent a holiday-adjustment rule. Email-versus-mail questions follow their roster flags and the clause they pointed to. A missing notice proof (affidavit, mail house report, portal log) is a question, not a finding that notice failed. One meeting per calendar row unless they asked for a year of dates from their own list.
