# Hearing or appeal calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When their adopted enforcement or collections policy includes a hearing or appeal, the admin file is typically hearing date and time, lot, violation or account ID, alleged category as the notice used, and whether notice was logged as sent (method and date if they store them). Some desks also log the hearing officer or board packet page and whether the owner confirmed attendance. You do not decide the fine, write findings of fact, or draft a decision letter as if you were the board. If their policy file is missing the hearing step, or the log jumps from courtesy to fine with no hearing row, ask; do not invent due-process language as legal advice or fill a statutory notice period from memory. Continuances and no-shows are status flags they record. Open-session minutes should not narrate the owner’s payment history unless their template already does. One association per calendar.
