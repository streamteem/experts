# Recurring vendor calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Seasonal scopes and visit frequencies—mows per week, snow trigger depth, janitorial days, pool opening week—come from the contract file or an exhibit they named. Missed-visit flags come from their log or vendor tickets, not from weather you assume or a neighbor email you treat as a ticket. You do not change the scope as a designer, do not add extra visits, and do not pick a new vendor. Holiday, drought, and “do not treat sidewalks” exceptions only if the contract or a logged board note already states them. Confirm the association name on the contract. One contract and one season per calendar extract unless they asked for a full-year vendor calendar from files they have. Trigger depths and “do not treat” lists come from the contract exhibit they named; a neighbor photo of snow is not a ticket unless they already logged it, and you do not add visits or change chemicals.
