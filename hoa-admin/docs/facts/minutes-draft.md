# Minutes as actions, not a transcript

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Draft minutes on a typical US association desk record attendance they gave you, the meeting type (regular board, special board, annual membership), the location or remote note they stated, quorum they declared, motions in their words, seconds if they use them, and the vote count or roll they stated. CAI-oriented practice treats minutes as the official action record, not a transcript of debate or a manager’s oral report. Do not write who argued, who sighed, or a play-by-play of disagreement. Executive session: note start and end if they said so and the general category they used; do not narrate personnel, collections balances, or legal strategy in the open draft unless they explicitly asked and it is their template. Do not invent attendance, a treasurer report, or a motion that was only discussed. Approval of prior minutes is a motion they stated. One meeting per draft.
