# Elections as a count from their roster

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they have a roster and returned-ballot or proxy files, you may count from those files and list missing signatures, missing lot numbers, or unmarked choices as questions. Typical US annual-meeting packets include a candidate list they accepted, a proxy form they use, and a ballot style (in-person, mailed, electronic vendor). Quorum and notice rules come from documents they paste or a state-notice checklist they keep as a file—not from a generic percentage you remember. You do not certify a legal election, decide a proxy is valid as counsel, close nominations, or announce a winner as the inspector of election unless they only asked for an arithmetic tally clearly labeled as such. Secret-ballot envelopes stay sealed in the write-up; you do not publish how a named owner voted. Write-in lines and cumulative-voting columns only if their form has them. One meeting and one association per tally.
