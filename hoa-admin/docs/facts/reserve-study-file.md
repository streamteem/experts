# Reserve study as a file they keep

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

CAI National Reserve Study Standards describe a budget-planning report: a component list, a condition or remaining-life view, fund status, and a funding plan. Typical US associations keep a PDF with a report date, preparer firm, and a level they already labeled (full, update, or desktop as the report says). Your job is whether the PDF is in the folder, the report date, the association name on the cover, and whether they asked you to copy a printed number (percent funded, recommended contribution) into a board list. You do not restamp the study as an engineer or reserve specialist, do not add or delete components, and do not move operating cash into reserves as a decision. A missing study or a date older than they expected is a question for the board, not a study you invent. Threshold funding versus full funding is their policy language if they have it—do not pick a method.
