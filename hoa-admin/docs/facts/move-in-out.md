# Move-in and move-out checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many associations use a move-in or move-out form, an elevator-pad or common-area deposit, a roster and fob update, and sometimes a moving-day reservation on the amenity calendar. Completeness is the form fields they require, deposit amount as their form states, proof of mover insurance if they require it, and whether the roster row was marked for update. High-rise condos often add elevator-pad hours; garden associations may only need a dumpster or parking note. You do not set a transfer fee, capital contribution, or move fee as law, and you do not screen tenants as fair-housing counsel or a credit decision-maker. Missing insurance for a mover, if their form requires it, is a question. Elevator-outage windows come from their calendar, not a building-code opinion. One move event per row. Refund of a pad deposit is their ledger task; you list whether the checklist says complete.
