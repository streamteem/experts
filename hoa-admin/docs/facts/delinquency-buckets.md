# Delinquency buckets from export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Delinquency buckets are the same aging export with a filter they asked for, for example sixty-plus or ninety-plus, listing lots and amounts as the software coded them. Typical US ledgers also show late fees, interest if they assess it, and collection-cost subcodes; keep those separate if the export does. Next step in their adopted collection policy is a label on the write-up, not a letter you author as a collector. FTC Fair Debt Collection Practices Act text is orientation for why this Expert does not play collector, threaten credit reporting, or imply a lawsuit. You do not call an owner. Prepaid and credit-balance lots do not belong in a delinquency filter. Confirm community name and as-of date. One association per list. Owner names on a delinquency list stay in the work folder they use for the board’s closed packet; they do not go on a website draft or an open-minutes exhibit.
