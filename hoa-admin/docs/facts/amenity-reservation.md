# Amenity reservation log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Clubhouse, cabana, pool-party, and court reservations typically record date and hours, lot, contact name as they store it, deposit if their form has one, guest count if required, and insurance if they require a renter’s or event certificate. Completeness is those fields against their reservation form and any blackout dates on their calendar. You do not waive a deposit, do not interpret noise or alcohol CC&Rs as a party permit, and do not approve a use the calendar already shows as closed or reserved. Overlapping bookings are a question. Refund rules come from their form, not from a courtesy you invent. Confirm the association name on the form. One amenity and one date per row. Insurance certificates for events, if their form requires them, are a presence check like vendor certificates: named insured and date as printed, not a coverage opinion, and you do not waive the requirement.
