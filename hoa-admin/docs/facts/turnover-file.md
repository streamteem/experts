# Developer turnover file presence

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they are in developer transition from declarant control to owner control, a checklist of governing documents, recorded plats, as-builts, warranties, reserve study, contract assignments, and bank-signature materials may exist. Presence is yes or no for each item they named, with the filename and date if shown. You do not litigate turnover, do not certify the building, and do not declare the developer still in control or already out. Missing warranties, missing as-builts, or missing bank cards are questions for the board and counsel. Confirm the association name on each file. One transition checklist per community. A punch-list from a walk they already held is an exhibit, not an inspection you perform. Bank cards, recorded plats, and warranty binders are presence checks on their transition list; you do not certify completeness to a buyer or a lender, and you do not declare the declarant period over.
