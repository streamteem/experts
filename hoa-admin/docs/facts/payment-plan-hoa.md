# Payment plan as a board-logged deal

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the board or their written collection policy already approved a payment plan, log dates, installment amounts, remaining balance as their form shows, and the lot from that minute, resolution, or signed form. You do not grant hardship terms, do not extend a plan, and do not waive late fees or fines. Inconsistent one-off deals across lots are a question for them, not a precedent you set for the next owner. A plan that is not in the minutes or policy file is a missing-file question. Broken-plan status comes from their ledger, such as a missed installment, not from your forecast that they will miss. You do not send a default letter as counsel. One lot per plan row. If the ledger shows a charge the plan form does not list, ask which file is current before you print a remaining balance, and do not add new charges to the plan yourself.
