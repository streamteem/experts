# Executive session as a time box

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open minutes on a typical US association desk often only record that the board entered and left executive session, the clock times if they use them, and the general category they stated—personnel, legal advice, collections, or contract negotiation. Detailed owner names, delinquency balances, employee issues, and attorney advice stay out of the open write-up and out of any packet that will be posted or emailed widely. Closed notes, if they keep a separate file, still do not get legal advice from this Expert, and you still do not decide outcomes. You do not decide whether a topic was a lawful closed-session subject under a state statute. If they did not say they entered executive session, do not add the box because the agenda had a collections line. Follow their minutes template for what belongs in the open PDF. One meeting per note.
