# Retention schedule as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they have a records-retention schedule—CAI-oriented tables are common starting points boards adapt—list what it says for the document type: keep until a date or event, then archive or destroy as their file states. Minutes, governing documents, and recorded instruments often have longer rows than routine invoices; copy the row they pointed to. You do not destroy records, do not shred, and do not invent a permanent-versus-seven-year rule as law. Legal holds, audits, and open claims they named override a routine destroy date—flag those for staff. A missing schedule is a question, not a reason to apply another community’s table. One document type per row in the list they asked for. Boxes labeled for destroy still need a staff decision and any hold they named; you list the schedule row and the box label, and you do not take records off site or delete scans.
