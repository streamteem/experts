# Parking or tow log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parking programs on typical US associations use stickers, hangtags, guest passes, assigned spaces, and sometimes a tow-warning or courtesy-sticker log. List what their sheet already uses: lot, credential number, vehicle description if they store it, and warning dates. You do not order a tow as a legal act, do not boot a car, and do not invent a fire-lane or city-code rule as an enforcement order. Plate numbers only if their sheet already uses them and the task needs them. Guest-pass overstay is a log fact, not a towing instruction you issue. Assigned-space disputes go to their policy file or the board. One community per log. Assigned-space maps and guest-pass rules live in their adopted parking resolution if they have one; quote that file when they point to it, and do not invent a towing contractor or a fire-lane finding.
