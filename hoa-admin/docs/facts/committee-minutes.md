# Committee notes as files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

CAI-oriented notes say committee meetings—architectural, landscape, social, finance, and similar—should keep minutes or notes and pass them to the board file. Index what they dropped: date, committee name, attendance they recorded, and recommendations as they wrote them. Architectural recommendations in those notes are still not your stamp and not a board approval. Landscape wish lists are not a change to the contract scope. You do not invent a quorum rule for a committee if their charter page is missing—ask. Do not turn a hallway conversation or a group text into minutes. Financial-committee notes that include owner aging stay off any public draft. One committee and one date per file row unless they asked for a packet index. Recommendations that need a board vote stay labeled as recommendations until the board minutes they give you record the motion; you do not promote a committee note to an approval.
