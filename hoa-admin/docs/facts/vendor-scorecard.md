# Vendor performance as dated notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Missed service dates, callbacks, and incomplete work from their work-order or contract log can be listed as dated questions for the board or manager. Typical fields: vendor, contract line, promised date as the file, actual date as the log, and a short note in their words. You do not terminate a contract, do not accuse fraud or theft, and do not withhold payment. Pattern language such as “always late” is not for the write-up unless each date is on the log. Snow and landscape missed-visit rows belong with the contract calendar they filed. One vendor and one contract per scorecard unless they asked for a seasonal vendor index with the community named. Each missed date needs a source row on their work-order or ticket log; do not summarize “poor performance” without those dates, and do not recommend termination or a withhold as if you were the board.
