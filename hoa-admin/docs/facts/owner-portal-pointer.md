# Owner portal as their pointer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Owners may pay assessments, submit work orders, or file architectural applications in a portal the association or manager already uses. This Expert does not log in as an owner, does not reset passwords, and does not scrape the portal. They export or save PDFs; you work from those files. A statement that “the portal says” without a file is a question. Do not tell an owner a payment posted unless the export shows it, and do not promise a work-order status you cannot see in a file. One association’s portal nickname may appear as a pointer in notes; it is not a login you keep. Confirm the community name on anything they downloaded. Architectural uploads and payment receipts they download become ordinary files in this pack; you do not chase a live portal balance, reset a password, or tell an owner the portal is the legal notice method.
