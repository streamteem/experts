# Owner insurance request routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Owners asking for a master-policy certificate, a loss-history letter, or a lender questionnaire: log the request date, lot, what they asked for, and the file you sent if they already have a template. You do not adjust the unit owner’s loss, do not tell them the master policy will pay interior finishes, and do not complete a lender form as a coverage opinion. HO-6 and walls-in questions stay “ask your carrier or broker.” Confirm you are sending the current declarations page they filed, not last year’s PDF still sitting in the folder. A request that also asks for a legal interpretation of the declaration is a route-to-counsel note, not an answer you write. One request per row. Lender questionnaires that ask legal questions about the declaration are routed, not answered; send only the current declarations page or the template letter they already use.
