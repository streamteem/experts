# What they post versus what stays in the folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some associations post approved minutes, adopted budgets, and governing documents on a website or owner portal; they typically do not post owner aging, full rosters with phones, or executive-session detail. Follow their written posting list or portal checklist, including whether drafts are never posted. You do not build a marketing site, do not invent an “all minutes must be public” statute as advice, and do not upload a draft they have not approved. Extra owner phone numbers and emails stay off a public draft. Confirm the association name on anything that will be posted. One posting batch per list. Draft minutes, owner aging, and extra phone lists stay off the public post even if a director asks informally; follow the written posting list, confirm the association name on every file that will go up, and do not build a marketing page from this pack.
