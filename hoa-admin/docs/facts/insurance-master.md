# Association master policy as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The association master policy PDF typically shows carrier, agency, policy number, period, and printed limits or deductibles for property and often general liability. Some packets add directors-and-officers, fidelity or crime, and umbrella lines as separate files; index each as present or missing. Certificate requests from owners, escrow companies, or lenders are a routing log: date asked, what they asked for, and the file you sent if they already have a template. You do not say a loss is covered or excluded, do not advise an owner to skip HO-6 or walls-in coverage, and do not interpret a condo deductible as the owner’s obligation as counsel. Confirm the association name and location on the declarations page. Expired period or a binder with no following policy is a flag for staff and their broker. One policy period per index row unless they asked for a coverage-file list.
