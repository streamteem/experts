# Pet registration as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Registration completeness if they use a pet form: lot, type, name or description they require, and tag or photo if the form asks. Some communities also store a vaccination page they already collected; presence yes or no, not a veterinary opinion. Assistance-animal or reasonable-accommodation requests are a routing file to the board or counsel with the date received; you do not decide fair-housing outcomes, do not demand a diagnosis, and do not write a breed ban as law. You do not invent a weight limit they did not adopt. Missing registration when their rules require one is a question on the violation or admin list, not a removal order. One lot per form row unless they asked for a community index. Vaccination pages they already collected are presence only; you do not decide a bite incident or a dangerous-dog outcome, and you route those files to the board or counsel as they ask.
