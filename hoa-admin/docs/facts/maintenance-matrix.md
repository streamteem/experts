# Maintenance responsibility matrix

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they have a chart of who repairs what—unit interior, limited common, common, owner versus association—quote the row they pointed to and name the file and date. Typical US condo matrices split doors, windows, plumbing stacks, and HVAC in ways that differ by community and by original declaration; townhome and single-family charts look different again. Do not borrow another association’s chart. Disputes about limited common elements, exclusive-use yards, or party walls go to the board or counsel. You do not rewrite the CC&Rs in a matrix you invent, and you do not assign cost because a vendor “usually” bills the HOA. A missing matrix is a question, not a reason to guess from a photo or a work-order history. One association per quote. Limited-common rows often differ by building phase; if the matrix has versions, quote the version they pointed to and ask when two charts disagree on the same component.
