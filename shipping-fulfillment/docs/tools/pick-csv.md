# Pick export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The pick CSV is their WMS or spreadsheet export of orders to pull: typically order, sku, qty, and sometimes wave, location, or lot. Typical US SMB desks dump this once per wave or per day. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a pick qty. Checks look for order, sku, and qty when the workflow asks. This tool starts pick-wave and short-ship packs. No WMS password belongs in the folder. Distinct from a receiving import.
