# BOL PDFs

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bills of lading they printed or the carrier portal saved sit here as PDFs. You check completeness against order, scale, and NMFC files. You do not invent weight or class on the form. You do not sign as carrier. Missing ship-to or payment term is an ask. This tool is the source for BOL packs. A packing slip in the same folder is a different document — keep names clear. No portal login is stored here.
