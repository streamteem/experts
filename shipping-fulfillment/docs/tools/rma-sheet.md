# RMA sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The RMA workbook lists authorization number, sku, qty, reason, and restock or scrap path. You list open and received-as-they-coded rows. You do not invent an RMA. Restock qty stays as they wrote it. This tool is the source for rma-returns packs. A vendor RMA on inbound freight is usually receiving; keep direction labeled if both appear. Credits are not payments you apply. Open versus received-as-they-coded stays in their status column. You do not close an RMA to tidy aging. Photo filenames they listed can ride along; you still do not invent damage.
