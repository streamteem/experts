# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the combined list you build for a pack: picks, exceptions, holds, or a day close. Typical US SMB desks live in Excel or a CSV. You do not invent keys (order, sku, qty, tracking) that the source files omitted. Checks often look for order, sku, and qty on work files. This tool is the usual write-up source after a specialized export is pulled. No password belongs in the workbook.
