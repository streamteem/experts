# Carrier / tracking export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The carrier or multi-carrier export holds tracking or PRO, status, exception codes, and sometimes billed weight or manifest id. Typical US SMB tools export end of day. You list exceptions as coded. You do not invent status. This tool feeds tracking-exception and end-of-day manifest workflows. No login is stored here. Rate dollars on the same dump are quotes or invoice-adjacent figures — still not a payment you make. Exception codes stay as the carrier printed them. You do not rewrite a code into a friendlier word that hides the fail. Use this dump for tracking-exception and EOD compares only from files they saved.
