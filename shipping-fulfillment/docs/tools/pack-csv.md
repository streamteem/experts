# Pack export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The pack CSV is what they recorded as packed: order, sku, packed qty, carton or pallet id, and weight if the scale already wrote it. You compare to pick and to the order. Shorts stay visible. You do not type a packed qty you were not given. Checks look for order, sku, and qty when asked. This tool feeds pack-exceptions and short-ship lists. Voided cartons should be flagged as they coded them, not reused.
