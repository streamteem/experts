# Manifest

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A manifest is the end-of-day or pickup list of live labels and freight bills handed to a driver or uploaded to a carrier: tracking or PRO, recipient, and weight if the file has it. Typical US SMB parcel desks close a manifest in the multi-carrier tool. You list what their manifest export shows. You do not add a voided label. You do not invent a pickup confirmation. A BOL tender list for LTL may be a separate PDF — keep the name they used. The manifest is not a payment and not AES. If a labeled shipment is missing from the manifest, that is an exception. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s close. This Expert compares labels to the manifest. They click close and they hand the driver the packet. A driver signature on a paper scan sheet they scanned is a file, not a signature you invent. Soft close versus hard close words stay as their tool exported them. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s pickup time. They hand the packet over.
