# Billed weight from the invoice file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Billed weight is what the carrier invoiced — actual, DIM, or a greater-of rule — copied from the freight or parcel invoice PDF or audit export they stored. Typical US SMB audits compare billed weight to scale weight. You list both when both files exist. You do not invent a billed weight to finish an audit. You do not pay the invoice. A large gap is an exception or a claim-file routing question, not a silent edit to the scale export. If only the invoice exists, say scale is missing rather than treating billed as the dock truth. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s freight-audit split (this desk versus AP). This Expert lists billed versus scale. They or AP dispute. You never send money. Ground versus air billed-weight rules stay as the invoice printed them. You do not average billed and scale to make a tidy number. Starter until they teach who at *this* shop audits parcel invoices. They or AP dispute.
