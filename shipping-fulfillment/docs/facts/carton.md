# Carton

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A carton is one outbound box or package with an id, a size from their carton-size file if they use one, and a scale weight when they weighed it. Typical US SMB ship desks assign carton numbers in the WMS or write them on the pack sheet. You copy size from their carton-size sheet or a measured-dimension file they stored — you do not guess length, width, or height to finish a DIM calc. You do not invent a carton count so the order looks complete. Overpack (a carton inside a carton or a bag in a box) stays as they recorded it. Voided cartons stay voided; do not reuse a voided label number without asking. Multi-carton orders need each carton listed. A carton is not a pallet. If the label PDF and the pack CSV disagree on carton count, quote both. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s box catalog. This Expert lists cartons from files. They choose the box on the bench.
