# Lot on the label or slip

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their item or customer file requires lot or batch on the label, packing slip, or ASN, the lot comes from the pick or pack export they already allocated. Typical US SMB food, chemical, and medical-adjacent shops print lot. You copy the lot. You do not invent a lot to finish an ASN. You do not pick a “nice” expiration. If the required lot is blank on the pack file, stop and ask. Multiple lots in one order stay on multiple rows. Inbound lot receiving is the wholesale desk; this desk only uses lots they allocated outbound. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s lot-on-ship rule. This Expert flags missing required lots. They scan the carton. FEFO or FIFO is their allocation rule if written; you do not pick the oldest lot from memory. Mixed lots need mixed rows. Expiration dates copy only if the pack export has them. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s lot-on-ship customers.
