# End-of-day close

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

End-of-day close is their checklist that the day’s live labels are manifested, voids are voided, shorts are listed, holds and will-calls are not on the street manifest, and scale exports are saved. Typical US SMB ship desks close once per calendar day per carrier account. You assemble the close pack from those files. You do not invent a close timestamp. You do not mark a day closed if the manifest file is missing — say missing. You do not pay daily pickup fees. Tomorrow’s wave is a different date. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s EOD list. This Expert lists open exceptions at close. They lock the day in the WMS or the carrier tool. Unclosed carrier accounts stay listed by account nickname they used. Tomorrow’s preprinted labels, if any, stay off today’s close. You do not invent a balanced postage total. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s EOD owner. They lock the day.
