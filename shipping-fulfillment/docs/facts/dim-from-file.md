# Dimensions from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Length, width, and height used for dimensional weight come from a carton-size file, a measured-pack export, or a WMS dimension field they stored — never from a glance or a catalog memory. Typical US SMB parcel desks get hit with DIM charges when the box is large and light. Carrier DIM pages (UPS, FedEx) are orientation for why the charge exists; they are not a license to invent inches. You do not compute a billed DIM weight unless their export already shows the carrier’s billed weight or they asked you to apply a divisor they wrote in a file. Missing dimensions: ask or leave blank. Do not fill 12x12x12 to finish a rate shop. If scale weight and DIM billed weight both appear on a carrier invoice file, list both; do not “fix” either. Starter until they teach how *this* shop measures boxes. This Expert copies inches from files. They measure on the bench.
