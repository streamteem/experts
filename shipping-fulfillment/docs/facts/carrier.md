# Carrier

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Carrier is the named parcel, LTL, or courier account on the order or the rate-shop export they saved — UPS, FedEx, USPS, an LTL name, or a local courier. Typical US SMB shops keep a default carrier per service level and overrides on the order. You copy the carrier as written. You do not switch carriers to “save money” from a remembered rate, and you do not pay a carrier invoice. SCAC or account nicknames stay as their file labels them. If the order says one carrier and the label PDF shows another, quote both and ask; do not void the label without asking. A third-party billing account is still their file, not a carrier you invent. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s carrier roster. Distinct from fleet dispatch: this desk ships sold goods, it does not build a driver’s stop list unless they taught that overlap. You never store a carrier-portal password in this folder.
