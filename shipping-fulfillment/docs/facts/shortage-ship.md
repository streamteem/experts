# Short ship

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A short ship is outbound qty packed or shipped less than the order qty they still wanted. Typical US SMB e-comm and wholesale desks short when the bin is empty or a line is damaged at pack. Say short. Do not write shipped-complete. Remaining qty stays on a backorder or a cancel question they decide — you do not zero it to tidy the ship board. Pick qty, pack qty, and order qty all belong on the short list when they differ. This is the outbound twin of a receiving short; do not mix the two packs. You do not invent a packed qty. You do not hide the hole in a gift note or a comment. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes partials versus cancels. This Expert lists shorts. They tell the customer or they release a backorder ship later.
