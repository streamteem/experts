# Restock after a return

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Restock is the decision and quantity that goes back to available inventory after an RMA inspect. Typical US SMB shops split good, damaged, and scrap on the RMA sheet. You copy those qtys from their file. You do not invent a restock qty to fix on-hand. You do not perform the cycle count. If restock is owned by wholesale receiving or inventory, say so and hand off the RMA row — this fulfillment desk does not hide a damaged return as good stock. Missing inspect notes: ask. You do not issue a customer credit as payment; that is sales or books. Starter until they teach who *this* shop lets restock. This Expert lists intended restock from the RMA sheet. They or the stockroom put it away. Damaged returns coded scrap stay off available. You do not move qty in the inventory export. If the RMA sheet and a receiving note disagree, quote both. Starter until they teach who owns putaway after a customer return.
