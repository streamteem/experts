# Proof of delivery

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

POD is the carrier’s delivered scan or signature file they saved: date, time, name if present, and exception notes. Typical US SMB shops pull POD after a shortage or damage dispute. You record yes/no and the filename. You do not invent a signature or a delivered time. “Delivered” on a verbal call with no file is not POD. A photo of a porch they stored is evidence they filed, not a stamp you create. Missing POD on a claim pack is an ask. Distinct from fleet dispatcher POD on their own trucks: this desk uses the for-hire carrier’s file. Starter until they teach how *this* shop stores POD. This Expert lists presence. They pursue the carrier portal. You never pay a claim as settlement. A delivered scan without a name is still POD if that is all their export has — say what is present. You do not type a signature to help a claim. Starter until they teach where *this* shop files POD PDFs. Distinct from a shop will-call handshake.
