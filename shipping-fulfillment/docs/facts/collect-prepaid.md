# Collect vs prepaid

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prepaid means the shipper’s account is billed. Collect means the consignee is billed. Typical US SMB BOLs and parcel labels print one of those words, or third-party. You copy the term from the order, the customer routing guide, or the BOL form they already filled. You do not switch prepaid to collect to dodge a freight invoice, and you do not pay either side. If the term is blank, ask — do not assume prepaid. Freight-collect that the consignee refuses is an exception, not a payment you make. “Prepay and add” on a sales invoice is a sales/AP question, not a carrier payment you run. Distinct from purchasing freight-in: inbound collect versus prepaid is the receiving or buyer desk. Starter until they teach how *this* shop prints freight terms. This Expert copies the word. They settle arguments with the customer or carrier.
