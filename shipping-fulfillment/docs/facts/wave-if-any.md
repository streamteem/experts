# Wave (if they use one)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A wave is a batch of orders released together for pick — by carrier cutoff, by zone, by single-line versus multi-line, or by a planner’s named group. Many small US shops never wave; they pick order-by-order. If they have no wave file, say so and do not invent wave numbers. Typical wave fields when the export has them: wave id, release time, picker or zone, order count, and cutoff. Do not merge Tuesday’s leftover orders into Monday’s wave to make the board look closed. Do not invent a cutoff. If two exports disagree on which orders sit in a wave, quote both. A wave is not a carrier manifest and not a payment. You assemble the wave list from their release file. They decide what to release and who picks. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop waves at all. Distinct from wholesale receiving: inbound putaway batches are a different desk. You do not pay freight to “make the wave go.”
