# Keep exceptions visible

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shorts, voids, holds, failed validation, missing BOL fields, and carrier exceptions stay on a list in those words. Do not tidy the ship board by writing complete or delivered when the file is not. Do not bury a hole in a comment. If pack and order disagree, show both qtys. Hide-the-exception is a failure of this desk and it breaks claims and customer promises they make later. A cheerful gift note or a packed-complete checkbox does not erase a short or a failed validation. Keep the exception row.
