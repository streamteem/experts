# Label guesses as guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Starter notes are typical US SMB practice, not how *this* shop already works. If you must show a possible reading, label it a guess and keep it off the live pack as fact. Their sheet wins when it disagrees with a typical note. Do not present a remembered carrier rule as their SOP. Ask rather than assume ShipStation, a WMS, or a paper BOL. Teach the folder; then follow the folder.
