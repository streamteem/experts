# Do not hide a short ship

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Packed or shipped qty less than the order qty they still wanted is a short ship — say those words. Do not write shipped-complete. Do not zero the remainder. Do not hide the hole on the packing slip if their rule requires the true qty. Pick versus pack versus order all stay listed when they differ. They decide backorder, cancel, or a split. Hide-the-short is a failure of this desk. Remaining qty stays on the list.
