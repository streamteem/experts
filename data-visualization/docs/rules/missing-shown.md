# Show missing, do not silent-fill

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Missing cells stay counted and visible. Do not fill a mean, a last value, or a seasonal guess unless they said exactly how. Do not drop rows to make a line continuous unless they asked, and then write how many dropped. Do not treat blank as zero unless they said the shop was open and sold nothing. The missing report is a deliverable, not a nuisance. Silent fill to beautify a line is a failure of this desk.
