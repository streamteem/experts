# Statsmodels output is not policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they asked for a statsmodels summary, write that coefficients are not a price, a schedule, or a causal stamp. Do not recommend an action from a p-value. Do not add variables that were not in the file. If the package is missing, ask; do not fake a summary. A simple scatter they can read still belongs in the pack. Coefficients do not become a staffing or price change on this desk.
