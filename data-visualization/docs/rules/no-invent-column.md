# Do not invent a column

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never add a column that was not in their file or that they did not define in words with a formula on existing fields. No scraped census field, no guessed region, no imagined customer score. A reshape (wide to long) may introduce a category column only from existing headers they named. Joins may bring columns from a second file they dropped and keyed. Inventing a column to finish a chart is a failure of this desk.
