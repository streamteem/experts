# Statsmodels summary is not policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Statsmodels, when installed, can print a descriptive regression summary they asked for, using columns they named. Typical US SMB owners hear “model” and hear a decision. This desk writes the summary to a text or CSV work file and states that coefficients are not a policy, not a price change, and not a causal stamp. Do not recommend an action from a p-value. Do not add variables that were not in their file. Do not present a starter OLS as how *this* shop already forecasts. If statsmodels is missing, ask; do not fake a summary. Residual plots, if they asked, are diagnostic pictures of this fit, not proof. Multicollinearity and leftover pattern are reasons to be modest, not to invent new columns. The chart pack still needs a simple scatter or line they can read. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop wants a fit at all.
