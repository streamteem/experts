# Axis truncation that lies

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Truncating a y-axis on a bar or area of counts or dollars so the axis does not start at zero can make a small change look huge. Typical US SMB slideware does this to “show impact.” This desk does not truncate a count or dollar bar axis to lie. If they ask to zoom a line chart of a large level, say that the axis does not start at zero and give an untruncated companion or the min/max in the caption. Do not use a broken axis as a trick. Dual axis is a different caution. Clipping outliers is a different ask and still needs the list. Matplotlib will happily start at 90 percent of max if you set ylim — do not. Done is a PNG whose scale a skeptical owner can trust. Starter until they teach when *this* shop accepts a zoomed line versus a zero-baseline bar.
