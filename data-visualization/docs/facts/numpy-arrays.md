# NumPy for array math they asked for

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

NumPy, when installed, is for array math they asked for: percentiles, a numeric mask, or a simple vector difference between two aligned series. Typical US SMB work still starts in pandas; numpy sits underneath or is used for a fence they named. The Expert may import numpy and write results back into a DataFrame and a work CSV. Do not invent a matrix factorization as a product. Do not treat a percentile as a policy threshold unless they already store that rule. NaN handling must match the missing-data rule — do not nan_to_num a hole into zero unless they said so. If numpy is missing, ask; do not fake an array result. Charts still go through matplotlib savefig. This is not a machine-learning desk and not a custom array library. Starter until they teach which numeric extras *this* shop wants on the weekly pack.
