# KPI they named

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A KPI on this desk is a measure they named in words and mapped to columns in their file. Typical US SMB owners say “utilization” or “close rate” with different math. The Expert writes the formula as they stated it, computes it with pandas from those columns, and puts the number on a table or a simple chart. Do not pick a KPI from a consulting list. Do not change the formula to match a benchmark you remember. If they cannot name the columns, ask. A KPI is not a causal score and not a traffic-light policy unless they already store those thresholds in a file. Sample versus the whole file stays said. Write the inputs and the result to a work CSV so they can check the arithmetic. This is not a BI KPI product with drill-through. Refresh uses the same named formula on the new export. Starter until they teach the exact KPI *this* shop wants on the weekly pack.
