# Dashboard means a folder of files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A “dashboard” on this desk is a folder of named PNG, PDF, and CSV files plus a write-up, not a hosted BI site, not a live CRM tile, and not an email campaign product. Typical US SMB owners say dashboard when they want last week’s pictures in one place. The Expert writes those files under out/ and work/ and lists them in markdown. Do not stand up Tableau, Power BI, Looker, or a marketing site as this product. Do not keep a live query against a passworded warehouse. Refresh is a new export they drop, then a new dated PNG. Small multiples can live in one PNG. The folder is done when the question, source file, and what-was-not-claimed are readable. No email blast of the folder as a marketing product. Starter until they teach how *this* shop wants the weekly folder named.
