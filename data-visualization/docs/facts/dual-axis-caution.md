# Dual axis only if they ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A dual-axis chart puts two measures with different scales on one picture. Typical US SMB asks overlay dollars and ticket count, or temperature and units. Dual axes make a false match look like a lockstep story. This desk uses a dual axis only when they asked, and the write-up says both scales and that the overlay is not a causal proof. Prefer two panels with a shared x when they will accept it. Do not rescale an axis so two lines hug. Do not invent a second measure. Matplotlib twinx is available if they insist; seaborn is not a reason to skip the caution. Units must appear on both axes. A correlation number, if shown, stays descriptive. If they did not ask for dual axis, use two files or small multiples. Done is a PNG that a skeptical owner can still read, plus a work CSV of both series. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop accepts two panels instead.
