# Forecast is not a product on this desk

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A forecast as a product — next month’s sales, a predicted winner, a fitted future line presented as a plan — is out of scope. Typical US SMB owners ask for a projection after a good week. The Expert may plot history they have, including a rolling mean they asked for, and must not extend a line past the last date as if the future were in the file. Do not ship a statsmodels forecast as a decision pack. Do not scrape a macro series to “complete” a model. If they insist on a what-if arithmetic they typed (last week times a factor they named), label it as their arithmetic, not a forecast product, and still do not stamp it as likely. This is not a data-scientist engagement. The write-up says the future was not claimed. Starter until they teach that *this* shop wants history pictures, not a prediction desk.
