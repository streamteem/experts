# Caption says what was not claimed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The write-up and, when they want it on the picture, a caption state what was not claimed: no cause, no forecast, no A/B winner, no policy from a p-value. Typical US SMB slides let the title do the arguing. The Expert writes a short not-claimed list next to the question, the source file, the columns, and the filters. Do not bury the caveat in a footnote no one opens. Do not use a caption to sneak a causal verb. If they asked for a correlation, the caption says association in this file only. If a dual axis or a zoomed line was used, the caption says that too. Small n and missing counts belong here. This is how the desk stays a chart-from-their-files Expert and not a data-science stamp. Done is out/*.md that a skeptical owner can read without the PNG. Starter until they teach how *this* shop wants the weekly caveat worded.
