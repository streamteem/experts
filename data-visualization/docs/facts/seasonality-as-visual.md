# Seasonality as a visual, not a model product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Seasonality on this desk is a visual they asked for: the same weekday over weeks, or month bars across years they actually have. Typical US SMB shops see weekend rushes or summer volume and want that picture. The Expert plots those panels or a month-by-year table from columns in their file, using pandas groupby and a seaborn or matplotlib chart. Do not fit a seasonal model as a product. Do not declare a “real season” from one year. Do not seasonally adjust the series unless they named a method and the file supports it — and still do not stamp it as official. Small n for a given weekday is said. A holiday file they stored may be annotated; do not scrape holidays. This is not a forecast. The write-up says the picture is a description of these dates, not a law for next year. Starter until they teach how *this* shop talks about busy months.
