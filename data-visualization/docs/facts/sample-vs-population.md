# Sample versus the whole file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Most files on this desk are an export, a date slice, or a location slice — a sample of what the shop could have recorded, not a census of the world. Typical US SMB owners say “everyone” when they mean “everyone in this CSV.” The Expert writes whether the chart is all rows in the file after filters, a random sample they asked for, or a truncated export. Do not call the result the market or the city. Do not weight up to a population you do not have. If they sampled rows to make a scatter readable, say the sample size and the rule. If the export dropped voided tickets, say that if their file notes it. This is not a survey-statistics product. pandas sample is only used when they asked. The write-up keeps the wording modest. Starter until they teach what *this* export includes and excludes.
