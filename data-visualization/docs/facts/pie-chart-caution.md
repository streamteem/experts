# Pie chart caution

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pie or donut shows shares of a whole they named. Typical US SMB decks use pies for everything. Pies are hard to compare, especially with many slices or similar shares. This desk prefers a bar or a table unless they asked for a pie and the slice count is small. If they insist, label slices with the percent and the base, use a colorblind palette, and do not explode a slice for drama. Do not use a pie for time. Do not use a pie when parts do not add to the named whole. matplotlib pie is available; seaborn is not a reason to skip the caution. Write the share table to work/ so the picture can be ignored. This is not a branding product. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop still wants a pie for the board pack.
