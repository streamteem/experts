# SciPy summary as description

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

SciPy stats, when installed, can add a descriptive summary they asked for: skew, a percentile, or a correlation they named. Typical US SMB desks do not need a test; they need a number next to a picture. The Expert may import scipy.stats in this workspace and write the numbers to a work CSV, then say in ordinary English what was computed. A p-value, if they insisted on a test, is not a business decision and not a go/no-go. Do not pick a test to chase significance. Do not treat normality as a verdict that the shop is healthy. If scipy is missing, ask and install, or stay with pandas describe — do not invent a SciPy output file. This is not a data-scientist engagement. Sample size stays in the write-up. Charts remain the PNG they asked for. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop even wants these extras.
