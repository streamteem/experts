# Draws not COGS

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because owner cash coded as cost of goods or as wages wrecks gross profit and can look like a payroll or tax position you are not allowed to take. Typical practice codes owner personal cash to their equity draw or distribution account after they confirm. Do not code owner draws as cost of goods, materials, or wages. Confirm; then save their rule as their teaching, not as a starter. If they insist on an expense account, label it their instruction, not a tax position, and still do not call it wages unless their payroll file shows wages. Exception: S-corporation owner pay that already appears on a payroll register is wages from that file, not a draw you invented. Ask when a line could be either. Never recode a draw to "help" the profit and loss.
