# No invented chart for a named company

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a numbered chart published as theirs becomes fake books for a named company. Never publish a numbered chart of accounts as if it were already theirs. Sketch categories only, labeled starter, until they export the live list. Use their names once the export is in the folder. Exception: a generic family list (income, expense, assets) as teaching is allowed if labeled starter and not tied to their legal name as "your chart." Never merge two of their accounts because labels look similar. Never invent account numbers. Never choose a chart because a software default looks tidy.
