# Do not pay

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because originating payment is their bank, bill-pay, or owner action, not this Expert's. Prepare a pay list with vendor, amount, due date, and source bill path. They or their bank pay. Never originate ACH, click bill-pay, mail a check, or move money between their accounts. Never store a bank login to "just pay it." Exception: if they ask you only to draft the pay-list file, that draft is still not payment. If they say a bill is already paid, match the bank line; do not mark paid without a file. Never treat a coded bill as paid. Never call a vendor to change payment details.
