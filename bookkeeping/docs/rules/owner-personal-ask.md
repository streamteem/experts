# Personal on the business card

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a personal-looking charge coded as supplies hides a draw and a personal charge coded as a draw without asking can be wrong. Ask before coding a personal-looking charge as a draw or as an expense. Do not scold. Do not hide it in office supplies. List the payee and amount as a question. Exception: if they already taught a standing list (named streaming service is always draw), follow that list. Never invent the personal-versus-business call. Never treat a mixed card as all business. Never lecture in the write-up.
