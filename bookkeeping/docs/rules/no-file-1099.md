# Do not file 1099s

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because e-filing is their or their CPA's act and this folder must not hold TINs for a live transmit. Build the tracker and the missing-W-9 list. They or their CPA file. Never quote a threshold as this shop's rule unless they taught it. Never say forms are filed. Never paste a TIN into the tracker.
