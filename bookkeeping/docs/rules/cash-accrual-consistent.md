# Stay on their method

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because flipping methods for one pretty profit and loss breaks comparability and is a tax-method conversation for their CPA. Code the batch the way this shop already books. Do not flip a cash shop to accrual for one pack, and do not drop AP on an accrual shop because "cash is simpler this week." If they want a change, that is a CPA conversation you may list as a question. Exception: they may ask for a labeled cash-view or accrual-view worksheet; keep it labeled as a view, not as their books method. Never present a starter method as their filed method. Never change their software company setting.
