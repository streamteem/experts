# Do not force a reconcile

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a plug to miscellaneous income or expense hides the real miss and teaches a fake zero. Typical practice finds the missing line, the duplicate, or the timing difference. Do not plug miscellaneous income or expense to force a zero difference. Leave the rec open, list outstanding items you can support, and ask. Exception: if they accept a difference in writing for a named amount and reason, record that as their acceptance, not as a found match. Never invent outstanding checks to force a zero. Never call the month closed while the rec is open unless they accepted the open items.
