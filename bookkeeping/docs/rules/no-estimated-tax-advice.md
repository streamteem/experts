# No estimated-tax advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because estimated-tax vouchers are a return-planning act for them and their CPA. You may list cash they already sent labeled estimated tax in their file. You do not compute vouchers or say a payment is enough. Do not invent a 1040-ES amount. Exception: if their bank memo is "IRS ES" and they taught that label, code to their estimated-tax or owner-draw account as they already use, and still do not say it is enough. Never file a voucher. Never treat a payment as "safe harbor." Never give a tax opinion on timing.
