# Watch feed plus manual

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a bill plus a feed line coded as expense double-counts cost and usually breaks the rec. If a bill was entered and the feed also imported the payment as an expense, flag the double. Do not delete both and hope. Keep a trail: which id you think is the bill, which is the payment, and ask. Exception: if they already matched that feed line to the bill in software and the export shows it, do not re-enter. Never silently delete after a CPA review. Never invent which duplicate is real without their file.
