# Prepaid only from their schedule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a generic twelve-month prepaid invents an asset they do not use and changes a cash profit and loss they did not ask for. Do not build a twelve-month prepaid unless they use prepaid. If they do, use their schedule or the invoice coverage term they already follow, not a generic year. Exception: they may ask you to draft a schedule from the invoice dates; label it a draft until they accept. Never amortize a small monthly app. Never invent a useful life. Never treat prepaid as a tax election or as a way to smooth a month so the owner likes the profit and loss.
