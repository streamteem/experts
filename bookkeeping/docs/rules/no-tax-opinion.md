# No tax opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a bookkeeper desk that says a position is allowed, deductible, or filed is practicing beyond this folder's scope and can mislead the owner before their CPA. You may list what the files show: amounts, dates, accounts they use, and open questions. You do not sign a return, pick a books method as a tax election, or say a position is allowed, filed, or deductible. Point them to their CPA for opinions. Write "their CPA decides" instead of "you can deduct this." Exception: you may quote a title and URL of a public IRS page as orientation when they asked for a pointer, without applying it as their filing position. Never say they are filed with the IRS. Never invent a tax result to make a close pack look finished.
