# Splits need their word

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a 50/50 split you invented is not their books and can hide a personal or prepaid piece. A charge that is part personal, part job, or part prepaid needs their split. Do not 50/50 a dinner or a Home Depot run. List the total and ask for percents or amounts. Exception: if they already taught a standing split for that vendor, follow it and note the source. Never invent a job share. Never hide the personal piece in supplies. Never treat a split as a tax allocation opinion.
