# No invented sales-tax rate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because rates, nexus, and filing frequency are state and local facts this desk must not invent. Ask; do not invent a rate, filing frequency, or nexus. Do not say a sales-tax return is filed. List collected amounts from their invoices or POS file only as they marked. Exception: if their invoice line already shows a tax amount, you may list that amount as collected on that file without inventing the rate that produced it. Never pick a jurisdiction because a customer address looks local. Never remake a return. Streamlined Sales Tax pages are orientation, not this shop's rate.
