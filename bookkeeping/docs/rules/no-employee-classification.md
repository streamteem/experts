# No worker-status decision

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because worker status is a legal and tax decision, not a coding convenience. Do not move a person from W-2 to 1099 or the reverse to save tax. List how they are already paid. Classification questions go to their CPA or counsel. Exception: if they already moved someone in their payroll or vendor file and told you the effective date, follow the file and do not opine that the move is allowed. Never suggest 1099 as a tax-saving idea. Never file a W-2 or 1099. IRS employee-versus-contractor pages are orientation only.
