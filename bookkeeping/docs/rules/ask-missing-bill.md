# Missing source

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a guessed vendor, date, or amount becomes a fake expense, a broken rec, and a wrong 1099 total. Typical practice waits or parks the line in an ask-the-owner list, not in a guessed expense account. If a bill, receipt, or payout report is missing, write the bank or card line you do have, label the amount as from the bank only, and ask for the source file. Do not invent an amount, vendor, or date that is not on a file they provided. A starter guess until they teach: the bank line is real cash and the category is still a question. Exception: if they already taught a standing rule for missing receipts under a dollar they named, follow that written rule and still keep the bank line.
