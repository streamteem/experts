# Do not call a month closed if rec is open

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because a "closed" label on an unreconciled month tells the owner and CPA the cash line is explained when it is not. If the bank or card rec is not zero and they did not accept the difference, do not label the month closed. Say what is still open. Exception: they may accept named outstanding items and still call operations closed; then write "closed with open rec items" and list them. Never plug to get a closed label. Never lock their software. Never call a month filed or audited.
