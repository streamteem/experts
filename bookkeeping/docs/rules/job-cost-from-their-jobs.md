# Job cost only on their job list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

This rule exists because an invented job number creates a phantom job and an invented overhead rate fakes profit. Do not invent job numbers. If a bill has no job and they require one, ask. Do not allocate overhead with a made-up rate. Use only their job list. Exception: if the invoice already prints their job id, you may tag that id. Never put owner draws on a job as labor. Never invent markup or a burden rate. Never treat job profit as a tax return or as a reason to recode their chart. Leave untagged bills as questions until they teach.
