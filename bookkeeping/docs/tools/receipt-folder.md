# Receipt photos and PDFs

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The receipt folder is photos, email PDFs, or a capture-app export used to match support to card and bank lines. Typical file shape is image or PDF files plus an index CSV with date, amount, vendor as printed, and filename. Match to card or bank lines for the period they named. Ask their drop folder name. Done looks like a match sheet with status (matched, unmatched receipt, unmatched line) and an exceptions write-up. Do not invent payees. Do not keep card PAN images; if a photo shows a full number, do not copy it into docs/. Do not treat a chat recap as the receipt when they said a file would arrive.
