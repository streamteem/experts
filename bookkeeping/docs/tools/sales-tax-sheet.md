# Sales tax working sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The sales-tax working sheet lists invoices or POS rows and tax collected as they marked. Typical file shape is date, amount, tax_collected, and taxable-or-not as their file already coded. No invented rates, nexus, or filing frequency. Filing is theirs or their CPA. Done looks like work/tax-collected.csv and a questions write-up that asks who files and what their nexus list is. Never say a return is filed. Never pick a jurisdiction from a ship-to address. Streamlined Sales Tax orientation does not give this shop a rate.
