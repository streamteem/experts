# Processor payout report

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The processor payout report ties Square, Stripe, Clover, PayPal, or similar to the bank. Typical file shape is payout date, gross, refunds, fees, net, and payout id. Match net to the bank line. Ask which processor. No dashboard login here. Done looks like a match of net to bank plus a labeled question on gross-versus-net if they have not taught it. Do not invent fee percents. Do not double-count POS sales plus the payout. Do not treat a 1099-K as the monthly books. Do not store processor passwords.
