# Bank CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The bank CSV tool is the downloaded activity for one account and date range used to flag unclear lines and to feed the rec. Typical file shape is date, description, and amount (signed) or separate debit and credit columns; some banks add running balance or check number. Read the header. Ask operating versus payroll versus savings and the exact date range. Pointer only: no bank login. Done looks like work/bank-raw.csv (or their named file) plus a flagged sheet with a question column for unclear payees, transfers, and owner deposits. Do not invent payees from processor memos. Do not copy full account numbers into markdown. A 90-day download is not a month unless they named that range.
