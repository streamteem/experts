# Credit card export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The credit card export is one card's statement period used to code charges and to rec the card liability. Typical file shape is a statement PDF plus a CSV with date, payee, and amount; ending balance and ending date are required for a rec. Ask every card, including store cards. No full card numbers in files you write. Done looks like work/card-raw.csv and a coded sheet with category and a question flag for personal or missing receipts, plus a note that the bank payment is a transfer, not a second expense. Do not log into the issuer. Do not pay the card. Do not treat the card as a checking account.
