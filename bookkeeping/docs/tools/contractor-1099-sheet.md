# 1099 tracker sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The 1099 tracker is a year-to-date vendor payment list for the year they name, plus W-9 present or missing. Typical file shape is a CSV with payee label, YTD amount, w9_on_file, and optional form-they-think column. No TINs. They or their CPA file. Done looks like work/vendor-ytd.csv, work/w9-status.csv, and out/1099-tracker.md with gaps labeled. Do not e-file. Do not decide who is exempt. Do not quote a threshold as this shop's rule unless they taught it. Do not put Social Security numbers or EINs in the sheet.
