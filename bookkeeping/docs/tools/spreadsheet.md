# Spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The spreadsheet tool is the working book for bills, registers, journals, close inputs, and question lists this desk produces without logging into their software. Typical export or file shape is a CSV they already use or a new CSV with their column names: at least date, amount, and account or category, plus vendor or memo when they have it. Google Sheets or Excel is only a label for how they handed you the file. Ask which file is source of truth versus a working copy. No passwords, no sheet-link logins stored here. Done looks like a CSV under work/ that the column checks can see, plus a short write-up that labels guesses and questions. Column names are theirs. Do not invent a numbered chart as columns. Do not paste TINs or full account numbers into a sheet you author.
