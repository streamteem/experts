# AP aging export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The AP aging export is open vendor bills used to build a pay list and close notes. Typical file shape is vendor, bill number, bill date, due date, and open amount. Cash shops may instead have a folder of unpaid PDFs; list those as a pay list, labeled not from the register if true. Done looks like an open-bills CSV or pay list the owner can act on, with questions for missing due dates. They pay. You do not originate payment. Do not enter every PO as AP. Do not invent terms. Do not mark a bill paid without a bank or card file.
