# AR aging export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The AR aging export is the open-invoice list used to age balances and list credits and odd rows. Typical file shape is customer, invoice number, invoice date, due date, open amount, and sometimes bucket. Ask invoice-date versus due-date aging and their bucket labels. Done looks like work/ar-aged.csv with customer, amount, and bucket, plus a questions write-up. You do not email customers unless they said that is the job and the file is ready. You do not write off. You do not apply a payment because the amount matches. No customer card numbers on the sheet you author.
