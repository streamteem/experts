# Owner contributions

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk an owner contribution is owner money into the operating account, or a transfer from a personal account they already treat as capital. It is equity, not sales. It exists so a large deposit is not booked as a miracle month of revenue. Next files are the bank-questions pack and the equity rollforward. Typical fields are date, amount, from-account if they said, and the equity account they use (Owner contribution, Capital, Member contribution). Files are the bank line and their word. Cadence is whenever a large unexplained deposit appears. The bookkeeper asks; the owner labels. Shops vary. Loan proceeds, customer deposits, and sales can look similar on a bank memo. Ask before coding a large unexplained deposit as income. A starter guess until they teach: large odd deposits are questions labeled contribution versus sales versus loan, not silent income. Common mistakes are coding "online transfer" as sales, mixing contributions with customer retainers, and treating a reimbursed personal expense as income. Never invent their entity's capital account name, never treat a contribution as taxable income as an opinion, never move money, and never assume a starter "must be capital" story is theirs.
