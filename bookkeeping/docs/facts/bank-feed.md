# Bank export

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk the bank export is the downloaded activity for one account and date range: a CSV, OFX, or QBO file, or a feed that already landed in their software. It exists so coding, the rec, and the "what is this line" list start from the bank's record, not from memory. Next files are the bank-flagged questions sheet and the reconcile pack. Typical fields are date, description or memo, amount (signed or separate debit and credit columns), and sometimes a running balance or check number. Column names vary by bank. Files are the download they provide. Cadence is weekly or at statement end. The bookkeeper reviews; they download. This Expert does not log in. Shops vary: operating, savings, payroll, and tax-reserve accounts are different files. Feeds can duplicate a manual entry. Ask which account and which date range before you treat a file as complete. A starter guess until they teach: one operating checking CSV for the period they named; still ask about other accounts. Common mistakes are treating "last 90 days" as a month, mixing two accounts in one sheet, and coding a transfer as income because the memo is vague. Never invent a payee from a processor name, never store a bank login or full account number, never originate a transfer, and never assume the feed replaced the statement for the rec.
