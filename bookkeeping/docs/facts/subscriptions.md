# Subscriptions and SaaS

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk software and SaaS charges hit the card or bank every month, or once a year. Monthly software usually hits expense. Annual prepay may be prepaid or expense-when-paid. Personal or family subscriptions on the business card are an ask (draw versus business). The list exists so recurring charges are coded the same way each month. Next files are the card-coding pack and their software list if they keep one. Typical fields are vendor, amount, cadence, account name, and personal-or-business question if unclear. Files are the card CSV and invoices in email. Cadence is monthly coding. The bookkeeper codes; the owner cancels or confirms personal. Shops vary. Ask this shop's software list and prepaid habit. A starter guess until they teach: monthly to software expense on their chart; annual small amounts expense-when-paid unless they already prepaid; personal-looking names as questions. Common mistakes are inventing a prepaid schedule for a small app, hiding a personal streaming charge in office, and creating a new account per app. Never invent prepaid, never log into the app, never cancel or buy software, and never treat a subscription as deductible as a tax opinion.
