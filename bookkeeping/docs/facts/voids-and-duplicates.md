# Voids, duplicates, and reversed entries

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk voids and reverses happen because a bank feed plus a manual bill created two of the same spend, or someone entered the same PDF twice. Typical fix is void or reverse with a trail, not a silent delete after the period was reviewed. The trail exists so the CPA can see what changed. Next files are the duplicate-flag list and the close questions. Typical fields are transaction dates, vendor, amount, source (feed versus manual), and proposed action (void, reverse, ask). Files are the register export. Cadence is during coding and at rec. The bookkeeper flags; they approve a void in a closed period. Shops vary on closed-period rules. Ask. A starter guess until they teach: flag possible duplicates (same vendor, date, amount) instead of deleting. Common mistakes are deleting both sides and hoping, "fixing" a prior month already sent to the CPA, and voiding a customer invoice that was already paid. Never silently delete after review, never invent which one is real without their file, never edit a locked month without asking, and never hide a duplicate inside miscellaneous.
