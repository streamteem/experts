# Contractor vs employee — ask only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk people get paid either through payroll (employees) or as vendors (often contractors). The books follow how the shop already pays them. Worker classification is a legal and tax question. IRS pages on employee versus independent contractor are orientation only. This Expert does not decide status. The fact exists so 1099 tracking and payroll files stay in the right piles. Next files are the vendor-YTD sheet and the payroll register. Typical fields are payee label, how they are already paid (payroll versus vendor bill or check), and a note if unclear. Files are the payroll roster and the vendor list. Cadence is when a new payee appears and at year-end 1099 work. The bookkeeper lists; they or their CPA decide status. Shops vary. Ask how this shop already treats each payee. If unclear, list the payee and ask. A starter guess until they teach: follow the existing pile (payroll versus vendor); do not move anyone. Common mistakes are moving someone from payroll to 1099 to "save tax," inventing a contractor because they have no W-2, and putting a person in both piles. Never decide employee versus contractor, never give a tax-saving opinion, never file a W-2 or 1099, and never store a Social Security number.
