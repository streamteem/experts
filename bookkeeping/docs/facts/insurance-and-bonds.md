# Insurance and license bonds

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk insurance and license bonds are general liability, workers compensation, auto, and surety or license bonds. Auditable policies may true-up at year end. Premiums may be prepaid or financed monthly. The pile exists so a workers-comp deposit is not wages and so a finance-company draft is not a mystery expense. Next files are the invoice, the finance agreement if any, and prepaid if they use it. Typical fields are policy type, vendor or finance company, amount, coverage dates if on the bill, and account name. Files are binder or invoice PDFs and bank drafts. Cadence is when billed and at audit true-up. The bookkeeper codes; they bind coverage. Shops vary on finance companies (loan-like monthly payments) and on prepaid. Ask their account names. A starter guess until they teach: code to their insurance account; flag a workers-comp deposit as a question, not wages. Common mistakes are expensing a workers-comp deposit as wages, ignoring a year-end audit bill, and inventing a prepaid schedule. Never invent coverage, never file a claim, never treat premium as deductible as an opinion, and never pay the finance company.
