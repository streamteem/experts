# W-9 on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk a W-9 is the vendor's taxpayer-ID form collected before paying a new service vendor. This desk only notes path-to-file and missing or present. IRS requester instructions exist for electronic W-9s and TIN matching. Those are orientation. The note exists so the 1099 tracker is honest and so missing forms are a question list, not a surprise in January. Next files are the W-9 status CSV and the tracker pack. Typical fields are payee label, received date if they store it, file path or "missing," and who asked the vendor. Files are PDFs in a folder they name, not in docs/ as recopied IDs. Cadence is at vendor setup and at year-end gap review. The bookkeeper flags; they collect. Shops vary on where PDFs live (email, a drive, software attachments). Ask. Backup withholding is a tax decision. Flag missing W-9; do not withhold. A starter guess until they teach: present or missing only; no IDs in the write-up. Common mistakes are transcribing Social Security numbers or EINs into chats and CSVs, storing a signed W-9 in a public write-up, and deciding backup withholding. Never copy SSN or EIN into docs/ or work CSVs, never withhold, never file, and never treat a missing W-9 as "they are exempt."
