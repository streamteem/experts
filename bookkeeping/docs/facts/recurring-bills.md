# Recurring and memorized bills

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk recurring or memorized bills are rent, software, loan payments, and retainers that look the same each period. Shops store them as memorized transactions, standing bills, or a checklist. They exist so monthly overhead is not re-invented from a blank page and so a missed rent shows up when the bank line has no match. Next files are the vendor-bills pack, the card or bank coding, and the close "expected but missing" list. Typical fields are vendor, usual amount, usual account, cadence (monthly on the first, weekly), and last date entered. Files are their memorized-bill export, a rent ledger, or a simple recurring list they keep. Cadence is each period before close. The bookkeeper proposes the list from last month; they confirm changes. Shops vary. Some enter a true AP bill each month; cash shops only code the bank. Amounts change (rent bumps, annual price changes). Ask this shop's list and whether a missing expected bill is a question. A starter guess until they teach: copy last period's recurring names as questions, not as posted amounts. Common mistakes are posting this month from last month's amount without looking at the new invoice, entering a memorized bill and also coding the bank as expense, and inventing a lease. Never invent rent or a lease term, never pay, never treat a starter recurring list as their contract, and never store a landlord portal login.
