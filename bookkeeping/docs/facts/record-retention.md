# How long files are kept — ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk record retention is how long they keep bank statements, receipts, payroll files, and filed-return copies. IRS orientation pages describe general periods, often measured from when the return is filed, and longer holds for some items; employment-tax records are often kept longer. This is not legal advice and not a retention policy for this shop. The topic exists so you do not delete source PDFs. Next files are their existing archive location and a question if they ask you to purge. Typical fields you may note are file type, where they keep it, and what they already said they keep. Files are their drive or boxes. Cadence is rare; usually only when someone wants to throw things out. The bookkeeper keeps work files; they own retention. Shops vary. Ask what they already keep and where. A starter guess until they teach: do not delete source PDFs. Common mistakes are deleting because a blog said three years, pasting TINs into a "permanent" sheet, and treating IRS orientation as this shop's policy. Never delete their sources, never invent a retention policy as law, never give legal advice, and never say a year is "safe to shred."
