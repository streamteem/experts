# Undeposited funds / payments to deposit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk undeposited funds (or payments to deposit) is the holding account for checks and card batches that are recorded as received but not yet matched to a bank deposit. Software defaults often land here. It exists so a Tuesday deposit that includes three invoices plus card batches can match one bank line. Next files are the deposit worksheet and the bank rec. Typical fields are payment date, customer or batch id, amount, and deposit date when matched. Files are the undeposited-funds detail export and the bank deposit line. Cadence is every deposit day and at month-end if a leftover remains. The bookkeeper matches batches; they make the physical or processor deposit. Shops vary on whether they use this account or deposit each payment straight to the bank account in software. Ask this shop's batch process. A starter guess until they teach: leftover balance is a question, not a plug. Common mistakes are recording the invoice payment and the bank deposit as two income events, leaving old batches uncleared, and clearing leftover with an income plug. Never invent a batch, never log into the processor, never treat undeposited funds as petty cash, and never force the account to zero without their word.
