# Bank reconcile

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk a bank or card reconcile is the proof that the statement ending balance and date match the books for that account, with outstanding checks and deposits-in-transit listed. It exists so the month-end pack and the CPA handoff can say the cash line is explained, not hoped. Next files are the outstanding-items CSV, the rec write-up, and the close status sheet. Typical fields are statement ending date, statement ending balance, book balance, outstanding checks, deposits in transit, and the difference. Files are the statement PDF they provided plus the worksheet. Cadence is monthly per account, sometimes weekly for a high-volume operating account. The bookkeeper prepares; the owner or CPA confirms matched. Vendor help pages for QuickBooks Online and Xero treat a zero difference as the software finish line. This shop must say what matched means here. Shops vary on cutoff for old uncleared items and whether they rec cards the same way as banks. Ask. A starter guess until they teach: one account, one statement period, difference left open if it is not zero. Common mistakes are plugging miscellaneous income or expense to force a zero, clearing old checks without asking, and calling the month closed while the rec is open. Never invent outstanding items, never force a plug journal, never log into their bank, and never say the rec is "IRS approved."
