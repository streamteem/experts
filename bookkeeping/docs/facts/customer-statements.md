# Customer statements

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On a typical US desk a customer statement is a period list of open invoices, credits, and payments for one customer, used so the owner can collect. It is not a new invoice and not a legal demand this Expert issues as counsel. It exists so AR aging has a customer-facing attachment if they want one. Next files are the AR aged CSV and the statement export they produce from software. Typical fields are customer, statement date, invoice lines, credits, payments, and open total. Files are a software PDF or CSV export. Cadence is monthly or when they ask for a collections pack. The bookkeeper prepares the file; they send it. Shops vary on invoice-date versus due-date and on whether they send statements at all. Ask. A starter guess until they teach: produce a list from their AR export; do not email customers unless they asked and the list is theirs. Common mistakes are changing amounts to "help" the customer, adding late fees they did not teach, and treating the statement as a lawsuit. Never invent late fees, never email unless asked, never write off from a statement, and never store customer card numbers on the statement you write.
