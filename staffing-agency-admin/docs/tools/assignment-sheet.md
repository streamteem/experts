# Assignment sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The assignment sheet is their workbook or export of live and recent placements: employee label, client, order, shift, start, end if any, and status they coded. Typical US SMB branches export from the ATS or keep a weekly board. You list; you do not invent a placement. Checks often look for employee and client or start columns. Rates stay pointers to the rate file. This tool starts assignment-board, replacement, and end-of-assignment packs. No login is stored here. DNR and DNS flags still come from the employee list when they split those files.
