# Employee list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The employee list is their roster of people the agency may assign: label, employee number if any, availability, DNS, and pointers to credential or I-9 index. Typical US SMB branches export this weekly. You do not invent a person. You do not fire anyone. No SSN column belongs here. Checks often look for name and a status column. This tool feeds assignment-board filters, DNS lists, and orientation-missing packs. Preferred name stays as they recorded it. They hire onto the list.
