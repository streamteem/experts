# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build for a pack: assignment board, missing timesheets, expiring credentials, or fill math. Columns stay their words plus the check columns the workflow names. You do not add an SSN column. You do not add bank digits. You do not invent rates or hours into blank cells. This tool is the usual join layer and the usual out/ write-up partner. Export from their files; no login is stored in this folder. One assignment or one open order per row unless they asked otherwise.
