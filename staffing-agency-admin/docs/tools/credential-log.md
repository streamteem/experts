# Credential log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The credential log lists person, credential type as they named it, issued or expiration date, and scan-present if they track scans. Typical US SMB staffing desks sort by expiration for the week. You do not invent a type or a date. You do not copy license numbers when dates will do. Checks often look for name and a date column. This tool feeds credential-expiration packs. Background and drug-screen dates may live here only if they already put them here — still no verdict and no result copy. No vendor-portal password belongs here.
