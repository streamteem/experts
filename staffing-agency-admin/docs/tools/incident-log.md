# Incident log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The incident log is their dated list of site incidents and injuries: person, client, date, form-present, and who they said must receive the pack. You list and route. You do not decide fault or compensability. You do not copy extra medical narrative. Checks may look for date and name columns. This tool starts incident-routing packs. Near-miss versus injury stays their label. No carrier-portal password belongs here. OSHA temporary-worker pages are orientation, not a finding you write.
