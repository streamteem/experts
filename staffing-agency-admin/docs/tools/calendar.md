# Assignment / start calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The calendar is their dated view of starts, expected ends, orientations, and client shutdowns they already wrote. Typical US SMB branches keep a shared calendar export or a sheet of dates. You do not invent a start. You do not move an end to tidy fill. Checks may be a file-exists on the export they dropped. This tool supports assignment-board packs with the assignment sheet. Holiday dates still come from calendar-holidays when the workflow names that file. No calendar-account password is stored here.
