# Client list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The client list is their short file of clients and sites: name as they typed it, site if they split sites, signer name if they store one, conversion-fee pointer, and any client-level do-not-send notes they already wrote. You do not invent a client. You do not store a portal password. Checks may look for client and site columns. This tool joins open orders and DNS/DNR packs. Not a CRM. Prospect rows stay as they coded them. They own the account names.
