# Timesheet CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The timesheet CSV is their period export: employee label, assignment or order, dates, punches if they keep them, hours, OT as they coded, and approval status. Typical US SMB staffing offices download from a clock, a client portal, or a keyed workbook. You do not invent hours. You do not store a portal password. Checks may look for name and hours or date columns. This tool feeds timesheet-completeness packs. Missing weeks stay missing. Client-sign columns stay as they named them.
