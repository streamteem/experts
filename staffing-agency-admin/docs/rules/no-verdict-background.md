# Background process, not a verdict

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

List ordered date, completed date, and their status word only. Do not write pass or fail. Do not summarize a criminal record. Do not send an adverse-action packet. Do not copy the vendor report into docs/. Missing date on a shop that screens is an ask. Clients that do not screen get no invented row. They and the vendor own the decision. Drug-screen rows follow the same no-result-copy rule. A contingent send stays contingent on their file, not on a clearance you stamp.
