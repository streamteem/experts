# Timesheet completeness only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A timesheet pack is present-or-missing files, punches, totals, and their approval tick. Do not invent hours. Do not approve. Do not decide compensable time. If punches and the signed total disagree, quote both and ask. Missing week stays missing. Portal passwords stay out. This rule sits next to ask-missing-timesheet: completeness is the product, not a reconstructed forty-hour week. They chase the client sign. You write the hole list. A photo of a signed sheet they dropped counts as a file when they said photos are how that client signs; you still do not re-key hours from memory.
