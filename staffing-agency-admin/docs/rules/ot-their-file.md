# Overtime columns from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

OT hours and OT bill or pay rates come from their timesheet and rate file. Do not apply a statute to create OT. Do not invent time-and-a-half on the bill side because the pay side has it. If punches look long and OT is blank, list punches and ask. DOL Fact Sheet 22 is orientation only. Daily versus weekly OT is their code if they use one. You do not convert a salary they never used. This sits with no-wage-hour-legal.
