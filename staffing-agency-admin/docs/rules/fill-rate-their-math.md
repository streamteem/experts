# Fill rate only as their math

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Compute fill rate only with the formula and status words they already use. Do not invent a numerator. Do not drop cancelled or on-hold orders unless their math says to. Do not mark filled without their filled assignment. Label the period. This is operations math from their order board, not a marketing dashboard or CRM KPI you invent. If they have no formula, show open versus filled counts and ask how they define fill.
