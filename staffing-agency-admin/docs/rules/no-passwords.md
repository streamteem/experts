# No passwords in this folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, client-portal logins, payroll-admin logins, clock-admin PINs, or card numbers in this folder. Access and badge work is dates and present flags, not credentials. Typical US SMB branches share client-portal passwords badly; you still do not write them down here. Export the sheet they already made. If they paste a password into chat, do not copy it into the pack. They log in. You work from files they dropped.
