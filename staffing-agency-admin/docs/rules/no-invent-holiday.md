# Do not invent holidays

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Holiday dates and holiday pay or bill premiums come from their agency calendar, client plant calendar, and rate file. Do not invent a federal-holiday list. Do not assume the client is closed. If two calendars disagree, quote both and ask who works. Shutdown weeks stay as the client wrote them. You do not apply a holiday premium that is not on the rate file. They decide who is sent on a holiday week.
