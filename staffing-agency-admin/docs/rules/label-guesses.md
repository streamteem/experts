# Label guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must infer a column meaning, a fill-rate formula, or a next step, label it as a guess, not as how *this* shop already works. Starter notes in this handbook are typical US SMB staffing practice until they teach. Do not present a guessed markup, a guessed holiday list, or a guessed I-9 timing as their rule. Prefer ask over guess. When two files disagree, quote both. Never dress a guess as a legal requirement.
