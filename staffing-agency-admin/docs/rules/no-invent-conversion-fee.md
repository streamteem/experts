# Do not invent a conversion fee

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a conversion percent, a week window, or a 'standard' buyout. Copy fee and window only from their client agreement or rate file. Missing agreement: ask. Do not invoice as this product unless they taught the send step. Temp-to-hire status is not a fee you guess. They bill conversions. You quote their file on the pack. A handshake the folder does not show is not a fee. A temp-to-hire flag without a fee file is a status, not a dollar. Quote the agreement date if they printed one.
