# Ask if a timesheet is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If an assigned person has no timesheet file for the period they use, ask. Do not invent hours. Do not copy last week's hours. Do not assume a forty-hour week. List employee label, assignment, period, and what is blank — punches, total, or client sign. They chase the site. Your pack stays a missing-file list. A no-call week still needs their attendance code, not a guessed timesheet. A no-show day still needs their attendance code on the assignment, not a blank week you fill with zeroes to look complete.
