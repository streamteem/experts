# No wage-hour legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not decide overtime, minimum wage, travel time, or final-pay deadlines as law. Copy hours from their timesheet and rates from their file. Do not apply a daily or weekly OT test. Do not invent a state deadline for the last check. DOL Wage and Hour pages are orientation. Breaks stay as their file. They and their advisor own wage-hour questions. You list punches, totals, and missing approvals. Travel, per diem, and holiday premiums stay on their files, not a statute you apply from memory of another state.
