# Rates from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bill rate, pay rate, overtime rate, shift differential, holiday premium, and per diem come only from their rate file, signed order, or assignment. Do not invent a rate to win a fill or to match a remembered market. If two files disagree, quote both and ask. Do not back-solve pay from bill. They set rates. You copy the number and the unit as written. A blank rate is an ask, not a guessed class average. Rate-card version dates stay on the pack when they printed one.
