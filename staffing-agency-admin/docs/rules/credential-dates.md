# Credential dates from their log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

License and credential work is type plus the dates on their log, and a present-or-missing scan if they track scans. Do not invent an expiration. Do not decide the license class is correct as a board. Do not copy license numbers into docs/ when dates will do. Expired on their sheet is a flag for them, not a stop-work order you issue. Do not invent a required cert from a remembered OSHA list. They decide who may still go to the site.
