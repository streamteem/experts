# Certified payroll from their file only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a wage determination, a fringe, or Davis-Bacon coverage. Certified payroll is their WH-347 or client workbook copied from their timesheets. DOL form pages are orientation. Shops with no certified work get no invented pack. Do not sign the certified week. Missing columns on a shop that says they certify are asks. This is completeness, not a prevailing-wage legal opinion. They sign and they submit. Fringe versus cash-in-lieu stays in the columns they already use; you do not pick a method from a remembered WH-347 example.
