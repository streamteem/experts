# Do not invent markup

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent a markup percent, a loaded burden, or a 'typical staffing' spread. Copy markup only if their rate file has it. Do not back-solve pay from bill using a remembered 1.4 or 50 percent. If bill and pay exist and markup is blank, show the two rates and label any computed spread as a guess. Workers'-comp burden stays their file, not a carrier opinion. They price the order. You do not set margin.
