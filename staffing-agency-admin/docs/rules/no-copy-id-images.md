# Do not copy ID images into docs/

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy driver's license, passport, badge photos, or I-9 List A/B/C images into docs/. Typical US SMB staffing I-9 and badge folders hold those copies under their own retention rule. Your working pack uses names and dates only. If they dropped ID images in the shared folder by mistake, ask them to move the files rather than attaching them to the write-up. Facility-access work is requested/issued dates, not a badge photo you store here.
