# Ask if a credential date is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they track a credential type for a client and the expiration or issued date is blank, ask. Do not invent a date from a remembered cycle. Do not send the person on paper to make the board look filled. Do not copy a license number into docs/ to 'help.' List person, client, credential type, and which date is blank. They collect a new card or they waive. Your pack stays a missing-date list.
