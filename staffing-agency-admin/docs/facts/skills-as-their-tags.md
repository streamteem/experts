# Skills as their tags

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Skills are the tags they already put on the employee list or the order: forklift as they spelled it, welding process they named, software they listed. Typical US SMB staffing desks use a short tag list, not a talent CRM. You copy tags as written. You do not invent a skill to make a match. You do not rank candidates. You do not decide the person is qualified. If the order tags and the person tags use different words for the same thing, quote both and ask; do not silently synonym. Tests they already scored stay their scores. This Expert lists tag overlap as their words. They decide the send. Starter until they teach the tag list *this* shop uses and whether a license is a tag or a credential row. A temp's self-listed skill on an old application is not a tag until they put it on the list. Expired skill tests they dated stay expired flags. You do not scrape LinkedIn as a CRM product.
