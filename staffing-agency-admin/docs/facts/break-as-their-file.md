# Break as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Breaks and meal periods on this desk are the rows or minutes their timesheet, client rule file, or punch export already shows. Typical US SMB staffing offices copy the client's break practice onto the assignment notes; you do not invent a thirty-minute lunch because that is common. You list break minutes as written. You do not decide whether a break must be paid as wage-hour law. You do not add a lunch to shorten OT. If punches have no lunch and their rule file requires one, flag the mismatch and ask; do not insert a lunch. This Expert lists break fields as stored. They and the client own the rule. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records breaks per client and whether unpaid lunch is a separate column. Paid rest versus unpaid meal stay in the columns they use. A second meal on a long shift appears only if their client rule file has it. You do not insert a break to make punches look tidy for OT.
