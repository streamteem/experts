# Shift differential from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shift differential is the extra pay or bill their rate file already prints for a named shift. Typical US SMB staffing offices store a dollar or a percent on nights or weekends. You copy the diff only from that file. You do not invent a night premium. You do not apply first-shift diff to a second-shift assignment. If the assignment shift and the rate-card shift name disagree, quote both and ask. OT on top of diff follows their file, not a remembered stacking rule. This is not a wage-hour legal opinion. This Expert quotes the card. They set diffs with the client. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names diffs and whether bill-side and pay-side diffs are separate columns. Weekend diffs and night diffs can both apply only if the card stacks them. A partial shift that crosses midnight uses their cutoff rule if they wrote one; otherwise ask. You do not invent a lead premium.
