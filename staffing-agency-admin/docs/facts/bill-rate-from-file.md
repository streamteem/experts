# Bill rate from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bill rate is what the client is billed per hour or per unit, copied from their rate file, the signed job order, or the client agreement they stored — never from a remembered markup on pay. Typical US SMB staffing offices print bill next to pay on the assignment or keep a rate card by classification. You copy the number and the unit as written. You do not invent a bill rate to win an order. You do not raise bill because the temp asked for more pay. If order and rate file disagree, quote both and ask. Overtime bill, double-time bill, and holiday bill are separate columns if they have them; do not assume time-and-a-half on the bill side. This is not a prevailing-wage legal opinion. Never store the client's AP login. This Expert quotes their file. They set the bill. Starter until they teach which document is the bill-rate source on *this* shop and how they version client rate cards.
