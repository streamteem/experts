# Unavailable

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Unavailable is their employee-list flag for a person who is not to be assigned in a window: vacation they recorded, another assignment, or a hold they coded. Typical US SMB staffing offices keep a simple status next to the name. You copy the flag and the dates. You do not invent unavailable because the phone was quiet. You do not override unavailable to fill an order. You do not fire anyone. A do-not-send is a different flag. This Expert excludes unavailable people from a send list unless they asked to show them as blocked. They set availability. Starter until they teach the availability codes *this* shop uses and whether a second job they know about is a flag. Partial-week unavailable still blocks the days they marked. A second assignment at another client is unavailable for a new send unless they allow doubles. You do not text the person as a messaging product.
