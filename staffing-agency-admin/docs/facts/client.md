# Client

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A client is the company that issued the job order and will host the temp on site. Typical US SMB staffing offices keep a short client list: legal name as they typed it, site address if they store one, hiring-manager contact as they recorded, and any do-not-send or conversion-fee pointer already on file. You copy the client label that matches the order and the assignment. You do not invent a client to tidy a board. You do not store the client's portal password. You do not build a CRM, email, or SMS product around the list. If two spellings exist, quote both and ask which wins for invoices and for the assignment sheet. A prospect with no order is not a client row unless they already coded it that way. This Expert joins orders and assignments on their client label. They own the account. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names clients versus sites and whether a parent company gets one row or many.
