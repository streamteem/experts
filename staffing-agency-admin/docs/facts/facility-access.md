# Facility access

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Facility access is the client's badge, gate, or visitor process as they already wrote: requested, issued, or still open. Typical US SMB staffing desks log a request date and an issued date so day-one is not a surprise. You list those dates and present-or-missing. You do not invent a badge number. You do not store door codes or badge PINs in docs/. You do not disable a badge yourself. Off-assignment access still-open is a flag for them. This is not a locksmith product. This Expert lists still-needed access on the start pack. They and the client issue badges. Starter until they teach how *this* shop requests access per site and whether a visitor badge is a different row. Escort-only or visitor-only statuses they wrote stay those words. A lost-badge row stays visible. You do not store a PIN reset. After-hours gate lists they keep are a row if their access file includes them.
