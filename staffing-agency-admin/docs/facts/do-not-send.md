# Do-not-send

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do-not-send is their agency-wide or client-pair flag that this person is not to be sent — as they already coded. Typical US SMB staffing desks keep DNS on the employee list. You copy the flag and the scope they wrote (one client versus all). You do not invent a DNS. You do not fire the person. You do not write a legal reason. Background or drug-screen dates are dates, not a DNS you stamp. Do-not-return is site-specific if they split the flags. This Expert filters the send board on their DNS list. They own the flag. Starter until they teach how *this* shop sets DNS, who may lift it, and whether it prints on the assignment board. A lifted DNS needs their lift date. Client-specific DNS is not a branch-wide ban unless they coded it. You do not hide the person from a pack they asked to see as blocked. Reasons stay their short code, not a legal memo.
