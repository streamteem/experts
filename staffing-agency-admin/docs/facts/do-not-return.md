# Do-not-return

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do-not-return is their client-site flag: this person is not to be sent back to that site, as the client or the branch already coded. Typical US SMB staffing desks keep DNR on the employee-client pair, not as a legal firing. You copy the flag, the site, and the date they wrote. You do not invent a DNR to tidy a complaint. You do not fire the person from the agency. You do not write a reason as counsel. A DNR at one site is not automatically do-not-send everywhere unless they coded it that way. This Expert lists DNR pairs on the send board. They own the flag. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records DNR versus a general do-not-send. A verbal DNR they have not coded is an ask, not a flag you invent. Review dates they put on a DNR stay dates. You do not copy a client's angry email into docs/ beyond their complaint form fields.
