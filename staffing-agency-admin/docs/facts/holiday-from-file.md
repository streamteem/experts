# Holiday from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Holidays on this desk are the dated list they stored — agency holiday calendar, client plant calendar, or both. Typical US SMB staffing offices bill or pay a holiday premium only when their rate file says so. You copy the dates and the premium only from those files. You do not invent a federal-holiday list. You do not assume the client is closed. If agency and client calendars disagree on a date, quote both and ask who works. Shutdown weeks stay as the client wrote them. This Expert builds a holiday-shift list from their calendars plus open assignments. They decide who is sent. Starter until they teach which calendar wins for *this* shop per client. Worked holiday versus not-worked holiday pay are different columns if they split them. Floating holidays they grant stay on their file, not a federal list. You do not close the plant because a typical factory nearby is closed.
