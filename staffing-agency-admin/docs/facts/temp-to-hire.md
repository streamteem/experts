# Temp-to-hire

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Temp-to-hire is their status on an assignment that may convert: the window they wrote, the conversion-fee pointer, and whether the client already asked. Typical US SMB staffing offices flag TTH on the order. You copy the flag and the dates. You do not invent a conversion date. You do not decide the person is now the client's employee as counsel. You do not fire the temp off the agency. If the window ended and no conversion file exists, list the end and ask; do not assume converted. Hours during the window still need timesheets. This Expert lists TTH windows from their file. They negotiate the convert. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks TTH versus straight temp and who updates payroll status. A client 'we will hire next month' email they saved is a note, not a conversion date. Benefits waiting periods are their file if any, not a statute you apply. Do not move the person to the client's payroll as this product.
