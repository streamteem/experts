# Drug-policy acknowledgment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A drug-policy acknowledgment is the signed or dated page that says the person received the agency or client substance policy they already use. Typical US SMB staffing packets include a one-page ack separate from a drug-screen date. You list policy name, version if printed, and ack date. You do not treat a signed ack as a legal waiver you interpret. You do not invent a policy they do not use. If the ack version and the live policy PDF disagree, quote both and ask. Screen dates stay on the screen log, not merged into this ack. This Expert builds a missing-ack list. They collect signatures. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop uses an agency policy, a client policy, or both. A client host policy ack is not the agency ack. Electronic acks they exported as a dated row count as present when that export is in the folder. You do not explain what 'reasonable suspicion' means as counsel.
