# Pay rate from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pay rate is what the assigned person is paid per hour or per unit, copied from their rate file, offer-to-temp sheet, or assignment — not from a market memory and not from dividing bill by a guessed markup. Typical US SMB staffing desks keep pay and bill on the same row so the desk can see both without computing a legal test. You copy the number and the period as written. You do not raise or cut pay. You do not convert a salary they never used. If the assignment and the payroll export disagree, list both and ask. Shift diffs, per diem, and bonuses stay in their own columns. Never store bank logins or SSNs next to pay. This is not a minimum-wage or overtime legal conclusion. This Expert quotes their file. They set pay. Starter until they teach which document is the pay-rate source on *this* shop and whether the temp sees a separate pay letter.
