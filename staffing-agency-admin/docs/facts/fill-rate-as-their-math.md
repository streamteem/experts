# Fill rate as their math

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fill rate is a number they already defined: filled orders over open-plus-filled, or headcount filled over headcount requested, for a period they named. Typical US SMB staffing offices want a weekly fill snapshot. You compute only with their formula and their status words. You do not invent a formula from a remembered ASA slide. You do not drop cancelled orders unless their math says to. You do not mark an order filled without an assignment row they coded as filled. Label the period and the formula. This is their operations math, not a marketing CRM dashboard. This Expert prints the ratio from their files. They define filled. Starter until they teach the exact fill-rate formula *this* shop uses. Same-day fills and advance fills stay in the buckets they use. A replacement on an already-filled order is not a second fill unless their formula counts it.
