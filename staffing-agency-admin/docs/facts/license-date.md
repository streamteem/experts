# License date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A license date is the expiration or issued date they recorded on the credential log for a license type they already named. Typical US SMB staffing offices sort a weekly expiring-soon list so a temp is not sent after the date on the card. You copy the date as written. You do not invent an expiration from a remembered two-year cycle. You do not copy a license number into docs/ if they asked for dates only — prefer type, person, and expiration. Past-due on their sheet is a flag, not a board finding you write. Reciprocity or out-of-state stays labeled as they filed it; you do not declare it valid in this state. This Expert builds a date list. They decide the send. Starter until they teach how *this* shop wants 'expires within N days' computed and which calendar they use.
