# Per diem from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Per diem is the daily or weekly stipend on their rate file, travel policy, or assignment if they use one. Typical US SMB staffing offices that run travel or remote sites store a number and a start rule. You copy the amount and the unit as written. You do not invent a GSA rate. You do not decide tax treatment as counsel. You do not send money. Missing file: ask. Receipts they require are present-or-missing, not a travel blog you write. Housing, if any, is a separate fact. This Expert lists per diem as filed. They pay per their process. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop uses per diem and which assignments it attaches to. Half-day or travel-day rates appear only if the file has them. You do not decide accountable versus non-accountable as tax counsel. Duplicate per diem and a meal receipt they already coded stay as they coded.
