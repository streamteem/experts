# Union if they use it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Union, if the order or the person is under a hall or a CBA they already named, is a label and a dispatch or clearance date they recorded. Typical US SMB staffing offices that never touch union work have no such rows — do not invent them. You copy the local name as they typed it and the clearance present-or-missing. You do not interpret the CBA as counsel. You do not invent a wage from a remembered scale. Referral hall rules stay their file. This Expert lists clearance ticks. They call the hall if that is their process. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop runs union orders and where the clearance log lives. Steward names they recorded are contacts, not a CBA you interpret. Travelers versus local hands stay as they labeled them. You do not invent a dispatch number. Missing clearance on a union order is an ask, not a send.
