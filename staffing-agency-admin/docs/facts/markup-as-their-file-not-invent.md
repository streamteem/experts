# Markup as their file (do not invent)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Markup or spread is the relationship they already recorded between bill and pay — a percent, a dollar, or a loaded burden they wrote on the rate card. Typical US SMB staffing offices store a target markup by client or by class; some never write a percent and only keep the two rates. You copy the markup only if their file has it. You do not invent a 1.5 or 50 percent because that is 'typical staffing.' You do not back-solve pay from bill using a remembered burden. If bill and pay are present and markup is blank, leave blank or show both rates and label any computed spread as a guess. Workers'-comp class and burden lines stay as their file, not a carrier opinion you write. This Expert does not set margin. They price the order. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop stores markup as a field or only as the two rates.
