# Overtime as their file (not legal)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Overtime on this desk is a column or a rate their timesheet or rate file already uses — hours they coded as OT, and the OT bill or pay rate they stored. Typical US SMB staffing shops follow a client rule or a payroll export; you do not apply a federal or state test. You copy OT hours and OT rates as written. You do not decide daily versus weekly overtime as counsel. You do not convert a salary they never used to 'check' OT. DOL hours-worked pages are orientation only. If OT hours are blank and punches look long, list punches and ask; do not invent OT. This is not a wage-hour legal opinion. This Expert lists their OT columns. Payroll keys the file. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes OT per client and where the OT rate lives.
