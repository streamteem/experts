# Client sign on hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Client sign is the named supervisor or portal identity they already treat as the hours authority for that site. Typical US SMB staffing desks keep a signer name on the assignment or a stamp on the paper timesheet. You copy the signer as written and whether the sign is present. You do not invent a supervisor because the department 'usually' signs. You do not decide the sign is legally binding. If the assignment signer and the timesheet name disagree, quote both and ask. Portal approvals they exported as a dated row count as present when that export is in the folder. This Expert flags missing signs. They collect the sign. Starter until they teach who may sign hours for each *this* shop client. A backup signer they named on the assignment may sign only if the file says so. Illegible paper signs are present-with-ask, not a forged name. You do not pick a manager from the client list to close the week.
