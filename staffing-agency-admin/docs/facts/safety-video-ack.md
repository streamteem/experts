# Safety-video acknowledgment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A safety-video acknowledgment is a dated tick or signature that the person watched the video they already named — agency video, client site video, or both. Typical US SMB staffing desks collect a one-page ack or an LMS export. You list person, video name as they labeled it, and ack date. You do not invent a required video from a remembered OSHA list. You do not mark complete without their date or file. Expired refresh dates are due flags, not a citation. No LMS password belongs here. If agency and client videos both exist, keep two rows. This Expert builds a missing-ack list. They assign the video. Starter until they teach which videos *this* shop tracks per client and how they version the ack. A quiz score they stored is their number, not a pass you invent. Spanish or other-language versions keep the version string they printed. A group sign-in sheet is one date per name, not one tick for the crew.
