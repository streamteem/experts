# Perm / direct-hire if they use it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Perm or direct-hire, if they run that line, is a search they already logged: order, fee as their file, and a hired date they wrote — not a recruiting CRM product. Typical US SMB mixed shops keep perm folders separate from temp assignments. You list completeness: resume present, fee file present, start as they dated. You do not invent a perm fee. You do not rank candidates. You do not email or SMS a pipeline as a product. Shops that are temp-only should not get invented perm rows. This Expert lists their perm-file ticks. They search and they invoice. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop does perm and which folder is not the temp assignment board. Retained versus contingency is their fee-file label. A temp who later goes perm still needs the conversion file if that is the path they used, not a perm order you invent. Do not store a candidate CRM password.
