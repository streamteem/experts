# Employee label

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Employee label is the name or employee number the agency already uses on the assignment, timesheet, and credential log — preferred name if they recorded one, plus their ID if they mint IDs. Typical US SMB staffing desks keep an employee list separate from the client list. You copy the label they stored. You do not invent a person. You do not reuse a closed person's number unless their file says they recycle. Legal name for payroll and I-9 stays on those forms and is not copied with an SSN into docs/. A 1099 or contractor row they already coded stays on that scheme; do not mint a W-2 employee number for it. This Expert joins timesheets and credentials on their label. They hire onto the agency roster. Starter until they teach how *this* shop labels people versus how the client badge system names them.
