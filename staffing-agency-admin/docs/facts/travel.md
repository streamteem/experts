# Travel as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Travel on this desk is mileage, report-pay, or travel-time rows their policy or assignment already uses. Typical US SMB staffing offices that send people between sites keep a mileage rate on a file. You copy the rate and the miles they recorded. You do not invent miles from a map. You do not decide whether travel time is hours worked as wage-hour law. You do not store an EZ-Pass login. Portal-to-portal questions stay with them and their advisor. This Expert lists travel rows as written. They approve the miles. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records travel versus regular punches. Report-in pay and mileage are different rows if they split them. A client-site move mid-week needs their new assignment or a travel row they wrote. You do not invent a commute from home as hours worked. A parked-at-site move they already coded as travel stays travel; a normal commute they left off the file stays off.
