# Replacement

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A replacement is a new assignment row or a linked row they already use when a temp leaves, no-shows, or the client asks for someone else. Typical US SMB staffing offices keep the original assignment id on the replacement. You list both rows. You do not silently overwrite the first name. You do not invent a replacement to hide an open order. You do not fire the first person. Rates on the replacement come from their file, not from a guessed emergency premium. Credential dates still apply to the new person. This Expert shows the chain. They pick the replacement. Starter until they teach how *this* shop links replacements versus opening a new job order. Skill tags and credential dates on the new person still have to meet the same order. Bill and pay on the replacement can differ only if their rate file says so. You do not promise the client a name you invent.
