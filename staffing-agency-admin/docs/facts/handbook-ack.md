# Handbook acknowledgment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A handbook acknowledgment is the signed or dated page that says the person received a named agency handbook version. Typical US SMB staffing offices use a last page or an HR-folder PDF plus a signature sheet for W-2 temps. You list employee label, handbook version printed on the ack, and the ack date. You do not treat a missing ack as proof they never received the book, and you do not treat a signed ack as a legal waiver you interpret. If the ack version and the current handbook file disagree, list both and ask. You do not invent an ack date. You do not rewrite handbook rules as employment-law advice. Client-site rules are a different packet if they use one. This Expert builds a missing-ack list. They collect signatures. Starter until they teach how *this* shop versions the agency handbook versus client packets.
