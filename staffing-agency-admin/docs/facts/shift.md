# Shift

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A shift is the client's named window on the order or assignment: first, second, nights, weekends, or a clock range they wrote. Typical US SMB staffing desks copy the shift from the job order onto the assignment sheet so timesheets and holiday diffs join cleanly. You do not invent a shift because the temp 'usually' works days. You do not move someone to another shift to cover a no-call. If the order says 6a–2p and the assignment says 7a–3p, list both and ask. Shift differential dollars come only from their rate file, not from a remembered plant premium. A rotating shift stays as they coded the rotation. This field is operations, not a wage-hour legal finding about what counts as a workday. This Expert lists shift as written. They confirm the window with the client. Starter until they teach the shift codes *this* shop uses and whether the order or the assignment wins when they disagree.
