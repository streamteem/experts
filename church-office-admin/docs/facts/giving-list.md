# Giving list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A giving list is the house-of-worship office export or workbook of gifts received in a period they named: a Sunday offering, a midweek drop, or an online window they saved. Typical US congregation offices pull this from ACS, FellowshipOne, Planning Center Giving, Pushpay, Tithe.ly, Realm, Shelby, or a numbered sheet the counters already totaled. Columns they usually keep are a gift handle, date, amount, fund or envelope code, batch, tender type, and a donor label only as thin as their process needs. You copy those rows. You do not invent a gift. You do not invent a fund. You do not decide the gift is deductible. You do not add a household from memory of last Easter. If the export and the count sheet disagree, quote both and ask which the bookkeeper treats as live. Extra PII — full account numbers, card PAN, a Social Security number — stays out of docs/. This desk is not the nonprofit grant desk: there is no award letter and no funder portal. Completeness is present versus missing codes and a write-up. They post the books. Starter until they teach how this office names batches and who may see the donor column.
