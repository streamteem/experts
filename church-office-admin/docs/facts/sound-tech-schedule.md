# Sound tech schedule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The sound or AV schedule is their roster of who runs board, slides, or microphones on a dated service or event. Typical US churches keep Planning Center Services, a volunteer workbook, or a paper rotation. You copy the assignment. You do not invent a tech. You do not run the board. If a wedding or funeral is on the calendar and the tech cell is blank, flag it. Backup names appear only if they stored them. Livestream operator, if separate, stays on the livestream file. You do not recruit by SMS. Volunteer background dates apply only if their policy tags this role. Equipment passwords and console logins never belong in this folder. If two people are assigned to one board and their rule is one, still list both and ask; do not delete. They schedule. You list holes against the week calendar. Starter until they teach how this office names AV roles and which service times need a tech.
