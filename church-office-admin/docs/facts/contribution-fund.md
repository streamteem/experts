# Contribution fund coding

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Contribution-fund coding is how a single gift is split or tagged to funds on the giving list: one envelope with general and missions boxes, or an online gift with a fund dropdown. Typical US church software stores a fund id per gift line. You copy those lines. You do not move a split to general to make the batch tidy. You do not invent a split percent. If the donor note says building and the dropdown says general, quote both and ask; do not recode. Restricted-versus-unrestricted codes follow their chart. Memorial and designated notes are extra tags, not a silent new fund. You do not decide the split is deductible by fund. Missing fund on any line of a split is a missing-code row. They recode after they talk to the donor if their process says so. This Expert lists blank and conflicting codes. Starter until they teach how this office enters splits and which fund wins when a note and a box disagree.
