# Dual control as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Dual control is the written cash-handling rule they stored: two unrelated people with the plate, a sealed bag, or dual safe access as they described it. Typical US church finance policies and ECFA-oriented checklists talk about this; you still use their PDF, not a generic standard as a stamp. You compare the count-team roster and the signed count sheet to that file. You do not invent a dual-control law. You do not mark them in compliance. If the file says two and the sheet has one signature, flag it. You do not recount. You do not walk the deposit to the bank. Sealed-bag numbers, if they use them, are copied as written. Empty policy: ask. This is not a fraud investigation. They fix the process. You pack exceptions. Starter until they teach which finance-policy PDF is current and whether online-only weeks still require a sheet.
