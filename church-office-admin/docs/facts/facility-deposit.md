# Facility deposit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A facility deposit is an amount and status on their building-use or wedding file: received, held, refunded, or applied as they coded it. Typical US church offices take a refundable hall deposit or a wedding hold. You copy the amount from that file. You do not invent a deposit. You do not take a card or cash. You do not refund. You do not store PAN. If the fee sheet and the signed form disagree, quote both and ask. Applied-to-fee versus refundable stay as they labeled them. You do not decide a damage charge. Kitchen or alcohol add-ons are separate lines if their file splits them. Missing status with a date on the calendar is a flag. They receive and they refund. This Expert lists deposits versus events still on the calendar. Starter until they teach this office deposit schedule and who may mark refunded.
