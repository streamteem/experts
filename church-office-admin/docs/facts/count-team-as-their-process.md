# Count team as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The count team is the people and rotation they already wrote for counting offerings: names, pairs, and which Sundays they serve. Typical US churches keep a teller schedule and a dual-control note. You copy the schedule. You do not invent a pair. You do not count the money. You do not assign a related pair if their process forbids it — flag the roster against their process file; they change the pair. Signatures on the count sheet should match the team they listed for that date, or you quote the mismatch. Pastors or bookkeepers on the team appear only if their process allows it; you do not invent a segregation rule from a textbook if their file is silent — ask. You do not recruit counters by email blast. Background dates apply if their policy tags tellers. They staff the team. You list gaps and process mismatches. Starter until they teach who may serve on this office's count team and how they rotate.
