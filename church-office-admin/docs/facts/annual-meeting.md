# Annual meeting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The annual or congregational meeting is a dated event on their calendar plus the packet they already require: agenda, prior minutes, budget they will present, and nomination or report pages they stored. Typical US churches set this meeting in bylaws or a handbook; you copy dates from that file or from the calendar they marked. You do not invent a quorum rule. You do not invent a vote outcome. You do not chair the meeting. Budget numbers come from their budget file, not a remembered year. Giving charts, if they asked, use their closed-year export and still are not a tax opinion. You do not mail a proxy campaign as a CRM product. Room holds for the hall belong on the room calendar. They call the meeting. You pack present versus missing pages and calendar collisions. If bylaws and the announced date disagree, quote both and ask. Starter until they teach how this office calls the annual meeting and which packet list is required.
