# Key log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The key log is their list of who holds a key, fob, or alarm token they already issued. Typical US church offices keep a workbook: person, key number, issue date, return date, and a staff or volunteer flag. You copy those rows. You do not invent a key number. You do not issue a key. You do not store a door code, alarm password, or lockbox combination in this folder. Missing return dates on keys their policy says are temporary are flags. If the log and a verbal they still have the fellowship-hall key disagree, the log wins until they update it. Vendor keys stay on vendor rows. You do not hide a lost-key note to tidy the board. After a role ends, their process may require a return; you list still-out, they collect. This is not an access-control product you configure. They cut keys and they change cores. You pack exceptions. Starter until they teach how this office numbers keys and who may authorize a new issue.
