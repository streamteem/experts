# Vendor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A vendor on this desk is a name on their vendor list or a bill they stored: plumber, copier, lawn, insurance agent, or a musician they treat as a vendor. Typical US church offices keep a workbook: name, what they do, W-9 present as they coded it, and a default account. You copy those rows. You do not hire. You do not pay. You do not invent a 1099 opinion. W-9 and COI are present or missing. You do not store a bank account number. Member-volunteers who invoice once still follow their vendor file if they used one. If two legal names appear, quote both and ask. Recurring utilities stay vendors if they listed them that way. This is not the nonprofit grant subrecipient desk. Purchase orders, if they use them, come from their PO file. They choose vendors. You list completeness for the pack they asked: missing W-9, missing COI, or a bill without a vendor row. Starter until they teach how this office adds a vendor and who may approve one.
