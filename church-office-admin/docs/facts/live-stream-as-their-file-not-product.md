# Livestream as their file, not a product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Livestream work on this desk is their dated file: which services they stream, who operates, and a link they already published — not a streaming product you build and not a YouTube channel you run. Typical US churches keep a simple schedule plus a Planning Center note. You copy that schedule. You do not invent a stream. You do not go live. You do not store platform passwords. View counts they exported may be listed if they asked; you do not invent a reach claim. Copyright or music-license opinions are out of scope. Funerals and weddings stream only if their file says they do. Bulletin or calendar mismatches (file says stream, calendar says private) stay flagged. This is not an email or social CRM product. They operate the stream. You list dates and missing operators. Starter until they teach which services this office streams and where the operator roster lives.
