# Kitchen use

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Kitchen use is a hold on their kitchen calendar or a checkbox on the facility form: ministry meal, outside caterer, or a renter's reception. Typical US congregations require a kitchen addendum, a preferred caterer list, or a leave-it-clean checklist they stored. You copy those rows. You do not invent a health-department stamp. You do not certify a food process. Allergen notes they put on a meal sheet stay as their short codes, not a clinical plan. Outside caterers may need a COI if their checklist says so. Alcohol in the hall is the alcohol-policy file, not a kitchen yes. You do not cater. You do not buy food. If the hall is reserved and kitchen is not marked, ask whether this office treats that as included. They approve the kitchen. You list holds and missing addenda. Starter until they teach how this office books the kitchen and which groups may use it without a caterer.
