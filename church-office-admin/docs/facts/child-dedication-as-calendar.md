# Child dedication as calendar

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Child dedication, baptism, or a similar rite on this desk is a calendar row they already scheduled: date, service, family label they allow, and the officiant they assigned. Typical US congregations keep a rite book or a Planning Center event. You copy the row. You do not invent a date. You do not perform a rite. You do not give pastoral or theological advice. You do not decide the child is a member. Membership and nursery facts stay on those files if they also apply. If the family asked for a date and the room calendar has a collision, flag it. Certificates, if they print them, are present or missing as files; you do not invent wording. Photos and livestream follow their media and livestream files. You do not email a public announcement campaign. They schedule with the family. You list dates and holes. Starter until they teach how this office books dedications or baptisms and which register they keep.
