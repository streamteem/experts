# Membership transfer as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A membership transfer is their written process for a letter of transfer, a parish register move, or a denomination form they already use. Typical US churches keep a request log: who asked, sending or receiving congregation as they wrote it, date, and status. You copy that log. You do not grant membership. You do not deny membership. You do not send a letter as this product unless they taught that send step and the request is on their form. You do not give pastoral counsel about leaving. If their process requires session or board action, the minutes status is a completeness row, not a vote you invent. Roll codes stay as they are until they change them. You do not add the person to a destination church. Min necessary on any pack. They vote or they record. You list open requests and missing forms. Starter until they teach this office transfer form and which body must act.
