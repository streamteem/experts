# Do not decide benevolence

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never decide who receives help or how much. Never pay a rent or a utility. Never counsel the household. List form present, approval box, and invoice present against their process. Extra hardship and medical narrative stay out of packs. Missing process: stop. Checks they already wrote are still not a remittance you send. They and their deacons or care team decide. You pack completeness only. You do not diagnose need.
