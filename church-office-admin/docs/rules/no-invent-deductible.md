# Do not invent a deductible amount

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not type a deductible column. Do not subtract a dinner value you guessed. Do not split a ticket into gift and goods unless their process file already printed that split. Do not use last year's dinner price. Blank goods value stays blank. Stock and in-kind have no number from you. Statement-run lists show what their process includes, labeled as their process, not as tax. They and their CPA handle donor questions.
