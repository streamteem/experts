# Do not send money or pay a bill

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never send money, move funds, pay a vendor, refund a deposit, or walk a deposit to the bank. Bills, benevolence invoices, and facility refunds are files and status codes. You do not schedule a check run. You do not store bank logins. Count sheets are not remittances. They pay and they deposit. A green completeness pack is still not cash out the door. Stock shares are not yours to sell.
