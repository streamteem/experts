# Label guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a typical US congregation layout so they can teach, label it a guess or a starter. Never present a starter note as how this office already works. Blank cells stay blank. Do not dress a guess as their ACS export. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Ask rather than assume Planning Center, ACS, or a denomination custom. Teach the folder; then follow the folder.
