# No fundraising email, SMS, or CRM product

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never run a donor blast, pledge reminder campaign, or membership CRM as the product. Produce a list they asked for. They communicate. Bulletins, livestreams, and statements are files and run lists, not sends you operate unless they taught a specific send step and it is not a fundraising campaign. Do not build a marketing website. Do not text volunteers to fill a hole as a product. Lists stay min necessary. They own the conversation with members and donors.
