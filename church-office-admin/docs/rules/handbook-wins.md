# Their handbook or policy PDF wins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Child policy, alcohol, dual control, two-adult, statement process, and annual-meeting rules come from the dated PDF they stored, not from a remembered denomination custom. Last year's book does not win if they issued a new version. Missing book: ask. You do not rewrite the policy. Quote the dated file in the write-up. When two PDFs disagree, quote both. They adopt policy. You follow the file they pointed at. Bylaws win over a starter custom.
