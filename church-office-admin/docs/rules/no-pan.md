# No card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store card numbers, CVV, or magnetic data. Online gifts are export rows, not cards you run. Facility deposits and bills are amounts and status codes. Authorization slips with visible PAN stay out of docs or get redacted by them. Processor portals are theirs; you do not save a screenshot of a full card. This rule sits next to no-account-numbers. They (or their processor) take the card. You never do.
