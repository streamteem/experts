# Do not change the membership roll

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not add, remove, or transfer a member. Do not send a church letter unless they taught that send step and the request is on their form. Do not decide good standing. Status codes stay as exported until they change them. Directory lists use only columns their process allows and are still not a blast. Roll versus giving-household mismatches are questions, not merges. They vote or they record. You list the log.
