# Do not value in-kind gifts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not appraise goods, vehicles, or services. Do not pick a thrift-store number. Do not complete Form 8283 as an appraiser or as the congregation. Copy only the description and any value the donor already put on their form. Publication 561 is orientation for why you stop. Stock values come only from a broker letter they stored, copied as printed, still not your opinion. They accept goods. You list the form.
