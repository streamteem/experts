# Do not invent a fund

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not open a fund, merge funds, or default a blank to general. Copy codes from their chart and the gift row. Memorial and designated notes are not a new fund until they opened one. If the note and the dropdown disagree, quote both and ask. Restricted versus unrestricted stays their code. You do not reclassify to tidy a board pack. They map software codes to the books. Missing code is a question, not a guess.
