# No safeguarding legal opinion

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never write a legal opinion on child-protection law, mandatory reporting, or whether a person is safe. Never investigate an allegation. Never decide a report must be filed as counsel. Quote their dated policy and list missing training or background dates they already require. Child Welfare pages are orientation only. Extra incident narrative stays out of weekday packs. They apply the policy. You index files and date columns. Missing policy: ask before you invent a hallway rule.
