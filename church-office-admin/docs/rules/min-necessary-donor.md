# Minimum necessary donor fields

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keep the thinnest donor label their process needs: envelope number, household id, or a mail name on a statement-run they will send. Do not add street, email, phone, birth date, or family notes to a weekday completeness pack. Never copy SSN, card PAN, or full account numbers into docs/. Anonymous rows stay anonymous. Hallway prints drop extra columns. Membership and volunteer packs follow the same min-necessary idea. They decide who may see a full household record.
