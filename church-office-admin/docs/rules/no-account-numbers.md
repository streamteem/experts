# No full account numbers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store a full bank account, routing line, or check MICR in this folder. Count sheets list cash and check totals, not copied account digits. Stock-broker letters should be listed as present; do not retype account numbers into a CSV. Vendor ACH files stay in their AP process, not in docs/. Last-four if they already printed a last-four and asked for it is still their field, not a number you invent. They deposit. You list totals.
