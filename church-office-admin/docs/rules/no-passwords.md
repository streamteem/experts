# No passwords or portal logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, giving-software logins, livestream logins, or alarm codes in this folder. Exports and PDFs they dropped are the inputs. If a workflow needs a fresh export, they export. Door codes and lockbox combinations are also forbidden. Screenshot folders must not show full credentials. This rule is shared across desks and stays on this one. Key logs list who holds a key, not the secret. Processor and calendar logins are included.
