# Starter notes are not this office

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not present these handbook notes as how this congregation already works. Typical US house-of-worship office practice is a guess until they teach. Their fund chart, calendar product, and statement process win. Ask rather than assume ACS, Realm, or a parish custom. After they teach, their files are the handbook. Do not paste a starter paragraph into a bulletin or a statement as church policy. Label leftover guesses as guesses.
