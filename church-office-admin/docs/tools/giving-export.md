# Giving-software export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The giving export is the CSV they saved from ACS, Realm, Planning Center Giving, Pushpay, Tithe.ly, or their workbook. Typical columns are gift id, date, amount, fund, and batch. You copy rows. You do not invent a gift or a fund. No software password belongs here. Min necessary donor fields only. This tool starts giving-batch, missing-fund-codes, statement-run, and pledge-versus-received packs. Draft versus posted stays as they coded it. Checks often look for amount and fund.
