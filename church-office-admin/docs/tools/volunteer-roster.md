# Volunteer roster export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The volunteer roster is their team export: person handle, ministry or role, and dates if they schedule. Typical offices use Planning Center Services or a workbook. You do not add a name. You do not decide clearance. Background-date columns copy as they stored them. No scheduler password belongs here. This tool starts volunteer-roster and background-dates packs. Checks often look for volunteer and ministry. Min necessary on hallway prints. Blank roles stay blank and flagged.
