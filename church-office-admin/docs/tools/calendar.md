# Room and building calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The calendar file is their room, wedding, funeral, and event holds: date, room, and who they named. Typical offices export Planning Center Calendar or a sheet. You copy holds. You do not invent a booking. If two calendars disagree, the pack quotes both. No calendar-login password belongs here. This tool starts the week-calendar pack and supports facility work when a request must match a hold. Checks often look for date and room.
