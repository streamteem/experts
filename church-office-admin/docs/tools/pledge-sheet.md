# Pledge sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The pledge sheet is their commitment or estimate-of-giving file: household handle, pledged amount, period, fund if they split, and received if they already joined it. You copy those numbers. You do not invent a pledge from last year. You do not send a reminder campaign. This tool starts pledge-versus-received. Joins to the giving export use their ids. Anonymous pledges stay as they coded them. No password belongs here. Checks often look for pledge and received when they asked for fulfillment.
