# Count sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The count sheet is their signed cash-and-check total for a batch or Sunday. You copy amounts and signatures. You do not recount. You do not store check account numbers. Variances versus the giving export stay listed. This tool supports giving-batch-from-export. Dual-control signatures compare to their process file, not to a textbook. Empty sheet: ask before you treat the software export as the only count. They deposit. You list the sheet next to the export. Checks look for batch and amount when the workflow asks.
