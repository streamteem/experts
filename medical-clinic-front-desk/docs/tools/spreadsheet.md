# Clinic spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Wait-list, referral log, records-release log, no-show, sliding assignment, or day-close workbooks if they live outside the EHR. Their columns win. Export to CSV for checks when you can. Do not add a no-show-fee column or a CPT column they do not have. Do not invent statuses. Prefer chart number over stacked identifiers. Card numbers, full SSNs, and income dollars stay out. If two sheets disagree with an EHR export, quote both and ask. Starter / guess until they teach which workbooks *this* clinic actually uses.
