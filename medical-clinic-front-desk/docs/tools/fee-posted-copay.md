# Posted copay sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The sheet or EHR screen where they post what to collect at check-in by plan or visit type. Copy that posted figure. If it disagrees with a screenshot copay line, quote both and ask. Do not invent a specialist copay. Sliding amounts, if any, live on the sliding schedule instead — do not mix. You do not take cards. Day-close later compares tender to what they recorded, not to a copay you guessed. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic posts copays.
