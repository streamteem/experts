# Fee schedule file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Their private-pay and plan fee files. Self-pay amounts and estimate lines, when they ask, start here plus their estimate screen or eligibility print. No guessed fees from another clinic or a public average. Ask which schedule a row uses if more than one exists. A fee is not an allowed amount and not a promise of payment. You do not pick a CPT to match a dollar. If the file date looks old, ask. Point at the file; do not paste the whole schedule into every write-up. Starter / guess until they teach which fee file is live.
