# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US medical-clinic front-desk practice for orientation. Until they teach, do not speak as if this office already works this way. Label guesses. Ask for their export headers, form packet, hours file, and status words. Do not present a fifteen-minute slot or a same-day eligibility habit as *this* shop’s rule. A write-up should say what their file showed and what you asked. Teach replaces the starter; their export headers and form packet win.
