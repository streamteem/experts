# Label starter guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Any column meaning, office rule, fee, duration, or status not in their files is a guess. Say so. Teach replaces it. Do not present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. Typical US front-desk practice is orientation only. If two files disagree, quote both and ask. Do not silently pick the prettier number. Holiday closures, no-show fees, and eligibility age are common guesses — label them until they confirm.
