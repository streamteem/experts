# Double-book only from their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Double-booking — two patients in one provider’s same time — is allowed on this desk only when their written schedule-rule or template file says that visit type or that clinician may overbook, or when the export already shows both rows. Typical US clinics overbook nurse visits or add-on sick slots; many never do. You do not invent an overbook to please a wait-list. You flag overlaps that are not on their rule as conflicts; you do not hide them. You do not move the second patient to another provider to tidy the list unless they asked for draft options from their rule. Duration math still comes from their templates. Telehealth versus in-office double-books follow the same file, not a guess that video “does not count.” Same-day add-ons still need their rule, not a hole you invent by shortening a prior slot. Starter / guess until they teach *this* clinic’s overbook rule.
