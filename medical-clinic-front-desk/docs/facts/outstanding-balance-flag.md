# Outstanding-balance flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An outstanding-balance flag on a day list or registration row is a copied ledger fact: they have a balance on the account as of the export, sometimes with a bucket. Typical US clinics show it so staff can follow their collection script. You copy the flag or the amount their export shows. You do not invent a collect-at-check-in amount beyond what they posted. You do not waive. You do not refuse care as a clinical decision. You do not send a collections letter. Insurance pending versus patient balance stay split if their file splits them. Family guarantor totals stay at the export’s level. Do not re-age a row. Card numbers stay out. Prefer chart or account number. A payment-plan row, if present, is additional context from their plan sheet, not a reason to hide the balance. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic wants balances shown on the day list.
