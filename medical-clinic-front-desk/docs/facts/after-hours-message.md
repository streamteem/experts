# After-hours message as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

After-hours calls and answering-service messages in a typical US clinic become a log: time, caller label they allow, reason wording as taken, and which on-call person their roster named. You list from their answering-service email, voicemail transcript they saved, or on-call sheet. You do not triage as the clinician. You do not tell anyone it can wait. Emergency wording flags staff now. You do not diagnose. You do not prescribe. You do not invent the on-call name from last month’s roster if today’s file is missing — ask. Do not copy extra clinical detail into docs/ beyond what the log already stores for the morning huddle. Hours files still define when “after hours” starts; if the hours file is missing, ask. Do not invent an on-call phone from memory and do not store that number as a password or personal cell in docs/. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic files after-hours messages.
