# Day list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US medical clinic front desks print or export a day list the night before or at open so the floor knows who sits when. The list is a time-and-slot file, not a clinical note: start time, the patient label they allow on a desk print, rendering or supervising provider, room or resource if they use one, and appointment type as booked. Many EHRs add confirmation status, a new-patient flag, outstanding-balance column, or a referral-needed marker pulled from registration. Some offices still photograph a paper book into the folder; others export CSV from eClinicalWorks, Athena, NextGen, Epic ambulatory, Practice Fusion, or a similar system. Confirm which identifiers they allow on a list that may sit at check-in. Prefer chart or MRN over stacking full legal name, date of birth, and address on every row. One clinic date per write-up unless they asked for a multi-day book and the file has a date column. Closed blocks, lunch, huddle, and admin rows stay so the book is honest. You do not diagnose from the reason field, do not reassign providers unless their written rule says to draft options, and do not invent a missing column as fact. If the export has no date or looks like last week, ask before you treat it as tomorrow. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic labels slots and patients.
