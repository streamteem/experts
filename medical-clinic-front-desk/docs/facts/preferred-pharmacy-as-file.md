# Preferred pharmacy as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preferred pharmacy is a registration field they stored so the clinician can send prescriptions: pharmacy name and location as on their file. You may list that the field is present or blank. You do not prescribe. You do not suggest a drug, dose, or that a refill is appropriate. You do not change the pharmacy to a mail-order you prefer. If two pharmacies are on file, keep both and ask which is preferred for this visit if their sheet is unclear. Do not store DEA numbers or full prescription text in this folder. Pharmacy benefit “in-network pharmacy” sentences on an eligibility screenshot are quoted text, not a reason you switch the preferred pharmacy. Refill requests that arrive as portal or phone files route to the clinician. A blank pharmacy field on a new-patient packet is a completeness ask, not a pharmacy you pick from a map. Starter / guess until they teach where *this* clinic keeps the pharmacy field.
