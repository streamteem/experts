# Sliding visit type

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sliding schedules, when they exist, often apply only to named visit types: medical visit, dental if they have it, lab they listed, or not to extra retail items. Copy the visit type on the appointment and the types listed on their sliding schedule. If the booked type is not on the schedule, say it is not listed — do not invent a slide for a procedure or a specialist visit they excluded. You still do not pick CPT to move the visit into a sliding type. Same-day add-on types need their rule. Sliding-not-invent still applies to the dollar. New-versus-established does not by itself create a slide. If they have no visit-type column on the sliding file, ask which types slide. Telehealth versus in-office of the same medical type still follows their schedule, not a slide you invent because the visit was video. Starter / guess until they teach which appointment types *this* clinic slides.
