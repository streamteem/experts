# Workers’ compensation as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Workers’ compensation visits in a typical US clinic need a claim number, employer or carrier as they recorded, date of injury as on their form, and whether the carrier letter or adjuster note they require is in the folder. Completeness only. You do not decide compensability. You do not give a legal opinion that the injury is work-related. You do not choose work-status restrictions as the clinician. Copy work-status forms as present or missing after the clinician already completed them — you do not fill restrictions. Billing a WC carrier versus the health plan is their billing rule file, not a coverage promise you invent. If the claim number is missing, ask; do not invent one. Do not copy extra incident narrative into docs/. Photo ID and employer cards follow minimum necessary. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic flags WC appointments and which forms they require before the slot.
