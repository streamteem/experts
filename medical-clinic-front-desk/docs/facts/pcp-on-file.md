# PCP on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Primary-care provider on file is the name they recorded in registration or that the eligibility screenshot printed as PCP or attributed provider. Typical US plans care about this for referrals and some copays. Copy both if they disagree and ask which the clinic treats as live — do not silently pick. You do not decide the patient must change PCP. You do not invent that this clinic is the PCP because the patient booked here. Referral-in and referral-out paperwork may need the PCP name as already on the form. You still do not choose CPT. Least PHI: name as stored, not the PCP’s home address. If the field is blank, say blank. Do not change the PCP field to make a referral form look complete. A screenshot that names a different PCP is a question for staff, not a coverage opinion you issue. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic updates PCP when a screenshot differs from registration.
