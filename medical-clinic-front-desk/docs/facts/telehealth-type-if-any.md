# Telehealth type if they use one

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Telehealth on a typical US clinic book is an appointment type or place-of-service label they already created: video, audio-only, or e-visit as they named it. Copy the type. You do not decide the visit is clinically appropriate for video. You do not pick a telehealth CPT. You do not promise the plan covers telehealth — that sentence comes only from a dated eligibility screenshot or their written telehealth-billing note. Consent-for-telehealth forms are completeness. You do not run the video product. Passwords to Zoom or the EHR waiting room stay out of this folder. If the type is in-office on the book but someone asked to switch to video, that is a reschedule they approve, not a silent type change. After-hours portal messages are not automatically telehealth visits. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic labels and consents telehealth.
