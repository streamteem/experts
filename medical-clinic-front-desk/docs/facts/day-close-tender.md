# Day-close tender

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-close tender is their end-of-day list of what was collected: cash, check, card-batch total as their merchant or EHR report shows, and sometimes other tenders they named. Typical US clinic desks also tie copay counts to the day list. You copy amounts and tender types from their close report or drawer sheet. You do not take cards. You do not store PAN or check account numbers. You do not send a deposit or move money. Short-or-over versus their expected total is a flag from their math, not a silent fix. Insurance posting is not tender. Sliding or write-off adjustments stay on the adjustment report, not mixed into cash as if collected. One clinic date and one location per close pack unless they asked otherwise. If the close file is missing, ask; do not invent a batch total from copay guesses. Starter / guess until they teach which close report *this* clinic runs.
