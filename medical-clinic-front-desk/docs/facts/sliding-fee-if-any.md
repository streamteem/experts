# Sliding-fee schedule if they have one

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some US clinics — especially FQHC and look-alike or charity-care desks — keep a sliding-fee or discount schedule by income band and visit type. If they have that file, you may list the band they already assigned and the visit-type price they posted for that band. If they have no sliding file, say so; do not invent a federal poverty-level table or a discount percent. HRSA sliding-fee pages are orientation for clinics that use that program, not a schedule you apply to a private practice that never asked. Assignment of a patient to a band is their process after they reviewed documents they collect — you do not assign the band from a story. Visit types that are not on the sliding schedule stay off it. You still do not diagnose or choose CPT to reach a cheaper band. Copay-versus-sliding is their posted rule. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* clinic slides and which file is live.
