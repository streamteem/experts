# Do not invent a slide

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A sliding discount that is not on their dated schedule, or a patient not yet assigned to a band on their sliding log, is not a number you invent to be kind. Typical US desks get asked to “just take twenty percent off.” That is their write-off or charity code, not a slide you create. You list the posted sliding amount only when both the schedule file and their assignment for this patient and visit type exist. Missing assignment: ask. Expired sliding application on their date rule: say the dates and ask. You do not apply another clinic’s poverty guidelines. You do not mix a commercial copay screenshot with a slide to invent a lower collect. Courtesy write-offs stay their adjustment code. This fact exists so packs stay honest when someone wants a discount that is not in the folder. Starter / guess until they teach *this* clinic’s charity versus sliding codes.
