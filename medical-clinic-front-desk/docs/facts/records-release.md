# Records-release log

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Records release at this desk is a log: request date, requester type as they coded (patient, another clinic, payer, attorney), ROI present or missing, what was requested as their form states, what was sent as they recorded, date sent, and fee if their policy file names one. Typical US clinics track this in the EHR or a workbook. You list completeness and status as their log shows. You do not release records yourself. You do not decide what is “the designated record set” as counsel. HHS access-right pages are orientation; their process file wins. You do not copy the chart into docs/ to fulfill a request. Patient-directed access versus a third-party request stay as they labeled them if the log splits those. Missing ROI on a third-party request: ask, do not ship. Fees come from their posted records-fee sheet, not a remembered per-page guess. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic logs releases.
