# Payment plan as their sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Payment plans at a typical US SMB clinic live on a workbook or EHR payment-plan screen: chart or account, starting balance as they posted, installment amount, frequency, and start date as they agreed. You list those fields from their sheet. You do not invent terms. You do not run the card. You do not store PAN. You do not threaten collections. Missed-installment flags stay as they coded them. A plan is not a waiver of the rest of the balance unless their adjustment file says so. If they have no payment-plan sheet, say so — do not draft legal installment language. Family or guarantor accounts stay at the level their sheet uses. Outstanding-balance flags on the day list are separate from whether a plan exists; quote both if they asked. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic writes payment plans.
