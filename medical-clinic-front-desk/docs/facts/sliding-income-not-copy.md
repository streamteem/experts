# Sliding income figures stay off work files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Income used to place a sliding band is sensitive. Typical US sliding desks still see pay stubs and tax forms at the window. Your work file should not become a second copy of those numbers. Record that a named document type is present, dated, and matches their checklist — not the dollar amounts, not the employer’s full payroll print, and not a full SSN from the tax form. They (or their sliding officer) compute the band. You do not compute federal poverty level. You do not paste W-2 wages into markdown. If they asked for a completeness CSV, use document-type and yes-or-no columns. If a scan is in the folder for them, point at the filename; do not transcribe it. This is minimum necessary applied to financials they collect only for sliding. Starter / guess until they teach who at *this* clinic may see the income packet.
