# Self-pay as their posted path

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Self-pay on a typical US clinic book means they coded the visit as no insurance billed, or insurance not used, and a posted self-pay fee or time-of-service amount from their fee-schedule or self-pay sheet. Copy that posted amount. You do not invent a cash-pay discount. You do not pick CPT to build a self-pay ticket. A superbill they already generated may be what they hand the patient; you still do not create codes. Sliding-fee, if any, is a different file — do not mix a slide you invented with self-pay. Prompt-pay discounts exist only if their sheet says so. You do not take card numbers into this folder. You do not send the patient a payment link as a product. If they asked for an estimate, copy their estimate screen or fee-file lines they pointed to, labeled not a coverage quote. Starter / guess until they teach *this* clinic’s self-pay sheet and when they use it.
