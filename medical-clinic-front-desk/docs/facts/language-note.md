# Language note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preferred language on a typical US registration file is a scheduling and interpreter-flag fact: the language they recorded, and whether their interpreter process is already marked as needed. Copy the language as stored. Do not guess language from a surname. Do not write that the patient “speaks enough English.” HHS limited-English-proficiency pages are orientation, not a legal opinion you issue about what the clinic must provide. You do not hire an interpreter as this product. You flag the row for staff when their sheet says interpreter needed and the day’s booking does not yet show one — still their call. Do not put extra family-member names into docs/ beyond what the registration row already uses for the flag. Written materials in another language are present or missing as files they keep; you do not translate clinical content. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic records language and interpreter need.
