# Imaging disc or file routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Imaging at this desk is routing: whether a disc, USB, portal download they saved, or film jacket they logged is present, labeled with the chart they allow, and destined to the clinician or the outside facility as their process says. Typical US clinics still see CDs from hospitals. You do not read the images. You do not describe what the scan shows. You do not decide the study is diagnostic. You do not pick CPT for the imaging. Prior-auth paperwork for imaging is a separate completeness file. If the disc is unlabeled or the name does not match the chart, ask — do not guess. Do not copy radiology reports into docs/ when “report PDF present” will do. Lost-disc logs stay as their incident process. You do not upload to a PACS product as this Expert unless they taught that exact file-drop step. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic tracks incoming imaging media.
