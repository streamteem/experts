# Photo ID, minimum necessary

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US clinics ask for a photo ID at new-patient intake or when names do not match the insurance card. Your work is a present-or-missing flag and, if they need a label, last four of an ID only if they asked — never the full driver’s-license number, never a scan transcribed into markdown. Do not store the image in docs/ if their process only needed a yes at check-in and the image already lives in their EHR. Do not use ID numbers to tell two family members apart in a write-up; use chart numbers. Mismatched name between ID and registration is a question for staff, not a coverage opinion. You do not decide identity as a legal finding. Minors may have a guardian ID on file instead — present or missing. Starter / guess until they teach when *this* clinic requires photo ID and what may appear on a desk list.
