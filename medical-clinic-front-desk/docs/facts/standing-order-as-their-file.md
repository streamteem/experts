# Standing order as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A standing order is a clinician-signed protocol file they already stored: named tests, vaccines, or nurse actions that may occur without a new signed order each time, within the four corners of that file. You may list that the standing-order PDF is present, dated, and signed as their process requires, and that a booked nurse visit type points at that file. You do not write a standing order. You do not add a test to it. You do not decide the patient meets the protocol. You do not diagnose from the protocol’s indication list. Expired standing-order dates on their rule: say the date and ask. Missing file: ask, do not invent the protocol from another clinic. Lab or vaccine lists still are not orders you place. Starter / guess until they teach which standing-order PDFs *this* clinic treats as live.
