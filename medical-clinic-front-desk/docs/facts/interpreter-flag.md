# Interpreter flag

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An interpreter flag on the day list or registration row means they already marked that the visit needs language access. Typical US clinics use staff interpreters, a vendor line, or a video cart — which one is on their process file, not a vendor you hire. You copy the flag and the language they stored. You do not cancel the visit because the flag is set. You do not say the patient can get by without an interpreter. You do not diagnose through a family member. If the flag is set and the day’s booking does not show an interpreter resource they usually schedule, flag that gap for staff. Do not put extra household names into docs/. HHS LEP pages are orientation, not a legal stamp. After-hours interpreter need still follows their on-call process. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic books interpreters against slots.
