# Lab order routing, not the result

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lab work at this desk is routing of an order or requisition they already placed: lab name, patient label, order date, whether the paper or electronic requisition is in the folder, and whether the specimen was marked collected as their log says. You do not interpret results. You do not say a value is normal or high. You do not add tests. You do not diagnose from a pending order. If a result PDF landed in the folder, you may log that a named result file is present for staff — you still do not quote values into docs/ unless they asked only for a filename-present flag. Standing laboratory orders are their file, not an order you write. Missing requisition on a booked lab-only slot: ask. You do not call the reference lab as a product. Do not copy extra clinical indication narrative into the work sheet. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic logs lab send-outs versus in-house draws.
