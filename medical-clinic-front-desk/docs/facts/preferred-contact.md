# Preferred contact

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preferred contact is the method they stored: phone, SMS, email, portal, or mail. Typical US clinic registration has a preferred number and a consent-to-message flag. Copy those for lists they will use to reach the patient. You do not send the message. We do not sell a messaging product. Do not invent that a reminder went out because confirmation is blank. Extra numbers and emails the task does not need stay out of the write-up — minimum necessary. Do not store passwords to the patient portal. A portal message they exported as a file is a different fact; this field is only the preference label. If preferred contact is blank, say blank; do not pick mobile versus home. Bad-number flags on their sheet stay as written. Do not use a work number from an old card image if the registration row disagrees — quote both and ask. Starter / guess until they teach which contact fields *this* clinic treats as live.
