# Guardian and minor paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Minors on a typical US clinic book need a guardian or accompanying-adult process they already wrote: who may consent, who may pick up records, and which forms the guardian signs. Copy their relationship and form-completeness flags. You do not interpret a custody order. You do not decide who has legal authority if two adults disagree — that is staff plus their counsel process. A custody-order PDF in the folder is present or missing; you do not summarize the order into docs/. Adolescent confidentiality flags they already coded stay as coded; you do not invent state minor-consent law. Preferred contact for the guardian versus the teen follows their registration fields. Intake and NPP acknowledgments for minors follow their packet, with guardian signature present or missing. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic books minors and who must be present.
