# Blocks and huddle rows

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closed blocks, lunch, huddle, admin, meeting, and catch-up rows belong on the day list so the book is honest. Typical US clinics hold huddle in the first slot or at midday. Copy block reason as they labeled it. Do not fill a huddle block with a patient to hide unused time unless their written rule says the block is releasable and they asked. Do not delete lunch to make utilization look high. Provider-column still applies: a block on one clinician is not a hole on another unless they said so. Calendar-blocks files and the EHR export should be compared when both exist; if they disagree, quote both and ask. Double-book rules do not silently eat a huddle row. One date per list. If the blocks file has no date, ask before you treat a recurring lunch as tomorrow’s close. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic names and releases blocks.
