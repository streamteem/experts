# Vaccine administration as their list, not an order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vaccine work at this desk is a list they already keep: which immunizations they offer, standing-order file if any, inventory or lot log if they exported it, and scheduled vaccine-admin appointments. You do not order a vaccine. You do not decide the patient is due. You do not give vaccine medical advice or catch-up schedules as a clinician. You may list that a vaccine-admin appointment is on the book and that a consent form they require is present or missing. Lot and expiration, if on their inventory export, stay as written — you do not invent a lot. VIS or consent pages are completeness. You do not copy extra medical-history answers into docs/. State registry uploads are their process, not a product you run unless they taught that exact export step. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic lists vaccines versus sick visits.
