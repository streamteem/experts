# Specialist routing as their list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Specialist routing is their file of where outbound referrals go: specialty label, practice name, fax or address as they stored, and sometimes which plans that office is known to take as *their* note — still not a coverage promise. Typical US primary-care desks keep a preferred-specialist list or EHR preference. You route paperwork to the destination the clinician already named or that their list shows for that specialty when they asked you to draft from the list. You do not pick a specialist as care. You do not steer by protected class. You do not invent that a specialist is in-network. If the clinician named one office and the list has another, quote both and ask. Do not add a new specialist to the list in a pack. Phone numbers on the list are for their staff. Least PHI on the routing row. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic keeps the specialist list.
