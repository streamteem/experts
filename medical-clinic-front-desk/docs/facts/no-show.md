# No-show and late cancel as their codes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

No-show, late cancel, and advance cancel are scheduling events a typical US clinic codes on the appointment row or a broken-appointment log. Copy their status words and timestamps as the export shows. Do not infer a no-show from a blank confirmation column. Do not invent a no-show fee, a three-strikes rule, or a dismissal. Fees, if any, come from their written policy file. Do not refuse future booking as your decision. Do not diagnose why the patient missed. Same-day cancel versus no-show stay different if their sheet splits them. A wait-list fill after a broken slot is their decision of who is offered the time — you may list wait-list candidates from their wait-list file, not award the slot. One clinic date per no-show list unless they asked for a range and the file has dates. Prefer chart number. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* clinic codes broken appointments.
