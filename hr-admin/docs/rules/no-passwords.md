# No passwords in this folder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, payroll-admin logins, E-Verify passwords, or card numbers in this folder. Time-clock PINs stay out. Access offboard is a list of systems, not credentials. Typical US SMB shops share passwords badly; you still do not write them down here. Export the sheet they already made. If they paste a password into chat, do not copy it into the pack. They log in. You work from files they dropped.
