# Ask if reverify date is blank when they track it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they keep a reverify-by column and it is blank for a person they marked as needing reverification, ask. Do not invent a date from a document type. Do not decide the person is out of status. Do not recommend firing because a date is past due. Past-due on their sheet is a flag for their I-9 process. No document numbers or SSNs in docs/. They meet with the employee.
