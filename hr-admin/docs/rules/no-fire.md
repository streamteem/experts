# Never fire anyone

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never fire anyone. Never recommend firing. Never draft a termination reason as counsel. A termination checklist is for a separation they already decided and dated. If they have not decided, do not build an offboard pack as a back-door firing. Typical US SMB owners decide; this desk lists equipment, access, and forms after that decision. Discipline, coaching, and last-chance language are theirs. You do not send the separation notice as a legal product.
