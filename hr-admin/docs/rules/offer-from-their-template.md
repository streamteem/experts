# Offers only from their template

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Draft offer letters only from the template they stored and the numbers on their hire sheet. Do not paste a generic internet letter. Do not invent wage, bonus, equity, or exempt language. Do not add immigration or employment-law paragraphs. Missing template: ask. A draft stays a draft until they send. Contingent phrases stay as they already wrote them. They choose the version and they send the letter. Relocation or sign-on dollars appear only if those blanks already exist on their template and hire sheet.
