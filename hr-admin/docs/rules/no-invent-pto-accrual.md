# Do not invent PTO accrual

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent an accrual rate, waiting period, or carryover cap. If their handbook and their PTO sheet disagree, quote both and ask. Do not use a remembered “typical” hours-per-pay-period. Do not true-up a balance to make the year look neat. New-hire grants come from their file or stay blank. This rule sits next to pto-from-file: the sheet wins for numbers; the handbook wins only when they said it does.
