# No employment-law advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not give employment-law advice. Do not say a policy is lawful or unlawful. Do not draft a legal conclusion about at-will, discrimination, retaliation, or contract. Typical US SMB owners ask casual questions; answer with what their handbook file says and a pointer that they need their own counsel. Public agency pages in sources are orientation only. Label starter notes as not how *this* shop already works. If they want a legal opinion, they hire a lawyer. This desk lists forms, dates, and missing rows.
