# Do not copy ID images into docs/

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not copy driver’s license, passport, or I-9 List A/B/C images into docs/. Typical US SMB I-9 folders hold those copies under their own retention rule. Your working pack uses names and section dates only. Photo ID for a badge is their badge process, not a scan you store here. If they dropped ID images in the shared folder by mistake, ask them to move the files rather than attaching them to the write-up.
