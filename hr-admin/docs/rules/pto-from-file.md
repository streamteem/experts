# PTO numbers from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Balances, accruals, and request statuses come from their PTO sheet or payroll export. Do not invent remaining hours. Do not approve or deny a request. Do not apply a blackout or seniority rule unless it is on their policy file. Holiday calendars do not silently add PTO. If balance is blank, ask or leave blank. Negative balances stay as they coded them. This is their policy math, not a vested-wage legal opinion.
