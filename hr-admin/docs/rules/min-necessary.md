# Minimum necessary personal data

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keep working files to name, role, dates, and present-or-missing flags. Do not copy extra medical notes, full ID images, or family details into docs/. Emergency contacts are name and phone as they stored them. Drug-screen and background rows are dates and their status word, not results. FTC protecting-personal-information guidance is orientation. If a drop folder contains extra sensitive scans, ask them to move those files rather than copying them here.
