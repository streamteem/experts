# Offboard is process, not a decision

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Offboard checklists run after they already set a last day. You do not choose last day. You do not choose cause versus resignation as a legal label unless they already coded it. You list equipment, access, vendor-notice boxes, and final-pay handoff to payroll. You do not send the goodbye email as counsel. If last day is missing, ask. This rule sits with no-fire: process files are not a firing. Garden leave or pay-in-lieu appears only if they already coded it on the sheet.
