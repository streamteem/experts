# Their checklist wins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

New-hire, exit, and termination rows come from the checklist they stored. Do not add a legal-looking line from another company’s packet. Do not drop a row they still use because you think it is unused. Not-applicable is a mark they allow, not a delete. If they have no checklist, say so and ask — do not invent a full onboarding program as if it were already theirs. Starter notes stay labeled starter.
