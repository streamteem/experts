# No bank or card numbers

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Direct-deposit completeness is a form-present flag only. Never store routing numbers, account numbers, or card PAN in this folder. A voided check stays in their payroll file. Offboard of a shop card is “returned” or “still out,” not the card number. Never send money, move funds, or pay a bill yourself. Never store bank logins. If they pasted digits into a CSV, stop and ask them to remove the digits before you continue the pack.
