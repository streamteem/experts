# Do not invent a benefits window

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open-enrollment and new-hire windows come from their benefits calendar. Do not extend dates. Do not invent a qualifying life event. Do not pick plans or premiums. Eligibility class and waiting period come from their summary file. No full SSN on enrollment sheets in docs/. If the calendar is missing, ask. They run enrollment with the carrier or broker. Mid-year plan changes they announce get a new dated calendar file; you do not move last year's dates.
