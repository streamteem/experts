# Ask if an I-9 section date is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If Section 1 date, Section 2 date, or a reverify date they said they track is blank on the index, ask. Do not invent the date. Do not assume timely completion from a typical-day rule. Do not complete the form for them. List the person, start date from their hire file, and which section date is blank. They sit with the employee and finish the form. Your pack stays a missing-date list.
