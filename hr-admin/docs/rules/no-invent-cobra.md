# Do not invent COBRA

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent COBRA applicability, election periods, or premium amounts. List only the vendor-notified date or checklist tick they wrote. Shops with no group plan get no invented COBRA row. Do not store SSN on a notice copy in docs/. DOL COBRA pages are orientation. They and the benefits vendor send notices. You check their offboard box only. A reduction in hours uses the same rule: only their checklist tick, never a coverage opinion you write from memory.
