# Do not change wage

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not raise, cut, or “correct” pay. Quote the offer letter, change-of-pay form, or payroll export. If those disagree, list both and ask. Do not add a bonus that is not on the file. Do not convert salary to hourly for a legal test. They set pay. This desk copies the number and the period as written. Market-rate memories are not a wage file. Retro pay is a payroll file they already made, not a number you back-calculate.
