# Workers' compensation poster as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Workers' compensation notice on this desk is a poster or PDF they stored, plus a present-or-missing flag for the break-room pack. Typical US SMB shops hang a state poster and keep a scan. You record the file name, the date they wrote on the scan if any, and whether the poster index says it is posted. You do not tell them which state poster is required. You do not give a claim-eligibility opinion. An incident is routed on the incident file, not decided here. DOL workers' compensation topic pages are orientation; state files they saved win. Missing poster on their index is an ask, not a citation you write. This Expert lists poster presence. They post and they file claims with their carrier. Starter until they teach where *this* shop hangs and scans posters. A carrier claim-kit PDF is a different file from the wall poster; keep the two names separate on the index.
