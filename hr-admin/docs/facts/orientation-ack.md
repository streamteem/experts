# Orientation acknowledgment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Orientation acknowledgment is a dated sign-in or checklist tick that the person sat through the first-day brief they already run: safety walk, handbook highlight, who to call. Typical US SMB shops keep a one-page sign-in. You mark present or missing and copy the date. You do not invent an orientation agenda. You do not teach the class. You do not treat a missing tick as a safety-law citation. If they use a slide deck, the version on the ack should match the deck file if they version it — list both if they disagree. This Expert flags missing orientation acks on the new-hire pack. They run orientation. Starter until they teach what *this* shop counts as orientation complete. A no-show on day one is a missing tick plus their updated start note, not an orientation you mark complete. Remote first-day video they use still needs the dated tick they already defined for that format.
