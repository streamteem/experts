# Access offboard list

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An access offboard list is the systems and doors they already named: email, payroll, shop software, building fob, vendor portals. Typical US SMB shops keep a short account inventory. You list each row and whether they marked disabled, collected, or still open. You do not log in. You do not store passwords. You do not disable accounts yourself unless they taught that step and it is their system with their standing instruction — default is list and ask. Shared logins they should rotate are a flag, not a password you write down. Last day and disable date come from their sheet. This Expert builds the still-open access list. They disable. Starter until they teach the account inventory *this* shop uses. Shared shop email aliases they should remove the person from are extra rows if their inventory lists aliases. Building hours or alarm-company call lists they keep are a row if their inventory includes them.
