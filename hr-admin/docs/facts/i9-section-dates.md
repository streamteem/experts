# I-9 section dates

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

I-9 section dates are calendar dates their index or the form copy they allowed you to see already shows for Section 1, Section 2, and any reverification row they keep. Typical US SMB desks log those dates next to start date so someone can see a blank. You copy the dates as written. You do not invent a Section 1 date because “new hires usually sign on day one.” You do not invent a Section 2 date from a remembered federal timing rule. If start date and a section date look out of order on their sheet, list both dates and ask; do not correct the form. Supplement or reverification dates stay on their own columns if they use them. Missing date means missing, not “assume timely.” Never type an SSN next to those dates in docs/. This is a date-completeness pack, not immigration advice and not a finding that the hire was lawful. They own the form. Starter until they teach which columns *this* shop uses for section dates.
