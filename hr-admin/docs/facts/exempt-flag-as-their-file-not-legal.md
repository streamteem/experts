# Exempt flag as their file (not legal)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Exempt versus nonexempt is a flag on their hire sheet, payroll export, or offer template — a label they already chose. Typical US SMB payroll systems store that checkbox. You copy the flag. You do not reclassify a job from duties you read. You do not say the salary meets a federal or state threshold. You do not give a wage-hour legal opinion. If the flag is blank, ask; do not infer from job title. DOL Wage and Hour pages are orientation only. A mismatch between title and flag is a question for them and their advisor, not a ruling you write. This Expert lists the flag as stored. They and counsel own classification. Starter until they teach where *this* shop stores the exempt flag. A later change of flag needs their change-of-status form; do not flip the checkbox because duties 'look managerial.'
