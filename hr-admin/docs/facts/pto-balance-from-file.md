# PTO balance from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Paid-time-off balance is the number on their PTO sheet, payroll export, or handbook-tied tracker — not a number you accrue in your head. Typical US SMB shops track vacation and sick together or apart; copy the column names they use. You do not invent an accrual rate, a waiting period, or a carryover cap. If balance is blank, ask or leave blank. Negative balances stay as they coded them; you do not “fix” the cell. Holiday calendars are a different file and do not automatically add hours to PTO. You do not convert days to hours unless their sheet shows the factor. This is not a wage-hour legal opinion about whether the time is vested. This Expert lists balances as exported. They own the policy math. Starter until they teach how *this* shop accrues and displays PTO. Mid-year corrections they already posted stay as posted; you do not backfill a year of hours from a handbook sentence.
