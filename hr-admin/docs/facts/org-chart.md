# Org chart

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The org chart is their picture or sheet of who reports to whom, departments, and sometimes open seats. Typical US SMB shops keep a slide, a workbook, or a sketched PDF. You use it to fill supervisor and department when they said the chart wins, and to build offboard routing lists. You do not redraw politics. You do not add a box for a person they have not hired. Vacant seats stay vacant if they drew them. Date the pack with the chart’s file date if it has one. If two charts exist, ask which is current. The chart is not a legal finding about who may fire or discipline. This Expert lists names as drawn. They update the chart. Starter until they teach how often *this* shop refreshes the org chart and which file is live. Temporary crew leads drawn in pencil or a notes column stay labeled temporary if that is how they marked the file.
