# Handbook version

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Handbook version is the label on the PDF or print they stored: year, revision letter, or “effective” date. Typical US SMB handbooks change every few years and old acks linger. You record the version string exactly. Packs that compare acks to the live book use this string. You do not silently treat last year’s book as current. You do not write new handbook chapters. You do not interpret a policy as employment law. If two PDFs both look current, ask which wins. A draft handbook stays draft. This Expert matches version labels. They publish the book. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names handbook versions and where the live PDF lives. Inserts and one-page policy addenda they issued after the book keep their own version labels beside the main book string. Spanish or other-language books they issued keep a version string too; match ack language to the file they signed if they track that.
