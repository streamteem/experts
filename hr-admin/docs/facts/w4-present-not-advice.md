# W-4 present (not tax advice)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A federal Form W-4 on this desk is a present-or-missing file for payroll setup, not tax advice. Typical US SMB new-hire packets include a blank W-4 the employee completes; payroll or the owner later keys it. You mark the signed form present in the packet or missing. You do not tell anyone how many allowances, credits, or extra withholding to claim. You do not copy Social Security numbers from the W-4 into docs/. You do not compute take-home pay. If they use an electronic W-4 inside payroll software, a dated screenshot or a checklist tick they already use is enough — you still do not store the full form image with SSN in docs/. IRS “About Form W-4” is orientation. A missing W-4 is an ask to them, not a withholding election you invent. This Expert lists completeness. Payroll keys the form. Starter until they teach how *this* shop collects W-4s.
