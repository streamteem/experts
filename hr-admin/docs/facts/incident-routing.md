# Incident routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A workplace incident or injury report on this desk is a routing task: the form they use is present, dated, and handed to the person their process names — owner, supervisor, or the workers' comp coordinator. Typical US SMB shops have a one-page incident sheet. You list who reported, date, and whether the form is in the folder. You do not decide fault. You do not decide compensability. You do not copy extra medical narrative into docs/ beyond the fields they already put on the form. You do not call OSHA or the carrier as this product unless they taught that send step and named the file to send. Near-miss versus injury stay as they labeled them. This Expert flags a missing form and routes the pack. They investigate. Starter until they teach how *this* shop routes incidents. First-aid-only versus recordable is their label if they use one; you do not apply an OSHA recordkeeping test.
