# Work location

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Work location is the site, remote, or hybrid label on their hire sheet or roster. Typical US SMB shops have one shop floor and a small office; some add a second warehouse or a home-office flag. You copy the label they used. You do not invent a remote policy. You do not decide tax nexus or unemployment-state from a home address as counsel. If they need a state withholding form for a location, that is a present-or-missing form on their list, not a tax opinion. Multi-site people use the split they wrote. Address on a W-2 process stays in payroll, not duplicated with SSN here. This Expert lists location for posters, supervisors, and schedules. They assign sites. Starter until they teach the location list *this* shop uses. Traveling or job-site people use the location code they assigned for that week if their roster has one, not a site you guess.
