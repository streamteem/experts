# Supervisor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Supervisor or “reports to” comes from the offer, the org chart, or the hire sheet they stored. Typical US SMB shops write one name; dotted lines are extra and only if their chart shows them. You copy the name as written. You do not assign a manager because the department “usually reports to operations.” If the org chart and the offer disagree, quote both and ask. A vacant supervisor box stays vacant — do not park the person under the owner unless the file says so. Name changes after a reorg need an updated chart or a change note. This field is for routing PTO and equipment, not a legal finding about who may discipline. You do not decide reporting lines. They maintain the chart. Starter until they teach which file wins for supervisor on *this* shop’s roster. Interim or acting supervisor labels stay as they wrote them; you do not promote anyone on the pack.
