# Leave as their code (not FMLA opinion)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Leave of absence on this desk is a code or label they already use: personal, medical as they coded it, military as they coded it, or a vendor-leave file. Typical US SMB shops are not all FMLA-covered; you do not decide coverage. You copy the leave type, start, and expected end from their log. You do not say a leave is FMLA-protected or not. You do not compute job-restoration rights. DOL FMLA pages are orientation only. Medical details stay out of docs/ beyond the code they already stored. A missing medical note is present-or-missing on their checklist, not a diagnosis you read. Intermittent leave hours come from their time file, not from a guessed statute. This Expert lists leave rows as coded. They and their advisor decide leave law. Starter until they teach the leave codes *this* shop actually uses.
