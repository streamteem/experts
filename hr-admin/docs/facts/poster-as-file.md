# Labor poster as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Federal and state labor posters on this desk are files and a posted-or-not flag on their poster index. Typical US SMB shops hang a combined poster and keep a photo or PDF. You list poster name as they labeled it, source file, and the date they last checked the wall if they log that. You do not tell them which posters the law requires. You do not design a poster set as counsel. DOL poster pages are orientation for names they may already use. Missing on their index is an ask. You do not print and hang as this product unless they taught that step and named the file to print. This Expert lists the index. They post. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tracks poster files versus the physical wall. A laminated combo poster and a stack of single-topic printouts are both rows if their index lists both.
