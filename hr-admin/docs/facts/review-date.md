# Review date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A performance-review date is the date on their review calendar, anniversary field, or intro-end field. Typical US SMB shops review at ninety days and annually, or they skip formal reviews. You list the next due date from their sheet. You do not invent a cycle. You do not write the review scores. You do not tie a missed review to a firing recommendation. If they use hire-anniversary math, use the start date they named and their written offset; label the result as computed from that file. A skipped year stays skipped until they add a row. This Expert builds a due list. They hold the conversation. Starter until they teach how *this* shop schedules reviews. Self-review worksheets they use are present-or-missing forms, not scores you fill. A canceled review stays canceled until they add a new date. Skip-level or owner-only reviews stay on the calendar they wrote, not a cycle you invent from another shop.
