# Driver file if they use one

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If they have people who drive for the shop, they may keep a driver file: license-present flag, expiration, insurance certificate if they require one, and an MVR-ordered date as a date only. Typical US SMB shops that do not put people on the road have no such file — do not invent one. You list dates and present-or-missing. You do not copy license numbers or MVR results into docs/. You do not decide who is insurable. You do not give a DOT legal opinion. A missing MVR date on a shop that says they pull MVRs is a completeness ask. Personal-vehicle versus shop-vehicle flags stay as they wrote them. This Expert lists the driver-file index. They and their insurer decide driving. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop keeps a driver file. Personal-auto insurance they require for errands is a certificate-present flag, not a coverage opinion.
