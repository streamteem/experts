# Emergency contact

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Emergency contact is a name, relationship if they collect it, and a phone number the employee gave. Typical US SMB new-hire sheets have two contact slots. You copy what they recorded onto the checklist as present or missing. Use minimum necessary: do not copy extra family medical notes into docs/. Do not store an SSN on the contact row. A blank second contact is not an error unless their form requires two. Updating a contact is their form; you do not invent a spouse or parent from an old application. Preferred language or “do not call at work” notes stay as they wrote them. This file is for their after-hours process, not a medical record. This Expert flags a missing contact on the new-hire or annual-update list. They call in an emergency. Starter until they teach how *this* shop refreshes emergency contacts.
