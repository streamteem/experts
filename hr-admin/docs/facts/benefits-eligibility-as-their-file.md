# Benefits eligibility as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Benefits eligibility is the waiting period, class, and plan names on their benefits summary or handbook excerpt — not a coverage opinion. Typical US SMB shops have a first-of-the-month-after-sixty-days rule or none. You copy class (full-time as they defined it, part-time as they defined it) and the waiting-period sentence they stored. You do not decide full-time from hours you reconstruct. You do not promise a plan pays a claim. Hours that drive eligibility come from their time file if they use hours, not from a guessed statute. Missing summary: ask. This Expert dates “eligible as their file” on a new-hire pack. They and the carrier enroll. Starter until they teach the eligibility file *this* shop uses. Rehires use the waiting-period sentence they wrote for rehires, not a guessed bridge of old service. Variable-hour or seasonal classes use the class name they printed, not a guessed look-back period.
