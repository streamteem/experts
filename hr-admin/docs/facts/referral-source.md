# Referral source

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Referral source is the label they already put on the application or applicant sheet: walk-in, employee name, job board as they wrote it, school, or agency. Typical US SMB shops want a simple source column for later ads they decide. You copy the label. You do not invent a source to make a recruiting report look complete. You do not decide which source is “best.” Paid-board invoices are a different file and not a payment you make. Employee-referral bonuses come only from their bonus file if they have one — list the rule as written, do not invent a dollar. This Expert lists source as coded. They buy ads. Starter until they teach the source list *this* shop uses. Agency invoices, if present, are a file-present flag for AP, not a bill you pay. Career-fair or school-name sources stay as written even when the posting also ran online.
