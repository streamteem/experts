# Timekeeping setup as a checklist row

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Timekeeping setup is a checklist row: person added to the clock, app, or paper timesheet list they use. Typical US SMB shops use a biometric clock, a PIN, or a spreadsheet. You mark that they said the person is in the system — from their export or a supervisor note. You do not store PINs or clock-admin passwords. You do not invent a schedule. You do not decide whether time is compensable as wage-hour law. Missing from the clock export after start date is an ask. Salaried people may still have a row if they use exception time; copy their rule. This Expert flags missing setup. They add the login. Starter until they teach how *this* shop adds a person to timekeeping. A missed clock export after day one is still an ask even if paper timesheets exist, unless they said paper is the system.
