# Badge and keys issued

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Badge, fob, and key issue is a log: item, person, date out, date back. Typical US SMB shops keep a key-hook sheet. You list issued versus not issued from that log on the new-hire or offboard pack. You do not invent a key number. You do not hide a missing key on offboard. Door codes never go into docs/ — say “code issued by them” if that is their process. Lost-item rows stay visible. This is not a locksmith product and not a lockout. This Expert compares issue log to the person. They cut keys and they collect. Starter until they teach how *this* shop logs badges and keys. Spare keys they keep in the office stay on the office hook list, not assigned to a person you pick. Contractor or visitor badges they log separately stay off the employee issue list unless they said to merge.
