# Start date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Start date is the first-day date on their offer letter or hire sheet. Typical US SMB desks also store a hire date in payroll that sometimes differs from first day on site. You copy the date they asked you to use and name the source file. You do not invent a start date from a remembered two-week notice. You do not move a start date to make an I-9 timing story look tidy. If offer start and payroll hire date disagree, list both and ask. Orientation date is not automatically start date. A delayed start needs their updated letter or note; do not guess the new Monday. Rehires use the date on the new file, not the old tour, unless they wrote a bridging rule. This Expert dates rows from their paper. They pick the first day. Starter until they teach how *this* shop distinguishes offer date, start date, and payroll hire date.
