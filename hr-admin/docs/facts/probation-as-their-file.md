# Probation / intro period as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An introductory or probation period is only what their handbook, offer, or policy file already prints — length and any review date they wrote. Typical US SMB shops mention ninety days; many do not. You copy the length and the end date if they computed it, or you compute the calendar end only from the start date plus the length on their file and you label the math. You do not invent a ninety-day rule. You do not say probation removes legal rights. You do not fire or extend probation. If handbook and offer disagree on length, quote both and ask. A review meeting is their calendar, not an automatic termination. This Expert dates the intro window from their paper. They decide what happens at the end. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop uses an intro period and which file states it.
