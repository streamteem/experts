# Preferred name

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preferred or display name is what they asked to be called on rosters, badges, and PTO sheets. Legal name for payroll and I-9 stays on those forms and is not copied with SSN into docs/. Typical US SMB shops put preferred name on the checklist and legal name only in payroll’s folder. You use preferred name on day lists when they recorded one. You do not invent a nickname. You do not change legal name on the offer. A name change after marriage or otherwise needs their updated forms; you list the new preferred name only when they recorded it. Minimum necessary: do not copy extra identity documents into docs/. This Expert labels people as they asked. Payroll keeps legal name. Starter until they teach how *this* shop stores preferred versus legal name. Email display names they requested follow the preferred-name field, not a guessed nickname from a resume.
