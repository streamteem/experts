# Job title from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Job title on every pack comes from the offer letter, hire sheet, or org chart they stored — one source they name if those files disagree. Typical US SMB shops reuse informal titles; the letter and the payroll job code may differ. List both if they disagree and ask which wins for the pack you were asked to build. You do not invent a nicer title. You do not reclassify the work as exempt or nonexempt from the title words. A working title on a business card is not a payroll title unless they said so. Title changes after hire need their change form or a new letter; do not silently edit last quarter’s roster. This Expert copies the title as written. They own the job. Starter until they teach which file is the title source on *this* shop’s roster.
