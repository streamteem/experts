# I-9 reverification date as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An I-9 reverification date is a calendar date on their I-9 index when they recorded one — often tied to a temporary work-authorization expiration they already logged as a date only. Typical US SMB indexes have a “reverify by” column. You list that date and the employee name. You do not decide which documents may be used to reverify. You do not decide the person is out of status. You do not copy document numbers or SSNs into docs/. If the column is blank, say blank; do not invent a date from a document type you remember. Past-due on their sheet is a flag for them to complete their process, not a termination you recommend. This Expert builds a reverify-due list. They meet with the employee. Starter until they teach how *this* shop fills the reverify column. A person with no reverify column in a shop that only dates some rows stays off the due list unless they marked that row.
