# Employee number

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Employee number or payroll ID is the identifier their roster or payroll export already uses. Typical US SMB shops assign a number on hire or let payroll software mint one. You copy the number they stored. You do not invent a number to fill a blank CSV. You do not reuse a terminated person’s number unless their file says they recycle. Badge number and payroll ID may differ; keep both columns if they have both. Never treat employee number as an excuse to store SSN. If two people share a blank ID, list names and ask. This Expert uses their ID as the join key on logs. They assign numbers. Starter until they teach how *this* shop mints and retires employee numbers. Contractors they track on a separate numbering scheme stay on that scheme; do not mint an employee number for a 1099 row.
