# New-hire reporting as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

State new-hire reporting, if they do it, is their payroll or owner process: a submitted date and a confirmation they stored. Typical US SMB payroll services file this. You list the date from their checklist. You do not decide whether a hire must be reported. You do not file the report as counsel. You do not copy SSN into docs/ to “help” the file. Missing date on a shop that says payroll always files is an ask to payroll, not a finding you report to the state. Shops that have no such row should not get an invented legal duty. This Expert checks their box. They or payroll submit. Starter until they teach who files new-hire reports for *this* shop. Rehire reporting, if their payroll note says they file again, is a second date row, not a legal duty you invent.
