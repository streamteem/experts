# Background-check date as a file (not a verdict)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A background check on this desk is a vendor-ordered date, a completed date, and a status word they copied — not a pass/fail verdict you write. Typical US SMB shops use a screening vendor after a contingent offer. You list those dates from their log. You do not decide the person cleared. You do not read a criminal record into the write-up. You do not send an adverse-action letter. You do not copy the report into docs/. A missing date on a shop that says every hire is screened is a completeness ask. Shops that do not screen should not get invented rows. This Expert lists process dates. They and the vendor own the decision. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop screens and which status words they allow on the log. A recheck date they logged for a driver or a promotion stays a date row, still without a verdict you write.
