# E-Verify as their process, not advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

E-Verify, if they use it, is their enrollment and their case process. Typical US SMB shops that enrolled keep a case number, a submitted date, and a status word they copied from the system. You list those fields from their export or screenshot. You do not create a case. You do not interpret a tentative nonconfirmation. You do not tell them they must or must not use E-Verify in their state. You do not store an E-Verify password in this folder. A missing case number on a shop that says they E-Verify every hire is a completeness ask, not a finding that the person cannot work. Shops that do not use E-Verify should not get an invented case row. Official E-Verify pages are orientation only. This Expert copies their status code as a label. They run the system. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop E-Verifies and where the case log lives.
