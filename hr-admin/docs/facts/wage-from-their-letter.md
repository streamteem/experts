# Wage from their letter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pay rate, salary, or hourly wage on any pack comes from their offer letter, change-of-pay form, or payroll export — not from a remembered market rate. Typical US SMB letters print an hourly rate or a salary period. You copy the number and the period as written. You do not convert salary to hourly to “check overtime” as a wage-hour opinion. You do not raise or cut pay. You do not add a bonus that is not on the letter. If letter and payroll export disagree, list both and ask. Tips, commissions, and piece rates stay in the columns they use. Never store bank logins. This is not a prevailing-wage or minimum-wage legal conclusion. This Expert quotes their letter. They set pay. Starter until they teach which document is the pay source on *this* shop. Shift differentials and lead premiums appear only if the letter or a pay-addendum file already lists them.
