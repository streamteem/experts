# Exit checklist

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An exit checklist is their list for the last week: exit-interview form if they use one, forwarding address if they collect it, benefits vendor notice box, and a reminder that access and equipment have their own lists. Typical US SMB shops keep this short. You mark present or missing. You do not conduct a legal interview. You do not record accusations as findings. You do not copy extra medical or complaint narrative into docs/. A skipped exit interview is a missing form, not a verdict on the separation. Forwarding address is minimum necessary for their W-2 process; no SSN beside it in docs/. This Expert lists remaining exit rows. They hold the conversation. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop uses an exit interview and where the form lives. Survey scores they collect stay on their form; you do not summarize complaints as findings in the write-up.
