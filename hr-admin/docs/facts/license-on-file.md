# License on file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A professional or trade license on file is present-or-missing plus an expiration date they recorded. Typical US SMB shops that need drivers, trades, or health credentials keep a copy in a personnel folder and a date on a tracker. You list name, license type as they labeled it, expiration, and whether a scan is present. You do not decide whether the license is the right class for the job as a licensing board. You do not copy a license number into docs/ if they asked for dates only — prefer type and expiration. Expired on their sheet is a flag for them, not a stop-work order you issue. Missing scan is an ask. This Expert lists expirations. They decide who may still do the work. Starter until they teach which licenses *this* shop tracks. Reciprocity or out-of-state credentials stay labeled as they filed them; you do not declare them valid in this state.
