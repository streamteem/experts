# Department

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Department, team, or cost center comes from their hire sheet, org chart, or payroll export. Typical US SMB shops use a short list: shop, office, warehouse, sales. You copy the label they used. You do not invent a department to make a headcount pie look balanced. If the person splits time, use the split they wrote or ask; do not allocate hours yourself. Department drives who approves PTO only when their policy file says so — you still do not invent the approval path. A rename of a department needs their updated chart. This Expert lists department as a roster column. They assign people to teams. Starter until they teach the department list *this* shop uses and which file is canonical. Cost-center codes, if they print them, stay next to the department name and are not a budget you invent. A person on loan to another team still uses the home department they recorded unless they filed a change.
