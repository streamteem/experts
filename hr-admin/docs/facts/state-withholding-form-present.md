# State withholding form present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many states have their own employee withholding form; some shops also keep a residency or locality form. Typical US SMB desks in those states put the state form in the same new-hire packet as the federal W-4. You mark present or missing from their checklist and the packet folder. You do not choose a state filing status. You do not invent which form a state requires. If they operate in more than one state, use the form list they keep per work location — do not guess the right state from a memory of tax maps. No SSN from the state form goes into docs/. A shop in a state with no separate form should not get an invented missing-form row; say their checklist does not use one. This is completeness only, not a payroll tax opinion. They collect the form. Payroll or their CPA keys it. Starter until they teach which state forms *this* shop uses.
