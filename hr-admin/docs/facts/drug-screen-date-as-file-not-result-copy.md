# Drug-screen date as a file (not a result copy)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A drug-screen row is an ordered date, a collected date if they log it, and a status they already coded — not a laboratory result pasted into docs/. Typical US SMB shops that screen use a clinic or a vendor. You list dates and their status word. You do not copy positive/negative language into the shared folder. You do not decide fitness for duty. You do not invent a panel. A missing date on a shop that requires a screen is an ask. Shops that do not screen get no invented row. Medical detail stays out. This Expert lists process dates. They and the clinic own the result. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop drug-screens and how they want status coded without result text. Random or post-accident screens they already coded keep that code; you do not order a test.
