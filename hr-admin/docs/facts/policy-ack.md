# Policy acknowledgment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some shops collect stand-alone acknowledgments for IT, safety, harassment, driving, or confidentiality policies in addition to the handbook ack. Typical US SMB packets include one or two extra signature pages. You list each policy name and version as printed on their form, plus present or missing. You do not merge two policies into one row to tidy the sheet. You do not invent a policy they do not use. You do not explain what a confidentiality clause “means” as counsel. If a policy PDF in the folder has a different version than the signed page, quote both and ask. Electronic acks they export as a dated row count as present when that export is in the folder. This is completeness, not a finding that the person is bound. They collect the pages. Starter until they teach which stand-alone policies *this* shop still uses beside the handbook.
