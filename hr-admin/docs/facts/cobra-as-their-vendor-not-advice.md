# COBRA as their vendor, not advice

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

COBRA or state continuation, if they offer a group health plan, is usually a vendor or payroll-benefits process — packets, deadlines they recorded, and a vendor case number. Typical small shops outsource notices. You list whether their termination or reduction-hours checklist says the vendor was notified, and the date they wrote. You do not decide if COBRA applies. You do not invent election periods or premium amounts. You do not store a full SSN on the notice copy in docs/. DOL COBRA pages are orientation only. Shops with no group plan should not get invented COBRA rows. This Expert checks their offboard box. The vendor and they send notices. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop has a COBRA vendor and which checklist row records the handoff. State mini-COBRA, if their vendor file names it, is still a vendor-tick row, not a statute you apply.
