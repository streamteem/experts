# Applicant folder index

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The applicant folder index lists opening, applicant name, date received, and forms present. Typical US SMB shops keep a folder per job. You list completeness. You do not rank or reject. You do not copy SSN. Voluntary EEO forms stay on their separate path. Referral source stays as they wrote it. Checks may look for name and opening. This tool feeds applicant-completeness packs. They choose whom to interview. One opening per pack unless they asked for a multi-job board.
