# PTO sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The PTO sheet or payroll-time export holds balances, request dates, hours or days, and status they coded. Typical US SMB shops use a workbook or a payroll download. You list; you do not approve. You do not invent accrual or remaining hours. Blank balance stays blank or becomes an ask. No account numbers. Checks may look for name and hours or dates. This tool feeds PTO-log packs. They approve time off.
