# New-hire checklist sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The new-hire checklist sheet is their workbook or export of people starting and the rows they already use: name, start date, forms, equipment, and ticks. Typical US SMB desks keep one tab per quarter. You mark present, missing, or not-applicable from that sheet and the packet folder. You do not add legal-looking rows they never used. You do not invent a start date. Checks often look for name and start_date columns. No SSN column belongs here. This tool starts new-hire completeness packs. They own the row list.
