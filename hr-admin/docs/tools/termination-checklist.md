# Termination / offboard checklist

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The termination or offboard checklist is their process after they already set a last day: equipment, access, payroll handoff, vendor-notice boxes. You mark present or missing. You do not fire anyone. You do not invent last day. You do not store passwords or card PAN. Checks may look for name and last_day. This tool feeds offboard packs. Final-pay numbers still come from payroll files, not a legal deadline you invent.
