# Offer-letter template

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The offer template is their Word or PDF form with blanks for name, title, start date, and pay as they designed it. You fill only from their hire sheet and this template. You do not paste a generic letter. You do not invent wage or exempt language. A filled draft stays a draft. They send. If the template is missing, ask. This tool supports new-hire packs that include a draft letter they requested. No SSN on the draft in docs/.
