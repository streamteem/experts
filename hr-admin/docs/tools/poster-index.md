# Poster index

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The poster index is their list of poster files or wall-check dates: name they use, file pointer, last checked if they log it. You list present or missing. You do not decide which posters the law requires. DOL poster pages are orientation for names they may already use. This tool feeds poster-and-handbook-version packs with the handbook PDF. They hang the wall. You do not invent a required set. Photo of the wall is evidence they stored, not a poster you invent.
