# Training log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The training log lists person, course as they named it, completed date, and due or refresh date. Typical US SMB shops export from an LMS or keep a workbook. You do not invent a required course. You do not mark complete without their date. No LMS password belongs here. Checks may look for name and due_date. This tool feeds training-due packs. Certificates they stored are present-or-missing flags, not files you recopy with extra personal data.
