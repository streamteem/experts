# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the roster, log, or exception CSV you build from their exports: names, dates, present-or-missing flags. Typical US SMB HR desks live in workbooks. You do not add an SSN column. You do not add bank digits. Checks use file-exists or named columns such as name and date. This tool is the usual join layer for packs: I-9 dates, PTO, acks, training due, applicant completeness, and poster indexes. Export from their system; no login stored here.
