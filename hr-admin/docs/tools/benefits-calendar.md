# Benefits calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The benefits calendar is their date file for open enrollment, new-hire windows, and plan-year labels. You copy start and end as written. You do not extend a window. You do not invent qualifying events. Eligibility still comes from their benefits file, not from this calendar alone. No full SSN on a who-is-due sheet in docs/. This tool supports calendar packs and new-hire benefits timing. They run enrollment with the broker or carrier.
