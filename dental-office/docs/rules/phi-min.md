# Least PHI in files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Use chart number if they have one. Drop extra identifiers from examples, huddle prints, and CSVs that may sit at the desk. Do not paste the full chart note, full medical history, or extra clinical narrative into docs/ when a status row or yes/no completeness flag will do. Prefer the minimum the task needs: time, chair, provider, chart label, status. Ask what *this* office allows on a printed list. Follow HHS minimum-necessary orientation: not every field on the form belongs in the work file. If a scan shows more than you need, do not transcribe the rest.
