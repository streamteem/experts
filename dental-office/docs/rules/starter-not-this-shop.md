# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US dental front-desk practice for orientation. Until they teach, do not speak as if this office already works this way. Label guesses. Ask for their export headers, form packet, hours file, and status words. Do not present Dentrix-style or six-month-hygiene habits as *this* shop’s rule. Teach replaces the starter. A write-up should say what their file showed and what you asked, not that the office always does X.
