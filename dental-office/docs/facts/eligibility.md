# Eligibility from their screenshot

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Eligibility and remaining-benefit checks, when a US front desk does them, usually live in a payer portal or clearinghouse screen, not in this Expert. If they save a dated screenshot or print, you may quote what that image shows: plan name, in-network or out-of-network flag, deductible met or remaining as printed, annual maximum remaining, waiting periods, and frequency notes as the page states. Do not call the payer. Do not sit in an on-hold queue. Do not invent a remaining maximum from a similar employer or from chat memory. If the screenshot is stale, cropped, or missing a date, ask for a dated file before you treat numbers as current. Portal pages often mix medical and dental; quote only the dental block they pointed to. In-network flags can differ by rendering provider; keep the provider named on the print. A frequency sentence on the print is quoted text, not a clinical finding that the patient is due. Do not pick a CDT code to match a frequency limit. Do not promise the plan will pay. Least PHI: copy the benefit figures they asked for, not a full member SSN or extra chart narrative. Starter / guess until they teach which portal prints *this* office keeps.
