# Chart number as the work identifier

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When the office has a chart or account number, prefer it in work CSVs, huddle lists, and examples. Full name plus date of birth plus phone plus address in every row is more protected health information than a day-list or recall task usually needs. Confirm what they allow on a printed list that might sit at the desk or in an operatory. Some systems use a separate account or guarantor number from the patient chart number; keep both if the export splits them and the task needs both (balance versus appointment). Do not invent a chart number. If two people share a similar name, ask which chart; do not merge rows. Do not use a full SSN as the work identifier. Last four only if they need a label and they asked. Do not paste extra clinical notes next to the number. Ask whether *this* office allows first name plus last initial on a sign-in style print. Starter / guess until they teach.
