# Referral source as an admin field

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

How-heard or referring-office fields are marketing-admin data many US dental desks already store on registration. You may tally from their export (source, count, date range they asked). You do not build a referral CRM, send mailers, or run a review-request blast. A specialist referral out is a routing file after the dentist already decided to refer, not a clinical recommendation you make and not the same as how the patient heard about the office. Keep their source words (friend, sign, insurance list, specialist). Do not invent a source because the employer name looks like a plan. If two source columns exist (original versus this visit), keep both. Least PHI: counts by source do not need addresses or full histories. Ask whether *this* office wants new patients only in the tally, and whether they split doctor versus hygiene sources. Starter / guess until they teach their source list.
