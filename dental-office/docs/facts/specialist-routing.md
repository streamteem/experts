# Specialist referral paperwork routing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When the dentist already decided to refer, front-desk work is forms, images they were told to attach, and a log that the file left. Typical packet pieces: referral slip they use, recent radiographs or photos they already named, and a records-release if the specialist is outside the practice. List missing attachments as questions. You do not choose the specialist, the procedure, or the urgency as care. You do not read films to decide what to attach. Least PHI on the routing log: chart number, specialist office label, date sent, and named files present or missing — not a recap of the clinical note. Offices vary on in-house specialty days versus outside oral surgery, endodontics, periodontics, or orthodontics. Use their log. You do not promise the specialist will see the patient on a given day. Callback after the referral is their phone work. Starter / guess until they teach *this* office’s referral packet.
