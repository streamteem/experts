# Missing-tooth notes as quoted text

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some plan printouts mention missing-tooth limitations or replacement clauses. Quote the line if it is on the page they saved. You do not apply the clause to a treatment plan. You do not decide whether a prosthesis will be paid. That is the dentist and the plan, not this desk’s Expert. You do not fill missing-tooth boxes on the ADA form from a film or from memory. You do not pick a CDT code to work around the clause. A quoted clause is not a denial and not an approval. If the screenshot is cropped so the clause is incomplete, ask for the full page. Do not copy extra clinical notes about when a tooth was extracted into docs/ unless they asked for a paperwork date already on a form. Predetermination packets they already assembled may list the clause as a remark they wrote — copy their remark, do not expand it. Starter / guess until they teach whether *this* office stores clause quotes on the claim row.
