# Recall list as a file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US dental offices export a recall or hygiene-due list from the practice system so the desk can work overdue and coming-due visits. Typical columns: patient or chart label they allow, last visit or due date, preferred provider if they store one, appointment type they use for hygiene, and a contact method already on the registration form. Your job is the list file they can send from their own phone, text, postcard, or recall module. You do not run reminders as a messaging product and you do not invent that a patient is clinically due. If the export has no due-date column, say so and ask; do not fill dates from a generic six-month rule. Their interval — often six months for hygiene in many shops, sometimes three or four for periodontal maintenance they already coded — is theirs to confirm. Some lists mix doctor recare, child prophy, and perio maintenance in one export; keep their type labels. Respect a hours-and-holiday file before you draft Saturday rows. Prefer chart number in the work CSV. Do not copy extra phone numbers or clinical notes into the write-up when a due date and label will do. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office names recall types.
