# Day sheet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical US dental front desks print or export a day sheet the night before or at open so the floor knows who sits when. The sheet is a chair list, not a clinical note: start time, the patient label they allow on a desk print, rendering provider, operatory or chair, and appointment type as booked. Many practice systems add confirmation status, a new-patient flag, or an outstanding-balance column pulled from the ledger. Some offices still photograph a paper book into the folder; others export CSV from Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, SoftDent, or a similar system. Confirm which identifiers they allow on a list that may sit at the front desk. Prefer chart number over stacking full legal name, date of birth, and address on every row. One clinic date per write-up unless they asked for a multi-day book and the file has a date column. Closed blocks, lunch, and huddle rows stay so the list is honest. You do not diagnose from the reason field, do not reassign chairs or providers unless their written rule says to draft options, and do not invent a missing column as fact. If the export has no date or looks like last week, ask before you treat it as tomorrow. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office labels chairs and patients.
