# Insurance as paperwork

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Front-desk work on dental benefits in most US offices is paperwork completeness, not plan adjudication. Typical claim or registration packets need subscriber name as printed, member ID, group number, patient-to-subscriber relationship, date of service, treating provider, and whether named attachments their process requires are in the folder. You may list missing fields on a claim image, ADA Dental Claim Form, or clearinghouse print they provide. Offices vary on whether they check eligibility the same day, a week ahead, or only for larger restorative blocks. A filed or submitted date in their export is a file fact. A promise that the plan will pay, that a frequency is met clinically, or that remaining benefits cover a proposed visit is not a file fact unless that exact sentence is on a dated screenshot they saved. You do not decide medical necessity, do not pick or swap CDT codes as treatment, and do not tell a patient their patient portion is final if the file still says pending. Do not copy a full SSN or extra clinical narrative into docs/ to “complete” a claim row. Ask which boxes *this* office treats as required. Starter / guess until they teach.
