# Lab case log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Workbook or practice-system export of lab cases sent and due: chart label, case type as entered, lab name, sent and due dates. Use it for huddle status only. You do not change shade, design, material, or the lab. You do not pick a CDT code for the case. A late case is a flag for staff, not a clinical remake note. If the log and the seat appointment disagree, quote both and ask. Do not copy the full lab prescription narrative into docs/. Starter / guess until they teach how *this* office tracks cases.
