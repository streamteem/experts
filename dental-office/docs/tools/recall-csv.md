# Recall export CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hygiene or recall due list from their system. Typical columns: chart or patient label they allow, due date, type they stored, preferred provider if present. They send the reminders. You shape the list file. You do not message patients. If due date is missing, say so; do not fill a generic six-month date. Respect their hours-and-holiday file. Do not add codes or say the patient is clinically due. Prefer chart number. Extra phones the send-list does not need stay out of the write-up.
