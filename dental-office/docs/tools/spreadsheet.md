# Office spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Recall, claims tracking, lab log, broken-appointment, wait-list, or callback sheets if they live in a workbook. Their columns win. Export to CSV for checks when you can. Do not add a broken-fee column or a CDT column they do not have. Do not invent statuses. Prefer chart number over stacked identifiers. Card numbers and full SSNs stay out. If two sheets disagree with a practice-system export, quote both and ask. Starter / guess until they teach which workbooks *this* office actually uses.
