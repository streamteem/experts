# Late return

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Late return means the contract due date or hour passed and the unit is still out in their file. Typical US SMB yards grace a clock hour and then bill another day from the rate file. You list overdue contracts from the reservation or contract export. You do not invent a late fee. You do not invent another day rate. If their grace rule is missing, ask. Off-rent call-off that they already stamped is not late even if the truck has not picked up — unless their file says otherwise. You do not threaten collections or a lawsuit. You do not store a card to “keep billing.” Starter until they teach *this* yard’s grace and who calls the renter. Pickup tickets often spawn from this list. Hour overage is a different charge. They decide the call and the fee. You produce the overdue list and write-up.
