# Boom versus scissor if they rent aerials

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Boom and scissor are different aerial classes when they rent them. Typical US SMB yards will not silently substitute one for the other. You keep the class as the reservation and fleet file show. You do not invent a working height or outreach. Indoor-height asks are questions, not an engineering stamp. Operator-manual and licensed-operator asks often apply to both; still no license stamp from you. OSHA aerial-lift pages are orientation, not a citation you issue. If they do not rent aerials, say the class is not on their fleet file. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s height bands and which booms are tow-behind versus self-propel. Rates come from the aerial columns on their rate file, never from a remembered national book. Weather and outdoor notes matter more on booms; still their call. Substitution across boom/scissor needs their rule and usually a renter call.
