# Fleet cycle count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A fleet cycle count is their periodic yard walk or scanner file versus the fleet export: serials seen, missing, or in the wrong bay. Typical US SMB yards count high-theft and high-dollar classes more often than bulk tools. You compare count to book. You do not invent a serial seen. You do not hide a miss to tidy utilization. In-shop and on-rent units should appear as their status says; a miss on an on-rent row is a different question than a miss on available. Theft process starts only when they say so. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s count frequency and whether attachments count separately. GPS last ping can support a miss; it does not replace the count file. You do not diagnose a unit that was counted with a leak. They slot, they investigate, they adjust the book. You produce the exception list and write-up.
