# Indoor versus outdoor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Indoor versus outdoor is a site note and a machine-class filter: electric scissors for finished floors, rough-terrain or boom for outdoor dirt, as their item notes describe. Typical US SMB reservationists ask indoor/outdoor so they do not send a diesel into a warehouse. You copy the ask and the class notes from their file. You do not engineer ventilation, floor load, or emissions. You do not invent that a diesel is “fine indoors.” If the reservation lacks the ask and their process requires it for aerials, flag missing. Substitution must respect the same indoor/outdoor note. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s electric versus engine rules. Weather is a separate fact; indoor jobs still need height and door-width asks they wrote, not numbers you invent. You do not promise the unit will fit through a doorway unless their file has the dimension and they asked you only to copy it. Height engineering is out of scope.
