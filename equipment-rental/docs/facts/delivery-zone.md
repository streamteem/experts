# Delivery zone

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Delivery zone is their map, zip list, or mileage ring that prices a drop. Typical US SMB yards print zone A/B/C or charge per mile after a free radius. Zone dollars come only from that zone sheet or the rate file. You do not invent a zone from a map in your head. You do not invent mileage. If the jobsite zip is missing, ask. If two zone files disagree, quote both. After-hours delivery, if priced, is a separate column on their file. Liftgate, trailer, or escort notes stay as they wrote them; you do not engineer a route. FMCSA pages are orientation for their truck identity, not a dispatch legal opinion you stamp. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s rings and whether will-call is zone zero. You do not pay the driver or a hired hauler. Gate codes stay out of the zone pack. Address comes from the reservation or contract, not from a guessed subdivision name.
