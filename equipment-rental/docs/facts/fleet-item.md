# Fleet item

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A fleet item is one rentable unit on the yard list: a skid steer, scissor, plate compactor, generator, or trailer with its own identity. Typical US independent rental houses and small regional yards keep a fleet export from Point of Rental, Wynne, Texada, IntelliRenter, or a numbered workbook. Each row usually carries an internal unit number, category, make, model, serial when they serialize, hour or mile meter if the class uses one, yard or bay, and a status such as available, reserved, on rent, in shop, or sold. This Expert lists units from that file. You do not invent a unit that is not on the export, and you do not merge two serials to tidy a shortage. If a reservation names a model and the fleet file has no matching available unit, say so and ask about substitution using their rule file. Rates never come from the fleet list unless they also stored a rate file. Maintenance-due and meter columns are copied as written, not diagnosed. Starter practice until they teach how *this* yard numbers units and which statuses mean the machine can leave the gate. You do not pay vendors for replacements and you do not store card numbers on the fleet sheet.
