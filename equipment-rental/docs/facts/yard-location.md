# Yard or bay location

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Yard, branch, or bay is where the fleet file says the unit sits. Typical US SMB multi-yard groups move iron between lots; single-lot shops still use bay rows. You copy the location. You do not invent a transfer. You do not mark a unit available at Yard B when the file says Yard A. Reservation-day packs should filter to the yard they asked for. Cycle counts are per yard unless they asked for a company rollup. Delivery zones may differ by yard; use that yard’s zone sheet. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s location codes and in-transit status. You do not pay to haul between yards. GPS last ping is not a yard change until they update the book. Maintenance-due lists should keep location so they know which shop. Idle lists by yard help them, not a blended number that hides a dead lot.
