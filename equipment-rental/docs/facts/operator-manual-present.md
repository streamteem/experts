# Operator manual present

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Operator-manual present is a yes/no (or missing) on their outgoing checklist: the manufacturer book or the box they keep with the unit. Typical US SMB aerial and forklift rows care about this because the renter’s employer trains on that unit. You record present or missing as the inspection says. You do not write a training manual. You do not stamp the renter as trained because the book was in the box. If their process blocks checkout when the manual is missing, say incomplete. Digital QR manuals, if they use them, still need their “present” rule. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s sticker and book process. OSHA pages in sources are orientation, not a citation you issue. You do not invent a replacement-book fee unless their fee file lists it. Photos of the book in the holder help a completeness pack. Lost manuals at return follow the lost-item path. This is file completeness, not safety legal advice.
