# Deposit policy file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Deposit, security hold, or prepay amounts come from their deposit-policy file or the printed contract term, not from a remembered “one week plus tax.” Typical US SMB yards hold more for homeowners than for billed contractors, or they skip deposit when a COI and account file exist. You copy the rule as written for that renter type. You do not invent a deposit dollar. You do not run a card or store PAN to “place the hold.” If the policy file is missing, ask. If the contract shows a deposit that does not match the policy file, quote both and ask. Refund or apply-to-invoice is their office or books desk, not a payment you make. Lost-item or damage charges against a deposit still need their damage notes and rate or fee file. Starter until they teach how *this* yard codes cash, check, and card holds without putting card numbers in this folder. A deposit policy is not a collection lawsuit and not a mechanic estimate.
