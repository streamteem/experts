# Environmental fee as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Environmental, recovery, or similar add-on fees appear only as their rate file or printed contract clause shows. Typical US SMB rental contracts add a percent or a flat environmental line. You copy the printed amount or percent. You do not invent an environmental fee because “everyone charges it.” You do not invent a tax treatment. If the rate file is silent and the contract PDF shows a line, quote the PDF and ask whether that file is live. If both are silent, ask; do not fill the line. Fee is not a diagnosis of a leak and not an EPA legal opinion. Starter until they teach how *this* yard shows the fee on cash versus account contracts and whether tax-exempt jobs still see it. You do not present a starter percent as how *this* shop already works. They change the rate file. You list what is written on the dated file you were given.
