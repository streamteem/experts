# Substitution

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Substitution is putting a different unit or class on a reservation or contract because the reserved machine is down, late, or already out. Typical US SMB yards allow same-class swaps without a call and require a call when capacity, height, or power changes. Their substitution rule file wins. You do not invent an equivalent. You do not swap a scissor for a boom because both are “lifts.” You do not invent a rate for the substitute; price the unit they will actually send from the rate file. If the file is silent, ask before the pack says substituted. Serial and meter on the new unit must come from the fleet file. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s automatic versus ask-first swaps. Indoor-height and licensed-operator asks still apply to the substitute. You do not promise the substitute will finish the job. They call the renter unless they asked you only to list options from their fleet.
