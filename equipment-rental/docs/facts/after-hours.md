# After-hours pickup or delivery

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

After-hours is a pickup, drop, or return outside the posted counter hours on their hours file. Typical US SMB yards leave a key box or meet a contractor at dawn. You copy the after-hours flag and any fee from their rate or policy file. You do not invent an after-hours surcharge. You do not write lockbox combinations, gate codes, or alarm codes into docs/. If hours of operation are missing, ask before you label a time after-hours. Weekend and holiday rates are separate files; after-hours can stack only if their file says so. The reservation should show the arranged window. Starter until they teach how *this* yard staffs Saturday and who may promise a night drop. You do not promise the unit will start at 5 a.m. You do not diagnose a no-start on a dark lot as a mechanic; you log their note and point to inspection or shop. Card numbers do not belong on the after-hours note.
