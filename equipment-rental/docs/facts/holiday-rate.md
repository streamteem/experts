# Holiday rate from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Holiday rates and closed days come from their holiday calendar plus the holiday column on the rate file. Typical US SMB yards close the counter on a few federal holidays and still bill iron that stays out. You do not invent a holiday multiplier. You do not invent that the yard is closed. If the calendar and the rate file disagree on whether a day is billable, quote both. Weather holds on a holiday still need their weather note. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s list (and whether state holidays count). You do not promise a delivery on a day their calendar marks closed. Overbook pressure before a holiday weekend still follows their overbook rule. Tax and environmental lines stay as the dated rate file shows. They update the calendar. You list conflicts between reservations and closed days. Weekend rates are a separate column when both could apply.
