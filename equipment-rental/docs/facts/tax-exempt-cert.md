# Tax-exempt certificate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tax-exempt or resale certificates are PDFs they keep for municipalities, schools, or dealers. Typical US SMB yards charge tax unless a current cert is on file for that bill-to. You check present versus missing and the name match their checklist uses. You do not invent a tax rate and you do not invent an exemption. You do not give a sales-tax legal opinion. Expired cert dates on their log stay expired until they file a new one. IRS rental-income pages in sources are orientation for their books, not a reason to skip tax. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s state form names and who may waive tax. If the contract shows exempt and the folder has no cert, flag it. You do not store a card because tax is in dispute. They correct the contract. Environmental fees still follow their fee file, not a tax opinion you invent.
