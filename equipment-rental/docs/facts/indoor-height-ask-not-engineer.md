# Indoor height ask, not engineering

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Indoor height, door width, and floor notes are asks you copy onto the reservation when their process requires them. Typical US SMB aerial desks ask finished-ceiling height and whether the unit must roll through a standard door. You record the numbers they were given. You do not calculate a safe working height, a floor-load limit, or a tip-over opinion as an engineer. You do not invent a machine dimension; copy only the spec sheet they stored. If the renter’s height and the stored spec disagree, quote both and ask. Boom versus scissor still stays as they reserved. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s required asks. You do not promise the unit will fit. You do not stamp an OSHA-compliant setup. They or the renter’s competent person decide. Missing height ask on an indoor aerial reservation is a completeness flag when their form includes it.
