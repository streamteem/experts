# Certificate of insurance from the renter

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A renter COI is the certificate they collected from the renter’s insurer or agent, stored in the COI folder. Typical US SMB yards require COI on commercial accounts and sometimes on high-value aerials. You check presence, named insured versus renter name, dates that cover the rental, and additional-insured wording only as their checklist asks you to compare to their sample. You do not give a coverage opinion. You do not say the yard is “additional insured and safe.” You do not invent a limit. Expired or missing COI is a missing-forms row. Waiver or RPP is a different form and does not replace COI unless their policy file says it does. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s required wording and who may waive COI. NAIC consumer pages are orientation, not a certificate review stamp. You do not store the renter’s card because COI is missing. They hold the reservation or they waive in writing.
