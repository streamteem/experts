# Accessory kit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An accessory kit is the extra pieces that travel with a class or a serial: forks, buckets, pads, hitches, fire extinguishers, lift-gate remotes. Typical US SMB fleet files have a kit tab or a serialized-kit list. Outgoing inspection should confirm the kit they said goes with the unit. You list kit lines from their file. You do not invent a kit so the contract looks complete. You do not invent a kit rate; kit dollars come from the rate file if they price extras. Substituting a unit means checking the substitute’s kit, not assuming the same forks. Return inspection marks missing kit as lost-item. Starter until they teach which kits *this* yard always sends and which are on request. Manuals and CO2 bottles, if they track them, sit on the same list. You do not diagnose a leaking hose as a mechanic; you note the inspection words. Gate-safe accessories still need their list, not an engineering add.
