# Weekend rate from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Weekend rates come from the weekend column on their rate file or a printed weekend specials sheet. Typical US SMB yards sell Friday-to-Monday as one weekend on tools and party, and still bill Saturday as a day on some construction classes. You copy the column that matches the class and the dates they coded. You do not invent a weekend special. You do not apply a construction day rate to a party weekend without their file. If dates include a holiday weekend, also read the holiday-rate file; if both could apply, quote both and ask. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s Friday pickup cutoff. After-hours Friday night is not automatically a weekend rate. You do not present a starter weekend price as how *this* shop already works. Reservation-day packs should show the rate source file name and date, not a number from memory.
