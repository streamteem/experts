# Long-term versus event rental

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Long-term and event (or short party) rentals are different contract shapes on their files: monthly construction versus a weekend tent or scissor for a show. Typical US SMB yards use different rate columns, different deposit rules, and different COI asks. You label the contract as their type code shows. You do not invent a long-term discount. You do not apply the event minimum to a monthly without their file. Utilization for event iron can look spiky; keep their type filter on the report. Substitution rules may be tighter on event weekends. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s type codes and whether a four-week job auto-converts to month. Tax-exempt and PO rules often show up on long-term commercial, not on cash events — still from their files. You do not invent weekend rates for an event. They choose the type when the file is blank; you ask.
