# Renter account file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The renter account file is their customer row: cash versus charge, PO required, COI required, tax status, and delivery notes. Typical US SMB yards keep this in the rental system or a workbook. You use the flags as written when you check contract completeness. You do not invent an account. You do not invent terms. You do not store bank logins or card PAN on the account sheet. New cash renters still need whatever ID their process asks, min-necessary only. Account holds or “do not rent” codes stay as they coded them; you do not invent a credit verdict and you do not collect as a lawsuit. Starter until they teach how *this* yard opens an account. Job-site contacts are not bill-to. If two bill-to names appear on the COI and the contract, quote both. They decide whether the unit can leave. You list missing account fields they asked you to check.
