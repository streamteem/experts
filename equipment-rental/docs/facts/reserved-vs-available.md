# Reserved versus available

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reserved and available are different fleet statuses. Typical US SMB counters oversell a class if they treat reserved as still available; that is only allowed when their overbook rule says so. You keep the two words distinct on reservation-day packs. A unit that is reserved for Tuesday is not available for a Tuesday walk-in unless they released the hold. On-rent is a third state. In-shop is a fourth. Do not relabel reserved as available to make the board look open. Do not relabel available as reserved to hide idle. If the reservation CSV and the fleet CSV disagree on a serial, quote both and ask which is live. Starter until they teach how *this* yard expires holds at close of day. Rates do not change because of status. You do not invent a unit to cover a reserved hole. Substitution is the path they use when reserved iron is not actually available.
