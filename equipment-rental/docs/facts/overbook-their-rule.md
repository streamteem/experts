# Overbook only from their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Overbooking a class or a serial is allowed only when their written rule file says so — for example a stated percent of scissors on short local jobs. Typical US SMB yards either never overbook serialized iron or they overbook high-count tools. You do not invent an overbook percent. You do not double-book a named serial because “they always no-show.” If two reservations overlap and there is no rule file, flag the conflict and ask. Walk-ins that would overbook still need their rule. Substitution after an overbook fail is a later step. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s overbook and who may override. You do not promise a unit that exists only as hope. You do not hide the overlap inside a tidy reservation list. Utilization pressure is not a license to overbook. They decide to buy, sub from another yard, or decline the reservation.
