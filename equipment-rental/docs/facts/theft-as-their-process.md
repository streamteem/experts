# Theft as their process

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Theft or missing-unit process is the checklist they already wrote: last contract, last GPS ping if any, serial, police-report number they recorded, and insurer contacts they store. You assemble that file completeness. You do not invent a serial or a location. You do not call the theft a mechanic failure. You do not file a police report as this product unless they taught that send step and gave the facts. You do not give an insurance-coverage opinion. Damage waiver or RPP may or may not speak to theft; copy only their form’s printed words as a label, not advice. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s who-calls-whom list. Cycle-count misses can start this path; still they decide it is theft versus a yard mis-slot. You do not store PAN on a theft pack. They talk to the renter, the police, and their agent.
