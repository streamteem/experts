# Trailer

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A trailer is a fleet item that may rent alone or carry another unit. Typical US SMB yards track trailer serial or VIN, lights, plates, and whether the renter or the yard tows. You list the trailer from the fleet file and the contract line. You do not invent a hitch class or a towing capacity as an engineer. You do not invent a trailer day rate. If the reservation needs a trailer and the fleet shows none available, say so. Plate expiration, if they store it, is a file date, not a DMV legal opinion. Delivery on their truck versus renter-tow stays as the ticket says. Starter until they teach how *this* yard pairs machines to trailers and who inspects lights. Damage to deck or lights follows inspection-return and photos. You do not diagnose bearing noise. You do not store a card for the trailer deposit. FMCSA pages are orientation if they operate commercial trucks, not a stamp that their trailer is legal for a given highway.
