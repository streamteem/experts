# Lost item

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Lost or missing items are pieces the outgoing list or kit file showed that the return inspection marked not present: keys, forks, pads, pins, remotes, hoses, manuals. Typical US SMB yards bill from a replacement-price file or a vendor quote they stored. You list what outgoing had and what return says is missing. You do not invent a replacement price. You do not invent a part number. If the kit file never listed the piece, you cannot call it lost without asking. Serials on kit pieces, when they track them, must match. Photos of empty brackets help; they do not replace a missing price file. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s key and remote policy. Lost is not a theft claim unless their theft process file says to open that path. You do not collect as a lawsuit. You do not store PAN to take the charge. They post the contract or they waive.
