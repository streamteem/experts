# Off rent

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Off rent is the moment their process stops rental time: a renter call, a portal stop, or a written date on the contract. Typical US SMB yards distinguish off-rent time from physical pickup; a machine can be off rent and still sitting on a site waiting for a truck. You copy the off-rent stamp they recorded. You do not invent an off-rent time to shorten a bill and you do not keep the meter running past their stamp to fatten utilization. If the call note and the system stamp disagree, quote both and ask. Weekend, holiday, and minimum-day rules still come from their rate or policy file, not from a habit you remember. Off rent is not a damage verdict and not a payment. Late-return fees, if any, need their late-return rule. Starter until they teach whether *this* yard bills through the pickup day or through the call-off hour. You do not collect the balance as a lawsuit. You do not store a card to close the contract.
