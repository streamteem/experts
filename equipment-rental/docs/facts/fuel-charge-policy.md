# Fuel charge policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fuel charges come from their fuel-policy file: returned below a mark, per gallon, or a flat refuel. Typical US SMB yards send units out full and charge to refill. You copy the policy and the return-inspection fuel mark they recorded. You do not invent a per-gallon price or a flat fee. You do not invent a fuel percent to complete the sheet. If the inspection fuel field is blank, ask; do not guess from a photo unless they said the photo is the record and you can read their marked scale. Mixed gasoline and diesel units stay on the correct policy line if they split them. DEF or propane, if they rent those classes, needs their line, not a guessed add-on. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s full-to-full versus eighths. Fuel is not a mechanic diagnosis of a leak. You do not store a card to take the fuel charge. They post the contract using their fee file.
