# Weather hold as their call

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Weather notes are postpones, wind holds, or heat calls they recorded on the reservation or dispatch file. Typical US SMB aerial and crane-adjacent yards cancel high-wind days using their own threshold or the renter’s call. You copy their weather note. You do not invent a wind speed limit as an engineer. You do not diagnose a machine that sat in rain. You do not invent a weather-day credit; credits come from their policy file. If they asked for a list of Tuesday deliveries that they already marked weather-hold, list those tickets. OSHA heat pages are orientation for their own shop talk, not a work/rest stamp you issue to the renter. Starter until they teach how *this* yard codes weather versus renter-cancel. Off-rent and due dates still move only when they say so. You do not promise the job will run tomorrow. Delivery-zone fees on a killed trip follow their file, not a guess.
