# RPP as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rental protection plan, RPP, or similar branded add-on is only their printed form and price file. Typical US SMB yards renamed damage waiver as RPP. You record accepted or declined as the signed contract shows and copy the printed price from their file. You do not invent an RPP percent. You do not say RPP covers theft, downtime, or boom damage unless you are labeling a sentence that already appears on their form. You do not give a coverage opinion. RPP is not a substitute for renter COI unless their policy file says so. Starter until they teach how *this* shop uses RPP versus waiver versus insurance. You do not store a card used to buy RPP. You do not diagnose damage to decide if RPP “should” apply; they and their form decide. Missing form signature is a completeness miss when their process requires it.
