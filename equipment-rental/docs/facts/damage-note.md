# Damage note

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A damage note is their written description of a mark, bend, crack, or missing piece, tied to a unit, contract, and usually a photo. Typical US SMB yards write short notes on the inspection form (“right boom rub,” “windshield star,” “fork bent”). You copy the note. You do not upgrade it into a mechanic diagnosis (“failed hydraulic pump,” “needs new boom”). You do not invent a repair dollar or a downtime day. You do not invent a rate to bill the damage; their damage or parts-price file, if any, is the source, and many yards quote a shop or a dealer later. If outgoing already showed the same mark, say pre-existing as their forms compare. New versus old needs both inspections. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s wear-versus-charge words. Photos should match the note. You do not store a renter card to collect the damage. Collection as a lawsuit is out of scope. They decide waive, charge, or shop.
