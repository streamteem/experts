# Purchase order if commercial

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A customer PO is the contractor or facility number they require on commercial contracts. Typical US SMB account files say “PO required” for certain bill-to names. You check that the PO string is present when their account flag says so. You do not invent a PO number. You do not treat a job name as a PO unless they said that is how that account works. Missing PO is a completeness miss, not a reason to invent a rate or to send the unit. The rental contract is still their rental; the PO is the renter’s paperwork. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s account flags. You do not pay anyone from the PO. You do not store a card “until the PO arrives.” Tax-exempt certs are a separate file. If PO qty or dates disagree with the reservation, quote both and ask. They hold or they override in writing.
