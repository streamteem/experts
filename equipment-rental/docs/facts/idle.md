# Idle unit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Idle means available or otherwise not on rent in their status file, often past a number of days they care about. Typical US SMB yards list idle aerials and earthmoving first because those units are expensive to own. You list idle from the fleet export and the on-rent file. You do not invent a reason the unit is idle and you do not diagnose a leak as why it sits. In-shop is not idle unless they said to group it that way; keep the shop flag visible. Reserved-but-not-out is not idle if their rule counts a reservation as committed; quote their rule. You do not invent a discount to move idle iron. You do not transfer a unit to another yard in the pack as if it already moved. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s idle-days threshold and whether winter attachment season changes the list. Idle lists pair with utilization packs. They decide sales, rental-specials on their specials file, or a shop date.
