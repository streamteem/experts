# GPS or telematics if they have it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

GPS or telematics is an export they already run: location timestamp, geofence, or hour fields from a vendor they use. Typical US SMB yards put trackers on high-theft skid steers and aerials. You list what the export shows. You do not invent a location. You do not invent hours from a map. If GPS hours and the mechanical meter disagree, quote both; do not diagnose the sender. Portal passwords never go in this folder. Theft process may ask for last ping from their export; you copy that row. You do not promise the unit is at the pin. Starter until they teach which serials *this* yard tracks and how stale a ping may be. Utilization still prefers their rental-status file unless they said to use GPS engine hours. You do not geofence as a product. They call the renter or the police using their theft process.
