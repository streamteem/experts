# On rent

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On rent means the contract is open and the unit is charged as out in their system. Typical US SMB exports show on-rent rows with renter, out date, due date, and unit. Reserved-only rows are not on rent. In-shop units are not on rent unless their file says a replacement is already out. Utilization math uses on-rent time from their export, not a calendar you sketch. You do not mark a unit on rent to hide idle time. You do not invent a due date. If the fleet status says available and a contract still says out, quote both and ask which file is live. Delivery in transit is whatever status they coded; do not relabel it. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s status words and whether Saturday counts as on rent for weekly deals. On-rent lists feed reservation-day and utilization packs. Rates on those lists still come from the rate file already printed on the contract, never from a guessed book.
