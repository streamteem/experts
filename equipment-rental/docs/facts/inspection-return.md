# Return inspection

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Return inspection is the inbound checklist and photos when the unit comes back: hours, fuel, new damage, missing accessories, cleanliness. Typical US SMB yards compare return to outgoing. You list differences they recorded. You do not invent damage that is not on the sheet or photo. You do not invent a fuel or cleaning fee; those amounts come from their policy file after they decide the condition codes. You do not diagnose the cause of a leak or a dead battery. If return hours are below outgoing, flag a meter exception. If photos are missing and their process requires them for a charge, say photos missing. Starter until they teach how *this* yard codes fair wear versus chargeable. Inspection-return is not a lawsuit and not a collection letter. They apply charges on the contract using their fee file. You assemble the return-damage pack: inspection sheet, photos, and the outgoing baseline.
