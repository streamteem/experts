# Hour overage

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hour overage is extra hours beyond the allowance printed on their rate or contract file — often eight hours a day or forty a week on earthmoving. You compute only with their printed allowance and the meter difference they recorded. You do not invent an allowance. You do not invent an overage dollar. If outgoing or return hours are missing, ask; do not estimate. If their file has no overage column for that class, say none on file; do not import another yard’s rule. Weekend and holiday rates are separate from overage. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s daily versus weekly cap and whether travel hours count. Overage is not a diagnosis of hard use. You do not hide overtime hours to keep a customer happy. They post the charge from their fee file. Card PAN stays out of the overage pack.
