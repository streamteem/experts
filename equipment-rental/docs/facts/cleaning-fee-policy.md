# Cleaning fee policy

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cleaning, wash, or concrete-removal fees come from their cleaning-policy file after the return inspection codes the condition. Typical US SMB yards waive light dust and charge packed mud or wet concrete. You copy the code they wrote and the fee their file assigns to that code. You do not invent a cleaning dollar. You do not invent a “usual washout” price from another yard. If the inspection says dirty and the policy file is missing, ask. Photos they stored support the code; missing photos are a completeness note when their process requires them to charge. Cleaning is not a diagnosis of a clogged radiator. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s soil versus concrete versus trash codes. You do not promise the unit will go back out the same afternoon. Shop time for a wash still follows their maintenance or yard calendar, not a guess. They decide waive or charge.
