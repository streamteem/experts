# Transport run

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A transport run is their truck or hired-hauler schedule: tickets grouped by date, zone, and driver name they assigned. Typical US SMB yards run a morning drop list and an afternoon pickup list. You build the list from delivery and pickup tickets plus the zone sheet. You do not invent a stop. You do not invent a mileage fee. You do not invent an ETA. Driver names come from their roster note, not a guess. You do not certify the truck or the driver as legal. FMCSA pages are orientation. Gate codes stay out of the run sheet. If a unit is maintenance-due and still on the truck list, flag the conflict. Starter until they teach how *this* yard splits will-call versus delivered. You do not pay the hauler invoice. They dispatch. You produce the day’s ticket list and exception write-up.
