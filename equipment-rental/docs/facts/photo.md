# Inspection and damage photos

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Photos are dated images they stored of the unit, data plate, meter, damage, or delivery placement. Typical US SMB yards shoot four corners plus problem spots on a phone and drop them in a job folder. You list what is present and name files as they asked (unit, contract, in or out, date). You do not invent a photo and you do not crop away damage to tidy a pack. You do not diagnose from a photo as a mechanic. If a damage note has no photo and their charge process requires one, say photo missing. Serial-plate photos help a missing-serial ask. Meter photos support hour exceptions. Do not put renter driver-license images in docs/ beyond what their min-necessary process already stored, and never store card PAN photos. Starter until they teach *this* yard’s naming and whether delivery photos are required. Photos support outgoing, return, and theft files. They decide what to send to insurance.
