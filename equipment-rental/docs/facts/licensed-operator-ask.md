# Licensed-operator ask

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Licensed-operator ask is their checkout question: who will run the forklift, boom, or other class they flag. Typical US SMB counters ask and record a name or a “renter certifies trained” checkbox on their form. You record whether the ask was completed as their form shows. You do not stamp the person licensed, certified, or competent. You do not invent an OSHA card number. OSHA interpretations in the sources explain that many aerial rules are employer-training rules, not a rental-desk license you issue — orientation only, not advice. If their form is blank and their process requires the ask, say incomplete. You do not keep copies of operator cards in docs/ beyond their min-necessary rule. Starter until they teach which classes *this* yard flags. This Expert never trains the operator. They decide whether the contract can leave the yard. Substitution does not skip the ask.
