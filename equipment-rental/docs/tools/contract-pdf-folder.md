# Contract PDF folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Signed and draft rental PDFs they dropped sit here for completeness: names, units, dates, printed rates, waiver or RPP boxes, and pointers to COI or tax certs. You do not invent a rate line. You do not copy PAN if a scan shows a card. Draft versus out stays labeled. This folder is the source for contract-completeness and for tying inspections to a contract number. Missing required fields become asks. They sign. You list.
