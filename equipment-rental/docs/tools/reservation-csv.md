# Reservation export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The reservation CSV is holds and requested dates: renter, start, end, class or unit, delivery or will-call, and status reserved versus cancelled. Typical US SMB desks export the day or week. You do not invent a hold. Overlaps stay visible. Rates on this file are used only if they already came from the rate file. Checks look for date and unit or category columns when asked. No portal login belongs here. This tool starts the reservation-day pack and flags overbook questions.
