# Inspection sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Outgoing and return checklists: hours, fuel, damage notes, kit, manual present, signatures if they use them. You record completeness and differences. You do not invent fuel or hours. You do not diagnose. This tool is required for outgoing-inspection and return-damage workflows. Checks look for unit and contract or date columns when asked. Photos are a sister folder. They decide charges from their fee file after the sheet exists. Licensed-operator and height asks stay checkboxes, not stamps.
