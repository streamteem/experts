# Delivery zone sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The zone sheet maps zip, city, or mileage rings to a delivery price or letter. You look up; you do not invent a zone. Missing address: ask. After-hours columns apply only if present. This tool supports delivery-ticket packs. Checks can require zone and price or code columns they use. You do not pay freight. You do not engineer a route or promise a time window they did not write. Multi-yard shops use the sheet for the yard on the ticket.
