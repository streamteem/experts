# Rate book or price file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The rate file is the dated day/week/month book, plus weekend, holiday, environmental, and delivery columns they keep. You copy cells. You do not invent a price. Expired books are a question. Overrides need their exception note. Checks can be file-exists on the work extract. No competitor book in your head. This tool prices contract-completeness packs and any utilization-dollar view they asked for. Starter rates are not this file. Fuel, cleaning, waiver, and RPP still need their own policy lines when those sit outside the book.
