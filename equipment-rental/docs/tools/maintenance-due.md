# Maintenance-due file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The maintenance-due sheet or export lists unit, due date or due hours, and shop flags. You list overdue and conflicts with reservations. You do not invent an interval and you do not diagnose the repair. Checks look for unit and due columns when the workflow asks. No dealer-portal password belongs here. This tool drives the maintenance-due pack and should be compared to the fleet list so serials match. Overdue rows stay visible even when a reservation wants the unit.
