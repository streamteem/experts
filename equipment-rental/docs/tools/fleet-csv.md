# Fleet export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The fleet CSV is their unit list: unit id, category, serial if they use one, status, yard, and often meter and maintenance flags. Typical US SMB yards export from the rental system or keep a workbook. You list and compare from that file. You do not invent a unit or a serial. Status words stay as they coded them. Checks look for unit and status columns when the workflow asks. No system password is stored here. This tool feeds reservation-day, utilization, maintenance conflicts, and cycle-count packs.
