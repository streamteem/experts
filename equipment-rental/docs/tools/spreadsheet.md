# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV or workbook you build in work/ for lists: reservations, utilization, exceptions. Columns come from their exports, not from invented rates or hours. Typical US SMB desks live in Excel beside the rental system. Checks use csv-has-columns or file-exists as the step says. No password to a cloud sheet is stored here. This tool is the usual pack-out surface for write-ups they will read. Draft versus issued statuses stay as they coded them.
