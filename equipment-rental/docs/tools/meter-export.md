# Meter or telematics export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hour-meter or GPS/telematics exports they already ran: unit, reading, timestamp, sometimes a ping. You copy readings. You do not invent hours or a location. Portal passwords stay out. Disagreements with the inspection sheet are exceptions. This tool feeds meter-exception and overage-prep packs. Checks look for unit and hours or reading columns when asked. Theft packs may use last ping only as their export shows. You do not diagnose a sender that disagrees with the gauge.
