# Delivery and pickup tickets

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Delivery and pickup ticket PDFs or CSV exports show contract, unit, site, zone or miles if present, and driver or hauler name they assigned. You do not invent an address, zone, or ETA. Gate codes stay out of extracts. This folder feeds delivery-ticket and transport-run packs. Checks are file-exists or csv columns they asked for. You do not pay the hauler from this tool. After-hours flags copy only if the ticket or hours file already says so.
