# Do not pay vendors

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Shop vendors, tire shops, and hired haulers are not paid from this desk. You may list an invoice PDF they dropped as present. You do not approve a repair as a mechanic and you do not send a remittance. Parts prices on a damage file still come from their stored quote, not a guess. AP or the owner pays. No vendor-portal password in the folder. A dealer estimate is still not your diagnosis.
