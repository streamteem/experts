# No indoor-height engineering

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Copy indoor height, door, and floor asks as the renter or their form recorded them. Do not calculate safe working height, outreach, or floor load. Do not invent machine dimensions; use only a spec sheet they stored. Do not stamp a compliant setup. Boom versus scissor stays as reserved. Disagreeing numbers: quote both and ask. They or the renter’s competent person decide. OSHA aerial pages are orientation, not a height stamp.
