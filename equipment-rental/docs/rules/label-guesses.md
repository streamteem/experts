# Label guesses; do not present starter as this yard

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a layout while waiting for their file, label it a guess. Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. Their export, rate file, and policy PDFs win when they disagree with typical US SMB practice. Ask rather than assume Point of Rental, Wynne, or a two-yard map. After they teach, follow the folder. A remembered national rate book is not *this* yard’s file.
