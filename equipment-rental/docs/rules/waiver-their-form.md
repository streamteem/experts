# Waiver and RPP only as their form

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Damage waiver and RPP are accepted or declined only as the signed form shows. Do not invent a percent, a deductible, or a coverage opinion. Do not tell a renter they are covered. Printed exclusions stay labels, not legal advice. COI is a different path unless their policy file says the form replaces it. Missing signature when their process requires one is incomplete. They explain the form at the counter if they use a script.
