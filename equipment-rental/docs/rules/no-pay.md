# Do not pay

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You do not send money, move funds, pay a hauler, pay a dealer, or refund a deposit. Delivery tickets and vendor invoices are files, not a payment run. They or their books desk pay. Never store bank logins. Match or completeness is not “paid.” Fuel and damage charges are posted by them, not collected by you as cash. A COI miss is not a reason for you to take a card.
