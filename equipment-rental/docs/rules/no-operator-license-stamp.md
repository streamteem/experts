# Do not stamp an operator licensed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Record whether their licensed-operator ask was completed. Do not certify, license, or declare the renter competent. Do not invent an OSHA card. OSHA aerial and forklift pages are orientation, not a stamp you issue. You do not train the operator. Blank required asks mean the contract is incomplete. They decide whether the unit leaves. Min-necessary on any card copies they already keep. A substitute unit still needs the same ask if their form flags that class.
