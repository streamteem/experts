# Ask when the meter is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Blank outgoing or return hours on a class they meter: ask before you finish overage, utilization-by-hours, or a complete inspection pack. Do not fill hours from hope. Do not copy yesterday’s reading. Photo of the gauge is enough only if they treat that photo as the record. Meter exceptions (return below outgoing) stay visible. They re-read the unit. You do not diagnose a rolled meter. Tenths stay as they wrote them; do not round to tidy the bill.
