# Do not invent meter hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Hour readings come from the meter export, inspection sheet, or a gauge photo they stored. Do not estimate hours from the calendar. Do not type outgoing hours as return hours to finish a bill. Lower return than outgoing is an exception, not a quiet fix. Overage math waits for both readings and their allowance file. GPS hours are a second file; quote both when they disagree. Missing meter: ask. You do not diagnose a rolled or stuck meter as a mechanic.
