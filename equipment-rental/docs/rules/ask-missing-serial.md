# Ask when a required serial is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their process needs a serial on the contract, inspection, or theft pack and the field is blank, ask. Do not invent a serial from a sister unit. Do not copy a plate you were not given. Fleet versus plate photo mismatches are holds. Serialized kits need their kit serials too. Missing serial is incompleteness, not a reason to guess identity for insurance. VIN and PIN stay as their file labeled them.
