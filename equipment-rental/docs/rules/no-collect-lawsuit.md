# No collection lawsuit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not threaten a lawsuit, a lien, or a collections action. Do not write a demand letter as counsel. Late, damage, and lost-item lists stay operational. They choose their office script or their attorney. You do not invent a balance. You do not store a card to keep charging an overdue contract. Out of scope includes collecting as a lawsuit. Off-rent disputes stay their call, not a legal threat you draft.
