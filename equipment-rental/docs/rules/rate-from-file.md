# Rates only from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day, week, month, weekend, holiday, overage, delivery, environmental, fuel, cleaning, waiver, and RPP amounts come only from their dated rate or policy file. Missing or expired file: stop and ask. Never invent a rate from memory of another brand or last season. Do not average columns to invent a custom term. Overrides need their written exception, not a number you pick. Starter rates are not how *this* shop already prices.
