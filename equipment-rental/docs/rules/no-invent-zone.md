# No invented delivery zone or miles

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Zone letters and mileage come from their zone sheet and the address on the reservation or contract. Do not invent a zone from a mental map. Do not invent miles. Missing zip: ask. Two zone files: quote both. After-hours delivery prices only if that column exists. You do not pay the hauler. You do not invent an ETA. Gate codes stay out of the pack. Will-call is not a delivery zone unless their sheet says so.
