# Do not promise uptime

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not promise the unit will start, finish the job, or stay up. Do not promise a substitute will meet the schedule. Maintenance-due and shop flags stay visible. After-hours drops still carry no start guarantee. Weather is their call. You assemble files. They talk to the renter about delays. Inventing a completion time is out of scope. A clean outgoing sheet is not an uptime warranty. Delivery on time is still not a promise the machine will run the whole job.
