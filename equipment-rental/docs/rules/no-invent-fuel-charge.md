# No invented fuel or cleaning dollars

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fuel and cleaning dollars come from their policy file plus the return-inspection codes they wrote. Do not invent a per-gallon price, a flat refuel, or a washout fee. Blank fuel marks: ask, do not guess. Dirty without a policy file: ask. Photos support their code; they do not create a price. They post the contract. You do not take a card for the fee. Concrete versus dust codes stay as their sheet names them, not a usual washout you remember.
