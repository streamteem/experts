# Damage needs their photos when required

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their charge or insurance process requires photos, a damage note without a matching image is incomplete. Do not invent a photo. Do not crop away the mark. Name files to unit, contract, and in or out as they asked. Photos do not authorize a diagnosis or a repair dollar. Pre-existing needs the outgoing photo when they compare. Missing photos: say missing. They send images to an insurer if they choose. Card-PAN photos never belong in this folder.
