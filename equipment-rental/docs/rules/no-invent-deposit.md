# No invented deposit

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Deposit and hold amounts come from their deposit-policy file or the printed contract term. Do not invent “one week plus tax.” Do not run a card. Do not store PAN. If policy and contract disagree, quote both and ask. Refunds are their books process. Missing policy file: ask. Cash versus account differences stay as they wrote them. A COI account is not a deposit you invent. Event versus long-term deposits still follow the same stored file.
