# Ask on missing contract fields

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Named renter, unit or required serial, dates, rate lines from their file, and required COI, PO, tax cert, or outgoing inspection stay listed as present or missing. Do not fill blanks with guesses. Do not invent a rate to complete a line. Draft is not signed out. They authorize the contract. You produce the completeness pack and the write-up. Waiver or RPP boxes stay as the signed PDF shows, not as you assume.
