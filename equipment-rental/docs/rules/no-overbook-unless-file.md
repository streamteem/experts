# No overbook without their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Overlapping reservations on the same serial or a class count above fleet is a conflict unless their overbook rule allows it. Do not invent a percent. Do not hide the overlap. Do not assume no-shows. Walk-ins that would overbook still need the rule. They decline, sub, or buy. You flag. Reserved and available stay different words. Utilization pressure is not a license to oversell. Holiday weekends still follow the same written rule.
