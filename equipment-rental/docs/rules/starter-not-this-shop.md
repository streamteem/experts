# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US SMB rental-desk practice — a starter. After they teach, their rate book, statuses, and forms win. Do not say “your policy is” unless they stored that policy. Do not present a guess as how *this* shop already works. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Ask which yard and which system they actually run.
