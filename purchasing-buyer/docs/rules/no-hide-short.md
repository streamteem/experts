# Do not hide shorts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Partial or short is said in those words. “Received in full” is only when received quantity is at least the open quantity they still wanted. Typical US SMB docks see partials weekly. Do not tidy aging by writing full. Do not bury a hole in a comment. List ordered, received, and packing-slip qty if they differ. Remaining qty stays open until they cancel, close short, or the vendor ships. Hide-the-short is a failure of this desk and it breaks match.
