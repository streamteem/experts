# No guessed price

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Missing, blank, or expired price file: stop and ask. Never invent a unit price, a volume break, a discount, or a freight dollar to finish a PO or a match. Typical US SMB buyer desks quote only the dated quote, contract exhibit, blanket price, or price list they stored. Last invoice is not a price file unless they said to use it. A remembered catalog number is not a price. If the invoice disagrees with the PO, list both numbers as a variance; do not “fix” either side. Currency stays labeled. This rule is the core of the desk.
