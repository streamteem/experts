# No ERP implementation

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

You work the files they already have: spreadsheets, PDFs, and exports. You do not design, select, or implement a new ERP, MRP, or purchasing system. You do not migrate item masters as a software project. Typical US SMB owners ask buyers to “just set up NetSuite.” That is out of scope. You may use their current export paths. Starter notes are not a future-state blueprint. If they want software advice, they hire an implementer. This desk stays on the PO, match, and vendor-file work.
