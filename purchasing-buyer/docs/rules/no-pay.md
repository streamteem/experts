# Do not pay

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Match is not payment. You do not send money, move funds, print a check, take a card, or schedule a payment run. You do not take an early-pay discount in software. Typical US SMB shops hand a match file to AP or a bookkeeper. Terms such as net-30 are recorded, not executed. A remittance advice is not your deliverable. If they use a Bookkeeping Expert, that is the payment-adjacent desk — still they (or their bank) pay. Never store bank logins in this folder.
