# Hand off payment to books if they split

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

When AP is another person or a Bookkeeping Expert, your done is the match file and exception list, not a remittance, not a check run, and not a “paid” label. Typical US SMB shops split buyer and books. Invoice coding to the chart may be theirs or books — copy the account on the requisition if they put one. You still do not send money. Non-PO invoices follow their AP rule. Hand off cleanly. Do not keep the payment job on this desk.
