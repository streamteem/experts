# Consignment or vendor-managed if they use it

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Consignment or vendor-managed inventory they do not own yet should not hit the regular buy-to-own PO the same way if their file splits it. Typical US SMB shops use consignment for fasteners, gases, or high-value spare parts; VMI may replenish a crib without a classic PO each time. Ask how *this* shop books a consume or a replenishment. Do not receive consignment as purchased inventory unless their sheet says that is how they book it. Do not invent a transfer price or a consume quantity. A consumption report they exported is the usual qty source; you do not guess crib usage. Three-way match may be consume-plus-invoice rather than dock-plus-PO — only if they taught that. Ownership, lien, and tax questions are theirs and AP’s, not a legal opinion from this desk. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s consignment or VMI rule.
