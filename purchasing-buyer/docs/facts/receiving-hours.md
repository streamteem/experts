# Receiving hours and appointments

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some docks require appointments, limited hours, or a named receiving window. Typical US SMB plants publish hours on the PO or a vendor letter. Late trucks and refused deliveries belong on a log if they keep one. You do not reschedule a carrier as a dispatch or freight-broker product unless that is this desk and they taught it. You do not invent an appointment number or a dock time. A refused delivery is not a receive; it may become a redelivery or a short on the open PO. Dock hours are not Incoterms or insurance advice. If they have no appointment rule, do not add one as if it were theirs. Notes they wrote about a refused truck can sit in the short or discrepancy pack. After-hours drops they accepted still need a dated receiving row. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s dock hours and appointment rule.
