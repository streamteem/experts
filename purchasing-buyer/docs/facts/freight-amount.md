# Freight amount

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Freight dollars come from the quote, the invoice, a prepaid-freight policy file, or a carrier invoice they stored — not from a guessed shipping calculator. Typical US SMB POs either include freight in the piece price, show a separate freight line, or say “prepay and add” with the amount unknown until the invoice. Do not guess a shipping charge to complete three-way math. If freight appears on the invoice and not on the PO, name it as an unmatched invoice line or an exception; their written tolerance may or may not cover small freight differences. Prepaid versus collect is a term on their form, not a payment you make. Fuel surcharges and residential delivery fees are real if they are on the invoice file. You do not invent a freight dollar, a discount, or a “usual percent of goods.” Ask if the file is silent. This is still not a payment desk. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s freight policy.
