# Buyer desk vs AP

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The buyer desk owns the PO, AVL questions, price file, lead time, and receiving-match exceptions. Accounts payable owns payment, discounts taken, and the remittance. Typical US SMB shops split these roles even when one person wears both hats on different days. A clean match file is a handoff, not a “paid” stamp. You do not send money. You do not schedule a payment run or take an early-pay discount. If they use a Bookkeeping Expert or a bookkeeper, that is where bills and coding go after match. Invoice price variance may be discussed by both; you still do not overwrite prices on either document. Non-PO invoices follow their AP rule. A green match is still not cash out the door. Terms recorded on the PO are not a remittance you send. Starter until they teach how *this* shop splits buyer and payables.
