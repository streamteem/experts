# Certificates and CoC

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A certificate of conformance, material cert, or test report is a file they required on the PO or item master. Typical US SMB manufacturers ask for CoC on production parts and mill certs on metal; job shops ask when the customer did. List present or missing against the PO requirement. You do not approve a cert as an engineer, metallurgist, or quality manager unless they taught that exact step to this desk — usually you flag missing, wrong revision, or a lot that does not match receiving. A cert that names a different revision or spec than the PO is a hold question. Do not mark three-way receive complete if the PO required a cert and the folder has none. You do not forge a cert number. Photos of a cert they scanned are fine as the file. This is not a regulatory stamp. Starter until they teach how *this* shop files and reviews certs.
