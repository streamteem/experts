# Cancel or close short

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Closing a PO short — remaining quantity will not ship — or canceling a line is their decision, not a tidy-up you apply to the aging report. Typical US SMB desks cancel for a dead job, a substitute, a vendor who cannot ship, or a duplicate order. You list remaining open quantity and the last vendor note from the file. Do not zero an open line to clean aging. Do not write “closed” unless their export or a signed close-short note says so. A cancel may still leave a restocking charge or a vendor claim; those dollars come from a vendor file, not a guess, and payment of any charge is AP. Receiving that arrives after they closed short is a new question (keep, return, or new PO). You do not invent a cancel reason. You do not email the vendor a cancel as this product unless they taught that send step and the line is approved to cancel. Starter until they teach how *this* shop closes shorts.
