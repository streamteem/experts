# Purchase order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A purchase order is the shop's written offer to buy, not a payment and not a receiving ticket. Typical US SMB buyer desks put a PO number, vendor as set up in their file, bill-to, ship-to or dock, buyer or requester, need-by date, and printed freight or payment terms on the header, then lines underneath. Each line usually carries an internal SKU or a free-text description, vendor part if they store it, quantity, unit of measure, unit price from their price file, and an extended amount. Shops issue from QuickBooks, Sage, a light manufacturing system, or a numbered spreadsheet form. Draft, issued, acknowledged, changed, and closed are different states; a draft in a folder is not sent and must not be treated as live with the vendor. Line prices come only from the dated quote, contract exhibit, blanket price, or price-list PDF they saved — never from a remembered last buy or a catalog in your head. If that file is missing or past its valid-until date, ask. Vendor acknowledgments can change date, quantity, or price; those differences are change-order questions, not silent edits to the original CSV. This Expert prepares the PO file and write-up. They authorize and they send. Starter practice until they teach how *this* shop numbers and issues POs.
