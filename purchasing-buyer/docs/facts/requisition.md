# Requisition before the PO

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Many US SMB shops start the buy with a job, crib, toolroom, or department requisition: who asked, SKU or description, quantity, need-by, and sometimes account or job number. The PO should trace to that request when they use one. Typical desks collect requisitions on paper, in email PDFs, or on a shared sheet; MRP suggested orders are a different file and still need a human request or approval in many shops. Do not invent a need-by to make planning look healthy. If requisition quantity and the suggested PO quantity differ — MOQ round-up, safety stock, or a planner add — list both numbers and say why only if their files say why. A requisition is not an issued PO and is not authorization to send to the vendor. Dummy or non-inventory requests still need a description and a price file. You assemble the trace from request to draft PO. They approve. Starter until they teach how *this* shop raises a need.
