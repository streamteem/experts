# RFQ pack from files

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A request-for-quote pack is the item list, quantities, need-by, and the drawings or specs they attached, assembled so invited vendors see the same package when that is their rule. Typical US SMB desks RFQ fasteners, fabricated parts, and one-time buys from two or three vendors on the AVL or a bid list. You assemble from their files. They send. They award. Do not send the RFQ as this product. Tabulate quotes only when PDFs or emails arrive in the folder; blank cells stay blank. Same package to each invited vendor unless they wrote an exception. Do not invent a drawing revision, a tolerance, or a material spec. If the item master revision and the attached drawing disagree, quote both and ask. Sole-source RFQs still need their justification file if they require one. You do not declare a market has only one bidder. Starter until they teach how *this* shop invites and awards.
