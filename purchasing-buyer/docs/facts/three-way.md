# Three-way match

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Three-way match is the usual control that compares the purchase order, the goods receipt or receiving sheet, and the vendor invoice on SKU or description, quantity, unit of measure, and unit price before anyone in accounts payable pays. Typical US SMB desks run it in the accounting system, on a spreadsheet, or as a paper packet with the three files clipped together. If a leg is missing, name which file is absent — PO, receiving, or invoice — and do not call the pack a three-way match. Match is a completeness and exception list, not a payment, not a remittance, and not a “paid” stamp. A two-way match (PO to invoice only) is common for services and subscriptions because there is no dock count; it misses receiving shorts on physical goods. Do not relabel a two-way file as three-way. Quantity exceptions, price variances, freight not on the PO, and UOM mismatches belong on an exception list using their written tolerance if they have one. Sage-style and AP training treat the match as a payables control; this desk hands a clean or exceptioned file to AP. They pay. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s match rule.
