# Shortage and backorder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If receiving quantity is less than the PO open quantity the shop still wanted, the line is a short or a partial — say those words. Typical US SMB docks see partials every week: the vendor backordered, the truck left pieces, or the packing slip overstated what was in the carton. Do not write “received in full” when pieces are missing. The backordered remainder stays open until they cancel, close short, or the vendor ships. Hide-the-short is a failure of this desk and it breaks three-way match later. Compare dock count to the packing slip and to the PO; if those three numbers disagree, list all three. Photos or dock notes they stored belong in the short pack they send to the vendor. You do not invent a received quantity to tidy the aging report. You do not zero the open remainder. They contact the vendor unless they asked you only to list. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes shorts versus backorders.
