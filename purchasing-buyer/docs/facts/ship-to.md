# Ship-to and dock

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ship-to is the address or dock the vendor must use. Wrong ship-to is a real miss: tools to the office, resin to the wrong bay, a pallet to the warehouse when the job site was on the requisition. Typical US SMB shops have a default plant address, sometimes a second warehouse, and occasional drop-ship to a customer or job site. Use the address their PO process names — item master default, requisition override, or printed PO form. Drop-ship only if the requisition or job file says so, and then the receiving evidence may be a customer packing slip they forwarded rather than a dock count. Do not invent a job-site address from a memory of last month’s job. Bill-to and ship-to are not always the same. If the quote has a different ship-to than the PO form, quote both and ask. You do not reschedule a carrier as a dispatch product unless this desk was taught that step. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s docks and drop-ship rule.
