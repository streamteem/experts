# Vendor invoices (for match, not payment)

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor invoices they dropped are the third match leg, not a payment instruction you execute. You compare SKU, qty, UOM, and price to the PO and receiving. You do not pay, schedule, or mark paid. Proforma and acknowledgments are not invoices; name the document type. Freight or tax not on the PO is an exception. No AP-system password is stored here. This tool feeds the match pack. If the invoice is absent, name the missing leg instead of inventing amounts.
