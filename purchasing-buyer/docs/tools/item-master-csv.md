# Item master export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The item-master export carries internal SKU, UOM, revision, preferred vendor, min/max, and flags such as lot or cert required. Typical US SMB manufacturers export this from the ERP or keep a workbook. Stale masters cause bad POs. If master and quote disagree, quote both and ask. You do not invent a SKU or a UOM factor. You do not add a new item unless they taught that step. This tool supports quote-versus-master packs, UOM checks, and preferred-vendor versus AVL questions.
