# Requisition / need sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The requisition or need sheet is who asked, SKU or description, qty, need-by, and sometimes job or account. Typical US SMB floors use a shared workbook, email PDFs, or crib tickets. The PO should trace here when they use requisitions. You do not invent a need-by. If requisition qty and suggested PO qty differ, list both. A requisition is not authorization to send. Checks look for sku and qty on RFQ item files. This tool starts AVL-check and RFQ packs.
