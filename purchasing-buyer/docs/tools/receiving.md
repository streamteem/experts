# Receiving sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The receiving sheet or goods-receipt export is dock truth: PO line, SKU, qty counted, date, lot if they use it, and damage or hold flags. Typical US SMB docks write a workbook or scan a GRN. You do not type a count you were not given. Partials show this receipt, cumulative, and still open. Shorts stay visible. Damaged qty stays off the good column. Checks expect a receiving file with po, sku, and qty when the workflow asks for them. This tool is the second leg of three-way match and the source for short packs.
