# Blanket PO log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The blanket log holds blanket PO number, period, not-to-exceed or remaining qty if they track it, price-file pointer, and release history. Releases must carry the blanket number. You do not invent remaining NTE. You do not contradict the blanket price without asking. A release draft is still a draft. Checks look for a blanket_po column when the workflow says so. This tool is the source for blanket-release packs. They approve and they send the release.
