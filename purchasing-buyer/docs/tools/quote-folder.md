# RFQ / quote comparison folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The RFQ or quote-comparison folder holds multiple vendor PDFs for one item list. You tabulate price, validity, MOQ, lead time, and exceptions as written. They award. You do not pick a winner to hurry the pack. Blank cells stay blank. Same item list and specs to each invited vendor if that is their rule. Expired quotes stay labeled expired. No bidding-site login is stored here. This tool is the quote side of an RFQ pack after requisitions name the items.
