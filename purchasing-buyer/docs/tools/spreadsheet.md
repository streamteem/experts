# PO / open-order sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The open-order or PO spreadsheet is their export or working CSV: typically po, sku, qty, need-by, vendor, and sometimes unit price, UOM, and status. Typical US SMB desks export from QuickBooks, Sage, or a purchasing add-on, or keep a numbered workbook. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a PO number or fill blank prices. Export from their system; no login or password is stored in this folder. Checks look for po, sku, and qty columns on the work file. Issued versus draft should stay as they coded it. This tool is the usual source for open-PO, commitment, late-line, and change-order before/after packs.
