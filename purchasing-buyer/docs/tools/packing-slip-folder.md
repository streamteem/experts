# Packing slips

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Scanned packing slips, packing lists, and ASN files they saved sit here for comparison to dock count and PO. The slip is what the vendor thinks they shipped, not a receipt. ASN is a heads-up. List slip qty next to counted qty when they differ. Substitute SKUs on a slip are hold questions. You do not invent a slip line. Missing slip: say missing. This folder supports short packs and three-way evidence. No carrier-account login belongs here.
