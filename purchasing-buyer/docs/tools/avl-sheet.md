# Approved vendor list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The approved-vendor list is the export they maintain: vendor name, status, and sometimes commodity or expiration. A pack checks proposed names against it. You do not add a row as approved and you do not invent an AVL if they have none. One-time and sole-source names stay “not on list” until they add them. Inactive or conditional stays as written. No vendor-portal password belongs in the folder. Checks look for a vendor column. This tool gates PO drafts and RFQ invite lists when they use an AVL.
