# Quotes, acknowledgments, and contracts

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vendor PDFs and emails they dropped are the source for unit price, validity date, MOQ, pack, lead time, freight term, and acknowledgments. Typical US SMB buyers keep a folder by vendor and date. You copy numbers as written. You do not guess a price or a two-week lead time. Expired valid-until dates become asks. OA versus quote versus proforma stay labeled by document type. No portal login belongs here. This tool feeds quote-lead-MOQ packs, backorder promise dates, and change-order triggers. If the folder is empty, ask; do not use memory.
