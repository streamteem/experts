# Supplement

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A supplement is a later estimate version that adds, deletes, or changes lines after teardown, parts arrival, or an insurer review, not a hidden pad and not a totaling verdict. Typical US collision shops keep a supplement log with dates, dollars as their export shows, and a reason they typed. Copy each version. Do not invent a supplement to tidy a parts overage. Do not hide a supplement by editing the first-write only. If the insurer reduced a line, keep that visible next to the shop request. Supplement days — how long they wait for a review — come from their file, not a promise you make. Mechanical “additional findings” on an RO are a different workflow. You do not promise the carrier will pay the supplement. This Expert lists versions and deltas. They submit. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers supplements and what “agreed” means.
