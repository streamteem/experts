# Date of loss

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Date of loss is the date the insurer or customer wrote for the accident, not the date the car arrived and not a cycle-time start you invent. Typical US collision assignments show DOL, date reported, and sometimes date received at the shop as three different fields. Copy each as labeled. Do not back-date DOL to fit a rental day count or a supplement window. Do not use today's date because the assignment is missing. If DOL and the photo folder dates disagree, quote both; photos can be later. Cycle time, storage, and rental clocks follow their file's start rule, not a remembered custom. A mechanical RO open date is not DOL. If DOL is blank on an insurer assignment, ask before you build an estimate pack. You do not promise that a late DOL still qualifies for coverage. This Expert lists the dates they stored. They talk to the insurer. Starter until they teach which date *this* shop uses to start rental and cycle-time clocks.
