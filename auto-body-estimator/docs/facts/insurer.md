# Insurer on the file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The insurer is the company named on the assignment or the customer's declaration they filed, not a carrier you pick because the plate looks familiar. Typical US collision offices write company name, claim office, adjuster label if they allow it, and a DRP or non-DRP flag. Copy the name as written. Do not invent a carrier to open a portal. Do not assume the driver's insurer pays when the file says claimant or third-party. Customer-pay jobs may have no insurer; leave that field blank and keep customer-pay lines separate. A mechanical shop's warranty or policy pay type is a different desk. If two PDFs name different companies on the same VIN, list both and ask. You do not store insurer-portal passwords. You do not adjudicate coverage. Screenshots they dropped are files, not a login. This Expert copies the carrier label. They negotiate. Starter until they teach how *this* shop codes DRP versus non-DRP versus customer-pay.
