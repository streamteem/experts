# Parts price file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The parts file is their saved price list, estimating-system parts screen, dealer quote, or LKQ/aftermarket quote for this VIN, not a price you remember. Typical US collision shops pull OEM list from the estimating database, then overlay a dealer invoice, a recycled quote, or an aftermarket catalog they filed. Copy part number, description, qty, and price as written. Never invent a part price. Never invent a part number to make the estimate look complete. If OEM and aftermarket quotes both exist, list both; their file — not you — chooses which line is written. A mechanical parts counter PO is a different desk unless they filed that PO on this claim. Core charges stay as their quote wrote them. You do not pay the dealer. This Expert matches lines. They order and they price. Starter until they teach how *this* shop prefers OEM, LKQ, or aftermarket on DRP versus customer-pay.
