# Claim number

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The claim number is the insurer's handle for this loss, copied onto the estimate, photos, parts POs, and supplement log so those files stay tied. Typical US collision shops also keep a shop RO or estimate number that is not the claim number; keep both fields. Do not invent a claim number to tidy a board. Do not reuse last month's number because the VIN matches. If the assignment number and the estimate header disagree, quote both and ask which wins. A draft estimate without a claim number on a DRP job is a missing-field flag, not a reason to invent one. Customer-pay files may have no claim number; do not invent one. You do not call the claim paid because a number exists. CIECA-style electronic assignments still get copied as their export wrote them. This Expert traces files by the number they wrote. They open the claim with the carrier. Starter until they teach how *this* shop pairs shop RO numbers with claim numbers.
