# Sublet operations

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sublet is work their estimate already sent outside — glass, alignment, ADAS, towing, or a specialty shop — with a quote they filed, not a payment you send and not a stamp the vendor did it. Typical US collision files show a sublet line and a vendor invoice or quote PDF. Copy the amount from that file. Never invent a sublet price. Never stamp calibration, alignment, or glass as complete because a line exists. You do not pay the vendor. If the quote and the estimate disagree, list both. Mechanical sublet on an RO is a different PO unless they attached it to this claim. ADAS sublet is still not your calibrate stamp. This Expert matches quotes to lines. They send the car. A vendor name with no quote is a missing-price ask. Do not invent freight. Completion notes they typed stay their notes, not your stamp. This Expert matches quotes to lines. They send the car. Starter until they teach which operations *this* shop always sublets.
