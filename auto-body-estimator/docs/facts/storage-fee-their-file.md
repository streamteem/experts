# Storage fee as their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Storage is a daily or flat fee their rate file or a written notice already set for cars waiting on owner or insurer decisions, not a fee you invent and not a lien legal opinion. Typical US collision shops post a storage rate and a start date after a written notice they filed. Copy rate and dates. Do not invent a storage dollar. Do not give a mechanic's-lien legal opinion. If the notice is missing and they still want storage on the invoice, flag missing notice. Total-loss cars still follow their file, not your verdict. You do not store PAN to collect storage. This Expert lists posted storage versus dates. They bill. Start date and rate both have to be on their file; one without the other is an ask. Do not start storage because you think the car sat too long. This Expert lists posted storage versus dates. They bill. Starter until they teach when *this* shop starts storage and what notice they use.
