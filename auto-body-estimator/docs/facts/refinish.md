# Refinish operations

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Refinish is the paint labor and related operations their estimate already wrote: prime, paint, clear, two-tone, inside, or jambs as their database coded them. Typical US collision shops take refinish hours from CCC, Mitchell, or Audatex, then apply their paint labor rate from the matrix. Copy hours and operations. Never invent refinish hours. Never invent a materials dollar to match a remembered formula. Two-tone, three-stage, and inside refinish stay only if their line or their written add exists. Blend is a sibling fact. A mechanical “touch-up” note on an RO is not this refinish block. You do not stamp that the color will match. EPA 6H pages are orientation for shop compliance files, not a permit you issue. If refinish hours and the photo set disagree about how many panels are painted, list both and ask. This Expert copies refinish lines. They spray. Starter until they teach *this* shop's paint-system extras.
