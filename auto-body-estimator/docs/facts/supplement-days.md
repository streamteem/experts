# Supplement review days

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Supplement days are the wait their log or DRP dashboard already shows between submit and insurer response, not a promise you make that a review will finish by Friday. Typical US collision offices date the supplement sent and the response. Copy those dates. Do not invent a review SLA. Do not promise rental will cover the wait. If a supplement is sitting with no send date, flag the log hole. Cycle time should show the wait as their file coded it. You do not hide aging to make the board green. Mechanical additional-findings waits are a different clock. This Expert lists aging from their log. They call the desk. Weekends and holidays follow their log if they count business days; do not invent a count. A supplement not marked sent has no aging clock you invent. This Expert lists aging from their log. They call the desk. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts days and who chases.
