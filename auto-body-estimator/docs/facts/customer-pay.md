# Customer-pay lines

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Customer-pay is work or dollars the customer — not the insurer — is buying on this visit: betterment they accepted, unrelated damage, upgrades, or a purely retail estimate. Typical US collision shops keep CP lines on a separate pay type or a second estimate so DRP totals stay clean. Copy the split as they coded it. Do not roll CP into insurer lines to make a supplement look smaller. Do not invent a CP price. Deductible is not the same as CP unless they coded it that way. Mechanical customer-pay on a service RO is a different ticket. You do not store PAN to collect CP. If the customer refused CP and the estimate still has it, list both. This Expert separates pay types. They sell the CP. Starter until they teach how *this* shop prints CP versus insurer copies.
