# Teardown / disassembly

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Teardown is the authorized disassembly that lets them see hidden damage, not a license for you to add hours and not a supplement you invent. Typical US collision shops write a teardown authorization, then a first estimate that may say “teardown pending” on some lines. Copy their teardown hours from the matrix or the written auth. Do not invent teardown time. Do not treat teardown as a total-loss verdict. After teardown, new lines belong on a supplement they write, not on a silent edit you make. Photos after teardown should be named; if they are missing, flag. Mechanical disassembly for diagnosis is a different desk. If teardown auth is missing and the car is apart, that is an authorization-missing ask. This Expert lists teardown status versus auth. They tear down and they write the next estimate. Starter until they teach when *this* shop requires written teardown auth.
