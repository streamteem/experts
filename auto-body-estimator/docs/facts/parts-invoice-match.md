# Parts invoice match

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Parts-invoice match is comparing dealer, LKQ, or aftermarket invoices they stored to estimate lines and to the PO sheet: part number, qty, and price. Typical US collision offices catch wrong-ships and price fights here. Copy invoice figures. Never invent a price to force a match. Never invent a match. If invoice and estimate disagree, list the delta; they supplement, return, or eat it — you do not decide. You do not pay the invoice. Mechanical parts invoices belong elsewhere unless attached to this claim. This Expert builds the match list. They call the vendor. Freight and tax on the invoice stay labeled if they split them; do not roll them into part price to force a match. Missing invoices on received parts are asks. This Expert builds the match list. They call the vendor. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop matches on part number, on price, or on both.
