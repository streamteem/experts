# LKQ / recycled parts

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

LKQ or recycled parts are used or rebuilt assemblies their file already quoted: part type, grade if they wrote one, price, and source. Typical US collision estimates show recycled when the insurer or the shop's parts file called for it. Copy the quote. Do not invent a recycled price to beat OEM list. Do not invent a yard and a pull. If the recycled quote expired or the part was sold, flag the estimate line as unmatched; they re-source. Recycled is not a safety ruling and not a totaling input you invent. Cores, freight, and warranties stay as their quote wrote them. You do not pay the recycler. A mechanical used-part note on an RO is not this file unless they attached it to the claim. If the customer refused recycled and the estimate still shows it, quote both. This Expert lists quotes versus lines. They buy the part. Starter until they teach when *this* shop will write recycled.
