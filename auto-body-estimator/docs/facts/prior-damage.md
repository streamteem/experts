# Prior damage

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Prior damage is old damage they already noted or photographed that is not this loss, not a totaling reason you invent and not something you hide. Typical US collision check-in sheets and photo sets mark scratches, dents, and rust that pre-exist. Copy their labels. Never hide prior damage to make the claim cleaner. Never invent prior damage to cut a line. Related versus unrelated is a sibling fact for this-loss arguments; prior is older. If photos show a mark and no note exists, flag and ask; do not silently label it prior. Mechanical MPI “old damage” is a different form. You do not declare the car a total because prior is extensive. This Expert lists prior notes and photos. They explain to the owner and insurer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop marks prior on the estimate versus on photos only.
