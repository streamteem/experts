# Tow-in

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Tow-in is the wrecker ticket or assignment note that the vehicle arrived by tow, with a vendor and an amount if they filed a bill, not a payment you send and not a storage start you invent. Typical US collision files show tow on the assignment or a towing invoice in the folder. Copy vendor, date, and amount as written. Do not invent a tow charge. Do not pay the wrecker. If tow destination and vehicle-location disagree, list both. A mechanical shop tow on an RO is a different ticket unless attached here. Storage and keys still follow their logs.
