# Mechanical refer-out

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mechanical refer-out is a line or note that this collision file is sending drivability, airbag, or other mechanical work to a technician or another shop, not a diagnosis you write and not a service-advisor RO you invent. Typical US body shops note “refer to mechanical” or a sublet mechanical quote. Copy that note. Never diagnose the mechanical complaint. Never invent book time from a service labor guide you remember — that is the other desk. If they attached a mechanical RO or a scan, treat it as their file, not your cause. Airbag and SRS notes stay as they wrote them; you still do not stamp safe. This Expert flags refer-out so the estimate pack does not pretend the body shop diagnosed it. They dispatch mechanical. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop has an in-house mechanical stall or always sublets.
