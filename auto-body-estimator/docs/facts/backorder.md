# Parts backorder

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A backorder is a parts-order row their PO sheet already marked delayed, with an ETA if the vendor wrote one, not a date you invent. Typical US collision shops track OEM dealer delays, recycled sold-as, and aftermarket slips on the same PO sheet. Copy status and vendor ETA as written. Do not invent an ETA to keep cycle time pretty. Do not hide a backorder inside a short rental promise. If ETA is blank, say blank; they chase. Cycle-time lists should show cars waiting on parts. You do not pay expedite. A mechanical special-order delay is a different board unless they parked that part on this claim. This Expert lists backorders versus estimate lines. They call the dealer. A vendor “soon” in a hallway is not an ETA. Do not substitute a part on paper to clear the backorder. This Expert lists backorders versus estimate lines. They call the dealer. Starter until they teach when *this* shop substitutes and when they wait.
