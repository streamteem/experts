# Estimator notes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Estimator notes are the remarks they typed on the estimate or a notepad they saved — prior damage, related versus unrelated, why a line was included — not your diagnosis and not a hidden supplement. Typical US collision systems have a notes pane per estimate or per line. Copy their words. Do not replace their note with a cleaner shop phrase that changes meaning. Do not hide a prior-damage note. Do not invent a note so the insurer “will understand.” Mechanical advisor notes on an RO are a different voice. If notes and photos disagree, quote both. You do not put passwords or PAN in notes. This Expert surfaces notes on the pack. They write to the file. Line notes and header notes stay separate if they stored both. Do not move a prior-damage sentence into related-this-loss.
