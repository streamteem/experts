# Keys and fobs

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Keys are the count and tag on their key log for this VIN — door, fob, lockbox — not a code you store and not a promise a spare exists. Typical US collision check-in sheets count keys in and keys out. Copy the count. Do not invent a spare. Do not write key-fob programming hours unless their estimate already has that mechanical or sublet line. Gate or lockbox codes do not belong in docs. Mechanical key programming on a service RO is a different stall unless they filed it here. If the log says two in and one out, flag. Personal items are a sibling log. This Expert lists key counts. They control the board. A lockbox combination does not belong in docs. Missing fob batteries are a note they wrote, not a programming line you add. This Expert lists key counts. They control the board. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tags keys on DRP drop-offs versus tow-ins.
