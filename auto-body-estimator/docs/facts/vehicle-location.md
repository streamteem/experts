# Vehicle location

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Vehicle location is where their board or lot sheet says the car sits — stall, lot row, sublet, or customer still driving — not a GPS you invent. Typical US collision offices update location when the car moves from intake to teardown to paint to QC. Copy the last location they wrote. Do not invent a stall to make the board look tidy. If location says on-site and the tow ticket says still at scene, list both. Keys should match a car that is here; if keys are in and location is sublet, flag. You do not hide a car that is waiting on a total-loss decision. This Expert lists location versus stage.
