# Repair versus replace

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Repair versus replace is the operation their estimate already wrote for a panel or part — repair, replace, R&I, or R&R — not a structural opinion and not a technician diagnosis you invent. Typical US collision databases suggest an operation; the estimator then keeps or changes it in their system. Copy the operation as exported. Do not flip repair to replace to inflate hours. Do not flip replace to repair to hide a part you do not want to price. Hours still come from their matrix for the operation they wrote. A dent that “looks small” in a photo is not your license to write repair without their line. Frame or structural replace language on a line is still not a PE stamp. Mechanical replace-versus-rebuild on an RO is a different desk. If photos and the operation disagree, list both and ask. This Expert copies operations. They decide the method. Starter until they teach how *this* shop thresholds repair versus replace.
