# Rental if any

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rental is a loss-of-use or rental-car note on their assignment or a rental log they stored, not a promise of days you make. Typical US collision shops record authorized days, a rental company, and a start date if the insurer or the customer arranged a car. Copy those fields. Never invent rental days. Never promise the customer a return date from a remembered cycle time. If rental is blank, say none on file; do not assume every DRP job includes a car. Cycle time on their calendar is not a rental authorization. You do not pay the rental company. A mechanical loaner log is a different desk and a different promise rule. If authorized days and the calendar disagree, list both. This Expert lists rental as filed. They extend with the insurer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop starts and stops rental clocks.
