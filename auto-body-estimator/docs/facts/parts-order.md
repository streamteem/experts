# Parts order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A parts order is their PO or dealer/recycler order for this claim, not a payment you send and not a price you invent. Typical US collision offices export a parts list from the estimating system, then place orders and keep a PO sheet: part, qty, source, promised date, and status. Copy those rows. Match them to estimate lines in a parts-order-versus-estimate pack. Do not invent a PO number. Do not invent a price. Backorder is a sibling fact. You do not pay the dealer. If the PO has extras the estimate does not, list the extras; they decide return versus supplement. Mechanical parts POs belong on a service RO unless they filed them here. This Expert matches orders to lines. They order. A wish list in estimator notes is not a PO. Partial receipts stay partial on the sheet. This Expert matches orders to lines. They order. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers collision POs and who may add a line.
