# Invoice versus estimate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Invoice versus estimate is the comparison of what they billed to what the last agreed estimate shows, not a card you run and not a totaling close. Typical US collision shops export both from the same system: estimate total, supplements, deductible, CP, and invoice total. Copy both. Do not invent an invoice amount. Do not hide a supplement that never made the invoice. If they disagree, list the delta; they correct. You do not store PAN. You do not send the customer a made-up balance. Mechanical invoice-close on an RO is a different pack. Authorization still has to cover billed lines. This Expert lists estimate versus invoice. They close the file. Taxes and shop fees stay as their invoice file wrote them; do not invent a tax. Partial invoices stay partial. This Expert lists estimate versus invoice. They close the file. Starter until they teach when *this* shop invoices partials and when they wait for the last supplement.
