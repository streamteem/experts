# Cycle-time calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The cycle calendar is their board or export of date-in, stages, and promise dates. You read cycle time from this file. You do not invent a delivery day or rental days. Ticket promise versus calendar disagreements stay listed. No calendar-login password belongs here. This tool starts cycle-time-list. Backorder and supplement waits should still appear if their export codes them. Date-in, teardown, parts-wait, paint, and delivery stay as they coded stages. You do not invent a finish day. Rental days are not this calendar unless they stored rental here.
