# Procedure PDF folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The procedure folder holds OEM, I-CAR, or information-system PDFs they saved for this VIN and these operations. Index present versus missing. You do not invent a page. You do not stamp the work done. No information-system password belongs here. This tool starts oem-procedure-present. Weld, ADAS, and scan lines without a matching PDF become asks, not guessed procedures. Wrong-year or wrong-option PDFs stay flagged as mismatch, not present. You do not invent a page from memory. This folder is evidence of a file, not a stamp the work was done.
