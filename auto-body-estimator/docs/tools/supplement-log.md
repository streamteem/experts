# Supplement log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The supplement log is their dated list of estimate versions, submit dates, responses, and dollars as exported. Copy rows. Do not invent a supplement. Do not hide a reduction. No portal password belongs here. This tool starts the supplement-log workflow. Aging stays as they dated it. Checks look for claim and date when asked. Invoice close still needs the last agreed version. Sent and responded dates stay separate. Denied rows stay on the log. You do not invent a version to tidy dollars. Join to the estimate export only on keys they already use.
