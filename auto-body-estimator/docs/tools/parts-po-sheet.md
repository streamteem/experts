# Parts PO sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The parts PO sheet lists dealer, LKQ, and aftermarket orders: part, qty, promised date, status, and returns. Copy status. You do not invent an ETA or a price. You do not pay the vendor. This tool starts parts-order-versus-estimate. Backorders stay visible. Checks look for part and qty when asked. Extras on the PO that are not on the estimate stay listed. Backorder and return rows stay on this sheet. You do not invent an ETA. Match to estimate lines on part keys they use. You do not pay from this sheet.
