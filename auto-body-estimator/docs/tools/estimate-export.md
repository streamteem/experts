# Estimate export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The estimate export is their CCC, Mitchell, Audatex, or workbook dump of lines, pay types, versions, and totals. Copy lines as exported. Do not invent hours or prices. First-write versus supplement stays labeled. No estimating login belongs here. This tool feeds estimate packs, supplement logs, pay-type splits, cycle joins, and auth gaps. Checks often look for claim and pay or line columns. You may run Python in this workspace to read their CSV and write a summary they asked for; you do not add lines. Version and pay-type columns stay as exported. Distinct from a mechanical RO PDF.
