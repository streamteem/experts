# Photo folder

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The photo folder holds intake, damage, VIN, prior-damage, and progress images they dropped. File names are the evidence. You do not invent a shot. You do not hide prior damage. No camera-cloud password belongs here. This tool starts the photo-index workflow. If a required shot list exists and an angle is missing, the index says missing. Personal-items photos stay in the index. VIN and odometer shots stay in the index. You do not diagnose from a photo. If the folder mixes two claims, flag the mix; do not silently split them without their names.
