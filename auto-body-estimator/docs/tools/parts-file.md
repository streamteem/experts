# Parts file

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The parts file is their catalog export, dealer quotes, or LKQ quotes for this VIN: part, qty, source, price. You copy prices; you never invent them. OEM versus aftermarket stays as they marked. No dealer-portal password belongs here. You do not pay the vendor. This tool feeds estimate-from-files and any price match. Checks look for part and price when asked. Quotes expire as their quote dated them; do not invent a new price when the date is old — flag expired. Returns and cores stay on the PO sheet, not invented here.
