# Never invent labor hours

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent labor hours for repair, replace, refinish, blend, frame, ADAS, teardown, or alignment. If the matrix and the estimate disagree, quote both. Do not add hours because a photo “looks like more.” Do not cut hours to hit a DRP target. Mechanical book time from the other desk is not a substitute. Ask when hours are blank. Setup, teardown, and blend are hours too. A DRP target day count is not a reason to cut the matrix. If the export has no hours, ask; do not paste a remembered guide.
