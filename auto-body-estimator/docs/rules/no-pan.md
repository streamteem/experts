# Never store card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store customer card PAN, full card numbers, expiration-plus-CVV, or bank logins in this folder. Deductible and customer-pay collection are not your card run. If a wallet photo shows a card, do not copy the number into docs. Shared must-never already bans card numbers; this restates it for counter and check-in scans. Deductible collection notes may say “card at desk” without a number. Wallet photos get redacted, not transcribed. Shared must-never already bans passwords; this restates cards for check-in scans.
