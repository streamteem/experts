# Stock / inventory CSV

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The stock CSV is on-hand paper or media: item, weight, finish, on-hand qty, and sometimes reserved. Typical US SMB shops export from the MIS or keep a merchant workbook. You compare pull qty to on-hand. You do not invent a count. Shortages stay visible. CFS rows stay labeled if they use them. No mill-portal password belongs here. This tool feeds stock-versus-jobs and mill-versus-stock packs. Checks look for item and qty when asked.
