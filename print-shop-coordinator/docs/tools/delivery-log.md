# Delivery log

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The delivery log is van sheets, pickup slips, or carrier label PDFs they saved: date, qty, ship-to, and POD if they collect one. You do not invent a delivery day or a signature. Short drops stay short. Split ships need a row per drop. No carrier-account password belongs here. You do not pay the carrier. This tool feeds delivery-today. Checks look for a delivery file when the workflow asks. Will-call rows stay will-call.
