# Bindery list

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The bindery list is finishing steps and queue order: cut, fold, stitch, laminate, drill, insert, kit. Typical US SMB shops keep a second calendar because press-complete is not ship-complete. You copy steps from the ticket onto this list when you build the queue pack. You do not invent a bindery hour. You do not skip a step to make due-today possible. Outside bindery stays a vendor note, not a payment. This tool feeds the bindery-queue workflow.
