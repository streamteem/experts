# Job ticket sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The job-ticket sheet is their MIS export or workbook: job number, customer, qty, stock, due, proof status, and often press and bindery. Typical US SMB shops export from PrintSmith, Pace, or a numbered workbook. You list and flag from that file. You do not invent a job number or a due. Draft versus live stays as they coded it. No MIS password belongs here. Checks look for job, qty, and due when the workflow asks. This tool starts the job board, due-today, reprint, and bindery packs.
