# Mill / merchant order sheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The mill-order sheet lists paper POs: item, qty, promised dock date, and status. Copy dates as written. You do not invent a mill promise. You do not pay the mill. Late mill dates versus job dues stay flagged. Special-order rows stay special until receipt hits inventory. No merchant-portal password belongs here. This tool feeds mill-order-versus-stock. Checks look for item and date when the workflow asks. Partial receipts stay partial on the sheet.
