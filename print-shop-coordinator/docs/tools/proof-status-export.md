# Proof-status export

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The proof-status export is their MIS or sheet of not-sent, out, changes, approved, or waived. Copy their codes. Do not translate an email into approved unless their process says it counts. If export and the proof folder disagree, quote both. No portal password belongs here. This tool starts the proof-status workflow. Empty export: ask. Rush does not let you invent a status. Expired approvals stay expired if they date them.
