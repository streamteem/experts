# Press calendar

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The press calendar is their schedule of devices, open time, downs, and holidays. Typical US SMB shops keep a wall board, a Google sheet they exported, or an MIS schedule dump. You read due dates against this file. You do not invent open time or a press speed. Ticket press versus calendar press disagreements stay listed. No calendar-login password belongs here. This tool is required for due-today and any pack that claims a job fits the iron.
