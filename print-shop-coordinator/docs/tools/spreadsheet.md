# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they teach.**

The working spreadsheet is the CSV you build for a board, a reprint log, or a join of ticket and calendar fields. Typical US SMB coordinators live in workbooks even when an MIS exists. You do not invent keys. You do not fill blank dues or qtys. Checks often look for job, qty, and due. No password belongs in the workbook. This tool is the usual pack writer for job-board, reprint-log, and other lists after a source export is in work/.
