# Image resolution (their check)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Image resolution is their preflight check on raster images, usually a minimum effective ppi at 100 percent size. Typical US SMB checklists fail low-res logos and phone photos. Use their number only. Do not invent a 300-dpi rule if their sheet says something else. Do not upsample to “fix” a fail unless their checklist allows it — and say you did if they allow it. Screen shots of a PDF proof are not press images. Wide-format shops may allow lower ppi; still copy their check. You do not hide a low-res fail on a rush. You do not give a copyright opinion about a snatched web image. They ask the customer for a better file or they waive. Effective ppi after scale is what matters if their checklist says so. Line-art dpi can differ if they wrote a second row. Starter until they teach *this* shop's resolution check.
