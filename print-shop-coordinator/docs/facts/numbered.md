# Numbering

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Numbering is consecutive or set numbers on tickets, invoices, or forms: start number, end number, prefix, color, and crash versus inkjet versus press number. Typical US SMB shops write the start on the ticket and keep a log so two jobs do not overlap. Copy the range. Do not invent a start number. Do not skip a range to tidy a reprint. Variable-data numbers come from their merge file, not from a sequence you type. If the customer PO range and the ticket range disagree, list both. MICR or check numbering is their special process if they do it; you do not invent a MICR line and you do not store bank logins. Missing numbers after bindery are a reprint-reason question, not a silent reprint. Duplicate ranges across two open tickets are an ask. They own the log. Starter until they teach how *this* shop reserves number ranges.
