# Die (if any)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A die is a custom knife or a digital cut path for pockets, hung tags, or odd shapes. Typical US SMB shops keep a die number, a physical die location, or a cut-file name on the ticket. Copy that pointer. Do not invent a die. Do not treat a guillotine trim as a die. If the die is missing or the cut file is missing, flag; do not send the job to finish. New dies need their vendor note and time — you do not invent a due that ignores die make-ready, and you do not pay the diemaker. Proofs on die work should show the shape they approved. CFS dies the customer owns still need a condition note. You do not hide a missing die inside “cut it close.” They hang the die. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers dies.
