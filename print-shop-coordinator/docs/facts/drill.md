# Drill / holes

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Drill is hole punching: three-hole, five-hole, calendar holes, or a custom pattern. Typical US SMB tickets give hole count, diameter, and distance from edges when they are not using a house 3-hole. Copy those numbers. Do not invent a hole pattern from a remembered binder. Do not assume three-hole on an 8.5 by 11. Collate-then-drill versus drill-then-collate is their bindery note. If the customer PO says drilled and the ticket does not, flag. A die can punch holes too; if they named a die, use the die file, not a drill guess. You do not hide a missing drill step to make due-today possible. Sample-keep should show the holes if they keep samples. Plastic-comb or coil punch is a different bind step unless they wrote drill. Metric hole spacing stays metric if that is how they bought the die. Starter until they teach *this* shop's house drill patterns.
