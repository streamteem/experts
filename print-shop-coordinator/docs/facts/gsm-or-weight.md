# Basis weight or GSM

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

US commercial tickets still say pounds of text or cover (80-lb text, 100-lb cover) more often than grams per square meter; packaging and import sheets may show GSM or caliper. Typical US SMB coordinators copy the weight exactly as the ticket and the stock CSV write it. Do not convert 80-lb text to a GSM you remember, and do not treat cover pounds as text pounds. Caliper in thousandths is a third measure; use it only if their stock file has it. A mismatch between ticket weight and on-hand weight is a shortage or a substitute question, not a silent relabel. Metric jobs stay metric if that is how they bought the mill sheet. You do not invent a weight so a fold or a postage band looks right. You do not promise a stiffness or a postal thickness from a remembered chart. If weight is blank, ask. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop lives in pounds, GSM, or both.
