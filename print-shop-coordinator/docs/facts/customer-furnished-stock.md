# Customer-furnished stock (CFS)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Customer-furnished stock is paper or substrate the customer sent. Typical US SMB shops receipt it on a CFS note: count, size, weight, damage, and who owns leftover. Label it CFS. Do not count it as mill on-hand unless they receipted it into inventory that way. Ask if count or condition is missing. Do not run CFS that failed their check without asking. You do not invent a waste percent because CFS curls. Leftover CFS after the job is their return-or-keep rule, not a house restock you invent. Proof on CFS may shift color; that is their proof type question. You do not give a copyright opinion about labels already printed on furnished sheets. You do not store a card PAN on a CFS receipt. They accept or reject the skid. Grain and moisture notes they wrote stay on the receipt. Starter until they teach *this* shop's CFS receipt.
