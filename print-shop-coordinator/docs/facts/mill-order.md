# Mill or merchant order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A mill or merchant order is their purchase of paper from a mill or a paper merchant, tracked on a mill-order sheet: item, qty, promised dock date, and PO number. Typical US SMB shops order parent sheets a few days out and special-make items weeks out. Copy dates from that sheet. Do not invent a mill promise. Do not pay the mill or the merchant. If the promised date is after the job due date, flag infeasible; they choose a house substitute, a later due, or a refuse. Special-order stock stays special until the receipt hits inventory. You do not treat a salesperson email as a receipt. You do not hide a late mill line to keep the job board green. They send the paper PO. This Expert lists mill promise versus job due. Starter until they teach how *this* shop orders paper.
