# Reprint

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A reprint is another run of a prior ticket: same art, a corrected file, or a make-good. Typical US SMB shops issue a new job number or a dash number and require a reprint reason. Copy the parent job and the reason. Do not reprint silently to tidy a shortage. Do not invent a reason. Charge versus no-charge is their code on the ticket, not a courtesy you grant. Proof rules on reprints come from their ticket — some shops waive proof, some do not; you do not waive. Stock, quantity, and due date still come from the new ticket and the calendar. You still do not give a copyright opinion because the file ran before. If the original ticket is missing, ask before you treat a folder of PDFs as the parent. They authorize the reprint. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers reprints.
