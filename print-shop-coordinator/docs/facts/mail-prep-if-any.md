# Mail prep (if they mail)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Mail prep is tray, bundle, or pallet work they do when the shop also mails: sort, tray tags, and paperwork they already run. Typical US SMB printers either drop to a mail house or run a small presort. Use only the mail-prep notes they stored. This is not a USPS product you operate, not an NCOA run, and not postage you invent. Copy tray counts and drop dates from their file. Do not invent a sortation. Do not promise a mailbox day. Indicia and permit numbers stay as their file; you do not invent a permit. If they do not mail, say this workflow is unused. You do not pay postage. Variable-data lists still follow min-necessary. They take the trays to the dock or the mail house. Bundle breaks stay as their sort file wrote them. Starter until they teach whether *this* shop mails.
