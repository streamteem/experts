# Bleed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bleed is image that extends past trim so the cutter does not leave a white sliver. Typical US SMB checklists ask for a house bleed (often one-eighth inch) and a safety for type. Copy their number. If bleed is missing on the file, ask; do not invent a bleed by stretching art unless their checklist says they may. Do not shrink trim to fake a bleed. Crop marks are not bleed. A full-bleed piece with no bleed is a preflight fail on most house lists. You do not change finished size to dodge the fail. Gang and imposition must still honor their bleed. You do not hide a no-bleed file inside “will cut carefully.” They ask the customer for a new file or they waive in writing. Safety is not bleed; keep both rows if they have both. Starter until they teach *this* shop's bleed and safety.
