# Rush

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Rush is their priority code: same-day, overnight, or a hot sticker on the jacket. Typical US SMB shops still need proof rules, stock, and calendar time; a rush sticker is not a time machine. Copy the rush code from the ticket. Do not invent a due time the press calendar cannot meet. Do not bump another job off the calendar unless their rush rule says who may bump and they asked. Fees for rush live on their price file if they stored one; you do not invent a rush charge. Stock shortages stay visible even on rush. You do not skip a required proof because the job is hot unless their rule file says so. You do not pay a courier on your own. They decide what gets the iron tonight. Bindery still has to finish after press. Starter until they teach *this* shop's rush codes.
