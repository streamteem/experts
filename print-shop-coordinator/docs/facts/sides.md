# Sides printed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sides is whether the piece prints one side or two, and which inks sit on each. Typical US SMB tickets use 4/4, 4/0, 1/1, or words like “backs up.” Work-and-turn, work-and-tumble, and sheetwise are imposition methods, not a substitute for the sides field — copy them only if their imposition file or ticket wrote them. Do not assume a letterhead is 4/0 or a brochure is 4/4. If the PDF shows a back and the ticket says one side, ask; do not quietly add a back. Perfecting versus a second pass is their press call from the calendar and the ticket, not a speed you invent. Self-cover booklets still have outer and inner sides; use their page-count and sides notes. You do not change sides to fit a gang. Blank backs stay blank unless they change the ticket. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes sides and backups.
