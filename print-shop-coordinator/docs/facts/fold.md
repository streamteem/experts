# Fold

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fold is the named fold and the panel sizes: letter, z, half, gate, accordion, or a custom map fold. Typical US SMB tickets also say score or perforate when cover stock needs it. Copy the fold name and any panel diagram they stored. Do not invent a fold from a remembered brochure. Do not assume letter fold on an 8.5 by 11. Grain direction and score are their ticket or bindery note; you do not guess grain to prevent cracking. If the PDF imposition and the fold name fight, ask. A die-cut fold piece still needs the die file. Bindery time for a fold is not a due date you invent against the press calendar. Sample-keep copies should show the fold they approved. You do not change fold to fit a leftover sheet. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes folds and scores.
