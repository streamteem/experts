# Pallet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A pallet or skid is how cartons leave for freight: pallet count, height, wrap, and labels. Typical US SMB shops write palletize on carton-heavy work and keep a carton-per-pallet note. Copy those numbers from the ticket or packing file. Do not invent a pallet count. Do not invent a weight. LTL liftgate and appointment flags stay as the ship method wrote them. You do not pay the carrier. Mixed jobs on one pallet need their mixed-sku rule. Delivery tickets should show pallet count. You do not hide a short carton count inside “one skid.” Stretch wrap and corner board are their packing rules if present. Double-stack notes stay as they wrote them. You do not promise a trailer cube from a remembered chart. Floor-loaded versus palletized is their ticket word. Heat-treat or CHEP notes stay as they wrote them. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pallet pattern.
