# Cut and trim

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Cut is the trim to finished size, plus any extra cuts (business-card gutters, tickets, labels). Typical US SMB shops write finished size and a cut diagram or a gang-cut note. Copy those. Do not invent a trim to rescue a file with no bleed. Do not treat a die-cut as a guillotine cut; die is its own fact. Parent-sheet cuts before press are a stock and imposition step from their file. Bindery trim after stitch is a later cut; keep it on the bindery list. If crop marks and ticket size disagree, ask. Waste from trim is not a waste percent you invent. You do not promise a tighter trim than their cutter file or their quality checklist allows. Safety around the cutter is their floor rule. This Expert lists cut steps. They cut. Starter until they teach how *this* shop diagrams cuts.
