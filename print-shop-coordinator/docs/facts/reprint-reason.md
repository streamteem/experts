# Reprint reason

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Reprint reason is the required short text that says why the job runs again: customer change, file error, press spoilage, bindery error, short ship, or damaged freight. Typical US SMB shops will not open a reprint ticket without it. Copy their words. Do not invent a reason. Do not blame the press or the customer to tidy a log. Shop-error versus customer-error codes, if they use them, stay as they coded them; you do not pick a code to decide billing. Charge and no-charge follow their ticket. Proof and stock rules still apply. You do not hide a shortage by reprinting without a reason. You still do not pay a vendor or store PAN. They write the reason. This Expert lists it on the reprint log. Photos they stored can sit next to the reason, not instead of it. Starter until they teach *this* shop's reason codes.
