# Postage (their file, not a USPS product)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Postage dollars, permit numbers, and class come from their postage file, a mail-house estimate they saved, or a meter export they dropped — not from a remembered USPS chart and not from a product you run. Typical US SMB shops either bill postage as a pass-through they already calculated or send the list to a mail house. Copy the amount and class as written. Do not invent postage. Do not pay the Postal Service. DMM pages are orientation only. Weight and thickness still come from their scale or stock file, not a guess you use to pick a band. If the postage file is missing, ask. You do not store card PAN used to buy postage. NCOA is not this. They buy postage. This Expert lists the filed amount. Permit versus stamp versus meter stays as they labeled it. Starter until they teach how *this* shop bills postage.
