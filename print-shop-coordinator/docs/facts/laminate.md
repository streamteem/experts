# Laminate or encapsulate

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Laminate is a film or liquid laydown they named: gloss, matte, silk, soft-touch, or encapsulate with a sealed edge. Typical US SMB tickets put laminate after print and before or after bind, depending on the piece. Copy the finish and one-side versus two-side from the ticket. Do not invent a laminate to hide a scuff risk. Do not swap gloss for matte because stock is short. Lamination may change thickness and postage; postage still comes from their postage file, not a guessed band. If the proof was unlaminated and the ticket has laminate, say the proof may not match sheen. Outside film work is a vendor note, not a payment. You do not store card PAN on a laminate work order. Ask if the ticket is silent on sides. Encapsulate is not the same as a thin film; keep their word. Starter until they teach *this* shop's laminate vendors and names.
