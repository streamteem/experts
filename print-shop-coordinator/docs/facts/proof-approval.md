# Proof approval

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Proof approval is their written OK to print: a signed slip, a dated email they filed, a portal checkbox export, or a written waive. Typical US SMB shops will not hang plates or release a digital queue without it, except a reprint rule they wrote. Copy the date, the name, and the proof file name. Do not invent an approval. Do not treat “looks good” in a hallway note as approved unless they said that counts. Changes requested are not approval. An expired approval, if they date them, is an ask. Approval of a PDF is not approval of a later file; match names. You still do not opine that approved art is legal to print. You do not move the due date because approval sat. They chase sign-off. This Expert lists who and when. Starter until they teach *this* shop's approval evidence.
