# File preflight (their checklist)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Preflight is their checklist run against the customer file: bleed, fonts, images, color space, page size, and any house items they wrote. Typical US SMB prepress uses a PitStop profile, a written sheet, or both. Use only their checklist. Do not invent a dpi minimum, a bleed, or a color-space rule from a remembered textbook. Failures become a missing-items list, not a quiet fix you invent unless they taught a safe outline-fonts step and the checklist allows it. You do not give a copyright opinion because a logo failed. You do not mark preflight complete if the checklist file is missing — ask. Proofs should be of the file that passed, or say they did not. This Expert lists pass/fail as their rows. They fix or they send the customer a report. Starter until they teach *this* shop's preflight profile.
