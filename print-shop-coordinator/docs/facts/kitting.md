# Kitting

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Kitting is assembly of several printed or furnished pieces into a set, polybag, or box. Typical US SMB shops use a kit list: component, qty per kit, and how many kits. Copy that list. Do not invent a bill of materials. Short components stay visible; do not kit “almost complete” as complete. Customer-furnished pieces need a CFS count. Versioned kits follow the variable-data or version file they stored. You do not invent a pack time. You do not store PAN on a kit label. Delivery may be by kit count, not by sheet count — say which. If the kit list and the job tickets disagree, ask. They assemble. This Expert lists components versus on-hand. Polybag size and seal notes stay as their packing file. Missing one component still stops the kit count. Starter until they teach *this* shop's kit sheets.
