# Delivery ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A delivery ticket is the shop's evidence the job left: driver name, date, qty, ship-to, pallet or carton count, and a signature or pickup name if they collect one. Typical US SMB shops use a carbon book, a van sheet, or a carrier label PDF. Copy those fields from the delivery log. Do not invent a POD. Do not invent a delivery day to close a due. Short drops stay short. Split ships need a ticket per drop. You do not pay the carrier. You do not store card PAN on the ticket. If the delivery log and the job ticket qty disagree, list both. Will-call pickups still need their pickup slip. Photos of the skid belong if they stored them. They drive or they call the courier. Refused deliveries stay refused on the log. Starter until they teach how *this* shop closes delivery.
