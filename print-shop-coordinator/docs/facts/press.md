# Press assignment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Press is which device they assigned: a named offset unit, a digital press, a wide-format printer, or an outside press if they noted a trade shop. Typical US SMB shops put the press on the ticket and on the press calendar. Copy the name. Do not move a job to a faster device to invent a due date. Do not invent a press speed, impressions per hour, or a make-ready time. If the calendar shows the press down, flag the conflict; they reschedule. Digital versus offset is their ticket choice, not your upgrade. A trade-press note is still not a mill payment you send. If ticket press and calendar press disagree, quote both. You do not hide a backlog by dropping the job off the board. Safety and lockout are their floor rules; OSHA pages are orientation only. This Expert lists assignment versus calendar. They run the iron. Starter until they teach *this* shop's press names.
