# Ship method

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ship method is how the finished work leaves: customer pickup, shop van, courier, UPS or FedEx as their account, or an LTL pallet. Typical US SMB tickets name the method and the ship-to if it is not the billing address. Copy the method from the ticket. Do not invent a service level to hit a due date. Do not pay a carrier. Third-party billing and collect versus prepaid come from the ticket or customer PO, not from a remembered account. Address files they stored are the ship-to; do not “fix” a street. Liftgate or inside delivery stays only if they wrote it. Split-ship methods can differ by drop; keep each line. You do not store card PAN on a ship ticket. You do not promise a delivery day the carrier file does not show. Starter until they teach *this* shop's house ship methods.
