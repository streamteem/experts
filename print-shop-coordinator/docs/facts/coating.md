# Coating and varnish

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Coating is aqueous, UV, varnish, or a spot varnish they named, often written next to color. Typical US SMB tickets say flood AQ gloss or matte, or UV on the face only. Copy the coating and the sides. Do not add a coat to protect a job you worry about. Do not swap UV for AQ because the coater is free. Coating can change dry time and bindery start; still do not invent a due date. If the proof is uncoated and the ticket is coated, flag the sheen difference. Spot varnish needs their plate or digital mask file. You do not invent a coat weight. Outside UV is a vendor note, not a payment. Ask if coating is blank on a coated-stock job that their house rule usually coats. Soft-touch film is laminate, not AQ, unless they wrote otherwise. Starter until they teach *this* shop's house coatings.
