# Proof status

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Proof status is where that ticket sits in the proof cycle: not sent, out with customer, changes requested, approved, waived, or expired if they date approvals. Typical US SMB shops export status from the MIS or keep a proof-status sheet next to the PDF folder. Copy their code; do not translate “looks fine” in an email into approved unless their process says that email counts. Do not mark approved to free the press. A rejected proof returns to prepress; it is not a press-ready file. If status and the folder disagree — folder has a signed slip, export says pending — quote both and ask. Hard-proof and PDF-proof statuses can both exist on one job; keep them separate if they do. You do not invent a due time because a proof sat too long. They chase the customer. This Expert lists status. Starter until they teach *this* shop's status words.
