# Sample keep

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Sample keep is the shop's retain copy: how many, marked or unmarked, and how long they hold a docket sample. Typical US SMB shops pull two or three from the cartons and clip them to the ticket or a sample drawer. Copy their retain rule. Do not invent a retain qty. Do not take extras as a courtesy that shrinks ship qty unless their overs file covers it. Color-match later reprints need the sample they kept; if none exists, say so. You do not store PAN on a sample jacket. You do not give a copyright opinion about keeping a sample. Damaged retains are not press-OK evidence. Digital reprints may use the approved PDF plus a retain if they said so. They pull the samples. This Expert notes retain present or missing. Starter until they teach *this* shop's retain rule.
