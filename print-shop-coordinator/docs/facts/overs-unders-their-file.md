# Overs and unders

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Overs and unders are the shop's written allowance to ship a little more or less than ordered. Typical US SMB commercial tickets point at a house percent, a trade-custom note, or a hard “no unders” on the customer PO. Copy the rule from their file. Do not invent an industry percent. Do not apply a remembered “ten percent overs” because the press ran long. If the customer PO forbids overs, that file wins over a house custom. Ship qty versus ordered qty belongs on the delivery ticket and the job board. Waste is not the same as overs; waste is what never shipped. You do not hide a short ship inside overs unless their file says that short is an allowed under. They explain overs to the customer. This Expert lists the written allowance and the actual ship qty. Starter until they teach *this* shop's overs file.
