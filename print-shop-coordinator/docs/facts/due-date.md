# Due date

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Due date is when the ticket says the job ships or the customer picks up, read against their press and bindery calendar. Typical US SMB shops write a ship date, a customer date, and sometimes an in-plant date. Copy those dates from the ticket. Never invent a due time the press cannot meet without their calendar. If the customer PO date is sooner than open time on the calendar, flag infeasible; they choose overtime, a split, a later date, or a refuse-the-rush. Do not hide the conflict by moving the due date. Do not use a remembered “two-day digital” rule. Proof wait time is not a date you guess; if proof is still out, say so. Holidays follow their calendar file. You do not promise a carrier delivery day. This Expert lists ticket date versus calendar. They promise the customer. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes due versus ship.
