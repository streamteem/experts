# Ink

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ink is process CMYK, named Pantone or house mixes, and any special (metallic, fluorescent, magnetic) the ticket lists. Typical US SMB shops pull ink from a mixing book or a digital ink catalog. Copy names from the ticket and the Pantone file they stored. Do not invent a PMS to match a logo from memory. Do not mix a formula. Density and LAB targets come from their color-standard file if they have one. Ink on CFS stock can shift; that is a proof question, not a formula you invent. You do not pay the ink vendor. Waste ink is not a waste percent you guess. If the ticket says process and the proof shows a spot, ask. Copyright still is not yours to opine because a brand ink is named. Dry-back notes stay as their file if they keep them. Starter until they teach *this* shop's ink kitchen notes.
