# Stitch or bind

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stitch or bind is how pages become a book: saddle stitch, side stitch, perfect bind, coil, wire-o, or paste. Typical US SMB shops write stitch count, spine width, or coil color when those fields exist. Copy the bind type from the ticket. Do not invent a page count to make a saddle-stitch possible. Do not swap perfect bind for stitch to hit a due date. Cover and text stocks must match their ticket; a mismatch is a stock question. Creep, grind-off, and hinge score are their bindery notes if present — do not invent them. If the estimate says saddle and the ticket says perfect, list both. Outside bindery still needs their PO to that vendor; you do not pay it. You do not hide a missing cover file inside “ready to stitch.” Starter until they teach *this* shop's bind vocabulary.
