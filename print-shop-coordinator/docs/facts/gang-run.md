# Gang run

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A gang run puts more than one ticket or more than one customer on one sheet or one digital nest. Typical US SMB shops gang business cards, postcards, and small posters when their gang file or MIS says so. Gang only if their file says this ticket may gang. Do not invent a gang to use leftover paper. Do not mix stocks, due dates, or color standards that their gang rule forbids. Each job number still needs its own qty, proof, and delivery. If one customer on a gang changes after proof, that is a change-after-proof question for that ticket, not a silent re-gang. Overs and waste on a gang follow their gang file. You do not hide a shortage of one SKU inside a gang sheet. They approve the gang. Shared due dates still must fit the press calendar. Starter until they teach *this* shop's gang rules.
