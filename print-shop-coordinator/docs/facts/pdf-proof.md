# PDF / soft proof

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A PDF proof is a soft proof they emailed or dropped in the proof folder, often with a request-for-approval note. Typical US SMB digital shops live on PDF proofs. The file name and date in the folder are the evidence, not a memory of what was sent. Do not call a working prepress PDF a customer proof unless they labeled it sent. Do not invent an approval because the customer opened the email. Low-resolution screen color is not their press standard; if they require a hard proof for match, the PDF is not enough. Fonts, bleed, and crop should still pass their preflight checklist on the proof file. If two PDFs exist, use the one their status export points at, or list both. You do not opine that the PDF is legal to reproduce. You do not promise the press will match the monitor. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names and files soft proofs.
