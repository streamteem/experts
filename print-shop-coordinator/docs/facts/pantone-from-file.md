# Pantone from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Pantone or other spot names come from the ticket and from the swatch file or book they pointed at (coated, uncoated, or a house book). Typical US SMB tickets write PMS 185 C or a house mix number. Copy the name and the book. Do not invent a PMS from a remembered logo. Do not convert a spot to process unless their file says convert and they approved the shift. Digital shops may simulate a spot; still label simulation if they do. If the proof used a different book than the ticket, ask. You do not pay for a new guide. You do not give a trademark or copyright opinion because a PMS is a brand color. Missing swatch: ask, do not guess from a monitor. Old versus new book years stay as they labeled the file. Starter until they teach *this* shop's spot-color file.
