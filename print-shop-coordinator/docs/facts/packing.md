# Packing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Packing is the slip, labels, dunnage, and carton marks that travel with the job. Typical US SMB shops tuck a packing slip and sometimes an invoice copy if their rule says so. Copy those rules from their packing file. Do not invent a slip line. Do not put card PAN on a slip. Job number, qty, and ship-to come from the ticket. Inserts and kits have their own pack notes. You do not promise the carton will survive a carrier you did not file. Photos of packed skids belong in their folder if they take them. A missing packing rule is an ask, not a guessed box. Delivery tickets close the loop. They pack. This Expert lists required marks. Fragile or keep-dry marks stay only if their file has them. Invoice-in-box follows their rule file. Starter until they teach *this* shop's packing marks.
