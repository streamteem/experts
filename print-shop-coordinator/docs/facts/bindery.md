# Bindery / finishing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Bindery is the finishing work after print: cut, fold, stitch, bind, laminate, drill, number, insert, or kit. Typical US SMB shops keep a bindery list or a second calendar because the folder can sit while the press is free. Copy bindery steps from the ticket in their order. Do not skip a step to make due-today look possible. Do not invent a bindery hour or a hand-work rate. If the bindery list and the ticket disagree, quote both. Outside bindery is a vendor note on their file, not a payment you send. Quality checks (page order, trim, stitch placement) follow their checklist if they stored one. A job can be press-complete and bindery-not-started; say that on the board. You do not hide a bindery backlog inside “on press.” They sequence the floor. Starter until they teach *this* shop's bindery queue words.
