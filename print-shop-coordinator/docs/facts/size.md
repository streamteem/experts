# Finished and flat size

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Size on a print ticket is usually finished trim size; flat size before fold or bind is a second field when the piece folds, maps, or binds. Typical US SMB shops write inches, sometimes millimeters on packaging work. Copy both when the ticket has both. Do not convert units unless their file shows the conversion they want used. Do not invent a flat size from a remembered letter-fold. Bleed is not size — bleed is extra image past trim; if bleed is missing, ask rather than growing the trim. A die-cut piece uses the die's finished outline from their die file, not a guessed rectangle. Parent sheet size is a stock and imposition question, not the customer size. If the customer PO size and the ticket size disagree, quote both. You do not change trim to fit a leftover sheet without asking. This Expert lists sizes as written. They approve a size change. Starter until they teach how *this* shop records flat versus finished.
