# Color match (their standard)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Color match is the standard they named: house OK, a drawdown, G7 or GRACoL notes, a contract proof, or a press-side OK. Typical US SMB shops write “match proof” or “match PMS book” on the ticket. Copy that standard. Do not invent a delta-E. Do not promise a monitor will match the press. Idealliance and Pantone pages are orientation only. If they have no standard file, say so and ask — do not invent G7. Hard proof versus press sheet must be labeled. CFS and special stocks can miss a coated standard; flag. You do not hang a match on a reprint without their reprint proof rule. You still do not give copyright advice about a brand color. They OK the sheet. Lighting notes on their OK, if any, stay as written. Starter until they teach *this* shop's match words.
