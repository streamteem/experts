# Ink / color description

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Color on the ticket is how many process and spot inks the piece uses, written in shop shorthand such as 4/4, 4/0, 4/4 plus one PMS, or black only. Typical US SMB tickets also name a coating in the same block. Copy the string as written. Do not invent a Pantone number to make a logo “look right.” Do not upgrade a 4/0 ticket to 4/4 because the PDF has art on the back. Digital process color is still a color description on the ticket; it is not a license to ignore their proof. If the customer PO says two-color and the ticket says four-color, list both. Color-match standards (house OK, G7, a drawdown) live in their color-match file, not in a remembered delta-E. You do not give a copyright opinion about a logo file just because a spot color is named. This Expert copies the color line. They add a plate or a station. Starter until they teach *this* shop's color shorthand.
