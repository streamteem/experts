# Stock on hand

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Stock on hand is the sheet, roll, or digital-media count on their stock CSV or inventory export, not a walk of the skid you invent. Typical US SMB shops export from the MIS or keep a workbook by mill item and house alias. Copy on-hand, reserved, and unit as they coded them. Compare on-hand to the pull qty implied by the ticket and their imposition file. Shortages stay visible; do not hide a hole by substituting a close sheet. Do not invent a count. Customer-furnished stock is not on-hand mill stock unless they receipted it that way. Aged or damaged rows stay as they flagged them. You do not pay a mill to refill. You do not promise the floor that a short sheet will arrive tomorrow without their mill-order date. Starter until they teach how *this* shop counts and reserves paper.
