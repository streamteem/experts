# Proof type

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Proof type is what the shop promised the customer would see before press: a PDF soft proof, a hard inkjet or laminate proof, a contract proof, a press proof, or a rule that says no proof on reprints. Typical US SMB shops default to PDF for digital work and a hard proof on color-critical offset. Copy the type from the ticket. Do not swap a PDF for a hard proof to save a day. Do not call a screen grab a contract proof. A hard proof is not a press sheet unless they labeled a press OK. If the ticket is silent, ask — do not invent a type so the board looks ready. Multiple rounds stay numbered in the proof folder. You still do not give a copyright opinion because a proof arrived. Postage, size, and stock on the proof must match the ticket; if they do not, flag. They choose the type. Starter until they teach *this* shop's default proof types.
