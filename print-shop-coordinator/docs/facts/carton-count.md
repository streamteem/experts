# Carton count

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Carton count is how many boxes ship and how many pieces sit in each box. Typical US SMB shops use a packing rule or a carton-size sheet: twenty-five per carton, a max weight, or a house box. Copy pack qty from that file. Do not invent a pack so the math looks neat. Do not change ordered qty to fill a carton. If the rule is missing, ask. Split ships need carton counts per drop. Labels should show job number and qty if their packing file says so. You do not invent a DIM weight. Overs that fill an extra carton still need their overs file. You do not hide a short last carton. They pick the box. Partial last cartons stay labeled partial. Kit jobs count kits, not loose sheets, if that is how they pack. Starter until they teach *this* shop's pack quantities.
