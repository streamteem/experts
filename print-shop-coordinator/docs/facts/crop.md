# Crop and trim marks

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Crop or trim marks show where the finished edge sits; registration and color bars may sit outside. Typical US SMB preflight wants marks on imposed forms and often on customer PDFs. Copy their mark rule. Do not invent marks that move trim. If crop marks and ticket size disagree, ask. Bleed lives outside crop; safety lives inside. A file cropped to trim with no marks can still be usable if their checklist says so — do not guess. You do not hide a size fight inside “close enough.” Die-cut pieces use die marks from the die file, not a rectangle you add. Imposition templates they stored already have marks; use those. Printer marks on a digital sheet still must match their finished size. Folding marks are not crop unless they said so. Starter until they teach *this* shop's mark set.
