# Split shipment

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A split ship is more than one delivery or pickup against one ordered quantity: a partial now and a balance later, or two ship-to addresses. Typical US SMB tickets write split qty and each method. Copy those lines. Do not invent a split to make due-today look complete. Do not hide the balance. Each drop needs its own delivery ticket. Overs, if any, follow their file on the last drop or as they wrote. You do not promise the balance date without the calendar. Customer PO lines that already split should match the ticket; if not, ask. You do not store PAN on either drop. Bindery may finish in waves; say which qty is ready. They tell the customer. Carton counts belong on each drop, not only the first. Pickup versus freight can differ by drop. Starter until they teach how *this* shop writes splits.
