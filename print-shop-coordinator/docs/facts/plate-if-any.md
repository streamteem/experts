# Plates (if offset)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Plates exist on offset and some flexo work: a CTP set, a plate count, and sometimes a hang or remake note. Typical US SMB offset shops track plates on the ticket after proof approval. Copy plate count and color names from their file. Digital toner jobs have no plates — do not invent a plate line. Do not hang plates before proof approval unless their rule file allows it. A remake plate needs a reprint or remake reason. You do not invent a plate cost or a burn time. You do not pay the plate vendor. If the imposition file and the plate count disagree, ask. Stored plates and destruction dates follow their file if they keep plates. You still do not give a copyright opinion about what is on the plate. Starter until they teach how *this* shop tracks plates.
