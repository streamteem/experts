# Imposition (their file)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Imposition is how pages or pieces sit on the sheet or roll: signatures, gutters, marks, and work-and-turn. Typical US SMB prepress drops an imposition PDF or a template name on the ticket. Use that file only. Do not invent an imposition to gang leftover space. Do not rotate a piece to save stock without their file. Marks, color bars, and collating marks stay as they placed them. If the imposition size and the stock parent size disagree, flag. Gang-run impositions need their gang file. You do not invent a press speed from the form. Bindery must follow the same imposition; if bindery notes fight the form, ask. You do not hide a bad form inside “will cut it out.” They own prepress. Reader spreads are not printer spreads unless they labeled them that way. Starter until they teach how *this* shop names imposition templates.
