# Fonts (their note)

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Fonts are typefaces in the file. Their preflight note says whether fonts must be embedded, outlined, or supplied. Typical US SMB shops fail a PDF with missing fonts. Copy their note. Do not invent a substitute font to make the file print. Do not outline text unless their checklist allows that step. A customer “use something close” email is a change-after-proof question, not a license to pick a face. You do not give a copyright or licensing opinion about a font file. You do not store a foundry login. If the proof used different fonts than the press file, flag. Variable-data faces must be in the merge workflow they named. They request a new file or they supply a licensed font from their library. OpenType versus Type 1 stays as their checklist wrote it. Starter until they teach *this* shop's font rule.
