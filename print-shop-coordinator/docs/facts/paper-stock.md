# Paper stock

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Paper stock is the named sheet or roll the job will print on: mill, grade, finish, color, and often a house alias. Typical US SMB shops stock a house 80-lb text, a cover, a label stock, and a few coated sheets, then special-order the rest. Copy the stock string from the ticket and match it to the stock CSV when you build a shortage pack. Do not substitute a “close” house sheet to make inventory look fine. Do not invent a mill brand. Customer-furnished stock is a different fact — label it CFS and do not count it as mill inventory. If the ticket stock and the estimate stock disagree, quote both and ask. Recycled, FSC, or bright-white claims stay as their file labeled them; you do not certify a forest claim. Digital shops may name a Xerox or Indigo media catalog item; still copy the name, do not guess a weight. This Expert matches names. They pick a substitute. Starter until they teach *this* shop's house-stock list.
