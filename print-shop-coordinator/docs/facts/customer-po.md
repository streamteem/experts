# Customer purchase order

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The customer's purchase order is their authorization to print, not a payment you take and not a card authorization. Typical US SMB commercial shops require a PO on agency, school, and plant work; walk-in retail tickets may have none. Copy the PO number onto the job ticket when their process does that, and keep the PDF in the customer-PO folder. Match quantity, size, stock, and due date on the PO to the ticket; if they disagree, list both and ask. Do not invent a PO number. Do not treat an email that says “go ahead” as a PO unless they filed that email as their authorization rule. A PO is still not a copyright clearance. Billing notes on a PO may name net terms; you still do not store card PAN, and you do not send money. If the folder has no PO and their ticket says PO required, flag missing. This Expert files and compares. They collect the missing PO. Starter until they teach when *this* shop will start without a PO.
