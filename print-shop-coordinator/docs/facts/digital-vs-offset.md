# Digital versus offset

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Digital versus offset is the process the ticket already chose, usually from quantity, versioning, or color needs they priced. Typical US SMB shops break around a few hundred to a few thousand, but that break is theirs, not a rule you apply. Copy the process from the ticket. Do not flip a job to digital to hit a due date their offset calendar missed. Do not flip to offset to “look better” without their change. Plates, ink keys, and make-ready exist on offset; they do not appear on a toner ticket. Variable data almost always stays digital unless they wrote an inkjet-offset hybrid. Proof type often follows the process — still copy their type. Pricing differences are on their estimate file if they stored one; you do not invent a click charge or a plate charge. You do not give a copyright opinion because one process makes more waste. Starter until they teach how *this* shop chooses digital versus offset.
