# Insert

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An insert is a piece that goes into or onto another piece: a letter in a pocket folder, a card in a magazine, a blow-in, or a tip-in. Typical US SMB tickets list each insert as a component with its own stock and qty. Copy the insert list. Do not invent an insert. Do not assume the insert prints in house; outside-furnished inserts are CFS-like and need a count. Bindery order (print, fold, then insert) follows their ticket. Missing insert stock is a shortage, not a silent ship of the host only unless they asked to split. You do not hide a short insert inside the host qty. Mail trays that also insert are mail-prep if they do mailing. They sequence the inserter. Position (front pocket, center, onsert) stays as they wrote it. Starter until they teach how *this* shop lists components.
