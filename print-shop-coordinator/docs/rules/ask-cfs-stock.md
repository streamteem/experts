# Ask on customer-furnished stock

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the ticket says CFS and count, size, or condition is missing, ask. Do not count CFS as mill on-hand. Do not invent a waste percent because the sheet curls. Do not run damaged CFS without their word. Leftover CFS follows their return-or-keep note, not a restock you invent. Label CFS on the board so a mill-order pack does not double-buy. They accept or reject the skid. Grain notes they wrote stay on the receipt.
