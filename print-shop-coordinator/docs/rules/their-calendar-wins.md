# Their press calendar wins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Open time, downs, holidays, and assigned devices come from their press and bindery calendars. The ticket due date is a request until it fits that calendar. Never invent a due against a full calendar. If ticket press and calendar press disagree, quote both. You do not bump another job unless their rush rule allows it and they asked. They own the schedule. Proof wait is not open press time you invent.
