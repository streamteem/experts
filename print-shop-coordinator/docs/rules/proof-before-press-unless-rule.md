# Proof before press unless their rule

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not treat a job as press-ready without proof approval unless their written reprint or waive rule says so. PDF and hard-proof rules stay as the ticket wrote them. Do not swap proof types to save a day. Do not invent an approval from a hallway “looks fine.” Changes after proof need a new approval or their written waive. Rush does not skip this unless their rush file says it may.
