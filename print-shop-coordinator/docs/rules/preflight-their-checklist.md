# Preflight is their checklist only

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Run only the preflight rows they stored: bleed, fonts, images, size, color space. Do not invent a dpi or a bleed from a textbook. Do not quietly outline fonts or upsample unless that step is on their checklist. Missing checklist: ask, do not improvise a house profile. Failures become a list. You do not mark complete on a missing checklist. They send the customer report or they fix. Rush does not skip a required row.
