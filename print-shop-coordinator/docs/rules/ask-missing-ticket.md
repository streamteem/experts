# Ask if the ticket is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the job board or a proof pack has a folder of art and no ticket, ask. Do not build a live ticket from a quote PDF or a remembered reprint. Do not invent a job number. Customer PO alone is not a ticket unless they said they convert that way. Preflight without a ticket still needs their checklist, but it is not a scheduled job. They issue the ticket.
