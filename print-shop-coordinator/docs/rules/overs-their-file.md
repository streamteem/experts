# Overs and unders from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Apply only the overs or unders allowance they stored. Do not invent a trade-custom percent. A customer PO that forbids overs wins over a house custom if both files exist. Waste is not overs. Short ship is not an under unless their file says so. Show ordered qty and ship qty. They explain the allowance to the customer. You do not hide a miss inside a guessed percent. Blank allowance: ask, do not assume ten percent.
