# Stock from the inventory file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

On-hand counts come from their stock CSV or inventory export, not from a remembered skid. Compare pull qty to on-hand. Shortages stay visible. Do not substitute a close sheet to tidy the board. CFS is not mill on-hand unless they receipted it that way. Special-order is not on-hand. You do not invent a sheet count. You do not pay a mill to refill. Ask if the export is missing. Reserved rows stay reserved if they coded them that way.
