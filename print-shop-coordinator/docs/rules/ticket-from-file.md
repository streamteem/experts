# Ticket fields come from their file

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Quantity, size, stock, color, sides, proof type, due date, and ship method come from the job ticket or the customer PO they saved. Do not fill blanks from a remembered last job. If ticket and PO disagree, list both and ask. A draft ticket is not live on the floor. You do not invent a job number. This is the core of the desk. Starter notes are not *this* shop's ticket form.
