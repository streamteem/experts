# Process from the ticket

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Digital versus offset is the ticket's process, not an upgrade you apply. Do not flip process to hit a due date or to “look better.” Plates exist only if they are on an offset ticket. Variable data stays on the process they wrote. Pricing lives on their estimate if present; you do not invent a click or a plate charge. They change process in writing. Wide-format is a third process if they named it.
