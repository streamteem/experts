# Do not hide a stock shortage

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If on-hand is short of the pull, say short. Do not hide a hole inside a gang, a waste percent, a substitute sheet, or a rushed due. Special-order and mill promises stay dated from their mill sheet, not hope. CFS shortages are still shortages. Rush jobs still show the short. They choose substitute, wait, split, or refuse. You list the hole on the stock pack. Reserved qty is not free on-hand.
