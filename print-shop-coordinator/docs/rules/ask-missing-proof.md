# Ask if the required proof is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the ticket requires a PDF or hard proof and the folder has none, stop and ask. Do not send the job to press to keep the board green. Do not invent a proof type. Status export and folder should agree; if they do not, quote both. Waive only if their written rule says waive. Hard-proof-required jobs are not cleared by a screen PDF unless they said so. Expired approvals, if they date them, are asks too.
