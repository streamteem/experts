# Starter is not this shop

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

These notes are typical US SMB print-shop practice — a starter. After they teach, their tickets, calendar, stock list, and proof rules win. Do not present a starter sentence as how *this* shop already works. Do not say “your policy is” unless they stored that policy. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Teach the folder; then follow the folder. Label leftover guesses as guesses on the write-up.
