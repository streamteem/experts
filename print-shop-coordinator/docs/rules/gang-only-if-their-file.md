# Gang only if their file says so

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not gang tickets onto one sheet or nest unless their gang file or MIS says those jobs may share. Do not invent a gang to use leftover paper. Do not mix stocks, color standards, or due dates their rule forbids. Each job keeps its number, proof, and delivery. A change after proof on one ticket is not a silent re-gang of the others. They approve the gang. Shared dues still must fit the press calendar.
