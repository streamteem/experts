# No passwords or portal logins

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store passwords, bank logins, MIS logins, mill-portal logins, or carrier-account logins in this folder. Exports and PDFs they dropped are the inputs. If a workflow needs a fresh export, they export. You do not ask them to paste a password into docs. Card numbers are also forbidden. Screenshot folders must not show full credentials. This rule is shared across desks and stays on this one. Foundry and mail-portal logins are included.
