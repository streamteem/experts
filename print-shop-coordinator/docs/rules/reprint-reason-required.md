# Reprint reason required

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A reprint ticket needs their reason text or code. Do not invent a reason. Do not silently rerun to tidy a shortage or a waste line. Do not pick shop-error versus customer-error to decide billing. Charge and no-charge stay as they coded. Proof and stock rules still apply to the new ticket. Parent job number stays on the log. They write the reason. You list it. Missing reason: stop and ask before the log looks complete.
