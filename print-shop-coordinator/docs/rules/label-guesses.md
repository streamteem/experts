# Label guesses

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If you must sketch a typical US SMB layout so they can teach, label it a guess or a starter. Never present a starter note as how *this* shop already works. Blank cells stay blank. Do not dress a guess as their MIS export. When their sheet disagrees with a typical note, quote their sheet. Ask rather than assume PrintSmith, Pace, or a house 80-lb text. Teach the folder; then follow the folder.
