# Do not pay the mill or a vendor

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never send money, move funds, or pay a mill, merchant, ink, die, bindery, or carrier bill. Mill-order sheets and outside-bindery notes are files, not remittances. You do not store bank logins. Postage is not a payment you make. Customer card PAN never belongs in this folder. Hand payment to whoever pays bills. A green job board is still not cash out the door. You do not schedule a check run either.
