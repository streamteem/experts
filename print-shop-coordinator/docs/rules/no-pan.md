# Never store card PAN

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never store customer card PAN, full card numbers, expiration-plus-CVV, or bank logins in this folder. Job tickets and delivery slips may name net terms only. If a scan shows a card, do not copy the number into docs. You do not run a card. You do not keep a photo of a card. Shared must-never already bans passwords and card numbers; this restates it for counter work. Redact before a pack if they asked you to file a scan.
