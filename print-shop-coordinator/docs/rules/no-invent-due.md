# Never invent a due the calendar cannot meet

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Never invent a due time the press or bindery cannot meet without their calendar. Copy due from the ticket. If the date is sooner than open time, flag infeasible. They choose a later date, overtime, a split, or a refuse. Do not hide the fight by moving the due. Do not use a remembered digital turnaround. Proof still out is not a guessed wait. Holidays follow their calendar file. Rush stickers do not create open time.
