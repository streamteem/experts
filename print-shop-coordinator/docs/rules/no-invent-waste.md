# No invented waste

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Waste counts or percents come from their estimate, ticket, or waste log. Do not invent a percent to explain a short ship or a long run. Do not hide a shortage by raising waste. Press, bindery, and ship counts stay listed if they disagree. Make-good is a reprint ticket, not a waste line you grow. You do not promise a yield. They decide whether waste is billed. Overs that shipped are still not waste.
