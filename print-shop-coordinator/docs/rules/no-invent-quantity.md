# No invented quantity

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Ordered quantity comes from the ticket or customer PO. Do not invent a qty to fill a carton, a gang, or a mill pack. If PO and ticket disagree, list both. Variable-data record counts come from their merge file. Split-ship lines must add up unless they closed short in writing. You do not change qty to make overs look neat. They change qty. Make-ready adds stay separate from ordered qty.
