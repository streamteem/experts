# No invented Pantone

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Spot color names come from the ticket and the swatch file they pointed at. Do not invent a PMS from a remembered brand. Do not convert spot to process unless their file says convert. If the book (C versus U) is missing, ask. Digital simulation stays labeled if they label it. You do not promise a match without their color-standard file. Pantone pages are orientation only. House mix numbers are not PMS names unless they said so.
