# No color promise without their standard

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not promise a press will match a monitor, a phone photo, or a remembered PMS. Match words come from their color-standard file or the ticket (match proof, match book, house OK). If that file is missing, ask. Do not invent a delta-E or a G7 claim. Hard proof and press OK stay labeled. They OK the sheet. You list the standard they named. CFS stock can miss a coated house OK.
