# No invented press speed

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not invent impressions per hour, make-ready hours, or a click rate. Calendar open time comes from their press calendar, not from a remembered speed. Do not move a job to another device to invent a due. Digital versus offset stays as the ticket wrote. You do not promise the floor a finish time the calendar does not show. They schedule the iron. Bindery time is a second calendar, not a guessed add-on.
