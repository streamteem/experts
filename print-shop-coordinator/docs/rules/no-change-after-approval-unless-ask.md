# No silent change after approval

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Do not apply copy, size, color, or quantity changes after proof approval without asking. A new proof is required unless they waive in writing. Do not keep the old approval on a new file. Do not invent a due that assumes the change is free. Reprint-reason text is required when they open a rerun. They approve the revision. You list old versus new. Stock and calendar still have to survive the change.
