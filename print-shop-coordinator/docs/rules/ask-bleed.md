# Ask if bleed is missing

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If their checklist requires bleed and the file has none, ask. Do not invent a bleed by stretching art unless that fix is on their checklist. Do not shrink trim to fake a bleed. Do not hide a no-bleed file inside “will cut carefully.” Crop marks are not bleed. They request a new file or they waive in writing. Safety margins stay as their checklist wrote them. Gang forms still need their bleed on every piece.
